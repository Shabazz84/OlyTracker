# Forward jump in the snatch. Reason #weightlifting #sport #pavlukhin

**Language:** Russian coaching instruction on pulling mechanics.

**Extracted Content:**

**1. Technique Cues (Clean/Snatch Pull):**
- Bar must stay close to body throughout extension; if lats/long head triceps aren't engaged, bar drifts forward
- Position: bar over mid-foot, shoulders over bar at start
- Cue: Use lats and triceps to *pull* bar into body during knee/hip extension—don't let the bar pull *you* forward
- If lats are weak, heavy weight will dictate positioning (loss of control)

**2. Exercise Recommendation:**
- Implies lat/tricep strength is limiting factor in pull mechanics

**3. Relevance to Athlete:**
- **HIGHLY RELEVANT**: Chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg) suggest posterior chain/lat weakness. This instruction addresses the mechanical compensation pattern—bar drifting forward increases back stress
- **Priority**: Strengthen lats/lat engagement in pulls before heavy snatch pulling
- **Integration**: Fits hypertrophy block goal; direct lat work (rows, pull-ups, lat pulldowns) should be prioritized to reduce back load and improve snatch pull stability

**Key Takeaway:** Address lat weakness to improve pull mechanics and reduce back injury risk during hypertrophy phase.
