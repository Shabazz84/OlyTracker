# A deep squat is a stretched soleus. #pavlukhin #kharkiv #squats

**Language & Content:** Russian-language mobility/flexibility instruction.

**Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:**

1. **Mobility Advice (HIGH RELEVANCE)**
   - Ankle flexibility is foundational for deep squatting
   - Distinguish gastrocnemius (straight-leg stretch) vs. soleus (bent-knee stretch)
   - Soleus is the primary squat depth limiter
   - Soleus stretch: bend knees, drive knees forward, feel stretch in calf
   - Gastrocnemius stretch: straighten leg, pull toes back

**Application to Athlete Profile:**
- **Critical priority:** This athlete has chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg; primary snatch limiter). Poor ankle mobility forces compensation into lumbar spine during bottom position. Soleus stretching should be part of daily/pre-training mobility.
- **Night shift context:** Mobility work is low-cost recovery during fatigue windows.
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Daily ankle mobility work will improve squat depth and reduce compensatory back stress before technique refinement.

**Recommendation:** Add 5-min soleus/gastrocnemius stretching to warm-up 3–4× weekly, prioritize bent-knee soleus work.
