# Насколько глубоко нужно приседать #тяжелаяатлетика #присед #павлухин

**Weightlifting content: YES (Russian)** — Jerk dip mechanics & squat depth standards.

---

**1. Programming/Technique Principles:**
- Jerk dip must reach adequate depth to use elastic recoil energy to drive upward
- Elite athletes don't waste extra effort going deeper than necessary, nor do they undershoot depth (no-rep territory)
- Stopping position in space is critical—no natural "bounce point" like in back squat

**2. Technique Cues (Jerk-specific):**
- Use elastic deformation energy to absorb and redirect upward
- Achieve sufficient dip depth for mechanics to work; avoid both excessive depth and underdepth
- Elite execution: economical depth, maximal efficiency

---

**Relevance to athlete profile:**
- **Direct application:** Push jerk is current technical standard; this dip-depth principle applies immediately
- **Back pain consideration:** Proper jerk dip mechanics (controlled, elastic recoil) reduce unnecessary spinal load vs. overshooting depth
- **Hypertrophy block opportunity:** Controlled jerk dips can build leg/core stability without loading back excessively

**Recommendation:** Prioritize jerk dip depth consistency during 6-week hypertrophy block; film to verify adequate depth before split jerk introduction.
