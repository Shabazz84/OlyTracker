**Weightlifting content: Yes**

---

**1. Programming Principles**
- Deep squatting emphasized as foundational for snatch, clean, and jerk mechanics

**2. Technique Cues**
- **Squat Depth**: Maximum depth required because snatch, jerk, and clean catch positions demand it
- **Purpose**: Utilize elastic energy from deep positions to absorb load and drive upward ("амортизировать вверх" = cushion/absorb upward)

**3. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
- **CRITICAL**: OHS limited at 50 kg; deep squat capacity directly impacts snatch receiving position. Prioritize squat depth work during hypertrophy block
- **Back pain consideration**: Deep squatting with chronic back pain requires strict form; may need tempo/paused work rather than heavy loads initially
- **Clean/Jerk**: Deep receiving positions essential—split jerk training should emphasize catch depth once technique allows

**4. Exercise Recommendation**
- Paused/tempo back squats and front squats to build depth tolerance safely given back history
