2. **Technique cues**: 
   - **Snatch/Clean Pull & Catch**: Engage the long head of the triceps to pull the bar close to the body during leg extension; prevent the bar from drifting forward or "pulling" the lifter away.
   - **Bar Path**: Keep shoulders over the bar at the start; ensure the bar stays directly above the mid-foot (metatarsophalangeal joints).
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specific, but implies strengthening the long head of the triceps and lats to control the bar path.

5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: 
   - **Chronic Back Pain**: The cue to "pull the bar close" using the back muscles (lats/triceps) rather than letting the bar pull the lifter forward is critical for maintaining spinal stability and reducing shear forces on the lower back during lifts.
   - **No Split Jerk**: While this text focuses on the pull/catch phase common to both Snatch and Clean, the emphasis on keeping the shoulders over the bar applies strictly to the overhead stability required for a Push Jerk.
