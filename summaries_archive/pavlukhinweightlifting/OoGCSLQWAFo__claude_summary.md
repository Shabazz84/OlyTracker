# Штангист должен приседать каждый день #тяжелаяатлетика

**Language:** Russian (Soviet-era weightlifting philosophy)

**Content Summary:** Philosophical approach to training stress and adaptation.

---

**1. Programming Principles**
- Keep body in constant stress state to drive adaptation
- Squatting frequency: ideally daily (small doses for weightlifters)
- Strength accumulates through frequent heavy movement exposure
- Training is a linear progression, not isolated sessions
- Higher-level athletes achieve smoother, more consistent progress curves
