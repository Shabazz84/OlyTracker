## 2. **Technique Cues for Back Squat**

**Bar Position & Posture:**
- Bar placement: **upper trapezius, below the base of neck** (not high on shoulders)
- Maintain **vertical torso** in Olympic lifting squats (differs from powerlifting)
- Forward knee translation is acceptable; wide stance not needed for Olympic lifting

**Stance & Depth:**
- Feet at **shoulder width** (minimal—wider stance is powerlifting-specific)
- **Maximum depth required** for Olympic lifting catch mechanics
- Deeper squat captures elastic energy for rapid ascent

**Movement Pattern:**
- Drive from **quads + glutes** (forward torso lean engages glutes during ascent)
- Use elastic bar deformation to rebound upward
- Athletes should "catch" depth precisely—not oversquat or undershquat

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Back squats in Olympic lifting context = catch position training + elastic energy utilization
- Differentiation: powerlifting uses wider stance, high bar placement for quad emphasis; Olympic lifting uses narrower stance, lower bar placement to engage posterior chain
