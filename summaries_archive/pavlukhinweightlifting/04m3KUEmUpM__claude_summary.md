# Низкий и высокий старт в рывке #павлухин #тяжелаяатлетика #рывок

**Content:** Weightlifting coaching - snatch technique

---

**1. TECHNIQUE CUES (Snatch)**
- **High vs. low start position**: High start position achieves faster pull velocity to extension (подрыв) compared to low start
- **Implication for this athlete**: With weak OHS (50 kg), repositioning mechanics matters. High start reduces time under tension but may expose mobility limitations. Low start allows more acceleration but requires stronger spinal stability with chronic back pain.

**2. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLE**
- Use snatch pull loads at ~100% 1RM snatch weight to test/compare positions

**3. RELEVANT TO ATHLETE PROFILE**
- **Back pain concern**: Emphasize high-start position (safer, less spinal loading time) over low start during hypertrophy block
- **OHS limiter**: Root issue may be position mechanics, not just mobility—high start reduces compensation patterns
- **Note**: Coach emphasizes proper technique acquisition early rather than relearning habits

---

**Recommendation**: Test high-start pulls at 80 kg snatch pull weight before committing to position change.
