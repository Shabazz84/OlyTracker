# В рывке, как в становой #тяжелаяатлетика

**WEIGHTLIFTING COACHING TRANSCRIPT - RUSSIAN**

---

**1. TECHNIQUE CUES (Clean/Snatch Pull):**
- Keep shins more vertical in setup
- Allow bar to naturally travel to hip/groin as knees extend—don't artificially pull it toward body
- Pull should be structured so bar arrives at hip naturally from proper positioning, not muscular yanking
- Emphasis on even, complete leg extension throughout pull

**2. RELEVANCE TO ATHLETE PROFILE:**
- **Directly addresses snatch/clean technique**: Vertical shin position and efficient bar path are critical for this athlete's weak OHS (50 kg) and Clean (80 kg). Poor pull mechanics may compound weak positioning in receiving positions.
- **Back-friendly cue**: Allowing bar to naturally float to hip (vs. aggressive chest-up pulling) reduces shear stress—beneficial given chronic back pain.

**3. NO PROGRAMMING, RECOVERY, OR MOBILITY ADVICE IN TEXT**

---

**Key takeaway for this athlete:** Focus on vertical shins and passive bar proximity—likely blocking snatch/clean technique progression more than strength currently.
