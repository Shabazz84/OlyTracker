**1. Programming Principles**
- Train high-threshold motor units (ВПД) early in sessions
- Primary work: **single repetitions** (одноповторная)
- Avoid metabolic fatigue/acidosis in this phase
- Emphasize intent: powerful, fast execution with technical precision
- Neural/CNS work (not hypertrophy-focused)

**2. Technique Cues**
- Frame motor task as "powerful and fast" while maintaining technical standards
- Cue neural drive as brain-muscle activation

**3. Relevant to This Athlete**
- **Strong fit for his 6-week hypertrophy block transition:** Starting with singles on high-threshold units builds strength foundation before hypertrophy work
- **Back pain consideration:** Single reps allow strict form control; avoid metabolic fatigue that might compromise lumbar stability
- **Night shift context:** Singles are less systemically taxing than volume-based work, potentially better tolerated with poor sleep recovery
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Early singles work on snatch/clean patterns could address mobility/stability deficits before hypertrophy
