# Толчок Штанги. Кристина и Тяжёлая Атлетика

## Key Points
*   **Training Frequency & Intensity:** Utilize high-frequency training (4 consecutive days) focusing on regular exposure to 80–90% intensity rather than low-volume max attempts. Prioritize the phosphagen system via low rep ranges (1–2 reps) for main lifts; reserve higher volume strictly for technique work and warm-ups.
*   **Reactive Periodization:** Abandon fixed dates in favor of daily readiness monitoring. If fatigue accumulates, immediately reduce volume or intensity rather than forcing a PR. Accept "spads" (performance drops) as natural physiological responses to accumulated fatigue.
*   **Injury Management & Recovery:** Do not push through sharp pain in the lower back or elbows; reduce load immediately if symptoms arise. Rest intervals must be determined by aerobic recovery (return of normal breathing rate/oxygen debt repayment), not fixed timers. Use taping for wrist/finger support if needed.

## Technique Cues
*   **Grip & Catch:** Avoid a tight "finger lock" grip which causes triceps tendonitis. Keep wrists relaxed to allow the bar to roll into the palm during the catch ("catch in flight").
*   **Push Jerk Execution:** Execute a short, fast dip (*korotko i bystro*) rather than a deep squat. The bar must travel strictly vertically and slightly back toward the shoulder line. Maintain high, active elbows throughout the movement.
*   **Post-Catch Transition:** Do not hold the rack position; transition immediately to standing to maximize speed and efficiency.
*   **Elbow Health:** Strengthen triceps and extension muscles specifically to withstand the sharp impact of the bar during jerks, preventing olecranon bursitis/tendonitis common in athletes with explosive overhead movements.

## Programming Principles
*   **Primary Lifts:** Snatch, Clean & Jerk (Push Jerk only), and "Clean from Chest" (thrusting).
*   **Contraindicated Movements:** **Avoid Deadlifts** entirely due to chronic lower back pain; avoid heavy squats in high volume. These place excessive axial load on the spine for this specific profile.
*   **Accessory Selection:**
    *   **Triceps Extensions:** Essential for strengthening the olecranon tendon and preventing elbow injury.
    *   **Pull-ups:** For shoulder and elbow stability.
    *   **Rack Position Presses/Jerks:** Light overhead work from a rack position to build confidence before transitioning to chest-to-jerk movements.
*   **Warm-up Protocol:** A dynamic warm-up is mandatory; tissue temperature must reach 37°C prior to loading to prevent injury.

## Athlete Relevance
*   **Back Pain Mitigation:** The program explicitly excludes deadlifts and heavy floor pulls, replacing them with "Clean from Chest" and Push Jerks to minimize erector spinae strain while maintaining Olympic lifting specificity.
*   **Night Shift Adaptation:** Given the Wed–Sun night shift schedule, recovery is highly individualized. The athlete must prioritize listening to daily fatigue levels over rigid programming; if sleep or energy is low due to shift work, volume/intensity must be reduced to prevent injury.
*   **Transition Strategy:** Leverage the athlete's powerlifting "fighting spirit" and intermediate strength base (102.5 kg BW) but redirect this mental toughness toward technical precision and speed rather than raw maximal loading.
*   **Specific Limiter Focus:** Address the 50 kg Overhead Squat limitation through specific triceps strengthening and technique refinement, as weak extension muscles are a primary driver of elbow pain in this demographic.
