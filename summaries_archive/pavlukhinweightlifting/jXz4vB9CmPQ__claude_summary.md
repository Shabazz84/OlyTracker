**1. Technique Cue (Snatch/Clean/Jerk)**
- **Foot position is foundational**: Athletes must maintain full foot contact throughout the lift (snatch and jerk emphasized). Heels or toes lifting = force loss. Full foot stability = max force expression.
- Implication for this athlete: Critical for improving 50 kg OHS and 80 kg clean—foot stability affects force transfer and overhead positioning.

**2. Relevant to Profile**
- **Chronic back pain context**: Full foot contact and stable base reduce compensatory lumbar stress compared to heels-up or toe-up positions (common stability leaks).
- Prioritize foot cue work before load increases in hypertrophy block.

**3. Assessment Principle**
- Don't evaluate movement holistically ("good/bad"). Instead isolate key technical faults (foot position first).

---

**Actionable takeaway**: In your 6-week hypertrophy block, film snatch/clean/jerk from the side and check foot position before progressing loads. Back-friendly baseline.
