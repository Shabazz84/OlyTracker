# Barbells, excess weight, and bodybuilding. Golgi receptors.

## Key Points

- **Programming**: 3–5x/week heavy training feasible; daily training possible if split into short sessions (~1hr each). Week-to-week load progression acceptable if technique remains solid.
- **Jerk Technique**: Back leg must bend in split jerk; push bar to forehead height and move under it (athlete currently push jerk only—critical limitation).
- **Back Pain Management**: Lower back pain ("wandering," non-acute) reduces power expression and limits performance. Root cause should be addressed through programming modification rather than relying on NSAIDs (meloxicam preferred, 5-day max use). Consider this a primary constraint, not just a symptom.
- **Recovery**: Foam rolling between sets; anti-inflammatory use only short-term while adjusting training.
- **Nutrition**: Protein based on **lean body mass**, not total weight. At 102.5 kg with estimated 15–20% body fat (~80–85 kg lean), target ~150g protein/day.

## Technique Cues

- **Core/Lower Back**: Keep lumbar spine neutral; initiate movement from flexion (not extension + flexion). Avoid ballistic/jerky reps on isolation work.
- **Split Jerk (required next step)**: Back leg MUST bend; push bar to forehead, athlete moves under. Film to verify bar trajectory.

## Programming Principles

- Load progression week-to-week is valid if technique quality maintained.
- Short, frequent sessions preferable to long single sessions given night shift schedule (Wed–Sun).
- 6-week hypertrophy block → technique/strength progression aligns with peaking structure (light/heavy/deload weeks into competition).

## Athlete Relevance

**Critical**: OHS 50 kg and no split jerk are primary limiters. Back pain will compound both. Prioritize neutral lumbar spine in all core work and split jerk development early in hypertrophy block. Protein intake realistic at ~150g/day. Night shift requires flexible, session-based recovery.
