# Отдых между подходами и митохондрии #тяжелаяатлетика

**Text Language & Content:** Russian discussion on overtraining recovery and conditioning.

---

**EXTRACTED COACHING POINTS:**

1. **Recovery/Rest Intervals**
   - Rest between sets: state-dependent (5 min some days, 2 min others)
   - Competition standard: 60 seconds between attempts
   - Overtraining prolongs recovery due to CNS fatigue, not just muscle soreness

2. **Cardiorespiratory Conditioning** (HIGHLY RELEVANT to this athlete)
   - Poor prep = inadequate mitochondrial density & respiratory capacity
   - Heavyweights often fail attempts due to breathing/fatigue, not strength
   - Solution: low-intensity steady-state work
     - Walking, cycling at low heart rate
     - Emphasis: breathing practice, frequent movement
   - Rationale: weightlifters need leg-specific mitochondrial capacity (legs drive all movements)

3. **General Philosophy**
   - Protect joints
   - Avoid sitting (athletes are sedentary)
   - Build aerobic base via easy, consistent activity

---

**RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE:**
- **Night shift + chronic back pain + low OHS** → aerobic conditioning & breathing work could improve recovery, reduce fatigue-related form breakdown, and support hypertrophy block sustainability
- **Push jerk only (no split)** → inadequate leg conditioning may explain reliance on upper body
- Low-intensity cycling/walking fits around night shift schedule
