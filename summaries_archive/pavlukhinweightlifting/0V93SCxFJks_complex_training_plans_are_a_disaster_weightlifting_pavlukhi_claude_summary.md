# Complex training plans are a disaster 🏋🏻‍♂️￼ #weightlifting #pavlukhin

**Language:** Russian coaching discussion about training periodization.

**Key Extractions:**

1. **Programming Principles**
   - Light/heavy training alternation: beginner = week light/week heavy rotation
   - Advanced athletes can handle 2-3 heavy weeks per 1 light week
   - Higher technical proficiency = lower fatigue accumulation

2. **Technique Principle**
   - Better technique = less muscular fatigue (relies more on tendon/connective tissue)
   - Technical, vertical movements are more efficient
   - Technique quality directly influences recovery needs

3. **Recovery Insight**
   - Previous session recovery influences next session readiness
