**EXTRACTED CONTENT:**

**2. Technique Cues – Jerk:**
- **Body drives the bar, not the arm.** Arm is passive during extension; it only "guides" the bar relaxed (like snatch principle).
- **Common error:** Pressing at lockout = incorrect. Arm engagement mid-drive = pressing, not jerking.
- **Lockout mechanics:** Simultaneous knee bend + arm fixation. Become "a statue" in that moment.
- **Grip/hold:** Not gripping hard; just holding/supporting on palms at lockout.
- **Reference:** Watch Torokhtiy's jerk for proper bar path.

---

**RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE:**

🔴 **HIGH PRIORITY:**
- Push jerk only = arm-dominant pattern likely developing. This cue ("body drives, arm follows") is **critical** for technical progression before split jerk introduction.
- Chronic back pain + improper arm engagement = increased spinal shear stress.

⚠️ **Application:** Emphasize hip/leg extension timing in jerk recovery drills; delay arm fixation until bar trajectory is established.
