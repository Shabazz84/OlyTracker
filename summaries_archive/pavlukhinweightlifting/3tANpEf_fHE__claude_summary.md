# Как Приседают Штангисты #тяжелаяатлетика #приседания

**Language Note:** Text is in Russian; translating for analysis.

---

**1. Programming Principles**
- Distinguishes between explosive/ballistic work (Olympic lifts) vs. constant-tension hypertrophy work (machines)
- Ballistic movements: load distributed across tendons + muscular contraction; less hypertrophy stimulus
- Machine/constant-tension work: sustained muscle tension → greater hypertrophy response

**2. Technique Cues**
- "Pull and explode" — rapid muscle contraction via tendon elasticity (amortization)
- Phases of relaxation between contractions in explosive movements

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Machine leg work (leg press variant) as hypertrophy alternative to oly lifts
- Implies constant-tension exercises more effective for muscle growth than ballistic movements

---

**Relevance to Athlete:**
✓ **Directly applicable:** 6-week hypertrophy block should prioritize constant-tension exercises (machines, slower tempos) over Olympic lift variations for leg/squat work
✓ **Chronic back pain concern:** Lying leg press potentially safer than heavy back squats; worth exploring
✗ **Push jerk only limitation:** This ballistic nature means less hypertrophy; leg press should supplement
✓ **Night shift fatigue:** Machine work is CNS-sparing vs. explosive training
