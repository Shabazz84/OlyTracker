# Поэтому ты мажешь при рывке #тяжелаяатлетика #павлухин

**Language:** Russian (not English text provided)

**Content Summary:** Coaching cue about elbow position during the pull phase—specifically that elbows shouldn't bend excessively during the explosion (подрыва/upward pull), or the bar won't travel forward properly.

**Relevant to athlete:**
- **Technique cue (Clean/Snatch):** Keep elbows extended/high during the pull; premature elbow bend kills horizontal bar trajectory
- **Applies to clean (80 kg):** May improve pull consistency and catch position
- **Not directly addressing:** OHS limitation (50 kg), chronic back pain, or push jerk → split jerk progression

**Actionable:** Reinforce vertical elbows in pull phases; minimal bend until third pull/turnover. Relevant for technique refinement phase (post-hypertrophy block).
