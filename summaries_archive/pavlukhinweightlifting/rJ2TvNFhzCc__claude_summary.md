**1. Technique Cues (Clean/Jerk Extension):**
- Knee extension must be *straight/direct*, not "wrapped around" or circular
- Avoid compensating with knee abduction/valgus collapse when knees track outward
- Bar path should be vertical and close to body during extension phase
- Drive legs straight up (extension), then pull bar close via lat/tricep engagement ("pulling toward you")
- Film from the side to verify bar travels vertically, not in an arc

**2. Relevance to Athlete Profile:**
- **High priority:** Chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg) suggests poor positioning in catch/recovery. This cue addresses bar path efficiency, reducing front-knee stress and rear-chain demand.
- **Push jerk limiter:** Clean extension quality directly impacts jerk rack position. Straighter leg drive = more stable dip position and better vertical jerk drive.

**Recommendation:** Apply this knee-extension cue to both clean pulls and clean singles before jerk work. Video analysis essential given back pain history (verify neutral spine during extension, not forward collapse).
