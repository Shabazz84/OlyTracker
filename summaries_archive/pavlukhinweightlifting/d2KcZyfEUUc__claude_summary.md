# Толчок Штанги. Кристина и Тяжёлая Атлетика

## Key Points

- **Competition lift priority**: Base 50–60% of training volume on snatch, clean, and jerk; minimize supplementals
- **Spinal loading**: Avoid heavy deadlifts due to chronic back pain; use rack squats instead
- **Jerk development**: Athlete must transition from push jerk to split jerk before hypertrophy block—current push jerk ceiling limits total output
- **Rest protocol**: Use extended rest (5+ min) based on breathing recovery, not fixed intervals
- **OHS bottleneck**: Wrist mobility/stability limits snatch progression; address before intensity work
- **Night shift fatigue**: Monitor volume after consecutive training days; athlete fatigued after 4 consecutive sessions

---

## Technique Cues

**Jerk (critical for this athlete):**
- Firm wrist grip; relax wrist during catch to allow bar to settle quickly
- Brief, explosive elbow turnover—avoid delayed catch
- Short, fast dip with vertical torso; knees forward
- Bar lands on shoulders with minimal horizontal travel; deep, narrow split
- **Common fault**: Decelerating bar mid-flight from tension/fear—relax and let bar settle fast

**Split jerk introduction:**
- Athlete demonstrates good split depth; focus on trusting the catch and avoiding tension-induced deceleration

---

## Programming Principles

- Base 50–60% of work on classic lifts; minimize supplemental exercises
- Use 1-rep or low-rep sets with extended rest (5+ min) based on breathing recovery
- Periodize with deload weeks after intensity spikes
- Emphasize frequent practice of competition lifts
- Avoid spinal loading (no heavy deadlifts); prioritize rack squats
- Include tricep/arm extension work to strengthen elbows for jerk safety
- Warm-up is non-negotiable—raise muscle/tendon temperature to prevent injury

---

## Athlete Relevance

- **Push jerk → split jerk**: Essential before 6-week hypertrophy block; current push jerk is artificial ceiling
- **OHS (50 kg)**: Wrist mobility/stability is primary snatch limiter; address immediately
- **Back pain management**: Deadlifts excluded; rack squats and explosive work prioritized
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Adjust volume expectations after 4 consecutive training days; monitor fatigue
- **Strength baseline**: Back squat 118 kg, clean 80 kg (gap indicates technique/positioning issue), jerk 65 kg—jerk development high priority
- **Mindset**: Athlete is tenacious but prone to exceeding adaptive capacity; coach must manage intensity to prevent burnout
