# Maximum repetitions don't tire the muscles #weightlifting #sport #pavlukhin

**Language:** Russian (coaching discussion)

**Relevant Extracts:**

1. **Programming Principles:**
   - Heavy singles (max effort work) don't cause muscular fatigue/acidosis or muscle soreness—fatigues CNS, not muscle tissue
   - **Volume should follow heavy work, not precede it** (do heavy first, then volume) because volume causes fatigue that impairs max-effort loading on tendons/CNS
   - Heavy work should be performed when fresh to maximize tendon/nervous system stimulus

2. **Recovery/Load Management:**
   - Distinguishes between CNS fatigue (from heavy singles) vs. muscular fatigue (from volume)
   - Example given: Performing 10 heavy presses (e.g., 100 kg × 10) depletes capacity for subsequent maximal pressing

   - **CRITICAL**: Given chronic back pain, this sequencing (heavy→volume) is relevant but requires careful monitoring
   - Night shift worker → CNS fatigue management especially important
   - Hypertrophy block (6 weeks) should follow heavy blocks with adequate recovery spacing
