# Merged Summary: Weightlifting-Relevant Content

## Key Points
- **Back pain management**: Stop squat progression when lower back tension appears; isolate core work (Superman holds, hip flexion) separately rather than relying on belt support at max loads.
- **Scissor position jerk mechanics**: Rear leg knee bend is critical—poor flexion causes hip/spinal compensation and rotation risk. Reference quality model: Ravil Jafarov technique.
- **Periodization**: 3–4 week blocks alternating classic lift focus → hypertrophy block prevents monotony and maintains adherence.
- **Load management principle**: Total stress (load × volume × duration) drives adaptation; variety sustains motivation.
- **Tendon/connective tissue adaptation**: Requires consistent training over months; gradual progressive introduction prevents Golgi tendon reflex blocking (e.g., start 30 kg scissor squats → progressive build).
- **Upper body stability platform**: Chest/anterior shoulder + upper back (traps) development supports clean/jerk receiving position.
- **Caloric approach**: ~2,800 kcal/day maintenance at 102.5 kg; flexibility on weekly averages over daily precision supports recomposition (fat loss + muscle gain).
- **CNS fatigue**: Cumulative stress (shift work, content creation, high training frequency) reduces performance ~10% during heavy cycles.

## Technique Cues
- **Jerk**: Scissor position—emphasize rear leg knee flexion to prevent spinal rotation and hip distortion.
- **Pull-ups**: Neutral/parallel grip distributes load evenly across forearms/lats, reduces bicep/anterior delt/elbow strain vs. wide grip.
- **Foundational strength enables technique**: Build upper body stability before expecting efficient classic lift mechanics.

## Programming Principles
- Alternate 3–4 week blocks: classic lift focus ↔ hypertrophy block.
- Avoid high-frequency daily maxes without proper tissue preparation.
- Adjust intensity based on readiness (CNS fatigue from night shift, recovery demands).
- Core/stability work separate from maximal squat loads.

## Athlete Relevance
- **OHS 50 kg limiter + jerk limitation**: Scissor squat work directly addresses jerk mobility/stability gap and tendon conditioning.
- **Chronic back pain + night shift**: High daily squat frequency inadvisable; prioritize selective core work, progressive tendon preparation, and load-managed hypertrophy block.
- **6-week hypertrophy block plan**: Aligns with periodization model; incorporate scissor squats (jerk prep), bench press, weighted pull-ups, and isolated core to build stability platform for split jerk progression.
- **Caloric target 2,800 kcal/day**: Supports recomposition at 102.5 kg; weekly flexibility suits shift work inconsistency.
