# BERESTOVTEAM COACHING PHILOSOPHY
## Synthesized for Intermediate Olympic Weightlifter (102.5kg BW, Night Shift, Chronic Back Pain)

---

## 1. CORE TRAINING PHILOSOPHY (5 Principles)

**A. Technique Precedes Load**
- Movement quality gates intensity progression. Master positioning before adding weight.
- Vertical bar paths (snatch/clean pulls, jerk drives) and rigid torso mechanics are non-negotiable.
- Night shift fatigue amplifies compensatory patterns—technical precision becomes *more* critical when CNS is taxed.

**B. Spinal Integrity Over Range**
- Chronic back pain demands absolute vertical torso maintenance in overhead positions (push jerk, OHS, standing press).
- Depth prioritized below stability—shallow squat with perfect spinal alignment beats deep squat with flexion/extension.
- Bar position (traps for squats, mid-palm for jerk) and controlled eccentric tempos reduce lumbar shear stress.

**C. Individual Variation is Normal**
- Hip height in pulls, grip width, dip depth, and stance geometry vary by morphology and mobility—no universal template.
- Assess and adjust based on *your* mobility baseline; widen/adjust positions only after demonstrating stability at current setting.

**D. Triple Extension Synchronization**
- Leg, back, and trapezius must fire simultaneously during pulls and jerks—never sequentially.
- Over-reliance on back extension (especially under fatigue) risks lumbar overload; prioritize leg-driven patterns.
- Straps recommended for pulls to isolate mechanical patterns from grip fatigue.

**E. Mobility as Root Cause**
- Shoulder/thoracic mobility deficit is the primary snatch bottleneck (50 kg OHS vs. 80 kg clean).
- Address mobility directly via warm-up and accessory work, not by compensating with stance/grip adjustments.
- Consistent mobility drills (band dislocates, 90/90 hip openers, thoracic rotation) unlock technical gains.

## 2. PROGRAMMING APPROACH

### **Periodization Structure (6-Week Hypertrophy Block → Strength)**

**Hypertrophy Block (Weeks 1–6) — *Current Focus***
- **Primary lifts:** Back squat (supplemental receiving position work), clean + push jerk
- **Volume emphasis:** 6–8 rep range at 70–80% intensity; controlled tempo (2-sec down, 1-sec up)
- **Half-squat/half-push combinations:** 3 reps + 1 full jerk at 70–80% (pairs technical work with loading)
- **Accessory frequency:** Push press 3–4x/week (jerk + OHS shoulder stability); standing press 3–4x/week (mobility focus); snatch pulls 2–3x/week (leg power emphasis)
- **Recovery-focused:** Lower CNS demand compared to strength phase; suits Wed–Sun night shift schedule

**Strength Phase (Weeks 7+) — *Post-Hypertrophy***
- Progress to 3–5 rep range, 85–95% intensity
- Introduce split jerk (footwork drill → low-load practice → gradual load increase)
- Snatch pulls progress to heavy singles/doubles
- OHS work escalates as mobility improves

### **Weekly Structure (Wednesday–Sunday Night Shift)**
- **Wed–Thu (post-sleep):** Technical/accessory focus (moderate intensity, precise cueing)
- **Fri–Sat (mid-week fatigue):** Moderate main lifts + higher-rep accessory work
- **Sun (pre-rest):** Light technical work, mobility emphasis
- **Avoid:** Heavy singles/2RM attempts on Sat–Sun (fatigue + sleep debt = compensation)

### **Intensity Management**
- **80%+ loads:** Use belt (secured at lumbar level, not too high)
- **Knee sleeves (7mm):** Maintain joint warmth during heavy back squat/half-squat work
- **Bar selection:** Use thin bars (15kg women's bar, 10kg) for explosive jerk/pull work with light loads to maximize bar whip and timing feedback
- **Load progression:** Submaximal (95–105% 1RM) before max attempts; emphasize eccentric control (lower bar 3–4 sec) under heavy overhead work

## 3. KEY TECHNIQUE CUES & EXERCISE PREFERENCES

### **SNATCH (Primary Limiter: 50kg OHS)**
- **Pull (Рывковая тяга):**
  - Setup: Shoulders over bar, neutral spine, full foot contact
  - Drive phase: Synchronized triple extension (legs + back + traps)—NO sequencing
  - **Critical:** Vertical bar path first, THEN into body; avoid premature thigh contact
  - Finishing cue: "Pull straight up; back extension follows naturally, not leads"
  - Common error: Premature spinal extension backward → bar drifts forward → poor OHS reception
  - **Cueing sequence:** Shoulder position → knee passage → full extension (avoid overthinking knee path; it's reflexive)

- **Snatch-Specific Accessories:**
  - **Snatch pulls from platform** (elevated start): Forces higher hip position, corrects early hip-rise error
  - **Snatch pulls from hip** (5–10cm descent): Explosive triple extension focus; use leg-dominant variant if back pain flares
  - **Snatch-grip overhead walk** (50% load, slow reps): Coordinates arm extension + leg descent, builds OHS confidence
  - **Snatch-grip receives from chest** (30–50kg, high reps): Rapid footwork + high-pull coordination
  - **Disc work (snatch on discs):** Progress disc height; forces harder leg drive; safer for back (straight-leg landing emphasis)
