## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS

### 1. **Exercise Recommendation**
- **Snatch-Grip Receives from Chest** (рывковым хватом уходы от груди)
  - Warm-up/technique drill combining two movements: high pulling position + receiving footwork
  - **Weight:** 30–50 kg max (light, not for loading)
  - **Reps:** 3–5 reps per set, high tempo/speed work
  - **Grip:** No straps/hooks—open grip to strengthen wrists
  - **Purpose:** Coordinate rapid footwork under the bar, reinforce stable torso position in high pull

### 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Starting position:** Fully extended legs, elbows high, bar pressed into chest, chin slightly raised
- **Bar path:** Must stay at same height—athlete moves *under* bar, not bar moves
- **Common error:** Leaning back or pushing bar forward (loss of stability)
- **Execution:** Hold pause in receiving position (1–3 sec), gradually increase pause duration; can perform without pause initially

### 3. **Recovery/Programming Notes**
- Use as warm-up combination (pairs well with: low-position pressing, walking, etc.)
- High-rep, low-load, speed-based exercise
- Emphasizes wrist stability and foot speed

## **RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE**
- ✅ **Useful for:** OHS/snatch receiving position (primary limiter at 50 kg)
- ✅ **Back-friendly:** Light load, controlled footwork
- ⚠️ **Caution:** Pause work requires solid core stability; monitor chronic back pain response
