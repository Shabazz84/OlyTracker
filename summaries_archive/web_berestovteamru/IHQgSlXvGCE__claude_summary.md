## 2. **Technique Cues**

**Lowering Barbell (Press/Push Jerk/Jerk from Rack):**
- **Dampen impact** via three mechanisms:
  1. Active arm control—don't fully relax; guide the bar down slowly
  2. **Knee bend (pause squat)** in catch position
  3. **Ankle articulation**: rise on toes → land on heels (eccentric ankle dorsiflexion)
- **Critical: Maintain vertical torso**—avoid leaning back; keep same posture as drive phase (hips back slightly, sitting into glutes)
- **For submaximal loads (95–105%, 1RM)**: lower behind neck onto **traps** (not neck), positioned same as back squat (OHS position)
- **Bar placement**: slightly below C7, on trapezius—avoid neck pressure

**Common Error**: Athletes lean torso backward during catch, overloading lower back and knees → injury risk.
