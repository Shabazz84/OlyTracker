## **RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

### **Technique Cue (Push Jerk):**
- **Backward displacement is an error** if >½ foot; minimal displacement (<½ foot) is acceptable but should be eliminated
- **Root cause:** Force vector directed backward/upward instead of purely vertical → reduced bar acceleration despite leg drive
- **Correction:** Direct all leg drive effort *straight up* into the bar overhead, not into ground behind you
- **Postural cues:**
  - Stay on the same spot (don't shift center of gravity)
  - Avoid compensating by shifting hips/torso forward (opposite error)
  - Drive effort "into the ceiling"

### **Relevance to Athlete:**
⚠️ **Critical:** With chronic back pain, backward displacement during push jerks increases shear stress. This cue aligns forces vertically—safer for lumbar spine.

Since athlete only does **push jerk (no split jerk)**, this backward-drive error is a priority technical fix before load increases.
