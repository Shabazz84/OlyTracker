## **1. Programming Principles**
- **Power Clean Pull (Протяжка на грудь)** = warm-up/accessory exercise, not primary strength work
- Used pre-clean to activate movement patterns
- Classified as a "strength exercise" but contextually a technical primer

---

## **2. Technique Cues for Clean Pull**
- **Starting position:** Hip height slightly elevated (like snatch pull), shoulders over bar
- **Foot stance:** Shoulder-width, toes slightly out (same as clean)
- **Grip:** Open grip preferred over hook grip
- **Movement:** Straight bar path—NO mid-thigh contact, NO full pull, NO squat
- **Arm mechanics (KEY):** 
  - Elbows drive strictly upward first
  - "Screw in" movement—internal rotation of shoulders
  - Keep bar close to body throughout
- **Primary error corrected:** Bar drifting away from body (common beginner flaw)

---

## **3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Clean pull as warm-up drill before main clean work
