**2. Technique cues**
*   **Catching the Bar (Snatch/Clean):** Do not drop the bar "hard"; actively "meet" it by slowing its descent with arms while simultaneously dropping into a squat.
*   **Ankle Mechanics:** Initiate the catch by rising slightly onto the toes ("metatarsals") to create height, then immediately land on heels to utilize the ankle as an additional shock absorber (like parkour landing).
*   **Torso Position:** Maintain strict vertical torso alignment; do not lean back. Keep hips "pulled back" as if sitting in a chair throughout the descent.
*   **Maximal Loads (>95%):** For single reps at max effort, catch the bar on the shoulders/upper traps (behind the neck) rather than the chest to avoid spinal compression from impact.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Drills:** Low-weight practice of "meeting" the bar with a toe-then-heel landing sequence.
*   **Variation:** Practice catching the bar on the upper traps/shoulders for high-intensity singles (Clean & Jerk or Snatch from rack).

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Spine Protection:** Explicitly warns against spinal compression injuries caused by dropping heavy weights onto the chest without amortization.
*   **Joint Health:** Emphasizes protecting knees and spine through proper ankle and knee flexion mechanics during the catch.
