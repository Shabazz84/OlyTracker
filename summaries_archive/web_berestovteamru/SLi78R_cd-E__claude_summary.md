## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS

**1. TECHNIQUE CUES (Primary Focus)**
- **Snatch-grip overhead walk**: Wide snatch grip, bar on shoulders behind head
- **Key coordination**: Legs descend (подседа), arms extend upward — opposite vectors
- **Common error**: Beginners grip too hard with arms, bar gets caught mid-range, no upward movement
- **Cueing progression**:
  - Start on balls of feet (easier knee flexion angle)
  - Slow reps with empty bar/stick to understand movement pattern
  - Gradually accelerate once pattern is locked in
- **Variation**: Bar set at shoulder height in racks (isolates arm/hand positioning, prevents bar dropout)

**2. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES**
- **Intensity**: ~50% of max (NOT a heavy lift—technique/neuromuscular focus, no leg drive)
- **Volume**: 3–5 reps per set (NOT max attempts)
- **Purpose**: Speed, coordination, neuromuscular synchronization—not strength
- **Cue**: Don't chase records on this movement

**3. RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE**
- **OHS 50 kg limiter**: This walk variation directly addresses overhead stability and arm extension coordination—likely beneficial for snatch positioning
- **Chronic back pain**: Wide-grip overhead carries may aggravate; recommend conservative loading, monitor spinal position
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Can fit as technical work, lower CNS demand than heavy strength
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: This is not hypertrophy work; would be accessory/technique session material
