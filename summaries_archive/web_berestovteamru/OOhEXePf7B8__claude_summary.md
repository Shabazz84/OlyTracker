## **Extracted Content:**

### **1. Programming Principles**
- Warm-up should precede every session
- General warm-up before technical/complex movements
- 10-15 reps per mobility exercise recommended

### **2. Technique Cues (Mobility-Focused)**
- **Spine extension:** Full back arch with exhalation/effort
- **Thoracic rotation:** Open chest, avoid shoulder pinching; emphasis on chest/rib expansion
- **Spinal mobility:** Keep spine extended (neutral to slight extension), maintain upright posture throughout—avoid rounding
- **Knee drive:** Elbows push knees outward; maintain tension, 10-15 sec hold
- **Shoulder mobility:** Band/stick shoulder pass-throughs; use snatch or slightly wider grip; rotate backward with shoulder elevation; narrow grip over time for increased mobility

### **3. Exercise Recommendations**
All performed with mat/carpet for comfort:
- Back extensions (cat-cow variant)
- Thoracic rotation flows
- Seated knee-to-chest rotations
- Forward fold to toes (hamstring/thoracic mobility)
- 90/90 hip opening (elbow-knee drives)
- Band/stick shoulder dislocates

### **4. Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- **Directly relevant to athlete:** Emphasizes spinal positioning (extension/neutral) and thoracic mobility—critical given chronic back pain
- Regular mobility work improves range of motion
- Adaptable difficulty (adjust grip width, use padding for knees)

### **5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **Chronic back pain:** Warm-up emphasizes maintaining spinal extension and neutral posture; avoiding rounding
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Shoulder and thoracic mobility drills directly address snatch bottleneck
- **Night shift fatigue:** Simple, efficient 10-15 rep warm-up format suits recovery needs
