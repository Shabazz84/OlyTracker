# Master Synthesis: Olympic Weightlifting Coaching Philosophy
## Athlete: 102.5 kg | BS 118 kg | Clean 80 kg | Jerk 65 kg (push) | OHS 50 kg | Chronic back pain | Night shift (Wed–Sun)

---

## 1. CONSENSUS PRINCIPLES (3+ Sources Agree)

1. **Technique/position gates load, always.** Nearly every source (Torokhtiy, Sika, Berestov, Webster, Pavlukhin, Catalyst, athletists, playlist) states positional integrity must precede intensity increases. Non-negotiable given back pain.

2. **OHS must be trained directly, not avoided.** The 50 kg OHS vs. 80 kg clean gap is diagnosed universally as a positional/mobility/stability deficit (thoracic extension, shoulder stability), not a leg-strength problem. Fix through progressive loaded OHS/snatch balance work, not heavier squatting alone.

3. **Chronic back pain requires eliminating heavy axial/spinal shear.** Consensus: avoid heavy conventional deadlifts and grinding heavy back squats; prefer front squat, pulls from blocks/hang, controlled tempo, neutral spine bracing.

4. **Night shift demands autoregulation over rigid calendars.** Sleep quality dictates training intensity daily (Pavlukhin ×2, Sika, ucvhbrb9z, power35, last_man, Torokhtiy). Heavy days reserved for high-sleep windows; technical/light work on fatigued days.

5. **Split jerk should be introduced technically now but not loaded heavily yet.** Multiple sources (Dozer, Torokhtiy, telegram_atletisty, Catalyst, last_man) recommend unloaded/light footwork drills during the hypertrophy block, deferring heavy split jerk to the strength phase.

6. **Submaximal, high-quality repetition beats max-effort singles during hypertrophy block.** 70–85% intensity, avoid grinding, is the dominant prescription across at least 6 sources.

7. **Deload every 4–6 weeks is standard** across Catalyst, Torokhtiy, ucvhbrb9z, Pavlukhin, and web_last_manorg.

## 2. CONFLICTS & RESOLUTIONS

| Conflict | Positions | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Training frequency** | 3x/wk (Torokhtiy, Catalyst, Sika) vs. 5–6x/wk (Dozer, Pavlukhin, telegram_pavlukhin) | Use **4 sessions/week**, fixed around night-shift recovery windows. Quality-frequency compromise; add optional 10–15 min mobility-only sessions on off days without loading. |
| **Primary squat pattern** | Back squat as main driver (athletists) vs. front squat/belt squat priority, back squat de-emphasized (zacktelander, power35, last_man, pavlukhin) | **Front squat becomes primary** squat pattern this block. Back squat retained 1x/week, moderate load, pain-gated. Add belt squat/leg press as back-sparing quad volume. |
| **OHS loading aggressiveness** | Load and progress now (Dozer, Torokhtiy, ucvhbrb9z) vs. keep very light/positional only (Sika, Webster, playlist) | **Progressive but gated loading**: start 40–45 kg holds/reps weeks 1–2, progress toward 50–55 kg by week 6 *only if* depth and rigid overhead lockout are maintained on video. |
| **Deadlift/pulling** | Some allow snatch/clean pulls freely; Pavlukhin explicitly bans conventional deadlift | **Use pulls from blocks/hang only** (reduced ROM = reduced spinal shear); RDLs light-moderate only; no floor conventional deadlift this block. |
| **Split jerk timeline** | Defer entirely to strength phase (Catalyst, playlist) vs. drill now unloaded (Dozer, Torokhtiy, telegram_atletisty) | **Drill footwork/positions unloaded (PVC/empty bar) 2–3x/week now**; keep all *loaded* jerk work as push jerk until strength phase. |
| **Rep-range for hypertrophy** | High rep 10–20 (Pavlukhin, catalystathletics) vs. low-rep 3–6 "sweet spot" (zacktelander, maxaita) | **Split by exercise type**: main lifts (snatch/clean/jerk) stay 3–5 reps; squats 5–8 reps; accessories/machines 8–15 reps. This satisfies both camps. |

## 3. UNIQUE CONTRIBUTIONS BY SOURCE

- **athletists**: "Train fresh, end hungry" sequencing; jerk-from-rack isolates fatigue from clean.
- **Catalyst Athletics**: Prilepin-based volume math; gripper/farmer's-walk work as an underrated OHS stabilizer.
- **Dozer**: Internal shoulder rotation cue for OHS stability; anthropometry-driven individualization (long limbs ≠ bad squat).
- **Max Aita**: Fault-driven corrective toolbox + self-diagnosis via video (where does the lift break: 1st pull/knee/turnover/catch?).
- **Pavlukhin (both)**: Machine-first hypertrophy to protect spine; wave-loading logic (fail a single → drop for volume, not grind).
- **Playlist/plf**: "Pusher pattern" diagnosis (quad-dominant vs hip-driven pull); forehead bar-contact cue for turnover speed.
- **Sika Strength**: Full ROM under load as the real mobility strategy; low-hang snatch preferred over high-hang (avoids masking weak 2nd pull); pain >6/10 = stop rule.
- **Sonny Webster (both)**: Confidence-gating ("don't attempt if you don't believe in the rep"); McGill Big Three as daily spinal-endurance warm-up.
- **telegram_atletisty**: Vorobyev's start-position rules (minimize shin-bar distance; shoulders strictly over bar); "rapid relaxation" concept—tension only when needed.
- **Torokhtiy (both)**: Diagnostic ratios (C&J should be 80–85% of front squat; snatch 78–83% of C&J) — useful benchmark for this athlete's actual gaps.
- **ucvhbrb9z**: Explicit wave scheme (5×5→4×4→3×3); volume-cut protocol triggered by sleep/mood score, not arbitrary.
- **Berestov**: Triple extension must be *simultaneous*, never sequenced (back-last firing = injury risk); thin/light bars for bar-whip feedback.
- **web_last_manorg**: Concrete belt squat/leg press substitution list; "retreat microcycle" (cut load 40%, switch to machines) as pain protocol.
- **power35 (LMS)**: Explicit three-mode cycling (High Intensity / High Volume / Micro-weight) gated entirely by pain status.
- **zacktelander**: Long-limb-specific biomechanics; belt squat and single-leg squat as *primary* (not accessory) leg-builders; 3–6 rep "sweet spot" to avoid back migration under fatigue.

## 4. PRIORITIZED PROGRAM ADJUSTMENTS FOR THIS ATHLETE

1. **Make front squat the primary squat pattern** this block (3–5×5–8 @70–75%); back squat 1x/week moderate (75–80%, pause-tempo, monitor pain); add belt squat or leg press 2x/week as back-sparing quad volume driver.
2. **OHS as daily technical anchor** (4x/week): light loaded holds/pauses progressing 40→50–55 kg by week 6, gated strictly by video-confirmed depth and rigid lockout — not by calendar.
3. **Replace conventional deadlift entirely** with snatch/clean pulls from blocks or hang position + light RDLs for posterior chain hypertrophy.
4. **Begin unloaded split-jerk footwork drills** 2–3x/week (empty bar/PVC) without adding load; continue all loaded jerk work as push jerk only.
5. **Fix training frequency at 4 sessions/week**, positioned around the Wed–Sun shift (e.g., Sun/Mon/Tue/Thu), with optional 10–15 min mobility-only sessions on remaining days.
6. **Adopt RPE/pain-gated autoregulation** instead of fixed percentages for all spine-loaded work; cap at RPE 7–8, drop 40% and switch to machines if back pain >3/10.
7. **Insert a deload every 4th week** (30–40% volume cut, intensity maintained ~60–65%).
8. **Daily non-negotiable warm-up**: McGill Big Three + thoracic CARs + ankle dorsiflexion + band shoulder circuit before every session.
9. **Add anti-rotation/core work 3x/week** (Pallof press, dead bug, bird dog, suitcase carry) — spinal stability, not an afterthought.
10. **Weekly self-video review** of OHS and jerk dip mechanics using Max Aita's diagnostic questions (where does it break — position, timing, or strength?).

## 5. TOP 10 TECHNIQUE CUES RIGHT NOW

1. **"Ribs down, chest tall"** — maintain thoracic extension (not lumbar hyperextension) in OHS, front rack, and jerk lockout.
2. **Meet the bar high and rigid** — lock the overhead position before sitting to depth in OHS/snatch catch.
3. **Full-foot contact throughout the pull** — no heel lift, no toe-bias; stabilizes bar path and back angle.
4. **Elbows high and forward in the front rack** — builds a stable shelf, prevents torso flexion under load.
5. **Vertical bar path, bar stays close** — "pull the bar back with lats," not arms; minimizes forward drift and lumbar shear.
6. **Simultaneous triple extension** — hips/knees/ankles finish together; never let the back extend early/alone.
7. **Shallow, controlled jerk dip** — vertical drive only, no forward collapse; head pulls back, not up.
8. **Scapular retraction/depression held throughout** — stabilizes shoulder girdle, reduces compensatory lumbar rounding.
9. **Brace before every rep** — neutral spine, McGill-style brace, applied identically to squat/pull/jerk.
10. **Split-jerk drill cue (unloaded)**: "front foot forward, back foot back, land shallow, catch active" — groove the pattern now without axial loading.
