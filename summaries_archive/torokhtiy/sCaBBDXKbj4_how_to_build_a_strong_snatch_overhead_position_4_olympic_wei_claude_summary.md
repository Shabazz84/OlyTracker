# Analysis: Alexei Torokhtiy Snatch Overhead Position

## Technique Cues (Snatch - Highly Relevant)

**Overhead Position & Elbow Safety:**
- Keep elbows directly overhead initially, but as load increases (90%+), natural elbow rotation forward is necessary and beneficial
- **Key cue: Drive chest forward** (not down) — this automatically rotates elbows forward, "locking" the arms and preventing elbow bend under heavy load
- At light loads (50-80%), straight arms are possible; at 90%+, chest-forward position becomes critical for arm integrity

**Shoulder Blade Stability:**
- Chest forward also pulls scapulae together, creating a stronger, more stable position than shoulders in neutral
- Finish overhead position with shoulders shrugged toward ears (degree varies: 50% = more shrug, 100% = minimal shrug)

**Avoid:** Externally/internally rotating elbows as a conscious movement — this locks arms but stresses shoulders. Let it happen as a byproduct of chest-forward drive.

## Relevance to Athlete Profile

⚠️ **CRITICAL FOR THIS ATHLETE:**
- **OHS = 50 kg (primary snatch limiter)** — This video directly addresses overhead stability. The chest-forward, shoulder-shrug position is essential for their snatch development.
- **Chronic back pain** — Driving chest forward may engage spinal extensors; monitor tolerance.
- Light load focus (50-80%) matches current clean/jerk levels; technique drilling before intensity is appropriate.
