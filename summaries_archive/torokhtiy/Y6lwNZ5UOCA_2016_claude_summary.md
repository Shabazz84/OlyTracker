# Интервью с украинскими медалистами Чемпионата Европы 2016

**Not about weightlifting coaching.**

This is a Russian-language transcript of interviews with Ukrainian Olympic weightlifters returning from the European Championships. While it mentions competition results and athlete names, it is primarily conversational/journalistic content about travel, luggage issues, and general impressions—not coaching instruction, programming, or technical analysis relevant to the athlete profile provided.
