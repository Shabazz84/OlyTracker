# SNATCH Balance/Press/Overhead [ENG SPA] Рывковый комплекс/TOROKHTIY(weightlifting)

# Analysis: Snatch-Grip Overhead Squat (Рывковый Швунг) Programming & Technique

## **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency**: Can be used more than monthly—recovery is fast
- **Intensity progression for beginners**: Work up to 70% of best snatch
- **Advanced structure**:
  - Up to 50%: 2 reps per weight (complex format: 2 press + 2 snatch pull + 2 OHS)
  - 50-90%: 1 rep per weight
  - 90-100%: Singles only
- **Complex format** (up to 50%): 2 strict press + 2 snatch pulls + 2 OHS
- **Goal**: Build to 105-110% of snatch best to overcome weight anxiety

## **Technique Cues**
1. **Rack height**: Set racks for comfortable bar unrack—no wasted energy
2. **Grip width**: Use snatch grip; narrow grip improves bottom mobility
3. **Foot placement**: Width matches your snatch landing position; toes slightly turned out (not parallel)
4. **Lumbar position**: Lower back extended; maintain neutral spine
5. **Arm lock (critical)**: Elbows must rotate forward—think "elbows to ears," creating full-body tension chain. This enables holding weight via skeletal stiffness, not just muscle
6. **Bottom position**: Find comfortable receiving posture *before* adding load—this is foundational
7. **Descent protocol**: Deeper squat for warm-ups; shorter squat at heavier weights to conserve energy
8. **Bar release**: Drop bar with straight arms only; don't bend elbows during descent (injury risk)

## **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Primary**: Snatch-grip push press + snatch pull + OHS (complex)
- **Frequency**: 2–3×/week is viable (fast recovery)
- **Mobility focus**: This complex uniquely builds bottom-position flexibility better than isolated OHS

## **Relevance to Athlete Profile**

🎯 **HIGHLY RELEVANT:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: This complex directly addresses your bottleneck. The protocol systematically builds bottom comfort and arm stability
- **Chronic back pain**: Requires strict lumbar positioning cue (back extended, neutral). Start conservative (70% max if new)
- **Night shift schedule (Wed–Sun)**: Fast recovery profile suits 2–3×/week inclusion
- **Push jerk only**: This addresses pressing strength + stability needed for split jerk development

**Recommendation**: Add 2×/week snatch-grip complex (50% protocol) during your 6-week hypertrophy block to build OHS capacity and spinal stability simultaneously.
