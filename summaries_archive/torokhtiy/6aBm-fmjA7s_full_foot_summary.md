# Full foot

1. **Programming principles**: None mentioned in this text.
2. **Technique cues**: "Full foot" (keep heels and toes grounded simultaneously); "Explode" immediately after establishing full-foot contact; Avoid rising onto toes too early to prevent cutting leg drive short or chasing the bar forward/backward.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specific beyond general lifting mechanics.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned.
5. **Profile relevance**: The cue regarding "chasing the bar" is critical for an athlete with chronic back pain, as poor foot drive often forces compensatory lumbar hyperextension to recover the bar path.
