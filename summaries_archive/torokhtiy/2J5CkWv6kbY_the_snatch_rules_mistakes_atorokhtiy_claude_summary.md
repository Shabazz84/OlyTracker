## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS

### 1. **Technique Cues - Snatch**
- **Grip**: Two methods shown—(a) one arm overhead, one parallel to floor = 90° angle; (b) one arm close to shoulder, other fully extended downward
- **Overhead position**: Elbows forward, scapulae retracted, shoulders shrugged toward ears; bar and arms function as one unit
- **Squat position**: Wide foot placement; abdomen between knees (if knees narrow, back rounds—major error)
- **Bar path**: Keep bar close to body throughout pull; avoid arcing trajectory (wastes time/energy, makes catching difficult)
- **Pull/extension**: Drive bar *toward* body, NOT body toward bar (common CrossFit error); maintain full foot contact, avoid rising on toes
- **Lockout fixation**: Scapulae together + shoulders to ears + elbows forward = critical finishing position
- **Deficit starting position work**: Pause above knee (2–3 sec), find center of foot by rocking forward/back; repeat paused reps above knee and at mid-shin to diagnose imbalances

### 2. **Programming/Methods**
- Paused snatch pulls (at multiple positions) for position refinement
- PVC stick used as cueing/correction tool
- Progression shown: light weight → technique focus → heavier load
