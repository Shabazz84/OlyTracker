# "TOROKHTIY online training GANG - 2/11" [ENG & SPA sub] (Weightlifting)

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Structure:** 3 main sessions/week (Mon/Wed/Fri or similar), with "auxiliary" technique sessions inserted between them.
*   **Intensity:** Main lifts utilize high intensity (80% Clean & Jerk, 90% Snatch Pulls/Clean Pulls). Auxiliary technique work uses very light loads ("small weight") to prioritize movement quality over fatigue.
*   **Frequency:** High frequency for specific movements (Snatch pulls and Clean pulls appear in almost every session; Squats appear frequently with overhead press variations).
*   **Periodization:** Linear progression within the week, moving from technique focus to higher intensity percentages as the week progresses.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Squat/Good Morning:** Knees must flare out ("spread"), hips pushed back, maintain a straight spine during descent, slow eccentric (down), fast concentric (up).
*   **Snatch Pulls to Stand:** Bar path must stay close to the body; strict fixation at the top; explosive drive using legs and back if no "dip" is present.
*   **Push Jerk:** Elbows tuck up, bar rests on clavicles/shoulders (no front rack dip mentioned), slow dip, fast push, firm lockout.
*   **Snatch Pulls from Hang:** Keep the angle of the body high during the stand-up phase; stay on the "full chain" (toes).
*   **Clean & Jerk Complex:** Elbows tuck forward in the squat position; hips back; slow descent, fast ascent.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Main Lifts:** Snatch Pulls to Stand, Clean from Racks, Push Jerk, Snatch Pulls (Hanging), Squat + Thruster, Overhead Squats (Snatch grip).
*   **Accessory/Strength:** Deadlift variations (Snatch Pulls, Clean Pulls) using straps; Back Extensions (Hyperextensions); Jumping exercises.
*   **Specific Variations:** Snatch pulls from blocks (elevated platform), "Low" squat cleans (clean + low squat complex).

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Hyperextension:** Perform with a pause at the top of the movement to maximize engagement.
*   **Load Management:** Explicitly advised to reduce weight if movements feel uncomfortable or too heavy ("do according to feeling").
*   **Jumping:** Includes plyometric work (jumps) as part of the weekly routine.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
*   **⚠️ Critical Conflict:** The text prescribes **Snatch Pulls and Clean Pulls from a hang position using straps at 90% intensity**. For an athlete with **chronic back pain**, this is high-risk without prior medical clearance or significant core stabilization work.
*   **Training Load:** The volume (3 main sessions + 2 auxiliary technique sessions) may be excessive for a night-shift worker (Wed–Sun schedule) trying to recover, potentially disrupting sleep cycles further.
*   **Technique Focus:** The emphasis on "slow eccentric" squats and strict bar paths aligns well with an intermediate athlete transitioning to Olympic lifting, but the high-intensity pulls contradict the need for back pain management.
*   **Jerk Style:** The text describes a push jerk ("elbows up," no split), which matches the athlete's current capability (no split jerk).
