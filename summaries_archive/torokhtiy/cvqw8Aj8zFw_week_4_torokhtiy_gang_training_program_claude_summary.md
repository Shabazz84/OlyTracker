# WEEK #4 / TOROKHTIY_GANG training program

# Analysis: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

**Language:** Russian (Olympic weightlifting coaching session with Julia Kalina, Olympic medalist)

---

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES
- **8-week program structure**: 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional sessions (Tue/Thu)
- **Week 4 is a deload week** ("расслабляющую" — relaxation week to offload before heavy work)
- **Exercise frequency**: Most lifts performed once per week in main sessions; some variations twice weekly
- **Example**: Snatch pulls 1x/week + snatch pulls from blocks 1x/week; jerk pulls 1x/week after jerk
- **Intensity**: No specific percentages given for most exercises; jerk pulls up to 115-120% of jerk max

---

## 2. TECHNIQUE CUES
**Snatch/Pulls:**
- Knees out, hips back
- Arms straight throughout
- 2-second pause in receiving position (especially during warm-ups; <60% can move freely)
- Knee and elbow alignment on same vertical line
- Flat feet (no rising on toes)
- Shoulder over knee, behind knee

**Jerk/Push Press:**
- Snatch pull + snatch push press: 2+2 format
- Focus on depth control and subsecond positioning
- Arms inserted *behind* (not in front) to hold weight overhead
- Knees out, hips back in dip

**Front Squat:**
- Bar on shoulders, not chest
- Elbows turned out, chest open
- Hips back/out
- Good mobility negates need for straps

---

## 3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS
- **Hyperextensions**: 3×10 @ 20 kg (warm-up); max: 70 kg
- **Snatch pulls from blocks**: 1x/week, no straps
- **Snatch pulls + snatch push press combo**: 1x/week (2+2 reps)
- **Front squat**: High volume, up to 195+ kg
- **Barbell bends (semi-range)**: 2x/week (before heavy snatch; competition alternative: static holds)
- **Jerk (classic 1+1 or 2+2)**: Max 141 kg (Kalina's PR)
- **Jerk pulls**: 1x/week after jerk; 115-120% of jerk max
- **Snatch pull + pull from blocks combo**: 2+2 reps; emphasis on angle/bar path

---

## 4. RECOVERY/MOBILITY
- Hyperextensions used as warm-up before each session
- Barbell bends as mobility/warm-up before snatch work
- Straps on knees have no effect on performance (opinion)
- Good shoulder/hip mobility reduces need for training aids

---

## 5. RELEVANT TO THIS ATHLETE

⚠️ **Critical mismatches:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg) with deload week**: This athlete needs aggressive OHS/mobility work, not a deload week (though may be appropriate in their cycle)
- **Split jerk missing**: Athlete only has push jerk (65 kg). This program heavily emphasizes jerk technique; split jerk development should be prioritized
- **Back pain + high volume front squat (195+ kg)**: Kalina uses forward torso lean; verify this doesn't exacerbate pain
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Main sessions Wed/Fri/Sun align poorly with recovery; Tuesday/Thursday optional sessions may be better for this athlete

✅ **Useful cues**:
- Knees out/hips back cues apply to snatch pull deficits
- 2-second pause in receiving position helps technique cement
- Snatch pull variations (blocks, different heights) directly address pull technique

---

**Bottom line**: This is a solid technical-focused deload; however, the athlete should prioritize split jerk introduction and OHS mobility work in the subsequent 6-week block.
