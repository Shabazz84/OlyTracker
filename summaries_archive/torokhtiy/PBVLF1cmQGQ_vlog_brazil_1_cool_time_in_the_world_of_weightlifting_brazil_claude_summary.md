## Extracted Weightlifting Content:

**1. Programming Principles**
- **Tor-kor (ab wheel rollout):** 3 sets × 10 reps sufficient for core work
- **Post-flight training approach:** Light 1-hour session, 4 exercises in 3–4 rounds (low intensity for recovery)
- **Core trio for strength:** Back squat, plank, tor-kor

**2. Technique/Exercise Recommendations**
- **Tor-kor progressions:** Start PVC pipe (8–10 reps), avoid bar initially; progress to paused work with straight arms; use padding under knees, cross legs
- **Shoulder stability:** Resistance band work emphasized for injury prevention
- **Jerk preparation:** Rack jerk focus (coach planning specialized upper body work)

**3. Recovery Notes**
- Acclimatization post-travel: Light warm-up focus first 1–2 days
- Massage, acupuncture for recovery
- Adjust intensity based on adaptation (climate/time zone)
