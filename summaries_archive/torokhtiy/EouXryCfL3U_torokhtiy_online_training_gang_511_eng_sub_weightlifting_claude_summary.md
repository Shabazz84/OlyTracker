# Weightlifting Coaching Transcript Analysis

## Programming Principles
- **Week 5 of program** (approaching midpoint); **3 training sessions/week structure**
- Sessions 1-2: **deload/moderate intensity** (preserving capacity for Session 3)
- Session 3: **high intensity/heaviest loads** (includes competition lift attempts)
- **Intensity progression**: 95% snatch pulls (Session 2, conditional on recovery); 90% jerk pulls (Session 3)
- **Frequency strategy**: Multiple squat variations prioritized across sessions

## Technique Cues

**Snatch Pull:**
- Smooth, gradual acceleration to knees → explosive drive/jump after knee extension
- "Stand smoothly to knees, then add speed and jump"

**Jerk from Blocks:**
- Smooth descent into dip; explosive drive up
- Elbow position critical (points forward in rack position)

**Clean to Low Squat:**
- Option to train with/without foot spread (emphasized: narrower stance = higher standing position)

**Front Squat (emphasized repeatedly):**
- Slow descent with **weight transfer awareness** (one muscle group disengages, another engages progressively)
- Explosive ascent (critical for standing with load in clean/jerk)

**Good Mornings:**
- Can reduce ROM (half-depth) to accommodate heavier loads
- Variation allows experimentation
