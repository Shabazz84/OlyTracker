# Full foot

# Analysis

**Relevance to weightlifting:** Yes, Olympic lifting technique focus.

## 1. **Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean)**
- **Stay flat-footed as long as possible** during leg drive phase
- Drive through **heels AND balls of feet simultaneously** for maximum vertical force
- Prevents early rise onto toes (cuts leg drive short)
- Analogy: Full palm contact > fingertip contact = maximum controlled upward power
- Prevents bar drift (forward/backward chase)

## 2. **Directly Relevant to This Athlete**

**Back Pain Consideration:** Full-foot stability improves bar path control → reduces compensatory loading patterns that aggravate chronic back pain.

**OHS Limiter (50 kg):** Cleaner leg drive mechanics will improve receiving position stability in snatch/clean, supporting eventual OHS strength gains.

**Push Jerk Dependency:** Efficient leg drive is critical since athlete lacks split jerk option—must maximize vertical power from legs to compensate for reduced positional options.
