# Analysis: Russian Weightlifting Coaching Transcript (Week 6 → 8-Week "Tarakan" Program)

## 1. Programming Principles
- **8-week program structure**: 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional accessory days (Tue/Thu)
- **Week 6 approach**: Maintain intensity, then reduce load/intensity in coming weeks
- **Intensity zones**: 40%, 70%, 70-75%, 95-100% (varying by exercise)
- **Rep schemes**: Singles and doubles; varied rep patterns (e.g., 1 clean + 1 jerk, then 1 clean + 2 jerks)
- **Control exercises (test lifts)**: One per week (Week 6 = Classic Jerk at 95-100%)

---

## 2. Technique Cues

**Clean/Jerk:**
- *Common error*: Partial catch (rack position with slight dip) ≠ actual clean—must stand fully before dropping
- Hold overhead position 2-3 seconds
- Full depth front squat: hips back, knees out, slow descent, fast ascent

**Snatch pull:**
- Slow eccentric (full foot contact)
- Emphasize leg drive

**Snatch pull + squat:**
- Work on feeling and technical quality over load
- Clean grip (no straps initially)

**Front squat:**
- Pair after jerks to fatigue same muscle groups
