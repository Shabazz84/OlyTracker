# Weightlifting Analysis: Snatch Pull Tutorial (Torokhtiy)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Snatch Pull intensity cap**: Max 100% (vs. Clean Pull which can exceed 100%)
- **Purpose**: Develop speed and amplitude specific to snatch
- **Recommended variation**: Snatch Pull from platform + Hang Snatch Pull (angle control focus)
- No explicit volume/frequency guidance given

## 2. **Technique Cues - Snatch Specific**
- **Grip width**: PVC test—hold horizontal, straighten other arm to 90°; project to barbell at pubic area
- **Starting position**: 
  - Legs shoulder-width; knees pushed out; torso *between* legs (deeper sit)
  - Lower back tightened; knees slightly ahead of elbows
  - *Keep angle*—pelvis moves parallel to shoulders (avoid "flying out" pelvis or leaning back)
- **Pull phase**: Stand as long as possible, keeping shoulder-knee-barbell alignment; maintain angles throughout
- **Finish**: Small back swing for additional amplitude/speed
- **Barbell path**: Straight amplitude; barbell moves *to you*, not vice versa
- **Stance**: Full foot contact throughout explosion (avoid getting on toes prematurely—learn pull *without* toes first, add later)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Snatch Pull (primary)
- Snatch Pull from platform + Hang Snatch Pull combo (excellent for angle control)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- None mentioned

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
- **High relevance**: OHS 50 kg is limiting snatch; snatch pull builds pulling strength without overhead demand
- **Concern**: Chronic back pain + emphasis on "tightened lower back" and maintaining spinal angles—coach must supervise form to avoid aggravation
- **Opportunity**: Pull variations bypass night shift fatigue better than heavy snatches; fits hypertrophy block well
