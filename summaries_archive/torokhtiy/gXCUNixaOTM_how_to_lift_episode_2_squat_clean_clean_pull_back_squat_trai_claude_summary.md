# How to lift / Episode #2: Squat Clean / Clean Pull / Back Squat training session

# Analysis: Russian Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Structure**: 4-exercise session (deadlift + clean complex, push jerk, accessory work)
- **Intensity**: 65-70% of best jerk; progressing to 90% on 3-rep push jerk
- **Volume/Intensity progression**: Week 2 increased intensity; this session is "medium volume, low intensity" for technique refinement
- **Frequency note**: Coach emphasizes deliberate, slow work to address technical errors before intensity increases

## 2. Technique Cues (Critical for this athlete)
**Clean/Snatch-relevant:**
- Relax arms; they function as "ropes" only for holding, not pulling
- **Shoulder shrug upward** at extension (more critical as weight increases; optional at 50-60%)
- Cover the bar with shoulders during pull and extension—don't throw shoulders/hips back
- Explosive triple extension (hip, knee, ankle); deep catch
- Maintain upright torso during pull; hips back, knees out
- Elbows high and fast; elbow rotation priority over shoulder elevation alone

**Push Jerk:**
- Knee angle in split: no greater than 90°
- Front and rear foot toes turned inward (mimics split jerk foot positioning)
- Shoulder blades packed; arms relaxed
- Lumbar spine tension critical; arms loose

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Dead lift + clean from floor/hang + push jerk** (to 65–70%)
- **Push jerk heavy singles** (up to 90% if easy)
- **Front squat** (leg loosening)
- **Accessory**: hyperextension (2×8), split squats (BW or empty bar, <90° knee angle), elbow rotation + press (seated if possible)
- **Warm-up complex**: empty bar—3 contacts, 3 high pulls, 3 cleans

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- **Pre-session**: PVC shoulder/elbow mobility work in pairs; floor shoulder stretch (2 sets, 15–20 sec each)
- **Contextual**: Coach advises rest over training if shoulders are compromised; prioritize technique work over intensity in fatigue

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Elbow mobility/positioning critical. Shoulder packing cue aligns with OHS needs.
- **Chronic back pain**: Emphasis on lumbar tension (not laxity), scapular packing, relaxed arms reduces compensatory strain.
- **Push jerk only**: Split squat foot positioning mirrors split mechanics—valuable transfer.
- **Night shift worker**: Recovery/mobility work prioritized; lower-intensity technique block suits circadian disruption.
- **Intermediate status**: Heavy emphasis on "light weight, perfect movement" before heavy loading—appropriate progression.

**Key takeaway**: Arm relaxation and shoulder coverage during pulls are primary technical focus; aligns with OHS mobility and back health.
