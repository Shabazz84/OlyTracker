# Pre-workout WARM-UP / A.TOROKHTIY (Weightlifting)

**Language & Format Note:** This is a Russian-language weightlifting warmup/mobility tutorial. Analyzing content relevant to the athlete profile.

## EXTRACTED CONTENT

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- Warmup: 20-30 min pre-training
- Cool-down: 15-20 min post-training
- Mobility work used on rest days OR before training (flexible approach)

### 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Snatch start position prep:** Deep forward fold (hands to feet) with knees spread wide, torso between knees. Hold at bottom with small oscillations to mobilize lower back and hips. *Directly addresses snatch setup issues.*
- **General posture:** Maintain neutral spine tension during loaded bending; avoid excessive spinal flexion.

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Bent-over holds** (40-50 kg or light bar/hyperextension): 3-4 sets with bottom pause
- **Shoulder band work** (smooth, controlled):
  - Shoulder dislocates: 10-15 reps, 2-3 sets
  - Overhead presses with band: 10-15 reps
  - Lateral raises: 10-15 reps
- **Goblet squats** (wide stance): 3-4 sets, pause bottom, neutral spine
- **Band stretching** (slow, methodical): multiple problem zones
- **Sciatic nerve mobilization** (foam roller/ball): slow, find tender points
- **Thoracic spine mobility:** cross arms, roll upper/mid back
- **Cool-down stretching & spinal traction**

### 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Shoulder band work: 3+ years effective for shoulder impingement prevention/rehab
- Sciatic nerve release → automatic hip/back relaxation
- Target "trigger points" (zones of discomfort) with sustained pressure
- Smooth, methodical work > aggressive/fast mobility
- Can use on rest days for general recovery

### 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
- ✅ **Chronic back pain:** Extensive lower back/sciatic focus highly applicable
- ✅ **Weak OHS (50 kg):** Shoulder mobility drills directly address overhead positioning
- ✅ **Snatch start position issues:** Deep fold mobility = critical for fixing snatch setup with weak OHS
- ✅ **Night shift recovery:** Recovery-focused mobility fits variable schedule
- ⚠️ **No programming detail** for 6-week hypertrophy block or strength periodization
