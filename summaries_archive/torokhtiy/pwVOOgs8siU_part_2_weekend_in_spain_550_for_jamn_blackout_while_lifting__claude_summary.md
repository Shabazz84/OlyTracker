**RELEVANT COACHING CONTENT:**

1. **Front Rack Position (Jerk/Clean)**
   - Full grip on bar safer than fingertip grip (reduces bar loss)
   - Elbows must position high enough to lift them upward
   - Some athletes can hold bar with shoulder shrug only (elbows down, bar stable on shoulders)
   - Proper positioning prevents wrist surgery and protects lower back

2. **Breathing/Bracing for Stability**
   - 1–2 second breath hold before lift (not 5–10 seconds)
   - Breath hold stabilizes core and expands chest cavity
   - Prevents bar vibration/resonance from continuous breathing

3. **Pause Work**
   - 3-second pause emphasized (likely in catch position) for motor control
   - Allows body to find optimal positioning

4. **Light Load Practice**
   - 40–50 kg for 5–6 sets recommended when uncomfortable with movement
   - Builds confidence before heavier loading
