# How to build a strong SNATCH OVERHEAD POSITION 4 Olympic weightlifting / Torokhtiy Tips

This text is a technical analysis of the overhead position in the snatch (specifically addressing elbow rotation and chest drive), not a general coaching transcript covering programming or recovery. Therefore, only points relevant to **Technique Cues** can be extracted:

**2. Technique cues for snatch:**
*   **Primary Cue:** Drive the **chest forward** (not down) when receiving the bar overhead. Do not focus on manually rotating elbows; let them rotate naturally as a result of chest movement.
*   **Elbow Position:** When driving the chest forward, elbows should point slightly up/forward (approx. 45 degrees). This "locks" the arms in extension, preventing them from bending under heavy loads (>90% 1RM) where pure arm strength fails.
*   **Scapular Action:** Driving the chest forward causes the shoulder blades to squeeze together, creating a more stable and secure overhead position compared to keeping shoulders independent (elbows up).
*   **Shoulder Engagement:** Finish the movement by lifting the shoulders toward the ears ("pulling shoulders up"), but prioritize this only after establishing the forward chest drive.

*(Note: The text does not contain information regarding programming, exercise selection for hypertrophy/back pain, or recovery strategies relevant to the athlete's specific profile.)*
