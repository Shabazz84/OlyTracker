# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Frequency**: 8 sessions/week + daily PVC complexes
- **Intensity Progression**: Week 1 capped at 60% to establish technique before heavy loading
- **Structure**: Two sessions daily (morning technique focus, afternoon higher intensity)
- **Periodization**: Technique/movement mastery phase → progressive loading phase
- **Load Management**: Reduced training load in Week 1 to prevent overtraining and allow adaptation

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Jerk**: Focus on dip mechanics, barbell shoulder position, and overhead lockout
- **Snatch**: Pull from rib position; hang snatch work; snatch stand practice
- **General**: Emphasis on understanding movement mechanics before adding weight

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Pull from rib (lightweight)
- Hang snatch
- Jerk from racks
- Snatch stand practice
- Daily PVC complexes (mornings)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- 2-hour rest between sessions
- Daily yoga + stretching emphasis
- Walking recovery
- Adequate sleep prioritized
- Body weight monitored daily

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
**Applicable**: Technique-first approach aligns with your snatch limiter (50kg OHS). Starting at 60% intensity suits hypertrophy block transition. Daily mobility/yoga beneficial for chronic back pain.

**Caution**: 8 sessions/week + night shift (Wed–Sun) = high fatigue risk. Back pain may worsen with this volume; needs adaptation.
