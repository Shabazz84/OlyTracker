## **2. Technique Cues for Snatch**

**Grip & Setup:**
- Grip width: theoretical grip (arm extended perpendicular to bar = 90° angle)
- Stance: shoulder-width, toes turned out ~15-20°
- Knees track toes (abducted position)

**Starting Position:**
- Hips back, slight lumbar arch
- Shoulders cover the bar (lateral view)
- Full foot contact maintained

**Pull Phases:**
- **First pull → second pull transition:** Keep knees tracking outward
- **Critical cue:** Stay on full foot as long as possible (not rising on toes early)
- **Second pull:** Hip and knee extension together (NOT chasing the bar with arms)
- **Arm pull:** Elbows high and close, pull vertically
- **Common error:** Rising to toes early = loss of leg drive

**Drill Progression:**
- Tall snatch (from upright position with momentum)
- Hang snatch variations
- Jumping (mimics pulling mechanics)

---

## **3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Snatch pulls from various heights
- Jump drills (full foot contact emphasis)
- Tall snatches with light load (develops arm/overhead timing)

---

## **4. Recovery/Mobility**
- Shoulder mobility warm-up ("good shoulder preparation")
- Bar mobility drills pre-snatch
