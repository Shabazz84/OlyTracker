# My workout:  Romanian Deadlift + Thruster [ENG SUB]

**1. Programming principles**
*   **Structure:** General warm-up (50 min low-intensity cardio) → Specific mobility/stabilization drills → Sub-maximal strength access → Main lift (Push Jerk from racks).
*   **Intensity/Frequency:** Main lift performed at 60–70% of max (1RMP), progressing to higher intensity if feeling good. Accessory work (Romanian Deadlift) at ~50%.
*   **Periodization:** Single-lift focus ("Road to Jerk") due to time constraints; high repetition of specific drills for stabilization and synchronization rather than varied Olympic lifting volume.

**2. Technique cues**
*   **Synchronization:** Arms must engage immediately after the final leg extension (no pause between legs straightening and arm press).
*   **The "Spring" Concept:** The body acts as a spring; knees/hips must compress vertically without lateral "play." A strong core ("stem") prevents the "spring" from buckling sideways.
*   **Receiving Position:** When lowering the bar overhead, hips must stay back (neutral pelvis) to avoid lumbar shear. Transition from toes to heels while absorbing impact through knees and posterior chain.
*   **Dip Mechanics:** Slow descent into the dip to check foot position (full foot contact), then explosive drive.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Warm-up:** Rowing or Cycling (50 min, low intensity).
*   **Mobility/Stab:** Elbow rotations, "Klones" over a box/bench, Scorpion squats (lunges with rotation), Core work from half-kneeling position.
*   **Accessories:**
    *   **Trasfers (Power Jerk/Overhead Squat hybrid):** 3 sets of low weight (20–40 kg) for stabilization and synchronization. Can include a pause in the dip to check foot placement.
    *   **Jerk Balance:** Low weight, focus on shoulder rotation and stability.
    *   **Romanian Deadlift (RDL) from blocks:** Using 10kg plates as risers (5–6 cm elevation). Focus on straight back, posterior chain activation, and grip strength (non-locking grip for wrist work).

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Shoulder/Spine Health:** Avoid high loads in "Jerk Balance" drills to prevent shoulder strain; use them primarily as warm-up/stabilization.
*   **Lumbar Safety:** Do not drop the bar onto the chest from overhead without proper hip hinge mechanics (hips back, knees forward) to avoid crushing the lower back and knees.
*   **Core Stability:** Emphasize building a "strong stem" in the dip to ensure vertical force transfer.

**5. Relevance to athlete's profile**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The transcript explicitly warns against dropping the bar onto the chest with a flat back, which causes lumbar shear. It emphasizes the hip-hinge receiving position and RDLs for posterior chain stability (core). *Action:* Strictly adhere to the "hips back" receiving cue; avoid high-volume overhead squats if they aggravate pain.
*   **Push Jerk Only:** The entire session is dedicated to the Push Jerk from racks, aligning with the athlete's current limitation (no split jerk).
*   **Night Shift/Time Constraints:** The coach notes skipping full Olympic lifting programs due to time scarcity and focusing on one lift ("Road to Jerk"). This supports a streamlined approach suitable for a night-shift worker.
*   **Hypertrophy Block Prep:** The inclusion of RDLs from blocks targets the posterior chain (glutes/hamstrings) with controlled tempo, which can serve as a foundation for hypertrophy before the main block begins.
