# Analysis: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript (Russian)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Snatch-focused training block** with structured exercise progression:
  - Snatch pulls from "rib" (mid-thigh): 3–4 sets × 5 reps
  - Snatch on plates (for leg drive emphasis): 2–3 sets during warm-up
  - Snatch from hang + snatch pulls
  - Finish ~50% of sessions with: snatch pulls, push press, or snatch-grip squats
- **Intensity management**: Emphasizes controlled force application matching load (e.g., 20 kg bar ≠ 200 kg effort)
- **Warm-up strategy**: Lower catch positions during warm-up (below standard), then standard positioning from 50%+ onward
- **Recovery integration**: Active recovery (pool, breaks) emphasized

## 2. **Technique Cues**
**Bar path/positioning:**
- Keep bar as close to body as possible throughout pull and catch
- Prevent shoulder blade retraction (common fault causing bar drift)
- Pull elbows high to reduce distance between bar and body
- Full knee extension + bar "ringing" (audible contact)

**Catch mechanics:**
- Half-squat catch → full squat (one continuous movement, not stop-and-go)
- Lower catches during warm-up to prepare for heavy attempts
- Control shoulder position in split jerk (noted as challenge vs. push jerk)
- Leg drive emphasis (snatch on plates specifically targets this)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Snatch pulls from rib** (mid-thigh pulls) — primary accessory
- **Snatch on plates** (2–3 sets, light) — teaches leg drive/jump
- **Snatch from hang**
- **Clean pulls + clean in power position** (for elbow turnover correction)
- **Front squat + clean combinations** — angle reinforcement for jerk position
- **Push press + power jerk variations** (coach prefers push jerk for this athlete)
- **Snatch-grip squats** — final session exercise

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Active recovery breaks (pool/environment change) shown effective
- Spiritual/mental recovery emphasized alongside physical
- No specific mobility prescription provided; technical coaching prioritized
