# "TOROKHTIY online training GANG - 6/11" [ENG SUB] (Weightlifting)

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 3 main sessions + 2 supplementary sessions per week (Total: 5).
*   **Structure:** Each session contains 3–5 exercises, including one "control" (max effort) lift.
*   **Intensity:** Snatch pulls and snatches from floor performed with high frequency; Snatch Jerks (Snatch Push Press) at 85–90% of max jerk; Clean & Jerk control lifts at ~90%.
*   **Repetition Strategy:** Emphasis on "no pause" in the squat during jerks/snatch jerks to maintain dynamic flow.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Snatch from Floor (Start):** Control lumbar extension; do not let the lower back collapse ("not insert"). Rise slowly until knees, then explode upward.
*   **Snatch Pulls:** Can rise onto toes *only if* executed correctly; otherwise, start without toe rise to master mechanics first.
*   **Snatch Push Press (Ryovok Shvung):** Do not stop at the bottom of the squat. "Sit and immediately stand." Stopping creates a dead weight requiring 20–30% more effort to restart.
*   **Clean & Jerk (Squat):** No hard stop/pause at the bottom of the receiving position during learning phases; maintain dynamic movement.
*   **Snatch Fixation:** Squeeze shoulder blades together and lift shoulders up to secure the bar behind the head.
*   **Jerk Stability:** Hold the jerk for 2–3 seconds after extension; if swaying left/right, foot placement is unstable (not on full foot).

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Hyperextensions:** Mandatory warm-up/activation. Use a box to sit comfortably and feel spinal stretch/discomfort before starting.
*   **Snatch Pulls (Ryovkaya Tyaga):** Performed in straps initially, then without straps for grip adaptation. Focus on lower back tension.
*   **Snatch Push Press:** Progress weight gradually; focus on continuous motion from the bottom.
*   **Clean & Jerk Control Lifts:** 90% intensity (adjustable based on feel).
*   **Snatch Pulls to Snatch (Ryovkovaya Protyazhka):** Straight leg pull, explosive after knees, no dip/pull under.
*   **Jerk from Racks:** No pause in the squat; focus on full foot stability.
*   **Back Squats:** Soft descent, fast ascent, followed by small jumps to fatigue legs (use caution before high-weight weeks).

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Hyperextension Modification:** Sit comfortably on a box during hyperextensions to feel spinal stretch and manage lower back discomfort.
*   **Grip Care:** Use tape or bandages for fingers during snatches without straps to adapt them to competition conditions.
*   **Caution:** Be careful with high-intensity leg fatigue (jumps after squats) as the following week involves heavy competitive lifts.
