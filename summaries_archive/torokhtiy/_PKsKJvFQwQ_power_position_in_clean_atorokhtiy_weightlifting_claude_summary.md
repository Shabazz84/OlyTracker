### 2. Technique Cues – JERK (Primary Focus)

**The "Подрыв" (Drive/Extension)** – described as the critical power phase:
- **Position setup:** Bar above knees, hips back, knees out, shoulders over bar/knees aligned
- **Grip:** Lock grip preferred (or straps); arms relaxed – test by shaking arms to confirm no tension
- **Drive mechanics:**
  - Pull bar off legs with straight arms
  - Contact bar to thigh (~2/3 up thigh or mid-thigh)
  - **Strike the bar against the leg** while extending hips upward
  - Finish with elbow pull-up (high elbow finish)
  - Can rise to toes to feel the "release"
- **Repetition:** Train this frequently, especially if underdeveloped

**Accessory drill for drive strength:** Weights positioned off-center on bar (partial loading), straps mandatory, to isolate the drive pattern without grip constraint.
