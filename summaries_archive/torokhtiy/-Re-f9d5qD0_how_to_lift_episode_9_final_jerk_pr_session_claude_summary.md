# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Main lift**: Push jerk (толчок)
- **Structure**: Front squat + jerk up to 50% load; 50%+ jerk only
- **Intensity progression**: Working toward 75%+ with 1–2 additional sets after technical mastery
- **Frequency note**: Single session focus on jerk

## 2. **Technique Cues (Jerk-Specific)**
- **Dip/catch position**: Shallow dip ("полусед" / half-squat recommended over full depth when pain present)
- **Drive mechanics**: Fast, powerful drive from dip; "short descent" into receiving position
- **Bar timing**: Follow the bar; use bar momentum to aid recovery
- **Chest**: Open chest during catch; maintain rigid core/trunk
- **Recovery**: Fast, explosive drive out of dip—slow descent wastes force

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Front squat + jerk complexes (warm-up progression)
- Elbow circles + front squat
- PVC press (standing or from full squat)
- Jerk to split stance (footwork practice)
- Push press (half-squat version)
- Wall practice for dip positioning

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility & Pain Management**
- **Knee pain (end-range squat)**: Athlete reported pain below kneecap in full depth; pain-free in half-squat
- **Coaching response**: Modify depth, monitor; pause session if pain worsens
- **Recommendation**: Practice half-squat variations; reassess in 2 days

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Application**
- **Chronic back pain + night shift**: Half-squat jerk work reduces spinal load vs. full receiving position
- **Push jerk only**: Session aligns with current ability; no split jerk complexity
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: No direct snatch work here, but core stability cues transferable
