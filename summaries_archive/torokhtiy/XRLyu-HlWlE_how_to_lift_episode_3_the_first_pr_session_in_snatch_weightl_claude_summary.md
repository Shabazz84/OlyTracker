## 2. Technique Cues (Snatch)

**Critical for this athlete (50 kg OHS limiter):**

- **"Cover the bar with shoulders as long as possible in the pull"** — maximize bar proximity and stability
- **"Jump UP, not backward"** — avoid horizontal displacement (coach corrects athlete pulling back)
- **Vertical extension emphasis** — distinguish from back swing
- **"Don't control the bar, turn your brain off"** — achieve automatic movement pattern
- **Hip positioning**: "Don't drive hips forward" — avoid lumbar stress (relevant to chronic back pain)
- **Catching position**: "Sit down quickly once the bar is high" — emphasizes adequate pull height
- **Fatigued lifting**: Train snatches while tired (simulates competition conditions)
