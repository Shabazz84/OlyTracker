# PART 3: My attempts in Functional Fitness / A.TOROKHTIY

This text is not about weightlifting; it is a transcript of a CrossFit training session focusing on gymnastics, high-repetition squats for endurance, and metabolic conditioning (row/burpees/box jumps). It contains no specific advice regarding Olympic lifting technique, hypertrophy programming for strength athletes, or management of chronic back pain in the context of weightlifting.
