# 5 Best Mobility | Flexibility Drills for Clean, Front Squat, Jerk / Torokhtiy

**1. Programming principles**
*   **Sets/Reps:** 2–3 sets per exercise; hold static positions for 10–20 seconds (exercises 1 & 2) or 20–30 seconds (exercises 3, 4).
*   **Intensity:** Low to moderate. Use bands, empty bars, or light weights (10–15 kg progressing to 20 kg for elbows). No heavy loading recommended in this specific drill set.
*   **Frequency:** Not specified, but implies daily mobility work due to the nature of "preparation" drills.
*   **Periodization:** Described as a preparatory block focusing on range of motion (ROM) and stability before heavier lifting.

**2. Technique cues**
*   **Elbow Position:** Keep elbows forward; do not let them drift back or out to the side. Target 180-degree elbow rotation in the clean position.
*   **Bar Path/Contact:** Bar must rest on the shoulders (deltoids), not the collarbones/clavicles. If mobility is poor, elbows drop, causing bar instability.
*   **Front Squat Grip:** Use straps to prevent grip failure; ensure the lifter does not "open" their grip and let the bar slide down onto fingers.
*   **Strap Usage:** Wrap straps exactly once around the wrist/bar (no double wraps) to avoid shoulder injury if the bar drops backward.
*   **Movement Tempo:** Slow descent, fast ascent in front squats; slow, controlled rotations for elbow drills.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
1.  **Band Elbow Press:** Anchor a resistance band at chest height, press down with elbows while holding the bar to simulate clean rack position (10–20s hold).
2.  **Stick/Pole Rotation:** Hold a stick or pole behind the back; rotate arms to stretch shoulders/elbows without moving the elbow joint laterally.
3.  **Overhead Squat Position Hold:** Hold a barbell overhead with a "jerk grip" (wide), squat down slowly, and hold the bottom position to find shoulder stability.
4.  **Elbow Rotation Drill:** Bar on chest; rotate elbows from back to front while keeping the bar height constant (start with stick, progress to 10–20 kg).
5.  **Strapped Front Squats:** Front squats using straps for grip security, focusing on high elbows and posterior hip movement ("butt back").

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Mobility Focus:** Prioritize shoulder internal rotation and thoracic extension to allow the bar to sit securely on the shoulders rather than the collarbones.
*   **Grip Security:** Use sweat-absorbing straps (not slippery ones) to maintain a fixed grip position during squats, preventing the bar from sliding forward.

**5. Relevance to athlete's profile**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The text emphasizes keeping the bar on the *shoulders* rather than the collarbones/clavicles. This is critical for your back pain; if elbows drop (common with poor mobility), the bar shifts to the clavicles, increasing shear force and spinal compression.
*   **OHS Limiter (50 kg):** Exercise #3 (Overhead Squat hold) directly addresses the shoulder stability needed to support overhead weight without collapsing.
*   **No Split Jerk:** The text focuses heavily on the "rack position" for the clean, which is foundational for a push jerk but less relevant for split mechanics; however, the elbow mobility drills are essential for stabilizing the bar during the dip and drive of a push jerk.
*   **Intermediate/Transitioning:** The progression from stick -> 10kg -> 20kg in Exercise #4 prevents injury while building the specific motor patterns needed to transition from powerlifting (back squat focus) to Olympic lifting (rack position focus).
