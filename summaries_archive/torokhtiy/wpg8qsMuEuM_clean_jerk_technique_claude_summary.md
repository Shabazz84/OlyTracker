# Analysis: Jerk (Толчок) Coaching Transcript

## Relevant to This Athlete

**TECHNIQUE CUES — JERK:**
1. **Rack Position (груди):** Elbows HIGH, shoulders act as "stands." Bar sits in groove between shoulder and neck. Allows finger relaxation; prevents carotid artery compression.
2. **Drive Phase:** Chest up via deep breath. Dip 10–15% of height, hips back (NOT knees forward), knees out. Slow down, fast up (spring effect).
3. **Press:** Drive bar BEHIND head, not forward. Scapulae retracted, shoulders shrugged. Critical for lockout stability.
4. **No Pause in Split:** Continuous motion — pull → dip → drive → receive. Pausing kills bar momentum.
5. **Lockout Fixation:** 2–3 sec overhead hold post-press (mandatory for competition).

**CLEAN PULL BASICS (relevant foundation):**
- Pull initiates at upper-mid thigh (lower than snatch), not hip
- Sequence: leg extension → arm pull (NOT arm-first)
- Full foot contact during pull, can transition to toes
