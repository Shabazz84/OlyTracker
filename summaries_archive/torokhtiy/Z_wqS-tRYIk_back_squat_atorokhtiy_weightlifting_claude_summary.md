# Back SQUAT / A.TOROKHTIY (weightlifting)

# Weightlifting Analysis: Back Squat for Olympic Lifting

## Programming Principles
- **Frequency**: 2-3x/week
- **Hypertrophy/Prep Phase**: 4-6 sets per session (volume focus)
- **Pre-Comp Phase**: 2-3 sets, even 1 set (intensity focus, reduced volume)
- **Rep Tempo**: Slow eccentric, explosive concentric; maintains tension throughout
- **Breathing**: Inhale at rack, exhale post-rep, repeat per rep (or single breath if preferred)

## Technique Cues (Back Squat Emphasis)
1. **Setup**: Hip back first, slight forward torso lean before descent
2. **Descent**: Slow, controlled — don't collapse; load muscles progressively
3. **Knee Position**: Keep knees "covered by shoulders" — knees track outward, NOT forward (load hips/legs evenly, not knees)
4. **Drive**: Explosive ascent from bottom; develops rate of force development for pulling
5. **Footwear**: Olympic shoes with heel essential; if none available, elevate heel with plate

## Recovery/Injury Notes
- **Avoid belts long-term** (atrophies stabilizer muscles/connective tissue)
- Minimize knee wraps (compression over support; bodyweight-style wraps preferable to powerlifting wraps)
- **Forgo wrist wraps, focus on core bracing**
- Prioritize controlled movement to prevent injury

---

## ATHLETE-SPECIFIC RELEVANCE
⚠️ **Chronic back pain + night shift (Wed–Sun fatigue)**
- Slow eccentric + forward lean cue may aggravate; prioritize **neutral spine positioning** during descent
- Light loads early; manage volume (4–6 sets may overtax fatigued nervous system)
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)** → back squat depth/mobility work critical; this squat protocol (hip back, slight forward lean) builds necessary dorsal chain tolerance
- **No split jerk** → relies on leg drive; strong squat velocity essential

**Recommendation**: Monitor back pain response to forward lean cue; modify to vertical torso if needed.
