# TOROKHTIY's training CAMP (April 15-29, 2017)

This text is not about weightlifting coaching for an intermediate athlete with chronic back pain; it is a promotional testimonial and advertisement for a specific Olympic weightlifting training camp in Ukraine led by Alexei Taroti. It lacks specific programming principles, technique cues, or recovery advice tailored to the user's profile (night shift work, injury history, push-jerk limitation).
