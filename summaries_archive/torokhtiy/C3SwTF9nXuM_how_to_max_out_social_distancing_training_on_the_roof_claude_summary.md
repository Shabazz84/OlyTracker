# Analysis: Russian Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## Programming Principles
- **Elbow rotation drill**: 3-4 sets x 10 reps (or 2 sets sufficient); light loading
- **Clean/jerk work**: ~40-50% intensity mentioned
- **Front squat**: Variable loading with straps during warm-up (3-4 sets)
- **Session structure**: Chest-heavy day → shifted focus to legs + lower back + cleans + squats + accessory work
- **Travel hack**: Prioritize leg training before flight days (rest during travel)

## Technique Cues

**Elbow Rotation Drill** (setup on rack with collars/weights):
- Start: elbows at "12 o'clock" (high/vertical)
- Rotate elbows forward with slight squat, finish at "3 o'clock" (270° rotation)
- Purpose: Develops elbow turnover for clean catch position

**Clean pull/extension**:
- Stay on full foot—don't shift to toes
- Drive via 1st and 2nd pull, full extension
- Finish with arm/elbow drive upward + torso DOWN
- Avoid hitching/squatting back; if losing position, reduce weight

**Front squat with straps** (lighter loads):
- Maintain bar on palm center; open grip slightly but bar stays secured by straps
- Prevents grip collapse onto fingers
- Improves front rack position for clean/push press

## Exercise Recommendations
1. **Elbow rotation drill** (mobility + catch prep)
2. **Front squat with straps** (warm-up, 3-4 light sets)
3. **Full front squats** (main work)
4. **Clean pulls/cleans** (technique emphasis)

## Recovery/Mobility Advice
- **Straps during warm-up** help address front rack mobility limitations
- Don't force heavy weight with poor mobility—reduce load
