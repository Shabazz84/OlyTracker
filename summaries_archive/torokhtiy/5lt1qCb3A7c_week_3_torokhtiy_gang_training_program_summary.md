# WEEK #3 / TOROKHTIY_GANG training program

**Note:** The provided text is a transcript of a Russian weightlifting coach explaining an 8-week training program ("Tarakhan Gun Project"). It contains specific technical cues and exercise selections relevant to your profile.

### 1. Programming Principles
*   **Structure:** 3 main sessions (Mon, Wed, Fri) + 2 accessory sessions (Tue, Thu). Each session has exactly 4 exercises.
*   **Periodization:** The current phase is weeks 3 of an 8-week block. Weeks 1–2 were "introductory" to build tolerance; Week 4 will be a deload week. Intensity increases progressively through the block.
*   **Volume/Intensity:**
    *   Main lifts are performed with controlled percentages (not maxed out immediately).
    *   Complex movements (Snatch + Snatch from Hang, Clean + Squat + Jerk) use 2 sets per weight to prioritize technique over load.
    *   Squats on the chest and back are alternated in volume (3s, 4s, or singles) based on coach variation.
*   **Frequency:** High frequency (5 days/week), but with specific rest days built into the weekly cycle.

### 2. Technique Cues
*   **Snatch & Clean (General):** Keep the bar as close to the body as possible throughout the pull; avoid "jumping" on the bar too early. Maintain a full grip (do not hold on fingers only).
*   **Clean:** Control the bar descent; ensure the bar is caught in the "full hand," not sliding off the fingers during the catch.
*   **Snatch Pulls:** Maintain a consistent body angle; keep shoulders over the bar at all times to prevent shifting center of gravity onto toes (losing leverage).
*   **Squats (Front & Back):** Keep the spine absolutely straight/flat. Do not let hips rise faster than shoulders ("standing up too early"). For front squats, ensure elbows are high and chest is up.
*   **Snatch from Hang:** Perform a 2–3 second pause at the bottom of the squat position to feel bar path (forward = error; backward = error).
*   **Hyperextensions:** Pause for ~1 second at full extension to engage the posterior chain correctly rather than using momentum.

### 3. Exercise Recommendations
*   **Posterior Chain:** Hyperextensions (with pause), Snatch Pulls, Clean Pulls.
*   **Olympic Lift Variations:**
    *   Clean + Front Squat + Jerk (Complex).
    *   Snatch + Snatch from Hang (from platform or hang position).
    *   Clean and Jerk variations with straps to protect forearms/masses.
*   **Accessory/Strength:** Back Squats, Front Squats (lower % than back squats), Good Mornings (slow eccentric, fast concentric, knees bent slightly).
*   **Explosive Power:** Box Jumps/Plyometrics from a full squat position (no countermovement/sway).

### 4. Recovery or Mobility Advice
*   **Rest Days:** The program explicitly schedules Week 4 as a "deload/unloading" week for recovery after the first 3 weeks of ramping up.
*   **Shoulder/Forearm Care:** Use lifting straps on pulling movements (Clean Pulls, Snatch Pulls) when working at higher percentages to reduce grip fatigue and protect calluses/masses.
*   **Breathing:** Emphasize proper breathing mechanics during complexes (inhale before the pull, exhale upon standing/squatting).

### 5. Relevance to Athlete Profile
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The coach emphasizes strict spinal alignment in squats and front squats ("spine must be straight") and uses hyperextensions with pauses to strengthen the posterior chain without excessive load. *Caution:* Ensure the "Good Morning" variation (slow down, fast up) does not aggravate pain; start light as advised by the coach (max 70kg for him).
*   **Push Jerk Only:** The program includes "Clean + Squat + Jerk" complexes and specific jerk work within the flow of lifts. Since you do a push jerk, focus on the "full squat" position in the complex to build the necessary leg drive without needing split mechanics.
*   **Night Shift Worker (Wed–Sun):** The program runs Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat/Sun. This aligns perfectly with your off-days if you can train Tue/Thu as well, or at least hit the main sessions on Wed/Fri/Sun. Ensure sleep hygiene is prioritized during the "deload" week to aid recovery from night shifts.
*   **OHS Limiter (50kg):** The program includes Front Squats and Snatch Pulls which are critical for overhead stability and strength transfer, though specific OHS volume isn't explicitly detailed in this transcript snippet beyond general squatting variations.
