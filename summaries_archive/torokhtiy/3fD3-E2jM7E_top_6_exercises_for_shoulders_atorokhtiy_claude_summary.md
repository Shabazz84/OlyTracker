## Extracted Content:

**1. Programming Principles**
- Accessory work: 2–3 sets per exercise
- Rep ranges: 6–10 reps (most exercises); 8–10 reps (dumbbell work)
- Intensity: Light-to-moderate for warm-up/accessory (bands, bodyweight); progressive loading where applicable

**2. Technique Cues**
- **Seated/Split-Squat Press:** Scapular retraction + shoulder elevation for stable lockout; transition from seated bench → full squat depth progressively
- **Overhead Stability (press/snatch grip):** 4+ sec holds overhead; minimize weight, maximize hold duration for shoulder stability
- **Split-Squat Press Combo:** Strict split position (front leg loaded, back leg >90°); press 4–5 reps, then descend into split squat until knee touches floor

- Band shoulder circuit (lateral raises, reverse flyes, rotations 10–20 reps)

- Dumbbell bent-over lateral raises (elbows/pinkies drive up; slow eccentric) — 8–10 reps
- Seated barbell press from squat position (scapular engagement focus)
- Overhead hold + stabilization work (minimal load, maximal time under tension)
- **Split-squat press + squat combos** (advanced finisher)
