# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis: Week 3 of 8-Week Program

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Periodization**: 8-week block; Weeks 1-2 were introductory/ramping; Week 3 is work phase; Week 4 is deload
- **Structure**: 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional supplementary (Tue/Thu)
- **Per session**: 4 exercises each
- **Sets/Reps**: Variable within week (4×4, 3×3, singles mentioned); specifics tied to percentages in written program
- **Load philosophy**: "Technique first, not maximal strength" emphasized repeatedly

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**

**Clean:**
- Bar path: Keep bar as close to body as possible
- Grip: Use full hand grip (not fingertips); prevents slipping
- Catch: Secure bar in full grip at catch position

**Snatch + Snatch from Hang:**
- Fixation: Hold 2-3 sec pause in full squat to feel bar position (forward = you know the error; back = control it)
- "Hang" defined: ~2-3cm above platform (or below knees, depending on athlete)
- Emphasis on pausing during warm-ups, optional at heavier loads

**Snatch Pull (Ryvkovaya Tyaga):**
- Maintain torso angle; shoulders always cover the bar
- Common error: Athletes lean back, shift center to toes, lose stability → compromises heavy lifting

**Front Squat:**
- Upright torso; keep chest up, shoulders elevated
- Common error: Rounding back (flexed spine)
- Knees: Bend and splay outward to distribute load away from lower back

**Good Mornings (Naklony):**
- Knees slightly bent, spread outward
- Slow descent, fast extension
- Error: Straight knees + spinal flexion → excessive lower back stress

**Box Jumps:**
- Full squat start (no rocking/bounce)
- Trains explosive power

## 4. **Recovery & Mobility**
- **Straps usage**: Employed in pull variants and front squats to spare forearms/hands, preserve skin
- **Breathing note**: Warns athletes will be fatigued; "endure it—it's the base phase"
- No specific mobility prescription mentioned

## **Key Takeaway**
This is a **hypertrophy/base-building phase** (8 weeks total) emphasizing movement quality and GPP via complexes and varied rep schemes. Week 3 intensifies after Week 1-2 ramp; Week 4 deloads. **Critical for this athlete**: Address Wed shift conflict and monitor lower back in high-volume front squat work.
