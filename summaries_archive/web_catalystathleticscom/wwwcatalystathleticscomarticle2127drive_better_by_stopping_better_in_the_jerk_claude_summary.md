# Analysis: "Drive Better by Stopping Better in the Jerk"

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique Cue (Jerk):**
- Master the *brake* in the dip—abruptly arrest downward movement to maximize elastic rebound
- Poor braking appears as "bogging down" (slow direction change, forward balance shift)
- This is **directly relevant**: athlete does push jerk only, suggesting likely dip/drive issues; improving brake mechanics could unlock split jerk progression

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- Jerk dips
- Jerk dip squats
- Jerk springs
- Quarter squat jumps
- Depth drops/jumps
- Build squat strength with upright posture (addresses chronic back pain + weak OHS)

**Programming Principle:**
- Prioritize squat strength foundation for jerk drive capacity
