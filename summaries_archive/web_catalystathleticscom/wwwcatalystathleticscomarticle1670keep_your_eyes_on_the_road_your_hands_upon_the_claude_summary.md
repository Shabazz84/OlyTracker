# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1670/Keep-Your-Eyes-on-the-Road-Your-Hands-Upon-the-Wheel-Committ/

**This text is about program design philosophy, not technical coaching or programming specifics.**

**Key Takeaway for This Athlete:**

**Programming Principle:** Foreman emphasizes *prioritization and specificity*—if Olympic lifting is the goal, ancillary training (conditioning, met-cons, gymnastics) must be carefully managed to avoid excessive fatigue that compromises lift quality. He advocates intelligent organization, not elimination, of secondary goals.

**Relevance to This Athlete:**
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun):** Recovery is already compromised; adding high-volume conditioning could sabotage the planned 6-week hypertrophy block. Prioritize lifting first, condition secondarily if at all.
- **Intermediate transitioning to OWL:** Has room for a focused, specialized block. The hypertrophy phase should be *protected* from competing stimuli (running, met-cons).
- **Chronic back pain:** Extra fatigue from conditioning exacerbates this. Keep additional work minimal during the 6-week block.

**Actionable:** If conditioning is desired, place it *after* lifting and only if energy permits. Make explicit choice: Is the priority hypertrophy + technique, or diluted split training?
