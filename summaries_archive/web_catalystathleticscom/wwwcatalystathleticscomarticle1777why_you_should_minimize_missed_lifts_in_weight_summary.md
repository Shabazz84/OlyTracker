# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1777/Why-You-Should-Minimize-Missed-Lifts-in-Weightlifting-Traini/

1. **Programming Principles**: Limit missed lifts to a very small percentage of total volume; if misses are frequent, the prescribed weights/reps are unreasonable and must be adjusted immediately. Establish strict rules beforehand for accepting a miss versus repeating a set to prevent "training yourself not to be prepared." Misses should never become routine or mentally accepted as normal practice.

3. **Exercise Recommendations**: None provided; the text discusses general training philosophy regarding lift success rates, not specific exercises.

4. **Recovery or Mobility Advice**: None provided.

5. **Athlete Profile Relevance**: The article is highly relevant to the athlete's need for mental discipline during a hypertrophy block. As an intermediate transitioning to Olympic lifting with chronic pain and night shift fatigue, the risk of "practicing to miss" due to poor recovery or suboptimal programming is high. The text warns that missing 10% (as noted in the comments) may be acceptable for technical work but requires strict adherence to not repeating sets indefinitely, which prevents wasting limited energy on failed attempts and reinforces focus—critical for an athlete with a demanding schedule and pain management needs.
