# Weightlifting Analysis: Fixing Droopy Elbows in the Jerk

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Strengthening exercises**: Dip-pause-push press and dip-pause-hold (5 reps/set)
- **Loading**: Dip-pause-hold performed at maximum jerk weight
- **Intensity/rep structure**: 5 reps with pauses to develop postural stability under heavy load

## 2. **Technique Cues for Jerk**
- **Setup**: Shoulders not slouched, butt positioned properly, weight on heels
- **Dip phase**: 
  - Inhale before dip
  - Dip butt *through* heels (not forward onto toes)
  - Maintain upright torso posture
- **Problem**: Elbows dropping during dip transition indicates either weak postural stabilizers or faulty movement pattern

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Dip-pause-push press**: 1-second pause in bottom, then drive (addresses transition weakness)
- **Dip-pause-hold**: Dip, hold 1 second, stand up—no drive phase. Done at max jerk weight, 5 reps/set
- Both emphasize *postural strength* through isometric holds

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Not addressed in text

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
**High relevance** — Athlete has **push jerk only** (no split jerk) and weak OHS (50 kg). Droopy elbows and poor rack stability are likely limiting jerk development. These dip-pause exercises would strengthen torso/shoulder stability without requiring split jerk technique. Recommended for hypertrophy block before strength phase.
