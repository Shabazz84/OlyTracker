# Analysis: Greg Everett on Lower Back Strengthening

## **Directly Relevant to This Athlete:**

**Exercise Recommendations (Back-Specific):**
- **Floating snatch deadlifts** — primary tool for lower back strength + extension maintenance + proper first pull positioning
- **Snatch segment deadlifts/pulls** — 3-sec pauses at 1" off floor and knee
- **Back extensions** (spine flexion/extension, not hip hinge):
  - Daily warm-up: 3×10 unweighted OR 1–2 sets static holds (10–30 sec extended position)
  - 1–2×/week: 3×10 weighted (light dumbbells → barbell behind neck), 1-sec hold at extension
- **Weighted planks** + general ab work for trunk stability

**Technique Diagnosis:**
- Likely issue: rushing bar off floor (missing solid arch setup) OR shoulders too far in front of bar (excessive forward lean in first pull)
- Both place excessive demand on lower back → solve via position/technique focus, not strength alone

**Recovery Note:**
- Avoid serious fatigue on back extension holds; build conservatively over time

## **Programming Context:**
Daily back work (light) + 1–2× weekly weighted back extensions fit easily into a hypertrophy block without interference.

## **Critical Gap for This Athlete:**
Article doesn't address night shift recovery impact on back health or loading tolerance—coach should monitor fatigue accumulation (Wed–Sun schedule).
