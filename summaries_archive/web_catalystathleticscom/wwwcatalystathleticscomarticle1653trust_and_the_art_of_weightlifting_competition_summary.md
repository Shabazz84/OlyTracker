# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1653/Trust-and-the-Art-of-Weightlifting-Competition/

This text is an inspirational essay on the psychology of competition, trust, and adrenaline in Olympic weightlifting; **it contains no specific programming principles, technique cues, exercise recommendations, or recovery advice.**

While the author mentions visualizing lifts ("digging heels," "popping bar into hips") and the mental shift from viewing nerves as fear to excitement, these are general motivational concepts rather than actionable coaching directives for an intermediate athlete with chronic back pain or specific training constraints.
