# Analysis: Getting the Bar Past the Knees in the Snatch & Clean

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique cues (HIGH PRIORITY—directly addresses snatch limiter):**
- **Mental frame**: Think of first pull as a squat—"hold the bar and stand up" (not isolated knee-pushing)
- **Starting position criteria**: Bar over balls of foot, knees pushed out to sides inside arms, knees slightly over bar, shoulders directly above bar
- **First pull execution**: Maintain constant back angle; legs extend while shoulders stay over bar (no early hip extension)
- **Common error**: Pulling bar back into legs = technique fault, not knee positioning issue
- **Shin position**: Should have forward inclination at knee height; vertical shins too early forces premature hip extension

**Exercises for technique refinement:**
- Segment deadlift/pull with pauses (1" off floor, knee, mid-thigh) — excellent for this athlete's chronic back pain + OHS deficit
- Floating deadlift on riser — builds "squat up" feel safely
- Slow-pull snatches/cleans — integrates positioning into full lifts

**Recovery/mobility note:**
- Segment work naturally strengthens pulling posture without load stress (relevant to chronic back pain)
