# Analysis: "Rocking to Heels in Pull & Staying Flat-Footed" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Tempo work emphasized**: Slow, controlled pulls to ingrain correct positions neurologically before adding speed
- No specific set/rep schemes provided; focus is corrective/technique-based

## 2. **Technique Cues (Clean & Snatch)**

**Problem Diagnosis:**
- Early heel rise ≠ necessarily a problem if balanced and timed correctly
- Issue arises when heel rise accompanies **forward body motion** (chasing bar, excessive hip extension forward, or excessive early knee extension)

**Key Cues:**
- **Stay balanced across the entire foot** through mid-thigh in first pull
- **Keep bar close** to legs without dragging (prevents forward chase)
- **Second pull**: Drive **vertically and forcefully** with legs; let hip extension happen naturally (don't push hips forward)
- Actively guide bar close to body as it travels upward
- Maintain **shoulders directly above or slightly ahead of bar** through pull

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

- **Halting deadlifts** (slow tempo, up AND down)
- **Pauses** at: separation, knee, mid-thigh—hold between each
- **Power position snatches/cleans** (feet flat, knees bent, vertical trunk)—hold 1–2 sec before lifting; no quick bounce
- **Pull + snatch/clean complex** (slow pulls preceding lifts)
