# Analysis: "Pulling Position at the Knee: Get It Right" by Greg Everett

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean Pull):**
- **Shins upright and nearly vertical** at knee
- **Shoulders directly above to slightly in front of bar** until bar reaches lower thigh (ideally mid-thigh)
- Shoulders should **never be behind the bar** at this point
- Premature shoulder retreat causes: reduced hip extension power, bar drag on thighs, forward knee shift, poor bar path, potential forward jump

**Why This Matters for Your Profile:**
- Your **OHS (50 kg) is your snatch limiter**—likely due to pulling mechanics or confidence under load
- Poor pulling position (shoulders behind bar early) → weak extension → difficulty receiving snatch at depth
- Chronic back pain + night shift fatigue → sloppy form more likely; maintaining shoulder-over-bar position is non-negotiable to protect spine

**Application:**
- During your 6-week hypertrophy block: film pulls frequently; prioritize pulling **position** over load
- Use hang positions to drill staying over the bar; this directly addresses snatch weakness
- Clean pull mechanics are foundational for your jerk transition (split jerk teaching requires confident pulling mechanics first)
