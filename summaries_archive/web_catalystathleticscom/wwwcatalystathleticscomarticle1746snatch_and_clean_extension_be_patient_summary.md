**2. Technique cues for snatch/clean**
*   **"Be Patient":** Do not initiate the final explosion (jump) too early. Wait until the bar is at mid-to-upper thigh (clean) or hip level (snatch).
*   **Bar Path Control:** Actively use the lats to push the bar *back into the hips*, not just near them. This prevents shifting balance forward and over-reaching.
*   **Contact Point:** The bar must touch the body/hips during the pull. If it never touches, the lift is flawed. A "pop" sound upon contact is normal but should not be forced as a goal.
*   **Shoulder Position:** Keep shoulders over the bar until upper thigh/hip level to maintain balance before exploding.
*   **Arm Position:** Keep arms long and loose (not stiff) while pushing with lats; avoid "stiff-arming" the bar away after extension.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Drills:** Deficit snatch/clean and close grip snatch are recommended to learn explosion based on bar position rather than timing.
*   **Video Study:** Review slow-motion archives or specific DVDs (e.g., Greg Everett's) to analyze frame-by-frame mechanics.

**5. Relevance to athlete profile**

*   **Push Jerk Limitation:** The article emphasizes "snapping hips open" and jumping to generate bar height.
