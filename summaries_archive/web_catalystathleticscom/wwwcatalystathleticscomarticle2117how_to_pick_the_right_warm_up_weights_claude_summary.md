# Analysis: Warm-up Weight Selection for Olympic Lifting

## Relevance to Athlete Profile
**High relevance.** This article directly addresses competition/training warm-up sequencing—critical for this athlete's transitional phase and chronic back pain management.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Warmup progression approach:** Build from empty bar → working weight using multiple small jumps (NOT large single jumps)
- **Percentage-based framework:** Typical range 27%–94% of competition max across 10–18+ warmup lifts
- **Individual variation:** Top lifters use 27%–37% starting points; jumps range 5–15% increments
- **Competition vs. training:** C&J warmups are shorter with bigger jumps (body already fatigued post-snatch)
- **Avoid over-warming:** Excessive volume (9+ lifts) causes fatigue degradation for actual attempts
