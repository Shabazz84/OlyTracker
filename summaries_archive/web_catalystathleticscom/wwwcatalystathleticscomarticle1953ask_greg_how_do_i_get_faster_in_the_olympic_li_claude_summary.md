# Analysis: "Ask Greg: How Do I Get Faster in the Olympic Lifts?"

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- Train speed with **50-70% intensity** (lighter loads for rate of force development)
- Separate speed work from heavy strength work in same session (e.g., 60-70% speed sets *before* 80%+ heavy work)
- All concentric movements performed with **maximal intent/aggression** unless specific reason otherwise
- Prioritize timing and skill development over pure muscular speed

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Pull under the bar** must be "just as violent and aggressive" as the upward acceleration
- Focus on continuous second & third pulls (no hesitation at top of extension)
- Emphasize **speed of direction change** (lifting→pulling under) over absolute bar speed
- Maintain proper balance, close bar contact, appropriate lower body drive without upper body tension

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Hang/block lifts (snatch, clean)
- Power variations
- High-pulls, muscle snatches/cleans
- Tall snatches/cleans
- Dip snatches/cleans
- Jumping exercises

## 4. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Tall snatches/cleans and block work will improve pull-under speed and mechanics, directly translating to faster positioning under bar
- **Back pain consideration**: Block/hang work reduces spinal load vs. full pulls from floor—good for chronic pain management
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Can integrate speed work at 50-70% *before* heavier hypertrophy sets to build power foundation
