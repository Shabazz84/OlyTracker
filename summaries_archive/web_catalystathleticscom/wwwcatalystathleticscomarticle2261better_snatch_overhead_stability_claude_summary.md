# Analysis: Better Snatch Overhead Stability by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Low-hanging fruit first**: Hold lifts overhead forcefully 3–5 seconds in every set (snatch, snatch balance, OHS)
- **Complexes**: Add OHS and/or snatch balance to end of snatch/power snatch sets
- **Standalone exercises**: Overhead squat, heaving snatch balance, snatch balance, snatch push press, press in snatch, overhead carries
- **Minimal systemic fatigue approach**: Snatch push press recommended to avoid excessive leg volume

## 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch Overhead Position)**
- **Shoulder blade foundation**: Retraction + upward rotation; squeeze upper inner edges of shoulder blades together
- **Head position**: Push head forward through arms (enables full scapular retraction)
- **Trunk**: Slight forward lean to orient arms vertically, bar over base of neck
- **Elbow**: Neutral rotation (~halfway between straight down and straight back)
- **Wrist/hand**: Bar rolled to back of forearm (like squat rack cradle), not high in fingers; NOT aligned straight with forearm

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Strength builders**: Overhead squat, heaving snatch balance, snatch balance, snatch push press
- **Mobility/technique**: Press in snatch (Sots press), snatch-grip press behind neck
- **Stability work**: Overhead carries (add instability with rubber band-hung weights)
- **Progression**: Start with holds, then complexes, then add standalone work

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Press in snatch builds whole-body mobility and comfort in position
- Overhead carries combine time under tension with dynamic stability

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **CRITICAL**: OHS is stated limiter (50 kg); this article is directly applicable—heavy focus on overhead position structure and OHS work
- **Chronic back pain**: Forward trunk lean cue may help reduce shear stress; emphasis on position integrity over loading initially fits hypertrophy block plan
- **Push jerk only**: No split jerk = overhead stability even more critical for jerk progression; snatch push press recommended here aligns well
- **Night shift fatigue**: Low-hanging fruit (holds) adds minimal volume; snatch push press avoids extra leg fatigue
