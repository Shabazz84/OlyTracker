# Analysis: Hip Position in Squats & Cleans

## Technique Cues (Most Relevant)
- **Hip shooting back problem has two causes:**
  1. **Limited ankle mobility** → shins can't move forward adequately → legs rebound backward
     - Solution: Improve ankle mobility + actively lead with chest out of bottom
  2. **Hips rise faster than shoulders** → indicates quad weakness relative to hip strength
     - Solution: Pause squats (maintain posture threshold), slow eccentrics, drive head/shoulders up first

- **General cue:** "Drive your head and shoulders up first and follow the bar with the rest of your body"

## Exercise Recommendations
- **Pause squats** (high priority for this athlete—see below)
- Slow eccentric squats
- Both used to reinforce proper positioning without momentum-driven errors

- **Chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg)** → Hip/quad strength imbalances and ankle mobility likely contributors
