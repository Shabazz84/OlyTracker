**1. Programming principles**
*   **Frequency:** Trunk stability work (flexion, lateral flexion, rotation, isometric, anti-rotation) should be included **every training day**.
*   **Intensity/Volume:** Bracing must be forceful even during light warm-ups; consistency in bracing quality is prioritized over load.
*   **Periodization:** No specific block structure mentioned, but the text emphasizes immediate correction of technical faults to ensure longevity before heavy loading.

**2. Technique cues for Split Jerk**
*   **Trunk Position:** Maintain a solid, neutral spine; avoid lower back hyperextension or abrupt hinging at the thoracolumbar junction.
*   **Back Leg:** Keep the back knee **slightly bent**; straightening it pulls the pelvis into anterior rotation (causing lumbar extension).
*   **Bracing:** Pressurize and brace the trunk forcefully to prevent the lower back from extending as a compensatory mechanism.
*   **Drive:** Drive the bar high enough to avoid "wedging" under it; avoid pushing/driving arms forward which causes overbalancing on the front foot.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Mobility Work:** Specific shoulder and thoracic spine mobility drills (referenced as videos in source).
*   **Trunk Stability:** Daily exercises targeting flexion, lateral flexion, rotation, isometric stability, back extension, and anti-rotation for the abs.

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Mobility Focus:** Prioritize improving shoulder and thoracic spine mobility to prevent compensatory lumbar hyperextension.
*   **Hip Flexor Flexibility:** Address hip flexor tightness to reduce anterior pelvic rotation in the split position.
