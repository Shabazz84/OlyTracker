**Relevant Takeaways for this Athlete's Profile:**
The only applicable advice from this text is **simulation training**:
1.  **Environment Simulation:** Since the athlete has chronic back pain and works nights, they should practice lifting in "hot/stifling" conditions (simulating a crowded warm-up room) to avoid thermal shock on meet day.
2.  **Bar Familiarity:** Use competition-style collars during heavy lifts to prevent the bar from feeling different due to weight distribution changes.
3.  **Routine Consistency:** Strictly adhere to a planned warm-up progression rather than a "laissez-faire" approach, even if it requires being assertive about securing equipment.
4.  **Caffeine/Nutrition Protocol:** Do not test new caffeine or food strategies; the athlete must practice their exact pre-lift nutrition/caffeine timing during training to avoid digestive issues or under-fueling on competition day.
