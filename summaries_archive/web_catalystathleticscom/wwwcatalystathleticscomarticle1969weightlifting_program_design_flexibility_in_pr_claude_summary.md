# Analysis: Weightlifting Program Design: Flexibility in Prescriptions

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Prescribed vs. Unprescribed Intensity**: Use percentages/weights when coaching remotely or managing lifters who lack self-awareness about loading. Avoid prescriptions for beginners (unstable 1RMs, rapid progression) or when maximal daily effort is the goal.
- **Periodization Approach**: Plan macrocycle/mesocycle structure upfront; fill in microcycle details as the block progresses using accumulated training data.
- **Flexibility Within Structure**: Prescribe intensities as a framework, then adjust daily based on lifter appearance/feel. Minor tweaks preserve intent; major changes (skip workouts, drop exercises) acceptable when needed.

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Auxiliary exercises (presses, good mornings, stiff-legged deadlifts) best left to feel/progression cues rather than strict percentages.
- Exercises without recent 1RMs or where 1RMs are inadvisable: determine intensity by feel and coach observation.
