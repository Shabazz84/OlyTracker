**2. Technique cues**
*   **Snatch/Clean Foot Spacing:** Distinct foot spacing is required for the pull vs. the squat/receiving phase; do not keep feet narrow (pulling width).
*   **"Too Wide" Cue:** Avoid jumping feet out excessively, creating a "wide-stance powerlifting back squat" that limits depth and max weight potential.
*   **"Too Narrow" Cue:** Avoid keeping feet too close ("bunched up"), which causes the butt to stick back, torso to lean forward, and creates an unstable, inefficient position.
*   **The Fix:** Spread feet wider during the turnover to bring the hips closer to the heels, allowing for a more upright torso and deeper bottom position.
