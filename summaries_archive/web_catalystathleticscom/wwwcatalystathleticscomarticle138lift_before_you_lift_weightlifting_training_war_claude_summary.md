# Analysis: "Lift Before You Lift: Weightlifting Training Warm-ups" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **General volume**: 3–5 sets of 3–5 reps for warm-up exercises
- **Intensity**: Light weights; avoid loading to show off
- **Placement**: Immediately before associated lifts; don't exhaust before heavy work
- **Periodization principle**: Use these drills as teaching/reinforcement tools throughout training blocks

## 2. **Technique Cues**

**Sots Press (Press in Snatch/Clean)**
- Fully locked-out overhead position required
- Feet flat; avoid rolling to inside edges or dropping knees inward
- Smooth, fluid movement—don't grind reps or hunch into poor posture

**Muscle Snatch & Muscle Clean**
- Aggressively pull elbows high *before* turnover
- Internal arm rotation in starting position
- No straps (builds grip + hook-grip release timing)
- Muscle clean: maintain full grip on bar as long as possible; avoid releasing to fingertips early

**Tall Split Jerk & Jump/Drop to Split**
- Head pulled back; bar ~vertically above shoulders
- Elbows spread to sides (underneath bar)
- 50-50 weight balance between feet
- Step feet only—no forward chest lean
- Hold split 3–5 seconds for comfort/strength adaptation
- Front foot recovers first (~1/3 back), then rear foot

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Priority for this athlete**: 
  - **Sots Press (snatch)** → directly addresses overhead squat limitation (50 kg OHS)
  - **Muscle Snatch** → reinforces turnover mechanics
  - **Tall Split Jerk** → builds split comfort (currently push jerk only; needs split jerk development)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Proper warm-up prevents joint stiffness ("creaky knees, hips, ankles, elbows")
- T-spine/shoulder mobility cues: foam roller/half-roll perpendicular to spine, arms overhead; hanging shoulder stretch; behind-the-neck pressing
- Sots press doubles as mobility + strength work (lower & upper body)

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
| Issue | Recommended Solution |
|-------|---------------------|
| **OHS @ 50 kg** (primary snatch limiter) | Sots Press in snatch 3×5–5 reps; addresses mobility + stability |
| **Push jerk only** (no split jerk) | Tall Split Jerk + Jump/Drop to Split drills; build 3–5 sec holds |
| **Chronic back pain** | Light-loaded drills with proper posture; avoid grinding reps or hunching |
| **Night shift schedule** | Efficient 10–15 min warm-up protocol pre-lift (these exercises kill two birds) |

**Key insight for 6-week hypertrophy block**: Use these drills *before* strength/technique sessions to prep without compromising main lift capacity.
