### 2. **Technique Cues (HIGHLY RELEVANT)**

**Foot Spacing in Snatch/Clean Receiving Position:**
- **Too Wide**: Feet jump excessively outward → wide powerlifting squat stance → limits depth → power snatch/clean only → weight ceiling
- **Too Narrow**: Feet don't move from pull position (8-9" apart) → bunched bottom → butt sticks out → torso leans forward → technical disaster overhead/on shoulders → unsafe & inefficient

**Correct Approach**: Wider foot spacing in receiving position vs. pulling position brings butt closer to heels → upright torso → stability & safety
