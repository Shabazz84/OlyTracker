## Key Takeaways (For Future Reference)

### Technique Cues – Split Jerk (When Progressing)
- **Front knee:** Directly above ankle or *slightly behind* it (NOT in front). Improves stability and prevents forward shift under load.
- **Back knee:** Unlocked, slightly bent (not straight, not 90°). Back knee should sit *behind the hips* when viewed from side.
- **Rationale:** Allows both knees to bend together → depth straight down; prevents forward lean and hip flexor strain.

### Training Recommendations
- Hold all jerk recoveries 2–5 seconds (improves position awareness, builds strength)
- If landing poorly: correct position, hold, then recover
- Accessory work: drop-to-split, jump-to-split, jerk balance, tall jerk
