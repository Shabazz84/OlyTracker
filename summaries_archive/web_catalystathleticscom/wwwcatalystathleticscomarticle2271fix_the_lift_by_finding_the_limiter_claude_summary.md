# Analysis: "Fix The Lift by Finding The Limiter" by Greg Everett

## Programming Principles
- **Limiter-based approach**: Diagnose bottlenecks (strength vs. technique vs. mobility) and prioritize correcting the limiting factor for maximum ROI
- **Lift ratio assessment**: Compare Olympic lift performance to basic lifts (squat/deadlift). Large gaps indicate technique/mobility issues, not strength deficiency

## Technique Cues
- None specific; article emphasizes diagnostic methodology over direct cues

## Exercise Recommendations
- Use **positional testing** to diagnose:
  - OHS stability → snatch mobility limiter
  - Front squat → clean mobility limiter  
  - Rack/overhead position → jerk mobility limiter
- If positions are solid in isolation but poor during lifts → **technique is the limiter**
