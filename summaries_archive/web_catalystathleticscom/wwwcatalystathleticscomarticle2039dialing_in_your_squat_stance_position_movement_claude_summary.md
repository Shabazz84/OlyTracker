# Analysis: Squat Technique Article (Greg Everett)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Volume strategy**: Majority of squatting volume below weight threshold where position breaks down; limit heavy/imperfect reps
- **Accessory integration**: Pause squats (strengthen bottom range, prevent forward tip); front squats (quad emphasis)
- **Movement quality over speed**: Slow, properly positioned squat > fast, poorly positioned squat

## 2. **Technique Cues: Back Squat**
- **Stance**: Heels slightly wider than hip width, toes out 20–30°; foot/thigh should align when viewed from above
- **Trunk control**: Neutral-to-slightly-extended lumbar spine + minimized thoracic kyphosis = continuous arch
  - *Breathing*: Deep breath filling bottom-to-top; lock at throat; squeeze trunk circumference
  - *Cue*: Push abs DOWN (not in); maintain trunk breadth
- **Bottom position**: Knees fully closed; weight balanced across foot (slight heel preference); back extended; head up
- **Ascent**: Sit DOWN and stand UP—minimize hip drift backward; avoid forward tipping (hips shooting up faster than chest)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Mobility (high ROI)**: Spiderman lunge + squatting ankle stretch (every warm-up, between sets, daily)
- **Superhero complex**: Alternate spiderman lunge + superman hold
- **Back extension work**: Glute-ham bench or prone floor (isolate spinal extensors; exaggerate extension)
- **T-spine**: Foam roll with abs tight, back flat (avoid TL junction folding); static holds over roller, arms overhead
- **Unilateral work**: Lunges, step-ups, 1-leg RDLs (hip stability for valgus knee control)

## 4. **Recovery / Mobility Advice**
- Daily spiderman lunge + ankle stretch
- Back extension drills in warm-up and between squat sets
- Thoracic foam rolling (proper form critical)
- Static T-spine holds
- Emphasis: mobility + stability work must match squat frequency

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**

| Issue | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Chronic back pain** | Critical: Neutral lumbar arch + trunk pressurization essential; back extension isolation drills; avoid thoracolumbar hyperextension folding |
| **OHS limiter (50 kg)** | Weak squat = weak OHS; squat depth/control directly limits overhead position; this article addresses foundational squat mechanics |
| **Night shift (fatigue)** | Quality reps << quantity reps; use rest-pause/pause squats to reinforce position under fatigue |
| **Intermediate level** | Likely has stance/mobility asymmetries; spiderman + ankle stretch must be consistent; pause squats will expose quad/positioning weaknesses |

**Key priority**: Fix forward tipping (hips back, chest forward) by building relative quad strength via pause squats + proper-position volume before heavy loading.
