**5. Relevant to This Athlete's Profile:**

**Mental Framework (Most Relevant):**
- Core message: Frame all 3 attempts (snatch + C&J) as goals, not just the opener
- Language matters—avoid "just make my opener" self-talk; instead think "90, 93, 95"
- Build expectations of perfection across all 6 attempts, not acceptance of 2/6 (33% success)
- High-level competitors don't miss; success compounds from making attempts, not surviving

**Application for this athlete:**
- Given intermediate level with back pain + night shift fatigue, especially relevant: maintain high expectations even when training conditions are suboptimal. Mental toughness offsets external stressors.
- Before 6-week hypertrophy block, adopt Foreman's framing: plan progression across all attempts, not just "getting on the board."
- Particularly valuable for split jerk development (currently push jerk only)—mentality of expecting success on higher attempts will support technical risk-taking needed to learn the split.
