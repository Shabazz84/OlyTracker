# Analysis: Breathing & Breath Control for Olympic Weightlifting

## Relevant to this athlete:

**1. Technique Cue (CRITICAL for back pain):**
- Pressurize torso via full diaphragmatic breath (fill belly, not just chest)
- Tighten abs/back musculature around pressurized trunk—maintain broad base, don't "hollow"
- Maintain pressurization throughout lift to stabilize spine and reduce intervertebral disc pressure
- **Directly applicable:** Reduces spinal injury risk during heavy squats/cleans (critical given chronic back pain)

**2. Breathing Protocol:**
- Take fresh breath between each rep (not one breath per set)
- Hold breath through sticking points; release small amounts of air with sound if dizzy
- During second pull explosiveness, controlled air release is beneficial

**3. Back Pain Relevance:**
- Proper pressurization improves force transfer from legs→barbell, preserving desired spine extension
- Reduces intervertebral disc pressure
- **Key:** Avoid "hollowing" abs (common mistake) which reduces stability

**4. Dizziness Concern:**
- Valsalva + breath-hold can stimulate vagus nerve → reduced BP/HR
- If occurring during cleans (likely front squat dizziness), release small controlled amounts of air to maintain pressure without syncope
- Check bar rack position isn't compressing carotid arteries
