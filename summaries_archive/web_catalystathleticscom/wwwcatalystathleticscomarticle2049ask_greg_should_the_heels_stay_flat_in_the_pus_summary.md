**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Heel Position:** Heels should remain flat *only* during the dip; they must naturally lift (plantar flex) once knee extension begins to generate maximal force. Keeping heels perfectly flat throughout indicates a lack of drive or premature termination of the push.
*   **Dip Mechanics:** Dip and drive must be vertical, flexing/extending at the knees only. Avoid pushing hips back (hip-centric dip).
*   **Intent:** Treat the push press as a "leg exercise" first; focus on aggressive, maximal force generation into the ground rather than upper body strength.
*   **Timing:** The dip and drive mechanics must be identical to the jerk.
