**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Static Overhead Strength:** Implement 3–5 second holds overhead in snatch balances, overhead squats (OHS), and jerk supports to build stability under load.
*   **Timing & Aggression Drills:** Use drop snatches, tall snatches, and power position snatches to force immediate elbow extension ("punching up") upon bar receipt.
*   **Frequency/Volume:** No specific sets/reps provided; focus on quality reps where the athlete aggressively locks elbows immediately after receiving the bar.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **"Punch" Aggressively:** Do not delay turnover or push-under; immediate full-force extension is required to lock elbows before settling weight.
*   **Elbow Locking Timing:** Arms must be fully locked *at the moment of reception*, not after a secondary bend-and-extend motion.
*   **Shoulder Blade Fixation:** Receive bar slightly forward (not too far back) to fix shoulder blades and create a strong base; avoid "soft" systems.
*   **Wrist/Hand Position:** Keep bar over mid-hand/palm, avoiding excessive wrist extension or holding too far on fingers which causes instability at higher loads.
*   **Internal Rotation Cue:** Focus on internal rotation of the humerus during pull-under and squeeze deltoids toward ears to reduce "bounciness."

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Drop Snatches & Tall Snatches:** For timing and aggressive turnover mechanics.
*   **Snatch from Power Position:** To reinforce proper bar path and lockout placement.
*   **Jerk Supports / Overhead Squats (OHS):** With the target competition weight to test static holding strength with locked elbows.
*   **Static Holds:** Hold snatches/OHS overhead for 3–5 seconds before recovering.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Mobility Check:** Test shoulder mobility by extending elbow fully while arm hangs at side; restrictions here cause "drag" on the punch and prevent timely lockout.
*   **Elbow Structure:** Verify anatomical ability to hyperextend/fully extend elbows with arm hanging; if restricted, this is a primary mechanical blocker.
