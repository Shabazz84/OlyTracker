# Analysis: "Squat Stance & the Olympic Lifts" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Stance consistency**: Use ONE squat stance across all variations (back squat, front squat, OHS, snatch, clean, power variations)
- **Depth standard**: Always squat to full depth with upright posture and proper spinal extension
- **Recovery protocol**: 2-3 second pause in bottom position of snatches/OHS to rebuild depth habits
- **Exercise**: Pause back squats recommended for strengthening deepest range

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Squat position**: Maximal depth + most upright posture possible + proper spinal extension + balanced feet
- **Knee tracking**: Knees should move over toes (thighs roughly parallel to feet); acceptable given femur length and anatomical variance
- **Common error**: Shallow squats = shallow receiving positions = missed lifts or instability; don't cheat depth
- **OHS/snatch receiving**: Hold bottom 2-3 seconds before standing (builds confidence and positional strength)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Pause back squats
- OHS and snatch balance variations
- High-hang/high block snatches (forces deep pulling position)
- Flexibility work (hips, hamstrings for depth maintenance)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Address flexibility limitations preventing full-depth squat with spinal integrity
- Work flexibility until athlete can maintain back arch at full depth
- For injuries: squat as deep as safely tolerable without re-injury risk
