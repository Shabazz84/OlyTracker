# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2103/The-Clean-Turnover-Spin-the-Bar/

1. **Programming principles**: Not discussed; text focuses exclusively on technique mechanics rather than sets, reps, intensity, or periodization.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - "Elbow pivots around the bar" (opposite of a curl).
   - Move bar actively into proximity to shoulders *before* elbows rotate.
   - Pull elbows up and out; do not just "hump" the bar.
   - Bring bar high and back toward shoulders prior to spinning elbows.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specific; refers generally to "The Clean Turnover."
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned in this excerpt.
5. **Athlete profile relevance**: No specific adaptations for chronic back pain, night shift work, or the push-jerk-only constraint are addressed; however, the cue to avoid "humping" the bar and instead actively pulling elbows up/back is critical for an athlete with chronic back pain to reduce spinal shear during turnover.
