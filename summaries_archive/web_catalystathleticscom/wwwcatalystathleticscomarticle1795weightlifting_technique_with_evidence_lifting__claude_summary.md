# Analysis: Weightlifting Technique Article - Foot Movement in Snatch/Clean

## Relevant Principles for This Athlete

**1. Technique Cues (Primary)**
- **Foot lifting during turnover is NOT inherently bad** — world-class lifters actively lift feet 5-6" off platform during descent into receiving position
- **Individual variation is normal** — some elite lifters keep feet closer; both approaches work if executed powerfully
- **Consistency matters more than style** — best lifters look identical on 50kg and 150kg (this athlete needs motor pattern drilling across intensities)
- **Avoid universal "errors"**: rounded backs, looping pulls, jerking in front of face — these ARE inefficient

**2. Programming/Coaching Principle**
- Focus on **what works for the individual**, not dogmatic rules
- Skill development requires **repetitive, grooved motor patterns** — consistency across warm-up to max attempts

## Application to This Athlete's Profile

**DIRECTLY RELEVANT:**
- **OHS limiter (50kg)** — technical consistency in receiving position is critical. Whether feet lift or shuffle, the pattern must be reliable and repeatable
- **Push jerk only (no split jerk)** — foot mechanics less complex; focus on consistent turnover pattern
- **Chronic back pain + night shift fatigue** — avoid over-cueing foot movement; let natural pattern emerge from sound pull mechanics instead
