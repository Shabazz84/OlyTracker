# Analysis: Push Press Article

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- Push press positioned as intermediary between press and jerk
- Emphasis on leg-driven mechanics for transfer to jerk
- Better leg drive = handling more weight = improved lockout strength

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Aggressive leg drive** is primary (not upper body press)
- Ankles should extend slightly if drive is sufficient
- **Flat feet = red flag** for weak/premature leg drive cutoff
- Smooth arm press (not abrupt deceleration when transitioning from legs to arms)
- Bar position, hand placement, and arm position should mirror **jerk rack position**

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Push press as staple for strength training programs
- Utility: bridges press and jerk; improves jerk lockout strength and transfer
