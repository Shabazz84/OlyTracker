# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis: Training Camp Article

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Bulgarian-style approach** (high frequency, daily maxes) adapted for intermediate athletes
- **Final structure (6 days/week):**
  - **MWF (heavy days):** Front squat 70–75% 1×4 | Snatch max + 80–85% 2×3 | C&J max + 80–85% 1×3 | Back/front squat max + 80–85% 3×3
  - **TThSa (lighter days):** Front squat 70–75% 1×4 | Power snatch/snatch 75–80% 2×5 | Power clean/C&J 75–80% 2×5 | Push press/snatch push press 3×5 | Snatch/clean pull 80–85% 2×5
- **Key insight:** Full Bulgarian daily maxes proved unsustainable; lighter back-off work essential for recovery
- **Duration limit:** 21 days max; longer periods risk psychological/physical burnout without pharmaceutical support

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- None explicitly stated in article

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Front squat (primary strength builder)
- Back squat
- Snatch & power snatch
- Clean & jerk & power clean
- Push press/snatch push press
- Snatch/clean pulls
- Hyperextensions & abs work

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Alternating hot/cold showers post-workout
- 5–10 min easy swimming post-session
- Supplements: creatine, L-arginine, beta-alanine, ZMA (on heavy days)

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
⚠️ **NOT directly applicable.** This article describes elite-level camp programming. For your profile:
- **Chronic back pain** = avoid daily max back squats; prioritize front squat & technique work
- **Push jerk only** = split jerk development needed before full Bulgarian protocol
- **OHS 50 kg** = mobility/thoracic limitation; address before max front squat density
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** = incompatible with 6-day Bulgarian structure; prefer Mon–Tue–Fri heavy days + lighter Wed/Thu/Sat
- **6-week hypertrophy block first** = smart; build work capacity before intensity density
