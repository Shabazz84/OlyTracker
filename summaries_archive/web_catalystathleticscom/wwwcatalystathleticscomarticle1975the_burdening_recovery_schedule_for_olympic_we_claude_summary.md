# Analysis: Burdening & Recovery Schedule for Olympic Weightlifting

## 1. Programming Principles
- **2–3 weeks burdening + 1 week recovery** is the standard approach
- **4-week mesocycle options:**
  - Build 3 weeks → deload week 4
  - Build 2 weeks → deload → peak week 4
- **Lower burden/gradual progression** = longer burdening periods possible
- **Bulgarian-style** (daily heavy singles, low volume) allows continuous training without planned deloads due to minimal fatigue accumulation
- **Flexibility required:** plans must adjust based on athlete response

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Recovery scheduling is individualized based on: age, experience, training volume, restoration management, training phase
- Natural recovery can replace planned deloads when using ultra-low volume (Bulgarian approach)

## 5. Relevance to This Athlete
**Highly relevant:**
- **6-week hypertrophy block** = higher-rep/volume phase → can likely sustain 3-week burdening before deload (lower intensity initially)
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** = likely variable recovery quality → may benefit from more frequent, shorter burdening cycles (2-week build + 1-week back-off in 3-week mesocycles)
- **Chronic back pain** = prioritize flexible programming; intensity/volume adjustments mid-block are critical
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)** → hypertrophy block should emphasize high-rep OHS/squat work during burdening phase

**Key takeaway:** Use 2-3 week burdening; monitor response closely given back issues and shift work.
