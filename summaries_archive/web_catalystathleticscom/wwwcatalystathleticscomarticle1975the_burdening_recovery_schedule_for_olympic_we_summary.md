1. **Programming principles**
   - **Burdening/Recovery Cycle:** Adopt a 3-week burdening block followed by 1 week of recovery (deload) as the primary mesocycle structure for intermediate athletes. Alternatively, use a 2-week high-burden build with a peak in week 4 if the athlete tolerates intensity better than volume.
   - **Intensity/Volume:** Early phases should prioritize higher reps/volume and lower intensity to re-condition the body; intensity can be pushed harder over shorter (2-week) periods compared to longer (3-week) ones.
   - **Flexibility:** Plans must remain flexible; adjust based on fatigue management rather than rigid adherence to a schedule.
