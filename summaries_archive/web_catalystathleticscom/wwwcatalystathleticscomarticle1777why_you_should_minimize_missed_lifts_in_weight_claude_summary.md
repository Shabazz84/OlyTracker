# Analysis: "Why You Should Minimize Missed Lifts in Weightlifting Training"

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Miss rate target**: Keep misses to a small percentage of total reps (not a "big chunk"). ~10% miss rate on technique-limited lifts is acceptable if non-load-related.
- **Programming adjustment**: If missing frequently, the prescribed weights/reps are unreasonable—adjust the program rather than force bad reps.
- **Elite standard**: Top lifters make 4–5 of 6 attempts consistently; poor miss rates correlate with poor competition performance.
