**This article is not a training/programming text—it's motivational/lifestyle advice for older weightlifters starting the sport.**

---

**Relevant to this athlete:**

**Recovery & Injury Management (directly applicable):**
- Longer warm-ups and warm-downs essential
- Quality sleep prioritized
- Invest in recovery modalities (massage, chiropractic, quality mattress, good nutrition)
- Acknowledge slower recovery vs. younger lifters
- Back injury risk is higher at this age—treat proactively

**Contextual Fit:**
- Night shift work + full-time job = time/stress constraints mentioned as major challenges
- Chronic back pain fits the "injury-prone" reality discussed
- OHS limitation (50 kg) may reflect mobility/stability issues common in older lifters—prioritize shoulder/thoracic mobility work alongside hypertrophy block

**Not Directly Applicable:**
- No programming specifics (sets/reps/intensity)
- No technical cues
- No exercise recommendations
- General philosophy only: "train the way you can, not the way someone else can"

**Action:** Use this as permission to scale intensity appropriately, prioritize recovery windows around night shifts, and invest in targeted mobility for thoracic spine/shoulders before the 6-week hypertrophy block.
