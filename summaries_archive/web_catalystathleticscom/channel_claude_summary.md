# **CATALYST ATHLETICS COACHING PHILOSOPHY**
## A Synthesis for Your Intermediate Olympic Weightlifter

---

## **1. CORE TRAINING PHILOSOPHY**

### Five Principles

1. **Specificity Over Generality**
   - Build strength in the snatch/clean & jerk positions and squat; don't chase generic markers (deadlifts, bench press primacy, conditioning isolation). Olympic lifts themselves are the primary strength drivers at sufficient intensity (60%+ of competition max).

2. **Technique Precedes Load**
   - Never exceed technical ability under load. Establish positional integrity and movement consistency before aggressive intensity. Each repetition either reinforces good or bad patterns—there is no neutral rep. Correct technical faults through isolated drills, then progress to loaded training exercises.

3. **Periodization Balances Structure with Responsiveness**
   - Plan macrocycles (6-12 weeks) in mesocycles (4-week blocks) with weekly adjustments. Rigid programming fails night-shift workers and athletes with chronic pain; micro-adjust daily based on fatigue/pain status while honoring long-term intent.

4. **Recovery Capacity is the Limiting Factor**
   - Heavy strength + hypertrophy + high volume + poor sleep = injury. Prioritize uninterrupted recovery days, manage fatigue accumulation, and reduce non-essential work first (conditioning, accessory exercises) when fatigued. Strength work itself preserves fitness better than you expect.

5. **Correct the Bottleneck, Not the Strongest Link**
   - Identify the primary limiting factor (your OHS at 50 kg vs. clean at 80 kg) and prescribe accordingly, even if it feels unbalanced. Athletes naturally gravitate toward strengths; coaches enforce weakness work.

## **2. PROGRAMMING APPROACH**

### Structure: Your 6-Week Hypertrophy Block + Transition

**Weeks 1-6: Hypertrophy Block**
- **Frequency**: 3 days/week (compatible with Wed–Sun night shift; allows Mon/Tue/Thu or Sun/Tue/Thu/Fri scheduling)
- **Session structure**:
  - Day 1: **Snatch focus** (snatch, snatch pull, front squat, OHS emphasis)
  - Day 2: **Clean & jerk focus** (C&J, clean pull, back squat or front squat, jerk drive work)
  - Day 3: **Lower body + OHS emphasis** (front squat or back squat, OHS 3-5 sets, accessory hypertrophy)

**Main Lift Rep Ranges**:
- Weeks 1-4: Snatch/C&J at 3-5 reps per set (technical emphasis + volume); squats at 6 reps (hypertrophy)
- Weeks 5-6: Transition to 5 reps (reduce soreness, allow CNS recovery before strength block)
- Intensity progression: 60% → 80% over 6 weeks (avoid excessive frequency of near-max attempts; reserve 3-5 heavy singles for week 6)

**Accessory Work (2-3 min rest; GPP-focused)**:
- **Back health** (critical given chronic pain): Weighted back extensions, GHD reverse crunches, reverse hyperextensions (emphasis over heavy deadlifts)
- **Grip strength** (OHS limiter): 3x/week gripper work (5-sec holds, 5×8-12 reps moderate → 5×15-20 light → 5×3-5 heavy), snatch-grip hangs, farmer's walks
- **Core**: Hanging leg raises, ab wheel, jack knives, planks, anti-rotation holds
- **Shoulder pre-hab**: Band external/internal rotation (3x/week post-session), dynamic stretching, flexibility work
- **Avoid**: Heavy barbell deadlifts, conditioning (metcons, aggressive row/ski), excessive pressing volume without balancing pull/posterior chain work

**Load Selection**: Weight-by-feel (weight increases weekly if form holds); same weight across all sets per exercise; no touch-and-go reps (reset between lifts).

**Periodization Approach** (after 6-week block):
- **Weeks 7-12**: 4-week technique/strength cycle (snatch/C&J 1-3 reps, intensity 75-95%, high frequency)
- Structured taper into competition phase (if applicable)
- Deload every 4-6 weeks (reduce volume 15-25%, intensity 10-15%)

### **Key Programming Adjustments for Your Athlete**

| Constraint | Program Response |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Chronic back pain** | Front squat priority over back squat (safer on spine); avoid heavy posterior chain (RDL, good mornings at <5 reps); light back extensions/hyperextensions OK; manage rest periods (3-5 min if needed); use belt conservatively; pause squats to build positional strength; monitor every set for pain (distinguish soreness from injury). |
| **OHS 50 kg limiter** | 3-5 OHS sessions/week (primary snatch limiter); allocate ~20-30% of snatch-day volume to OHS; prioritize full-depth squat catches (2-3 sec pauses) to build positional strength; pair with grip work (weak hand security likely contributor); address mobility (wrist extension, ankle dorsiflexion, thoracic spine, hip internal rotation). |
