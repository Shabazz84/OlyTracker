# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1845/Understanding-Sleep-for-Optimal-Recovery-Productivity/

**NOT WEIGHTLIFTING-SPECIFIC COACHING CONTENT**

This is a general sleep physiology article applicable to recovery, but contains no programming, technique cues, or lift-specific guidance.

**RELEVANT TO THIS ATHLETE:**

**Recovery/Sleep (Critical Priority):**
- Night shift worker (Wed–Sun) = severe sleep disruption risk
- Article emphasizes: 8 hours uninterrupted sleep is non-negotiable for training recovery
- **Key actionable point:** If <8 hours available, wake during light sleep stages (REM/Stage 1-2) rather than deep sleep (Stage 3-4) to minimize grogginess and training quality loss
- First 4 hours contain most deep sleep; prioritize consistent sleep timing over total hours if constrained
- Recommendation: Use sleep cycle app/smart alarm to optimize wake time within available window

**Relevance to profile:**
- Night shift schedule directly undermines hypertrophy block effectiveness and back pain recovery
- Chronic back pain + sleep deprivation = compounded CNS/recovery deficit
- Consider consulting coach on training time shift or sleep strategy before starting 6-week block
