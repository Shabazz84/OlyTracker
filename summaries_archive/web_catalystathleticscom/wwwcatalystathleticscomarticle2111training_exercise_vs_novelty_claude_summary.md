# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2111/Training-Exercise-vs-Novelty/

**Not a training prescription.** This is a general philosophy article about program design principles.

---

**Relevant to this athlete:**

**Programming Principle:**
- Avoid chasing novelty exercises seen on social media. Focus on foundational work appropriate to *your* level, not elite lifters' random content. Critical for an intermediate athlete building a systematic hypertrophy block.

**Implicit coaching principle:**
- Programming must match athlete development stage. Elite-level exercises aren't automatically beneficial for intermediate lifters—progression should be deliberate and individualized.

---

**Application for this athlete:**
Given chronic back pain, night-shift fatigue, and weak overhead position (OHS 50 kg), prioritize **boring, proven exercises** (pause squats, front squats, block work, pressing variations) over trendy movements. No magic bullet—consistency with appropriate volume/intensity matters more.
