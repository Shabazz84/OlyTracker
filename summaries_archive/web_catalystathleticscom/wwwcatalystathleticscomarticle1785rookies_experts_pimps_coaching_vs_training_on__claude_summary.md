**Not about programming/technique for this athlete.**

This article is a coaching philosophy piece about *when athletes/coaches are ready to self-program*, not about programming principles, technique cues, or exercise recommendations.

**Relevant to athlete's context:**
- **Coaching emphasis**: This athlete (intermediate, transitioning to OL, with chronic back pain and night shift constraints) should *prioritize working with an experienced coach* rather than self-programming. Foreman's core message: build experience under expert guidance first before self-coaching.
- **Implication for 6-week hypertrophy block**: This athlete would benefit from a coach designing it, not DIY programming.

**Key quote applicable here:**
> "Neither athletes nor coaches are qualified to start building their own programs until they've had a significant body of experience where they've worked at the 'learner level' under people who are real experts."
