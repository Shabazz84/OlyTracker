# Merged Summary: Greg Everett's 3-Day Olympic Weightlifting Program

## Key Points

- **3-day/week template**: Day 1 (Snatch), Day 2 (Jerk), Day 3 (Clean & Jerk); optimal spacing Mon–Wed–Fri
- **4–6 week progression cycles**: Triples → Doubles → Singles, increasing 5–10% weekly; track rep PRs before heavy singles
- **Set/rep structure**: Competition lifts ≤3 reps; accessories in fixed rep ranges (3–5 sets) for 3–4 weeks, then rotate variations
- **Substitution flexibility**: All exercises (pulls, presses, squats) are replaceable based on athlete weaknesses
- **Deload protocol**: After 3–4 weeks, drop 10–20% of weight and reduce volume 20–40%, OR add weight Weeks 1–3 then max Week 4
- **Rest periods**: 1.5–2 min between competition lift sets; reset feet between reps (no touch-and-go)

---

## Technique Cues

- **Snatch turnover**: Use muscle snatches, tall snatches, snatch balances as positional primers; actively push bar toward body during extension
- **Bar path control**: Pull elbows high and to sides; keep bar close to prevent forward flight
- **Split jerk standard**: Program prescribes split jerk (not push jerk)—requires regression/progression plan for push-jerk-only athletes

---

## Programming Principles

- **Front squat** (Day 1, 3s): Lower back demand than back squat; fits chronic back pain constraint
- **Back squat** (Day 3, 5s): Most demanding; monitor volume or cap reps for back pain management
- **OHS exposure**: Day 2 lightweight technique work + snatch balance + OHS complexes recommended; critical for weak overhead position
- **Pulls**: Program separately at 90–110% of competition lift; reinforce bar path and third-pull timing
- **Accessory selection for back pain**: Prioritize rows, paused pin squats, hamstring curls, core stability work over pure volume
- **Night shift compatibility**: 3-day program easily fits Wed–Sun schedule (2 sessions before night shifts)
- **Hypertrophy block adjustment**: Current template is strength/technique-focused; shift to 5–8 rep ranges to support 6-week hypertrophy goal

## Athlete Relevance

| Issue | Program Fit | Action |
|-------|------------|--------|
| **OHS limiter (50 kg)** | Strong fit; Day 2 + warm-up complexes | Prioritize snatch balance, OHS, tall snatches |
| **Push jerk only (no split)** | **MISMATCH** | Plan 2–3 week split jerk progression before heavy singles |
| **Clean/Jerk gap (80/65 kg)** | Addressed via Day 2 jerk focus + push press | Will improve but split jerk mastery required |
| **Chronic back pain** | Front squat preferred; back squat volume cap needed | Use paused pin squats, accessory posterior chain work |
| **Back squat 118 kg, clean 80 kg** | Clean pulls, snatch pulls at high intensity recommended | Respect strength disparity; use accessories strategically |
| **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** | 3-day frequency is ideal | Run Mon–Wed–Fri or Tue–Thu–Sat; flexible 4th day optional |
| **6-week hypertrophy block** | Current template unsuitable; needs modification | Increase reps to 5–8, accessory volume, then transition to strength phase |
