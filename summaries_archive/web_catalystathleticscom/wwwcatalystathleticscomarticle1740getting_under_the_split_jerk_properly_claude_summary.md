# Analysis: Split Jerk Technique Article

## Directly Relevant to This Athlete

**Jerk Technique Cues (CRITICAL — athlete uses push jerk only):**
- Dip and drive must be **vertical**, not forward
- Bar moves slightly **backward** into final position
- **Hip positioning**: Keep hips under bar (feels like pushing hips forward slightly); don't lead with chest
- Leading with chest pulls hips out, destabilizes base
- Front heel reaches forward; back foot stays close to floor
- Weight balanced evenly front/back foot
- Lock shoulder blades back, bar over base of neck

**Why This Matters for Your Athlete:**
- Currently at 65 kg (push jerk only) — lacks split jerk capacity
- Split jerk is prerequisite for higher jerk totals given 102.5 kg BW
- Chronic back pain + improper forward drive = increased spinal load risk
- This article's vertical drive emphasis aligns with safe loading mechanics
