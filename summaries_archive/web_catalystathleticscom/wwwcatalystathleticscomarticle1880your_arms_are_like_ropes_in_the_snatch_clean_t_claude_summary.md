# Analysis: "Your Arms Are Like Ropes in the Snatch & Clean"

## 1. **Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean**
- **"Arms like ropes" cue**: Useful for beginners to reduce premature arm pull, but **overuse causes bar to loop forward** (away from body)
- **Critical correction**: Arms must actively participate via **slightly-upward, slightly-backward elbow motion** during transition to receiving position
- **Proper execution**: "Pull yourself under the bar" — arms follow through to keep bar close to abdomen/chest
- **Timing principle**: Know HOW and WHEN to use arms; they work synergistically with hip drive, not passively
- **Bent-arm cleaning**: Valid alternative; some elite lifters use it effectively despite "arms bend, power ends" dogma
