# Analysis: "Ask Greg: Long Limbs & The Pull"

## Relevance to Athlete
**Highly relevant.** This athlete's 102.5 kg BW with long limbs (implied by low OHS/snatch vs. squat ratios) and chronic back pain will benefit directly.

---

## 1. **Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean**

**First Pull (Setup & Positioning):**
- Bar over balls of foot or toward toes (long-limbed athletes)
- Weight balanced over whole foot
- Knees out as far as possible inside arms
- Shoulders directly above or slightly in front of bar
- **Key for this athlete:** Wider stance + aggressive knee spread to reduce thigh fore-aft distance

**Setup Modifications for Long Limbs:**
- Start bar slightly farther forward over foot (clears shin space, delays bar-shin contact)
- Shoulders slightly farther forward over bar (higher hips relative to knees, better margin of error)
- Experiment with wider stance (prevents extreme ankle angles that weaken ground drive)

**Second Pull:**
- **Critical:** Patient initiation—stay over bar until bar approaches mid-thigh before opening hips
- Don't cut pull short; trust acceleration/elevation to create time and space
- Complete extension and actively lift bar in turnover during squat-under

## 4. **Key Insight for Back Pain**
Higher hip position (shoulders forward, wider stance) may reduce lumbar strain during pull. Worth testing alongside mobility work for OHS limiter.
