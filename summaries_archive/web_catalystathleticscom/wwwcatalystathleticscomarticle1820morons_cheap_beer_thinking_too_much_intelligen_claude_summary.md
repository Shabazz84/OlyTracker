# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1820/Morons-Cheap-Beer-Thinking-Too-Much-Intelligence-Weightlif/

**Not about programming or technique.** This is a motivational/philosophical essay on overthinking in weightlifting, not a coaching guide.

**Relevant takeaway for this athlete:**

**Mental/Behavioral:** As an intermediate lifter prone to analysis paralysis (common with intelligent athletes), avoid bombarding yourself with excessive cues and technique thoughts during lifts. This is especially relevant given your chronic back pain—overthinking form can create tension and worsen pain. The "5-second rule" mentioned in comments (setup in 5 seconds, lift) could help you execute rather than ruminate.

**Bottom line:** Train smart between sessions, then trust your body during execution. Particularly useful for your night shift fatigue situation—mental clarity matters when sleep-deprived.
