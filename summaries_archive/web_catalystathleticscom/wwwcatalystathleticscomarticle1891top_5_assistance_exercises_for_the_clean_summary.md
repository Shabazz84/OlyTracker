# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1891/Top-5-Assistance-Exercises-for-the-Clean/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** Assistance exercises (Power Clean + Clean, Halting Deadlift) should be integrated frequently; the article notes Power Cleans are "present very frequently" in programs.
*   **Intensity/Volume:**
    *   **Clean Pulls:** 90–105% of best clean for strength/speed.
    *   **Sots Press:** Low intensity initially ("Heavy weights not necessary"); focus on position quality, increasing weight only as mobility improves.
    *   **Halting Deadlift:** Focus on holding the top position (shins vertical) for 3 seconds to build postural strength.
*   **Periodization/Order:** Use Sots Press at the **beginning** of a workout for mobilization and activation; use Power Cleans or Pulls later in the session for specific strength/power work.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Turnover (Clean):** Focus on elevating the bar better, changing directions immediately at the top of the pull, and pulling under quickly to avoid the "bar crashing" onto shoulders.
*   **Starting Position:** Keep shoulders in front of the bar until it reaches mid-to-upper thigh; shins must be approximately vertical before initiating the second pull.
*   **Bar Path:** Maintain maximal proximity of the bar to the body throughout the lift.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
1.  **Power Clean (+ Clean Complex):** Specifically for improving turnover speed and preventing the bar from falling onto the shoulders.
2.  **Halting Clean Deadlift:** To develop specific position strength (shoulders forward, knees back) at mid-thigh height.
3.  **Clean Pulls:** General strength/speed for first/second pulls (90-105% intensity).
4.  **Clean Pull on Riser:** To force reliance on legs rather than hips for the initial pull off the floor.
5.  **Sots Press (Press in Clean):** For ankle, hip, and thoracic spine mobility; strengthens trunk stability and upper back extensors.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Mobility Focus:** Prioritize ankle, hip, and thoracic spine extension via the Sots Press.
*   **Postural Strength:** Address "position strength" deficits (inability to maintain shoulders forward) which often cause form breakdown under heavier loads.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The article explicitly warns against the bar crashing onto the lifter during the turnover, noting it causes "unnecessary wear and tear to your back." Implementing **Power Cleans** (to improve turnover height) and **Sots Presses** (for trunk stability/thoracic extension) directly addresses this risk.
*   **Intermediate/Transition:** The text emphasizes that position strength is often lacking even in technically proficient lifters; the **Halting Clean Deadlift** is recommended to bridge the gap between technique knowledge and physical capability at heavier weights.
*   **Push Jerk Limitation:** While the article focuses on the clean, the **Sots Press** recommendation for "trunk stability during receipt" is critical for an athlete relying solely on a push jerk (which demands high thoracic mobility and core rigidity compared to a split jerk).
