## Key Content Extracted

### Technique Cues (Split Jerk — not applicable to athlete)
- **Tall jerk drill**: Isolate footwork/balance on flat feet or balls of feet; hold split 2–3 sec; light load (empty bar–30% max jerk)
- **Jerk balance drill**: Dip straight down, drive straight up; bar moves back/up as front foot steps forward into split; equal balance between feet on landing
- **Core principle**: "Bar goes up and back, foot and hips go forward" (avoid diving head forward)

### Exercise Recommendations

**For dip/drive mechanics:**
- Push press (feet stay planted; forces balanced drive)
- Push jerk (feet connected; emphasizes rhythm & leg drive quality)
- **Complexes**: Push press → push jerk → split jerk (or push jerk + split jerk variations)

**For split position/strength:**
- Jerk balance drill
- Drop to split / jump to split
- Walk to split / step to split
- Lunges & split squats (unilateral strength)
- Jerk behind the neck in split

**For inadequate drive (short-stepping):**
- Jerk drives
- Power jerks
- Jumping squats / quarter squats
- Back squat jumps

### Programming Principles
- Use light loads for technique/footwork work (empty bar–30% max)
- Prioritize **accuracy over load**—heavy weight reinforces errors
- Complexes combine balanced movement with split practice
