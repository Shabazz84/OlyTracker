**This text is about kettlebell swings, not Olympic weightlifting technique or programming directly relevant to the athlete's barbell training.**

However, one point applies:

**Recovery/Mobility Advice:**
- Overhead KB swings require adequate **shoulder and thoracic spine mobility** + trunk stability before attempting. This athlete's OHS at 50 kg (a primary snatch limiter) suggests mobility/stability gaps—traditional KB swings to chest height may be safer for conditioning work without aggravating these limitations or chronic back pain.

**Bottom line:** Traditional swings (chest-level) are more appropriate for this athlete's profile than overhead variants during the hypertrophy block. Focus on hip snap/lower back/glute conditioning without upper body compensation.
