# Analysis: "It's a Power Snatch If It's Above Parallel" by Matt Foreman

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- Power snatch/clean are **assistance exercises only** — not primary training tools for competitive OLifters
- If using them: catch at **high receiving position (~quarter-squat depth)**, NOT near-parallel
- **Primary focus should be full-depth snatch/clean** to develop proper technique and receiving position mastery
- ⚠️ **Critical for your athlete:** Shallow power catches risk poor timing/position that compromises full lift technique — especially problematic given their OHS is a primary snatch limiter (50 kg vs 80 kg clean). They need full-depth reps to build receiving position strength and stability.

**Programming Principle:**
- Power variations should **not be overemphasized** in a competitive lifter's program
- Use them situationally as supplemental work, not as ego-focused max attempts

## Not Directly Addressed
- Periodization structure (relevant to their 6-week hypertrophy block)
- Recovery/mobility for night-shift workers or chronic back pain
- Snatch-specific technique cues beyond receiving position depth

---

**Key Takeaway:** For this athlete transitioning to OL with weak OHS, prioritize **full-depth snatch/clean reps** over power variations. Power work can supplement, but shouldn't distract from building proper bottom-position mechanics.
