# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2126/Ask-Greg-Learning-to-Actively-Pull-Under-the-Bar/

### 1. Programming Principles
*   **Frequency:** High-pulls and muscle snatches should be integrated regularly as technical drills; tall snatches are recommended specifically as a "technique primer" before main snatch work in the same session.
*   **Intensity/Volume:** Focus on quality over load for these specific drills (high pulls, muscle snatches). The text implies using lighter loads to isolate the mechanics of the third pull and turnover rather than maximal intensity.
*   **Periodization:** These exercises are presented as technical corrections within a broader training block, not as standalone hypertrophy or strength blocks.

### 2. Technique Cues
*   **Elbow Position:** Aggressively pull elbows "up and out" during the third pull.
*   **Scapular Action:** Retract shoulder blades to maintain maximal proximity of the bar to the body.
*   **Timing Sequence:** Execute a correct lower-body hip drive first, followed immediately by an active arm pull (elbows up/out), *then* turn over the bar.
*   **Turnover Mechanics:** Use the momentum from the upward pull to accelerate downward and turn the bar quickly; avoid "muscling" through the turnover or simply falling under the bar passively.
*   **Punch:** Execute an aggressive, quick punch straight up against the bar during the turnover phase of muscle snatches.

### 3. Exercise Recommendations
1.  **Snatch High-Pulls:** To work timing and mechanics of the initial downward pull (ensure elbows go up/out).
2.  **Muscle Snatches:** Used as an extension of high-pulls to practice the full sequence: lower body drive → active arm pull → turnover/punch.
3.  **Tall Snatches:** Recommended as a technique primer before main snatch work; focus on the aggressive elbow pull and quick turnover without bending knees initially.

### 4. Recovery or Mobility Advice
*   *None explicitly mentioned in this text.* The article focuses strictly on technical execution of the third pull.

### 5. Relevance to Athlete Profile
*   **Technique Limiter:** This directly addresses the athlete's likely struggle with "falling under" the bar due to a lack of active arm engagement, which is critical for an intermediate lifter stuck at 80kg Clean/65kg Jerk.
*   **Back Pain Consideration:** The cue to "maintain maximal proximity of the bar to the body" via scapular retraction and proper elbow positioning reduces shear force on the lower back by keeping the load close to the center of gravity, which is vital for an athlete with chronic back pain.
*   **Jerk Limitation (Push Jerk Only):** While the text focuses on the Snatch/Clean turnover, the principle of "active pulling" and avoiding passive falling applies equally to the jerk; however, since this athlete uses a push jerk, they must ensure their active pull under the bar in the clean does not compromise their stability for the subsequent push.
*   **Night Shift/Recovery:** The recommendation to use these as "primers" allows for technical work without excessive systemic fatigue, which is beneficial for a night-shift worker with limited recovery windows.
