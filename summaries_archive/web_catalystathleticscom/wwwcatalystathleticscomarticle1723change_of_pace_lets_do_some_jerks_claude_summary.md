## 2. Technique Cues for Jerk
**Key Points (Highly Relevant to Athlete's Push Jerk Only):**
- **Jerk is a leg lift, not upper body** — dip-drive phase generates power, not arm strength.
- **Medium-split position preferred** over deep splits — emphasize driving bar *higher*, not splitting deeper.
- **Main focus: dip-drive strength**, not split depth (no ceiling on drive force; split depth has physical limits).
- **Teaching cues:**
  - Relax hands before dip
  - Dip butt between heels
  - Big chest
  - Punch over ears
  - Big air before dip

**Caution for this athlete:** Upper body weakness (65 kg push jerk) is likely *not* the limiter if dip-drive mechanics are poor. Focus on explosive leg drive first.

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Behind-the-neck jerks** — useful for beginners/intermediate lifters to groove overhead lockout; *avoid as primary work* (doesn't transfer to competition).
- **Rack jerks** — recommended for intermediate+ lifters developing competitive technique.
