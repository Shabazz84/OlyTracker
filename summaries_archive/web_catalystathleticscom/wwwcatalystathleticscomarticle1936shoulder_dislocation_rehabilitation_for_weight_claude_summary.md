# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1936/Shoulder-Dislocation-Rehabilitation-for-Weightlifting-Part-2/

**Not about weightlifting programming or technique for this athlete.**

This is a **shoulder dislocation surgery recovery memoir** (labrum + rotator cuff repair). While it documents rehabilitation exercises post-op, it contains no programming principles, technique cues, or training guidance applicable to your intermediate athlete's 6-week hypertrophy block.

**Minimal relevance to athlete profile:**
- Chronic back pain → article doesn't address it
- Night shift recovery → not discussed
- Snatch/OHS limiter (shoulder mobility) → article is post-catastrophic injury, not preventive mobility work

**One tangentially useful point:** Everett notes front squats with cross-arm grip and strap-assist tolerated better than back squat during shoulder rehab, which could inform back pain management—but this is incidental, not prescriptive for your athlete.
