# Analysis: Meet Week Training by Matt Foreman

**Relevant to Athlete's Profile:**

1. **Programming Principles**
   - Meet week = taper week (7 days pre-competition)
   - "Nothing you can do to make yourself stronger" in final 7 days—risk overtraining
   - Light taper after hard training blocks restores readiness
   - Sample plan: Mon (3×80%), Wed (5×60%), rest/light bar work other days
   - *Relevant*: After your 6-week hypertrophy block, this structure applies when transitioning to a competition phase

2. **Key Principle for Your Context**
   - Don't attempt PRs 4–5 days before competition
   - Opener selection: use weights consistently hit with zero misses in training (not recent PRs)
   - Example: If PR = 80 kg, 1st attempt = 75–77 kg

3. **Recovery/Mindset**
   - You should feel "tired but not annihilated" pre-taper
   - Proper taper creates barbell feeling "too light" in warmups—psychological advantage

**Not directly addressed:**
- Your chronic back pain management
- Night shift recovery strategies
- OHS limiter (50 kg) specific programming
- Split jerk transition

**Bottom line:** This is competition prep strategy; apply after your hypertrophy block when you enter strength/technique phases before a meet.
