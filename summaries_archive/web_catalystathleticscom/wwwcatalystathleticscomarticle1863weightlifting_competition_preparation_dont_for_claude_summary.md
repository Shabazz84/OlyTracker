### 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Rest periods (meet prep):** Reduce to ≤2 min in final weeks before competition (conditions physical/mental adaptation)
- **Rest between lifts:** Practice 10-min rest between snatch and clean & jerk on heavy days to simulate meet environment
- **Warm-up structure:** Establish fixed, practiced progression; never change approach day-of

### 2. **Technique Cues**
- None specific to snatch, clean, or jerk technique provided

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Use competition collars in training (changes bar feel/sound)
- Load bar exactly as it appears in competition (red plates, etc.)
- Practice on different platforms, facing different directions
- Lift on different bars between sets
- Train in hot environments (simulate warm-up room conditions)

### 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Plan warm-up timing based on lifting time-of-day (afternoon lifters may need extra mobility for early-morning meets)
- Manage caffeine consistently (type, timing, dose)—don't experiment meet-day
- Avoid new foods on meet day
