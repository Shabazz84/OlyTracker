# Analysis: "When & How to Think for Weightlifting Technique Correction" by Greg Everett

## Relevant to This Athlete

**1. Technique Cues & Mental Framework**
- Maximum 3 thought items per lift (one per phase: setup, motion, finalization)
- Keep cues to single words or brief phrases (e.g., "flat feet," "locking back arch")
- Avoid overthinking *during* execution; reserve detailed analysis for between sets or post-session
- Directly applicable to athlete's OHS limiter (50 kg) and snatch technique work

**2. When to Think**
- **Between sets**: Analyze previous rep feedback from coach/feel
- **After training**: Journal observations while sensations are fresh
- **Before training**: Review notes, plan corrections for session
- **NOT during lift**: Minimize conscious thought to prevent failure

**3. Recovery/Confidence Angle**
- Address self-doubt (relevant for night shift worker managing fatigue & chronic back pain)
- Build confidence through disciplined mental practice, not overtinking

## Not Directly Addressed
- Programming structure (no sets/reps/periodization)
- Back pain management strategies
- Night shift considerations
- Specific split jerk progression

**Recommendation**: Use this framework for snatch/OHS technique refinement during 6-week hypertrophy block—limit cues to 1–2 items per session targeting overhead stability/mobility limitations.
