## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile:

**1. Psychological/Mindset (Most Relevant)**
- **Over-analysis trap:** Intermediate lifters commonly nitpick their technique despite performing lifts correctly. This creates unnecessary anxiety and undermines confidence.
- **Action:** You likely benefit from *accepting good reps* rather than constantly diagnosing minor flaws. Your OHS weakness is clear; focus coaching attention there, not on every snatch detail.

**2. Coach-Athlete Dynamic**
- Effective coaching requires **knowing when to give feedback vs. when to stay silent** and build self-reliance.
- Coaches should affirm correct movement, not criticize everything.
- *Implication for your night-shift schedule:* If working with a coach, establish clear feedback protocols so coaching cues don't create decision fatigue during your limited training windows.
