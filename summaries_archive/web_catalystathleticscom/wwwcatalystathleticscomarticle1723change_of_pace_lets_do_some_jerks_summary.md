# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1723/Change-of-Pace-Lets-Do-Some-Jerks/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Focus:** Prioritize leg drive (dip-and-drive) over upper body pressing strength.
*   **Structure:** Use "Change of Pace" variability; do not rely solely on high-intensity max attempts for jerks.
*   **Progression:** Establish a consistent, solid "medium-split" position first before attempting to deepen the split or increase load.

**2. Technique Cues (Jerk Focus)**
*   **Core Concept:** Jerking is a leg lift; arms only extend/lock overhead.
*   **Dip Phase:** Dip butt between heels; take a "big air" breath before dipping.
*   **Hands:** Relax hands completely before initiating the dip.
*   **Drive/Lockout:** Punch bar directly over ears with a big chest; drive bar higher rather than splitting deeper (avoid deep lunge positions).

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Drill:** Behind-the-Neck Jerks (BTN) – recommended for beginners/intermediates to facilitate overhead lockout mechanics and reduce shoulder strain during learning phases.
*   **Competition Specifics:** Practice rack jerks from the front of the neck for competition readiness, but use BTN as a teaching tool if front-rack pain exists.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Shoulder Health:** If collarbone bruising occurs in the front rack position (common with improper elbow turn), switch to Behind-the-Neck Jerks temporarily to allow healing while fixing the underlying technical fault.
*   **Mindset:** Distinguish between "knowing" technique and "applying" it; focus on execution over theory.

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance (102.5 kg, Push Jerk Only, Back Pain)**
*   **Critical Warning:** The text explicitly advises against relying on upper body strength to "muscle" the bar, as this leads to shoddy technique. For an athlete with chronic back pain, this is vital: if you lack leg drive, your back will compensate during the jerk.
*   **Split Jerk vs. Push Jerk:** The text argues for a "medium-split" over a deep split or push jerk for elite efficiency. However, since your profile specifies **Push Jerk only**, you must apply the "leg lift" principle strictly: maximize dip-drive height to compensate for not using leg drive from a split catch.
*   **Grip Width:** Ensure your jerk grip allows you to rack cleans comfortably; do not force a wide grip if it compromises the clean or causes shoulder/back strain.
*   **Back Pain Management:** If front-rack pain (collarbone/shoulder) is present, utilize Behind-the-Neck Jerks as a temporary technical fix, but prioritize correcting elbow position to prevent recurrence.
