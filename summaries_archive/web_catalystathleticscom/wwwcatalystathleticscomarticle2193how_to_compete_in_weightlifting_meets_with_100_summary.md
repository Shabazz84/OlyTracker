# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2193/How-To-Compete-in-Weightlifting-Meets-With-1000-Lifters/

This text is **not** about programming, technique, or recovery for an intermediate athlete; it is a guide on **mental resilience and competition etiquette** for handling chaotic environments at large weightlifting meets. It contains no specific advice regarding hypertrophy blocks, split jerk mechanics, back pain management, or shift work scheduling.
