**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 5 days/week is the standard recommendation for this level; avoid 3-day blocks (Mon-Wed) to prevent fatigue accumulation.
*   **Schedule Structure:** Preferred pattern: Monday (Big), Tuesday (Little), Wednesday (Big), Friday (Big), Saturday (Little). Alternative: Mon-Thu-Sat with specific intensity modulation.
*   **Intensity/Volume Modulation:** Strictly alternate "Big" days (heavy Snatch/C&J, Squats, Pulls; high systemic tax) with "Little" days (speed work, technique, overhead stability; lower systemic tax).
*   **Periodization Logic:** Heavy days should be followed by lighter recovery days. Do not schedule heavy lower-back accessory work on Little days if it compromises the subsequent Big day.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Big Days:** Heavy Snatch, Clean & Jerk, Pulls, Squats, and lower-back accessory work (e.g., SLDL).
*   **Little Days:** Speed variations, technique drills, Dip Power Snatches, Push Presses, Jerk Supports, Overhead Stability.
*   **Accessory Placement:** Bodybuilding or overhead stability work fits best on Little days; heavy lower back work belongs at the end of Big days.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Rest Days:** Essential for spreading training burden; avoid 3-day consecutive blocks (e.g., Mon-Wed) to prevent stiffness and deconditioning.
*   **Fatigue Management:** Schedule rest days after "double-whammy" weeks (two heavy days in a row).
*   **Timing:** Heavy lower back work should be completed early enough on Big days to allow recovery before the next session.
