# Analysis: "Cardio for Olympic Weightlifting?" by Greg Everett

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Don't do dedicated cardio for weightlifting performance.** It creates contradictory muscular adaptation demands and diverts finite recovery resources.
- **If adding conditioning for general health:** reduce weightlifting volume to maintain total training balance.
- **Methodology:** Use circuit-style work (20–60 sec per activity, rotating movements) to stress cardiovascular system without local muscular fatigue.
- **Avoid monostructural cardio** (continuous running/rowing/biking).

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Circuit conditioning:** Rotate between bike, rower, treadmill + accessory work (abs, bodybuilding accessories) with minimal rest.
- **Prioritize upper body/core work in circuits** to minimize leg fatigue (legs are primary in lifting).
- **Walking:** insufficient stimulus unless high intensity/terrain.
- **Don't use cardio for weight cuts** — achieve via nutrition instead.

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Contradictory stimulus (max strength + endurance) impairs recovery from sport-specific training.

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
**Highly relevant to this athlete:**
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun):** Already energy/recovery-constrained. Avoid dedicated cardio; if doing conditioning, use low-volume circuits during low-intensity recovery days.
- **Chronic back pain + OHS limiter (50 kg):** Prioritize upper-body/core circuits, minimize leg-dominant cardio (bike/run).
- **6-week hypertrophy block:** Cardio will interfere with muscle building. Focus nutrition on surplus instead.
