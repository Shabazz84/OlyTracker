# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/120/The-Bench-Press-Benefits-and-Risks/

**Relevant to this athlete's profile:**

1. **Programming principles**: Bench press used cyclically (6-12 weeks) for mass gain, not year-round. Fits the athlete's 6-week hypertrophy block goal.

2. **Exercise recommendations**:
   - **Prefer incline bench over flat bench** — easier on shoulders, better angle for overhead demands
   - Prioritize **push press, snatch push press, press** over bench press (more specific to Olympic lifting)
   - Pair with **horizontal/vertical pulling** and **vertical pressing** in equal/greater volume
   - **Shoulder pre-hab exercises** essential (athlete has chronic back pain; shoulder health critical for jerk development)

3. **Recovery/mobility advice**:
   - Stretch **after every set** during pressing blocks to prevent mobility loss
   - Bench pressing can reduce shoulder mobility without aggressive balancing work — critical given athlete's limited OHS (50 kg)
   - Essential for this athlete with split jerk deficit and chronic back pain

4. **Specific concerns for this athlete**:
   - Bench press is "rough on shoulders" — relevant given chronic back pain and limited OHS
   - Upper back/shoulder mobility is "critical for weightlifters" — athlete's OHS is primary snatch limiter
   - Risk of reduced shoulder mobility could worsen jerk position security

**Bottom line**: Bench press acceptable in 6-week hypertrophy block if incline variation, paired with pulling/mobility work, and shoulder pre-hab prioritized.
