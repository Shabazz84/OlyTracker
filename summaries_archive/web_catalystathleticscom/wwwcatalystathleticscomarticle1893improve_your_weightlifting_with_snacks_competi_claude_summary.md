# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1893/Improve-Your-Weightlifting-With-Snacks-Competition-in-the-Gy/

**Assessment: Not directly applicable to this athlete's needs.**

This article is about a motivational competition format ("snacks game") using subjective technique scoring (1-3 scale) to drive intensity and technique awareness during group training. While the underlying principle—competitive pressure improving focus—has merit, the content provides:

**Relevant principle:**
- Competitive/judgmental environment can sharpen technique execution and intensity

**Not relevant:**
- No programming guidance (sets/reps/periodization)
- No specific technique cues for snatch, clean, or jerk
- No exercise recommendations
- No recovery/mobility advice
- No consideration of chronic back pain, night-shift fatigue, or split jerk development

**For this athlete:** The snacks game *could* work in group settings to improve focus during the 6-week hypertrophy block, but only if:
1. Judging criteria emphasize proper positioning over speed (especially OHS depth/positioning to address the 50 kg snatch limiter)
2. Exercises don't aggravate chronic back pain
3. Night-shift recovery isn't further taxed by added psychological stress
