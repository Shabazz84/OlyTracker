# Analysis: Clean Turnover Article by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Exercise selection**: Use technical drills as standalone work OR at beginning of clean sessions
- **Volume**: 3-5 reps per set for technique work (prevents fatigue degradation)
- **Complexity**: Progress from simple (rack delivery) → compound (muscle clean + clean complexes)
- **Intensity**: Keep technique exercises light (empty bar to moderate weight) to avoid reverting to bad habits

## 2. **Technique Cues - Clean Turnover (HIGHLY RELEVANT)**
**Key principles to make clean resemble front squat:**
- **Elbows**: Turn out early, keep out; bend elbows move UP/OUT (not back) during pull-under
- **Bar proximity**: Violent aggressive pull under → bar/shoulders become axis for elbow pivot
- **Shoulder blade retraction**: Row bar in as elbows come around (prevents forward bar position)
- **Rack position**: Receive with open/partial grip, shoulders push UP into bar (no crashing)
- **Timing**: Complete turnover as HIGH/EARLY as possible to allow eccentric squat component

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Rack Delivery**: Scarecrow position → turnover to rack (3-5 reps, light weight)
- **Muscle Clean**: Focus on upper body turnover precision (3-5 reps)
- **Tall Clean**: Emphasize pull-under speed, high rack position, immediate tightness
- **Block/Hang Clean**: Develop explosiveness, faster turnover
- **Complexes**: Muscle clean + clean OR Power clean + clean (progressively emphasize clean portion)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Smooth bar delivery prevents excessive downward force and instability
- Adequate eccentric squat component (higher rack position) aids tension development and stretch reflex for recovery
- Flexible shoulder mobility enables closed-grip racking (optional if causing slow turnover)

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Application**
**HIGHLY RELEVANT given athlete profile:**
- **Clean 80 kg / OHS 50 kg limiter**: Poor turnover likely contributes to weak rack position stability
- **Back pain + night shift fatigue**: Smooth, efficient turnover (vs. crashing bar) = less spinal loading
- **Push jerk only (no split)**: Improving clean turnover → better rack position stability → transfers to stronger jerk base
- **6-week hypertrophy block ahead**: Start with 1-2 turnover drills weekly now; scale up after block
- **Recommendation**: Prioritize tall cleans, muscle cleans, and power clean + clean complexes to lock in mechanics before strength work
