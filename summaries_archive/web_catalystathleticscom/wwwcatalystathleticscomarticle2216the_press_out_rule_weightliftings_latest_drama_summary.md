# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2216/The-Press-out-Rule-Weightliftings-Latest-Drama-Generator/

This text is an editorial discussing officiating rules regarding elbow lockouts (press-outs) in Olympic weightlifting competitions; it does **not** contain specific programming principles, technique cues for lifts, exercise recommendations, or recovery advice relevant to the athlete's profile.

**Summary of Relevance:**
The only applicable insight is a procedural rule: Athletes with anatomical limitations preventing full lockout (e.g., due to chronic back pain affecting posture or age-related joint issues) must **show their compromised position to referees before the lift starts**. This allows officials to judge based on that specific, legal deviation rather than penalizing it as a press-out.
