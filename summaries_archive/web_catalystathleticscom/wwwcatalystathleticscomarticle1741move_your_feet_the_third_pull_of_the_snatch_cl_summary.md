1. **Programming Principles**: Not discussed; text focuses exclusively on technical execution cues rather than sets, reps, intensity, or periodization structures.
2. **Technique Cues**: 
   - **"Lift your feet" actively** to land flat and directly under the bar (avoid landing only on balls).
   - **Think "lift knees"** rather than kicking feet back ("donkey kick") during the turnover.
   - **Sit hips straight down** with a vertical torso; avoid pulling hips back post-extension.
   - Aim for a **"jump-and-stomp"**: moderate elevation to guarantee zero resistance against downward movement, avoiding excessive air time (12 inches).
3. **Exercise Recommendations**: None specific; general advice includes mobility work (MobilityWod) and massage therapy if elbow lockout is limited by soft tissue or joint issues.
4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**: Mentions that poor elbow lockout often stems from shoulder girdle, thoracic spine, ankle, or hip inflexibility rather than biceps; suggests dedicated mobility work to address these areas.
5. **Athlete Profile Relevance**: 
   - **Highly relevant for "Push Jerk Only" limitation**: The text emphasizes landing flat and stable under the bar, which is critical for a push jerk athlete who lacks the split mechanics to absorb force dynamically.
   - **Back Pain Warning**: The cue to "sit hips straight down with vertical torso" directly addresses chronic back pain by reducing shear forces compared to pulling hips back or over-rotating.
   - **Night Shift/Recovery**: While not explicitly mentioned, the emphasis on precise, low-air-time footwork reduces systemic fatigue and injury risk for a shift worker needing efficient recovery windows.
