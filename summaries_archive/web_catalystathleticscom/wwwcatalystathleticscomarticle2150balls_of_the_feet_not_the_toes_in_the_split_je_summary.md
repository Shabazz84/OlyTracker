2. **Technique cues**: Keep back foot on the balls of the feet with heels raised; do not rise onto toes to prevent instability/spinning. Maintain an upright trunk during the split rather than diving head/chest forward under the bar. Focus on lifting/reaching the front foot to naturally position the back foot.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: Split jerk drills focusing on receiving position stability and upright torso mechanics.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None provided in this text.
5. **Profile relevance**: The "upright trunk" cue is critical for an athlete with chronic back pain to avoid excessive forward flexion under the bar.
