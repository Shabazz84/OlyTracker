# Analysis for Intermediate OLift Athlete (102.5kg, Clean 80kg, OHS 50kg)

## Technique Cue for Snatch (Highly Relevant)

**"Spread the Bar" Correction:**
- **Problem:** Tight grip overhead slows elbow extension and limits lockout
- **Better cue:** Push palms *away from elbows* through open hands, not pulling
- **Application:** Helps with hand flip and bar punch during receiving position
- **Direct relevance:** This athlete's OHS (50kg) is a primary snatch limiter—loose grip + scap activation will improve overhead stability and control

## Recovery/Mobility Advice (Relevant to Profile)

- **Related resources mentioned:** "Flexibility for the Overhead Squat" and "Overhead Stability in the Snatch" articles
- Scapular activation + loose grip = better shoulder health for chronic back pain management (reduces compensation patterns)
