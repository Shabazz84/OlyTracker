# Analysis: "Fix the Hips Shooting Up in the Snatch & Clean" — Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Position-specific strength emphasis**: Reduce loads if needed to reinforce correct positions—strength gains in wrong positions won't transfer to competition lifts.
- **Exercise selection**: Pulls, deadlifts, pause squats, floating pulls/deadlifts, pulls from risers.
- **Tempo work**: Pauses (post-separation, at knee) and slow eccentrics to reinforce proper posture.

## 2. **Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean**
- **First pull**: Maintain constant back angle from start (bar separation) to knee.
- **Hip rise problem**: Hips should NOT shoot up disproportionately to bar/shoulder rise; indicates weak legs or positional breakdown.
- **Cue**: "Push legs through the floor to drive chest up" (squat-like mechanics).
- **Bar speed vs. hip speed**: Judge pull quality by **bar motion**, not hip velocity—hips rising fast with slow bar = problem.

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Pause pulls/deadlifts (esp. at knee and post-separation)
- Pulls from risers (smaller knee angles)
- Pause back squats (small knee angle strength)
- Floating pulls/deadlifts with controlled descent
