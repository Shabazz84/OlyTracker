# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2153/There-Are-Different-Levels-of-Old-Masters-Weightlifting-Prog/

**RELEVANT CONTENT FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**Programming Principles:**
- Older/mature athletes need LESS volume, frequency, and average intensity than young lifters
- Start conservatively and build up rather than overloading initially
- Avoid chronic fatigue that prevents accurate assessment of weekly progress
- Individual variation is huge—experimentation needed to find what works for YOUR body

**Recovery/Mobility Advice:**
- Shoulder/rotator cuff work mentioned as alternative training (relevant given your back pain)
- Squatting variations, unilateral leg work, good mornings, trunk work, jumping listed as productive alternatives when upper body is limited
- Safety squat bar option noted for shoulder issues

**Relevance to Your Profile:**
- Night shift work (Wed–Sun) = irregular recovery patterns → conservative volume approach is prudent
- Chronic back pain + intermediate level = avoid high-frequency beating; quality over quantity
- 6-week hypertrophy block ahead = start moderate, assess weekly response without accumulated fatigue
- Push jerk limitation + low OHS (50kg) suggest trunk/mobility constraints—trunk work emphasis aligns with article's recommendations

**Key Takeaway:** Don't maximize training stress from day one. Build progressively to identify what YOUR body tolerates and progresses with—especially critical given shift work and back issues.
