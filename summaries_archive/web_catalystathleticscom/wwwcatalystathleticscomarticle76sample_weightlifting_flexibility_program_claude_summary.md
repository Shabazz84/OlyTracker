# Analysis: Greg Everett's Sample Weightlifting Flexibility Program

## Relevant to This Athlete

**1. Recovery & Mobility Advice (PRIMARY RELEVANCE)**
- Daily flexibility program prescribed for lifters with mobility limitations
- **Foam Roll:** T-spine, glutes, adductors/hamstring origin, quads
- **Stretches (2 min each):** Door jamb pec stretch, bar hang, Spiderman hold, squatting ankle stretch, OHS x30
- **Activation:** Superman hold (10 sec), mini-band squat (x15)
- **Key insight:** Consistency matters more than volume; static stretching acceptable when mobility is the primary limiter (applies here—OHS 50 kg is stated limiter)
- **Timing:** Can be done daily before/after training or whenever; post-training preferred while warm

**2. Exercise Recommendations**
- **OHS x30** (unloaded) as mobility drill—directly addresses his 50 kg OHS weakness
- Bar hang (accumulated 2 min) for shoulder/thoracic mobility
- Mini-band squat for glute/knee activation

**3. Contextual Fit**
- ✅ Addresses his primary snatch limiter (OHS/overhead mobility)
- ✅ Simple, non-excessive volume (feasible for night shift worker)
- ✅ Includes glute/lower back work (relevant to chronic back pain management)
- ⚠️ No specific cues for back pain; may need additional assessment

**NOT covered:** Programming principles, technique cues, periodization details for his 6-week hypertrophy block
