# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1923/How-To-Be-A-Better-Competition-Weightlifter/

This text is **not** a technical coaching guide for programming or biomechanics; it is a mental preparation article focused on competition psychology and etiquette. Consequently, there are no specific sets/reps, technique cues, exercise recommendations, or recovery protocols relevant to your hypertrophy block, chronic back pain, or night shift schedule in this text.

The only applicable advice for your profile is:
*   **Competition Mindset:** Treat the meet as "just another day at the office" (relaxation) and do not let the pressure of a first high-level meet cause you to open with weights above your consistent training maxes ("plan with your brain, not tattoos").
