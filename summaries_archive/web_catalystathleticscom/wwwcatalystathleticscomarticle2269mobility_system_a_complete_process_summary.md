# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2269/Mobility-System-A-Complete-Process/

1. **Programming principles**: Not discussed (text focuses exclusively on mobility systems).
2. **Technique cues**: None provided; text emphasizes "Full ROM Training" to improve stability and secure range of motion during lifts.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: 
   - *Morning*: Arm circles, leg swings, trunk rotations.
   - *Pre-Training*: Foam rolling/ball work on problem areas, dynamic flow sequences, preparatory movements.
   - *Intra-Workout*: Static stretches for priority limiters (avoid stretching muscles currently being used).
   - *Post/Bedtime*: Slow flow work followed by static-passive holds covering major points and specific problem areas; light foam rolling.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: Advocates a "complete system" rather than single cures. Emphasizes reducing general stress, using soft tissue work pre-training, stretching while warm post-training, and maintaining loose tension before sleep to aid recovery from night shifts.
5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: 
   - *Chronic Back Pain*: The text warns against "fixating on a single cure" and suggests addressing weak muscles (Weak Muscle Strength Work) as a cause of tightness/pain, which is critical for back pain management.
   - *Night Shift Worker*: Specifically recommends morning dynamic mobility to "neurologically reset muscle tension/length after a night of short muscles," directly addressing the stiffness caused by sleep cycles and lack of movement during shifts.
   - *OHS Limiter*: Suggests using post-training static stretching for shoulders (a common limiter) when warm to increase range safely.
