# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/34/Integrating-the-Olympic-Lifts-with-CrossFit/

## Key Points
- **Isolation before integration**: Learn Olympic lift technique separately before performing fatigued; critical for this athlete's foundation-building phase
- **Weakness-driven programming**: Athlete's OHS (50 kg) is primary snatch limiter—requires dedicated accessory work
- **Frequent, light technique work**: Can be performed daily without recovery interference; pairs well with mobility on off-days
- **Poor fatigued form embeds bad patterns**: Relearning is harder than learning correctly—prioritize technical consistency over volume
- **Recovery as hard constraint**: Night-shift fatigue + chronic back pain demand conservative loading and excellent recovery planning
- **Training interference matters**: Leg-heavy sessions before technique days degrade quality; plan sequencing carefully

## Technique Cues
- Legs/hips drive the bar; arm reliance = loss of power
- Technical consistency improves both safety and efficiency
- Poor fatigued technique reflects poor non-fatigued technique, not intensity
- Emphasize non-fatigued practice to establish correct motor patterns

## Programming Principles
- Build 6-week hypertrophy block (base strength phase) before technique/strength emphasis
- Pair snatch accessories with OHS weakness: e.g., snatch push press (1) + overhead squats (3 per rep)
- Use snatch balance for technique/strength work
- Avoid high-rep barbell Olympic lifts in metabolic conditioning; substitute dumbbell or alternative modalities
- Schedule light technique work on recovery/off-days alongside mobility
- Manage training interference: avoid leg-heavy sessions before Clean/Snatch days

## Athlete Relevance
- **OHS deficit (50 kg)**: Directly limits snatch potential—prioritize overhead position and bottom-squat strength
- **No split jerk**: Overhead position work carries over; consider split jerk introduction post-hypertrophy block
- **Night shift + chronic back pain**: Conservative loading, technical precision, and recovery prioritization are non-negotiable
- **Intermediate baseline**: Ready for structured accessory work; hypertrophy block appropriate before technique refinement
