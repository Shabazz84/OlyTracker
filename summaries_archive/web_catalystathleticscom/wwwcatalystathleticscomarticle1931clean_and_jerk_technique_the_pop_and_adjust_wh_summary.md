**2. Technique cues for snatch, clean, or jerk**
*   **Pop-and-Adjust (Clean & Jerk):** Use only if front rack flexibility prevents keeping a closed fist during the clean catch.
    *   *Mechanism:* At the top of the standing phase of the clean, add a slight leg drive to pop the bar off the shoulders.
    *   *Action:* While the bar is in mid-air (bottom/middle of neck), re-grip into a closed fist or widen the hand placement before standing motionless for the jerk.
*   **Fingertip Jerk:** An advanced variation where the athlete stands with the bar on fingertips, drives it up with legs, and closes the grip mid-drive (requires high skill/kinesthetic awareness).
