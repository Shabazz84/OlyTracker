# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1974/Shoulder-Dislocation-Rehabilitation-for-Weightlifting-Part-3/

**Article Focus:** Post-operative shoulder dislocation rehabilitation for a competitive weightlifter (Greg Everett's personal case study).

---

## **Relevant to This Athlete's Profile:**

### **1. Mobility & Shoulder Limitations**
- **Key principle:** Aggressive daily mobility work (minimum 1x daily, ideally 2-3x) is critical for overhead positions
- **Most effective tools:** Hanging from pull-up bar (pronated/supinated), sleeper stretch, Apley stretch, foam roller flexion work, softball underarm rolling
- **Relevant:** Athlete's 50 kg OHS (primary snatch limiter) suggests shoulder mobility/stability deficits—mirror Everett's early limitations

### **2. Strength-Before-Skill Approach**
- Mobilty improved fastest once strengthening work increased (push-ups → bench → loaded movement)
- **Daily shoulder/scapular stability work:** Band internal/external rotation, scapular retraction/depression, 30° abduction (supraspinatus), rows, standing Ys, protraction
- Build foundation before returning to complex lifts

### **3. Conservative Lifting Progression**
- Everett's timeline: ~6 months post-op before attempting snatches; cleans lagged behind due to turnover demands
- **Workarounds before full capacity:** Muscle snatches, muscle cleans, hang variations, pull work, drop-to-split training (no full jerks)
- Avoid high-velocity transitions early

### **4. Programming Structure (Relevant)**
- **Frequency:** 3 days/week sustainable with work constraints
- **Structure example:** Squat + pressing + pulling strength / Squat + pull variations / Squat + good morning + clean pull + shoulder/arm work
- **Daily extras:** Back extensions, GHD raises, weighted ab work
- Fits night-shift athlete's recovery needs

### **5. Back Pain Context**
- Article emphasizes daily back/ab work (weighted back extensions, GHD, heavy/light ab exercises)—applicable to chronic back pain management
