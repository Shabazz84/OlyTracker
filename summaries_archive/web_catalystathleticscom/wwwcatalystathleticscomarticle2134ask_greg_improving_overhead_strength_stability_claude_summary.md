# Analysis: Greg Everett on Overhead Strength & Stability

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**Critical Issue:** Your OHS is 50 kg (snatch limiter) while back squat is 118 kg—this mirrors the diagnostic problem discussed. Everett's framework directly applies.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency:** 2 days/week significant overhead strength work minimum (preparation phase)
- **Approach:** Regular exposure over time; gradual strength progression alongside squats
- **Technique integration:** Add 3–5 sec static holds to *all* overhead lifts (snatch, OHS, jerk, push press, snatch push press) without extra programming time

---

## 2. **Technique Cues (Overhead Position)**
- **Shoulder blades:** Retracted + upwardly rotated; forcefully squeeze upper inside edges together
- **Humeral rotation:** Elbows ~neutral (snatch); slightly more abducted (jerk)
- **Hand/wrist:** Bar in palm slightly behind forearm center; heel of palm facing up; grip only tight enough for control
- **Trunk:** Slight forward inclination to place bar over back of neck; maintain balance

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Priority 1:** Jerk supports & jerk recoveries (overhead support strength)
- **Priority 2:** Push press, snatch push press (pressing strength)
- **Add-on:** Extended static holds in bottom/locked-out positions

---

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Diagnose mobility bottleneck *first*—if poor mobility is the limiter, mobility work is prerequisite; if mobility is adequate, focus shifts to strength/stability/position
- Not explicitly covered for chronic back pain

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **Your OHS (50 kg) is the snatch limiter** → Everett prioritizes diagnosing the source. Likely candidates: poor overhead position mechanics, inadequate support strength, or mobility. Static holds + jerk supports/recoveries directly address this.
- **Push jerk only** → Snatch push press (hybrid, not strict) is recommended; split jerk development not discussed here but overhead position principles transfer.
