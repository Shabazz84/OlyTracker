**2. Technique Cues (Snatch Overhead)**
*   **Elbows:** Lock out completely into slight hyperextension ("bone lock") rather than stopping short; squeeze elbows directly into extension. Avoid "pulling the bar apart" as it tightens the grip and hinders elbow extension.
*   **Scapulae:** Pinch the top inside edges of shoulder blades together (retraction) to create a solid foundation, allowing upward rotation for arm rise. Do not shrug.
*   **Head/Neck:** Push head forward through the arms; avoid looking straight up or tucking chin back (prevents proper scapular position). Caution: do not push too far forward or chest will lean excessively.
*   **Hands/Wrists:** Bar sits slightly behind centerline of forearm in palm; allow wrist extension (do not force neutral grip) to let the hand settle. Grip must be relaxed overhead to maintain this position.
*   **Grip Width:** Balance is key; too wide causes over-rotation and drops bar behind, making elbow extension harder.
