# Analysis: Greg Everett on Late-Start Weightlifting & Plateauing

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**Programming Principles:**
- Reduce training volume 2-3 weeks, then gradually ramp back up to find sustainable stimulus level
- Lower volume/frequency capacity than younger lifters due to biological age
- Focus on core lifts (snatch, clean, jerk, squats, pulls) — avoid accessory complexity
- Match training program to training age (not just biological age)

**Recovery & Lifestyle (Critical for this athlete):**
- Allocate larger portion of training time to: foam rolling, stretching, contrast hydrotherapy, massage
- Prioritize sleep, nutrition, preventative measures
- Accept that durability/volume capacity is lower than early-starters — this is biological reality

**Mindset:**
- Distinguish between normal training discomfort/stalling vs. signs of overtraining
- "Beating up" feeling suggests program mismatch, not age ceiling

## Direct Relevance
Your athlete fits the late-start profile: intermediate with 2-3 years competitive experience, chronic back pain, shift work (sleep compromised). The prescription to reduce volume, emphasize core lifts, and heavily invest in recovery aligns with managing back pain + night shift fatigue. The 6-week hypertrophy block should be carefully dosed to avoid exacerbating his durability issues.
