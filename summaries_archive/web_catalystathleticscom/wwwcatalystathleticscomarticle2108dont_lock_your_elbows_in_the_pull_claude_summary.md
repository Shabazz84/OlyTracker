# Analysis: "Don't Lock Your Elbows in the Pull" by Greg Everett

## Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean Pull)

**Primary Issue:**
- Lifters locking elbows in extension during pull → forces bar forward around shoulder joint
- Arm bend comes too late (after bar already drifting)

**Correction:**
- Arms should be **passive/straight** — achieved by NOT bending them, not by active extension
- Extension happens as byproduct of maintaining position, not muscular effort
- Allows earlier arm bend during transition under the bar

---

## Relevance to This Athlete

**High Priority:**
- OHS 50 kg is primary snatch limiter; locked elbows likely contributes to forward bar path
- Elbow lock creates positional instability during catch → aggravates chronic back pain
- Fix: passive arm position improves positioning mechanics, reduces compensatory stress

**Application:**
- Cue during snatch/clean pulls: "Hold the bar, don't extend"
- Film early pulls to identify elbow locking pattern
- This may unlock OHS progress (catch stability → confidence to load deeper)
