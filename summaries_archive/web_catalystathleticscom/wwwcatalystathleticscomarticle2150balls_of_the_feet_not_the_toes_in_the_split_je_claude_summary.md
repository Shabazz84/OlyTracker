**WEIGHTLIFTING TECHNIQUE ARTICLE - SPLIT JERK FOOTWORK**

---

**1. TECHNIQUE CUES FOR JERK:**
- **Back foot positioning:** Balls of foot flat/full contact with floor (not on toes)
- Back heel raised, but weight distributed evenly between feet
- Keep back foot close to platform during split (minimal lift)
- Maintain upright trunk—avoid diving head/chest forward under bar
- Cue: "Lift and reach the front foot; back foot will follow"
- **Why:** Toe-heavy positioning = unstable, allows foot spinning/slipping, prevents adequate weight support on back foot
