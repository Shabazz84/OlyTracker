# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2265/Muscle-Snatch-The-How-is-in-the-Why/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Sets/Reps:** Not explicitly defined in this text; the focus is on strict execution rather than volume schemes.
*   **Intensity:** High intensity of effort regarding bar speed; the exercise fails completely if momentum is insufficient.
*   **Frequency:** Implied high frequency for skill acquisition ("train or test"), but specific weekly frequency is not stated.
*   **Periodization:** Used as a diagnostic tool to verify bar speed and turnover mechanics rather than a primary hypertrophy or strength builder.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Elbow Position:** Maintain elevated elbows throughout the turnover; do not allow them to drop.
*   **Back Angle:** Keep the back extended through the turnover phase (do not round).
*   **Turnover Motion:** Force an external rotation of the arms rather than allowing the elbows to "flip under and in front" of the bar to press it up.
*   **Speed Dependency:** The lift must rely on existing bar speed; if momentum is inadequate, the movement should fail or be modified into a press (indicating poor speed).

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Tool:** Muscle Snatch (performed with strict "Catalyst Athletics" execution standards).
*   **Alternative Speed Checks:** Touching the bar to a pipe set at a specific height or using a landmark on the body (e.g., sternum) to objectively measure elevation and speed.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   None provided in this text segment.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
*   **Critical for Snatch Limiter:** Directly addresses the athlete's 50kg OHS limitation by forcing reliance on bar speed rather than upper body pressing strength, which is crucial given their "push jerk only" background (no split jerk).
*   **Back Pain Consideration:** The strict cue to maintain an extended back through turnover may aggravate chronic back pain if the athlete lacks current mobility or stability; requires careful monitoring.
*   **Skill Transfer:** Helps transition from a powerlifting mindset (strength-focused) to Olympic lifting by emphasizing the "turnover" motor pattern, which is often neglected in intermediate athletes.
