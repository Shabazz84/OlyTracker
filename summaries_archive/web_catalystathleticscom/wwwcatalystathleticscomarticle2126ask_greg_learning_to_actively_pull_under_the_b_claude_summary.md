# Analysis: "Learning to Actively Pull Under the Bar" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- No explicit sets/reps/intensity prescribed
- Progression: High-pulls → Muscle snatches → Tall snatches (technical primers before main snatch work)
- Tall snatches recommended as technique primer within workouts

## 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch-Focused)**
- **Third pull (actively pulling under):** Pull elbows **up and out**, retract shoulder blades
- Maintain **bar proximity to body** throughout
- **Muscle snatch:** Aggressive/quick punch **straight up** against bar (turnover phase)
- **Tall snatches:** Aggressive elbow pull accelerates descent; don't muscle the turnover
- Key error: Falling under bar passively with arms rather than *actively pulling*

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- High-pulls (timing & initial downward pull mechanics)
- Muscle snatches (extension of high-pull; turnover practice)
- Tall snatches (best used as technique primer before main snatch)
- *Emphasis: Execution quality critical for all three*

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
None discussed.

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
**HIGHLY RELEVANT:**
- OHS 50 kg (primary snatch limiter) → Weak turnover/positioning likely a factor
- **Tall snatches & muscle snatches should anchor snatch technique work** in 6-week hypertrophy block
- Low clean (80 kg) relative to squat suggests turnover/receiving position weakness
- No split jerk → May also have passive arm pull issue in jerks

**Recommendation:** Integrate high-pulls + muscle snatches + tall snatches early in sessions before fatigue.
