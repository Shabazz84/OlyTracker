# Analysis: "The Jerk Technique Nobody Talks About"

## Relevant to This Athlete

**CRITICAL: NOT APPLICABLE TO YOUR PROFILE**

This article discusses the **flex jerk** (elastic/oscillation-based jerk), which requires:
- Heavy loads to generate bar whip (≥120 kg on men's bar)
- Advanced proficiency and natural elasticity

**Your situation:**
- Currently push jerking at 65 kg (far below the threshold for bar oscillation to function)
- Intermediate phase, not yet ready for advanced technical adaptations
- Should master standard **dip-drive technique first**

---

## Key Takeaways for Your Coaching

1. **Jerk Priority: Master dip-drive fundamentals**
   - Depth: noticeable knee bend (several inches), not shallow bounces
   - Focus: aggressive leg drive upward with control and accuracy
   - Reference: Tatiana Kashirina's slow-motion C&J demonstrates proper depth

2. **Don't chase flex jerk yet**
   - Foreman/Everett agree: learn basics first
   - Elastic/flex jerks only work with sufficient load and are for exceptional athletes
   - Teaching standard dip-drive prevents bad habits that fail once weights increase

3. **For your split jerk transition** (when ready)
   - Build on solid dip-drive mechanics first
   - Shallow, reactive dip comes much later if at all
