# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1743/Athlete-Selection-for-Weightlifting/

This text discusses **talent identification and selection criteria for elite national weightlifting programs**, focusing on anatomical suitability (e.g., limb ratios, height/weight ranges) and testing protocols for children aged 11–14. It does **not** contain specific programming principles, technique cues, exercise recommendations, or recovery advice relevant to an intermediate adult athlete's training block.
