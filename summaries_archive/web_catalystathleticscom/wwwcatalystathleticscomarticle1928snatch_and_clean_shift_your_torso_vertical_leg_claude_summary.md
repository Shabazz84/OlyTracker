# Analysis for Your Profile

## Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)

**Hip Drive Cue—Directly Applicable:**
- **"Shift your torso vertical and get your legs under the bar"** → jump and shrug
- Torso vertical (waist to shoulders) after bar passes knees
- Thighs underneath bar near hip joint
- Then drive/extend (resembles a jump) + shoulder shrug

**Relevance:** Your Clean at 80 kg and OHS at 50 kg suggest hip drive weakness, not just arm/pulling power. This cue may help transfer leg strength to the bar.

## Critical for Your Situation

1. **Back pain + hip drive:** Vertical torso position emphasis might reduce lumbar strain vs. forward-lean pulls. Test cautiously.

2. **Snatch limiter (50 kg OHS):** Article notes hip drive separates elite from mediocre—strong hip drive in snatch pulls will demand stronger receiving position. OHS work remains primary block focus.

3. **Coaching method:** Article stresses hands-on coaching (manual body manipulation) works better than cues alone. Seek in-person coaching for hip drive—critical for your transition phase.
