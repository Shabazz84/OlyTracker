1. **Programming Principles:** The text does not specify sets, reps, intensity, frequency, or periodization structures; it focuses exclusively on the timing of the transition between the second and third pulls (overlap vs. separation).

2. **Technique Cues:** 
   - **"Push Through the Pull Under":** Maintain ground pressure with legs while initiating the pull under to preserve bar upward momentum.
   - **Continuous Motion:** Treat the second and third pulls as a single fluid action, not two distinct segments; never stop pulling the bar up.
   - **Forced Down:** Visualize trying to muscle the bar up (like a muscle snatch) but being forced down by the weight, rather than actively dropping under it.
   - **No Slack:** Ensure there is no moment of slack in the system between extension and turnover.

3. **Exercise Recommendations:** 
   - Snatch/Clean from Power Position (to isolate the drive-to-turnover transition).
   - Complexes: Snatch/Clean Pull + Snatch/Clean (to replicate extended pull feeling).
   - Tall Snatches/Cleans: Start flat-footed and push up onto toes as you begin pulling under.
