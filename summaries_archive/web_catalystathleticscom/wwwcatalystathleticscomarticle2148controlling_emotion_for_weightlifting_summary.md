# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2148/Controlling-Emotion-for-Weightlifting/

This text is a mental performance article focused on emotional regulation, not a technical or physiological training guide. It contains **no** specific programming principles (sets/reps/intensity), technique cues for lifts, exercise recommendations, or mobility advice relevant to the athlete's physical profile (back pain, night shift, hypertrophy goals).

While it emphasizes **sleep** as critical for emotional stability and suggests **meditation/visualization** for stress management, these are general psychological strategies. The text does not address the specific constraints of a night-shift worker, chronic back pain, or the transition from intermediate strength to Olympic lifting technique.
