**RELEVANT EXTRACTIONS:**

**Recovery & Mobility Advice:**
- **Tumbling warm-up routine** (5 min): dynamic mobility through full ROM (shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, wrists, elbows)
- Benefits: coordination, agility, balance, body control, injury prevention, fall mechanics
- Specific drills: forward/backward rolls, cartwheels, bear crawl, crab crawl, hopping progressions

**Why relevant to this athlete:**
1. **Chronic back pain** → tumbling teaches core "connectedness," dynamic spinal mobility, and safe falling patterns
2. **Night shift worker** → 5-min warm-up efficient for fatigue management
3. **OHS limiter (50 kg)** → cartwheel/inversion work addresses shoulder mobility and overhead positioning
4. **Hypertrophy block prep** → mobilizes joints pre-training to reduce injury risk during volume work

**Caution:** Back pain may restrict certain rolls (especially backward rolls/extensions). Modify or skip if contraindicated; consult coach before implementing full routine.
