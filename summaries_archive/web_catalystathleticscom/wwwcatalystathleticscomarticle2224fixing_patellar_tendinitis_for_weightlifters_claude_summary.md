# Weightlifting Analysis: Patellar Tendinitis Protocol

## Relevance to Athlete
**HIGH** – Directly applicable. Athlete has chronic back pain; knee health critical for squat-dependent program (BS 118kg, OHS only 50kg suggests quad/knee limitations).

---

## 1. **Recovery & Injury Management**

**Warming Up Protocol:**
- 2-3 min easy bike (low resistance)
- 10-20 foam roller passes on quads (midline emphasis)
- Dynamic warm-up: leg swings, knee circles, unloaded squats

**Patellar Tendinitis Prevention/Treatment:**
- **Stretching:** Rectus femoris + hip flexors before/after training AND between sets
- **Strengthening:** Leg extension negatives – 3×10, 5-6 sec eccentric, 3x/week (end of workout)
- **Accessory:** Hamstring curls 3×10-15, unilateral, 2-3x/week
- **Massage:** Cross-friction patellar tendon, 3-5 min daily, moderate pressure
- **Contrast therapy:** Hot/cold cycles to enhance blood flow (avoid ice alone)
- **Avoid NSAIDs** – impairs tendon healing

---

## 2. **Technique/Programming Cues**
None specific to snatch, clean, jerk in this text.

---

## 3. **Key Principle for This Athlete**
Identify cause (volume spike, shoe change, exercise addition). Reduce/eliminate offender, then reintroduce gradually post-healing. Prevents re-injury during 6-week hypertrophy block.

---

## 4. **Critical Note**
Chronic back pain + night shift work (Wed–Sun) = fatigue management crucial. Adequate warm-up (especially knee prep) non-negotiable given his low OHS (50kg) relative to BS (118kg).
