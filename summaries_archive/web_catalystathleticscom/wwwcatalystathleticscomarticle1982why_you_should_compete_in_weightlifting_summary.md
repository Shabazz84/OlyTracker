# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1982/Why-You-Should-Compete-in-Weightlifting/

This text is an opinion piece arguing for the psychological and competitive benefits of competing in weightlifting; **it contains no specific programming principles, technique cues, exercise recommendations, or recovery advice relevant to your profile.**

The article focuses on:
1.  **Motivation:** Using competition as a goal to drive training consistency and diet adherence.
2.  **Mental Toughness:** Exposing lifters to stress (crowds, travel, poor venues) to build resilience.
3.  **Experience:** The value of the "meet day" atmosphere regardless of medal success.

**Relevance to your profile:**
While the text confirms that competing can improve accountability and mental fortitude for an intermediate athlete, it offers **zero actionable data** regarding:
*   How to structure a 6-week hypertrophy block.
*   Specific cues for your OHS or push jerk limitations.
*   Strategies for managing chronic back pain during training cycles.
*   Recovery protocols for night shift workers (Wed–Sun).

To achieve your goals, you would need to apply the *mindset* of "peaking" described in this article to a technical program designed by a coach familiar with your specific injury history and sleep schedule.
