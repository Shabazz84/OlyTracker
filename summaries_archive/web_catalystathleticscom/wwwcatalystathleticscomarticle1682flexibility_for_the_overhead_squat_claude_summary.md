# Analysis for Intermediate OLer (102.5kg, OHS 50kg limiter)

## HIGHLY RELEVANT TO THIS ATHLETE

**1. Programming/Exercise Recommendations**
- **OHS frequency**: Practice overhead squat daily (even shallow/ugly reps) to build position and ROM
- **Back squat positioning**: Must mirror OHS mechanics (upright), not low-bar style
- **Accessory work**: Back squats & good mornings teach back extensor engagement, stretch hip extensors/adductors
- **3×15 wall-assisted OHS** (3ft from wall, morning/evening) = practical progression tool

**2. Mobility/Recovery (Critical for this athlete)**
- **Priority stretches**: Spiderman lunge, Russian baby maker, Kossack, hamstring variations, calf (bent-knee), thoracic spine
- **T-spine mobilization** (major focus): Half foam roller or towel under spine, work bottom to top of thoracic region—often the hidden OHS limiter
- **PNF protocol**: 30-45sec hold → 6×6sec contract/relax → 30-45sec hold
- **Daily routine**: Dynamic ROM morning (arm circles, leg swings) + foam roll + stretch throughout day
- **Ankle mobility**: Bent-knee calf stretch in squat position (pressing forearms on thighs)

**3. Technique Cues**
- Verify **stance width & grip width** first (poor setup = chronic limitation)
- Fight to achieve correct upright position, sit as deep as possible, then "reach further even if position slips"
- **Shoulder flexibility check**: If you can hold bar overhead standing with slight forward lean, shoulders are adequate—issue is squat depth, not shoulders
- **Avoid hyperlordosis**: Engage lower abs + upper back tension to prevent lumbar compensation

**4. Directly Applicable to Back Pain**
- Lower back tightness during OHS often signals **insufficient hip/hamstring/T-spine mobility**, not back weakness
- Proper back squat mechanics (upright, mirroring OHS) + core bracing will help
- T-spine mobility work is essential before loading OHS

**5. Periodization Note**
- Flexibility work should precede heavy loading—"scorched-earth" 2-3 week general mobility phase before focused OHS training

---

**Key takeaway**: This athlete's 50kg OHS is a mobility problem (especially T-spine + ankle + hip flexors), not strength. Daily practice + systematic stretching will unlock gains before the 6-week hypertrophy block.
