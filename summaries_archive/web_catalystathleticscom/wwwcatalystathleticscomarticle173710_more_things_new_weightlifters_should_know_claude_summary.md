# Analysis: "10 More Things New Weightlifters Should Know" - Mike Gray

**Relevance:** General foundational weightlifting advice. Limited direct application to this athlete's specific profile.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **80-80 Rule (Tip #13):** 80% volume at 80% intensity during deload weeks—applicable for structuring the 6-week hypertrophy block with planned backoffs
- **Block Work (Tip #8):** Emphasized for finding power position; relevant given athlete's weak overhead position (50 kg OHS)
- **Avoid Volume Creep (Tip #1):** "More isn't always better"

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Tip #12:** Keep logbook with mental cues ("Chest Up," "Pull & Punch")—useful for addressing chronic back pain and OHS limitations
- No specific snatch/clean/jerk cues provided

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Tip #3:** Conditional programming—if fatigued, prioritize power versions → pulls → reduce full lifts
  - *Relevant:* Night shift worker needs flexible fatigue management
- **Tip #8:** Block work (below/above knee, mid-thigh pulls) for power position development
  - *Relevant:* Addresses weak OHS; front squat/power position work needed

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- **Tip #9:** Immediate post-workout nutrition—emphasize timing over convenience
  - *Relevant:* Night shift schedule complicates nutrition timing
- **Tip #13:** Plan deloads proactively; 2–3 week cycles

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **Weak OHS (50 kg):** Block work emphasis aligns with need for position/strength work
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun):** Tip #3 conditional work fits fatigue variability
- **Chronic back pain:** No direct advice; logbook cues (Tip #12) valuable for movement quality tracking

---

**Key Takeaway:** General reference; apply 80-80 deload rule and conditional fatigue-based programming to your hypertrophy block.
