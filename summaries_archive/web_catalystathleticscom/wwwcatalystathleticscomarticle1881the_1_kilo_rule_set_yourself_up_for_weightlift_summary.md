**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Progression Strategy:** Prioritize small, frequent improvements (1 kg PRs) over infrequent large jumps to ensure constant measurable progress and maintain motivation.
*   **Attempt Planning:** Use decreasing increments as the lifter approaches a maximal weight (e.g., 4–5 kg jumps dropping to 2–3 kg near the top).
*   **Confidence Threshold:** Only attempt weights with >99% confidence; otherwise, stick to conservative, pre-planned sequences.
*   **Frequency:** Aim for success on every testing attempt rather than stalling through repeated failures of known weights.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**

*   **Testing Protocol:** Begin testing attempts at 1 kg above the current PR; if successful, immediately attempt higher weights. If confidence is low, stop after a successful PR rather than pushing for a miss.
