# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2029/Hand-Wrist-Position-Overhead-in-the-Snatch-Jerk/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Sets/Reps:** Use low-volume holds (e.g., 3–5 sets of 10-second holds) for overhead stability drills; do not exceed weights that force position breakdown.
*   **Intensity:** Keep overhead work sub-maximal to prioritize technical integrity over load.
*   **Frequency:** Practice correct hand/wrist positioning frequently during warm-ups and technique blocks.

**2. Technique Cues (Snatch & Jerk)**
*   **"Cradle the Bar":** Mimic a squat rack post; the bar must rest in the "crotch" of the hand (where palm meets fingers), not on the fingertips or behind the wrist.
*   **Wrist Extension:** Actively extend the wrist so the heel of the palm points somewhat toward the ceiling; avoid neutral wrists which cause instability and pain.
*   **Bar Placement:** Position the bar slightly *behind* the midline of the forearm for maximum stability (prevents forward/backward sway).
*   **Grip Tension:** Grip only as tightly as necessary to maintain control. "Choking" the bar creates elbow flexor tension that slows extension and causes instability.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Overhead Holds:** 10-second holds with proper hand/wrist positioning (no weight exceeding current stability limits).
*   **Jerk Supports:** Static overhead holds to build wrist strength without dynamic stress.
*   **Wrist Curls:** Targeted strengthening for the hands and wrists.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Ankle Mobility Check:** Address ankle immobility first; lack of lower body mobility forces compensatory wrist/shoulder pain.
*   **Pain Management:** If a neutral wrist position causes pain, immediately correct to extended wrist positioning (bony support vs. muscular strain).

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
*   **Hook Grip Release:** As an intermediate male lifter transitioning to Olympic lifting, you likely need to learn how to *release* the hook grip during the turnover to allow the hand/wrist to settle into the correct overhead position (unlike women or those with slender hands).
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The article notes that poor lower body mobility forces upper body compensation. Improving ankle/hip mobility is critical to reducing the strain on your back caused by compensatory overhead mechanics.
*   **Jerk Grip:** Do not maintain the hook grip during the jerk; release it once the bar is stabilized overhead.
