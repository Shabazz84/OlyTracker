4.  **Recovery/Mobility Advice:**
    *   **Knee Sleeves:** Avoid bulky neoprene sleeves (e.g., Rehband) as they may push the patella out of position; prefer thin, warm-only sleeves or nothing at all unless pain forces use.
    *   **Wrist Wraps:** Use only as a "last resort" for soreness that prevents standing the pain; avoid bulky wraps if you struggle to keep hands closed on the bar.
5.  **Relevant to Athlete Profile:**
    *   **Back Pain:** The text explicitly states: *"If you're relying on sleeves or wraps to help you stand up... then your legs aren't strong enough."* It advises against using gear as a crutch for pain; instead, build strength first.
    *   **Belts:** Use only for Clean & Jerks/Squats/Deadlifts above 80% intensity (approx. >95kg for you). Do not use for Snatch unless necessary to avoid bar buckle contact. Prefer a buckle belt over Velcro or thick powerlifting belts which ruin squat depth.
    *   **Straps:** Use only on assistance pulls (deadlifts/pulls); do not use on actual lifts unless hands are shredded, as they compromise turnover speed and wrist safety.
    *   **Newbie Rule:** As an intermediate transitioning athlete, the text advises starting with just shoes/shorts/shirt for as long as possible; using gear prematurely is "pitiful."
