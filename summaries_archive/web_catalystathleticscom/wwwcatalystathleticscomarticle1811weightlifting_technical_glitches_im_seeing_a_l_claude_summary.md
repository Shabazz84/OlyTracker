## 2. **Technique Cues: Jerk (Split Jerk)**

**Front Leg:**
- Front shin should be **vertical ("like a fencepost")** when locked overhead in split position
- **Problem:** Shin tilted forward → knee over toe → poor weight distribution
- **Cue:** "Longer step forward with front foot" or "Even weight front/back foot"

**Back Leg:**
- Heel **off the floor** with moderate knee bend (NOT planted flat)
- Back foot pointed **straight forward**
- Ensures even weight distribution
