# Analysis: "The Trouble with Pulls" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Pull loading**: Don't blindly follow 80–105% of snatch/clean max for intermediate+ lifters; newer/technically-challenged athletes need heavier pulls relative to standard percentages
- **Progressive approach**: Start conservatively, increment gradually until lifter moves properly at intended effort level
- **Cycle-dependent loading**: Early cycle = lighter, manageable pulls; late cycle = challenging pulls (still technically sound)
- **Avoid the trap**: Don't underload pulls just to stay within percentage ranges—limits strength development

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- Maintain smooth pull-off from ground with tension set against bar
- Keep chest up, shoulders over bar
- Push longer at top (full extension)
- Pull elbows upward to keep bar close
- Avoid getting pulled forward/over bar

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Use heavy pulls (exceed standard %) for lifters with strength surplus relative to technique
- Two-bar method for novices: heavy pull single/double → step to lighter bar for full lift (builds confidence, improves PR rates)
