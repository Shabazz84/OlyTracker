# Analysis: Greg Everett - Sample Bodybuilding Work for Weightlifters

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency**: 1x/week per movement group (upper push, upper pull, legs) = 3–4 days total
- **Sets/Reps**: 5x8–12 (main compounds); 3x10–15 (accessories); 3x15–30 (calves/isolation)
- **Philosophy**: Organize by *movement type* (vertical/horizontal push/pull), not body parts
- **Periodization**: "Beach work" variation—intentionally loose planning to maintain engagement

## 2. **Exercise Recommendations**
**Upper Body Push**: Bench, incline bench, dips, upright rows, lateral flyes, front raises  
**Upper Body Pull**: Pull-ups, chin-ups, bent rows, T-bar rows, cable rows, dumbbell rows  
**Legs**: Minimal additional work (weightlifting provides sufficient volume); Bulgarian split squats, lunges, step-ups once/week + SLDLs/good mornings  
**Arms**: Curls (barbell/dumbbell), wrist curls, tricep extensions, close-grip bench

## 3. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Individual exercise tolerance matters (avoid exercises causing chronic pain/injury—e.g., author avoids skullcrushers due to tendinitis)
- General principle: "Plenty of pulls" and posterior chain work assumed in main program

---

## **Relevance to This Athlete**

✅ **Highly relevant for 6-week hypertrophy block**:
- Upper body push/pull work addresses OHS deficit (50 kg is primary snatch limiter)
- Shoulder pressing, rows, and vertical work directly support overhead stability
- Can operate 3–4 days/week around night shift schedule

⚠️ **Chronic Back Pain Concern**:
- Avoid heavy bent rows, T-bar rows, and heavy SLDLs
- Prioritize: pull-ups, incline pressing, light good mornings, RDLs (controlled)
- Substitute cable rows or dumbbell rows for safer spinal loading

⚠️ **No Split Jerk**:
- Dip/close-grip bench work supports push jerk strength but won't directly address split jerk mechanics if that becomes future goal
