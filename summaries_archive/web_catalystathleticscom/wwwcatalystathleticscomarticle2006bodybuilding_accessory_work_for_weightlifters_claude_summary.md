# Analysis: Bodybuilding Accessory Work for Weightlifters

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Frequency**: 1 day/week per bodypart/movement type
- **Reps**: 8–15 range (higher reps, lower intensity)
- **Volume**: 3–5 sets of 1–3 exercises; 50–150 total reps/session
- **Rest**: 60–90 seconds (short periods for hypertrophy)
- **Timing**: Use during preparation phase (early cycle); eliminate 1–2 weeks before competition
- **Variation**: Change exercises frequently; vary rep ranges; progress weight over time
- **Priority**: Accessory work only—never compromise primary weightlifting programming

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Full Range of Motion** (critical): Avoid limited ROM used in bodybuilding; train complete lockouts for mobility, stability, and tendon strength
- **Concentric Speed**: Always move explosively/maximally fast on pressing and leg work; slow eccentrics acceptable

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Choose exercises that **reinforce weightlifting function**, not interfere with mobility
- **Avoid**: Exercises limiting joint lockouts or creating tendinitis risk
- **Schedule smartly**: Pair upper-body pressing beach work with jerk days; don't blast shoulders/triceps day before heavy snatch work (risk fatigue/injury)
- Example: Presses, rows, curls, triceps work

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- **Tendon strength**: Higher reps + lower weight strengthen tendons better than heavy single reps
- Full ROM prevents tendinitis; incomplete recovery aids hypertrophy stimulus

## 5. Relevant to This Athlete
✓ **6-week hypertrophy block aligns perfectly** with article's recommendation for early-cycle beach work  
✓ **Chronic back pain**: Avoid excessive volume that taxes recovery; use beach work strategically  
✓ **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Schedule pressing beach work on jerk days; avoid pre-snatch leg/shoulder blasts  
✓ **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Shoulder stability/mobility work could support snatch development  
✓ **No split jerk yet**: Upper-body pressing accessories support push jerk strength

**Key caveat**: Keep accessory work subordinate to snatch/clean/jerk progression.
