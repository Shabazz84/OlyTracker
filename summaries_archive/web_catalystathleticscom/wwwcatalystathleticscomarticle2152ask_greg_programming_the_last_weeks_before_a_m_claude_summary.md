# Analysis: Programming the Last Weeks Before a Meet

## 1. Programming Principles (Peaking Strategy)
- **Final 1-2 weeks**: Reduce fatigue while maintaining strength/speed/technique—*not* a time to improve
- **Heavy lift timing**: 
  - Advanced/heavier lifters: Last heavy C&J & squat at 2 weeks out
  - Intermediate/lighter: ~10 days out (relevant for this athlete)
  - Final snatch: 7-10 days out
- **Squat**: 70-75% intensity, final heavy single ~2 weeks out
- **Pulls**: Heavy at 3 weeks out, then 90-100% doubles in week 2
- **Openers**: Hit ~1 week out before tapering

## 2. Taper Week Templates (Athlete-Dependent)
**For those needing high recovery** (potentially relevant—night shift worker):
- Mon: 3-5 snatch/C&J singles at 80%
- Wed/Thu: 5-6 reps at 60% only

**For intermediate recovery** (likely this athlete):
- Mon: Singles 85-90%, pulls & squats moderate
- Wed/Thu: Snatch/C&J singles 80-85%, pulls/squats 80-85%
- 1-2 other light days

## 3. Relevant to This Athlete
- **Night shift schedule (Wed–Sun)**: Plan peaking accordingly; recovery is critical
- **Chronic back pain**: Taper high-fatigue accumulation; lighter squat work (70-75%) suits this better
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Front squat emphasis during taper may help without excessive spinal load
- **Push jerk only**: No technical constraint in taper—volume reduction is primary focus

## Key Takeaway
Taper aggressively given back pain + shift work. Prioritize the "high-recovery" or "intermediate" model to minimize cumulative fatigue before peaking.
