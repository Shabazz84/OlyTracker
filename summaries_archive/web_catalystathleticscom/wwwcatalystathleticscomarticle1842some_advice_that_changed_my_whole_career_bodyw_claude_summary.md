# Analysis: Bodyweight in Weightlifting by Matt Foreman

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**1. Programming/Periodization Principle**
- Bodyweight gain as foundational priority before pursuing competition strength
- Foreman's case: 90 kg → 105 kg (+15 kg) in 2 years = proportional snatch/C&J gains (120→137.5 kg SN, 150→175 kg C&J)
- **Application:** Your 102.5 kg at 5'11" aligns with Foreman's optimal range. The 6-week hypertrophy block suits bodyweight accumulation before technique work.

**2. Practical Nutrition Advice**
- No obsessive macro tracking needed; "just ate more of everything"
- Simple foods: meat, pasta, milk, pizza
- Caveat: Foreman admits vegetables were neglected (suboptimal)
- **Note:** Back pain consideration—ensure adequate micronutrients during bulk

**3. Mindset/Priority**
- Competition success in OLifting requires bodyweight as *primary* goal when undertrained
- Fitness (conditioning) secondary to total strength
- Weight class advantage: staying heavier trumps cutting for lighter class

## NOT Directly Addressed
- Technique cues for snatch/C&J
- Recovery/mobility strategies
- Night shift considerations
- Chronic back pain management

**Key Takeaway for You:** Your OHS (50 kg) is your limiter; gaining 5–10 kg bodyweight + hypertrophy work should naturally improve overhead strength proportionally, supporting snatch progress.
