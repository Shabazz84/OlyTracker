# Analysis: Snatch Crashing from Floor (Greg Everett)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Complexes emphasized**: Power snatch + snatch, hang snatch + snatch
- **Rationale**: Build bar connection and appropriate receiving height progressively
- **Intensity**: Same weight across complex reps to standardize turnover position

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Core problem**: Dropping under bar rather than *actively pulling yourself under it* at the height achieved
- **Key cue**: "Stay connected to the bar throughout the third pull"—meet the bar at actual pull height, not predetermined low position
- **Power snatch + snatch**: Secure bar at same overhead height in both reps; snatch simply adds full squat depth without position change

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Power snatch + snatch complex** (primary for this athlete)
- **Hang snatch + snatch complex** (easier progression; builds bar connection before floor pull)

## 4. **Relevance to Athlete Profile**
⚠️ **High relevance**: This athlete has OHS 50 kg (snatch limiter) and presumably crashes in the catch. This article directly addresses floor-based catching issues. Complexes fit within hypertrophy block structure.

**Not directly addressed**: Back pain management, night shift recovery needs, or push jerk → split jerk progression.

---

**Action**: Integrate power snatch + snatch complexes early in 6-week block to establish bar connection before heavy loading.
