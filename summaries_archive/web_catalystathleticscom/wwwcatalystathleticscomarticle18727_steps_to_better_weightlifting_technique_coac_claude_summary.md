## Key Extracts Relevant to Your Profile:

**1. Programming Principles**
- Mention of "optimal dosages" (sets/reps) based on neural fatigue and individual impact
- Integration timing: complex remedial work early (fresh CNS); simpler work after main lifts or end of session
- Periodization note: remediation should be removed once resolved, not performed indefinitely

**2. Technique Cues**
- **Jerk example:** Common error = "stick your head through" cue misses real cause (locked knees in dip → hip flexion → forward drive). Coaches must understand biomechanics, not just symptoms.
- **Snatch recovery example:** If athlete dumps weight forward from deep squat, prescribe **overhead squats with pause at bottom + chest-lead emphasis**

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Use remedial exercises to clarify movement concepts (e.g., paused OHS for snatch stability)
- Select based on diagnosed cause, not symptom
- Demonstrate and ensure competent execution
