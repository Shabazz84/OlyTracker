# Analysis: "Why Your Lifts Just Aren't Getting Better" by Mike Gray

## Relevant to This Athlete:

**1. Programming Principles**
- Avoid adding/cutting volume blindly; requires strategic reevaluation
- Return to basics: snatch, clean & jerk, squat, pull—executed well
- Periodization: Consider structured blocks (aligns with athlete's 6-week hypertrophy plan)

**2. Strength Development (Critical)**
- **Front Squat for clean recovery** — directly applicable (athlete gets pinned on cleans at 80 kg with 118 kg BS)
- **Pulls for floor weakness** — relevant to snatch/clean
- Squat strength is foundational; athlete's OHS (50 kg) is severely limiting snatch progression

**3. Recovery & Injury Management (Highly Relevant)**
- **Address nagging injuries immediately** — athlete has chronic back pain; this must be evaluated/treated before intensity increases
- Sleep quality critical for shift workers (night shifts Wed–Sun will impair recovery)
- Warm-up protocol, nutrition, sleep emphasized as non-negotiable for intermediate lifters

**4. Environmental/Coaching**
- Training alone or with poor coaches causes bad habits — get form critiques regularly
- Consider coaching certification to deepen technique understanding

**5. Mindset**
- Avoid complacency; set attainable goals and update them
- Stalling is normal; requires systematic problem-solving, not just volume adjustments

## Key Caution:
Article doesn't address **back pain management** or **shift-work recovery optimization**—both critical for this athlete before hypertrophy block.
