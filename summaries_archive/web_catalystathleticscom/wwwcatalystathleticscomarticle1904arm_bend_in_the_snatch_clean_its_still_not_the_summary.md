**2. Technique cues for snatch/clean**
*   **Primary Cue:** Keep arms long and relaxed; do not intentionally bend elbows during the pull.
*   **Elbow Position:** Elbows should be pulled backward slightly to allow them to move up and out maximally for downward speed, rather than bending early which causes "crashing" of the bar onto shoulders.
*   **Grip Width (Snatch):** Place bar in the crease of hips when hanging at arms' length (just above pubic bone).
*   **Grip Width (Clean):** Hands should be half a fist to one fist-width outside shoulders; forearms must remain vertical in the rack position.
*   **Hip Dominance:** Rely on hip extension rather than arm pulling; if using a "hip-dominant" style, ensure proper setup so arms don't need to bend to bring bar to hips.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **To fix timing/balance:** Halting snatch/clean deadlifts, segment pulls, and segment snatches/cleans.
*   **To raise bar without arm bending (for specific body types):** High-thigh hang shrugs (shrug shoulders back/up to elevate bar into hip crease).
