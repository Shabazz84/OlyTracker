# Weightlifting Analysis: Athlete Mobility Workout Article

**Not a programming/technique article.** This is general mobility education content.

## Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:

### 1. **Recovery & Mobility (Directly Applicable)**
- **Frequency:** 2–3 mobility sessions/week minimum (avoid daily overtraining)
- **Key areas to target:** Lower back, hips, hamstrings, ankles, shoulders
- **Specific exercises recommended:**
  - Cat-Cow stretch (lower back mobility)
  - Child's Pose (hip/lower back relief)
  - Sphinx Pose (lower back/core strength)
  - Pelvic Tilt (lower back + hip stability)
  - Hip Bridge (glute/hamstring activation)

### 2. **Critical for This Athlete's Profile**

| Issue | Article Recommendation |
|-------|------------------------|
| **Chronic back pain** | Cat-Cow, Sphinx Pose, Pelvic Tilt; consult healthcare provider before aggressive extension work |
| **Low OHS (50 kg)** | Shoulder mobility drills; chest/upper back stretching |
| **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** | Light stretching on recovery days helps prevent stiffness |

### 3. **Timeline Expectations**
- Visible mobility gains: **weeks–months** of consistent work
- Results support 6-week hypertrophy block (mobility prevents compensations during loading)
