**2. Technique Cues (Warm-up Specific)**
*   **Wall Slides:** Keep arms outstretched against the wall; bend elbows on the way down into a "crab-like" position. Crucial for those with injured shoulders.
*   **Band Pull-Aparts:** Keep the body static; only hands move to pull the band horizontally. Focus on upper back and rear delts, avoiding yanking motions.
*   **Scapular Push-ups:** Dip torso while arms remain static (no circular motion); focus on shoulder blades moving up/down into sockets.
*   **YTW:** Lie prone, lift legs/feet off ground; keep back static. Only move arms to form Y, T, or W shapes. Focus on traps and rhomboids.
