# Analysis: Push Jerk vs Push Press

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**HIGHLY RELEVANT** – This athlete currently does **push jerk only (no split jerk)** and has chronic back pain + limited OHS (50 kg).

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- No specific sets/reps/intensity guidance provided
- Push press noted as producing greatest power at ~75% 1RM (individual-dependent)

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**

**Push Jerk:**
- Dip into quarter squat, torso upright
- Explode extending hips/knees to drive bar
- Drop under quickly, bend hips/knees, catch with arms extended, elbows locked
- Stand up to finish
- *Requires timing, coordination, quick footwork*

**Push Press:**
- Dip to quarter squat
- Explosive leg drive (dip → drive → press in one motion)
- Press bar overhead after leg drive—**no second knee bend**
- Upper body finishes the lift

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**For this athlete:**
- **Push press may be safer for chronic back pain**: Simpler mechanics, one smooth motion, less time under load in flexed position
- Push jerk demands more mobility (ankles, hips, thoracic spine, shoulders) and stricter overhead position—risky given back pain + low OHS (50 kg)
- Consider both for overall development but **push press as primary** given pain profile

---

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**

**Push jerk mobility demands (HIGH for this athlete):**
- Shoulder, hip, ankle, thoracic spine, and back flexibility essential
- Requires quick coordination + heavy catch in squat
- Push press needs less mobility—mostly shoulder, minimal hip/ankle demand

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Notes**

⚠️ **Red flags for this athlete:**
- OHS limited at 50 kg = severe overhead/mobility/stability deficit
- Chronic back pain + frequent catch-position loads (push jerk) = aggravation risk
- Night shift (Wed–Sun) = recovery compromised; prioritize simpler, lower-stress overhead work

**Recommendation:** Emphasize **push press** in hypertrophy block to build overhead stability safely; delay heavy push jerk work until OHS improves and back pain managed.
