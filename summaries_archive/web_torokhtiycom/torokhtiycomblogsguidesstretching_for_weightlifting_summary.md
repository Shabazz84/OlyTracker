# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/stretching-for-weightlifting

**1. Programming principles**
*   **Frequency:** Perform stretching/flexibility drills 2–3 times per week minimum.
*   **Pre-Workout (Dynamic):** Limit duration to 30–60 seconds per position; focus on blood flow and pliability. Avoid prolonged static stretching before lifting as it can reduce explosive force output.
*   **Post-Workout (Static):** Hold stretches for 2–3 minutes per major muscle group (hamstrings, hips/quads, back/torso) to aid recovery and lengthen fibers.
*   **Structure:** Combine soft tissue mobilization, joint mobilizations, and motor control drills before lifting; prioritize static stretching after lifting.

**2. Technique cues for snatch, clean, or jerk**
*   *Note: The text does not provide specific technical cues for the barbell lifts (snatch/clean/jerk) themselves.*
*   **General Posture:** Emphasize maintaining a neutral spine and proper posture during lifts to prevent injury; mobility deficits in joints can compromise form even with high strength.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Pre-Workout Dynamic Circuit:**
    *   Arm Swings (2 sets x 15 reps).
    *   Leg Swings (Front/Back, 2 sets x 15 reps per leg).
    *   Torso Twists (2 sets x 15 reps).
*   **Post-Workout Static Circuit:**
    *   Sitting Hamstring Stretch.
    *   Pigeon Quad Stretch (hold 2 mins/side; use incline bench if hip ROM is limited).
    *   V-Sitting Stretch (targets hamstrings/lower back).
    *   Chest Stretch (doorway stretch).
    *   Leaning Triceps Stretch.

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Timing:** Do not rest immediately after lifting; perform static stretching to flush metabolic byproducts and start muscle repair.
*   **Duration:** Post-workout stretches should be held for 2–3 minutes per side/group (significantly longer than pre-workout).
*   **Pain Management:** Address lack of joint mobility as a potential trigger for pain/discomfort; systematic stretching reduces sensitivity to tension and prevents strains.

**5. Specifically relevant to this athlete's profile**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The text explicitly identifies "lack of joint mobility" as a cause for pain and recommends the V-Sitting Stretch (hamstrings/lower back) and Pigeon Pose (hips/quads) to alleviate tightness that contributes to lower back strain.
*   **OHS Limiter (50kg):** Recommends Chest Stretches and Leaning Triceps Stretches to improve upper body mobility for overhead positions, which is critical given the athlete's primary limiter in the Overhead Squat.
*   **Transitioning Athlete:** Highlights that strength alone is insufficient; speed, coordination, and flexibility are required to execute Olympic lifts correctly without injury.
*   **Night Shift/Recovery:** Emphasizes post-workout stretching for reducing soreness and speeding up recovery, which is vital for an athlete with a non-standard schedule (Wed–Sun) who needs efficient tissue repair between shifts.
