# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/power-clean-progression

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** The text suggests 2–3 sessions per week for athletic training and 1 session/week for Olympic integration (contradictory; athlete should aim for the higher frequency given their intermediate status).
*   **Intensity/Volume:** Working weights of 60–80% of 1RM for 1–3 reps.
*   **Periodization:** Structured progression from foundational drills to full lifts over a 6-week block, emphasizing "training technique until you can't do it bad."
*   **Note on Context:** The text heavily promotes a generic "Boost explosive strength in 6 weeks" product with 3 intense sessions/week, which may be too high volume for an athlete with chronic back pain and night shift fatigue.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Stance:** Shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out, weight evenly distributed (use the "one foot on instep of other" trick only to find width).
*   **Grip:** Hook grip; elbows just outside knees at setup.
*   **First Pull:** Flat back, engaged core, shoulders over bar, patience (slow start), shins vertical.
*   **Second Pull/Finish:** Explosive hip extension, drive bar up, rapid elbow rotation/wrap under the bar ("catch" position).
*   **Catch:** Stable half-squat, elbows high, chest up, upright torso in front squat position.

**3. Exercise Recommendations (Progression Steps)**
1.  Front Squat (emphasizing high elbows/chest).
2.  Muscle Clean from Hip (hip extension + elbow wrap).
3.  Power Clean from Hip (transition to catch).
4.  Hang Power Clean Above Knees (focus on second pull).
5.  Knee Pause Power Clean (control transition from floor/knee).
6.  Full Power Clean.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Mobility:** The text notes that "limited mobility" may cause struggle in the muscle clean phase and implies improvement over time, but offers no specific mobility drills.
*   **Recovery:** Mentions "adequate recovery" generally and warns against training technique until failure under metabolic fatigue (functional fitness context).

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The text emphasizes a "flat back," "engaged core," and "strong setup." This is critical for the athlete, but the recommended 3x/week high-intensity program may exacerbate pain if not managed carefully.
*   **Night Shift Worker (Wed–Sun):** The text suggests training 2–3 times per week but does not account for circadian rhythm disruption or recovery needs specific to night shifts. The "metabolic fatigue" focus in functional fitness sections is likely counterproductive for a sleep-deprived athlete.
*   **Push Jerk Only:** The article focuses exclusively on the Clean and Power Clean; it offers no guidance on the Jerk (specifically the push jerk limitation or transition to split).
*   **OHS Limiter:** The text mentions "Front Squat" as a key drill for the catch position, which indirectly supports overhead stability but does not address the specific 50kg OHS weakness.
*   **Intermediate Status:** The progression steps (1–8) are well-suited for refining technique, though the athlete may need to skip early drills if they already possess basic competency.
