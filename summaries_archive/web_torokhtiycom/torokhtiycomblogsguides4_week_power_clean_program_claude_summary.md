# Analysis: 4-Week Power Clean Program

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Frequency:** 3 sessions/week
- **Duration:** 4 weeks (article mentions 6-week option available)
- **Intensity:** 60–90% of max clean, progressing 2–3% weekly
- **Structure:** Week 1 focuses technique; Weeks 2–4 intensify load
- **Rep schemes:** Mostly 3–5 reps; accessory work 6–20 reps
- **Periodization:** Linear progression with variation (hang, deficit, blocks, complexes)

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Clean High Pulls:** Develop explosive pulling power and reinforce bar path
- **Hang Power Clean:** Improve catching mechanics and timing
- **Power Clean + Front Squat complex:** Enhance clean transition
- **General emphasis:** Proper form prioritized to prevent injury

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Main:** Power clean, clean high pulls, hang power clean, clean pulls
- **Squats:** Front squat (80%), back squat (80%), Bulgarian split squats
- **Pulls:** RDLs, single-leg deadlifts, lat rows
- **Conditioning:** Sled pushes, sprints, depth jumps, barbell squat jumps
- **Mobility:** 10–15 min daily (hips, shoulders, back, hamstrings)

## 4. Recovery & Mobility
- 10–15 minutes post-session mobility work (circuit-based)
- Focus areas: hips, shoulders, back, hamstrings
- "Adequate recovery to prevent overtraining"

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
⚠️ **Moderate fit, with cautions:**
- ✅ Power clean + squat focus aligns with snatch/jerk foundations
- ✅ Single-leg deadlifts, unilateral work aids back stability
- ✅ Mobility emphasis suits chronic back pain
- ⚠️ **Missing snatch work** (athlete's primary limiter: OHS 50 kg)
- ⚠️ **No split jerk progression** (athlete only has push jerk)
- ⚠️ **Night-shift schedule** not addressed (recovery demands high)
- ⚠️ 4 weeks conflicts with stated 6-week hypertrophy goal
