# **Torokhtiy Coaching Philosophy & Program Design**
## Synthesis for Intermediate OLY Athlete with Chronic Back Pain & Night Shift Schedule

---

## **1. CORE TRAINING PHILOSOPHY**

### Principle 1: **Technique First, Load Second**
- Movement quality is non-negotiable; load progression follows technical competency
- Poor technique under fatigue (night shift fatigue for this athlete) accelerates injury and plateaus
- Especially critical: OHS limiter (50 kg) and chronic back pain require controlled, perfect repetitions

### Principle 2: **Periodization Around Individual Constraints**
- Standard periodization (foundations → strength → power → peak) adapted to athlete's specific context
- 6-week hypertrophy block BEFORE strength/technique refinement is the correct sequencing (builds work capacity and joint resilience for chronic pain)
- Night-shift worker (Wed–Sun) requires condensed session density (45–60 min) with 2–3 full training days + optional accessory/mobility days

### Principle 3: **Strategic Accessory Focus on Individual Limiters**
- Weak OHS (50 kg vs. 118 kg BS) = thoracic/shoulder mobility + overhead stability focus (snatch balances, hip snatch, sots press)
- Chronic back pain = posterior chain strengthening (hyperextensions, good mornings) + anterior core + mobility integration
- Push jerk only = overhead stability prerequisite before split jerk introduction (post-foundation phase)

### Principle 4: **Frequency-Based Autoregulation**
- 3–4 training days/week optimal for intermediate lifter with recovery constraints
- Intensity/volume adjusted based on daily readiness (RPE, sleep quality, session performance)
- Deload every 4–6 weeks (especially critical with night-shift circadian disruption)

### Principle 5: **Dual-Focus Squat Emphasis**
- Front & back squats both prioritized (foundational to all Olympic lifts)
- Intermediate athlete: Front squat ~90% of C&J; Back squat potentially 110–120% of C&J (this athlete: BS 118, C 80 = 147% ratio—strong relative backend)
- Squat frequency: 2–3×/week across main lifting days

## **2. PROGRAMMING APPROACH**

### **Structure: 3-Day Split (Adjustable for Night Shift)**

**Day 1 (Snatch Focus):**
- Snatch or snatch variation (60–80% intensity, 3–5 reps × 3–5 sets)
- Back squat (hypertrophy: 6–8 reps × 3–4 @ 70–75%; strength: 3–5 reps × 3–4 @ 85–90%)
- Snatch accessory (hip snatch, snatch high pull, OHS, or tall snatch)
- Core/mobility (10–15 min)

**Day 2 (Clean & Jerk Focus):**
- Clean or clean variant (60–80%, 2–5 reps × 3–5 sets)
- Front squat (hypertrophy: 6–8 @ 70–75%; strength: 3–5 @ 85–90%)
- Jerk variant (push press, jerk dips, jerk balance) or push jerk work
- Clean pull or accessory pull (clean high pull, hang clean)
- Core/mobility (10–15 min)

**Day 3 (Accessory/Strength Emphasis):**
- Secondary squat variation (pause squats, safety squat bar, or Hatch-model dual squat focus)
- Posterior chain (hyperextensions 3–4 × 4–6, good mornings 3 × 5)
- Unilateral work (split squats, single-leg deadlifts for back stability & symmetry)
- Upper back (rows, pull-ups, lat work for shoulder health)
- Core circuit (3–5 min)

**Optional Day 4 (Active Recovery/Mobility):**
- Light cardio (20 min easy cycling/walking)
- Comprehensive mobility routine (15–20 min: cat-cow, child's pose, hip flexor stretch, hanging lat stretch, thoracic mobility)
- Foam rolling/stretching

### **Periodization: 6-Week Hypertrophy Block + 6+ Week Strength/Technique**

**Weeks 1–6: Hypertrophy Block (Current Goal)**
- Rep ranges: 6–8 main lifts, 8–12 accessory
- Intensity: 70–80% estimated 1RM (moderate loads, moderate-high volume)
- Focus: Movement quality, back/joint adaptation, work capacity, overhead stability (OHS emphasis)
- Squat frequency: 2×/week (front + back distributed across 3 days)
- Progression: +2–5 lbs weekly (minimal jumps to allow perfect technique)
- Rest intervals: 1.5–2.5 min between main sets
- Key accessory: Hip muscle snatch 4×3 @ 50–55 kg (builds OHS confidence); pause OHS 3×5 @ 40–45 kg
- **Deload week 4:** Reduce volume 30–40%, maintain intensity around 60–65%
