### Relevance to This Athlete:

**High relevance** — but for recovery/mobility, not training structure.

**Key Takeaways:**

1. **Recovery/Mobility Advice:**
   - Lat tightness impairs overhead movements (directly relevant: athlete's OHS 50 kg is primary snatch limiter)
   - Tight lats restrict shoulder mobility and spinal stability — both critical for clean/jerk/snatch
   - Recommend static lat stretches (30–60 sec holds) post-workout; dynamic stretches pre-workout
   - Suggests 5x/week stretching → 2–6 weeks for noticeable flexibility gains

2. **Specific Relevance to Athlete's Issues:**
   - Chronic back pain + night shift work → lat tightness likely from postural stress (sitting). Lat stretches may help spinal stability.
   - Low OHS (50 kg) suggests limited shoulder/thoracic mobility — hanging lat stretches and unilateral variations could help overhead positioning.

3. **Best Stretches for This Athlete:**
   - **Hanging lat stretch** (static or dynamic) — builds shoulder stability for overhead work
   - **Unilateral fixed bar lat stretch** — directly addresses asymmetries in overhead mobility
   - **Standing lateral stretch** — minimal recovery demands (useful with night shift fatigue)

**⚠️ No programming, periodization, or technique cues included.**
