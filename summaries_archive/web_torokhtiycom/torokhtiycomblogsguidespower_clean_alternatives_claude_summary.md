---

## 2. Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean/Jerk)
- **Clean High Pull**: elbows up/out, focus on pulling phase without turnover demands (good for strength development)
- **Hang Clean**: emphasizes explosive pull + turnover phases; good for technique refinement before full clean
- **Power Snatch**: wide hook grip, half-squat catch, full-body development
- **Muscle Clean**: simplified pulling without squat catch (useful for teaching mechanics)

---

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
**Most relevant for this athlete:**
- **Clean High Pulls** — builds back/shoulder strength for jerk without back stress; improves pulling pattern
- **Hang Cleans** — reduces floor ROM demands (relevant for chronic back pain); emphasizes technical phases
- **Kettlebell Swings** — posterior chain emphasis; lower wrist pressure; easier on joints
- ⚠️ **Power Snatch** — helpful for snatch coordination BUT 50 kg OHS is limiting; front squat/OHS work is priority

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Exercise variety reduces "mileage" on same joints/tendons → injury prevention
