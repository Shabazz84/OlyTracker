# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **3-day split**: Day 1 (Snatch), Day 2 (C&J), Day 3 (Accessory/Strength)
- **Intensity levels**: Up to 70% for Olympic lift variants; 90% for pulls/squats
- **Set/rep scheme**: Typically 3–4 sets × 3–6 reps for Olympic lifts; 3–4 sets × 4–5 reps for strength
- **4-phase periodization**:
  - Weeks 1–4: Foundation (technique focus, light weight)
  - Weeks 5–8: Strength Development
  - Weeks 9–12: Power/Speed (lighter weight, faster pace)
  - Weeks 13–16: Peaking (singles/doubles, maximal effort)
- **Progression**: 2–3% weekly increases; deload every 4–6 weeks

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Snatch**: Explosive hip extension, vertical bar path, full extension + aggressive catch
- **Clean**: Bar path feeling, elbow rotation emphasis
- **Jerk**: Control dip & drive for shoulder strength
- **Overhead Squat**: Balance, stability, depth focus

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Snatch High Pulls, Power Snatch, Overhead Squats, Snatch Deadlifts
- Muscle Squat Cleans, Power Clean + Jerk, Clean Pulls, Front Squats
- Push Press, Deficit RDL, Back Squat, Rows, core/mobility drills

## 4. **Recovery & Mobility**
- Dynamic stretches pre-session (shoulders, hips, ankles, wrists)
- 10 min accessory/mobility work daily
- Emphasis: Sleep quality, rest priority, training logs, listen to body

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Program includes frequent OHS (3×6 @ 60%) — good match
- **No split jerk**: Program uses Power Clean + Jerk (likely power jerk) — aligns with current ability
- **Chronic back pain**: High-volume Back Squats (4×5 @ 90%) on Day 3 may aggravate; consider load reduction or alternatives
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: 3-day frequency workable but may need flexible scheduling
- **Hypertrophy goal (next 6 weeks)**: This template leans technical/strength; lacks dedicated hypertrophy rep ranges (8–12 reps). Recommend modifying to 4–8 rep schemes post-program.
