    *   **Mobility/Dynamic:** Arm circles, leg swings (forward/back/side), dynamic lunges, inchworms, torso twists, air squats.

*   **Duration:** 10–20 minutes total, depending on session intensity (Light: 8-10 mins; High Intensity/Complex Lifts: 15-20 mins).
