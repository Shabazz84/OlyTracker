# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/10-week-olympic-weightlifting-program

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Structure:** 3-day weekly split (Day 1: Snatch focus; Day 2: Clean & Jerk pull/squat focus; Day 3: Overhead/Jerk stability).
*   **Progression:** Linear periodization over 10 weeks. Weeks 1-2 are foundational; Weeks 3-6 add 2-3% load weekly; Weeks 7-8 shift to power (65-75%, lower reps); Weeks 9-10 peak at 80-90%.
*   **Intensity/Volume:** Starts moderate (50-75%), peaks high (90-100%). Volume decreases as intensity rises in later weeks.
*   **Deloading:** Recommended every 5-6 weeks or if technique falters.

**2. Technique Cues & Drills**
*   **Snatch:** Focus on "turnover speed" and "under-the-bar speed." Use *Muscle Snatch* for upper body strength, *Power Snatch + Hip Snatch* for trajectory, and *Push Press + Balance* for drop-under stability.
*   **Clean:** Refine transition phases using *PVC Muscle Clean + Front Squat + Press*. Use *Hang Power Cleans* to isolate the pull.
*   **Jerk:** Emphasize "overhead stability" and "precise technique." Use *Push Press + Jerk* drills to merge strength with timing.
*   **General:** Film sessions regularly for form checks; prioritize bar trajectory over load.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Lifts:** Back Squat, Front Squat, Snatch, Clean & Jerk (including variations like Power/Clean).
*   **Accessories:** Gakk Squats, Split Squats, Wall Squats, Barbell Squat Jumps, Hyperextensions/Supermans.
*   **Overhead Strength:** Push Presses, Overhead Stability drills.

**4. Recovery & Mobility Advice**
*   **Warm-up:** Dynamic stretches focusing on shoulders/hips (Day 1) or wrists/shoulders (Day 2). Include specific activation (Hyperextensions, Monkey Jumps).
*   **Cool-down:** Mandatory 10-15 minutes of mobility and stretching post-session.
*   **Recovery Strategy:** Listen to the body; integrate reduced intensity sessions if fatigue accumulates.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile (Intermediate, Chronic Back Pain, Night Shift)**
*   **⚠️ Critical Mismatch:** The text is a **general beginner/intermediate 10-week program**, not tailored for an athlete with **chronic back pain**. It prescribes high-volume heavy squats and jumps without specific modifications for spinal health.
*   **Night Shift Constraint:** The text suggests a standard 3-day split but offers no guidance on sleep hygiene, circadian rhythm management, or training timing adjustments for Wed–Sun night shift workers.
*   **Back Pain Risk:** Exercises like *Gakk Squats*, *Wall Squats*, and heavy *Front/Back Squats* (90-100%) are prescribed without cues for bracing or pain mitigation. The athlete's chronic back pain requires a modified approach (e.g., reduced axial loading, core stability focus) not found in this generic text.
*   **Overhead Limiter:** While the text mentions *Push Press + Balance*, it does not specifically address the athlete's 50kg OHS limitation with targeted hypertrophy or mobility work for the shoulders/upper back beyond general "stability."
