# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/2-day-olympic-weightlifting-program

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 2 days/week (one day Snatch focus, one day Clean & Jerk focus).
*   **Structure:** Warm-up → Specific Lift Variations → Accessory Strength → Cooldown/Mobility.
*   **Intensity/Volume:**
    *   *Snatch Day:* High Pulls (4x6), Hang Snatch ~70% 1RM (4x3), Snatch Pulls ~90% 1RM (4x4), Front Squats ~90% C&J 1RM (5x3).
    *   *C&J Day:* Muscle Clean + Thruster (3x6), Clean & Jerk ~80% 1RM (4x2+2), Clean Pulls ~100% 1RM (3x3), Back Squats up to 105% C&J 1RM (4x3).
*   **Progression:** Incremental weight increases ("small increments") with logging of subjective feel.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Snatch:** Focus on "elevation of the bar" and the "explosive second phase" (pulling under the bar) via Hang Snatches.
*   **Clean & Jerk:** Emphasis on "functional power, speed, and explosiveness." Use Muscle Squat Clean + Thruster to link movements.
*   **General:** Warm-ups must target specific joints: shoulders/hips/ankles for snatch; wrists/shoulders/hip flexors for C&J.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Snatch Focus:** High Pull from knee, Hang Snatch, Snatch Pull (90%), Front Squat.
*   **C&J Focus:** Muscle Squat Clean + Thruster, Clean & Jerk, Clean Pull (100%), Back Squat (up to 105%).

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Warm-up:** Dynamic stretches only; target shoulders, hips, ankles, wrists, and hip flexors.
*   **Cooldown:** Slow, static stretches targeting worked muscles for flexibility and recovery.
*   **Rest:** Emphasize that rest days are critical for muscle recovery and injury reduction.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile (Intermediate, 102.5kg, Back Pain, Night Shift)**
*   **⚠️ Critical Mismatch:** The text recommends a **2-day/week** frequency. For an athlete with chronic back pain transitioning from powerlifting, this low frequency may be insufficient to build the specific motor patterns needed to stabilize the spine under Olympic loads safely.
*   **Back Pain Risk:** The program prescribes heavy Back Squats (up to 105% of C&J max) and high-volume pulling without mentioning spinal bracing or pain management strategies, which is risky for a chronic back pain sufferer.
*   **Night Shift/Recovery:** The text advises "proper rest" generally but offers no specific protocols for shift workers (e.g., sleep hygiene, light exposure timing) to mitigate the fatigue from Wed–Sun shifts.
*   **OHS Limiter:** The program includes Front Squats and Back Squats which are relevant, but lacks specific overhead stability work (like strict press or OHS variations) to address the 50kg OHS limiter directly.
*   **Jerk Style:** The text mentions "Clean & Jerk" generally; it does not specify the *push jerk* vs. *split jerk* distinction, which is crucial for this athlete who currently only uses a push jerk.
