# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/4-day-olympic-weightlifting-program

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Structure:** 4-day weekly template (3 main lifting days + 1 technical/day).
*   **Intensity/Volume:** Uses percentage-based loading relative to 1RM (e.g., 50%, 65%, 75%). Typical structure involves warm-up sets followed by work sets in the 2–4 rep range at moderate intensities.
*   **Periodization:** Suggests a linear progression over 16 weeks: Weeks 1-4 (Technique), 5-8 (Strength), 9-12 (Power/Speed), 13-16 (Peak/Taper).
*   **Frequency:** High frequency for Olympic lifts, with specific focus on "technical training" at low intensity (up to 40%) on Day 4.

**2. Technique Cues & Drills**
*   **Snatch:** Focus on *Reverse Hyperextensions*, *Muscle Snatches*, and *Overhead Squats*. Specific drill: *Snatch from Blocks* with progressive loading.
*   **Clean:** Emphasis on *Power Clean below knee*, *Clean Balance*, and *Clean Pulls* (up to 100% intensity).
*   **Jerk:** Focus on *Pause Jerk*, *Press in split position*, and *Overhead Split Squats*.
*   **General Cues:** "Warm Body Cold Mind" philosophy; prioritize form over weight in early phases.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Lifts:** Back Squat, Snatch (from blocks), Clean & Jerk variations, Overhead Squat.
*   **Accessory/Strength:** Gakky Squats, Good Morning + Squat combos, Bench Press, Reverse Plank, Core strengthening.
*   **Prehab/Mobility:** Dynamic mobility for shoulders/hips/ankles/wrists, PVC Hip Clean Balance, Rubber Band Shoulder drills.

**4. Recovery & Mobility Advice**
*   **Warm-up:** Mandatory 7–12 min dynamic mobility before every session.
*   **Cool-down:** Dedicated 10-min flexibility/stretching blocks focusing on strained areas.
*   **Lifestyle:** Emphasize "quality sleep," detailed workout journaling, and regular deload weeks. Nutrition is cited as pivotal for recovery.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile (Intermediate, Back Pain, Night Shift)**
*   **⚠️ Critical Mismatch:** The text recommends a **4-day program** with high volume and intensity progression designed for "experienced lifters" or those who can "choose number of session." This conflicts with the athlete's goal of a **6-week hypertrophy block** first, as the text pushes immediately into technique/strength phases without a dedicated hypertrophy phase.
*   **Back Pain:** The program includes *Reverse Hyperextensions*, *Good Mornings*, and heavy *Back Squats*. While these are standard for lifters, an athlete with **chronic back pain** needs to be extremely cautious with the "Good Morning + Squat" compound on Day 2 and high-volume squats without prior medical clearance or specific modification.
*   **Night Shift (Wed–Sun):** The text suggests a flexible schedule ("choose number of session... according to recovery"), which is beneficial for shift workers, but the program assumes consistent daily availability. The athlete must manually adjust this rigid template to fit their Wed-Sun work block and ensure sleep quality on off-days.
*   **OHS Limiter:** The program explicitly includes *Overhead Squats* and *Snatch Sots Press*, directly addressing the 50kg OHS limitation, but these are embedded in a high-intensity technical day rather than a dedicated hypertrophy context.
