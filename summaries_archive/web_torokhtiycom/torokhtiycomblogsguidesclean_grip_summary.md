### 2. Technique Cues (Clean Grip Focus)
*   **Grip Width:** Set slightly wider than shoulder-width. A specific cue provided: **"Thumb-length away from thighs"** when standing with arms at sides to find optimal width.
    *   *Too Narrow:* Low point of contact, weak power generation, inefficient bar path.
    *   *Too Wide:* Difficult starting position, unstable front rack, elbows forced down.
*   **Grip Type:** Recommends transitioning from overhand to **Hook Grip** (thumb wrapped under fingers) for security and timing, despite initial thumb pain (2-week adaptation).
*   **Setup Cues:**
    *   Wrapping thumb with middle then index finger (for hook grip).
    *   Symmetrical hand placement.
    *   Neutral wrist position.
    *   Keep arms relaxed; generate power from legs/hips, not a tight grip.
