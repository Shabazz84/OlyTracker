**Mental preparation/confidence management** (highly relevant given this athlete's chronic back pain and conservative push jerk limitation):
- Pre-lift visualization is critical before approaching heavy attempts
- Belief/confidence directly impacts execution likelihood
- Ego management essential: **stop attempts before losing confidence rather than risk injury with "hit and hoping"**
- Especially important for heavier weights and longer lifting careers

**Application note:** Given chronic back pain, this athlete should particularly heed the "stop before losing belief" cue to avoid compensatory mechanics or bailing unsafely.
