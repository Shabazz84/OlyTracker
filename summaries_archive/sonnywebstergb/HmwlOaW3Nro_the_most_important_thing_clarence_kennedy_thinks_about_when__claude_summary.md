# The most important thing Clarence Kennedy thinks about when he squats!

**Relevant Extractions:**

1. **Programming/Technique Principle:**
   - Torso position is critical for Olympic weightlifting—maintain upright posture in back squat to develop quad dominance and transfer to clean/jerk positions.

2. **Technique Cue (Back Squat):**
   - Stay upright (avoid leaning forward)
   - Drive knees forward
   - Focus on quad loading
   - High-bar squat style recommended for OLY athletes

3. **Relevance to This Athlete:**
   - **HIGHLY RELEVANT:** Athlete has chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg). Forward lean during squatting stresses lumbar spine. Upright positioning + quad emphasis = reduced back stress + better OHS transfer. This directly addresses his primary snatch limiter (OHS weakness) and pain management.
