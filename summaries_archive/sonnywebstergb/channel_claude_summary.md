# SONNY WEBSTER COACHING PHILOSOPHY
## Applied to Intermediate OLY Lifter (102.5 kg BW | Clean 80 kg | OHS 50 kg | Chronic Back Pain)

---

## 1. CORE TRAINING PHILOSOPHY

**Five Principles:**

1. **Bar Path & Position Before Load** — Vertical bar path, consistent positioning, and receiving stability trump absolute strength. Master movement *pattern* before chasing weight.

2. **Mobility ≠ Passive Stretching** — ROM and positional capacity are *performance limiters*, not optional. Systematic mobility work (thoracic extension, ankle dorsiflexion, hip/shoulder mobility) directly unlocks lift capacity and reduces compensation patterns.

3. **Receiving Position Mastery First** — Squat snatch and full-depth OHS precede power variations. Deeper catches improve mechanical efficiency and reduce bar height requirements—critical for weak overhead athletes.

4. **Controlled Tempo Builds Strength** — Deliberate eccentric control, especially in warm-ups, establishes positional integrity and transfers to heavy loads. Rushing warm-ups guarantees poor top-set mechanics.

5. **Movement Visualization & Confidence Gating** — Only attempt lifts when confident in the execution pattern. Stop before losing belief rather than risk compensatory injury; mental clarity is a prerequisite, not a luxury.

## 2. PROGRAMMING APPROACH

**Structure for 6-Week Hypertrophy Block:**

| Phase | Focus | Key Exercises | Notes |
|-------|-------|---------------|-------|
| **Weeks 1–2** | Mobility + Positional Work | Snatch balance, OHS, front squat (unbelted), wall slides, duck rocks, banded lockouts | Low intensity; establish baseline ROM. Thoracic/ankle/hip mobility daily. |
| **Weeks 3–5** | Hypertrophy + Technique | Full snatch (85–88% perceived intensity), front squat, clean, jerk progression, snatch balance accessories | Build quad/core capacity; address OHS limiter. Tempo-controlled warm-ups (3-sec eccentrics). |
| **Week 6** | Taper + Technique Consolidation | Lighter snatch/clean/jerk work; snatch balance; mobility maintenance | Prepare for strength block. Monitor back pain trends. |

**Intensity Guidelines:**
- **Main lifts** (snatch, clean, jerk): 70–80% perceived effort—prioritize technique fluency
- **Accessories** (OHS, snatch balance, front squat): 65–75%
- **Jerk work**: Emphasize split jerk *sequencing* (full extension → foot split) over push jerk only
- **Strength block (post-6 weeks)**: Escalate to 85%+ once positional foundation is solid

**Periodization Logic:**
- Mobility work reduces back strain and unlocks OHS depth → addresses primary snatch limiter
- Hypertrophy block builds quad/core resilience for clean/squat demands
- Tempo control in warm-ups prevents rush-driven compensation
- Transition to strength block once OHS reaches 65–70 kg and snatch bar path stabilizes

**Night Shift Adaptation (Wed–Sun):**
- Schedule mobility/technical work on fatigue days (lower CNS demand)
- Front-load heavy work on better-rested days if possible
- Keep sessions 45–60 min; avoid extended warm-ups
- Nutrition/hydration non-negotiable (matches Sonny's Powerade + simple carb protocol)

## 3. KEY TECHNIQUE CUES & EXERCISE PREFERENCES

**SNATCH:**
- **Bar path**: "Lift between two boxes" — vertical movement only; prevents forward/backward jumping
- **Pull sequencing**: Chest-lead (maintain back angle) until mid-thigh; avoid early knee extension ("stripper snatch")
- **Setup**: Feet inside shoulder width; bar over lace of shoe; look up/above eyeline
- **Receiving**: Snatch balance and full OHS are primary movers (power snatch = accessory only)

**CLEAN:**
- **Meeting point**: Catch bar at its highest point post-pull; actively decelerate into bottom position
- **Bar path**: Keep bar close through knees; shoulders stay over bar during transition
- **Front rack**: Wrist/forearm pain = mobility issue (lats/triceps/shoulders), not weakness. Upper thoracic extension critical.
- **Dip phase**: Control the descent deliberately; achieve full extension *before* initiating split

**OVERHEAD SQUAT (Primary Snatch Limiter — 50 kg):**
- Snatch balance (4–5x/week at 65–75%): Builds receiving strength without full snatch complexity
- Wall slides + banded lockouts: Establish shoulder/thoracic mobility
- Sotts press + weighted hip glides: ROM capacity + load tolerance
- Full OHS: 2x/week at controlled tempo; glute squeeze + neutral spine (back pain management)

**SQUAT (General):**
- **High-bar positioning** (quad emphasis): Preferred for OLY athletes; reduces lumbar compensation
- **Front squat** (unbelted warm-up sets): Identifies stability deficits; transfers to OHS/clean rack
- **Tempo cue**: 3-sec eccentric in warm-ups → establishes position control → transfers to loaded sets
- **Sticking point**: Maintain upright torso; prevent hip shoot-back; prioritize position over speed in hole

**JERK PROGRESSION:**
- Current state: Push jerk only (65 kg)
- **Pathway**: Establish split jerk mechanics via controlled dip + full extension sequencing
- Clean fronts build jerk capacity (strong front squat = strong jerk foundation)
- Push jerk remains sub-dominant accessory; split jerk is primary strength driver
