# Are jumping when you Snatch? 🤔

**SNATCH TECHNIQUE CUE:**
- **Problem:** Jumping forward/backward = kicking bar away or shoulder throw-back
- **Cue:** Imagine lifting between two boxes; forces all movement vertically
- **Benefit:** Improves bar path consistency, keeps athlete in receiver position

**Relevance to athlete:** Critical for this lifter—OHS at 50 kg is primary snatch limiter. Vertical bar path and stable positioning will improve catch mechanics and confidence in overhead position, directly supporting snatch development during the upcoming technique block.
