**2. Technique Cues — Jerk**
- **Problem**: Rushing the dip causes the bar to descend while the athlete drives upward, creating instability and excessive apparent weight
- **Solution**: 
  - Control the dip deliberately
  - Maintain bar contact throughout
  - Achieve full extension *before* initiating the split
  - This sequencing improves split jerk execution

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
**High relevance**: This athlete uses only push jerk (no split jerk). The cue "control the dip, maintain contact, then split" is directly applicable to building split jerk mechanics—a potential tool to improve jerk numbers (currently 65 kg) and address the gap between Clean (80 kg) and Jerk (65 kg). The emphasis on *sequencing* (extension before split) is foundational for introducing split jerk safely given chronic back pain concerns.
