# Quickest way to improve your SNATCH!

1. **Programming principles**: The text emphasizes high-intensity, low-volume work focused on "control" and stability in the receiving position rather than maximal load or volume accumulation. It implies a technique-first approach where the athlete sets grip and body tension before moving the bar.

   - **Bar Path**: Maintain a consistent bar path (staying on the same part of the bar) from start to finish.
