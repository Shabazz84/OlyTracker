# Merged Coaching Summary: Russian Olympic Weightlifting Session

## Key Points
- **Quality over quantity**: Single reps prioritized for technical assessment; multiple reps only if form is sound
- **Technical work intensity**: 75–85% for correction; minimum 60–70% to maintain skill transfer; avoid heavy singles when errors emerge
- **Post-heavy-block programming**: 5 exercises, moderate reps, ~2 hours; technical work + hypertrophy without CNS overload
- **Recovery protocol**: 1–2 days light activity sufficient after high-volume sessions; avoid heavy cardio (noted to impair strength progression)
- **Exclude painful movements**: Coach explicitly advises removing exercises causing discomfort, especially relevant to back health

## Technique Cues

**Jerk:**
- Vertical torso ("корпус ровно")—no forward lean; maintain upright position through dip/drive/recovery
- Fix gaze on wall point; eliminate head movement during dip/drive/recovery
- Keep chin up during clean pull/drive; downward chin disrupts positioning
- Proper dip angle with centered hips (avoid trapped position)
- No unnecessary hand repositioning post-pull; fingers crimp/secure bar quickly

**Pull & Snatch:**
- Avoid "overpull with arms"—pull high but don't excessively extend; establish power output point
- Chest stays upright; prevent torso collapse forward during pull
- Slow positional pulls (4-count) before explosive finish to oxidize muscle and preserve explosivity
- Clean pull: Drive powerfully through legs; minimal arm pull; bar rises via leg drive, not hand drag

**Jerk Pulls from Blocks:**
- Start at 80% of best jerk, potentially increase 10 kg
- Don't reduce weight for pulls after clean & jerk work
- Emphasize controlled descent and explosive power finish

## Programming Principles
- Technique correction occurs at 75–85%, not max attempts
- Minimum 60–70% load to maintain skill transfer and movement pattern
- Rep schemes: Singles for quality assessment; higher reps only if technically sound
- Slow pulls (4-count) build positional patience and explosivity
- Avoid high-rep work at intensity where form breaks (noted form boundary ~120 kg for pulls)
- Belt use: Minimal impact on pulls; more useful for squats and jerk recovery

## Athlete Relevance

**Chronic Back Pain:**
- Exclude movements causing discomfort; chest-up cue is critical for back health in jerk/pull positions
- Vertical torso in jerk directly reduces back stress—likely contributor to 65 kg power jerk plateau

**Weak Overhead Position (OHS 50 kg):**
- Slow positional pulls + vertical torso emphasis will build stability for overhead positions
- Vertical jerk positioning (no forward lean) improves lockout confidence and prepares for split jerk introduction

**Night Shift Schedule (Wed–Sun):**
- Align high-intensity sessions around sleep patterns; use 1–2 lighter sessions or recovery days post-overwork
- Light activity days (non-training) sufficient for recovery

**6-Week Hypertrophy Block:**
- Slow pulls = muscle oxidation/time under tension (hypertrophy-aligned)
- Maintain form standards; plan intensity increases conservatively to avoid back aggravation
- 5-exercise, moderate-rep structure fits hypertrophy goals without CNS overload

**Jerk Progression (Current 65 kg, Power Jerk only):**
- Pause jerk drills and pulls from blocks build positional control needed for split jerk introduction post-hypertrophy block
- Quality reps at 75–85% intensity ideal for technical preparation
