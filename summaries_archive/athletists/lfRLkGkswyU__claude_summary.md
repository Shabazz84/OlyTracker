# Merged Weightlifting Coaching Summary

## Key Points

- **Pause-based technical work** at 70–80% develops positioning awareness and prevents early hip rise (common error in intermediate lifters)
- **Pull mechanics fundamentally:** simultaneous leg + back + trap extension (not violent hip contact); excessive early speed wastes effort
- **Receiving position correctness** (full foot contact, chest upright, shoulders covered) prevents forward collapse
- **Submaximal intensity allows extended pauses**; 90–100% requires speed and eliminates pauses
- **Cardiovascular conditioning** (small doses 1–2×/week) improves HR recovery between sets and supports heavy volume tolerance
- **2-day rest windows** after heavy sessions support recovery (aligns with this athlete's Sat–Sun availability post Wed–Sun night shift)

---

## Technique Cues

**Snatch/Clean Pull:**
- Stand tall before extending; maintain tightness in lats/arms before hip extension
- Avoid early hip rise (taskover)—leads to back dominance over leg drive
- From floor: extend back, engage arms, feel foot pressure throughout
- Snatch pull to above-knee pause (5 sec isometric) develops takeoff angles and prevents premature extension

**Receiving Position:**
- Full foot contact + chest upright + shoulders covered = stable positioning that prevents forward fall
- Correct angles force body stability

**Jerk (Power/Push Only):**
- Head position critical—avoid blocking bar path
- Power jerk acceptable for intermediate strength building; split jerk deferred to later phase

---

## Programming Principles

**Session Structure (Example Friday Session):**
1. Warm-up: Good morning + snatch grip push press (~70% snatch max; 5 reps + 2 squats/set)
2. Main: Snatch pulls from above knee (paused variations to develop positioning)
3. Secondary: Back squats (80–85% × 3 reps × 3–4 sets) + snatch grip push press
4. Accessory: Jerk push (dumbbell or barbell)
5. Recovery: 2-day rest after heavy loading

**Load Principles:**
- 70–80% allows pauses; 90–100% requires speed
- Increment snatch pull/jerk push EMOM by 10 kg until form breakdown
- ~3 min rest between sets (autoregulate by HR recovery to ~100 bpm)
- Back squat sequenced last to avoid pre-exhausting legs before heavy work

**Rationale:** Lower-level athletes tolerate higher volume because technique (not strength) is the limiting factor.

## Athlete Relevance

**Directly Applicable:**
- **OHS 50 kg limiter** → Snatch grip work (push press, pulls) + pause positioning drills address shoulder mobility/stability
- **Chronic back pain** → Isometric pull work strengthens stabilizers; avoid excessive forward bending; consider spinal extension tool for relief
- **Night shift Wed–Sun** → Friday session shown; 2-day Sat–Sun rest window accommodates schedule
- **Intermediate transition to OL** → Submaximal pause work + above-knee variations build positional strength without max-effort risk
- **Power/push jerk only** → Acceptable for hypertrophy block; split jerk deferred to later phase

**Pre-Hypertrophy Block:**
- Current session is skill/positioning-focused; incorporate 1–2×/week light cardio now to improve HR recovery capacity before entering 6-week high-volume phase
- Load/fatigue management essential given night shift schedule and chronic back pain
