## Key Points

- **Competition prep protocol**: 1 week pre-competition, perform 3 singles at ~95% on snatch and clean to establish opening attempts and gauge daily readiness.
- **Back squat stance mechanics**: Wider stance enables deeper squat with better load distribution and upright torso; narrow stance creates acute bottom angles and forward collapse risk.
- **Elbow positioning in squat**: Point elbows down/parallel to spine (not back) to maintain chest position and torso rigidity.
- **Back pain management**: Chronic spinal issues (disc hernias, nerve pinching) necessitate conservative loading; referenced case study limited back squat to 80 kg max.

---

## Technique Cues

- **Back tension**: "Tense back, meet bar firmly" for stability and control.
- **Shoulder position**: Avoid shoulder forward positioning; ensure active shoulder engagement during pull.
- **Squat integrity**: Maintain chest forward throughout descent; prevent forward collapse by controlling elbow angle.

---

## Programming Principles

- **Pre-competition walkup**: Establish openers via 3 singles at ~95% effort 1 week out; functions as psychological readiness assessment and technical validation.
- **Load variation**: Different stance widths and elbow positions alter movement leverages; potential tool for accessory work targeting weak points.

---

## Athlete Relevance

**High relevance to chronic back pain & OHS limitation:**
- Back squat capped at 80 kg (referenced case); your athlete's 118 kg squat may exceed safe spinal tolerance in hypertrophy block.
- OHS (50 kg) is snatch limiter—upright squat positioning and elbow mechanics directly transferable to overhead position.
- Wider stance + controlled elbow position may reduce anterior load on lumbar spine; prioritize these cues in accessory squatting.
- **Action**: Assess back squat tolerance early in 6-week block; consider tempo/stance modifications over maximal loading.
