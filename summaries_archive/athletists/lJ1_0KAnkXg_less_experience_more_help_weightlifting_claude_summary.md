# Merged Summary: Russian Weightlifting Coaching Guidance

## Key Points

- **Periodized variation for hypertrophy**: Manipulate weight, volume, rep ranges, and tempos (pause squats, isometric holds, jump finishers) to shock the system within a structured block
- **Pre-tension protocol**: Always approach loaded positions with active back/core tension; avoid dynamic loading from relaxed positions (critical for back pain management)
- **Full range of motion emphasis**: Prioritize longer muscle lengths in single-leg work and squats over short-range variations; deep positions build mobility and strength capacity
- **Moderate loading for accessory work**: Use moderately heavy weights on technical variations to build confidence before heavy singles; jump squats and plyometric finishers use 50–60 kg range
- **Exercise selection for weak points**: Varied squat stimulus (pause, tempo, pin positions) directly addresses OHS limiter (50 kg) via upper back tension and stability

## Technique Cues

**Squat/Back Tension:**
- Narrower grip increases upper back tension (if mobility allows); elbows back slightly activates upper back
- Pre-tension approach: engage back and core before loading; never load from relaxed positions
- Grip width individually dependent; consistency in back tension is priority

**Pause Squats:**
- 3–5 second hold in bottom position (hypertrophy stimulus; addresses sticking points)
- Full range of motion; knees travel past toes

**Jump Squats (Finisher):**
- 3×6 reps, moderate weight (50–60 kg)
- Full foot contact throughout; no early rise to toes
- Complete knee extension at top; land on full foot
- Emphasis: maximize ground contact duration for force production

**Single-Leg Work (Scissors/Lunges):**
- Maintain upright torso; full range of motion (deep front knee bend)
- Front leg takes load; elbows bent
- Targets glutes and quads; twice-weekly frequency historically effective

## Programming Principles

- **Variation as stimulus**: Shock via weight, volume/reps, exercise selection, or tempo (not just intensity progression)
- **Hypertrophy block application**: Combine heavy-moderate loads, pause/tempo variations, and plyometric finishers over 6 weeks
- **Confidence building**: Use moderately heavy weights on technical variations before progressing to max singles
- **Belt/sleeve use**: Knee sleeves from warm-up for mobility; belt at 80–90%+ intensity only
- **Accessory frequency**: Tuesday/Saturday single-leg work; finishers after main lifts

## Athlete Relevance

**Directly applicable (6-week hypertrophy block):**
- Pause squats (3–5 sec holds) and jump squat finishers (50–60 kg) fit hypertrophy/confidence-building phase
- Upper back tension work addresses OHS limiter (50 kg); varied squat stimulus builds mobility + stability
- Scissors/lunges on lighter training days suit night-shift schedule (lower CNS demand)

**Critical for chronic back pain:**
- **Pre-tension protocol essential**: non-negotiable before any loaded squat position
- Avoid box squats from relaxed positions; prioritize full ROM with active stabilization
- Full-foot contact in jump squats reduces spinal shear stress
