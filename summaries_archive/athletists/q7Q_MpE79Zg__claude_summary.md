# Merged Weightlifting Summary

## Key Points
- **Post-competition periodization**: Heavy volume weeks following competition, avoiding maxes but emphasizing reps and volume
- **Back pain management**: Avoid high-load barbell good mornings (especially straight-leg); substitute bent-knee variants at high reps (15–20+) with light-moderate weight (~30–40% front squat)
- **Exercise complexity**: Clean/snatch from below-knee positions with jerk combinations develop technique and CNS capacity without maximal loading
- **Mobility constraint**: Wrist/shoulder flexibility insufficient for clean and OHS; tape support required for finger security

## Technique Cues

**Good Morning (Barbell Bent-Over Row)**
- Perform with bent knees, not straight-leg, to distribute load across hip joint and back
- Maintain upright torso; avoid excessive forward lean
- Safer for chronic lower back pain than straight-leg variant

**Snatch from Below Knee (with straps)**
- Lower bar nearly to platform; pause briefly and feel full foot contact
- Keep bar close to legs throughout; knees track outward slightly
- Maintain hip angle—avoid shifting forward to ease the pull
- Hold tension in bottom position; don't collapse or shift center of mass

**Clean from Below Knee + Jerk Combination**
- Use finger tape (not straps) for cleans to protect wrist/shoulder mobility at 80–90% loads
- Secure ring finger lock with tape support
- Emphasize controlled jerk drive and descent to floor touch

## Programming Principles
- 3-day post-competition split: two moderate-volume days, one heavy day (90% snatch + preceding volume + heavy clean)
- High-rep, moderate-weight accessory work prioritized over heavy back-loaded movements
- Below-knee variations appropriate for technique development before heavier loading phases

## Athlete Relevance
**Critical for this athlete:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Wrist/shoulder mobility insufficient—prioritize tape support and mobility drills
- **Chronic back pain**: Heavy good mornings contraindicated; use high-rep (20+) bent-knee variants instead
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Volume-emphasis programming matches stated goal
- **Clean/jerk at 80/65 kg**: Below-knee complexes and jerk pulls appropriate before heavier split-jerk progression
- **Night shift fatigue**: Assume volume-based work more sustainable than daily maximal efforts

**Not addressed**: Split jerk training progression, shift-work recovery strategy.
