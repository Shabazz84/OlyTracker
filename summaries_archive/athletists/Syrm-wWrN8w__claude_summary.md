# MERGED SUMMARY: Olympic Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## Key Points

- **Injury risk is highest on light weights**, not just maximal loads; vigilance required on all sets, especially warm-ups
- **Back pain management**: Belt usage at 80%+ recommended; secondary activities and cumulative fatigue often underestimated as injury causes
- **Training by feel within structure**: Adjust intensity/volume daily based on readiness; overtraining is common
- **4-week competition prep model**: Week 1 full volume → Week 2 slight reduction → Week 3 heavy singles → Week 4 deload
- **Grip strength development**: Use simple (non-hook) grip during eccentric phase; transition to straps only when grip limits bar position

## Technique Cues

**Snatch Pulls (floor + above knee):**
- Explosive triple extension finish ("dzinks")
- Simple grip for strength; straps acceptable once grip becomes limiting (~80kg unstrapped → 90kg strapped threshold)

**Snatch from Blocks:**
- Set below knee level
- Use straps to avoid repositioning delays
- Prioritize explosive finish

**Overhead Press from Low Squat:**
- Light weight for strict pressing if press << pull strength (e.g., 45kg pull, work 30-35kg press)
- Avoid heavy jerk-assisted reps on light weights
- Use leg drive only on heavier weights

**Segmented Pulls (4-rep protocol at ~110% max):**
1. Pull to mid-shin, lower
2. Pull above knee, lower
3. Full pull to catch position, lower
4. Full explosive pull
- Builds positional strength through range

## Programming Principles

- **Exercise combination strategy**: Mix pull variations (snatch pulls, blocks, segmented) with overhead work to prepare for competition lifts
- **Sets/Reps**: Snatch pulls ~6 sets per feel; snatch from blocks 2 reps at 85%
- **Daily adjustment**: Follow plan but modify based on athlete feel and readiness
- **Variability emphasis**: Change schemes, weights, and exercise order; 3x/week minimum frequency with adequate recovery days

## Athlete Relevance

**Direct application to your profile:**

- **Chronic back pain + night shift**: "Train by feel" philosophy is essential given sleep disruption and variable recovery. Back pain = belt at 80%+ and strict technique on ALL sets (even warm-ups). Your fatigue level will fluctuate Wed–Sun; adjust volume accordingly.

- **OHS limiter (50kg)**: Overhead press from low squat directly targets your primary snatch weakness. Prioritize this in your 6-week hypertrophy block.

- **Push jerk only**: Heavy overhead pressing emphasis aligns with your jerk technical development before split jerk introduction.

- **Intermediate level**: Segmented pulls and exercise combinations match your developmental stage better than max-effort singles.

- **Back pain caution**: Light-weight injuries are real (coach injured 70kg pull despite feeling fine). Expect soreness from unrelated activity (night shift fatigue, secondary movement patterns). Prioritize low-load technique work during weeks of high cumulative fatigue.
