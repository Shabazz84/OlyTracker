# Better Bracing: Expand Your Ribs Sideways

**Relevant to this athlete:**

**Breathing/Bracing Technique (High Priority - Chronic Back Pain)**
- Cue: Expand ribs **sideways** during inhalation with bar loaded
- Prevents excessive lumbar arch (critical given chronic back pain history)
- Maintains trunk tension while allowing adequate breath for bracing
- Avoid drawing abs inward simultaneously—this limits expansion

**Application:** Front rack position (clean/jerk setup) and back squat are immediate priorities. Sideways rib expansion is safer than chest/arch dominance for athlete managing back issues.
