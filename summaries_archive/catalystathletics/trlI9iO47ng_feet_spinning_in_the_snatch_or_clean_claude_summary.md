# Feet Spinning in the Snatch or Clean

# Analysis

## Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)

**Foot Stability Issue:**
- **Problem**: Feet spinning during pull indicates pressure release or heel/toe shift
- **Solution**: 
  1. Maintain even pressure across whole foot (balanced contact)
  2. Drive continuously into floor through entire pull—no backing off during transition to final acceleration
  3. Never relax pressure; this allows foot to lose ground contact and spin

**Application to Athlete**: Relevant for snatch work. At 50 kg OHS (snatch limiter), foot stability during pull could be contributing to technical breakdown. Worth diagnosing before increasing snatch volume in hypertrophy block.

## Relevance to Profile

- **Snatch technique focus**: Directly addresses a mechanical flaw that limits positions later in the lift
- **Not mobility-specific**: Addresses positioning/tension, not mobility (though chronic back pain may benefit from stability work elsewhere)

---

**Summary**: Practical technical cue on maintaining foot drive pressure to prevent foot spin during pulling phases.
