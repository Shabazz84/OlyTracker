## 2. **Technique Cues: Snatch & Clean**
**Foot Movement Timing:**
- Feet should move **only after peak extension** is completed
- Stop pushing floor once extension peaks—continuing creates resistance to downward acceleration
- Choice between lifting feet high vs. keeping them close both work; execution matters more than method
- **Common error:** lifting feet too early (sacrifices power at top of pull)

**Key principle:** Reduce floor pressure to accelerate downward under the bar. Timing > method choice.

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**

**DIRECTLY RELEVANT:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Foot transition efficiency under load critical for overhead position. Poor foot timing delays getting under weight, worsening overhead stability demands.
- **Push jerk only (no split):** Foot placement precision essential for stable receiving position; high foot lift might help vertical acceleration into jerk dip.
- **Back pain:** Focus on **finishing the pull** (not overshooting extension) reduces compensatory lower back strain. Poor foot timing often correlates with early lumbar flexion.

**Recommendation:** Apply this principle—perfect extension *before* moving feet—to all pulls to reinforce efficient positioning and reduce injury risk during the hypertrophy block.
