# Finish the Pull After Contact

# Analysis

## Technique Cue (Snatch/Clean)
**Bar contact timing & mechanics:**
- Contact occurs at ~vertical trunk with knees still bent—NOT end of extension
- Common error: pulling under prematurely upon feeling contact
- Cue: treat contact as *continuous upward slide* during ongoing triple extension, not a stopping point
- Think "push bar up body" via sustained leg drive & hip extension through top, not "pull under at contact"

## Relevance to This Athlete
**High priority for this athlete:**
- OHS 50 kg is primary snatch limiter → suggests weak receiving position/late pull mechanics
- This cue directly addresses premature pulling under, which prevents full extension height
- Improving extension completeness = taller pulls = easier to hit deeper OHS positions = addresses limiting factor
