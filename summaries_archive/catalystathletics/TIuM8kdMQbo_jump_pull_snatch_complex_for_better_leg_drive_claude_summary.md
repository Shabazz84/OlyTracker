# Jump-Pull-Snatch Complex for Better Leg Drive

**RELEVANT EXTRACTION:**

**Technique Cue (Snatch):**
- Athlete tendency: Cutting leg drive short in snatches
- Solution complex: Vertical jump → pole snatch extension → snatch
- Rationale: Vertical jump isolates explosive leg drive sensation; pole emphasizes legs over hips and upward bar acceleration; carryover to full snatch

**Exercise Recommendation:**
- Complex structure prioritizes leg drive engagement before technical snatch work

**Relevance to this athlete:** Directly applicable. OHS at 50kg (primary snatch limiter) suggests leg drive/extension deficiency may be constraining snatch development. This complex could address that mechanical gap before pursuing hypertrophy block.
