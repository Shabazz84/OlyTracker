# Don't Weaken Your Snatch Overhead with This Mistake

# Analysis

## Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:

**Technique Cue (Snatch/OHS):**
- Elbow position: aim for approximately **halfway between straight down and straight back**—not end-range internal or external rotation
- **Priority: fix shoulder blades first** to allow free arm movement and maximize stability
- Avoid rolling shoulders forward (common compensation for limited mobility)
- Test slight rotation variations while maintaining scapular position to find individual optimal stability

**Directly Relevant to Profile:**
- **Chronic back pain + low OHS (50 kg):** Poor shoulder stability overhead likely contributes to OHS limitation. Focus on scapular stability as prerequisite, not arm rotation alone
- **Shoulder mobility likely a limiter:** The text acknowledges athletes often feel "maximal internal rotation" due to mobility restrictions—test variations to find stable position without forcing rotation

**Application:**
Spend dedicated time in OHS holds/light OHS work prioritizing scapular positioning before progressing load. This addresses the stated primary snatch limiter directly.
