# Finish the Pull After Contact

1. **Programming principles**: None specified in text (focuses on movement mechanics, not volume/intensity).
2. **Technique cues**: Do not "quit pulling" or pull under immediately upon bar contact; treat contact as a sliding phase where the trunk is vertical and knees are still bent/forward, continuing leg drive and hip extension through the top.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specified (general Olympic lifting mechanics only).
4. **Recovery/mobility advice**: None specified in text.
5. **Profile relevance**: The cue to "continue sliding" rather than stopping at contact is critical for an athlete with chronic back pain, as it encourages maintaining leg drive and hip extension instead of prematurely rounding or hyperextending the lower back to pull under too early.
