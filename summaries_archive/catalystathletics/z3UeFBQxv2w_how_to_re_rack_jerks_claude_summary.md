# How to Re-Rack Jerks

**RELEVANT EXTRACTION:**

**Technique Cue (Jerk Re-racking):**
- Reduce bar height via arm/knee bend
- Stand tall on toes, bring shoulders to bar
- Guide bar down with arms to rack
- Absorb weight with knee bend
- Head back, set rack position
- Avoid catching bar in arms
- Maintain vertical, forcefully braced trunk

**Relevance to Athlete:**
Direct application for push jerk practice. Emphasizes controlled lowering and bracing—critical given chronic back pain. Safe re-racking mechanics reduce spinal load.

**Not addressed:** Snatch, clean, programming, or split jerk progression (athlete's stated limitation).
