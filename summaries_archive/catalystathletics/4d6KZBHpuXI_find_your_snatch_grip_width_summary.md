# Find Your Snatch Grip Width

1. **Programming principles**: None mentioned in this text; focuses solely on grip setup.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Grip width: Find position where bar sits in hip crease (soft tissue just above pubic bone).
   - Stability test: Lean over or lift a knee without the bar shifting or arms bending.
   - Contact point: Avoid direct contact with the pubic bone to prevent pain and loss of explosiveness; maximize bar-thigh interaction for stability.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None mentioned (specifically regarding grip setup only).
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned.
5. **Relevance to athlete profile**: 
   - **Chronic back pain**: The cue to avoid pubic bone contact is critical, as pain here can alter lifting mechanics and exacerbate compensatory strain on the lower back.
   - **Transitioning athlete**: Emphasizes that "unusual proportions" (common in new lifters) require individual adjustment rather than a rigid standard grip width.
