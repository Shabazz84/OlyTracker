# Push The Bar Back - Don't Pull It In

1. **Programming principles**: Not mentioned; text focuses on specific movement mechanics.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Keep bar close to legs without dragging.
   - Avoid "rowing" the bar up (excessive arm bend).
   - Cue: **"Push the bar back"** rather than pulling it in.
   - Action: Tighten lats and pull bar backward at its current height while keeping arms relatively relaxed to transmit leg/hip power.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None listed; text describes a technical adjustment for the pull phase of snatches/cleans.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: Not mentioned.
5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: 
   - The "push back" cue is critical for an intermediate athlete with chronic back pain to prevent excessive lumbar shear from aggressive arm pulling.
   - Emphasizing leg/hip power transmission helps compensate for the 6-week hypertrophy block's potential temporary strength dips before technique refinement begins.
