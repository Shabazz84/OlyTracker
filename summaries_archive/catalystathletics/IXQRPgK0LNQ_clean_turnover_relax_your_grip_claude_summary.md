# Clean Turnover - Relax Your Grip

# Weightlifting Analysis

## Relevant to Athlete's Profile

**Technique Cue – Clean Turnover:**
- Maintain solid grip through most of turnover for connection/control
- Relax grip and slide thumb out once elbows rise in front of shoulders
- Excessive grip tension slows turnover and limits elbow height
- Timing: relax after elbows begin coming up, not before

**Why relevant:** Athlete's Clean (80 kg) significantly exceeds Jerk (65 kg push only), suggesting turnover/rack position efficiency issues. Poor turnover mechanics may contribute to jerk limitations and back stress.
