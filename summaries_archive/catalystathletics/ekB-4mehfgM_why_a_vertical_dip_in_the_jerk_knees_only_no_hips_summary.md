**2. Technique cues for snatch, clean, or jerk**
*   **Jerk Dip:** Keep the trunk angle constant (do not hinge forward at the hips during the dip). The bar and hip joint must move vertically along parallel lines.
*   **Spine Position:** Maintain a neutral spine with natural lumbar curve; avoid flattening the lower back by tucking the pelvis or letting hips shift forward.
*   **Visual Check:** Watch the trunk angle, not just the hips/glutes (which can create an illusion of hinging).
*   **Power Transfer:** Hinging early misdirects force forward and reduces bar acceleration; keep the upper back rigid to prevent collapse.
