# Back Knee Bend in the Split Jerk

**Relevant to this athlete:**

**Technique cue (Jerk):**
- Back knee must stay bent (never straight) to: (1) absorb weight vertically, maintain balance, (2) reduce hip flexor tension that causes forward lean/lower back hyperextension
- Back knee position: behind hip
- Trunk: minimal forward lean (only for overhead position)
- Lower back: maintain neutral spine

**Relevance to profile:** Critical for this athlete with chronic back pain and push jerk limitation. Straight back leg hyperextension risk is a direct concern. Proper back knee bend + neutral spine = safer jerk mechanics and may allow faster split jerk transition (currently missing from toolkit).
