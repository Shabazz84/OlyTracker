# Upper Body is Just Extra to Keep the Bar Close

**TECHNIQUE CUE - JERK/PUSH JERK:**

1. **Bar path control**: Forward bar bounce stems from horizontal force, not vertical acceleration. Upper body damage control is limited.

2. **Root cause approach**: Fix the *pull* (primary bar path control), not compensatory upper body mechanics. The pull under is secondary/additive, not a substitute.

3. **Force direction**: Emphasize vertical acceleration in the pull phase to minimize horizontal force and prevent forward drift.

**RELEVANT TO ATHLETE:** This directly applies to his push jerk (65 kg). Before progressing or adding volume, diagnose if his jerk issues stem from poor pull mechanics rather than weak ankle mobility/split mechanics. May explain why he's stuck at 65 kg despite stronger squat/clean numbers.
