# The Year Plan for Olympic Weightlifting Training Programs

**PERIODIZATION & PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES**

- **Macrocycle structure**: Year broken into 12-week cycles (or shorter 6–8 week cycles for intermediate lifters with frequent local competitions)
- **For intermediate lifters**: Multiple competitions per year = multiple macrocycles; can peak for several meets annually
- **Macrocycle phases**:
  1. Post-comp: 8-week general strength/technique/capacity focus (high volume, variety)
  2. Competition prep: 12-week cycle → three 4-week mesocycles with progressive snatch/C&J emphasis
  3. Strength emphasis: 10–12 week blocks targeting weak points (e.g., leg strength)
  4. Final peak: 16-week build to primary competition
- **Transition week**: Lighter version of the *next* macrocycle (not previous), preps new movement patterns while deloading
- **Rest week ("ladies' choice")**: 2–3 gym days, easy, different, fun; aids physical/mental recovery

**TESTING**
- End of macrocycle testing weeks (squat, push press variations)
