# Reverse-Grip Low Cable Row | Olympic Weightlifting Exercise Library

**Relevance:** Not directly applicable to this athlete's Olympic weightlifting program.

**Exercise identified:** Cable row (seated or with hip hinge variation) — a horizontal pulling movement.

**Potential value for this athlete:**
- Back health: Could support chronic back pain management if programmed conservatively (light-moderate loads, controlled tempo, emphasis on scapular retraction without excessive lumbar extension)
- Posterior chain development: Useful accessory for pull strength, which can support clean technique and general resilience
- Night shift recovery: Low-impact option compatible with fatigue management

**Caveat:** Given chronic back pain + night shift fatigue, prioritize assessment of movement quality and loading before adding this. Rowing may aggravate back if lumbar extension dominates or fatigue compromises form. Consider back-friendly alternatives (prone-supported rows, band rows) first.
