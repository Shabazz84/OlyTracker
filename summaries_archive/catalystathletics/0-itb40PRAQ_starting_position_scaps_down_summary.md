# Starting Position: Scaps Down

1. **Programming principles**: None mentioned in this excerpt.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Pull shoulder blades down tight at the start and pull phase.
   - Use lats to extend the upper back and prevent bar swing forward.
   - Maintain tension to draw shoulder blades toward hips.
   - Do NOT retract or shrug; keep shoulders neutral or slightly protracted.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None mentioned in this excerpt.
4. **Recovery/mobility advice**: None mentioned in this excerpt.
5. **Profile relevance**: The cue regarding "shoulders slightly protracted" and avoiding a shrug is critical for your chronic back pain, as it promotes thoracic extension rather than lumbar rounding during the pull.
