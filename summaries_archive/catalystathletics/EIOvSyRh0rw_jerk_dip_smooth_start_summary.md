# Jerk Dip - Smooth Start

1. **Programming principles**: Not applicable; text focuses on single movement mechanics (jerk dip) rather than sets, reps, intensity, or periodization structures.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Initiate the jerk dip smoothly to maintain bar connection with the rack position; avoid abrupt starts that cause separation.
   - Grip should not be overly tight; arms must not push up into the bar before the dip.
   - Maintain tension in quads and glutes while standing straight (not hyperextended) to eliminate "slack" or free-fall time at the start of the dip.
