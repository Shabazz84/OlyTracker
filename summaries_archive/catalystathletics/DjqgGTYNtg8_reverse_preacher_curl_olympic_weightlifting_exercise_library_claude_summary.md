# Reverse Preacher Curl | Olympic Weightlifting Exercise Library

**Exercise Recommendation:**
- Preacher curls or bench-supported barbell curls (pronated grip) — arm hypertrophy/accessory work suitable for a hypertrophy block

**Relevance to athlete:**
- Low back-friendly (supported position) — appropriate given chronic back pain
- Useful arm/elbow prep or accessory during 6-week hypertrophy phase
- Minor relevance; not directly addressing snatch weakness (OHS limiter) or jerk split progression

**Overall:** Tangential accessory exercise. More directly useful for arm strength support than addressing primary technical/structural needs (OHS, split jerk development).
