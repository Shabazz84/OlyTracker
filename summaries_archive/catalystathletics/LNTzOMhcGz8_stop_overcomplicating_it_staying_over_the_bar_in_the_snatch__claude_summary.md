# Stop Overcomplicating It - Staying Over The Bar in the Snatch & Clean

# Analysis

## Programming Principles
None provided.

## Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)

**First Pull (Floor to Knee):**
- Maintain approximately the same back angle from start until bar clears knees
- Keep shoulders above or slightly in front of bar
- Shins not quite vertical; evenly balanced over whole foot
- Don't push knees back or lean shoulders forward—stay *over* the bar, not *farther* over it

**Common Errors:**
- Early hip extension/shoulder shift back → reduces leg extension and power
- Staying over too long → knees too extended, shoulders too far forward, bar slows, reduced leg drive

**Second Pull (Knee to Extension):**
- Begin opening hips only after bar passes knees
- This brings shoulders back naturally

## Exercise Recommendations

**Accessory Work** (for first pull positioning):
- Pull/deadlift variations with pauses (slow floor-to-knee)
- Slow eccentrics
- Snatch/clean pulls with pauses between floor and above-knee or slow first pull

## Athlete-Specific Relevance

**High relevance to OHS limiter (50 kg):** First pull positioning directly impacts receiving position stability. Clean/snatch pull work strengthens positioning for improved overhead control.

**Back pain consideration:** Maintaining neutral spine angle in first pull is protective; avoid excessive forward lean or early hip extension.
