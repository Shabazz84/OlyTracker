# My Elbows Can't Go That High for the Snatch!

**Extraction:**

**1. Technique Cues (Snatch):**
- Pull elbows up toward shoulders during snatch turnover
- Low elbows + bar swing out = common error
- High pull as diagnostic tool: if elbows rise higher in high pull than snatch turnover, the limitation is technical/strength-based, not structural

**2. Exercise Recommendations:**
- High pulls for snatch technique development and elbow positioning feedback

**3. Relevance to Athlete:**
- **Directly applicable**: OHS 50 kg (snatch limiter) suggests potential turnover/positioning issues. High pulls could help identify if athlete can achieve proper elbow height but fails to execute in snatch. Given intermediate status, this diagnostic approach is valuable before committing to extensive OHS work.
- Lower elbows may also relate to chronic back pain if compensating with forward lean; proper vertical pulling mechanics could improve positioning.
