# Snatch & Jerk Cue - Lock Out When Feet Hit

1. **Programming principles:** Not discussed; text focuses solely on a specific movement cue.
2. **Technique cues:** "Lock out at the same time the feet hit." Used to encourage faster turnover in snatch/clean and aggressive punching in the jerk. Note: This is an *effortful* cue, not a literal description (feet land first), but the intent should feel simultaneous.
3. **Exercise recommendations:** None listed; applies generally to Snatch, Clean, and Jerk mechanics.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice:** None mentioned.
5. **Athlete profile relevance:** The cue addresses the "aggressive turnover" needed for an intermediate athlete transitioning to Olympic lifts. However, it does not address the specific constraints of chronic back pain, night shift work, or the lack of split jerk technique (push jerk only).
