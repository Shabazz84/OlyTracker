# Improve Your Snatch Turnover

**RELEVANT CONTENT:**

**Technique Cues (Snatch):**
- Accelerate bar with leg drive
- Pull elbows up and out to keep bar close to body
- Squeeze back during turnover (keeps bar tight)
- Punch up forcefully into overhead lockout
- Coordinate foot pickup with turnover acceleration

**RELEVANCE TO ATHLETE:**
Highly relevant. OHS at 50 kg is stated limiter—these turnover/overhead positioning cues address that weakness. Back squeeze cue may help manage chronic back pain by promoting stability in overhead position. Emphasis on keeping bar close reduces shoulder mobility demands.

**LIMITATION:**
No programming structure, sets/reps, or recovery guidance provided. Text focuses narrowly on snatch turnover mechanics.
