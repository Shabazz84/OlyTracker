## 1. Programming Principles
- **Periodization approach**: Macrocycle → Mesocycle (3-5 weeks) → Microcycle (1 week)
- **Volume/Intensity progression**: Earlier mesocycles = higher volume/reps, lower intensity; later = lower volume, higher intensity, more specificity
- **Load prescription**: Transition from percentages to actual previous weights as athlete history accumulates (relevant for this athlete's 6-week hypertrophy block)
- **Program repetition**: If a cycle works, repeat it with increased weights (not same weights)
- **Adjustments within cycles**: Modify reps, sets, rest periods, or exercise variations based on athlete response
- **Training load tracking**: Volume × absolute weight (useful for monitoring recovery given night shift schedule)

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Emphasis on **exercise variations** (hang vs. block positions, power vs. full movements) to address specific weaknesses
- **Change accessory work regularly** for engagement and continued stimulus adaptation
- Adjustment strategy: keep primary lift stimulus, vary secondary lift mechanics
