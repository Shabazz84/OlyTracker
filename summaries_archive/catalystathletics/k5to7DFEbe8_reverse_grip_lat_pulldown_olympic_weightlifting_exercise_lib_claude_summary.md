# Reverse-Grip Lat Pulldown | Olympic Weightlifting Exercise Library

**Exercise Recommendation:**
- **Overhead Cable Row** (or similar vertical pulling variation)
  - Cues: Neutral spine, braced trunk, pull to collarbones, scapular retraction (down/back), controlled return
  - **Relevance to athlete:** Good accessory for thoracic stability and scapular health—useful given chronic back pain and low OHS (50 kg). Safer alternative to heavy barbell work on night shift fatigue.

**No periodization, technique (snatch/clean/jerk), or recovery guidance** provided in this text.
