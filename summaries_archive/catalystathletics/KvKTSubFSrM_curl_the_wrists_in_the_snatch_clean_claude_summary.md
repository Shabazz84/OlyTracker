# Curl The Wrists in the Snatch & Clean?

# Analysis

**Not primarily about weightlifting programming.** This is a technical breakdown of wrist mechanics in snatch/clean.

---

## Extracted Content:

**Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean):**
- **Wrist flexion is natural, not intentional** — occurs passively during pull-under and arm bending, not in pulls 1–2
- **Bar proximity** maintained via: posture + balance + continuous lat tension (not wrist curling)
- **Lat tension benefits:** keeps bar close AND locks trunk rigid for force transmission
- **Avoid intentional wrist flexion:** weakens chain, may reduce hip/leg force transfer to bar
- **Grip check:** If securing grip requires heavy wrist curl, grip is too deep in palm — reposition to natural wrist hang
