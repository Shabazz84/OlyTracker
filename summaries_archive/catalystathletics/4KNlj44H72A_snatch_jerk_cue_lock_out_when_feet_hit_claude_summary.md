# Snatch & Jerk Cue - Lock Out When Feet Hit

**Relevant Extracts:**

**1. Technique Cues:**
- **Snatch/Clean:** "Lock out at the same time the feet hit" — encourages faster, more aggressive turnover and rack position securing
- **Jerk:** "Punch" coordinated with foot landing
- **Key clarification:** Cue ≠ description; feet land first, but effort to synchronize lockout/punch with landing is the training stimulus

**2. Relevance to Athlete:**
- **High relevance to jerk weakness** (65 kg push jerk, needs split jerk): Aggressive punch timing is foundational; transitioning to split jerk requires this timing precision
- **Snatch technique (50 kg OHS limiter):** Faster turnover may help overhead position but won't directly address OHS strength deficit — still needs dedicated OHS volume
- **Back pain consideration:** Aggressive lockout timing with good foot timing may reduce compensatory lumbar stress vs. delayed/sloppy lockouts

**3. Not covered:** Sets/reps, intensity percentages, frequency, programming structure, or recovery specifics.
