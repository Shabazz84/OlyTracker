# Loosen Up Your Clean Rack With This Sequence

**RELEVANT TO ATHLETE:**

**Mobility/Recovery Advice:**
- Behind-the-neck rack mobility drill to improve rack position for cleans/front squats
- Progression: start narrow (clean grip), incrementally widen grip to end-range, return to clean grip
- Follow with controlled front squats emphasizing upright posture & high elbows
- **Directly applicable:** Athlete's weak OHS (50 kg) and chronic back pain suggest poor overhead position/thoracic mobility; this addresses shoulder/elbow positioning for cleaner rack positions that may reduce back strain

**Technique Cue:**
- Push shoulders/elbows forward while lifting elbows high (critical for stable rack position)
- Maintain upper back tightness and upright posture

**Integration:** Use as warm-up before clean/front squat work during hypertrophy block. May help establish better rack mechanics to support heavier cleaning as athlete progresses.
