# Overhead Mobility & Stability - Banded Air Slide on Roller

# Analysis

**Mobility/Recovery Advice:**
- T-spine foam rolling + banded wall slides (10-12 reps) for overhead mobility
- Progressive range: fingers off floor → touching floor
- Core bracing cue: maintain abdominal tension to prevent lumbar extension over roller
- Focus: shoulder opening

**Relevance to Athlete:**
- **High priority** — directly addresses OHS limitation (50 kg) and chronic back pain
- Wall slides safer than aggressive overhead stretching given back history
- Core tension protocol helps manage back issues during mobility work
- Overhead stability gains will carry to split jerk development (currently missing)

**Recommendation:** Integrate pre-lift, 2-3x weekly minimum. Essential prerequisite before addressing snatch/OHS weakness.
