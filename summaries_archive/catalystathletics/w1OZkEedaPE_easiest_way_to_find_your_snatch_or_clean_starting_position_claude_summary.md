# Easiest Way to Find Your Snatch or Clean Starting Position

**1. Programming Principles**
None explicitly stated.

**2. Technique Cues – Clean Starting Position**
- Deadlift light weight to top; descend slowly
- Below knee: find posture that keeps bar close to shins (no dragging)
- Balance sweet spot: hips not too high (bar swings away) / not too low (bar grinds shins)
- Maintain tension throughout descent
- Goal: even whole foot pressure

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Slow eccentric deadlifts as a diagnostic/positioning drill

**4. Recovery/Mobility Advice**
None.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
**Highly relevant.** This athlete's weak OHS (50 kg) and low clean (80 kg) suggest starting position issues. The slow descent drill addresses bar path precision and postural awareness—critical for cleaning efficiency. Given chronic back pain, the emphasis on finding neutral posture (not hyperextended or flexed) is protective. Recommend integrating this as a warm-up/prep work pre-clean sessions, especially during the hypertrophy block.
