## Key Points

- **Intensity formula cycling** (Funtikov-Bondarenko): Progress by varying load/rep schemes meaningfully week-to-week, not by tonnage alone. Example: 100 kg × 5 reps (~3,150 units) vs. 100 kg × 6 reps (~3,500 units) produce different stimulus despite similar tonnage.
- **Undulating periodization structure**: Progress tonnage weekly (12T → 15T → 17T) then deload (10T → 12T) before pushing toward peak (19T+).
- **Low-rep, heavy work > high-rep, light**: 5×5 @ 75% (~4,500 intensity units) produces far greater stimulus than 6×20 @ low load (~36 intensity units).
- **Don't repeat identical weight/reps across weeks** to avoid CNS fatigue and psychological plateau.

## Programming Principles

- Track **one fundamental lift per block** with systematic cycling protocols.
- Use **accessory exercises in stable rep ranges** while varying primary lift week-to-week.
- Sum weekly tonnage/intensity across all exercises to assess total load and stimulus.
- Apply intensity formulas to vary stimulus meaningfully—not just load progression.
