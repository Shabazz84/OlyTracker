# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## Key Points

- **Philosophy over dogma**: "Weightlifting is art…more than one way to skin a cat"—technique is individual, not formulaic
- **Max-effort approach**: Frequent heavy singles/doubles with small jumps (10–20 lb increments); add weight after misses
- **Total-focused mindset**: "Snatch what you can, clean & jerk what you must"
- **Session energy management**: Sugar/candy between snatch and clean-jerk for sustained performance across long sessions
- **Mental resilience matters**: Focus on effort over results; show no fear with loading

---

## Technique Cues

**Clean Pull (Ilia Llin model):**
- Long lever first pull, hips stay high at knee, hamstrings at stretch
- Continuous push into ground (almost like a squat)

**Clean Start:**
- "Superman pull"—avoid sitting too long in setup (overthinking risk)

**General Execution:**
- Early arm break cue: biceps flex early off floor in clean
- Individual variation exists; no single "correct" technique

---

## Programming Principles

- Heavy singles/doubles as primary work
- Frequent max-effort training (historical precedent: daily 5+ hour sessions)
- Nutrition strategy: carbs between lifts for energy maintenance
- Avoid overly restrictive programming that kills enjoyment
