### 2. Technique Cues (Focus: Jerk)
*   **Front Rack:** Push shoulders "up and forward" to create a platform; keep elbows slightly up, not pointing straight down.
*   **Dip Mechanics:** Keep hips directly over heels; knees move forward. Do not "sling shot" off the bottom; wait for the bar to flex back up (ride the whip) before driving.
*   **Drive:** Drive only to forehead height; maintain core tension and front rack position. Ensure the bar does not separate from shoulders until hips extend (prioritize legs over hands).
*   **Transition:** Execute "Tall Jerks" (dip, drive to forehead, punch under) to practice timing the catch at the peak of the bar's trajectory (weightless moment), rather than catching on the way down.
*   **Catch Position:** Hips must be square and directly underneath shoulders; equal bend in both legs (50% weight per foot); back glute active.
*   **Recovery:** Keep breath held (brace) until the bar is safely lowered; front leg returns first to keep hips moving straight up, not forward.

### 3. Exercise Recommendations
*   **Tall Jerk:** Specifically recommended for athletes struggling with transition timing and catching at the peak.
*   **Dumbbell Press:** Mentioned as a warm-up or accessory (though the speaker notes shaking from it).
*   **Singles/Priming:** Low volume, high-intensity singles (e.g., 80%) used for tapering.

### 4. Recovery or Mobility Advice
*   **Bracing:** Emphasizes maintaining intra-abdominal pressure ("brace") throughout the entire lift; do not exhale until the bar is down.
*   **Fatigue Management:** Warns against lifting daily when "back is stiff" or sore; suggests stopping if pain arises rather than pushing through.
