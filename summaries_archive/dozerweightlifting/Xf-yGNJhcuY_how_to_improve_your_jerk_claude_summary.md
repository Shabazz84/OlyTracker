## Jerk Technique Cues (Most Relevant - Athlete Only Push Jerks)
**Rack Position:**
- Shoulders up and forward to create lat tension and bar platform
- Grip width = personal preference; elbows slightly up (not pointing down)
- Bar on collarbone, not rolling below

**Pressure Point:**
- Ball of foot (shoelace line) preferred over heels
- Bar stays directly above pressure point throughout

**Dip:**
- Minimal emphasis (gains <1%)
- Keep bar on pressure point; hips back slightly, knees forward (mirrors snatch/clean power position)
- Wait for bar flex at bottom before driving—don't slingshot into downward momentum

**Drive:**
- Maintain core tension and front rack; pop bar to forehead height (~4 ft)
- **Critical**: Bar must NOT separate from shoulders before hip extension
- If hand-pressing occurs = loss of power/kilos; relax fingertips in dip if needed
- Drive bar to peak height so catch occurs at weightless moment (not on descent)

**Catch (Split Position):**
- Hips square, facing forward (active back glute)
- Hips under shoulders (no forward lean = unstable shoulder joint)
- Equal bend in both legs = 50/50 weight distribution
- **Recovery**: Keep breath in until bar clears hands; front leg back first (hips rise straight up, not forward)

**General**: Maintain brace throughout entire lift until bar is secured/released
