# 3 MISTAKES YOU'RE MAKING ON JERKS

# Analysis: Jerk Technique—Common Bad Habits

## Technique Cues (Jerk-Specific)

**1. Weight Shift in Dip & Drive**
- **Problem:** "Heels" cue causes backward weight shift, forcing forward bar drift during dip
- **Fix:** Load weight into forefoot/front-half of foot *before* dipping (not toes, full forefoot). Bar stays directly over this spot → straight vertical drive
- **Coaching:** Imagine loading power zone before dip begins; bar should not shift forward during descent

**2. Early Arm/Hand Activation**
- **Problem:** Pressing with arms before hips/knees lock out → reduced leg drive, press-outs, insufficient bar height
- **Fix:** Bar must stay on shoulders until hips/knees fully extend. Check video: if bar separates from shoulders before lockout, you're pressing early
- **Cue:** Relax hand tension slightly in front rack (don't open hands) so bar naturally sits on shoulders; maintain until hip extension completes

**3. Short-Stepping in Split**
- **Problem:** Front knee travels over toes (unstable); weight overloads front leg (70%+)
- **Fix:** 50/50 weight distribution between legs. Front knee stays on ankle or slightly behind. Hips drop straight down; feet move equal distance
- **Drill:** Pause jerks (3 sec) at catch up to 85% to feel weight distribution. Film from side
- **Cue:** "Slap" front foot down (heel first contact → full foot extends) for longer split; same knee bend in both legs
