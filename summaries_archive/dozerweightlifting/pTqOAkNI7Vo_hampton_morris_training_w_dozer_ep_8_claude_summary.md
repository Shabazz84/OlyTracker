# HAMPTON MORRIS - Training w/ Dozer Ep. #8

## Key Points

- **Recovery modality:** Dry needling (weekly) + sauna (4–5x/week, 20–40 min) are sustainable interventions; prioritize over expensive infrastructure (hyperbaric chamber, cold plunge)
- **Caffeine protocol:** Cold brew on heavy days; cutoff 2 PM (12 PM pre-heavy session) to avoid sleep disruption on night shift
- **Training frequency:** 6 days/week aligns with night shift (Wed–Sun); designate Thursday as recovery-focused
- **Rest between sets:** 1 min (light), +30 sec (complexes), 2–2.5 min (heavy lifts)
- **Nutrition timing:** Carbs + protein during training; eat immediately post-session
- **Accessory work:** RDLs, band posterior chain work, sandbag marches (hip flexor activation)

## Programming Principles

- Thursday designated recovery day (PT + sauna, lighter volume)
- Wednesday/Saturday heavy training days
- 6-day weekly structure compatible with night shift schedule
- Rest prescription scales with load intensity
