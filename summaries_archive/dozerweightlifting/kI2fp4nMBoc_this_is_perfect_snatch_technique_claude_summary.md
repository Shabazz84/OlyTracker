## Relevance to Athlete Profile

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Post-competition fatigue management**: After peak meets, expect 1-2 weeks of apparent strength loss due to adrenal fatigue, not deconditioning. Solution: prioritize sleep (10+ hrs/night) over pushing intensity immediately.
- **Volume/intensity shifts**: High volume → low volume/high intensity creates a "break-in week" before adaptation. Trust the process rather than panic.
- **Periodization reality**: Linear progression is impossible. Expect undulation: peaks, dips, recovery, repeat.

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
**Snatch:**
- **Stay connected to the bar throughout**: Most critical cue. No lag time between contact and catch—maintain tension until catching position (Jake exemplifies this).
- **Quad-driven pull from floor**: Cue = "keep hips down, push with quads, stop pulling with back/glutes."
- **Hip external rotation mobility critical**: One coach noted external rotation asymmetry causes hip internal rotation at bottom, creating positional dumping and compensation.
