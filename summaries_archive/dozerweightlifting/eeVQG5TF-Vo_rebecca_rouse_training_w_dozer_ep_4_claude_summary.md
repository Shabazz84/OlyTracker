# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis for Intermediate Athlete

## Key Points

**Source Limitation:** The provided transcripts are primarily lifestyle/interview content from an elite female weightlifter (~95kg snatch, ~116kg C&J). Direct coaching applicability to your intermediate profile is minimal.

**Actionable Recovery Protocols (Night Shift + Chronic Back Pain):**
- Target 8–9 hours consistent sleep; prioritize sleep quality over quantity
- Evening routine: blue light glasses post-sunset, dimmed lighting, phone-free 60 minutes pre-bed
- Sauna use supports thermal stress recovery (nearly nightly feasible for some athletes)
- Ice bath caution: 1×/week maximum; overuse (2×/day) causes negative adaptation
- **Training frequency sweet spot: 5 days/week** balances stimulus and recovery for older/recovery-limited athletes

**Psychological Resilience:**
- Compartmentalize bad sessions; don't catastrophize weekly variability
- Practice misses strategically (competition resilience) without habitually drilling failure
- Avoid over-reliance on external stimuli (music, social cues) for motivation
