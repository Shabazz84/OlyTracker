# Heavy Snatch and Clean and Jerk  (Ft. Zack Telander)

**Not about coaching the specified athlete.**

This is a casual gym vlog/product promotion transcript. While it contains general weightlifting discussion (snatch/clean & jerk technique cues, squat programming philosophy, power clean myths), it's not directed at or analyzing the intermediate athlete profile provided.

**Potentially relevant general principles extracted:**
- Snatch/clean/jerk require specific practice (not substitutable by squat strength alone)
- Low-rep speed work (5×5) preferred over high-volume sets of 10 for strength carryover
- Arm engagement in snatch: "feel the weight in your hands" + guide bar after leg drive
- Jerk: absorb bar in dip before jerking (sign of good jerker)
- Power clean ≠ snatch (typically 85–90% relationship, not 1:1)

**Not applicable to athlete:** Back pain, night shift, OHS limiter, split jerk deficiency, or hypertrophy block programming are not addressed.
