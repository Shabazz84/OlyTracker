# BRANDON VICTORIAN - Training w/ Dozer Ep. #5

# Analysis: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

**Not directly applicable to this athlete's profile.** This is a podcast/interview transcript between a coach and an elite weightlifter (Brandon Victorian, 89kg class, ~180kg snatch, 205kg power jerk), not a coaching plan or technique article.

## Marginally Relevant Excerpts:

**Programming Principles:**
- Brandon uses 5 days/week (Mon–Wed, Fri–Sat); emphasizes "leash on program" rather than maxing frequently
- Warm-ups: 10 mins for tech work, ~3 hours total if working to heavy singles; smaller jumps for snatch, bigger jumps (10kg) for clean/jerk after snatch fatigue
- Avoids two-a-days; views them as unnecessary unless splitting one session into morning accessory + evening lift

**Technique Notes:**
- Strap use: Brandon only restricts straps near competition; uses them freely otherwise (mental tool for focus)
- Clean issue: "bar moves fast, gets high, but mind doesn't believe in getting under" — addressed via heavy block clean cycles before Worlds
- Snatch mentality: "Doesn't think, just walks up and hits it" — competition mindset essential

**Recovery/Context:**
- Travel impact: 15-16 hour journey to Bahrain; arrived early, trained 9am next day at 85-90% (felt good)
- Hot gyms: Brandon trains better in heat (105°F+)
