# Analysis: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Frequency**: High-frequency snatching (daily for ~1 month) with minimal volume per session—intentionally avoiding overwork to manage fatigue
- **Intensity**: Submaximal singles (122 kg snatch) to accumulate stimulus without CNS burnout
- **Structure**: Snatch → Clean technique work → Jerk (planned in next session)
- **Philosophy**: Bare minimum effective dose when training frequently

## 2. Technique Cues: Clean (Primary Focus)

**Rack Position Mobility**
- Root cause of tight rack position: restricted thoracic spine (likely from prolonged sitting)
- Solution: Clean-grip SSB press, 2sec pause top, 3×5 to open thoracic mobility before cleaning

**Tall Clean**
- Keep back braced (shoulders lifted, pulled back, then pushed down)
- Elbows stay **above the bar until it passes chest**—then rotate
- Jump under once bar passes belly button
- Catch with elbows "really really high"

**Pull Mechanics (Floor to Power Position)**
- **Critical error to avoid**: Standing up *then* making contact with bar → leads to arm yanking, bar slamming, pain
- **Correct approach**: Pull from floor with **deep knee bend at contact**—allows legs to pump/accelerate the bar
- Dynamic start (from Ilia Lipai): Hips down-forward "swoop" → sets core into quads → perfect quad-dominant pull-off

**Power Position**
- Feet must remain **flat** (not on toes early—bar drifts forward ~3 inches if premature)
- Shoulders directly over bar
- Then: punch legs + pull under

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Clean-grip SSB press (3×5, 2sec pause) for thoracic mobility + rack position prep
- Tall cleans (technical proficiency before full cleans)
- Full cleans from floor with emphasis on proper knee bend at contact

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- Thoracic spine mobility critical (especially for desk/phone workers)
- SSB press with pause identified as effective intervention
- Supplement mention: Gorilla Mode pre-workout (avoid within 2 weeks of competition)
