# Merged Summary: Aaron Williams Training Analysis

- **Elite vs. intermediate context:** Aaron Williams (230+ kg C&J, 300 kg back squat) operates at a vastly different level; principles apply selectively, not numbers.

## Technique Cues

- **Core stability overhead:** Heavy C&J depends on exceptional bracing timing and minimal oscillation before re-dip; this applies to your athlete's push jerk development.
- **Split jerk absent:** Both Aaron and your athlete use power jerk only—split mechanics not discussed but remain a future progression option.

## Programming Principles

- **Warm-up structure:** Bar → 70 → 120 → 140 kg for snatch, then first working set; rest 2–3 minutes between sets (heart rate recovery without distraction).
- **Rep-based training:** Training with partners on rep schemes (not constant max attempts) improves consistency.
- **Top-end strength diminishing returns:** Once moving heavy loads fast, maxing above that provides minimal Olympic lifting carryover.
- **Volume varies by competition proximity:** Post-competition emphasizes technique; intensity phases emphasize strength.
