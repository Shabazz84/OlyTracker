## Relevant Extracts:

**Technique Cues (Clean):**
1. **Arm Bend** – Don't add arm bend during pull; maintain consistent bend from start through contact. Keep back braced.
2. **Pull-off Floor** – Shoulders over bar, weight flat through feet, big breath/locked core, drive quads into ground (not hips up first)
3. **Transition/Catch** – Elbows stay UP until past chest, then flip under. Keep knuckles DOWN (passive hands). Avoid early elbow drop (causes bar crash forward)
4. **Drill:** Tall cleans to reinforce elbows-up pattern

**Exercise Recommendation:**
- 4-week squat program (referenced as prerequisite for clean improvement, especially if leg strength is limiting)
