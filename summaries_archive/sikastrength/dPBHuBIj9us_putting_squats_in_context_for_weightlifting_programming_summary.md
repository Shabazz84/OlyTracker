# Analysis: Wendell's Squat Programming in Olympic Weightlifting

## Programming Principles
- **Squat frequency**: 2x/week sufficient for dedicated back squat strength
- **Intensity/Volume manipulation**: Ranges from very low intensity + high volume → higher intensity + lower volume, adjusted across training cycle
- **Snatch/Clean volume is largely irrelevant** to squat fatigue—treat as submaximal leg work; disregard for leg volume calculations
- **One-way street**: Heavy snatch/clean/jerk sessions require backing off squat volume that week; light snatch/clean sessions don't justify reducing squat work
- **Context-dependent peaking**: If squat reaches peak (week 2 of block example), maintain it at maintenance level while snatch/clean/jerk continue building; never restart heavy squat volume late in a block
- **Ratio example**: C&J 150 kg → need ~190 kg back squat minimum; ~170 kg front squat target

## Key Principle for This Athlete
**You should NOT reduce squatting because you're doing snatches/cleans.** Your submaximal Olympic lifting doesn't compromise squat strength gains. However, if you peak squats early in your 6-week hypertrophy block, maintain that level rather than reintroducing heavy volume later when snatch/clean/jerk work is intensifying.

## Relevant to Your Profile
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Adequate squat strength (118 kg BS) isn't the constraint—mobility/technique drives OHS deficit
- **Back pain + night shift**: Programming flexibility matters; 2x squat frequency should be manageable
- **Push jerk only**: Front squat work supports jerk strength directly
