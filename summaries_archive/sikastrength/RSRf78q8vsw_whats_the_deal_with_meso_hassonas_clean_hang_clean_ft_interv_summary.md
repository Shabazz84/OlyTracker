# Merged Summary: Clean Technical Development & Programming

## Key Points

- **Third pull control is your primary technical need**: Pull bar actively toward chest with knuckles down; avoid early turnover and bar crash. This is the weakest point for most intermediates and likely limits your split jerk progression (80 kg clean vs. 65 kg jerk gap).
- **Hand speed/turnover is the limiting factor** for jerk development; fast turnover builds confidence under heavy load.
- **Strong legs provide power for jerks and save cleans**; your back pain may benefit from deliberate squat positioning work.
- **Identify weak points each cycle** (legs vs. technique vs. hand speed) and address them separately before returning to full lifts.
- **6-week hypertrophy block is ideal timing** for technical refinement before strength phase.

## Technique Cues

**Clean progression (teaching order):**
- Repeatable start position: pause ~1 sec, tighten upper/mid-back aggressively
- Shoulder shrugs under bar (standing) → blocks → reduced height → floor (minimizes bar path errors early)
- Bar path: straight line up body; move knees back initially; keep torso upright around bar
- Second pull: accelerate bar upward (not forward); avoid excessive hip push
- Third pull: active pull toward chest with knuckles down; fast hand rotation critical

## Programming Principles

- **Touch-and-go hang power cleans**: 5–6 sets × 3 reps, light load (50–70 kg for you), 1–2×/week during new blocks—NOT near competition
- **Accessory logic**: Clean pulls, hand/heaving hand cleans, block work for acceleration/turnover without full ROM
- **Complexes** (clean + squat + jerk) for positional work and competition resilience
- Upper body strength work improves clean/snatch quality; manageable accessory volume fits night shift fatigue
- Master technique before loading; avoid comparing your style to others

## Athlete Relevance

- **OHS weakness (50 kg)**: Upper body strength work historically improves snatch quality and overhead position confidence
- **Back pain + night shift**: Light touch-and-go cleans are manageable; strong leg positioning during squats may alleviate chronic back stress
- **Push jerk only (65 kg)**: Emphasis on hand speed and upper body work aligns with jerk development before split jerk introduction
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Ideal for building upper body stability and leg strength while refining clean/jerk technique
- **Clean 80 kg vs. jerk 65 kg gap**: Turnover speed is the limiting factor; prioritize third pull + hand rotation drills
