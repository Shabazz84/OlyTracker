## Relevant Extracts for Your Athlete

### 1. **Technique Cues (CRITICAL for this athlete)**

**Start Position:**
- Maintain maximum back extension (upper + lower) from setup
- Don't relax into a rounded or flat back—actively create tension/arch
- Back extension enables powerful hip extension in the pull and catch

**Catch Position:**
- Hips *slightly behind heels* (not maximally forward)
- Maintain lower back extension through catch—keeps hips positioned correctly and eases bounce entry
- Upper back stays extended—prevents elbow/bar crash; maintains rack position

**Why it matters:** Rounded/flexed back = limited hip extension capacity + harder bounce catch + elbows drop.

---

### 2. **Exercise Recommendations**

**High-frequency front squatting** (multiple sessions/week) trains postural muscles isometrically in the extended-back position.

**Back extension work:**
- Lower back extensions (45°, prone)
- Stiff-leg deadlifts (RDLs) — mimic knee-drive position of clean
- Pulls: varied grip (clean-grip, snatch-grip), horizontal pulls, weighted pull-ups

**Mobility (often overlooked):**
- Thoracic spine extension/flexion
- Lat/external rotator releases
- Glute/hamstring lengthening (restriction here pulls hips under, losing back extension)
- Peanut ball work for lower back mobility

### 3. **Direct Application to Your Profile**

| Issue | Relevance |
|-------|-----------|
| **Chronic back pain** | Mobility + strength work essential; avoid forcing extension if pain present; consider physio assessment |
| **OHS limiter (50kg)** | Back extension + thoracic mobility directly improve overhead stability |
| **Clean (80kg)** | Back position is the bottleneck—address this before heavy clean-pulls |
| **Push jerk only** | Back extension strength improves power availability; front squat frequency helps |
| **Night shift recovery** | High-frequency front squatting is fatiguing; balance with mobility/recovery work |

### 4. **For Your 6-Week Hypertrophy Block**

- Add high-frequency front squatting (if tolerated by back)
- Emphasize back extension/pull variations
- Integrate thoracic + posterior chain mobility daily
- Monitor back pain—don't force positions
