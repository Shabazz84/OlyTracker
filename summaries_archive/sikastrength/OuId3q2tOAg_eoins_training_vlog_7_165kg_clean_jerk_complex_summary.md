# Analysis: Weightlifting Training Vlog

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Wave loading** for snatches (multiple waves per session; neurological potentiation benefit noted for experienced lifters)
- **Volume distribution**: 15–25 reps per lift across 1–1.5 weeks as intensity increases
- **Structure**: 2 volume squat sessions → 1 heavier day (10-day cycle)
- **Front squat prioritized** as primary leg strength driver
- **Clean front squat jerk complex** used to build volume/weight without maxing singles
- **Rationale**: Maintains jerk skill practice weekly without excessive loading

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Jerk**: Fast turnaround, explosive drive phase
- **Jerk (post-clean issue)**: Right heel drifting, bar pushing away at top — emphasize ground contact and heel stability
- **General**: Arch position before jerk should be neutral/straight, not over-arched

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Clean front squat jerk complexes (volume + skill)
- Clean deadlifts (220 kg, 2×3; upper back tension noted)
- Rack jerks (progression toward 205 kg goal)
- Power cleans during the week
