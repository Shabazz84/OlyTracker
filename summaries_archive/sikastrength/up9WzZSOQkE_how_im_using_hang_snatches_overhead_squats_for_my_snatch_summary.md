# Weightlifting Analysis

## Programming Principles
- **Variation selection for specific weaknesses**: Hang snatches (above knee) to address slow second pull; strict overhead squats to address weak/loose bottom position
- **Intensity over volume**: Working heavy singles/doubles rather than higher volume; athlete notes being in good fitness shape, needs heavier weights not more reps
- **Neurological/skill effect**: Pairing movements (power snatch + full snatch before overhead squat) to enhance lockout strength

## Technique Cues – Snatch
1. **Second pull**: Keep shoulders in front of barbell at above-knee position; avoid aggressive early shoulder retraction
2. **Hip drive**: Don't push hips into barbell too aggressively or lean away—maintain center of mass close to bar for power expression
3. **Bottom position**: Need stronger, more powerful lockout; currently "too loose" in receiving position

## Exercise Recommendations
- **Hang snatches above the knee** (worked up to heavy single)
- **Strict overhead squat** (worked up to heavy single/double; coach paired with power snatch + full snatch before OHS)
