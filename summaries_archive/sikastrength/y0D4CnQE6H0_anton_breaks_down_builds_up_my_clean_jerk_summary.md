## 2. Technique Cues (Clean & Jerk)

**Clean:**
- Start position: **Vertical shin angle, barbell over midfoot, chest up, strong arch (upper & lower back)**
- Knees: Keep back (avoid knees forward—forces barbell looping)
- Pull: **Shorter, faster extension** ("Ukrainian style"); avoid early arm pull; keep shoulders forward through transition
- Common fault: Knees drifting forward → barbell loops → compensation (shoulders back)
- Cue: "Don't pull with arms too early—load legs and back"

**Behind-the-Neck Split Jerk:**
- **Grip width**: Wider grip = more open chest (avoid narrow grip)
- **Stance**: Slightly wider than typical; toes straight ahead (avoid external rotation)
- **Common fault**: Hips/knees drifting forward → lower back butt-wink
- **Dip-drive**: Deeper dip, faster drive, full vertical extension
- **Advanced cue**: Use bar whip on heavier weights (feels like oscillation timing with the plates)
