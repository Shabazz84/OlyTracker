# Weightlifting Coaches React To 2016 European 85kg Weightlifting

## Key Points
- **Morphology consideration:** Lifters with long limbs relative to torso length struggle with overhead positions (directly relevant to your 50 kg OHS limitation).
- **Programming approach:** Romanian-style high-frequency snatch and clean & jerk (rather than variation-heavy Russian approach) may be more effective at intermediate level.
- **Contact quality:** Snatch contact is important but excessive contact reduces efficiency.
- **Consistency drives performance:** High-frequency dedicated practice (e.g., C&J 3x weekly for years) correlates with competition success.
- **Jerk mechanics:** Narrow wrist grip in catch, widens overhead at full extension.

## Technique Cues
- **Snatch/clean pulls:** Knees and barbell must move back off the floor together—avoid forward drift.
- **Jerk catch:** Narrow wrist position; prepare to widen grip overhead.
- **OHS position:** Your primary limiter likely compounds body morphology—requires targeted mobility work (not covered in source).

## Programming Principles
- Favor high-frequency, compound-focused training over excessive variation at intermediate level.
- Consistent weekly structure (fixed snatch/C&J days) supports adaptation and competition readiness.
- Front squat development less critical than efficient cleaning/jerking mechanics for total performance.

## Athlete Relevance
**Applicable:** Body morphology insight explains OHS struggles; high-frequency snatch/C&J approach supports hypertrophy block + subsequent technique work.

**Not addressed (seek structured coaching):** Back pain management during increased volume, split jerk progression (essential—your push jerk ceiling is limiting total), OHS-specific mobility, night shift recovery optimization.
