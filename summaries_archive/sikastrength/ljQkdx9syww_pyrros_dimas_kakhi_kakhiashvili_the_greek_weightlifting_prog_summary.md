# Analysis: Christos Yakovos Greek Weightlifting System

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES

**Template: Mon/Wed/Fri + Tue/Sat (5 sessions/week)**

**Mon/Wed/Fri structure:**
- Snatch: 50-80% (2 reps) → 90%, 95%, 100% (singles) → 85% (2 reps) → advanced: 95%, 100%
- Front squat: 90%, 95%, 100%, 85% → advanced: 95%, 100%
- Muscle snatch/clean: build to 100%
- Snatch/clean pulls: 6×2@110% or 4×3

**Tue/Sat structure:**
- Back squat: 90%, 95%, 100%, 85% → 95%, 100%, 85% (2×2)
- Muscle snatch: build to 100%
- Muscle clean: build to 100%
- Jerk from rack: build to 100%

**Key principles:**
- Frequent heavy attempts (3-5x/week snatches, cleans)
- Miss attempts: retry 3-4x even if failed (nervous system adaptation)
- 20-30min breaks between main exercises
- Dynamic max: next day's %s based on previous day's best attempt
- **This is a peaking/competition phase template** (assumes 8-10+ years training base)
- Not recommended for natural/unenhanced lifters except experienced athletes

## 2. TECHNIQUE CUES

**Muscle snatch/clean emphasis:**
- No-contact variations prioritized (not typical muscle snatch)
- Purpose: reinforce high elbows, delayed turnover, smooth bar path
- Keep knuckles down, elbows high as long as possible during turnover
- Avoid aggressive hip contact (causes early turnover)
- Use strategically before snatching/cleaning in warm-up (3-5 sets, light)

**Contact vs. no-contact:**
- No-contact muscle snatch: prevents "turning too fast" and loss of acceleration
- Contact muscle clean: teaches smoother, higher-elbow contact pattern
- High elbow priority > aggressive hip drive

**Coaching philosophy (Yakovos):**
- Observation first; change ONE thing at a time
- Example: elbow position, wrist angle
- Assess readiness (posture, fatigue) before heavy attempts
- "One sentence can change your whole world"

## 3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS

**For this athlete specifically:**

**CAUTION - NOT SUITABLE YET:**
- This Greek/Bulgarian peaking system requires 8-10+ years of structured training
- You're intermediate (~2-3 years equiv.), on night shift, with chronic back pain
- **Recommendation:** Use Russian/Soviet periodization model instead (higher volume phases → shorter taper)

**Applicable elements:**
- **Muscle snatches/cleans** (light, 3-5 sets): Directly addresses your OHS limiter (50 kg) by building turnover speed and elbow position before heavy snatching
- **Front squat emphasis**: Transfers to both squat and jerk; safer for chronic back pain than heavy back squats
- Front squat > back squat for you (given back pain + weak OHS)
- **Frequent light no-contact snatch work**: Builds technical proficiency without load stress

**Avoid:**
- This 5x/week heavy template (recovery risk with night shift)
- Maxing out 3-4x/session (unnecessary risk for intermediate)
- Jerk-from-rack without split jerk: You need split jerk technique first (push jerk won't scale to your BS/clean ratio)
