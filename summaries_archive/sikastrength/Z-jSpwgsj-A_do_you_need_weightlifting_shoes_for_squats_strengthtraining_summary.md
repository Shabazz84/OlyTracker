# Do You NEED Weightlifting Shoes For Squats? #strengthtraining

**Relevant to this athlete:**

**Equipment Recommendation:**
- Invest in proper weightlifting shoes for squatting. Critical for this athlete since OHS (50 kg) is their primary snatch limiter—shoes improve ankle dorsiflexion and stability, directly supporting overhead position and squat depth.

**Programming Note:**
- Equipment (shoes, sleeves, belt) enables heavier loading and strength gains. This athlete should use lifting shoes during the 6-week hypertrophy block and beyond to maximize squat development, which transfers to clean/jerk power.

**Not directly addressed:** Back pain management, night shift recovery strategies, or split jerk progression.
