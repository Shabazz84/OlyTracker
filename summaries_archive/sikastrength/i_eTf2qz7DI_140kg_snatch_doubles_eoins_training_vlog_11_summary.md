## Technique Cues

**Snatch:**
- When technique feels "off" but unidentifiable: pull faster to self-correct
- Issue noted: shoulders drifting behind bar

**Clean & Jerk:**
- **Pull emphasis**: Pull fast in first pull, *faster* in second pull
- Avoid bar contact/deceleration—focus on bar velocity maintenance
- Don't "smash the bar" (bar contact slows it down)
- **Jerk**: Drive bar straight up; avoid cutting drive short
- Be *active under the bar* while maintaining high pull velocity

- **Clean deadlifts** (unbelted) for upper back strength and chronic back tolerance
