# Weightlifting Analysis: Force-Velocity Curve Discussion

## Relevant to Athlete Profile

**Programming Principles:**
- Amateur lifters typically overspend on high-force training (heavy pulls, heavy squats) and underspend on velocity/power work
- Recommendation: Balance force adequacy (~20% above minimum needed) with velocity emphasis
- Avoid maximum strength work (near-max deadlifts) — negatively impacts speed via fiber type shift (Type 2X → 2A)
- Prioritize moderate-heavy loads moved fast over maximal loads

**Technique/Position Notes:**
- Elite lifters show sharp, fast "third pull" (getting under bar) — this velocity is trainable and increases from beginner→amateur→elite
- Snatch/clean acceleration phase force-velocity relationship predicts 1RM (elite lifters only currently)
- Amateur lifters often "over-pull" — excessive force production at top without adequate fast pull-under velocity

**Key for This Athlete:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)** + **push jerk only** = likely speed/coordination issue under load, not pure force
- Night shift + chronic back pain = avoid heavy maximal work; prioritize moderate loads + velocity emphasis
- 6-week hypertrophy block should include velocity work (jumps, fast pulls at submaximal weight), not just heavy slow work
- Front squat/back squat increases may plateau snatch/C&J if force already adequate; focus technique + speed instead
