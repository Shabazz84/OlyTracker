# Weightlifting Analysis

**Programming Principles:**
- German system emphasizes aggressive linear progression through 5-4-3-2-1 rep schemes across exercises without load changes over 10-12 week blocks
- Higher rep work (10RM) used periodically; not common but viable
- Japanese coaches employ higher rep training on occasion

**Relevant to Athlete Profile:**
- **10RM work** could support hypertrophy block goal (6-week phase planned)
- **Linear progression without load increases** may suit night shift worker (Wed–Sun) — simpler recovery management
- **Caution:** Aggressive linear progression + chronic back pain requires careful exercise selection and load monitoring; avoid excessive volume on posterior chain early
