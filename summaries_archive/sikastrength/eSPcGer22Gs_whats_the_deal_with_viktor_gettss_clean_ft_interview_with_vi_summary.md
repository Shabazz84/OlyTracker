# MERGED SUMMARY: Victor Getse Clean Technique & Programming

## Key Points

- **Clean frequency & variation strategy**: 3x/week heavy C&J (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2x/week lighter variations (hang clean, no-foot clean, power clean). Competition prep intensifies to 3–4x/week with maintained intensity variation year-round.
- **Low-rep, speed-focused training**: Never maximum effort all year; emphasis on bar speed and position over absolute load.
- **Two-phase pull with equal emphasis**: Heavy first pull (not "fast floor, faster second pull"); works when leg strength is the limiter vs. pulling capacity.
- **Fast under the bar**: Compensates for lower pulling power; critical for athletes with squat-to-clean ratio gaps.
- **Efficient catch**: Barbell reaches ~50–55% of lifter's height; avoid over-pulling and riding down.
- **Start position variability**: Customizable (aggressive vs. methodical); experiment given transition phase and back pain.

---

## Technique Cues

**Clean Fundamentals:**
- **Hook grip in rack position** — secures bottom position, enforces upright back, high elbows; smooths jerk transition (if wrist mobility allows).
- **Tight lats throughout** — treat back as isometric; maintain upper back tension without excessive scapular retraction. Consider barbell rows to reinforce this.
- **Explosion position cue**: "How to get higher before explosion"—avoid early knee straightening.
- **Fast under the bar** — use hang clean, no-foot clean, and power clean drills to develop this skill.

**Common Errors:**
- Straightening legs before glutes (knees collapse inward).
- Poor pressure maintenance on quads/knees in drive phase.
- Inadequate elbow rotation speed to rack position.

**Front Rack Position:** Elbow angle ~40–45° (not forced to 90°); critical for OHS mobility transfer.

---

## Programming Principles

- **Weekly structure (intermediate, night shift adjustment)**: 2x heavy C&J + 1x power variation (not full frequency if recovery limited). Quality > frequency.
- **Variation emphasis**: Hang clean (best return on investment), no-foot clean (technique + OHS mobility), power clean & jerk (3–4x/week alternate days).
- **Periodization framework**: 6-week hypertrophy block → 2-month technical/strength blocks with intensity variation maintained.
- **Rep scheme**: Low reps; prioritize speed over strain.
- **Recovery protocol (non-negotiable with night shift + back pain)**: Active + passive stretching post-training; focus on front rack and shoulder mobility.
- **Training age principle**: General base (coordination, plyometrics, running, jumping) built early; rapid skill acquisition possible with intensive variation work.

## Athlete Relevance

| Factor | Your Situation | Application |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| **OHS limiter (50 kg vs. 80 kg clean)** | Severe mobility/stability gap | Prioritize front rack mobility + no-foot clean drills; add bench stretch variations, shoulder work post-session |
| **Squat-to-clean ratio** | 118 kg BS : 80 kg C (1.48:1); 50 kg OHS suggests positional issue | Film cleans to assess back pain origin; likely positioning rather than load. Fast-under-the-bar drills critical. |
| **Chronic back pain** | Disrupts positioning and recovery | Post-training active/passive stretching non-negotiable. Barbell rows to reinforce lat tension. Assess if pain worsens with specific clean positions. |
| **Night shift schedule (Wed–Sun)** | Limited recovery + inconsistent training window | Reduce frequency to 2x heavy C&J + 1x power variation. Prioritize sleep/mobility protocol. Start position variability (aggressive vs. methodical) may reduce CNS demand. |
| **Push jerk only (no split)** | Upper body/timing limitation | Split jerk development necessary long-term; front rack mobility prerequisite. Jerk practice 2–3x/week minimum. |
| **6-week hypertrophy block** | Aligns with Victor's 2-month periodization model | Use block for front squat strength + mobility accumulation; high-rep hang clean (6–8 reps × 4–5 sets) for speed + volume |

**Priority Actions:**
1. Front rack mobility (wrist + shoulder) daily—non-negotiable for OHS progression.
2. Hang clean emphasis (high reps for speed/hypertrophy block).
3. Post-training stretching protocol (address back pain causation).
4. Film cleans to identify back pain trigger (positioning vs. load).
