# Why did Lasha Snatch Balance 230kg?

**This text IS about weightlifting** — specifically snatch balance analysis using Lasha Talakhadze as a case study.

---

## **RELEVANT EXTRACTIONS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

### **1. Programming Principles**
- **Overhead stability work frequency**: Every athlete should have overhead squats or snatch balance variants in program
- **Set/rep structure for stability**: 4–5 sets of 2–3 reps (e.g., 2 snatch balances + 2 OHS, or push press + OHS)
- **Progressive loading**: Build to ~15 kg above best snatch, then reduce weight and increase reps for time under tension
- **Rationale**: Snatching alone doesn't provide enough time under tension to build overhead stability; rack-based work is more controlled

### **2. Technique Cues for Snatch/OHS**
- **Foot movement decision**: No-foot variants are safer for near-maximal weights; foot movement adds variance/timing complexity (most appropriate for beginners)
- **Bar whip/timing at heavy loads**: Elite lifters use bar bend as a spring mechanism—maintain stable base and chase the upward energy; moving feet mid-cycle risks losing that stored energy
- **Confidence building**: Snatch balance drills build overhead confidence with absolute loads before competition attempts

### **3. Exercise Recommendations**
- **Snatch balance variations** (behind-neck, heaving, no-foot)
- **Overhead squat progressions**
- **Push press + OHS combinations**
