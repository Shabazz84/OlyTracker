# Why You Should Foam Roll #olympicweightlifting

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**Recovery/Mobility Advice:**
- Soft tissue work (baseball, foam roller) is valuable when muscles become tight/restricted mid-training block
- Particularly useful for this athlete: **back tightness** (chronic pain profile) and calf restrictions that limit squat depth/range of motion
- Can salvage poor sessions by temporarily loosening restricted muscles
- Timing: post-session, during training blocks

**Applicability:**
Highly relevant. With chronic back pain and a 6-week hypertrophy block ahead, proactive soft tissue maintenance (especially lower back, calves, hips) should be integrated post-session to maintain positions needed for squats and cleans. Critical for night-shift worker managing fatigue and stiffness.

**Note:** Research evidence is mixed, but practical application for positional restrictions is sound.
