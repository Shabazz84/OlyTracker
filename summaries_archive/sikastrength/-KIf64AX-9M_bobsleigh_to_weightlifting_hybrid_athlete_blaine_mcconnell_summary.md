# Merged Weightlifting Summary for Intermediate Athlete (102.5 kg, 80 kg Clean, 65 kg Push Jerk)

## Key Points

- **3-day Olympic lift split** (snatch + clean & jerk variations Mon/Wed/Fri) is effective for intermediate progression
- **Plyometric primers** (3x/week, pre-lift) build tendon resiliency and jerk strength without requiring separate sessions
- **Full range of motion under load** serves as primary mobility strategy—negates need for static stretching
- **2 complete rest days/week** minimum; recovery prioritized over session volume
- **6-week block structure** (low-intensity, high-frequency work) followed by peaking phase aligns with stated hypertrophy→technique progression

## Technique Cues

- **Depth jumps** (shock method): 3-week loading blocks → 2-week recovery → retest; correlates strongly with jerk improvements
- Achieve mobility through loaded end-range positions rather than passive stretching
- Plyometrics improve fascia elasticity and tendon resiliency—supports both strength and technical positions

## Programming Principles

- **Mon/Wed/Fri**: Snatch/clean & jerk variations (sport-specific focus)
- **Tue/Sat**: Accessory work (front/back squats, varied protocols)
- **Thu + 1 other**: Full recovery days
- Rotate plyometric variations every 4–6 weeks
- Prioritize sleep/nutrition recovery over additional modalities, especially during hypertrophy block

## Athlete Relevance

**⚠️ Critical modifications needed:**

1. **Chronic back pain**: Full ROM emphasis and depth jumps require pain-free baseline. Test carefully; may need additional rest days beyond 2/week.
2. **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Schedule 3 lifts around sleep availability. Consider **Mon/Tue/Thu or Tue/Thu/Sat** to avoid post-shift fatigue.
3. **OHS limitation (50 kg)**: Plyometrics + loaded mobility work should accelerate overhead position strength (primary snatch limiter).
4. **Push jerk only (no split)**: Depth jump focus directly addresses jerk deficit; prioritize this for the 6-week block.
5. **Recovery philosophy**: Minimal interference during hypertrophy phase—lift, eat, sleep. No sauna/contrast baths until competition approach.
