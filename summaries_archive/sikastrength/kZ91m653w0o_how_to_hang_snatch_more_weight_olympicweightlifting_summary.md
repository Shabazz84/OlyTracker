# How To Hang Snatch More Weight #olympicweightlifting

**1. Programming Principles**
- Above-the-knee hang snatch: emphasis on speed and heavy weight movement (power development)
- Distinct from full snatch positioning—not a position replication exercise

**2. Technique Cues (Snatch)**
- Get knees **underneath the barbell** (vs. back)
- Keep **torso/chest upright**
- Maintain **barbell close to legs** throughout pull
- Maximize power and force production through leverage efficiency

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Above-the-knee hang snatch as a speed/power developer (relevant for this athlete's weak OHS—addressing the pull/positioning before the overhead hold)

**4. Specific to This Athlete**
- **Highly relevant**: OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg). This cue work addresses pull mechanics and bar positioning, which feed into receiving position. The hang snatch variation is accessible given chronic back pain (shorter range, less spinal load than full snatch from floor).
- Consider: May need to pair with OHS-specific work since technique cues focus on the pull phase only.
