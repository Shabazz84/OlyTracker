# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
None explicitly stated.

## 2. Technique Cues
**Overhead Position:** Lat inhibition is a common barrier to good overhead mechanics. Address through progressive loading, not just stretching.

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Loaded lat mobility work** using cable machine, resistance band, or dumbbell
- Purpose: expose lat tissues to extended range of motion under external load
- Rationale: may address inhibition better than static/dynamic stretching or soft tissue work alone

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Lats often forgotten as overhead position limiters
- Progress from: static stretch → dynamic stretch → soft tissue work → **loaded external load work**
- Key principle: "doesn't matter why" the inhibition exists; exposure under load is the fix

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
**HIGHLY RELEVANT.** This athlete has OHS at 50 kg (major snatch limiter). Lat mobility/positioning directly impacts:
- Overhead stability in snatch
- Split jerk overhead position (future progression beyond push jerk)
- Back pain management (tight lats can increase lumbar compensation)

**Recommendation:** Incorporate loaded lat work into warm-up or mobility block, especially given chronic back pain and limited OHS.
