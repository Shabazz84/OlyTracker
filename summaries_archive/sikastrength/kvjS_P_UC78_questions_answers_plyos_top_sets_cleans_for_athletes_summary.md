# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Programming for Intermediate OL Athlete

## Key Points

- **Current status acceptable**: 118 kg back squat to 80 kg clean ratio is sound; no urgent need to reduce classic lift volume.
- **6-week hypertrophy block is viable** before transitioning to strength/technique phase—maintain snatch and clean at 1×/week minimum to preserve skill.
- **Front squat target**: Aim for 135–140 kg (based on 120 kg clean benchmark); current 118 kg back squat leaves room to build to 180–200 kg long-term.
- **Cyclic periodization > static programming**: Layer higher-volume phases → lower volume/heavier weight → peaking for better adaptation.
- **Stay in weightlifting-specific programming longer** rather than switching to pure strength phases—yields better long-term outcomes for Olympic lifters.

## Technique Cues

- **Squat form integrity**: Avoid form drift (forward lean, reduced depth, wider stance) during strength blocks to maintain transfer to weightlifting positions.
- **Movement quality over tempo**: During hypertrophy, prioritize progressive overload and movement quality; don't over-prioritize stretch reflex minimization.
- **Box jumps**: Useful for rapid contraction/relaxation motor patterns and explosive power—supports squat velocity.

## Programming Principles

- **Standard concurrent training framework**: Snatch 2–3×/week, clean & jerk 2×/week, squat 2–3×/week (~8–10 months/year).
- **Off-season blocks (2–4 months)**: Reduce classic lifts to 1×/week; prioritize strength/hypertrophy work targeting 15–25% squat increase.
- **Hypertrophy phase structure**: Main lift first (back squat for leg hypertrophy), then accessories; support heavy top sets with volume work.
- **Commit fully to each block** rather than mixing protocols—reinforces adaptation.

## Athlete Relevance

- **Night shift schedule (Wed–Sun)**: Requires intentional recovery windows; prioritize sleep and nutrition during hypertrophy block.
- **Chronic back pain**: Maintain squat form integrity and avoid compensation patterns (forward lean, wider stance). Back squat remains primary leg builder; monitor volume tolerance.
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Prioritize mobility and stability work post-hypertrophy block; OHS deficit is primary snatch bottleneck.
- **Push jerk only (no split jerk)**: Consider split jerk progression *after* strength block to build dip stability and jerk turnover capacity.
- **6-week hypertrophy block strategy**: Back squat focus (leg hypertrophy + strength carryover to clean), overhead work (push press), maintain snatch/clean 1×/week. Then transition to 4–6 week strength/technique block targeting split jerk and OHS.
