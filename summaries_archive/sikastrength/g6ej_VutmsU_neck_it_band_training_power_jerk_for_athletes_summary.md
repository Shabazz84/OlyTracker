**Jerk Technique (Chris Dance Q):**
- **Push Jerk vs. Split Jerk:** Coach recommends **behind-the-neck jerk** or **split jerk** over push jerk for most athletes
- **Grip:** Slightly wider than shoulder width (back squat grip position)—**avoid narrow grip** (doesn't increase upper body engagement, just makes lift harder)
- **Rationale:** Better teaches speed/power production and muscle relaxation-reengagement—key for athletic transfer
- **Note:** This directly applies—athlete currently does push jerk only and lacks split jerk. Behind-the-neck power jerk could be a useful intermediate progression.
