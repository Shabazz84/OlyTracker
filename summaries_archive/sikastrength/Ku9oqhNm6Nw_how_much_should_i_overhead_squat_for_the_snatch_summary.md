# How Much Should I Overhead Squat For The Snatch?

# Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **OHS volume**: 1-2x/week, 5-7 sets of 2-3 reps (never excessive)
- **OHS format**: Use in complexes (snatch + OHS, snatch balance + OHS, push press behind neck + OHS) rather than standalone
- **Priority hierarchy**: Snatch/clean/jerk are #1 priority; OHS shouldn't compromise recovery for these
- **Fatigue management**: Heavy OHS progression (5x5, 5x3) sacrifices recovery capital needed for main lifts

## 2. Technique Cues
- **OHS assessment**: Should snatch balance ≥best snatch weight; ideally 10-15kg more comfortably
- **Weakness indicators**: If unstable or weak-looking overhead, it's a limiting factor (will be "very apparent")

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
**For overhead weakness (mobility/shoulder issues over strength):**
- Strict press behind neck
- Push press behind neck
- Snatch balance
- Soft press

These address shoulder/mobility deficits with less load than OHS.

## 4. Relevance to This Athlete
**⚠️ HIGH RELEVANCE:** OHS is your stated primary snatch limiter (50kg vs 80kg clean). Current snatch balance capacity unknown—this is your diagnostic test. If SB ≥80kg, strength isn't the issue; address shoulder mobility/stability instead. If SB <80kg, minimal OHS volume (1-2x/week in complexes) fits your hypertrophy block while preserving recovery for snatch/clean/jerk work. Avoid standalone OHS progressions that drain resources from main lifts.
