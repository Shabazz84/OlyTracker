# A Different Way of Squatting For Weightlifting #olympicweightlifting

# Analysis

**Programming Principles:**
- Set of 10 reps on back squat (hypertrophy/leg development focus)

**Technique Cues - Back Squat:**
- Toes forward (minimal external rotation)
- Stance: outside hip width, narrower than shoulder width
- Minimal forward knee travel (relative to typical patterns)
- Quad-dominant positioning
- Avoids forcing toes out or exaggerated depth
- Purpose: maximize quad engagement over pattern development

**Relevance to Athlete:**
- **Highly relevant for this profile.** Given chronic back pain and weak OHS (50 kg), quad-dominant squatting reduces spinal load while building leg strength needed for clean/jerk. The conservative stance and forward knee position may be safer than wide, deep positioning.
- Fits 6-week hypertrophy block (set of 10).
- Complements snatch/clean development without compromising back health.
