# What Happens AFTER The Olympics!

**Not applicable to weightlifting coaching.**

This is a sports psychology video about post-competition mood states and depression in athletes—relevant to mental health but contains no programming, technique cues, exercise recommendations, or recovery/mobility advice specific to weightlifting training.

**Potential relevance to athlete only:** Given the athlete's night shift work and chronic back pain, managing pre-competition psychological fatigue (stress, tension, low mood) could moderate post-competition recovery. However, this isn't addressed in the transcript.
