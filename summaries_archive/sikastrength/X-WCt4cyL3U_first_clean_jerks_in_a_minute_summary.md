# Analysis: Weightlifting Training Vlog

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency**: Currently 1x/week full snatch; planning to scale to 5-6x/week as conditioning improves
- **Intensity**: Small jumps (10kg increments), working up conservatively from 50→70→100kg
- **Approach**: Technique-focused rebuilding phase; avoiding drastic corrections weekly, allowing adaptations to emerge naturally as volume/strength increase
- **Periodization**: Return-from-injury phase (3+ month layoff from clean/jerk); front squats reintroduced after ~1 month avoidance

## 2. **Technique Cues**
**Snatch:**
- Vertical shin angle in start (avoid knees too far forward OR too far back in heels)
- Focus: smooth contact, balanced second pull (avoid "smashy" hip extension)
- Issue noted: rushed second pull, drifting contact

**Clean (no-contact work):**
- Keep arms straighter longer in second pull (reduces tension)
- Emphasize timing, positional work, elbow speed
- No-contact work prioritized over actual contact for initial weeks

**General:**
- 45-60s static holds in front rack and squat bottom positions for mobility

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Full snatch 1x/week (no hang/foot snatches yet)
- No-contact cleans (replacing traditional cleans during recovery)
- Front squats: reintroduction phase; weight secondary to position comfort
- Back squats: primary leg strength driver (implied priority over front squats)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Extended static stretching: front rack position (45-50s holds) and bottom squat (50-60s)
- Addresses internal rotation tightness from non-sport activities
