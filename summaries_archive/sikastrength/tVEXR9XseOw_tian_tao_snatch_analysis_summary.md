# Analysis: Seek Strength - Tanto Technical Breakdown

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Static vs. Dynamic Start**: Recommend static start for intermediate lifters (less than 4-5 years experience) to prioritize **technical consistency** over power initially
- **Pull Volume**: Heavy snatch pulls/deadlifts 4-5 sets of 3 reps, 2x/week at ~90-100% working snatch weight. Doubles or triples the effective first/second pull reps (~30-40 reps/week vs. 20 from snatches alone)
- **Strength-to-Lift Ratio**: For 100kg snatch → 110kg+, back squat should reach ~200kg (currently athlete at 118kg BS to 80kg snatch = acceptable starting point, but needs BS development)
- **Power Snatch Frequency**: Once weekly if turnover speed is limiting
- **Progression Timeline**: Static start for weeks → add dynamic start once strength/consistency established (6+ months)

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Snatch Start**: Achieve consistency first (same shoulder position, hip distance, arm tension every rep) before adding dynamic hip movement
- **Barbell Path**: Sweep/pull barbell *back* toward body off floor (avoid forward bumping)
- **Turnover Aggression**: Active, rapid arm snap into lockout (not loose/passive). Aggressive pulling maintains bar position and prevents forward drift
- **Lockout Position**: Keep tension on barbell throughout (no "weightlessness")

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Power Snatches**: Light weight, fast turnover, high catch position (1x/week)
- **Muscle Snatches** (no-contact, hook grip): 40-50kg warm-up weights, emphasize high elbow pull and fast lockout—treat as speed/technique drill, not strength
- **Back Extensions + Abs**: Finish sessions (addresses postural weakness and lower back stability)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Minimal direct mention; implicit emphasis on **technical consistency reducing psychological fatigue** and injury risk
