# The Best Snatch Technique I've Ever Seen! #olympicweightlifting

**Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:**

1. **Technique Cue - Snatch:**
   - Maintain shin angle close to perpendicular as bar passes knee
   - This keeps bar path closer to athlete's center of mass
   - Improves balance and power production at hip extension
   - *Highly relevant: OHS is limiting factor (50 kg); improving bar position/balance may help*
