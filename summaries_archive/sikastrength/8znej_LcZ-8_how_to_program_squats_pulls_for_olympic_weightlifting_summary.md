# How To Program Squats & Pulls For Olympic Weightlifting

# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. **Programming Principles**

**Periodization & Peaking:**
- High volume/lower intensity → convergence → separate directions (strength/technique split)
- For this athlete: Peak squat ~week 14 (near competition), NOT simultaneously with snatch/CJ peak
- Front squat: 2:1 frequency ratio vs. back squat; one quality session/week sufficient
- Back squat drives front squat—prioritize back squat volume

**Pulling Strategy:**
- Pulls are **technique exercises first, strength second**
- Load pulls slightly ahead of main lift (e.g., if snatch 5×3@70kg, pull 5×3@75-80kg)
- For weak pullers: 30 reps/pull 3× weekly (90 reps/week) vs. squat maintenance
- Snatch pulls: 2× weekly; clean pulls: 1× weekly minimum (3 pulls/week if training 4× weekly)
- Rep ranges: 3–5 reps standard; avoid 8+ reps (excessive muscular endurance work)

**Front Squat Timing:**
- Can be same day as clean & jerk if athlete is strong; otherwise move to different day (e.g., Saturday) to maintain quality

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**

**Pulls (Snatch/Clean):**
- Same speed, tempo, angles as the lift from floor
- Reinforce position, not load—"don't muddy the water" with poor reps
- Extend fully before pulling under; avoid cutting extension short
- High pulls: require follow-through (bar continues rising after hip extension) — no "pull under" premature
- Clean grip deadlift: focus hip/knee/ankle extension + trap pull; **arm bending unnecessary** for most lifters

**Front Squat:**
- Secondary to back squat development
- Acceptable to get "grindy" at times; prioritize good technique
- If slow/hard at predicted clean & jerk weight → problem detected early

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**Deficit Pulls:**
- Not recommended unless specific positional issue exists (e.g., knee-forward, weak back angle)
- Changes start position → hard to maintain clean technique

**Strap Usage:**
- Learn to use straps; avoid losing intensity/hand care
- Straps allow heavier loading and more reps

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**

⚠️ **CRITICAL FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

1. **Chronic back pain + weak OHS (50kg):**
   - Back squat weakness (118kg) is a limiter for snatch progression
   - Pull volume strategy should emphasize **slow eccentric work** (5-sec negatives) on clean pulls to build postural strength
   - **Avoid deficit pulls**—keep start position clean

2. **Night shift worker (Wed–Sun):**
   - Consider peaking squat mid-week (Thurs) vs. weekend when fatigued
   - Ensure recovery days align with shift schedule

3. **Squat-to-Snatch Ratio Problem:**
   - BS 118kg vs. Snatch 80kg = weak lower body support
   - **Prioritize back squat hypertrophy block** — goal is reasonable (160–180kg BS before aggressive snatch peaking)
   - Front squat: 1–2× weekly for technique; let BS drive progress

4. **No Split Jerk:**
   - Front squat strength directly limits jerk capacity; focus front squat positioning before peaking

5. **6-Week Hypertrophy Block Strategy:**
   - High volume, lower intensity squat work is ideal prep
   - Keep pulls technical/light during hypertrophy phase
   - Plan peaking squat 2–3 weeks before final strength block max-out
