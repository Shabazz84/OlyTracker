# Best Hamstring Exercises For Weightlifting

**ANALYSIS:**

**Not directly applicable to this athlete.** This is a casual coaching discussion about snatch technique philosophy and hamstring exercises—valuable general content but lacks structured programming.

**Relevant extracts:**

1. **Technique principle (Snatch):** Split snatch inferior to squat snatch because it's slower and doesn't achieve as low/stable a position. Emphasizes the biomechanical principle: get one center of mass (barbell) under the other (body) as fast as possible in the most stable position. *Relevant: Athlete has very low OHS (50 kg)—likely needs squat snatch progression, not split.*

2. **Hamstring exercises recommended:**
   - RDLs from a block with shrug at top (allows fuller extension/contraction)
   - Nordic hamstring curls (though debate noted on specificity)
   - RDLs to knee height from blocks
   
   *Relevant: Could address explosive power deficits and reduce back strain during snatch pulls.*
