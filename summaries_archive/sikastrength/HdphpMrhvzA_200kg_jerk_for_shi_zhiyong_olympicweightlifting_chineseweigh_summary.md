## 1. **Technique Cues: Power Jerk**
- Elite power jerks involve a **forward jump** under the bar (not purely vertical)
- Forward movement aids bar positioning **behind the head**
- Vertical-only drive makes bar positioning harder to achieve
- **Caveat:** Forward jump technique is difficult for amateur lifters and often disrupts their mechanics when coached explicitly
