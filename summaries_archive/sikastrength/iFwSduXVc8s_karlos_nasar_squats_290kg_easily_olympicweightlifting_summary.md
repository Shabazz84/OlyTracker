**2. Technique Cues**
- **Back Squat:** Maintain tight upper back position; actively "knot the bar" and keep elbows behind the bar plane
- Prevents relaxation on traps; maintains upright torso throughout
- Drive upper back extension into the bar on the ascent (especially past parallel)
- Prevents forward torso lean

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
Back squat emphasized as a primary lift.

**4. Recovery/Mobility**

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
**HIGH RELEVANCE:** This cue is critical for this athlete:
- Back squat (118 kg) is a relative strength; proper upper back tension will improve positioning
- **Chronic back pain risk:** Anterior lean under load increases lower back stress. This cue directly addresses that—maintaining upright torso + upper back extension reduces shear forces on the lumbar spine
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Poor upper back tension in the squat directly transfers to weak OHS positioning. Perfecting this pattern will improve overhead stability

**Recommendation:** Implement this cue immediately during hypertrophy block squats.
