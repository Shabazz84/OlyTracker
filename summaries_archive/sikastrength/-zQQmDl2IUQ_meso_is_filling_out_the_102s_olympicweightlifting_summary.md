# Meso is Filling Out The 102s! #olympicweightlifting

**Not directly applicable to this athlete's training.**

This is a commentary on an elite athlete (180kg snatcher, 182kg PB) transitioning weight classes—context irrelevant to an intermediate lifter (80kg clean, 50kg OHS).

**One potentially useful principle:**
- **Expect slower snatch progress when gaining bodyweight/moving up in strength category.** Technique and mobility often lag behind absolute strength gains; expect 2-3 months adaptation, not immediate PRs.

For this athlete specifically: back pain + chronic mobility issues + light OHS suggest prioritizing OHS mobility work over expecting snatch jumps during the hypertrophy block.
