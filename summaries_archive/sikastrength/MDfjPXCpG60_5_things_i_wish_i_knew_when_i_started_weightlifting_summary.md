# 5 Things I Wish I Knew When I Started Weightlifting

# Analysis: Not Directly Applicable

This transcript is **not about weightlifting coaching or programming guidance**—it's a personal retrospective ("5 things I wish I knew") from an advanced/elite lifter reflecting on career mistakes.

## Potentially Relevant Takeaways for Your Athlete:

**Cautions that apply:**
- Back squat dominance ≠ snatch/clean/jerk progress (correlation ≠ causation)
- Excessive body weight reduces athleticism and speed
- Hamstring/posterior chain neglect limits extension speed and power
- Accessory work (snatch balance, hang variations) can steal focus from sport-specific reps

**What's NOT applicable:**
- Speaker is discussing 270+ kg back squats; your athlete is 118 kg
- Speaker's mistakes came at an elite level; intermediate athletes need different priorities
- The "specificity is king, do only snatch/clean/jerk" philosophy is advanced-level advice
