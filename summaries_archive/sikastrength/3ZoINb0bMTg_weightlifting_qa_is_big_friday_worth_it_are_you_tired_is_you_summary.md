# Analysis: Coaching Transcript on Max-Out Strategy & Programming

## **Programming Principles**
- **Conservative mid-block singles**: Build confidence, avoid early overshooting (creates false reads of readiness)
- **Progressive loading pattern**: Start conservative, increase ~2-3kg incrementally across the week
- **Avoid consecutive misses**: If missing at 80-90kg range, regress 5-7kg and rebuild rather than continue failing attempts
- **Volume before heavy singles**: Some lifters benefit from 2x2@80%, 3x1@85% before attempting maxes; others prefer direct heavy attempts
- **Data collection**: After 4-5 cycles, you learn your actual capacity and stop guessing
