# How To Hang Clean #olympicweightlifting

# Analysis for This Athlete

**Relevance:** Moderately relevant. Hang clean technique applies, but this athlete's priority is **snatch (50 kg OHS is limiter)** and split jerk development—not hand cleans.

---

## Key Extracts:

**Technique Cues (Clean):**
- Maintain full bar path distance from hang to bottom (avoid reducing to 2–3 inches)
- Don't turn hang clean into a "hip clean" → prevents poor arm position & bar crash
- Keep bar close throughout descent and direction change
- Apply force to maintain proximity during turnaround

**Relevance to This Athlete:**
- ✅ Clean is 80 kg (weak relative to squat) → technique efficiency matters
- ⚠️ **Back pain concern:** Aggressive hang cleans risk lumbar loading on the turnaround. May need lighter hang variations or focus on front rack position control
- ❌ **Split jerk gap:** Text doesn't address jerk; this athlete needs split jerk coaching (currently push jerk only at 65 kg)

---

## Recommendation:
Use these cues for **clean pulls & high-hang cleans** to build positional strength without heavy cervical/lumbar stress. Prioritize snatch technique work and split jerk introduction in the 6-week block. Consider programming lighter hang variations given chronic back pain.
