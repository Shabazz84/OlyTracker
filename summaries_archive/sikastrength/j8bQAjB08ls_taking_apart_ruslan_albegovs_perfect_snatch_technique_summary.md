# Analysis: Seek Strength Technique Sunday — Rosen Alba Snatch

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Dynamic vs. Static Start**: Dynamic start (hip pump) leverages stretch-shortening cycle for faster barbell acceleration; requires technical consistency built over thousands of reps
- **Pulls emphasis**: Russians prioritize snatch/clean pulls and back work; go heavy as technique allows (up to 120%+ of competition lift for unenhanced athletes)
- **Deliberate practice model**: Pick one technical approach (dynamic or static start) and stick with it for years; consistency >> variation

## 2. Technique Cues — Snatch
- **Dynamic Start**: Single hip pump (hips high → drop into position); hamstring ~parallel or slightly above
- **Second Pull**: Keep hips back as long as possible; minimize horizontal hip movement to stay balanced and apply vertical force; patient hip extension = longer second pull = barbell stays close to center of mass
- **Third Pull**: Aggressive extension through short distance (Rosen example)

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Snatch pulls** (heaviest priority; replicate joint angles)
- **Snatch-grip deadlifts** (up to 120%+ of snatch if technically sound)
- **Back extensions & Romanian deadlifts** (snatch/clean grip variations)
- Emphasis: Back strength ~ squat strength importance
