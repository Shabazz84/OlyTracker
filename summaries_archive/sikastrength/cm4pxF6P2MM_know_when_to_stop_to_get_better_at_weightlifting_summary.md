**Programming/Autoregulation Principle:**

- Recovery timeline: 4-6 weeks to rebuild volume before attempting heavy singles again.
- **For this athlete:** Critical—chronic back pain + night shift + hypertrophy block ahead = strict autoregulation needed. Avoid grinding singles when form breaks or fatigue accumulates.

**Coaching Insight:**
- Technical proficiency ≠ readiness to max. Speaker had "perfect" 120kg singles but shouldn't attempt 140kg—a warning sign for self-coached athletes.
- Value of external accountability (calling coach) to stop sessions objectively.
