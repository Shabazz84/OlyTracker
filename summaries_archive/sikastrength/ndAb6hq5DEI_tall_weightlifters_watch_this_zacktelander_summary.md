# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis: Secret Strength Session

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **12-week program structure**: 4 sessions/week, targets snatch, clean/jerk, squats
- **Technical priority first**: Establish technique baseline before intensity progression
- **Long adaptation window**: Expect 3-month training block minimum to ingrain technical changes before peak attempts
- **Retention expectation**: 80% technical retention over days at moderate weights (athlete-dependent)

## 2. **Technique Cues Directly Applicable to This Athlete**

### **SNATCH** (critical for 50kg OHS limiter):
- **Start position**: Hamstrings ~parallel, shoulders slightly *over* barbell (NOT low-hips/"Hungarian start")
  - Rationale: Longer femurs need this to keep knees out of way sooner, improve shoulder position
- **Knees**: NO aggressive knee push-out — let knees naturally *come back* during pull (not stay forward)
  - Issue: Forward knees = bar drifts away from center of mass
- **Positioning emphasis**: Hang over barbell more; this combats the tall-lifter tendency to sit too low

### **CLEAN** (65kg → improve speed under bar):
- **Start position**: Shoulders over (or slightly in front of) barbell, knees NOT in front of bar, ~45° hamstring angle
- **Pull under timing**: Prioritize *fast pull-under* over over-pulling height
- **Arm bend**: Keep arms straighter/relaxed through pull; bend arms last (simultaneous with pull-under)
  - Common tall-lifter mistake: Early arm bend ruins timing and reduces pull-under efficiency

### **JERK** (push jerk only):
- Not detailed in transcript

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Focus on snatch-specific work before expecting OHS gains (50kg OHS is primary limiter)
- Moderate weight cycling to practice and retain technical changes (80/20 rule)

## 5. **Relevant to Athlete Profile**
- ✅ **Tall/long-limb athlete**: All cues address femur/torso length issues (lower start position inefficient for this body type)
- ✅ **Back pain context**: Higher start position (vs. low squat position) may reduce back stress
- ⚠️ **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: No accommodation discussed; consider fatigue management during 6-week hypertrophy block
- ✅ **Intermediate level**: Technique refinement before PB attempts is appropriate

**Key Takeaway**: Implement higher start position in snatch/clean + straighter-arm clean pull now; allow 3+ months before testing maxes.
