## Key Extracts

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Avoid indefinite plateaus**: Athletes fixating on past PRs often undertrain. Better approach: "16 weeks push hard, then full 8-week break" rather than perpetual threading water at maximal weights.
- **General Physical Preparedness (GPP) foundation**: Pyramid model—aerobic capacity → strength → skill → sport-specific work. Most amateur weightlifters skip GPP, increasing injury risk and chronic underachievement.

### 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Standard convention is the goal**: Don't chase novel techniques; align with established technical models.
- **Compensatory patterns are "Band-Aid coaching"**: Low hips in start position, excessive toe-out, excessive lean-back at top may *feel* comfortable (especially for weak-legged athletes) but mask underlying deficits. They're backward steps from ideal technique.

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Neglect aerobic work is common error**: Include jog/cycle in warm-ups; don't substitute mobility/myofascial release for general conditioning.
- **General conditioning matters**: Athletes should maintain basic fitness (e.g., running 2K, carrying loads without breathlessness).

### 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- **Avoid "art not sport" fallacy**: Weightlifting requires traditional athletic development structure, including aerobic base and general conditioning—not just mobility work.
