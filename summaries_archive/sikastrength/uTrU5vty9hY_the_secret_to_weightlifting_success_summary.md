## 2. Technique Cues (Snatch Focus)
- **Bar path:** Pull barbell *back toward you* off the floor → into hips. Bar should not drift forward.
- **Body positioning:** Body moves *up then into the barbell* at the correct timing—not too early.
- **Patience in pull phases:**
  - Dynamic start: don't rush
  - Off-floor: be patient
  - Past knee: don't rush the transition
  - Hip contact: timing is critical; rushing hips = missed lift
- **Second pull ends** when hips contact the bar—anything after that is already a miss.
- **Heaviest lifts require no conscious thought**—rely on ingrained positioning from perfect practice.
