# Is Karlos Nasar's Snatch Technique Really That Bad?

# Weightlifting Analysis: Carlos Nazar Snatch Technique Video

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Pulls**: 2x/week in snatch style, 4–5 sets of 3 reps, 100–110% intensity (technical practice over max singles; heavy pulls cause technique drift and fatigue)
- **Power Snatch**: 1x/week, mostly volume with finishing singles; builds launch velocity without excessive joint stress
- **Volume over intensity** for strength building at specific joint angles

## 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch)**
- **Bar Path**: Must travel *back toward center of mass*, not forward (forward jump = loss of power & inconsistent catch)
- **Back Position**: Maintain strong, proud chest & arch throughout (critical at knee and hip extension). Loose back kills power and lets bar drift forward
- **Launch Velocity**: Must actively accelerate bar from above-knee to peak speed; this is non-negotiable for catch height
- **Hip Extension**: Requires tight back—rounded upper back reduces extension power significantly

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Prioritize **snatch pulls** (2x/week) over heavy singles
- **Power snatch** 1x/week to overemphasize speed in second pull without reinforcing poor catch positions
- Avoid excessive power snatch volume (encourages poor catching)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Heavy pulls are fatiguing to lower back; moderate volume (4–5×3 @ 100–110%) provides same strength gains without compromising subsequent sessions
- Implies sparing chronic back pain with submaximal intensity on pull work

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Weak back position directly limits overhead stability; pull work addresses this
- **Chronic back pain**: Avoid heavy singles; use moderate-volume pulls (100–110%) to build strength without aggravating spine
- **Push jerk only**: No split jerk means snatch catch stability is even more critical (less hip/knee absorption)
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Pull doubles/triples fit well; power snatch 1x/week maintains technique
