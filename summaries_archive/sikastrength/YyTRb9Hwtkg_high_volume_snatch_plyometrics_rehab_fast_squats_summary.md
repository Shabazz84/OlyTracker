## 2. **Technique Cues**
**Snatch:**
- **Start position**: Chin up, maximally extended upper/lower back, rigid, pulled up
- **Off-floor maintenance**: Keep shoulder position (avoid forward rotation)
- **Extension**: Vertical extension, full trap engagement, arm involvement
- **Diagnostic issue**: Shoulders rotating forward slightly = loss of back position; affects lift speed and precision
- **No-foot snatches**: Preferred variation to reinforce extension
- **Pull mechanics**: Coach notes pulling "super slowly off the floor" when position breaks; speed improves with correct start position

**Back squat**: Chest-up cue emphasized

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Remove**: Hang snatches (reinforcing poor lean-back pattern)

- **Assistance**: Back/pressing work for upper body muscle and jiu-jitsu carryover
- **Plyometrics**: Single-leg hops, rebound counter-movement jumps, box jumps
