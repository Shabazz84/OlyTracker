# Analysis: Training Frequency for Weightlifting

## Programming Principles
- **1x/week**: Minimal for intermediate lifters; acceptable only for very strong athletes focusing one lift/variation
- **2x/week**: Bottom end of acceptable; viable first 1–1.5 years (structure: 1 snatch focus + 1 C&J focus)
- **3x/week**: Sweet spot for long-term progress; sustainable for Masters lifters; 2–3 years consistent progress possible
- **4x/week**: **THE SWEET SPOT** for most lifters; highly productive for extended periods; can reach 120+ snatch, 150+ C&J
- **5x/week**: Reserved for serious competitors or part-time workers; necessary only for elite totals (140+ snatch, 180+ C&J)
- **6x/week+**: Counterproductive for most; increases injury risk, fatigue, and psychological load; diminishing returns
- **Double/triple sessions**: Not necessary unless national team athlete with full recovery infrastructure; adds fatigue without proportional gains

**Key principle**: Quality > Quantity. Build consistency first, then add frequency.
