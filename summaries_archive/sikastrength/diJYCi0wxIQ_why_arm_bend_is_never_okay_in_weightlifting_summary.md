# Why Arm Bend Is NEVER Okay in Weightlifting

# Analysis: Early Arm Bend in Clean

## Technique Cues (Clean-Specific)
**Critical Issue: Early arm bend is a primary technical flaw to eliminate.**

- **Legs must initiate pull**: Legs push ground → back pulls → arms remain rigid (not bent) during 2nd pull
- **Why it's harmful**:
  - CNS protective mechanism: bent arms signal weak muscles are taking load → barbell automatically decelerates
  - Loses mechanical advantage (legs far stronger than arms)
  - Compromises 3rd pull positioning (active arm pull under bar becomes mechanically difficult if arms pre-bent)
- **Coaching priority**: Fix early arm bend before addressing secondary issues (like back rounding)

## Relevance to This Athlete
**HIGH PRIORITY**: With a 118 kg back squat but only 80 kg clean, early arm bend is likely a major limiting factor. Fix this mechanical flaw before hypertrophy block to maximize subsequent strength gains.

**Secondary note**: Chronic back pain + arm bend compensation may be interconnected—correcting arm bend could reduce lumbar stress.
