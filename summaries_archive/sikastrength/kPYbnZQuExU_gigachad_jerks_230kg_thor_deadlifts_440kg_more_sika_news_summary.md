# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Source Review

## Key Points
- This source is primarily **analysis/commentary on elite lifters**, not a coaching program
- Limited actionable programming for a 6-week hypertrophy block
- Snatch acceleration is critical through 2nd/3rd pull—don't pull slow off floor
- Snatch balance work is sufficient at 100–120% of floor snatch; unnecessary to chase heavier loads
- Solid, aggressive catch position (fast foot movement) is essential in the clean; straps acceptable for block work

## Technique Cues
- **Clean catch:** Aggressive catch with fast foot movement; stabilization occurs during catch, not after
- **Snatch bar path:** Barbell stays centered; shoulders move in front of bar to balance center of mass
- **Snatch third pull:** Explosive pull-through with efficient knee retraction
- **Jerk rack position:** Elbows at ~45° angle (NOT horizontal)—creates stability and superior lockout safety; transfers directly to OHS stability

## Programming Principles
- Technique consistency must be maintained even at heavier loads
- No structured periodization or hypertrophy-specific guidance provided in source
- Snatch balance can be programmed efficiently without chasing maximum weights

## Athlete Relevance
**High priority areas:**
- Your **50kg OHS weakness** likely stems from mobility/stability (poor front rack position), not strength alone. Focus on 45° elbow positioning and snatch balance progressions
- Snatch acceleration drills address your overhead positioning limiter

**Not addressed in source:**
- 6-week hypertrophy block periodization
- Push jerk programming (only split jerk examples)
- Chronic back pain modifications
- Night shift worker recovery strategies

**Recommendation:** Seek dedicated coaching content for hypertrophy programming, back-pain-friendly squat variations, and shift-work recovery.
