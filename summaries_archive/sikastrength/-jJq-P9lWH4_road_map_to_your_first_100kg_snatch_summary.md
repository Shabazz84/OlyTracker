# Road Map To Your First 100kg Snatch

# Analysis: 100kg Snatch Milestone Content

## Relevance to Athlete
**Limited direct applicability.** Content targets lifters pursuing 100kg snatches; this athlete is at 80kg clean/65kg jerk with 50kg OHS (primary limiter). The foundational principles apply, but the athlete should prioritize OHS development first.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Snatch frequency:** 2–3x/week minimum for technique refinement
- **Avoid:** 2–3RM chasing; use singles to maintain heavy technique
- **Progression:** Build strength alongside technique, not at its expense
- **Auxiliary work:** Should complement, not replace, full lifts and squats

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Use classic European/Soviet technical model** — avoid quirky deviations
- **Common errors to avoid:**
  - Low hip start position
  - Shoulders too far behind bar
  - Minimal foot movement
  - High-hang snatches (beginner crutch)
- **Reference texts:** *Training of the Weightlifter* (Roman), *Trials of a Fractional Weightlifter* (Kirkowski)

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Power snatch target:** 75–80kg (indicator of 100kg snatch potential)
- **Snatch balance:** 110kg+ overhead strength indicator
- **Low-hang snatch:** 80–85kg (≈90% of floor snatch)
- **Avoid rep maxes** (2–3RM) until technique solidified

---

## 4. **Mobility/Recovery Advice**
- **Assess flexibility first:** Can you sit in snatch bottom & start position?
- **Then assess mobility control:** PVC pipe OHS, unloaded OHS quality
- **Common compensation patterns:** Wrist kickback (shoulder mobility), hip/ankle limitations → forward lean
- **Hypermobility issue:** Flexibility ≠ strength; needs stability training
- **Maintenance:** Initial mobility work → ongoing maintenance (less volume but consistent)

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Notes**
- **OHS at 50kg is primary limiter** — mobility/flexibility work critical before snatch progression
- **Back pain:** Mobility imbalances (ankle, hip, shoulder) compound spinal stress; address these systematically
- **Night shift:** 2–3x/week snatch frequency demanding; ensure recovery prioritized
- **6-week hypertrophy block first:** Defer snatch intensity; use block to build positional strength & mobility tolerance
