# How often should I stretch for Weightlifting?

**Relevant Extractions for This Athlete:**

**1. Programming Principles**
- Mobility work 2-3x/week (not 30-60 min daily—be efficient)
- Proper warm-up and cooldown integrated into training structure
- Preventive approach beats reactive injury management

**2. Technique Cues**
- **Snatch/Clean depth**: Improved mobility allows deeper catches (4-8 cm lower pull height possible), enabling better positioning under weight
- **OHS**: Mobility gains → stability gains → less effort maintaining overhead position → more energy for standing/pulling heavier loads

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Hit major mobility areas 2-3x/week: ankles, hips, thoracic spine, shoulders
- Anti-rotation and midline stability work (core stability maintains position without wasting energy)
- No specific snatch/jerk recommendations given

**4. Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Mobility issues develop fast (weeks/months); stay preemptive
- Problems often unexpected and indirect (e.g., ankle mobility → knee pain)
- Warm-up/mobility now prevents regret later

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **CRITICAL**: OHS at 50kg is primary limiter; mobility work directly addresses this. Depth + stability gains = better catch position + heavier overhead pulls
- **Chronic back pain**: Thoracic spine + hip mobility may reduce compensation patterns; core stability work essential
- Night shift schedule: Keep mobility routine short/efficient (2-3x/week fits better than daily)
