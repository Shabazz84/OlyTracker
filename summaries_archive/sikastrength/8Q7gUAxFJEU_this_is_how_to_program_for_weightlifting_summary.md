# This is How To Program For Weightlifting

# MERGED SUMMARY

## Key Points

- **Block structure:** 12-week training blocks recommended; start high volume/light weight, progress loads, maintain volume consistency. Restart block with slightly reduced volume (e.g., 6×2, 7×2 instead of 5×3).
- **Frequency:** 4–5 days/week standard; never more than 2 consecutive days. Quality over quantity; full rest days are invaluable for recovery.
- **Technique is non-negotiable:** Technical improvement must accompany weight increases. Habits regress under heavy load and require reinforcement every block.
- **Shift work scheduling:** Nights are "not real training" days—do 1 light session + mobility/soft tissue work only. Consolidate 3–4 training sessions on days off. 3–4 full rest days/week are essential.
- **Competition frequency:** 3–4 competitions annually (one per training block).

---

## Technique Cues

- **General principle:** As weight increases (e.g., 80 kg → 120 kg), maintaining technique becomes harder (e.g., "harder to keep a stronger back at 140 than at 80").
- **Technical issues persist across all intensities** but feel harder under load; cues must carry over low-to-heavy and be reinforced regularly.
- **Upper back work is critical** for this athlete—directly impacts snatch stability and chronic back pain management.
- **Don't introduce new variations late in block** (week 10+ is too late); heavy technique work belongs in early blocks.

---

## Programming Principles

**Progression Example:** 5×3 → 4×3 → 5×2 → 3×1 → max over 6–8 weeks.

**Exercise Selection:**
- Snatch, clean & jerk, and squat are non-negotiable.
- Variations address specific technical issues only (1:1 ratio with full lifts maximum).
- Keep complex total ≤3 reps (e.g., 2 high pulls + 1 snatch).
- Discontinue variations if ineffective after 4 weeks of testing.
- Don't add new variations in final 2 weeks of block.

**Strength Training (Back/Front Squat):**
- Progress concurrently with snatch/clean & jerk.
- Target range for 100 kg snatch: **160–180 kg back squat minimum** (preferably 180+). Plan ahead: if snatching 100 kg now, squat 180+ for future 110 kg snatch progression.
- Peak squat strength 1–2 weeks before competition (≥90% of max).
- Avoid maxing back squat day before max snatch (allow recovery buffer).

**Loading Philosophy:**
- Conservative loading; avoid ego lifts. If a rep/weight feels suboptimal, save it for a future session.
- High volume, low intensity early in block; work up to max over 6–8 weeks.

**Hypertrophy + Weightlifting Trade-off:**
- Feasible to add muscle, but **accept slight delay in snatch/clean progress**. Hard commitment to both = seesaw trade-off.

## Athlete Relevance

| Issue | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Night shift Wed–Sun** | Don't force full training Wed–Sun. Do 1 light session + mobility/soft tissue work only. Consolidate 3–4 training days on your 2 days off. Extensive soft tissue work manages cumulative fatigue. Caffeine is essential. |
| **Chronic back pain** | Upper back strength is critical—must be included weekly (non-negotiable). Avoid heavy back squat on same day as heavy snatch. Consider lower-volume variation weeks if pain flares. Strategic, conservative loading. |
| **Weak OHS (50 kg snatch limiter)** | Upper back work addresses this directly. After hypertrophy block 1, introduce overhead-specific variations (snatch balance, pauses). Test for 4 weeks; discontinue if ineffective. Consider snatch balance as assistance work. |
| **No split jerk (push jerk only)** | Strength training (front/back squat) is critical to support push jerk progression. Target **160–180 kg back squat minimum**. |
| **6-week hypertrophy block then technique/strength** | Aligns with block philosophy. Block 1: 5×3–4×3 moderate weights, high frequency on full lifts + upper back work. Block 2: increase intensity, introduce overhead variations to target OHS weakness. Ensure upper back included in both blocks. |
| **Realistic timeline** | Hypertrophy + shift work = longer timeline for snatch/clean PBs. Prioritize upper back + squat strength foundation before chasing max snatches. |

**Key Takeaway:** Consolidate training on your 2 days off (3–4 sessions); keep nights to light work + mobility. Prioritize upper back strength (addresses back pain and OHS limiter) and back squat progression (160–180 kg target) throughout both blocks. After hypertrophy block, introduce overhead variations. Conservative loading + quality rest days = sustainable long-term progress.
