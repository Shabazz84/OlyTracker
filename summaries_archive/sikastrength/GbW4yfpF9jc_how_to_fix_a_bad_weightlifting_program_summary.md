# How To Fix A Bad Weightlifting Program

# WEIGHTLIFTING COACHING ANALYSIS

## Programming Principles
- **Detecting overtraining**: Technical breakdown (early arm bend, knee position, jumping back) is the most sensitive marker; prolonged DOMS (72+ hrs) and psychological stress (training avoidance) are secondary indicators
- **Recovery from programming errors**: 
  - Finish the block with a max-out session (heavy singles) rather than stopping abruptly—provides objective data and psychological resilience
  - 10% stepwise intensity reduction across remaining block for Grade 2 errors
  - Volume pullback secondary to intensity (reduce sets/reps slightly, not exercise selection)
  - Frequency should NOT be reduced (skill-dependent sport; need barbell contact)
- **Reset protocol**: 1–2 weeks (preferably 2) at 60–70% intensity, 2–3x/week, before starting new block
- **Hard reset triggers**: Acute injury (6–8/10 pain), overtraining syndrome (erratic HR, poor sleep, irritability, elevated inflammatory markers)

## Relevant to This Athlete

**CRITICAL ISSUES:**
1. **Chronic back pain + night shift (Wed–Sun fatigue)**: Recovery is compromised. Close monitoring for prolonged DOMS and psychological stress is essential before volume escalation in hypertrophy block.
2. **OHS 50 kg (major snatch limiter)**: Watch for technical breakdown in OHS under fatigue—arm bend early, forward shoulder shift, rounded back at higher loads. This signals going too heavy.
3. **Push jerk only (no split jerk)**: Intensity on jerks will plateau faster; volume management critical to avoid shoulder/knee stress.
4. **102.5 kg BW + 80 kg clean**: High bodyweight-to-strength ratio suggests hypertrophy block is appropriate, but back pain necessitates conservative loading progression.

**Application:**
- During 6-week hypertrophy block: Monitor back soreness daily (should resolve within 48–72 hrs). If exceeds 72 hrs or shifts to pain (not soreness), reduce intensity 10% stepwise.
- Prioritize snatch technique cues (knee recovery, arm position) at 85%+ loads to prevent bad habits.
- OHS accessory work critical; scale volume if rounding appears under fatigue.
- Build in 1–2 deload weeks post-block given night shift fatigue accumulation.

## Technique Cues Mentioned
- Snatch/clean: knee recovery, bar path (avoiding loop), catch position
- Pull: arm bend timing (should occur *after* knee extension), shoulder position (avoid hunch/forward shift)
- OHS/jerk dip: shoulder stability, spinal alignment
