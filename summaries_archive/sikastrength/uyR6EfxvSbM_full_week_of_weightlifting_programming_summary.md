# Full Week Of Weightlifting Programming

# Analysis: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Structure**: 3 main days (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 3 accessory/weakness days (Tue/Thu/Sat)
- **Phase**: Prep phase—high volume, high variation (not GPP)
- **Rationale**: Avoids single-lift-per-day approach; requires psychological/physiological recovery between full days
- **Session goal**: 6 days solid training + 1 full rest day (Sunday)
- **Intensity management**: Uses "emotionless" loading to preserve psychological currency for competition

## 2. **Technique Cues & Exercise Recommendations**
- **Hand cleans above knee** (140 kg): Technical refinement; "needs to stay connected to bar, turn over faster"
- **No-foot snatches/power cleans**: Forces bar connection, faster turnover
- **Deficit cleans**: For athletes unorganized off-floor (back position/joint alignment issues)
- **Low-hang cleans**: Addresses rushed floor pull and poor back positioning
- **Overhead walks**: Developing leg strength for split jerk stability
- **Slow eccentric deadlifts (pause above floor)**: Back organization/integrity under load

## 3. **Recovery & Injury Management (Highly Relevant)**
- **Knee pain protocol**: Eliminates exercises causing discomfort immediately (power snatches initially); progresses cautiously
- **Distinction**: Tissue inflammation ≠ re-injury (important for chronic back pain athlete)
- **Front squats**: Reduced from 5×5/5×6 to 4×3 (110–120 kg) due to knee stress
- **Full rest day (Sunday)**: Critical for tissue adaptation

## 4. **Athlete-Specific Red Flags**
- **Night shift worker (Wed–Sun)**: No programming adjustments mentioned—*potential issue* for recovery
- **Chronic back pain**: Program emphasizes back organization (low hangs, slow eccentrics) but doesn't address night shift fatigue
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Heavy snatch work (120 kg) but limited OHS accessory; deficit cleans planned, not direct OHS work
- **Split jerk struggles**: Noted but secondary; focus on overhead walks for leg strength

## 5. **Key Takeaway for This Athlete**
Prep phase works well for re-establishing confidence post-injury. However, **night shift work + chronic back pain + current OHS weakness** requires explicit fatigue management and direct overhead squat accessory work before peaking in 6 weeks.
