# Q&A - Weightlifting is Hard, MMA S&C, Knee Sleeves, Stuck on 5x5?

# Merged Coaching Summary

## Key Points

- **Mobility is a skill requiring frequency, not intensity.** Daily work (shoulder/ankle mobility) degrades without practice—critical given OHS is primary snatch limiter.
- **Technical issues are practice problems, not quick fixes.** Backward snatch jumps and missing split jerk require 6+ months of repeated practice at 60–70% loads; heavy singles won't transfer motor patterns.
- **Intermediate lifters should progress weight weekly when technique is sound**, but avoid repeating identical loads in multi-week blocks. Use 80% volume completion as progression threshold; below 70% indicates reassessment needed.
- **Jerk is current limitation (65 kg vs. 80 kg clean).** Push jerk plateau suggests need for deliberate split jerk practice and pressing volume increase.
- **Recovery quality (sleep, nutrition) is non-negotiable before intensifying**, especially for night shift workers (Wed–Sun). Modest caloric surplus (200–300 kcal/day) sufficient for hypertrophy.

---

## Technique Cues

**Snatch vertical extension:**
- Must achieve "as tall as vertical as fast as possible"
- Backward jumping indicates premature bar loss or insufficient extension
- Implement corrections at 60–70% loads, not 90%+; expect 6+ months for consolidation

**Jerk development:**
- Push jerk plateau requires deliberate **split jerk practice**—current pattern override takes months
- Pressing mechanics: wider stance, toes out, neutral spine, glutes engaged
- 2× pressing per week minimum; don't force high-intensity jerk work until split jerk develops

**Motor learning principle:**
- Focus and repetition required; visual feedback (mirror cues) slows learning ~3× vs. proprioceptive practice

---

## Programming Principles

- **Frequency:** 4 days/week optimal (e.g., Mon–Tue heavy, Thu–Sat moderate for night shift adjustment)
- **Hypertrophy block structure:** Use 5×5 or similar moderate intensity; progress loads weekly if technique sound; avoid repeating same weights
- **Session quality threshold:** 80% rep completion to progress; 70–80% requires repeat after 2–3 days; below 70% reassess conditions (sleep, nutrition, external stressors)
- **Periodization:** All training progresses from less to more specific; 6-week hypertrophy block feasible before technique/strength work
- **Conditioning baseline:** Minimal prescription (4×30min steady cardio/week) fits night shift better than aggressive conditioning; strength training is priority

## Athlete Relevance

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| **OHS 50 kg limiter** | Mobility + motor control, not strength. Daily shoulder/ankle mobility work. Consistent practice at 60–70% snatch/snatch pulls (not heavy singles). Expect 6-month consolidation. |
| **Push jerk plateau (65 kg)** | 2× jerk/week with front squat emphasis. Deliberate split jerk practice required—expect months to override current pattern. Avoid high-intensity jerk until split jerk develops. |
| **Chronic back pain** | Prioritize high-bar back squat + front squat mechanics. Avoid box squats/assisted variants (lose proprioceptive feedback). Safety Squat Bar only for acute pain management, not progression. |
| **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** | 4-day/week schedule works (Mon–Tue heavy, Thu–Sat moderate). Prioritize sleep/nutrition quality over volume; don't increase frequency without proportional volume increase. |
| **6-week hypertrophy block** | Feasible. Use 5×5 moderate intensity; progress loads weekly; reassess 1RM post-block before technique work. Conservative progression intentional—don't stagnate. |
