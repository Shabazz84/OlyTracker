# Lu Xiaojun's Weightlifting Shoe Review

**This text is not about weightlifting coaching, programming, or technique.** It's a product review of the Larun Barbel weightlifting shoe.

---

**Relevant points for this athlete (if considering shoe purchase):**

1. **Heel height (~25mm)**: Appropriate for intermediate lifters; similar to Reebok Legacy 2 and Ramaleyo 2—good for snatch/jerk positions.

2. **Sole rigidity + light weight**: Supports force application through the floor (relevant for OHS/squat deficits given chronic back pain—needs stable base).

3. **Caution**: Material is stiff/synthetic initially—may cause discomfort during night shift fatigue. Break-in period required (several weeks).

4. **Price (~$220–250 USD)**: Reviewer suggests buying budget shoes first to test preference, especially relevant if this athlete hasn't settled on footwear yet.

5. **Sizing**: Runs ~0.5 size large.

**Not applicable to this athlete's stated needs:** No programming, mobility, or technique guidance relevant to the 6-week hypertrophy block or back pain management.
