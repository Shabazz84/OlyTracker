# Weightlifting Coach Reacts To Old School VHS Russian Weightlifting Camp!

**Not a coaching transcript or article.** This is a rambling commentary on a 2000s Russian weightlifting training camp documentary—mostly anecdotal observations about lifters, outfits, and tangential discussion rather than structured coaching content.

**Limited extractable content:**

1. **Technique cues (snatch):**
   - Full hip extension is most important; don't sacrifice it for speed under
   - Minimize horizontal hip travel distance; extend hips as late as possible
   - Consistent, repeatable technical model beats individual quirks
   - No-foot snatches effective if executed correctly

2. **Technique cues (clean):**
   - Minimize forward/backward hip extension; short, precise hip action
   - Reframe "contact" as "get under point"—pulling under matters more than contact itself
   - Aggressive turnover important

3. **Exercise notes:**
   - Low hang snatches recommended variation
   - Russian programming: ~20-30% work from blocks; may negatively impact floor speed for non-elites

4. **General:**
   - Consistent technical model + incremental adjustments = long-term progress (6 months–2 years for significant change)
   - Base programming works; individual adjustments secondary

**Relevance to athlete:** None directly applicable. Commentary offers snatch extension cues but doesn't address OHS limitations, chronic back pain, or split jerk development needed.
