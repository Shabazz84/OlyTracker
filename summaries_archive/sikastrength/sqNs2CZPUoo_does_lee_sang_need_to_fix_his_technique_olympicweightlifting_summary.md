# Does Lee Sang Need To Fix His Technique? #olympicweightlifting

**ANALYSIS**

**Not directly applicable to this athlete's programming.** This is commentary on Lee Sangyong's warm-up habits, not a structured coaching prescription.

**Relevant insight:**
- **Technique principle:** Avoid reinforcing poor positional habits during warm-ups, even at heavy loads. "How you lift heavy" doesn't justify biomechanically inferior positions—technique should be intentionally corrected, not justified by current ability.

**For this athlete specifically:**
- Critical given chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg). Front rack/receiving position discipline during warm-ups is essential. Don't default to compensatory lunging patterns (forward weight shift) that stress the spine.
- During the 6-week hypertrophy block, prioritize positional consistency on low-load reps to build proper motor patterns before heavier loading.
