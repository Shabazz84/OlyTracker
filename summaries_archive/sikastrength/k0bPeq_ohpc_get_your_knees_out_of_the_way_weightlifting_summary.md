# Analysis: Knee Passage Technique in Snatch & Clean

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique Cues for Snatch (PRIMARY FOCUS):**
- Barbell moves back off floor → knees move back → shoulders move forward
- Snatch knee angle: 140-150° (partial knee bend, NOT full extension)
- Clean knee angle: 145-155° (nearly straight legs)
- Knee passage ends at lower third of thigh
- **Critical for this athlete:** Weak OHS (50 kg) suggests back/mobility limitation; coach notes snatch knee passage issues stem from forward knee angle, which loads the back excessively
- Correction priority: **Cueing for proper knee movement before adding strength work**

**Technique Corrections (not strength additions first):**
- Start with correct setup and cueing—avoid forward knee angle
- Recognize: comfort ≠ optimal technique; retraining feels awkward initially
- Do NOT rely on "feels good" feedback

**Exercise Recommendations for Knee Passage:**
1. **Pause snatches/cleans** (best option—starts from floor, builds correct position)
2. **Pause pulls + full lift** (e.g., pause snatch → full snatch)
3. Hang variations (secondary; less ideal for floor-based correction)
4. Block variations (least effective for technique)

**Key Principle:**
Technique and training are inseparable—fix movement pattern first, add strength work second.
