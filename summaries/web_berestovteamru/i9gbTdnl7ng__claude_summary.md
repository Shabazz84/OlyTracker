## **Extracted Analysis for This Athlete:**

### **1. Programming & Exercise Recommendations**
- **Snatch pull placement:** Use as warm-up/opener for snatch sessions (not a primary strength builder)
- **Sets/Reps:** Not specified, but implied moderate volume for prep
- **NOT a direct snatch technique builder** — serves mainly as shoulder girdle activation and confidence gauge
- **Variant options:** Can be done with or without heel rise; can emphasize speed (with rise) or pure strength (flat-footed)

### **2. Snatch Pull Technique Cues**
- **Setup:** Higher hip position than snatch proper (tapers up, not down to floor) — creates more vertical bar path
- **Pull trajectory:** Bar travels straight up the body, NOT to hip like snatch
- **Back drive:** Primary driver (not legs); finish with elbows HIGH until bar clears head
- **Arm action:** Minimal—mainly accompany the pull; avoid early elbow bend (don't turn it into a press)
- **Finish:** Full leg extension, optional heel rise for speed; legs stay straight (no squat/catch)
- **Grip:** Recommend *regular grip* (not hook) to avoid technique corruption that might carry to snatch

### **3. Relevance to Your Profile**
- **OHS 50 kg limiter:** Snatch pulls won't directly fix this. Needs dedicated OHS work + shoulder mobility.
- **Chronic back pain:** Pull variations with high hips may reduce lumbar stress vs. full snatch. Monitor.
- **Push jerk only:** Snatch pulls won't transfer to jerk; address split jerk separately.

### **4. Key Coaching Point**
Snatch pull ≠ snatch technique work. It's a **warm-up + shoulder/back strengthener**, not technique polish.
