## 2. **Technique Cues: Split Jerk**

**Front Leg (Push Leg):**
- Knee angle must be **obtuse (wide), NOT acute** — prevents load collapse forward and torso fold
- Foot position: neutral or slightly inward (not exaggerated external rotation)
- Knee tracks straight forward
- Leg acts as a rigid support post

**Back Leg:**
- Slightly bent (not fully extended or relaxed)
- Foot position: neutral, weight on ball of foot (not toes)
- Acts as "shock absorber"

**Split Stance Geometry:**
- Maintain **parallel feet** (not single line) — wider base = more stability
- Slide feet along platform low and fast (not jumping/dropping from above)
- Front leg steps forward first, rear leg follows — creates momentum alignment with barbell drive trajectory

**Torso:**
- Keep vertical, not collapsed forward/back
- Use line drill: stand centered on line, split should land equidistant on both sides of line
- Can use stick/pole to monitor alignment

**Recovery Pattern:**
- Front leg steps back first, rear leg second — distributes load safely, maintains continuous bar-body interaction

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Line/chalk drill** (no load or light) — torso control & balanced split position
- **Stick/pole drill** — visual feedback for vertical torso
- **Bench between legs drill** — prevents collapse to single line (use light weight; caution for beginners)
