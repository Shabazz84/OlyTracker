# Push! The elastic property of the barbell

**This is a weightlifting coaching transcript** (in Russian, by Dmitriy Berestov).

---

## RELEVANT EXTRACTIONS FOR THIS ATHLETE:

### 1. **Exercise Recommendation**
- **Push Jerk with Bar Whip** (толчок с груди на упругое свойство грифа) — accessory drill using thin bars (15kg women's bar, or 10kg/5kg) with weight loaded at collar ends to maximize bar flex/whip
- **Primary benefit for this athlete**: Develops jerk timing/speed and bar feel — directly applicable since athlete only has push jerk, not split jerk

### 2. **Technique Cues - Jerk**
- **Dip speed**: Must be FAST/explosive, not slow and smooth (резкое движение подсед)
- **Bar contact**: Bar must stay on chest during dip — common error is bar separating from chest during descent
- **Drive phase**: Initiate explosive drive immediately after dip bottoms out
- **Position transition**: Must switch from down→up motion quickly; slow transitions = bar escapes upward
- **Chest position**: Bar sits on clavicles + shoulders (широкая точка опоры = more power)

### 3. **Relevant to Athlete Profile**
- ⚠️ **Back pain consideration**: This explosive jerk accessory may stress lumbar spine; needs careful loading (start very light)
- **OHS limiter (50kg)**: This drill doesn't directly address overhead stability but strengthens jerk lockout timing
- **Split jerk transition**: Once athlete progresses to split jerk, this drill will help with timing; currently less critical for push jerk

---

**Bottom line**: Useful accessory for jerk drive explosiveness, but **address chronic back pain first before heavy implementation**.
