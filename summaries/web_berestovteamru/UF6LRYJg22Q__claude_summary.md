## Key Points

- **Strap usage:** All pulls performed with straps; proper setup essential
- **Hip height variable:** Individualized per athlete leg vs. back strength ratio—no universal standard
- **Bar path:** Vertical first, then to body; avoid premature thigh contact that kills power
- **Triple extension timing:** Simultaneous leg + back + trapezius acceleration (not sequential)
- **Knee passage:** Reflexive; avoid overcueing to prevent artificial tracking errors
- **Common error pattern:** Back dominance while legs remain passive during pull

---

## Technique Cues

**Setup & initiation:**
- Shoulders over bar (neutral position)
- NO hip rise before bar breaks ground
- Keep bar close to legs via lat engagement

**Mid-pull acceleration:**
- Vertical bar rise first, then into body
- Avoid pinning bar to thigh prematurely
- Allow slight body-bar separation during knee passage

**Final extension:**
- Simultaneous triple extension: legs explode vertically while lats drive bar into hips
- Trapezius shrug is final coordinated component, not isolated afterthought
- Maintain full foot contact throughout (no heel→toe rocking)

**Knee passage (reflexive—minimal cueing):**
- Raise hips, then subtly "drive knees back"
- Don't fully extend legs early
- Cue: "Pass knees without hitting them, stay slightly bent"

---

## Programming Principles

- Use pulls with straps to reduce grip fatigue
- Pulls build foundational leg power for hypertrophy block
- Avoid overcueing during pull learning to prevent athlete confusion from slow-motion analysis

---

## Athlete Relevance

⚠️ **Chronic back pain + weak jerk (65 kg):** Over-reliance on back extension during pulls risks pain escalation. Prioritize **leg-driven pull mechanics** immediately.

⚠️ **OHS 50 kg (snatch limiter) & no split jerk:** Solid pull strength and clean stability are prerequisites before split jerk introduction.

✅ **6-week hypertrophy block:** Implement strap-assisted pulls with simultaneous triple extension emphasis to build leg power safely before technique refinement phase.
