## EXTRACTED CONTENT

### 1. **Technique Cues: Snatch & Clean Pulling Position**
- **Setup position:** Hip hinge with slight back angle, slight knee bend, shoulders depressed, neutral spine
- **Grip reference:** Bar should contact hip crease (snatch) or mid-thigh (clean/push jerk)—use a vertical stick against body to find correct grip width
- **The pull (tяга):** Keep bar close to body, "press" thighs but allow bar to glide along legs—don't lean forward

### 2. **Technique Cues: Extension (Подрыв/Pull-Under)**
- **Critical principle:** Simultaneous, explosive opening at hip AND knee joints + trap/shoulder activation
- **Common error:** Hip extends first, legs don't activate synchronously, or legs straighten passively
- **Bar contact:** Bar MUST contact body (groin for snatch, thighs for clean) before upward flight
- **Indicator of quality:** Characteristic "sound" of proper extension

### 3. **Avoiding Pubic Bone Impact (Relevant to this athlete's back pain)**
- **Primary cause:** Bar travels away from body during pull → gap forms → athlete reaches forward into impact
- **Correction:** Maintain bar proximity throughout pull; don't initiate extension with hip/anterior tilt
- **Secondary cause:** Premature back extension ("growing tall" before leg drive)—keep shoulders over bar until true extension moment
- **In clean:** Same principle; avoid reaching hip forward during pull
- **Note:** Bruising normal for beginners; body adapts over time

### 4. **Accessory Exercise: Bar Snap-Back Drill**
- Athlete releases/relaxes bar from pull position, allowing it to drop forward naturally
- Athlete then uses back muscles (lats) to "snap" bar back to body
- **Common error in drill:** Uses hip/quad extension instead of back muscles
- **Correction:** Engage lats to bring bar back, then extend—don't drive hips forward

### 5. **Recovery Note**
- Bruising from proper technique resolves with adaptation over time
