# Analysis: Bob Takano on Jerk Technique

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Jerk drives**: 4 sets of 3–4 reps, performed at session end as supplemental work
- Emphasis: Technical work often neglected compared to pulling technique

## 2. Technique Cues (Jerk-Specific)
**Jerk Drive:**
- Complete leg/hip extension (mimics jumping pattern)
- Elbows move out *before* arms push; premature arm involvement = body pushed back
- Full extension with bar getting maximum air

**Footwork:**
- Back foot must get traction first (prevents short-stepping front foot)
- Front foot short-stepping = body pushed back, misses in front
- Avoid leaning too far forward in dip/drive (causes front-foot short-step)

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Box jumps *backwards* (emphasizes hip extension, not just vertical)
- Standing long jumps (full hip/knee extension + ankle plantarflexion)
- Single-leg hops on stairs (start 2 steps, progress to 3–4; ideally 15+ step staircase)
- Jerk drives off blocks: 4×3–4

## 4. Relevant to This Athlete
- **Push jerk only**: This athlete lacks split jerk. Takano stresses footwork/splitting as critical—consider prioritizing split jerk fundamentals over pure technique refinement in hypertrophy block.
- **Chronic back pain**: Back foot traction drills and proper positioning may reduce compensatory strain vs. poor foot placement.
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Improved jerk footwork/positioning could transfer to better overhead stability.
