# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2127/Drive-Better-by-Stopping-Better-in-the-Jerk/

1. **Programming Principles**: Focus on building a greater base of squat strength with an upright posture to improve the ability to abruptly brake the downward movement in the jerk dip. Utilize elastic energy training through specific drills rather than high-intensity max lifting for this specific skill acquisition.

2. **Technique Cues**: The primary cue is "stopping better" (abrupt braking) during the dip phase of the jerk. The goal is to prevent "bogging down" (a slow, sluggish change of direction) and avoid a forward shift in balance by maximizing the use of elastic energy in both the barbell and body.

3. **Exercise Recommendations**:
   - Jerk dips
   - Jerk dip squats
   - Jerk springs
   - Quarter squat jumps
   - Depth drops/jumps
   - Squat strength work (specifically with an upright posture)

4. **Recovery or Mobility Advice**: No specific recovery or mobility advice is provided in this text; the focus is strictly on mechanical braking and strength development for the jerk dip.

5. **Athlete Profile Relevance**: The article addresses the athlete's "push jerk only" limitation by explaining how to maximize drive efficiency without a split, which relies heavily on the dip mechanics described here. However, it does not address the athlete's chronic back pain or night shift schedule; the recommendation for high-intensity plyometrics (depth drops/jumps) may need modification given the back pain history.
