# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Program Design for Your Profile

## Key Points

- **Percentage-based prescriptions are unreliable when limited by technique/mobility.** Your OHS (50 kg) is your snatch limiter, not raw strength. Base pull intensities on *feel*, not percentages of a compromised 1RM. Establish a higher baseline in weeks 1–2, then scale future increases from there.

- **Technique primers are essential before classic lifts.** Use 3–6 sets of light overhead squat variations, tall snatches, snatch balances, or drop snatches to address your OHS weakness and mobility.

- **Ab and back work must be consistent year-round**, especially with chronic back pain:
  - Daily: ab work (all training days)
  - 1–3x/week: back extensions, reverse hypers, back planks

- **Do not combine programs.** This destroys periodization. Select one program matching your actual needs (hypertrophy + technique).

- **Deload weeks are programmed in.** Reduce weights 20%, cut reps/sets between cycles; avoid complete breaks mid-cycle.

---

## Technique Cues

- **Snatch/clean pulls**: Address "moving too fast off the ground on first pull" with slow-pull snatches and segment snatches.
- **Front squat positioning**: Pain during heavy singles or recovery from bottom position requires medical assessment; do not aggravate existing back pain.
- **Overhead squat primers**: Essential weekly to build your OHS from 50 kg and improve snatch consistency.

---

## Programming Principles

- **Test accurate 1RMs before starting percentage-based work.** Rest one week after testing.
- **Adjust your 5-day template conservatively** for night shift (Wed–Sun). Don't layer multiple program modifications.
- **Lower-volume cycles recommended** during recovery constraints (e.g., night shift, chronic back pain).
- **Feel-based intensity scaling** is superior to blind percentage adherence when technique/mobility limits your lifts.

---

## Athlete Relevance

Your 6-week hypertrophy block should prioritize:
1. **OHS development** (via consistent technique primers) as the primary snatch bottleneck
2. **Adjusted pull percentages** based on sensation, not your current snatch 1RM
3. **Daily ab work + frequent posterior chain strengthening** (back extensions, reverse hypers) to manage chronic back pain
4. **Conservative template modifications** for night shift schedule without program mixing

Seek medical clearance for front squat pain before heavy singles.
