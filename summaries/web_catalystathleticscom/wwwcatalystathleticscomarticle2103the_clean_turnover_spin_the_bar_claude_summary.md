# Analysis: "The Clean Turnover: Spin the Bar" by Greg Everett

## Relevant Extract for This Athlete

**Technique Cue — Clean Turnover:**
- **Core principle:** Elbow pivots around the bar (not bar around elbow). Bar spins actively; bearings exist for this reason.
- **Key cue:** "Pull elbows up and out to move down under the bar" — bring bar as high and back toward shoulders *before* spinning elbows around it.
- **Common fault to avoid:** Passive turnover ("just hump the bar, pull elbows back, and hope you can stand up") causes crashing, delayed catch, and unstable bottom position.

## Relevance to This Athlete's Profile

**High relevance:**
- At **Clean 80 kg with poor turnover mechanics**, this athlete likely experiences:
  - Crashing catches (unstable in receiving position)
  - Excessive loading on the spine during catch — problematic given *chronic back pain*
  - Energy waste before the dip, limiting jerk potential (already weak at 65 kg push jerk)

- **Immediate application:** Prioritize turnover drills (tall cleans, power cleans, muscle cleans) to reduce spinal compression and improve catch stability during hypertrophy block.
