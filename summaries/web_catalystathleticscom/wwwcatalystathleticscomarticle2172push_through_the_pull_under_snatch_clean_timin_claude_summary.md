# Analysis: Greg Everett on Snatch/Clean Timing

## 1. **Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)**
- **Critical concept**: Overlap the 2nd and 3rd pulls—don't treat them as two distinct segments
- **Key timing**: Continue leg drive *into* the pull under (don't lift feet prematurely)
- **Mental cue**: "Pull the bar up" continuously; weight forces your body down, not vice versa
- **Foot mechanics**: Delay foot lift momentarily while maintaining ground pressure to preserve bar elevation
- **Common error**: Don't pause at full extension; never have "slack in the system"

**Practice methods:**
- Snatches/cleans from power position (focus on leg drive→transition)
- Pull + lift complexes (replicate extended pull feeling)
- Tall snatches (flat-footed, push onto toes as you pull under—exaggerates drive/pull overlap)
