# Analysis: Leg Drive in Snatch & Clean (Greg Everett)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
None explicitly stated. Article is technique-focused, not programming.

## 2. **Technique Cues – Snatch & Clean Pull**
- **Leg drive = knee extension + ankle plantar flexion** (concurrent actions)
- **Elevation:** Legs provide a solid platform; without active leg drive, hip extension force is absorbed by passive (soft) legs instead of transferring to the bar
- **Direction:** Leg drive keeps bar trajectory vertical; without it, hips drive forward excessively, pushing the bar forward
- **Balance:** Aggressive leg drive maintains forward/backward balance throughout the pull
- **Integration:** "Hips provide speed; legs provide elevation"—both must work together, not compete

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Pulls or high pulls** recommended to develop leg drive capacity

None provided.

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
⚠️ **HIGHLY RELEVANT:**
- OHS (50 kg) is snatch limiter; weak leg drive in pull directly undermines receiving position strength and confidence
- Chronic back pain may be exacerbated by excessive hip-forward lean (weak leg drive = forward bar path = spinal load)
- Cue: **"Push legs hard while hips explode"**—cue both simultaneously, not sequentially
- Prioritize pulls/high pulls in hypertrophy block to build leg drive stability
