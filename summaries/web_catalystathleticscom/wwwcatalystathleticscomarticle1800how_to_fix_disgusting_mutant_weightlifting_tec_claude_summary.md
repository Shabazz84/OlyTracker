# Analysis: Matt Foreman - "How To Fix Disgusting Mutant Weightlifting Technique"

## Relevant to This Athlete:

**Programming Principles:**
- **3-step technique rebuild model:**
  1. Strip to empty bar, teach correct movement (forget weight temporarily)
  2. Extended period (weeks/months) at light-moderate weights to ingrain pattern (extended time = 4-6 weeks minimum based on case study)
  3. Gradual weight progression with constant form monitoring; regress if technique breaks
- Real case: athlete moved 103kg (mutant form) → 70kg floor for 1 month → 75-80kg for 6 weeks → 85-90kg for 1 month → 95-100kg pre-competition
- **Key principle:** Expect 15-25 lbs gains after technique solidification (not before)

**Directly Applicable:**
- **Your OHS 50kg is your snatch limiter** — this article strongly suggests stripping snatch work back and re-grooving from empty bar/light weights rather than forcing heavy attempts. Your split jerk limitation may also mask poor receiving position mechanics.
- **Your chronic back pain** makes technique precision essential; poor mechanics amplify injury risk, especially in catch positions
- **Night shift work stress** makes disciplined, patient technique work harder but more necessary (can't afford injury setbacks)

**Not Covered:**
- Hypertrophy periodization (your 6-week goal)
- Specific mobility for back pain
- Recovery strategies for shift workers

---

**Bottom line:** Foreman advocates seeking professional coaching to rebuild your snatch/clean mechanics from the ground up before pursuing your 6-week hypertrophy block. Your low OHS suggests postural/receiving position issues worth addressing first.
