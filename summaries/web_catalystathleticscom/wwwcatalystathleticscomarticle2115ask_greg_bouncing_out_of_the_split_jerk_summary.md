**1. Programming principles**
*   **Warm-up Strategy:** Perform all warm-up jerks with a deliberate 2–3 second hold in the split receiving position to build stability and awareness.
*   **Top-End Work:** On working/heaviest sets where holding is impossible or unnatural, allow immediate recovery (bounce out) to avoid limiting top-end performance while training the nervous system for heavy loads.

**2. Technique cues**
*   **Diagnosis over Force:** Do not force a static hold if it masks underlying technical flaws; use the "hold" attempt as a diagnostic tool to identify balance issues, front-foot dominance, or forward bar drive.
*   **Receiving Position:** Focus on avoiding being too heavy on the front foot (caused by dipping/driving forward) and over-reaching with the back leg.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Split Jerk Variations:** Incorporate split jerks at sub-maximal weights specifically for the purpose of holding the landing position to improve eccentric stability.
