## Analysis: Training Through Injuries in Olympic Weightlifting

**Relevant to Athlete Profile:**

1. **Recovery/Injury Management (CRITICAL)**
   - Work *around* chronic back pain, not through it—eliminate aggravating movements
   - Be conservative; test new exercises cautiously and monitor 1-2 days post-execution for delayed pain
   - Use injury/limitation as opportunity to address weaknesses (e.g., OHS limiter, accessory work)

2. **Exercise Substitution Strategy**
   - Single-leg work (step-ups, split squats) as back-friendly alternatives
   - Belt squat option if back pain limits traditional squats
   - Look at *purpose* not just movement pattern—find alternatives that achieve similar effects
   - Front squat modifications (straps, handless rack position) if wrist/grip limited

3. **Programming Principle**
   - Prioritize weak points during injury phases (e.g., OHS work during 6-week hypertrophy block)
   - Stay engaged in training environment even if modifying intensity/movement selection
   - Night shift schedule demands flexibility—adapt rather than skip

4. **Mindset**
   - Creativity and adaptability essential for managing chronic pain while progressing
   - Maintain gym presence to preserve motivation/enthusiasm

**Gap:** Article doesn't address snatch/jerk technique, specific rep schemes for hypertrophy block, or shift-work recovery protocols.
