## 2. **Technique Cues (Highly Relevant)**
**Foot placement in snatch/clean:**
- Actively separate feet from platform (minimal air required)
- Replace feet **flat on whole foot**, not ball of foot
- Land **directly under bar** for balance
- Cue: "Lift knees" rather than feet to prevent backward kick
- Cue: "Sit hips straight down with vertical torso" (not pulling hips back post-extension)
- Avoid excessive elevation (12+ inches)—reduces downward acceleration resistance

**For this athlete:** Critical—his weak OHS (50 kg) suggests receiving position issues. Proper flat-foot landing directly under bar improves stability and confidence in the catch.
