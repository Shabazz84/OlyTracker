1. **Programming Principles**: Power snatches/cleans (PSN/PCL) should be treated strictly as **assistance exercises**, not primary lifts. They must be performed at high receiving positions (quarter-squat depth), never near parallel or full depth. Competitive lifters should prioritize full bottom-position movements for the majority of practice; overusing PSN/PCL risks disrupting timing and causing technical inconsistencies ("wobbles") in full lifts.

2. **Technique Cues**: 
   - **Depth Control**: Do not "brake" the bar to stay above parallel; this creates a hybrid movement that compromises the skill of catching low.
   - **Receiving Position**: Catch at a high position (quarter-squat) where the lift looks distinctly like a power variation, not a full squat with a brake applied.
   - **Focus**: Prioritize speed and force production in the second/third pull; do not focus on lifting maximal loads that tempt the athlete to drop depth for ego validation.

3. **Exercise Recommendations**: 
   - Use PSN/PCL as lighter variations for training days or for athletes lacking mobility to sit deep overhead.
   - Limit usage: Do not overemphasize these lifts in a competitive lifter's program; they are tools, not the main event.
   - Avoid using them to test personal records based on depth criteria.
