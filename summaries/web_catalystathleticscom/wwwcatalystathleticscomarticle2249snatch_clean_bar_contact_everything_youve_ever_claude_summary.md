# Analysis: Snatch & Clean Bar Contact by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
None directly stated. This is pure technique content.

## 2. **Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean**
**Bar Contact (Critical for this athlete):**
- Bar *must* contact body during proper extension—it's biomechanically forced, not optional
- Contact occurs when shoulders move behind bar (at vertical trunk position during scoop/double knee bend)
- **Key action:** Keep bar close to legs throughout pull without dragging; use back/shoulder tension to prevent forward drift
- Contact should be "brush" not "bang"—aim for upward bar momentum at contact, not collision that pushes bar forward
- Avoid premature contact (pushes balance backward; bar hits low on legs and gets knocked forward)

**Second Pull Position:**
- Wait until bar reaches thighs before initiating second pull
- Continue vertical floor drive through complete hip extension
- Stay "over" the bar to prevent early leg contact

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Deadlift/pull variations (build bar proximity/tension)
- Power position snatch/clean
- Snatch/clean with no jump
- Pull to hold + snatch/clean
- High-pull + snatch/clean

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
**Highly relevant:** OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg). Poor bar contact/positioning in snatch pulls directly constrains receiving position strength. This technical foundation should precede hypertrophy block.
