# Analysis: Jerk Style Comparison (Everett)

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Critical Issue: Split Jerk Transition Required**
- Athlete currently uses **push jerk only** (65 kg); text explains why split jerk is superior for competitive lifting and progression
- Text directly states: "New lifters are encouraged to learn, practice and train the split jerk until good reason is found to adopt another style"
- **Action**: Must develop split jerk competency now, not later—power jerk has limited ceiling

---

## Key Programming/Technique Points

**Why Split Jerk for This Athlete:**
1. **Receiving depth advantage** – allows ~parallel squat depth with much better knee mechanics than power jerk
2. **Bar position tolerance** – split accommodates imprecision; critical given athlete's limited OHS (50 kg) and likely mobility deficits from chronic back pain
3. **Stability** – broader base (width + fore-aft length) allows corrective foot adjustments to stabilize overhead
4. **Recovery ease** – can wedge up incrementally; power jerk demands single straight-up drive

**What NOT to Do:**
- Don't abandon split early because it feels awkward—"takes time to learn"
- Power jerk's apparent simplicity is a trap at heavier weights

---

## Recovery Note
- Text mentions split jerk is easier to recover from at depth—relevant to chronic back pain management (less demand on single explosive drive)
