# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1823/A-Different-Way-to-Set-Squat-and-Deadlift-PRs/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 4 days/week (Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat) or 3 days/week for those with additional conditioning work.
*   **Structure:** Olympic lifts are the primary focus; Squats and Deadlifts are treated as *assistance* exercises performed at the end of sessions.
*   **Volume/Intensity:** Competition lifts (Snatch, Clean & Jerk) utilize mostly singles/doubles (triples occasionally). Assistance lifts and squats use sets of 2–5 reps. Heavy max singles in assistance lifts are rare.
*   **Philosophy:** "Accumulated strength" drives PRs in Squat/Deadlift through high frequency of pulling movements and squatting patterns, rather than dedicated powerlifting cycles.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   *None explicitly detailed for specific lift mechanics (e.g., foot placement, bar path) within this text.* The focus is on program structure, not technical execution cues.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary:** Snatch, Clean & Jerk, Rack Jerk, Power Clean.
*   **Pulling Assistance:** Snatch pulls, Clean pulls, High Pulls (100 lbs above pull weight), Deadlift variations (Clean-grip DL/RDL).
*   **Squatting:** Back Squat, Front Squat, Hang Cleans from knee height.
*   **Overhead/Press:** Push Press, 3-position snatch pulls.
*   **Accessory:** Ab work every session; Pullups/Chinups (recommended on a lifting day, not rest days).

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   *None specific to mobility protocols found in the text.*
*   **Rest Days:** Explicitly recommended as "true REST" days rather than active recovery with pulling movements.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile (Intermediate, 102.5kg, Back Pain, Night Shift)**
*   **Back Pain Warning:** The article advocates for *no* dedicated powerlifting deadlifts and relies on high-volume pulling (C&J, pulls). For an athlete with chronic back pain, the cumulative fatigue from "lots and lots of reps" in a 4-day OL-focused program could exacerbate issues without careful load management.
*   **Night Shift Constraint:** The suggested schedule (Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat) conflicts with a Wed–Sun night shift. The athlete would need to adapt this template to fit their off-days, likely shifting the training block to Tue/Fri or utilizing a 3-day model on non-shift days.
*   **Squat Focus:** The text supports using Squats as assistance (Back/Front) rather than maxing out weekly, which aligns well with an intermediate athlete looking for hypertrophy and strength without overloading the spine with heavy singles immediately.
*   **Jerk Limitation:** The article mentions "Rack Jerk" and "Push Press," which supports the athlete's current push-jerk-only status, though it does not address the transition to a split jerk.
