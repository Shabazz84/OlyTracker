## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**

**Critical for this athlete (102.5 kg, OHS 50 kg limiter):**
- **Likely long-limbed:** At 102.5 kg with BS 118 kg but OHS only 50 kg (42% of BS), suggests **mechanical disadvantage in overhead positions** (longer femurs = longer resistance arm in squat, but also likely longer arms = poor OHS leverage)
- **Implication:** OHS deficit may be anatomical, not solely strength. Focus on technique refinement + positional drills rather than expecting dramatic OHS gains
