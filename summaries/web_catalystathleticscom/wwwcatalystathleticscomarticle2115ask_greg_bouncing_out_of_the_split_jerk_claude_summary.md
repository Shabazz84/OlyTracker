## **1. Technique Cue (Split Jerk)**
- **Stick the landing 2–3 seconds** in the split position before recovery
- **Diagnose underlying issues**: front-foot heaviness often masks poor dip, forward drive, or arm push
- **Avoid rushing recovery** early; it can mask balance/stability problems that limit max lifts

---

## **2. Key Principle for This Athlete**
> "The longer [the problem] goes unchecked, the more difficult it will be to correct."

**Applies here:** Athlete has chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg). Rushing through positions may compound postural issues. Deliberate position work in warm-ups is safer.

---

## **3. Programming Note**
- Warm-up jerks: enforce 2–3 second holds for stability/awareness training
- Top-end jerks: allow natural recovery once weight becomes prohibitive
- Carries over to strength phase

---

## **4. Relevant Gap**
