# Analysis: Competition Attempt Selection Article

**Relevance to this athlete:** Limited direct application. This is a competition strategy article; the athlete is in a 6-week hypertrophy block before technique/strength work—not competition prep.

## Key Extracts (if applicable to future competition planning):

### 1. **Testing & Timing Principles**
- Test maximal lifts 3–4 weeks before competition
- Last max C&J: 10–14 days out
- Last max snatch: 7–10 days out
- *Caveat:* "Lifters typically need more time...the older, heavier, and more advanced they are"
- **Relevance:** This athlete (102.5 kg, intermediate) will need longer recovery between heavy attempts and meet

### 2. **Opener Strategy**
- Opener = weight lifter has made "many times" with confidence
- For snatch under ~130–140 kg: avoid 5 kg jumps; prefer 3–4 kg progressions
- Example: 93→97→100 kg (4→3 kg jumps)
- **Relevance:** At Clean 80 kg, athlete should open snatch conservatively around 65–70 kg with 2–3 kg jumps

### 3. **Without Testing Max (Competition-Only Approach)**
- Open ≥1 kg higher than last meet's opener
- Use training cycle improvements (squats, pulls, variations) to estimate PR potential
- Conservative planning; adjust on meet day during warm-up

### 4. **Not Directly Applicable**
- Athlete is not prepping for competition now
- Back pain and OHS deficit (50 kg) are not addressed
- Night shift fatigue management not covered

**Bottom line:** Useful reference for future meet prep (6+ weeks out), but not actionable for current hypertrophy block.
