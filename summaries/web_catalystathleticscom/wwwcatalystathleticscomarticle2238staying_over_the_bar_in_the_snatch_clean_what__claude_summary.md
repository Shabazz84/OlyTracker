# Analysis: "Staying Over the Bar in the Snatch & Clean"

## 2. Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean
**"Staying Over the Bar" (First Pull Phase):**
- Shoulders approximately **above the bar until the knee**, then **slightly in front between knee and mid-thigh**
- Default: **shoulder joint directly above bar at start** (arms vertical)
- Maintain this position through first pull without opening hips early
- **Purpose:** Maximize balance, bar proximity, hip extension force, and allow vertical leg drive (not horizontal)

**Common Error:**
- Shoulders moving behind bar too early = reduced hip extension, backward balance shift, incomplete second pull, jumping away from bar

**Finish Position (Different Phase):**
- Shoulders DO move behind bar at top of pull (this is correct—don't confuse with first pull cue)
- Extended legs vertical, hips slightly hyperextended

**Proportions Matter:**
- Shorter legs/longer torso lifters: Move behind bar sooner (leverage advantage)
- Longer legs/shorter torso: Stay over longer (hip dominance advantage)

## 3. Exercise Recommendations (Strength-Skill Building)
- Halting deadlifts (pause at mid-thigh)
- Segment deadlifts/pulls (pauses at knee + mid-thigh)
- Segment snatches/cleans (pause at mid-thigh)
- Complexes combining paused variations with full lifts
- Slow eccentrics on pull/deadlift variations

**Critical Note:** Execute these in correct positions—you strengthen exactly the position you practice.

## 4. Relevance to This Athlete

**Highly Relevant:**
- **OHS 50 kg limiter + chronic back pain**: Poor "staying over bar" during snatch pull could be compensating with excessive forward/backward shifts, stressing lower back. Paused segment work should help establish stability.
- **Night shift worker**: Programmed paused work suits fatigue—lower CNS demand than max-effort pulls, fits recovery constraints.
- **Clean 80 kg vs Jerk 65 kg (push jerk only)**: Clean pulling mechanics directly impact catch position confidence; staying-over-bar cue improves clean consistency.
- **Back squat 118 kg, clean 80 kg**: Significant strength base; paused pulls at mid-thigh are accessible and won't overfatigue.
