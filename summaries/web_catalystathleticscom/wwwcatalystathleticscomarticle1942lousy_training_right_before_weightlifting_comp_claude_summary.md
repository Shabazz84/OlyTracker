**This article is about competition psychology and meet preparation, NOT training programming or technique.**

**Relevant to this athlete:**

1. **Meet Week Taper Principle** (applies to future 6-week block → competition phase)
   - Design meet week to freshen up and rest, not maintain intensity
   - Body may feel "ragged" 2–3 weeks pre-meet despite good preparation—this is normal, not regression
   - Sharp warmup performance on meet day often signals readiness despite poor training weeks prior

2. **Mental/Competitive Toughness**
   - Emphasized as critical factor; psychological resilience matters as much as programming
   - Relevant for night shift worker managing fatigue and chronic back pain—mental fortitude aids recovery tolerance
