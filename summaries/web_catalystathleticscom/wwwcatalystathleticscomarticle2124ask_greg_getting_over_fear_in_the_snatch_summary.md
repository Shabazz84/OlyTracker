# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2124/Ask-Greg-Getting-Over-Fear-in-the-Snatch/

1. **Programming Principles:** Use wave loading (oscillating weights) rather than linear progression when approaching max attempts to build confidence; ensure every rep from warm-up to max is technically consistent.
2. **Technique Cues:** "Arms like ropes" (relax arms during first and second pulls); maintain solid trunk positioning by addressing ankle/hip mobility; reinforce proper overhead stability via snatch presses, push presses, and snatch balances.
3. **Exercise Recommendations:** Overhead squats (OHS), snatch balances, snatch presses, and snatch push presses to build dynamic receiving strength and confidence.
4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice:** Address poor ankle and hip mobility to prevent structural collapse overhead; diagnose and eliminate pain or instability in wrists, elbows, or shoulders before increasing load.
5. **Athlete Profile Relevance:** The athlete's chronic back pain requires immediate diagnosis as a potential cause of "fear" (guarding behavior); the 50kg OHS limiter suggests prioritizing overhead stability drills over heavy loading; the night shift schedule necessitates strict attention to sleep/recovery to manage the psychological fear response.
