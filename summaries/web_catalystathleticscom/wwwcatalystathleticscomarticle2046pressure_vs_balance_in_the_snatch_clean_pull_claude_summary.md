## 2. **Technique Cues (High Relevance)**

**Key concepts for this athlete:**
- **Balance stays constant** — Line of Gravity (LoG) remains slightly behind mid-foot throughout the pull, NOT shifting forward with heels off ground
- **Pressure ≠ Balance** — Feeling pressure on balls of foot doesn't mean center of mass shifted forward; it's ankle extension while balance is maintained
- **Two pull tasks:**
  1. Stay balanced over same place (LoG behind mid-foot)
  2. Produce maximum force against ground
- **Natural progression:** Heel rises as extension finishes (pressure shifts forward); don't fight this
- **Self-check:** If jumping forward/backward = balance shifted (error)

**Applicable to athlete's 50 kg OHS limiter:** Poor pull balance will degrade receiving position stability.
