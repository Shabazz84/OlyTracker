# Analysis: "Finding Your Jerk Drive Stance" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Technique Cues – Jerk Drive Stance**
- **Foot position**: Heels at hip-width or slightly wider; toes out 10–20°; legs approximately vertical
- **Critical principle**: Ability to brake abruptly in the dip and reverse powerfully > perfectly vertical leg orientation
- **Knee initiation**: Start with knees *unlocked* and quads under tension (not hyperextended, not bent)—prevents freefall and bar loss of connection
- **Knees track feet** during dip (directional alignment)

## 2. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Jerk dip squats, jerk dips, jerk drives to test stance width and find strongest bottom position

## 3. **Relevance to This Athlete**

**CRITICAL**: This athlete **only has a push jerk** (65 kg). The article focuses on *drive stance for split jerks*. 
- For push jerk development: focus on dip depth, elasticity, and drive power still applies
- **Stance experimentation needed**: With longer legs relative to strength (BS 118 kg vs. Jerk 65 kg), a *wider stance* may be beneficial
- **Accessory suggestion**: Jerk dip/drive work to improve weak jerk drive before attempting split jerk progression
