# Analysis: "Before You Squat: Physical and Mental Preparation" by Greg Everett

## Relevance to Athlete Profile
**HIGH RELEVANCE** — This article directly addresses squat preparation, mobility, and mental focus. Given the athlete's chronic back pain and OHS limitation (50 kg), the mobility work is critical.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- No specific periodization guidance
- **Warm-up structure**: Empty bar → 50-70 kg jumps → working weight
- Squat placement: Can be end of session (after snatch/clean) or earlier; requires warm-up adjustment
- Mental preparation essential for heavy sets

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Squat depth**: Ability to sit comfortably to full depth without pain/discomfort is prerequisite for proper positioning
- **Unracking**: Drive bar out of rack with confidence and forcefulness (affects mental state and performance)
- **Foot mechanics**: Push feet into floor AND against band resistance (not knees independently)

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
**Foam rolling sequence** (tailored for squat prep):
- Upper back/T-spine (mobility bottleneck)
- Glutes, adductors, quads (VMO + lateral focus), calves
- **Deep stretch**: Wide stance, elbows between thighs, pushing femurs outward
- **Ankle mobility**: Foot flat, lean forward, close ankle joint
- Mini-band squats (slow, paused)

---

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Consistent thorough warm-up mandatory
- **Key for this athlete**: Calf/ankle mobility directly addresses lower leg pain limiting squat depth
- Gentle bouncing in paused bottom position aids stretching

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **Chronic back pain**: Emphasizes proper warm-up to avoid compensation patterns; suggests glute/adductor focus
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Ankle/calf mobility work directly transfers to overhead position
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Scheduling squat sessions requires consistent pre-squat protocol regardless of fatigue

---

## **Application for 6-Week Hypertrophy Block**
Implement full warm-up sequence before every squat session. Prioritize calf/ankle + T-spine mobility to address OHS ceiling.
