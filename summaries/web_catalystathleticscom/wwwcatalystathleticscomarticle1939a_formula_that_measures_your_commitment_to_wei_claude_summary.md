**Not directly applicable to this athlete's programming.**

This is a **mental/emotional article** about personal responsibility during training plateaus, not technical or programming guidance.

**Potentially relevant to athlete's profile:**

The five recovery/lifestyle categories Foreman outlines are worth the athlete's attention given their constraints:

1. **Body care** (stretching, mobility, massage) — critical given chronic back pain
2. **Sleep** — challenging with Wed–Sun night shifts; prioritize sleep quality/quantity around training days
3. **Nutrition** — support hypertrophy block + night shift schedule
4. **Stress management** — shift work naturally disrupts recovery
5. **Attitude** — realistic self-assessment of effort given life demands (night shift work)

**Action:** Athlete should rate themselves 1–10 in each category to identify which non-programming factors may be limiting progress, especially before attributing slow gains to poor programming during the 6-week hypertrophy block.
