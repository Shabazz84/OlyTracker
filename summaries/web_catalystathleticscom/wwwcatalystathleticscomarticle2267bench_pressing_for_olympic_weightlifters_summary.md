2. **Technique Cues**:
   - **Grip**: Use a relatively narrow grip.
   - **Elbow Position**: Keep upper arms within ~45 degrees from the sides; avoid flared elbows or wide grips which "grind shoulders."
   - **Warm-up**: Do not rush; adequate preparation is mandatory to prevent shoulder injury.

3. **Exercise Recommendations**:
   - **Primary Alternative**: Push press, jerk variations (dip and drive), and jerk supports (builds tricep strength + overhead mobility).
   - **Compensatory Work**: Vertical pulling (pull-ups/pulldowns) and horizontal pulling (rows) to maintain shoulder health.
   - **Mobility**: Passive static stretches for pecs/shoulders, dynamic arm swings/circles, and CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations).

4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**: Prioritize overhead range of motion maintenance. Ensure total upper body pulling volume exceeds pushing volume to balance the stress placed on the shoulder girdle.
