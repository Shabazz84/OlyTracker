# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1977/Improving-Your-Footwork-in-the-Split-Jerk/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Complexes:** Combine Push Press + Push Jerk (feet connected) + Split Jerk to enforce balanced dip/drive mechanics before footwork execution.
*   **Drill Loading:** Use empty bar or max 30% of best jerk for technique drills; load heavier only when movement accuracy is perfect.
*   **Duration:** Hold split positions for 2–3 seconds during drills to reinforce balance and stability.
*   **Frequency:** Focus on quality over volume; "More weight means nothing" if form degrades.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Balance:** Upon foot contact, hips must be under shoulders with weight evenly distributed front-to-back.
*   **Bar Path:** Push bar slightly *back* behind the head as the front foot moves forward (counteracts diving chest/head).
*   **Dip/Drive:** Dip straight down and drive straight up; feet stay connected to floor in push/push-jerk variations to prevent forward shifting.
*   **Timing:** Ensure sufficient speed/elevation on the drive; without it, "short-stepping" is inevitable due to lack of time/space.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Drills:** Tall Jerk (half-press flat-footed), Jerk Balance (step back 1 foot-length from split position).
*   **Strength/Position:** Push Press, Push Jerk (feet connected), Split Jerk Behind the Neck, Drop to Split, Jump to Split.
*   **Unilateral Strength:** Lunges and Split Squats for weak split stances.
*   **Power Drives:** Jumping Squats, Jumping Quarter Squats, Back Squat Jumps (to improve bar elevation).
