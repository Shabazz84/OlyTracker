**NOT PRIMARILY WEIGHTLIFTING-SPECIFIC**

This is a mental/psychological coaching article emphasizing mindset, preparation, and positivity—not technical or programming content.

---

## Relevant to This Athlete:

**Mindset & Preparation (Highly Relevant)**
- Study workouts hours before training (critical for night shift worker—do this before/after sleep)
- Visualize successful lifts with sensory detail before each session
- Reframe failures into specific, actionable task lists (e.g., "pull elbows up aggressively" vs. vague "snatch better")
- Use training journal to track subjective factors (sleep quality, mood, enthusiasm)—essential given chronic back pain and shift work impact recovery

**For This Athlete Specifically:**
- Post-snatch failures (OHS limiter at 50 kg), write 1–2 specific technical cues to execute next snatch session
- Note sleep/shift schedule effects in journal to identify patterns affecting performance
- Visualization crucial for building confidence in weak jerk (65 kg push jerk only)

**Coaching Implications:**
- Coach should balance critique with acknowledgment of effort/aggression (especially given chronic pain challenges)
- Positive cues aligned with action: "drive knees forward" vs. "don't stay back"
