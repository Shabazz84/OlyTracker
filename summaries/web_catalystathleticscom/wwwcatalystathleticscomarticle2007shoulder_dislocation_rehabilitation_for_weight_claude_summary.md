# Analysis: Shoulder Dislocation Rehab - Part 4 (Greg Everett)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Phase focus**: Strength/bodyweight recovery (4 weeks pre-competition cycle)
- **Squat work**: Daily heavy singles + 2x2 @ 90% (efficient strength recovery method)
- **Frequency**: 6 days/week split
- **Pull variation strategy**: Different variation each exposure (snatch pull on riser, segment pulls, slow eccentrics, etc.) to avoid monotony
- **Load selection**: "By feel" daily; progress week-to-week without grinding or failure
- **Philosophy**: Feel good post-workout, not destroyed

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- Limited direct snatch/jerk work during rehab phase (12 weeks out from competition, first 4 weeks "weightless")
- Emphasis on **postural strength & positioning** in pulls (not heavy loading)
- Muscle snatch + overhead squat complexes (mobility + strength)
- Tall cleans & muscle cleans (technical reinforcement without heavy load)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Core**: Weighted planks, GHD sit-ups, decline/standing crunches, plate twists (anti-rotation)
- **Leg accessory**: Step-ups, 2-leg bounding, box jumps (power maintenance)
- **Back**: Weighted back extensions, T-bar rows, stiff-legged deadlifts
- **Shoulder health**: Band work (internal/external rotation, IYT) at every workout start
- **Conditioning**: Weekly hill sprints (~100m steep grade)
- **Carries**: Farmer's walks, 1-arm DB overhead carries (shoulder stability)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- **Pre-workout ritual**: Band shoulder rehab/pre-hab (no skipping)
- Avoid pain-driven decisions; focus on positional quality
- Gradual return—no snatches/jerks early (surgeon-mandated)
- Bodyweight restoration priority (leg strength + hypertrophy work)

## 5. **Relevance to Your Athlete Profile**

| Your Issue | Everett's Solution |
|---|---|
| **Chronic back pain** | Daily squat singles + core emphasis (planks, GHD work, anti-rotation) |
| **OHS limiter (50 kg)** | Muscle snatch + OHS complexes; band shoulder work; frequent varied pulls |
| **Push jerk only** | Drop to split practice (Saturday); snatch push press + OHS pairing |
| **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** | *Not addressed*—consider front-loading Monday–Tuesday heavy work, lighter Wed–Sun |
| **6-week hypo block goal** | This template emphasizes daily leg work + varied accessory—directly applicable |

**Key takeaway**: Use variation in pulls/cleans, prioritize core/back stability, add band shoulder prep daily, progress squat strength via heavy singles method.
