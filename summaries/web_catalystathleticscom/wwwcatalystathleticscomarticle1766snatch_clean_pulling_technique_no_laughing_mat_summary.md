**2. Technique cues for snatch/clean**
*   **Toe-Extension Pulling:** Drive up onto the toes at the top of the pull to maximize vertical extension height ("tallest point"). This creates a "jump" sensation where feet slap the floor upon turnover.
*   **Flat-Foot Pulling:** Maintain foot contact with the floor as long as possible during the pull, allowing only a slight heel rise before full extension. This maximizes force transfer through the entire surface area of the foot.
*   **Avoid "Floating":** Do not extend so high that feet leave the ground excessively early (hang-time), causing a delay in turnover. Feet should connect with the ground as soon as the bar is turned over.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Tall Snatches / Snatch Balances:** Specifically recommended to practice connecting feet quickly with the ground after lifting them, ensuring no "floating" occurs during turnover.
