# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1788/Warm-up-Early-Weightlifting-Training-Mobility-Preparation/

**Relevant to this athlete's profile:**

**Recovery/Mobility Advice (Primary content):**
- **Separate morning mobility session** (5-20 min): dynamic stretches, foam rolling, static stretches, ab work, rotator cuff/prehab
- **Abbreviated evening warm-up** when training later same day
- Benefits: improved mobility over time, better recovery, addresses tendency to skip prehab when fatigued
- **Specific recommendation for night shift worker**: Do quick dynamic stretches immediately upon waking, then abbreviated warm-up before evening training session

**Application to this athlete:**
- **Critical fit**: Night shift schedule (Wed–Sun) makes early mobility work logistically challenging but valuable for chronic back pain management
- Could incorporate morning mobility on off-days (Mon–Tue) or brief session before sleep
- Foam rolling emphasis on glutes, quads, hamstrings, adductors relevant to squat/pull limitations
- Prehab (rotator cuff) supports jerk development and shoulder mobility for OHS (primary snatch limiter at 50 kg)

**Not addressed:** Programming, technique cues, or exercise-specific recommendations beyond mobility.
