# Analysis: "Loose Knees = Loose Jerks" by Greg Everett

## Relevant Content

**Technique Cue - Jerk:**
- **Pre-dip setup**: Unlock knees from passive lock and create quad tension before initiating jerk dip
- **Cue**: Minimal visible knee bend—shift from slack quads to active tension while maintaining heel contact and balance
- **Why**: Prevents loss of bar contact, forward weight shift, and disrupted dip-drive rhythm

**Directly Relevant to Athlete:**
- Critical for push jerk development (athlete's only jerk variant)
- Addresses common jerk control issues that could limit drive power
- Low implementation barrier—mental pre-lift checklist item

## Not Addressed
- Hypertrophy programming (sets/reps/intensity)
- OHS improvement strategies (primary snatch limiter)
- Back pain accommodation
- Shift work recovery considerations

---

**Recommendation**: Apply pre-dip knee tension cue immediately in push jerk sessions. This foundational fix may unlock better rhythm and bar connection before progressing split jerk (if coaches eventually recommend it).
