# Analysis: Top 5 Assistance Exercises for the Clean

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Intensity**: Power clean + clean complex; clean pulls at 90–105% of best clean
- **Exercise selection**: Varies by athlete need; assistance work supports strength, speed, position, timing, power, mobility
- **Complexity**: Front/back squat and clean itself are primary; assistance exercises are secondary

## 2. Technique Cues for Clean
- **Turnover phase**: Bar "crashes" onto shoulders due to grip release and poor bar proximity—power clean addresses this
- **First/second pull**: Shoulders stay in front of bar until mid-to-upper thigh; initiate explosive second pull from this position
- **Postural strength**: Maintain shins ~vertical, bar at mid-upper thigh, shoulders ahead of bar during pull

## 3. Exercise Recommendations (Ranked Value for This Athlete)
1. **Halting Clean Deadlift** — *Critical*: Addresses positional strength (shoulders ahead of bar); holds 3 sec at mid-thigh. Directly combats chronic back pain by enforcing proper posture under load.
2. **Power Clean** — Improves turnover speed and bar elevation; reduces harsh catches that aggravate back.
3. **Clean Pull on Riser** — Strengthens leg drive off floor; reduces hip dominance (helps with OHS 50 kg limiter).
4. **Clean Pull (standard)** — General pull strength/position; can use slow floor-to-mid-thigh variant for postural work.
5. **Sots Press** — Mobility-first; improves thoracic extension and ankle/hip mobility (relevant to OHS weakness and night shift postural stress).

## 4. Recovery & Mobility Advice
- Sots press recommended at workout start for ankle, hip, thoracic mobility and upper back activation
- Emphasize quality over load in mobility work

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
- **Chronic back pain**: Halting clean deadlift + power clean reduce "crashing" wear on back/knees/ankles
- **OHS 50 kg limiter**: Sots press and clean pull on riser address mobility and leg strength gaps
- **Push jerk only**: Stronger cleans → more stable rack position carryover
- **Night shift**: Morning/pre-shift Sots press combats postural fatigue
