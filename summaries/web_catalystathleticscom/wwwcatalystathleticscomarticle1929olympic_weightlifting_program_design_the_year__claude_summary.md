## RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:

**1. Programming Principles**
- Intermediate lifters work with **short macrocycles (6-12 weeks)** and make rapid progress
- Can peak for **multiple competitions per year** (e.g., 6 meets = 6 macrocycles averaging 8-9 weeks each)
- If long breaks exist between competitions, consider splitting into **two 6-week cycles** rather than one 12-week cycle
- Framework: Schedule macrocycles around competition dates, not the reverse

**2. Directly Applicable to This Athlete**
- **Intermediate level + no fixed competition schedule** = work with general plan until meet dates set, then adjust
- **6-week hypertrophy block fits the model**: can structure as first microcycle before technique/strength block (12 weeks total = typical intermediate macrocycle)
- No specific back pain or shift work guidance provided in text

**3. Not Covered**
- Snatch/clean/jerk technique cues
- Exercise selection for weak points (low OHS, no split jerk)
- Recovery/mobility strategies
- Chronic pain management

---

**Bottom line for this athlete:** The 6-week hypertrophy → technique/strength structure aligns with intermediate programming. Next priority: identify 1-2 target competitions to structure the full 12+ week block around.
