# Analysis: "Ask Greg: Hitching in the Snatch & Clean"

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Multiple-position lifts**: Incrementally lower starting positions (above knee → below knee → floor) to acclimate to full ROM while maintaining technique.
- **Tempo work**: 3-second first pull from floor to build tension and timing before returning to normal speed.
- Progressive integration of floor pulls as comfort increases.

## 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)**
- **Hitching fix**: Often stems from mental tension/explosive start. Solution: smooth bar separation from floor, not violent yanking.
- **Constant pressure**: Push continuously against floor throughout extension; never "let off."
- **Body-over-bar focus**: Move the body correctly; the bar follows. Avoid fixating on the bar itself (common error).
- **Extension patience**: Maintain tension entire lift; smooth acceleration after initial pull.

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Multiple-position lifts (graduated floor progression)
- 3-second slow first-pull drills from floor
- Regular pulls emphasizing body mechanics
