## 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean/Jerk)**

**Snatch & Clean:**
- **Feet reconnection:** Sharp, simultaneous clap = flat landing + aggressive foot drive (critical for this athlete's foot positioning)
- **Staggered foot sounds:** Two distinct claps = balance/mobility imbalance (relevant given chronic back pain; may indicate asymmetry)
- **Bar-body contact (hips/thighs):** Audible contact = proper timing, speed, and extension; silence = incomplete extension or forward imbalance
- **Timing:** Feet should hit floor *simultaneously* with bar lockout (snatch) or rack position (clean) in real time

**Jerk:**
- **Front foot contact:** Sharp clap = proper reconnection; absent sound = dragged/short step (relevant for push jerk transition to split jerk)
- **Both feet:** Should sound like single, unified reconnection—not staggered
