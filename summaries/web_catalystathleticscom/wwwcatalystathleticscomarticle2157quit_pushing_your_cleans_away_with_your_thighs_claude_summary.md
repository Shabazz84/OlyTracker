# Analysis: "Quit Pushing Your Cleans Away with Your Thighs"

## 2. Technique Cues (Clean)
- **Problem**: Bar running forward at top of clean → crashes into rack, athlete collapses forward
- **Root cause**: Early second pull (premature hip opening/trunk upright) when bar is low on thighs; knees drive forward too early, pushing bar away and shifting weight forward
- **Key fix**: **Wait until bar is higher on the thigh to explode** — requires strength, confidence, and timing discipline
- **Why it matters**: As loads increase, poor timing becomes more problematic

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Halting clean deadlifts** — build strength to maintain position longer
- **Segment deadlifts** — same goal
- **Segment cleans** — develop confidence and timing
- **Slow-pull cleans** — timing and patience
- **High-hang cleans** — improve explosiveness once positioning is solid
