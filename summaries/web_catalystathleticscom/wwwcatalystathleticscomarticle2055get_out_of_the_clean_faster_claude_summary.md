# Analysis: "Get Out of the Clean Faster" by Greg Everett

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Bounce training**: Integrate into ALL cleans and front squats (neurological pattern requiring practice)
- **Intensity/Intent**: Maximal effort acceleration throughout entire recovery (not just initial drive)
- **Supplemental work**: Box jumps, tuck jumps, broad jumps from squat bottom (with/without pause); back squat jumps; pause squats with max concentric acceleration
- **General strength**: Stronger squat = faster clean recovery

## 2. Technique Cues (Clean-Specific)
- **Bar placement**: Secure rack position immediately; prevent forward drift
- **Turnover timing**: Meet bar smoothly; avoid "crashing" which disrupts stretch-shortening cycle
- **Squat depth**: Full depth with upright posture required for elastic bounce
- **Trunk stability**: Pressurize and stabilize before lift; rigid trunk transfers leg force to bar
- **Acceleration cue**: Drive aggressively ALL the way up—don't decelerate mid-recovery

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Front squats with bounce
- Back squat jumps ± pause
- Pause squats (max concentric acceleration)
- Box/tuck/broad jumps from squat bottom
- Clean-jerks
- Back/ab work (postural strength)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- **Mobility prerequisite**: Full-depth squat with upright posture required to utilize bounce effectively
- Inadequate ankle/hip/knee mobility forces poor positioning → slower recovery

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
- **OHS 50kg limiter (poor mobility)**: This article directly addresses mobility as prerequisite for fast clean recovery. Athlete needs mobility work before expecting technical improvements.
- **Chronic back pain**: Postural strength emphasis (back/ab work, trunk stability, bracing) is critical—directly applicable.
- **Night shift fatigue (Wed–Sun)**: Intent/mindset cue ("you have to actually try") important given potential CNS fatigue.
- **Clean 80kg, BS 118kg ratio**: Below optimal (should approach 85%+ of squat)—squat strength development will unlock clean speed gains.
