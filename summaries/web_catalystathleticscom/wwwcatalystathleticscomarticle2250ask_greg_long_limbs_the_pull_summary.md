**2. Technique cues for snatch/clean:**
*   **Setup:** Place bar over balls of feet or slightly farther forward than usual to allow shin tilt; start shoulders slightly more forward over the bar.
*   **Knees:** Spread knees out laterally as far as possible to reduce fore-aft thigh distance; keep knees over the bar at the start.
*   **Stance:** Experiment with a wider stance to facilitate knee clearance without creating an extreme, weak angle against the ground.
*   **Second Pull:** Be patient; stay directly over the bar until it reaches mid-thigh before opening hips. Do not cut the pull short due to height concerns.
