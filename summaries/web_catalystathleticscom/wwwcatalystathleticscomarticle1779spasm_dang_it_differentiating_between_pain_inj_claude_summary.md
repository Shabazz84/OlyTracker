**ANALYSIS:**

**Relevant to Athlete's Profile:**

1. **Chronic Back Pain (CRITICAL)**
   - Article directly addresses lower back spasms in weightlifting
   - Foreman's anecdote: sharp pop in spinal erectors during snatch pull at 120kg, recovered within week through gradual loading
   - Key message: distinguish between spasm (muscle clinching, treatable) vs. actual injury (strain/tear, training-limiting)
   - Recovery methods: foam rolling, icing, massage therapy (chip away gradually over days/weekend depending on severity)

2. **Recovery Recommendations**
   - Massage therapy with sufficient pressure/force works best
   - Heat may be better than ice (per comments)
   - IMS (Intramuscular Stimulation) mentioned as effective for recurring spasms
   - Myofascial release therapy noted as helpful
   - Self-directed work between treatments recommended

3. **Relevant Context**
   - Spasms are common with age/training intensity—normal for hard-training athletes
   - Expected outcome: pain resolves, training continues (Foreman snatched 137kg same day)
   - Don't catastrophize early signs; test conservatively before major loading
