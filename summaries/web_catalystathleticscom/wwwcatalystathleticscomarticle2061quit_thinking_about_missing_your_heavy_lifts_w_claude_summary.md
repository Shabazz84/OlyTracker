**This text is about weightlifting sports psychology, not programming or technique.**

---

**Relevant to this athlete:**

**Mental/Performance Principle:**
- Avoid catastrophizing during warm-ups. Light lifts feeling suboptimal (e.g., 70 kg snatch feeling off) doesn't predict failure on heavy attempts (110 kg).
- Stay present: focus only on the lift immediately in front of you, not future attempts or projected failures.
- This is especially relevant given the athlete's **chronic back pain** — pain/discomfort in warm-ups can trigger false doubt. Distinguish between actual limitation vs. mental spiral.

**Key insight for this athlete:**
As an intermediate transitioning to OLY and working nights (fatigue factor), mental composure during suboptimal warm-ups is critical. Experience building this resilience takes time but is learnable.
