# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1714/Training-with-Pain-Its-Such-a-Happy-Thing/

This text is a sarcastic commentary on pain tolerance in weightlifting and does **not** contain specific programming principles, technique cues, exercise recommendations, or recovery advice suitable for an intermediate athlete with chronic back pain. It argues that some discomfort is inherent to the sport but warns against ignoring severe injury (e.g., "human centipede" level pain).

**Relevance to Athlete Profile:**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The author explicitly warns that while weightlifters must tolerate daily discomfort, one should not "fight through pain" if it prevents normal function (like crawling to the bathroom), advising professional medical consultation in such cases.
*   **Night Shift/Transitioning:** No specific advice is given regarding sleep schedules or transitioning from powerlifting to Olympic lifting.
