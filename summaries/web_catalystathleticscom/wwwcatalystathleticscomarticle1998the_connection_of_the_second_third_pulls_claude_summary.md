## 2. Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean
- **Second & Third Pull Integration:** They must be seamless, continuous—not separate actions. Second pull feeds directly into third pull with no hesitation or floating at the top.
- **Maximize Bar Elevation:** Drive legs and open hips explosively to create upward speed and vertical elevation. This creates time/space to get under the bar.
- **Timing:** Break from extended position *immediately* to move under—don't delay at the top of the pull.
- **Effort is identical in pulls vs. lifts:** The elevation effort matches snatch/clean pulls, but the lifter transitions under sooner in the actual lift.
- **Single continuous action:** Never pause, float, or relax between phases.
