## 2. **Technique Cues (Highly Relevant)**

**Starting Position:**
- Barbell over balls of foot, weight balanced across whole foot
- Shins forward, knees above/in front of bar
- Back arched, reinforced with air pressure and muscular tension
- Shoulders directly above or slightly in front of bar
- Arms internally rotated (elbows out)
- *Relevance: Critical for athlete's OHS limiter (50 kg) — starting position establishes foundational positioning*

**First Pull:**
- Separate bar from floor with legs (squat-like push)
- Keep bar close to legs without dragging
- Maintain back angle, shoulders over/slightly in front of bar
- *Relevance: Positioning phase before explosive work*

**Second Pull:**
- Coordinated explosive knee + hip extension (not dominant one)
- Push through full foot, let heel rise naturally
- **Actively bring bar toward body** (critical for snatch consistency)
- Weight stays over same balance point throughout

**Third Pull:**
- Active relocation under bar (not falling/dropping)
- Continue pulling bar upward, elbows up and out
- Snatch: punch straight up on bar
- Clean: drive elbows up, reach chest/shoulders to meet bar
- *Relevance: Clean technique directly applicable; snatch punch cue useful for 80 kg clean → jerk transitions*

**Receiving Position & Recovery:**
- Snatch: prioritize bar security over speed (athlete's weak point at 50 kg OHS)
- Clean: catch bounce and drive out immediately to preserve leg energy for jerk
- *Relevance: Athlete does push jerk only — clean recovery speed is critical to have fresh legs for jerk attempt*
