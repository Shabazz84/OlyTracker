# Analysis: "How to Quit Getting Stuck in the Clean" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- No explicit periodization/sets/reps provided
- Emphasis on targeted exercise selection to address specific technical deficits

## 2. **Technique Cues - Clean**
- **Balance**: Pressure across whole foot with slight heel preference; "load coil spring" evenly
- **Turnover speed**: Rack bar at same time feet reconnect with platform; aim for speed over power
- **Rack position**: Elbows/shoulders/head driven up aggressively throughout recovery; maintain hook grip until bar touches shoulders
- **Trunk**: Big breath locked in *before* pull; maintain maximum tension throughout
- **Mental approach**: Aggressive confidence; accelerate throughout entire recovery

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Pause front/back squats (posture + strength)
- Tall cleans, power cleans, high-hang/high-block cleans (turnover speed)
- Muscle cleans (turnover mechanics)
- Segment deadlifts/pulls, slow-pull cleans, clean pull + clean (balance feel)
- Back extensions with hold, crunches, planks (trunk activation warmup)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Mobility work for secure rack position (brief mention; not detailed)

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
- **HIGH PRIORITY**: Addresses clean as primary weakness (80 kg clean vs. 118 kg squat = significant gap)
- **Back pain + trunk pressurization**: Greg's emphasis on breath-lock and trunk tension directly applies; weak/inactive trunk exacerbates back issues
- **Squat posture**: Pause squats recommended—beneficial for postural control with chronic back pain
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Improved rack position & shoulder mobility from these drills may transfer to snatch
- **Night shift fatigue**: Aggression/confidence cues help combat low energy state
