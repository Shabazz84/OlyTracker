## Analysis: "Why We Turn The Feet Out In The Pull" by Greg Everett

**1. Programming Principles**
None specified in this article.

**2. Technique Cues for Snatch/Clean**
- **Foot turnout: 10–20 degrees** relative to centerline (default; adjust only if <10° or >20°, or if causing problems)
- **Rationale:**
  - Enables knees-out position in starting position and first pull (better alignment)
  - Allows hips to move closer to bar → more upright posture
  - Stronger (2.5–5 kg improvement in snatch/clean per Glyadkovsky & Rodionov study)
  - Permits natural hip movement, better back arch, smoother pull from floor

**4. Recovery/Mobility Advice**
None.

**5. Relevance to This Athlete**
**Highly relevant:** Your weak overhead position (50 kg OHS) and chronic back pain suggest postural issues. The upright posture enabled by proper foot turnout (hips closer to bar) could improve your pulling mechanics and reduce spinal stress. Verify your foot position is 10–20° out; this may help your snatch pull efficiency and back loading.
