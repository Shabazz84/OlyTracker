**2. Technique Cues (Jerk Focus)**
*   **Upper Body Action:** The lifter must "actively push down under the bar" rather than pull away; the upper body follows through violently against the bar once leg drive ends.
*   **Ground Pressure:** Upon full extension, pressure against the ground becomes minimal as the athlete pushes against the bar to move downward into the receiving position.
*   **Timing:** Feet must reconnect with the floor *before* elbows are fully locked out; legs tighten only after lockout is achieved.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Dual Approach:** Utilize both the **Jerk** and **Push Press** in tandem rather than choosing one exclusively.
*   **Rationale:** The Jerk offers superior CNS stimulation for explosiveness (faster neural patterns), while the Push Press mimics continuous upward motion better, though neither perfectly mirrors sport-specific actions.
