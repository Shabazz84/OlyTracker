## 2. **Technique Cues – Directly Relevant**

**Clean & Snatch Turnover (Primary Focus):**
- **Meet the bar at its actual height**, not by descending indiscriminately to rock bottom
- **Relocate your body under the bar** at whatever level you've lifted it to (analogy: front squat receiving vs. being dropped on)
- **Hold grip longer** through turnover—transition from tight pulling grip to loose pushing grip *as the hand turns over*
- **Maintain grip until elbows drive up in front** (clean), ideally until bar lands on shoulders
- Snatch: Hook grip naturally enforces this; punch bar overhead with looser grip

**Recommended Drills for Turnover Issues:**
- Tall muscle cleans → Scarecrow cleans → Tall cleans
- Muscle snatches, tall snatches, power snatch + snatch
- Rack delivery drills
- *Focus: maintain bar tension while pulling under, deliver bar directly to shoulders*

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
See drills above (tall variants, muscle variations).

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**

- **Back pain consideration**: Proper bar positioning (not crashing) reduces spinal load—technique correction aids pain management
