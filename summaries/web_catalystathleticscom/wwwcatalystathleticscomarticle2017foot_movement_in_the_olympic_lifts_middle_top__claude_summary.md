## 2. **Technique Cues: Snatch & Clean**
**"MIDDLE-TOP-MIDDLE" framework:**
- **MIDDLE (1st pull):** Feel bodyweight in middle of foot (between heel and toes) during floor-to-knee phase
- **TOP (finish/2nd pull):** Extend high onto toes when driving upward—encourages full plantar flexion/jumping action
- **MIDDLE (landing):** Land in middle of foot when jumping into catch position, NOT on toes (prevents forward instability) or heels (causes shin tilt & backward rock)

**Key cue:** Stomp feet to feel proper landing impact.

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
**Moderate relevance:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Poor foot positioning in pulls → poor catch mechanics. This cue chain may help stabilize overhead position.
- **Back pain:** Landing on toes risks forward instability; landing on heels risks backward rock. Middle-foot landing is neutral—potentially safer.
- **No split jerk:** Doesn't affect landing mechanics, but solid stability foundation aids future jerk progression.

**Bottom line:** Solid foundational technique article; useful for refining pull mechanics and catch stability, which could indirectly improve OHS strength.
