# Analysis: "Correcting Tipping Over the Bar in the Snatch & Clean" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Position-specific strength**: Train the correct posture exclusively; avoid reinforcing poor positions
- **Volume distribution**: Majority of volume at correct posture (even if slower/lighter); minimize heavy volume with poor form
- **Exercise variations**: Pulls, deadlifts on risers, pauses (3-sec at 1" off floor & knee), slow eccentrics (3–6 sec lowering)
- **Complexes**: Segment deadlift/pull → snatch/clean; slow-pull snatch/clean; segment snatch/clean

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Upright posture pull**: Chest up, hips in (resist hip rise before chest)
- **Root cause**: Natural body tendency to open knee angle (mechanical advantage); fix via leg strength, not cue reliance
- **Self-checks**: 3-sec pauses at 1" off floor and knee to assess position

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Front/back squats with upright posture (chest up, hips forward—primary intervention)
- Snatch/clean pulls and deadlifts (on riser)
- Pause deadlifts/pulls (3-sec pauses)
- Slow eccentrics (3–6 sec)
