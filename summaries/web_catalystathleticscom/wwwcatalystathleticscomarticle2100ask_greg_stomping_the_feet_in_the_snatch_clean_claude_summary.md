# Analysis: "Stomping the Feet in the Snatch & Clean" by Greg Everett

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique Cue — Foot Movement (Snatch & Clean):**
- Feet should lift and reposition **aggressively** to transition rapidly to receiving position
- Goal: **zero pressure against floor** during pull-under for maximal acceleration
- Reconnection should be **flat-footed at quarter squat depth**
- Audible "clap" is acceptable if it results from **speed/viciousness, not excessive elevation**
- **Minimize unnecessary foot elevation** — longer flight time = higher error risk in repositioning
- Individual variation exists (Russian lifters use higher elevation; Chinese lifters use minimal foot lift) — both effective, but teaching foot lift first is standard
- **Caveat:** Some elite lifters never lift feet; this is exception, not rule
