## 2. **Technique Cues — Snatch**

**Primary fault category: "Poor Overhead Structure"**
- Maintain slight forward trunk inclination (not excessive).
- Secure shoulder blade retraction as stable base.
- Avoid excessive arm angulation backward.

**Secondary faults:**
- **Diving head/chest forward** → squat *down*, not back; maintain upright posture; complete extension before pulling under.
- **Bar swing forward in third pull** → keep elbows turned out (internal rotation); activate lats from start; limit hip forward travel (hips stay behind ankle/shoulder vertical line in final extension).
- **Premature foot lift** → drive against platform throughout extension.

**Grip width:** Narrower grip improves control of backward momentum. If anatomically wide grip needed (long arms), use **shoulder elevation/retraction in second pull + delayed second pull initiation**.

- **Thoracic spine mobility** (kyphosis reduction) → improves shoulder blade retraction and arm position overhead.
