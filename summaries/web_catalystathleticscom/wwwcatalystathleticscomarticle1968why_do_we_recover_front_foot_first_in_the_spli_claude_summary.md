# Analysis: Split Jerk Recovery Technique

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**CRITICAL LIMITATION**: This athlete does **push jerk only—no split jerk**. This article is **not directly applicable** to current training, but understanding split jerk mechanics is valuable context if transitioning to split jerk in future.

---

## Technique Cues Extracted

**Split Jerk Recovery (future reference):**
1. **Recover front foot first** (step back ~1/3 distance)
2. **Back leg acts as a kickstand**, not primary support
3. **Move bar + hips back WITH the front leg** — common error is stepping back while leaving weight overhead
4. **Minimize horizontal bar movement** — prevents momentum that becomes uncontrollable overhead
5. **Recovery position = diagnostic tool** — ability to recover front foot first indicates proper balance (not forward-heavy in split)

**Coaching cue**: "Push back with front leg; don't just pull your base out from under the weight."
