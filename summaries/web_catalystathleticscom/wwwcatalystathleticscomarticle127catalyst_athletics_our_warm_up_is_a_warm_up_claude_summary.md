# Analysis: Catalyst Athletics Warm-up Article by Greg Everett

## Relevance to Athlete Profile
**Highly relevant.** This athlete has chronic back pain and works night shifts—both require deliberate, intelligent warm-up preparation rather than skipping or oversimplifying it.

---

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Warm-up purpose**: Training *preparation*, not a workout itself
- **Duration**: 12–15 minutes at steady pace
- **Structure**: 
  - Monostructural work (2–5 min): Light cardio or agility drills
  - Foam rolling (~10 passes per area)
  - Dynamic warm-up (joint mobility)
  - Static stretching (problem areas only)
  - **Corrective drills last** (after body is warm)

---

## 2. Technique Cues
None specific to snatch, clean, or jerk provided.

---

## 3. Exercise Recommendations (Directly Applicable)

**FOR THIS ATHLETE'S CHRONIC BACK PAIN:**
- **Thoracic spine foam rolling** (before shoulder warm-up)
- **Spiderman lunge** (hip mobility, hip flexor stretch)
- **Scorpion** (hip flexor stretch + glute activation to prevent lower back hyperextension)
- **Russian Baby Maker** (hip opener—addresses tight hips that load lower back)
- **Bow & bend** (hip hinge pattern with glute engagement)
- **Inchworms** (hip flexor mobility + controlled movement)

**FOR NIGHT SHIFT WORKER (Sedentary recovery):**
- Agility ladder work or partner medicine ball drills (more engaging than rowing)
- Leg swings, leg cradles, lunges with rotation (address immobility from sitting)

**PRE-LIFT CORRECTIVE WORK** (place *after* warm-up):
- Glute activation (bridges, X-band walks)
- Shoulder pre-hab (band external/internal rotation) — relevant for OHS/jerk development

---

## 4. Recovery & Mobility Advice
- **Pre-training foam rolling** (light, smooth) > post-training (aggressive)
- **Dynamic stretching pre-workout** > static stretching post-workout
- **Hip/spine mobility prioritized** for OHS and back pain management
- Glute activation prevents lower back compensation

---

## 5. Key Takeaway for This Athlete
Don't skip warm-up or over-complicate it. **12–15 min of deliberate preparation** (especially foam roll thoracic spine + hip mobility drills like Spiderman/Scorpion/Russian Baby Maker) directly addresses chronic back pain vulnerability and sedentary lifestyle stress. Use corrective glute/shoulder work *after* warm-up during hypertrophy block to bulletproof weak links (weak glutes, tight hips, poor shoulder mobility limiting OHS).
