# Analysis: Greg Everett on Olympic Lifting & Conditioning

**Relevance to this athlete:** Moderately relevant. Article is primarily CrossFit-conditioning focused, not Olympic weightlifting hypertrophy/technique. However, contains useful principles for integrating strength work.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Strength as foundation:** "First, strength drives everything." Pure strength must be developed before conditioning work is productive.
- **Strength ≠ Conditioning:** MetCons alone won't maximize strength gains—specialized work required.
- **Novice vs. Advanced:** Untrained athletes improve with anything; established trainees need periodized, specialized focus.
- **Hybrid approach:** Heavy lift + short conditioning is effective (e.g., 3×5 major lift + 5–10 min metcon).
- **Local muscular endurance:** Requires 15+ consecutive reps with minimal rest (compressed timeframe), not volume spread across workout.

**For this athlete:** The 6-week hypertrophy block makes sense *before* technique/strength work—matches Everett's philosophy of building strength foundation first.
