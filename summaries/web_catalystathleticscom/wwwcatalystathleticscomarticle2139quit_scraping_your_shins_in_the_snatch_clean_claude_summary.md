# Analysis: "Quit Scraping Your Shins in the Snatch & Clean" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
None provided in this article (technique-focused, not periodization).

## 2. **Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean**
**Primary Issues & Fixes:**
- **Shoulders behind the bar** → causes bar to swing back into legs
  - Cue: "Shoulders over the bar," "Stay over it," "Adjust hips higher (if appropriate)"
- **Excessive bar push-back** → athlete pushes bar too far back trying to keep it close
  - Cue: Avoid pushing bar back excessively
- **Premature second pull** → shoulders move behind bar too early (same effect as #1)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
Snatch/Clean deadlift variations (all address shoulder positioning):
- Snatch/clean deadlift
- Floating snatch/clean deadlift
- Halting snatch/clean deadlift
- Segment deadlift (pauses at 1" off floor, knee, mid-thigh)
- Combinations: deadlift + snatch/clean, slow-pull variants
