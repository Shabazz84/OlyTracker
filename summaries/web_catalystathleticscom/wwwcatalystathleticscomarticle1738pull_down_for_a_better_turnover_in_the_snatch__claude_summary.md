# Analysis: Pull Down for Better Turnover in Snatch & Clean

## 1. **Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)**
- **Third pull emphasis**: After hip/knee extension, actively PULL elbows high and out to sides—this drives downward momentum under the bar
- **Turnover is follow-through**: Speed comes from pulling down, not arm strength alone. External rotation strength is secondary to momentum
- **Tall snatches/cleans**: Use 25–30% of max (NOT 50%) for sub-maximal reps to ingrain the pull-down pattern; don't chase heavy singles

## 2. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Tall snatches and tall cleans as technical primers to reinforce third-pull acceleration

## 3. **Relevance to Athlete Profile**
⚠️ **High priority for this athlete:**
- **OHS 50 kg limiter + weak jerk (65 kg push only)**: Poor turnover mechanics directly limit receiving position strength. Mastering the pull-down will improve bar speed and confidence in the catch, especially critical since athlete lacks split jerk
- **Chronic back pain**: Proper pull-down mechanics reduce compensation patterns and reliance on arm/shoulder strength, lowering lumbar stress during turnover
- **Night shift fatigue**: Tall snatch/clean drills are technically focused but low-fatigue—ideal for CNS recovery

**Recommendation**: Integrate tall snatches/cleans 2–3× weekly during hypertrophy block as warm-up work to establish third-pull pattern before strength phase.
