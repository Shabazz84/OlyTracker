# Analysis: "Jumping Forward in the Snatch or Clean: Error Correction" by Greg Everett

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique Cues & Diagnostics (Snatch/Clean):**
- **Starting position balance:** Must establish correct balance early—weight too far back initially → overcompensation → forward jump
- **First pull shift:** Bar must move *slightly back* as it leaves floor; direct vertical bar path = forward balance drift
- **Hip tipping:** Hips rising faster than shoulders = shoulders forward = bar follows forward
- **Bar path past knees:** Bar continues subtle backward movement; prevents forward kick at contact
- **Third pull mechanics:** Elbows up/out; active bar control to maintain proximity during turnover and receiving position
- **Receiving position awareness:** Must actively bring bar back into final position (rack or overhead)

**Exercise Recommendations (Corrective Hierarchy):**

*Scorched Earth (Symptom Management):*
- Coach Mat: Thin (¼") rubber barrier ~3–5" in front of toes; gradually move closer as improves
- Overcorrection: Cue "jump slightly backward" to neutralize forward bias

*Finesse (Root Cause Fixes):*
- **Segment deadlifts/pulls** (focus on first-pull backward shift + bar path near knees)
- **Snatch/clean high-pulls** (address bar swing, leg drive, bar control)
- **Tall snatches/cleans** (third-pull mechanics & receiving position)
- **Overhead squats + snatch push press** (reinforce proper overhead/receiving position)
- Partial variations: lift-offs, halting deadlifts, segment snatches/cleans with pauses
