## 2. **Technique Cues for Snatch**
- **Jumping vs. foot lifting are different actions:** Jumping elevates center of mass; picking up feet causes it to fall. Many coaches confuse the two.
- **Full hip/knee extension is required** in the pull—this is not a "jump" because you're simultaneously pulling against the barbell, which redirects force downward.
- **Timing:** Pull the bar up fully → extend knees/hips maximally → THEN pick up feet and pull body down (like transitioning from high-pull to receiving position).
- **With light weights:** Control extension force (less aggressive) to avoid actual jumping; with heavy weights, use maximal force naturally.
- **Foot placement:** Individual; starting position similar to vertical jump stance; slight outward movement is natural and not detrimental to speed under.

## 3. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- **Critical:** Your OHS (50 kg) is the snatch limiter. This article emphasizes proper pull mechanics—ensuring maximal extension + aggressive pull-under will improve bar height and receiving position depth.
- **Light training weight warning:** At 80 kg BW with snatch ~80 kg, you may accidentally jump if pulling too hard with light warm-up weights. Control effort on light lifts to ingrain proper mechanics.
- **Coaching advice:** Find one qualified coach; conflicting advice (like you're experiencing with back pain + push jerk only) creates confusion.
