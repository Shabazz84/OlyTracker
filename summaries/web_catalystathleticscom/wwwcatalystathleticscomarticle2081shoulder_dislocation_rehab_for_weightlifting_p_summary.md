**This text is a personal case study/rehab log regarding shoulder dislocation recovery and medial epicondylitis; it does not contain specific programming principles, technique cues, or exercise recommendations for an intermediate athlete transitioning to Olympic lifting.**

While the author (Greg Everett) discusses general concepts like "low volume," "mobility restrictions in the rack position," and the importance of "leg strength" over competition lifts during rehab, these are anecdotal observations from a post-surgical elite athlete's perspective. The text lacks actionable protocols for your specific profile (hypertrophy block, night shift schedule, chronic back pain) or addresses the transition from push jerk to split jerk.
