# Analysis: Mental Blocks in Snatch & Clean & Jerk (Greg Everett)

## Relevance to Athlete Profile
**Highly relevant.** This athlete has a 50kg OHS (primary snatch limiter) and only push jerk—suggesting technique/confidence issues beyond pure strength. Mental block strategies directly applicable.

---

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES
- **Progressive Waves**: 3 waves to heavy single (e.g., 90-93-96 / 92-95-98 / 94-97-100) to build confidence near threshold weights
- **Threshold Training**: Work 3–5 singles at confident weight (e.g., 90–95kg), increase by 1–2kg next session over weeks—ideal for mental blocks disguised as physical ones
- **On-the-Minute (OTM)**: 5 singles × 3 progressive weights at 70–80% intensity (e.g., 70-73-76% or 70-75-80%), increase 2–3% if all made; builds consistency/confidence before heavy single
- **Baby Steps**: Use 1–3kg jumps instead of typical 5kg—reduces mental barrier despite more sets
- **Complexes/Reps Block**: 3–6+ weeks avoiding singles; use doubles, triples, complexes instead to rebuild confidence away from the problem weight

---

## 2. TECHNIQUE CUES & CONFIDENCE EXERCISES
**For weak overhead position/OHS (his primary snatch limiter):**
- Snatch + overhead squat
- Snatch + snatch balance (± snatch balance + OHS)
- Hold all snatches in bottom for 3–5 seconds
- Snatch balances, jerk supports, jerk recoveries

**For weak/timid pull:**
- Heavy pulls, pulls on risers, deadlifts, overloaded pulls from blocks at knee

**For fear of getting under:**
- Tall snatches/tall cleans
- Snatches/cleans from power position

---

## 3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS
- Address diagnosed weaknesses (his case: OHS is limiting factor)
- Confidence-building variations: snatch balances, overhead holds
- Pull strength: deadlifts, block pulls
- **Reps/doubles** as alternative to singles when mental block present

---

## 4. RECOVERY & MINDSET
- Focus on movement quality, not weight on bar
- Long-term perspective; one bad session ≠ career threat
- Avoid stress/self-loathing spirals (self-fulfilling prophecy)
- **Rest structure**: OTM followed by heavy single with ~2 min rest between jumps

---

## 5. ATHLETE-SPECIFIC NOTES
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** → prioritize recovery; OTM structure preserves energy better than repeated misses
- **Chronic back pain** → avoid excessive volume; threshold/OTM training more sustainable than grinding heavy singles
- **50kg OHS limiter** → immediate focus: confidence exercises (snatch balances, overhead holds) + complexes over next 3–6 weeks before hypertrophy block
- **Push jerk only** → mental block likely compounded; consider reframing as technique/strength gap, not confidence issue
