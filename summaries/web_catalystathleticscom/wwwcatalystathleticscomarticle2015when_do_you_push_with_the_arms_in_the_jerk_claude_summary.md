# Analysis: "When Do You Push with the Arms in the Jerk" by Greg Everett

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Jerk Technique (HIGH PRIORITY)**
- **Critical Issue:** Athlete uses push jerk only; no split jerk. This article is about *timing* arm drive in the jerk drive phase—directly applicable.
- **Key Cue:** Arms should push when the bar reaches maximal upward speed (generally when legs complete extension).
- **Strength vs. Elastic Jerk:** Athlete should self-assess which style fits:
  - **Strength jerker:** Deeper dip → slower transition → later arm push (wait for full leg extension)
  - **Elastic jerker:** Shallower dip → quick transition → earlier arm push (before full extension)
  - *Self-assessment method:* Explosiveness, vertical jump ability, or experimentation at 70-80% intensity with power jerks.

**Why This Matters for This Athlete:**
- Current jerk ceiling is 65 kg (push jerk only). Understanding arm timing will improve bar elevation and speed—critical for the 6-week hypertrophy block before strength phase.
- Low OHS (50 kg) is the limiting factor; this timing cue won't directly fix that, but cleaner jerks preserve more bar height.
