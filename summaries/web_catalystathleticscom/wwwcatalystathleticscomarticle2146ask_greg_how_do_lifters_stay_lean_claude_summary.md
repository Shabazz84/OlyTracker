# Analysis: "How Do Lifters Stay Lean?" — Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Conditioning approach**: Avoid dedicated cardio/CrossFit-style conditioning (counterproductive, drains recovery).
- **Alternative**: Build conditioning into existing program:
  - Quick warm-ups, minimal rest
  - Accessory circuits with little/no rest
  - Reduced inter-set rest overall
  - Avoid sitting during workouts

## 4. **Recovery & Nutrition**
- **Nutrition > training** for body composition (major calorie deficits via diet, not exercise)
- **Sleep, stress management, hormonal factors** are primary drivers of leanness
- **No universal formula**: test carb/fat ratios individually; prioritize training performance over leanness
- **Genetics dominate** natural leanness more than diet quality
- **Warning**: Adding conditioning work eats finite recovery resources (critical for night-shift workers like this athlete)

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
⚠️ **High relevance**: With chronic back pain and night-shift fatigue, adding dedicated cardio is **contraindicated**. This athlete should:
- Use inter-set rest reductions within snatch/clean/jerk work
- Prioritize sleep quality/stress management (difficult on night shift)
- Avoid competing recovery resources
