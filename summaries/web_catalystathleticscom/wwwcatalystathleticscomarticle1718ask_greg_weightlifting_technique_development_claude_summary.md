# Analysis: Ask Greg - Weightlifting Technique Development

## Relevant to Athlete Profile

**Programming Principles:**
- Start very light to develop consistency in basic positions; progress conservatively
- Load should be demanded by technique progress, not vice versa
- Build volume at a weight where technique is consistent (e.g., 70kg snatch), then small 1-2kg jumps
- Quality reps at lighter weights > fewer heavy reps with poor form
- Heavier attempts should be infrequent, representing small portion of total volume
- Accumulate huge volume of accurate practice to mitigate poor reps

**Technique Cues:**
- Master one consistent approach before exploring variations
- Hold bottom positions (relevant: snatch balance with 3-second holds)
- Don't power snatch—squat all reps to build receiving position confidence

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- **Snatch Balance (direct fix for OHS limiter)**: Hold 3 seconds in bottom every rep; use as reference point vs. OHS weight
- Graduate loading conservatively; small jumps prevent fear-induced technical breakdown

**Key Quote for This Athlete:**
> "You can always fine tune things later if you decide it's necessary. You do need some amount of weight to completely develop technique...Graduate your loading conservatively, always making technique a priority, and accumulate a huge volume of accurate practice."

## Critical Note
OHS limitation (50kg) is explicitly addressed: Snatch balance work + bottom-position holds > increasing OHS weight alone. Aligns with your 6-week hypertrophy block focus before strength phase.
