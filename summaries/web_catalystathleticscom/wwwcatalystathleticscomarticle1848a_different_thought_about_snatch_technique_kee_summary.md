**2. Technique cues for snatch, clean, or jerk**
*   **"Feel the bar pass close to the face":** A specific mental cue used by Matt Foreman to prevent "looping" (the bar swinging forward) during the second pull and transition. The lifter visualizes the bar brushing their face as it passes overhead.
*   **Timing Warning:** Must pair this with the instruction: *"Wait until the bar clears your head before you turn the wrists over."* Premature turnover while focusing on the face causes facial impact injuries (bloody noses, dented foreheads).
*   **"Scarecrow elbows":** Mentioned in comments as a cue to keep elbows high and prevent looping.
*   **Visual Target:** Focus on a fixed target throughout the lift; do not turn the head left/right during the catch.

*   **Chronic Back Pain & "Looping":** While the article doesn't mention back pain, it identifies "looping" (bar swinging forward) as a primary error often caused by not getting the torso vertical before the second pull. Correcting this loop can reduce shear forces on the lower back during the catch and transition.
