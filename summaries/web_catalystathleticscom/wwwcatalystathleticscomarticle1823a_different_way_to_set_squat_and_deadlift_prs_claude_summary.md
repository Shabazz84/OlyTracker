# Analysis for Intermediate OL Athlete (102.5kg, 80kg Clean, 50kg OHS)

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES
- **4-day structure** (adaptable to 3-day): High frequency squatting—3x/week via competition lifts + assistance
- **Rep scheme**: Competition lifts (singles/doubles, occasional triples); assistance/squats (sets of 5, 3, 2); avoid heavy singles and high-rep contrast
- **Key concept**: "Accumulated strength" through high-volume pulling (cleans, pulls) improves squat/deadlift despite not being primary focus
- **Evidence**: DL and SQ PRs increase substantially without dedicated max-effort DL work

None provided in this article.

## 3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS
**Sample 4-day template:**
- **Monday**: Snatch | snatch assistance (pulls, snatch-grip DL/RDL) | back squat | abs
- **Wednesday**: Rack jerk | power clean | pressing
- **Thursday**: Clean | clean assistance (pulls, clean-grip DL/RDL) | front squat | abs
- **Saturday**: Snatch | C&J | back squat | abs

**3-day variant**: Tue (Sn/hang cleans/FS) | Thu (rack jerks/3-pos pulls/push press) | Sat (Sn/C&J/BS)
