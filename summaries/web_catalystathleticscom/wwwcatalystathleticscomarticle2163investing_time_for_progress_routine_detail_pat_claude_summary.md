# Analysis: Greg Everett - "Investing Time for Progress"

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Consistency over speed**: Treat every rep identically (50kg warm-up = 150kg attempt protocol)
- **Extended holds**: Prescribe 3-second overhead holds post-snatch/jerk to expose stability deficits
- **Long-term investment**: Attention to detail early prevents career-long technical debt

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Snatch/Clean/Jerk approach**: Standardize chalk retrieval, walk-up, self-talk, grip sequence
- **Receiving position**: Hold bar overhead/rack position fully before dropping—don't bail early
- **Competition readiness**: Train for extended holds (meets require waiting for down signal)

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Extended holds in overhead positions** (snatch/jerk)—especially flagged for lifters with OHS weakness

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- Not addressed in text

## 5. Relevance to This Athlete
- **HIGH PRIORITY**: OHS 50kg is noted limiter. Extended hold training directly addresses overhead stability deficit
- **Practical fit**: Night shift work (Wed–Sun) demands routine consistency; standardized lift protocols help maintain quality despite fatigue
- **Chronic back pain**: Rushing bar drops may mask positional issues; full stabilization holds could reveal compensation patterns
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Implement holds as sub-maximal stability accessory work

**Key takeaway**: Invest 3–5 seconds per lift for overhead stability development before moving to strength block.
