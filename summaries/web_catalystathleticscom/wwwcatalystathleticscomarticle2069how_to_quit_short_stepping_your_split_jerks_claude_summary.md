## Relevance to This Athlete

## 2. **Technique Cues & Jerk Mechanics**

**Short-stepping root cause framework:**
- **Most common issue:** Inadequate bar elevation (drive) → not timing problem  
- **Secondary issue:** Weak dip/poor braking in dip bottom → prevents proper drive transfer  
- **Tertiary issue:** Timing mismatch between dip recovery and drive, or drive-to-split transition

**Key cue:** "If you drive the bar high, you have plenty of space and time to move your feet into a long split before the bar comes back down."

**Diagnostic test:** If **power jerk <85% of split jerk**, the problem is drive capacity, not footwork.

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**For Drive (elevation):**
- Push press, pause jerk, power jerk, push jerk, jerk drive, concentric jerk, jumping squat, back squat jump
- Complexes: jerk dip squat + jerk (forces aggression)

**For Dip weakness:**
- Jerk dip, jerk spring, jerk dip squat, push press, power jerk

**For Split position (if it's actually the problem):**
- Tall jerk, step to split, drop to split, jerk from split, split squat
