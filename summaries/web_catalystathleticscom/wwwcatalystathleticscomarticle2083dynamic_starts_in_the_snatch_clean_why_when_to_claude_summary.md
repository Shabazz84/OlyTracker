## 2. **Technique Cues**
**When to Introduce Dynamic Starts:**
- Only after establishing technical consistency in static starting position and first pull
- For lifters pulling 40%+ of deadlift max (this athlete: Clean 80 kg, likely ~160 kg deadlift, so already at threshold)
- Primarily benefits advanced lifters lifting near-maximal loads or with unfavorable proportions (longer-legged/taller)

**5 Dynamic Start Types Covered:**
- Sit-through, Hip pump, Slow descent, Low sit, The rip, Dive start
- Study finding: Double hip pump most effective (+2.5 cm pull height vs. static)
