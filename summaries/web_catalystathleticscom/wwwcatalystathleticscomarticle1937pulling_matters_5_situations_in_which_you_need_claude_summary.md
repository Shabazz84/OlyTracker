# Analysis: "Pulling Matters" by Sergey Bondarenko

## Relevance to This Athlete
**HIGHLY RELEVANT.** This athlete's weak OHS (50 kg) and modest Clean (80 kg) suggest pulling/extension weaknesses—pulls directly address this.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Base period:** Heavy pulls (100–150% of max lift) for 5 reps; hang/block pulls with 270 kg+ (for reference athlete)
- **Competition period:** Reduce pull frequency; work 2–3 reps only at 90–100% intensity
- **Intensity zones:** 90–100% for speed; 100–150% for stability (hang/block)
- **Macro placement:** Pulls suit base period well; taper closer to meet

**→ Applicable to 6-week hypertrophy block:** Use moderate pulls (90–110%) for 3–5 reps 2–3x/week to build pulling strength without compromising recovery on night shift.

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- Maintain **proper joint angles throughout** the pull
- Focus on **solid second pull finish with a shrug**
- **Keep bar close** in the second pull (common mistake)
- Use **hang pulls to isolate and groove** the second pull before full lifts

**→ Key for this athlete:** Hang pulls address his weak OHS by reinforcing extension mechanics before the catch.

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Hang pulls** (mid-thigh → knee → platform progression) for learning/correcting technique
- **Block pulls** and **hang pulls from various positions** for stability under heavy load
- **Combinations:** 1 hang pull (mid-thigh) + 1 hang pull (knee) + 3 hang cleans/snatches (primes movement pattern, reduces fatigue from high reps)
- **Pulls 90–100%** for speed development (closer to competition)

**→ Best fit:** Hang clean pulls + hang snatch pulls 2x/week during hypertrophy block; progress from knee to platform.

## Key Takeaway
**Integrate hang snatch/clean pulls 2–3x/week at 90–110% during the hypertrophy block.** Use hang positions (knee/mid-thigh) to isolate and correct his second pull weakness, directly addressing his low OHS. Pair with combination work (pull + lift) to maximize technical transfer with lower fatigue.
