# Analysis

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Technique Cue — Jerk/Overhead Position:**
- Jerk lockout issues often stem from **shoulder mobility, not elbow limitation**
- Diagnostic test: Extend elbow fully at your side. If extension there exceeds overhead extension, shoulders are the limiter
- **Solutions:**
  1. Widen jerk grip (may alter rack position negatively)
  2. Long-term: improve shoulder mobility + strengthen elbow extension

**Direct Application:**
This athlete has low OHS (50 kg), which is the stated snatch limiter. The article suggests checking whether the limitation is shoulder mobility or actual elbow extension. Given the athlete uses **push jerk only (no split jerk)**, improving overhead stability through shoulder work is critical for both snatch technique and jerk lockout security.

**For Chronic Back Pain:**
Indirectly relevant—poor overhead position compensation (soft elbows, forward lean) stresses the lower back. Fixing shoulder mobility and elbow extension may reduce compensatory strain.
