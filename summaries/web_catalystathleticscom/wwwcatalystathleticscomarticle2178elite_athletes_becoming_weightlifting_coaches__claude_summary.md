# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2178/Elite-Athletes-Becoming-Weightlifting-Coaches-Does-It-Work/

**Not applicable to weightlifting programming/technique analysis.**

This article discusses the **coaching philosophy and transition from elite athlete to coach**—not programming, technique cues, exercises, or recovery strategies relevant to your athlete's profile.

**Potential takeaway for your athlete:** If seeking a coach, prioritize one who:
- Understands they must adapt programming to *your* specific needs (night shift, chronic back pain, technical limitations)
- Doesn't blindly replicate elite-level workloads
- Focuses on *your* progress, not their credentials

For your 6-week hypertrophy block and technique work, you'll need content addressing programming structure, exercise selection for OHS limitations, and back-friendly modifications.
