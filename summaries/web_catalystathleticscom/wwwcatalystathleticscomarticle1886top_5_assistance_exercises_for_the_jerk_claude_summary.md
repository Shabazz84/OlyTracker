## 2. **Technique Cues**

**Push Press/Push Jerk:**
- Identical dip-and-drive to jerk
- Vertical bar path from front to back of torso
- Transition timing between leg drive and arm push
- Rate of force development critical

**Power Jerk (Less relevant):**
- Vertical dip/drive; bar moves **back behind neck** as trunk inclines forward
- Smaller margin of error than split jerk

**Jerk Dip Squat:**
- **Most relevant for chronic back pain athlete**
- Prevents hips shifting back, weight shifting forward
- Maintains heel pressure, quad tension throughout

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**Best Fit for This Athlete:**
1. **Push Press** — Builds dip/drive mechanics without split complexity; trains leg elasticity and timing
2. **Jerk Dip Squat** — Positional control; prevents compensation patterns that worsen back pain
3. **Jerk Support/Jerk Recovery** — Overhead stability (weak area: 65 kg jerk vs. 118 kg back squat ratio)

**Avoid/Defer:** Power jerk, push jerk behind-the-neck split (requires split jerk proficiency first)
