# Analysis: "Internal Rotation Overhead in the Snatch" by Greg Everett

## Relevant to This Athlete

**1. Technique Cue for Snatch (PRIMARY RELEVANCE – OHS is limiter)**

- **Overhead arm position**: Elbow crease oriented **halfway between forward and up** (or elbow point halfway between down and back)
- This is "neutral" IR/ER, NOT maximal internal rotation
- **Benefits**: Maintains scapular retraction/upward rotation, keeps arm connected to scapula, prevents elbow flexion stress, reduces shoulder strain
- **Critical for this athlete**: OHS at 50 kg is snatch limiter; chronic back pain suggests shoulder stability weakness. Neutral arm position (not aggressive IR) may reduce impingement risk and improve stability without compensation.

---

**2. Mobility/Recovery Advice**

- Arm position varies by individual shoulder mobility
- Excessive external rotation (like CrossFit teaching "show armpits") can cause chronic shoulder soreness
- Excessive internal rotation can cause impingement/tendon irritation
- **For this athlete**: With chronic back pain + low OHS, ensure shoulder mobility isn't restricted; neutral position may feel less aggressive than forced IR while building confidence overhead.

---

**3. Exercise Relevance**

- Indirectly supports daily overhead work (snatch, jerk, OHS) with proper cuing to reinforce neutral positioning
- Useful before hypertrophy block begins
