## 2. **Technique Cues for Snatch & Clean**
- **Timing**: Feet move *only after* peak extension is complete. Continuing floor pressure post-extension is counterproductive.
- **Core principle**: Remove floor pressure to accelerate downward under the bar—any remaining pressure resists that acceleration.
- **Two valid methods**: 
  - Lifting feet higher → ensures zero floor resistance, maximizes speed under bar
  - Keeping feet close → easier to finish the pull, but risks residual floor pressure if poorly executed
- **Common error**: Lifting feet too early (before power phase ends).
- **Key insight**: "Efficiency" debates are misleading—focus on *movement effectiveness*, not energy expenditure. Neither foot method is intrinsically superior; execution determines success.
