# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1868/Squat-Cycles-and-Other-Crap-That-Doesnt-Work/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Avoid "Squat Cycles":** Do not use long-term programs (e.g., Smolov) that prioritize squat volume/intensity as the #1 training priority to fix Olympic lifts. Squat strength is an assistance tool, not a cure-all.
*   **Transfer vs. Specificity:** Squat gains only transfer if the squat position mimics the receiving position of the snatch/clean (high bar, upright torso). Powerlifting-style "sit-back" squats have low transfer to Olympic lifts.
*   **Skill First:** Technical efficiency and skill development are primary drivers for increasing totals; strength is secondary unless technique is solid.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Positional Match:** Ensure the bottom position of your squat matches the receiving position of your snatch/clean (depth, torso angle, knee/hip alignment).
*   **Speed & Power:** Do not sacrifice speed and power for raw strength; high squat numbers do not guarantee fast Olympic lifts.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Focus:** Prioritize Snatch and Clean & Jerk variations over heavy back squats.
*   **Squat Selection:** Use "Olympic-style" squats (high bar, upright) rather than wide-stance powerlifting squats to maximize transfer.
