*   **Goal:** Select exercises that isolate specific deficits to allow high-repetition practice of correct mechanics without inducing fatigue that compromises the main lift.

**2. Technique Cues & Primers (Specific Fixes)**
*   **Snatch Poor Pull:** Lift-off, segment pull, or high-pull primers.
*   **Snatch Poor Finish:** Snatch from power position or snatch with no jump.
*   **Snatch Weak Lockout:** Drop snatch or tall snatch.
*   **Clean Poor Turnover:** Muscle clean, tall clean, or power clean.
*   **Jerk Forward Dip/Drive:** Jerk dip squat, jerk drive, or push press.
*   **Jerk Improper Split:** Jerk from split position or push jerk behind neck in split.
