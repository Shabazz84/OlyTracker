# Analysis: "Should I Give Up on the Split Jerk?" by Greg Everett

## **Relevant to This Athlete's Profile**

**CRITICAL:** This athlete is **push jerk only** with no split jerk experience. This article directly addresses the decision point he faces.

---

## **1. Programming Principles**
- **Time requirement:** Split jerk mastery takes time; don't abandon it prematurely before determining if poor performance reflects poor technique vs. genuine unsuitability.
- **Complexity hierarchy:** Split jerk takes time to learn, similar to snatch/clean acquisition curves.

---

## **2. Technique Cues for Split Jerk**

| Issue | Cue |
|-------|-----|
| **Driving forward** | Dip & drive must be identical to power jerk: straight down, straight up, complete. Only the split varies. |
| **Knee unlock** | Unlock knees before dip to create quad tension; prevents slack/freefall that shifts weight forward. |
| **Foot position** | Weight on heels with full foot contact (don't lose ball-of-foot contact). |
| **Breathing/bracing** | Full abdominal breath, lock it tight, activate glutes. Check balance after breath (breath itself can shift forward). |
| **Split execution** | Lift/reach *front* heel forward; drive hips under. Ignore back foot (it goes far enough naturally). Focus on pushing bar *back behind head*, not reaching head/chest forward. |
| **Overhead position** | Head/chest forward position should be reactive (consequence of pushing bar back), not initiated prematurely. |

**Assistance exercises:** Push press + split jerk complex, split position press/balance, power jerk + split jerk complex.

---

## **3. Why Split > Power for Most Lifters**
- Allows greater depth (~parallel squat), broader base (easier bar placement adjustment), least flexibility demand.
- Power jerk requires higher bar elevation, excellent overhead flexibility, and extreme precision—lower margin for error.

**For this athlete:** At 65 kg push jerk with only 50 kg OHS, flexibility is already limiting (OHS weakness). Split jerk is likely *better* long-term than staying with power jerk.
