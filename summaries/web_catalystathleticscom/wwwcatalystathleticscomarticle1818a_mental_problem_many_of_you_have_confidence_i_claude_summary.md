**RELEVANT TO THIS ATHLETE:**

**Mental Approach (Key for this profile):**
- Core issue: Confidence gap between "submaximal weights I'll MAKE" vs. "near-max weights I'll only TRY"
- Solution: **Commit to attacking every bar with conviction**, not just attempting
- Practical: This athlete's OHS (50 kg) and Jerk (65 kg) are significant limiters—mental confidence deficits compound technical/strength gaps

**Why This Matters for Your Athlete:**
- Chronic back pain + night shift fatigue likely amplify doubt on heavy attempts
- Snatch confidence particularly fragile given low OHS (50 kg vs. 102.5 kg BW)
- 6-week hypertrophy block should build confidence through volume before strength work

**Application:**
- During hypertrophy block, enforce "attack mentality" on all working sets to build conviction habits
- Before entering strength/technique phase, use confidence as a measurable metric alongside load
- Heavy attempts will fail without addressing the mental shift from "trying" to "making"
