# Analysis: "The Jerk for Sports" by Greg Everett

## Relevance to This Athlete
**Moderate relevance.** The article is conceptual (jerk vs. push press for sports) but this athlete currently does **push jerk only** — the article's discussion of technique mechanics is worth understanding, but limited direct applicability since split jerk isn't being trained yet.

---

## Key Extracts by Category

### 1. **Technique Cues – Jerk Mechanics**
- **Active push down** under the bar (not passive drop) — upper body drives against barbell as anchor while feet reposition
- Feet reconnect *before* full arm lockout; pressure against ground stays minimal to ensure complete elbow lockout before securing receiving position
- Follow-through in upper body does occur despite direction change (unlike pure push press)

### 2. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Push press and jerk both offer value; **use both in tandem** (per comment discussion)
- Jerk provides superior CNS stimulus for power athletes vs. push press alone
- **For this athlete:** Reinforces push jerk as valid strength builder, but eventual split jerk progression needed for full jerk development

### 3. **Programming Notes**
- No specific sets/reps/intensity guidance in article
- Emphasis on motor skill + strength + power training (applicable to 6-week hypertrophy block transition)
