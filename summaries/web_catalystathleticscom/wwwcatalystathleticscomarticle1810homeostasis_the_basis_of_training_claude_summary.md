## Extracted Content:

**1. Programming Principles**
- **Homeostasis concept**: Training works via progressive stress (eu-stress) that disrupts resting physiology, forcing adaptation. Gradual load increases → tissue adaptation (hypertrophy, strength, bone restructuring).
- **Key principle**: Balance stress with recovery, nutrition, and hormones to avoid dis-stress (overtraining, injury, illness).
- **Individual variability**: What's eu-stress for one athlete may be dis-stress for another—coaches must calibrate appropriately.
