# Analysis: Daily Overhead Lifts for Mobility

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**CRITICAL for this athlete** – directly addresses their primary limiter (OHS 50 kg) and chronic back pain.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency > Volume**: Daily overhead work beats weekly stretching; consistency prevents loss of range
- **Active Mobility as Skill**: Overhead stability requires regular nervous system practice, not just passive flexibility
- **Double-arm preference**: Reduces compensation; dumbbell/kettlebell work improves symmetry and demands

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- Emphasizes **strong, stable overhead position** as foundation
- Focus on **range of motion familiarity and comfort** through repeated practice
- Positioning reinforces mobility gains from other work

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
Include at least **one exercise from each category weekly**:
- **Jerk variants**: Split jerk (athlete needs this—only has push jerk), power/push jerk, pause jerk, behind-the-neck
- **Overhead Squat** (PRIMARY for OHS limiter): Snatch, OHS, snatch balance, press in snatch, push jerk in snatch
- **Push Press** variants
- **Press** variants
- **Overhead Carries**: DB/KB work for symmetry/stability demands
- **Vertical Pulls**: Pull-ups, hangs

**Off-gym days**: Hanging, door jamb hangs, dynamic warm-up mobility drills

---

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Daily overhead activity (even non-lifting) critical for night shift worker (Wed–Sun schedule allows off-gym day practice)
- Hanging/passive overhead positions on rest days
- These overhead drills enhance results of direct mobility work by reinforcing positions

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Application**
- **OHS 50 kg limiter** → Prioritize OHS variants + snatch balance + press in snatch daily
- **Push jerk only (no split jerk)** → Must add split jerk practice per Everett's recommendation
- **Chronic back pain** → Active overhead stability/mobility work may reduce compensation patterns; need coach oversight
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** → Structure off-gym days for hanging/mobility on Mondays/Tuesdays
