# Analysis: "How to Fix Powering More Than You Squat" by Greg Everett

## Relevance to Athlete Profile
**HIGHLY RELEVANT.** This athlete power jerks only (no split jerk) and has a weak OHS (50 kg vs. 80 kg clean). The article directly addresses the core issue: inadequate receiving position strength/stability limiting full lift performance.

---

## Key Extracts by Category

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Eliminate power variations** entirely; replace with full lift variations
- Add **squat to every lift accidentally powered** (remedial + punitive)
- Hold all snatches in bottom position **3–5 seconds**
- Sample template: Clean + Front Squat; Clean + Front Squat + Jerk
- **Frequency**: Squat more (both front squat and overhead squat)

### 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch & Clean)**
- **Snatch receiving**: Pull *under* the bar, not just power it
- **Overhead stability > strength**: Bar placement and stillness matter more than raw power
- **Front squat posture for clean**: Upright trunk + trunk stability to absorb load
- Build **snatch balance** to exceed best snatch by a comfortable margin

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Remove**: Power snatches, power cleans
- **Replace with**: High-hang/high-block full variations
- **Add**: Tall snatch/clean, snatch/clean from power position (builds pull-under confidence)
- **Mobility work**: Snatch/clean holding in bottom for time; frequent stretching daily
- **Strength**: Pulls and deadlifts off riser (weak pull diagnosis)

### 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Limited mobility is secondary issue—fix via **accumulated time in squat receiving positions**
- "Stretch your face off every day instead of searching for mobility shortcuts that don't exist"
- Hold bottom positions 3–5 seconds daily to build comfort and capacity

### 5. **Specific to This Athlete**
| Issue | Your Data | Recommendation |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **OHS weakness** | 50 kg (snatch limiter) | Build OHS to exceed best snatch (80 kg) + hold 3 sec; add snatch balances |
| **Only push jerk** | No split jerk | Front squat strength essential for receiving clean; same squat logic applies |
| **Back pain** | Chronic | High-block/high-hang variations may reduce lumbar load vs. full depth work initially; mobility priority |
| **Night shift** | Wed–Sun | Frequency challenges—prioritize quality squatting days when recovered |

---

## Bottom Line
**Core fix for this athlete**: Dramatically increase front squat & overhead squat frequency/volume. Build OHS to 80+ kg with 3-sec bottom holds. Remove power snatch; use high-hang/block snatch and snatch from power position to build pull-under confidence before chasing full squat snatch.
