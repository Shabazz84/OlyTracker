# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1898/How-to-Find-a-Weightlifting-Coach/

**This text is not about weightlifting programming, technique, or training—it's a guide on selecting a weightlifting coach.**

---

**Relevant to this athlete's profile:**

1. **Coach Selection Criteria** (most relevant): The athlete has chronic back pain, works night shifts, and is transitioning to Olympic lifting with significant technical gaps (no split jerk, weak OHS). This article emphasizes finding a coach with:
   - Competitive experience at national level minimum
   - Track record producing competitive lifters
   - Focus exclusively on weightlifting (not multi-sport coaching)
   - Training partners at similar/higher caliber

2. **Training Environment**: Emphasizes training partners are critical to success. This athlete would benefit from a gym with serious intermediate-to-advanced lifters to push progression.

3. **Practical Note**: The article stresses facility/equipment are secondary to coaching quality and atmosphere—relevant given the athlete's chronic back pain (needs proper setup but coaching expertise matters more).

---

**Bottom line**: This is a coach-selection article, not a training resource. For this athlete's specific needs (hypertrophy block, split jerk development, OHS mobility, back pain management, night-shift recovery), you need programming and technical guidance—not covered here.
