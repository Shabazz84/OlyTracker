# Analysis: "Get Cozy Down There" by Greg Everett

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- No new exercises required; add 2–3 seconds holding time in receiving positions
- Minimal time addition to existing workouts
- Applies to: snatch, power snatch, jerk, power jerk, overhead squat, snatch balance
- **For power variations**: hold before standing to avoid early recovery

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Snatch/Jerk receiving**: Hold bar overhead in bottom position for 2–3 seconds before recovering
- Hold an additional 1 second *after* standing before dropping
- **Power variations**: Complete lockout/rack position fully before initiating stand (prevents soft overhead, pressing out)
- **Overhead stability cue** (from comments): Imagine holding a coin between shoulders and neck (active shoulders)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Snatch balance** (for lockout stability)
- Overhead/pressing strength work (especially if elbow unlocks on max attempts)
- Avoid holding clean bottoms—use bounce instead (per Everett)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Not addressed in text

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
⚠️ **HIGHLY RELEVANT**: This directly addresses your **50 kg OHS limiter**. Weak/unstable receiving positions are masking overhead strength deficits. Implementing 2–3 second holds in all overhead work (snatch, power snatch, jerk, OHS) will expose and build the lockout stability you're lacking—critical before split jerk progression. Fits into your hypertrophy block with zero programming disruption.
