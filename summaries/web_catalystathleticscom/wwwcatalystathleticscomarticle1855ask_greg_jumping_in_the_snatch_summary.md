# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/1855/Ask-Greg-Jumping-in-the-Snatch/

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Intensity/Load:** For lighter weights (relative to bodyweight), control the force of extension rather than maximizing aggression; do not change the movement pattern, just reduce explosive intent until heavier loads are reached.
*   **Frequency/Progression:** Prioritize technique and speed over mixing heavy/light arbitrarily when lagging behind due to poor mechanics.
*   **Periodization:** Focus on a specific technical goal (foot placement) rather than fluctuating weights until the movement is mastered.

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **"Jump" vs. "Lift Feet":** Distinguish between elevating your center of mass (jumping) and lifting feet to fall under the bar (picking up feet). Lifting feet causes the body to fall, not rise.
*   **Timing:** You pull the bar up first; *then*, after full knee/hip extension, you begin pulling down by lifting your feet. Do not do both simultaneously.
*   **Arm Action:** The transition from "up" to "down" coincides with the moment you begin bending your arms (switching from high-pull mechanics).
*   **Foot Width:** Feet naturally move outward during the pull under; do not force a wide stance at the start unless it is an individual preference.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Snatch High Pulls:** Use as a reference for the "up" phase of the lift.
*   **Full Snatch/Clean:** Practice lifting feet significantly (5-6 inches) to facilitate dropping under the bar quickly with zero resistance from the body.
