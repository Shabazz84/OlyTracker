# Analysis: Snatch & Clean Pulling Technique

## Technique Cues (Primary Content)

**Pull Finish - Two Valid Approaches:**

1. **Toe-Extension Pulling** (more common at elite level)
   - Extend high onto toes at top of pull
   - Creates longest extension line; audible foot slap on landing
   - Examples: Zlatan Vanev, Behdad Salimi
   - Risk: Avoid excessive foot lift causing "hang-time" floating phase

2. **Flat-Foot Pulling** (legitimate alternative)
   - Minimal heel rise; maintain foot contact throughout pull
   - Allows continued force generation into floor
   - Example: Xiaojun Lu (especially in clean)
   - Valid but less commonly taught

**Key Principle:** No single "right" way; both produce world-class results.

---

## Relevance to This Athlete

**Critical for OHS/Snatch Issues (50 kg limiter):**
- Pull technique directly impacts receiving position mechanics
- Clean pull (80 kg) stronger than snatch, suggesting pull-to-catch timing/positioning issue
- Chronic back pain: Toe-extension pull demands greater trunk extension—may exacerbate. Consider flat-foot approach or modified toe-extension.
- Night shift fatigue: Master one consistent pull pattern (don't experiment constantly)

**During hypertrophy block:** Establish pull consistency before 6-week progression; back health prioritized.
