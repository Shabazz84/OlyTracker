# Analysis: "Your First Weightlifting Competition" by Greg Everett

## Relevance to This Athlete
**Moderately relevant.** General competition prep guide—useful but doesn't address this athlete's specific constraints (chronic back pain, night shift, OHS limitation, push jerk only).

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Peak for competition date** with tapering for freshness
- **Conservative attempt selection**: confidence-based openers, small jumps (smaller in snatch than C&J)
- Avoid greedy PRs at first meet; prioritize experience and making lifts
- No specific periodization details given

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Hold overhead securely** until referee down signal (critical rule)
- **No knee drops or platform contact** except feet
- Lock out lifts "forcefully" and maintain until down signal
- Don't rush once called—1-minute clock is ample time

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Load weights as competition will load them (including collars)
- Practice warm-up progressions for snatch and C&J
- Consider taking opening weight as final warm-up (especially snatch if nervous)
- **Avoid excessive warm-up sets**—loose up first, then bar work at opener weight

---

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Spend "extra time loosening up before touching bar"
- Minimal specific detail; prioritize freshness over additional volume

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Gaps**
- **No guidance on**:
  - Managing chronic back pain during competition
  - Night shift fatigue/recovery timing
  - OHS deficit strategy (snatch technique work needed pre-meet)
  - Push jerk pacing/confidence building
