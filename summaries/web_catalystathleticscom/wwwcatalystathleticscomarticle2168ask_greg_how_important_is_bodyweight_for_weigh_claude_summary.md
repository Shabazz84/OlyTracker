## **5. RELEVANT TO THIS ATHLETE'S PROFILE**

**Key takeaway for your 102.5 kg lifter:**

- **Bodyweight matters significantly in Olympic weightlifting** relative to height—leverage advantage favors being shorter for your weight class.
- **C&J affected more than snatch** by bodyweight changes; single pounds can translate to several kilos in total.
- **Body composition critical**: Gaining weight carelessly (high body fat) won't transfer to performance.
- **Individual response varies**: Some athletes notice dramatic 1–2 lb differences; others don't. Test how your athlete *feels* at different weights.

**Practical application**: At 102.5 kg, if your athlete is taller (5'10"+), consider whether strategic bodyweight increases with good body composition could boost C&J/squat during the hypertrophy block. If shorter, current weight may already be optimal leverage-wise. Prioritize *how he feels* during the night shift work—weight changes affecting energy/recovery should be avoided.
