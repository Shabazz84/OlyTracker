# Merged Summary: Masters Weightlifting Program Adapted for Your Profile

## Key Points
- 3 days/week, ~90 min sessions (compatible with Wed–Sun night shift; 2–3 rest days between sessions aid recovery)
- 6-week meet-prep cycle: Weeks 1–4 progressive intensity ramp (60–95%), Week 5 peak, Week 6 taper
- Volume reduces into final weeks; post-cycle recovery week recommended (reduce weight 10–15%, volume 15–25%)
- **Program is technique/strength-focused, not hypertrophy**—apply this *after* your planned 6-week hypertrophy block
- Heavy snatch/C&J 2–3x/week; pulls (snatch/clean) 3x/week at 80–95%
- **OHS work emphasized Weeks 1–4** to directly address your 50 kg ceiling (primary snatch limiter)

## Technique Cues
- Frequent technique complexes on lighter days: halting deadlifts, mid-hang variations, balance work
- OHS-specific progression to build stability and overhead strength
- Clean/snatch pull integration at 80–95% intensity for pattern refinement

## Programming Principles
- Alternating back squat + front squat (flexibility built in)
- Progressive intensity buildup over 4 weeks before peak
- Structured taper prevents fatigue into competition phase
- Frequent OHS work to remove technical bottleneck

## Athlete Relevance

**Strengths:**
- 3x/week frequency integrates cleanly with night shift (Wed–Sun) schedule
- Dedicated OHS volume directly targets your weakest lift relative to clean (50 kg vs. 80 kg)
- Lower overall volume than daily programs supports shift-work recovery

**Modifications Required:**
- **Substitute front squat for back squat** or reduce back squat frequency due to chronic back pain; coach confirms this flexibility
- Add targeted prehab: OHS mobility, thoracic extension, anti-rotation work (program lacks recovery/mobility guidance)
- Sequence: Run your 6-week hypertrophy block *first*, then apply this technique/strength cycle
