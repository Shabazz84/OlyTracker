Reduce all cues to single words or short phrases (e.g., "feet," "flat") to prevent cognitive overload during execution.

4. **Recovery or Mobility Advice:** None provided; the focus is on mental recovery through "suspension of disbelief" (confidence) rather than physical rest protocols.

5. **Athlete Profile Relevance:**
   *   **Chronic Back Pain/Intermediate Status:** The text warns against over-analyzing ("skull sweat") during lifts, which can lead to hesitation and poor mechanics that exacerbate injury risk for an athlete with back issues.
   *   **Night Shift Worker:** The advice to reserve detailed technical analysis for non-training times (post-training journaling) is crucial for a shift worker who may have limited cognitive bandwidth during training due to fatigue; it prevents "wheels grinding" when the body is already stressed.
   *   **Push Jerk Only:** While not specific to the jerk type, the rule of limiting thoughts to one per phase applies strictly here to ensure the athlete does not hesitate on the critical transition from front rack to overhead lockout.
