# https://www.catalystathletics.com/article/2035/Moving-from-Textbook-to-Idiosyncratic-Technique-in-the-Snatc/

**1. Programming principles**
*   **Consistency over Perfection:** Prioritize performing the exact same movement pattern every session rather than chasing technical perfection or mimicking elite variations.
*   **Baseline First:** Adhere to "textbook" technique for a significant initial period before attempting individual deviations.
*   **Avoid Early Experimentation:** Do not change technical approaches weekly; allow time to reach a baseline of proficiency before introducing idiosyncratic adjustments.

**2. Technique cues**
*   **Textbook Foundation:** Focus on fundamental principles of physics and biomechanics that apply to the vast majority of lifters.
*   **Proportional Variation:** Understand that "textbook" technique allows for variation based on individual body proportions, but these variations must not violate core movement principles.
*   **Stability:** The goal is technical consistency (doing the same thing every time) rather than a perfect but inconsistent execution.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   *None specific to exercises are listed in this text.* The article focuses on the philosophy of learning technique rather than prescribing specific lifts or variations.

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Mobility as a Prerequisite:** Do not attempt technical deviations if immobility is present; fix correctable weaknesses (like immobility) first to ensure deviations are not compensations for errors.
*   **Mental Recovery:** Acknowledge that early development can be "tedious, mundane, and boring," requiring patience rather than excitement about new trends.

**5. Relevance to this athlete's profile**
