# Analysis: Six Truths of Olympic Weightlifting Technique

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Truth 2 (Bar Proximity)** — CRITICAL for your athlete
- Keep barbell close to body throughout pull
- Poor bar path exacerbates weak OHS (50 kg limiter)
- Small distance errors magnify in complex movements

**Truth 3 (No Time Wasted at Top)** — Addresses technique transition
- Rapid shift from acceleration → pulling under bar
- Relevant for clean technique development (80 kg current)

**Truth 4 (Active Relocation)** — Directly applicable
- Pull/push under must be aggressive, not passive
- Essential for split jerk progression (currently push jerk only)
- Reduces reliance on upper body strength

**Truth 5 (Stable Receiving Position)** — Highly relevant
- Receiving position stability > specific joint angles
- Critical for your athlete: weak OHS indicates receiving position instability or squat depth/strength deficit
- Bar must sit on trunk, not arms

**Truth 6 (Consistency Over Style)** — Programming principle
- Emphasize repetition/volume for consistency
- Aligns with your 6-week hypertrophy block goal
