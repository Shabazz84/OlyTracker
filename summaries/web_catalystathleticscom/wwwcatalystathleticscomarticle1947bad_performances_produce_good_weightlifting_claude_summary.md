## Relevant to Your Profile:

**Mental Framework (Competition Mindset)**
- Avoid "hope I don't bomb" mentality → shift to aggressive confidence, not cautious avoidance
- Channel bad performances into "chip on shoulder" motivation for revenge/comeback
- Target balance: relaxation + confidence + internal competitive fire
- *Applies to you:* Your night-shift fatigue + chronic back issues may foster cautious mentality. Combat this by reframing setbacks as fuel.

**Recovery/Support Angle**
- Peer/coach support after failures critical for mental resilience
- Don't spiral into discouragement; use anger productively
- *Applies to you:* With back pain limiting potential, maintain aggressive mindset toward technical progress rather than surrendering to limitations.

## Not Covered:
- No programming, sets/reps, periodization details
- No specific technique cues for snatch, clean, or jerk
- No exercises or mobility work for chronic back pain
- No night-shift-specific recovery strategies
