# Analysis for Your Athlete Profile

## Relevant Extraction:

**1. Technique Cues for Jerk**
- Power jerk forces: solid dip/drive, balanced orientation, vertical drive (not forward), correct bar punch slightly back
- Develops elastic dip/drive and proper timing
- Exposes imbalances that split jerk can mask

**2. Programming (Power Jerk Usage)**
- Light weights with high speed (not heavy)
- Doubles/triples on lighter training days
- Technique primer before split jerks
- Complex: 2 power jerks + 1 split jerk
- Start light to develop feel; gradually increase for barbell whip/elasticity

**3. Relevance to This Athlete:**
⚠️ **High relevance** — Push jerk is your current only jerk option. Power jerk work directly transfers to split jerk development. Recommend:
- Use power jerks as technical foundation before split jerk introduction (lighter weights, speed focus)
- Post-hypertrophy block: integrate 2 PJ + 1 split jerk complexes to build split jerk mechanics progressively
- **Caution:** Monitor chronic back pain during jerk work; vertical drive emphasis should help vs. forward compensation patterns

**4. Not Addressed:** OHS limitation, back pain modifications, shift-work recovery, or periodization specifics for your 6-week block.
