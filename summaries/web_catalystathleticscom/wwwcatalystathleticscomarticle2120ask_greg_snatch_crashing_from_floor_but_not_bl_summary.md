**1. Programming principles**
*   **Structure:** Implement complexes (Power Snatch + Full Snatch or Hang Snatch + Full Snatch).
*   **Reps/Intensity:** Use moderate loads (same weight for both reps in the complex) to prioritize technique over max intensity initially.
*   **Frequency:** High frequency of turnover drills; focus on "staying connected" during the third pull rather than just pulling high and dropping.

**2. Technique cues**
*   **Core Concept:** Do not drop out from under the bar after the pull; actively meet the bar at the exact height it reaches.
*   **Visual Cue:** Secure the bar overhead in the *same position* as a power snatch before transitioning into the full squat depth (do not "brake" and hold high).
*   **Turnover:** Focus on speed under the bar specifically to match the pull height, rather than pulling higher indiscriminately.

**3. Exercise recommendations**

*   Hang Snatch + Full Snatch Complex.
*   Variations: Power Snatch + Hang Snatch.
