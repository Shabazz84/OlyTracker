# Analysis

**Text relevance:** Weightlifting coaching—specifically clean technique.

---

## 1. **Technique Cue: Clean Meeting Point**
- Meet the bar at its **highest point** after the pull (before descending into catch)
- Purpose: Enable bar deceleration into the bottom position
- Benefit: Gain control before changing direction; prevents weight "crashing down" during catch

## 2. **Technique Cue: Deceleration Control**
- Actively decelerate while descending under the bar
- Analogy: Like a heavy front squat descent—control the eccentric to manage direction change
- Result: Maintains form/shape and prevents loss of position standing up

---

## **Relevance to This Athlete:**
- **High priority.** At 80 kg clean, meeting point and deceleration are **technique foundations** before hypertrophy block
- **Back pain consideration:** Poor meeting point → uncontrolled catch → spinal stress. Clean deceleration control is injury-prevention critical
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** If catch position is uncontrolled, front rack position suffers → OHS ceiling. This cue directly supports overhead work

---

**Action:** Drill clean pulls → meet bar high → controlled descent into catch (light loads first, given back history).
