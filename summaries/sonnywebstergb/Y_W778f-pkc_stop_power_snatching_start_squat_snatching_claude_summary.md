**Programming & Technique Relevance:**

1. **Technique Cue - Snatch Receiving Position:** Developing squat snatch (vs. power snatch) efficiency allows deeper catch, reducing required bar height. Critical for this athlete given weak OHS (50 kg) — deeper squat receiving leverages better positioning and strength.
