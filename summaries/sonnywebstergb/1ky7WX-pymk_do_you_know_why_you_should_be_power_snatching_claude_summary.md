# Do you know why you should be power snatching? 🤔

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS:**

1. **Programming/Technique Priority:**
   - Squat snatch mastery should precede power snatch focus
   - Power snatch = accessory movement for speed/extension development only
   - Full snatch allows heavier loads than power snatch

2. **Direct Relevance to Athlete:**
   - **Critical:** OHS is stated limiter (50 kg). This aligns with coaching emphasis on squat snatch mastery—athlete needs to build OHS/receiving position strength before prioritizing power snatch
   - During 6-week hypertrophy block, should emphasize full snatch variations over power snatch to build squat capacity
   - Power snatch can support extension speed work *after* establishing squat snatch foundation

3. **Implication for Back Pain:**
   - Power snatch (less knee bend) may seem appealing for chronic back pain, but coaching advises against skipping squat snatch foundation—suggests gradual OHS/squat snatch progression with proper load management is necessary path

**Action:** Prioritize OHS and full snatch volume during hypertrophy block. Power snatch used sparingly for speed work only.
