# Why the TEMPO of your squats are so important!

# Extraction: Warm-Up Tempo & Control

**Programming/Technique Principle:**
- Use controlled tempo during warm-up sets (emphasize eccentric control)
- Consistent warm-up tempo → better positioning & force production on heavy sets
- Poor warm-up control (rushing) = inability to change direction in bottom position on top sets

**Relevance to This Athlete:**
- **Critical for chronic back pain management** — controlled descents reduce spinal loading
- **Especially important for OHS (50 kg limiter)** — bottom position control directly impacts snatch depth/stability
- **Supports transition goals** — deliberate warm-ups build technical foundation before intensity blocks

**Actionable:** Prescribe 3–5 tempo warm-up reps (e.g., 3-sec eccentric, pause 1-sec) before working sets in hypertrophy block.
