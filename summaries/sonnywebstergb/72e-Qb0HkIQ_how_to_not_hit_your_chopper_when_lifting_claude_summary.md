# How to not hit your chopper when lifting.

**Analysis:**

1. **Technique cues (Clean/Snatch):**
   - Keep bar close as it passes the knees
   - Shoulders stay over the bar during transition phase
   - Avoid leaning back behind the bar (causes exposure/injury risk)

2. **Equipment note:**
   - Recommends lycra shorts (protective)

3. **Relevance to athlete:**
   - **HIGH relevance** — Bar path control (keeping bar close, shoulder position) directly addresses snatch technique issues. Given athlete's weak OHS (50 kg), poor bar positioning in the turnover is likely a limiting factor.
   - Back pain concern: Leaning back behind the bar increases lumbar load; staying over the bar is safer for chronic back issues.

**Note:** Presentation is crude/colloquial but content is valid technical coaching.
