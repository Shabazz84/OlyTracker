# Do you know why you should be power snatching? 🤔

1. **Programming principles**: Power snatching should be restricted to accessory work focused on speed and extension; do not use it as a primary method for strength or technique development, as the full squat snatch allows for heavier loads.
2. **Technique cues**: Prioritize mastering the "squat snatch" position before attempting power variations; focus on generating speed and proper extension during the pull rather than rushing the bar overhead without receiving it under the hips.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: Use power snatches only as a secondary accessory movement for speed work, not as a main lift.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None provided in this specific excerpt.
5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: The text reinforces that an athlete with chronic back pain and limited overhead stability (OHS 50 kg) should avoid the high-speed, lower-stability power snatch as a primary movement; focusing on the squat snatch mechanics is safer for spinal loading and necessary to eventually increase their OHS strength.
