# Analysis: Front Rack Mobility & Clean Position

## Technique Cues (Clean)
- **Upper back rounding = mobility issue**, not technique failure
- Bar should rest on **shoulder platform** (upper traps/delts), NOT wrists
- Wrist pain indicates insufficient front rack position, not wrist weakness
- Key mobility components: **lats, triceps, shoulders, upper thoracic extension**

## Mobility Advice (Relevant to Athlete)
- **Upper thoracic mobility is priority** — enables upright torso in clean bottom position
- Loose lats/triceps/shoulders essential for clean rack position
- Addresses chronic back pain risk: poor front rack → forward lean → lumbar compensation

## Specific to This Athlete
- **Critical for 80 kg clean**: Better front rack mobility will improve clean mechanics and reduce back strain
- **Supports OHS work** (50 kg limiter): Upper thoracic extension transfers to overhead position
- Night shift recovery: Mobility drills are low-intensity, can be done flexibly
