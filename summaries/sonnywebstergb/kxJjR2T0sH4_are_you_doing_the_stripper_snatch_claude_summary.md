# Are you doing the STRIPPER Snatch?! 👠🏋️‍♀️

**Weightlifting Content: Snatch Technique**

1. **Technique Cue - Snatch Pull:**
   - Avoid "stripper snatch" (legs extending too early)
   - Lead with chest or maintain consistent back angle until mid-thigh
   - Prevents premature knee extension that shifts weight to toes
   - Result: Vertical bar path, cleaner receipt (avoids forward jump)

2. **Relevant to Athlete:**
   - **Critical for this lifter**: OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg). Poor bar positioning from early leg extension compounds this weakness. Chest-lead cue directly improves receiving position quality.
   - Back pain concern: Maintaining chest/back angle longer may reduce spinal stress in pull compared to aggressive early extension.

3. **Not Addressed:**
   - Sets/reps/intensity specifics
   - Recovery/mobility for night shift schedule or chronic back

**Recommendation for coach:** Pair this pull cue with overhead stability work (OHS progressions) to address the 50 kg ceiling.
