**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Snatch Pulls/Overhead Squats:** Focus on "slow descent and slow ascent" at 60-70% to find comfort in the bottom position (the "sit"). Hold the bottom for 2-3 seconds during warm-ups to identify pressure points.
*   **Snatch:** Maintain the angle of the torso during the pull; do not let the bar drift forward. Use leg drive ("jump") and spread feet wide.
*   **Clean & Jerk (Push Jerk focus):** Keep shoulders elevated, elbows up, and hips back during the front squat position to replicate the receiving angle of the clean.
*   **Snatch Pulls:** Do not use arms to lift the bar higher than the power position; let the speed from the leg drive dictate the height.
