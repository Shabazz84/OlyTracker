# 5 Best Mobility | Flexibility Drills for Clean, Front Squat, Jerk / Torokhtiy

**This is a weightlifting coaching video (Russian) on 5 mobility/prep exercises for clean & front squat.**

---

## **1. Programming Principles**
- **Frequency/Duration:** 2-3 sets per exercise, 10-30 second holds/reps
- **Intensity:** Light/bodyweight emphasis; progressively load (stick → 10-15 kg → 20 kg)
- **Focus:** Mobility/prehab work, not strength building

---

## **2. Technique Cues**
- **Band pull-throughs (Exercise 1):** Chest-height band grip, press down 10-20 sec, control elbow position
- **Overhead rotation (Exercise 2):** Elbow stays forward (not flared), shoulder/elbow mobility emphasis
- **Overhead hold (Exercise 3):** Jerk grip, deliberate descent, find comfortable depth, hold 20-30 sec
- **Elbow rotation drill (Exercise 4):** Elbows drive back→up maximally; keep bar height fixed (only elbows rotate); start with PVC/light weight; **avoid rushing progression**
- **Front squat with straps (Exercise 5):** Elbows high, slow descent/fast ascent; **single strap wrap only** (not double—unsafe); maintain shoulder/front rack mobility

---

## **3. Exercise Recommendations**
All 5 target **front rack mobility and shoulder/elbow positioning**—directly relevant to your **weak OHS (50 kg)** and **poor front rack mobility** likely limiting clean receiving position.

---

## **4. Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- Emphasize **elbow forward position** and **shoulder external rotation**
- Use straps to lock in rack position without compensations
- **Progress slowly**—mobility work requires patience, not force

---

## **5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
**HIGHLY RELEVANT to your profile:**
- OHS (50 kg) limiter = poor shoulder/elbow mobility → these drills directly address this
- Front squat/clean receive compromised by tight shoulders → strap front squats fix compensations
- Chronic back pain: front squat & overhead work unload lumbar vs. back squat
- **Recommendation:** Integrate these 2-3x/week in your hypertrophy block as prehab before main lifts
