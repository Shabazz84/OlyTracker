## 2. **Technique Cues: Overhead Position (Jerk/Snatch)**

- **Stability through integration**: Create "one system" by coordinating shoulders, elbows, and bar path—not isolated segments
- **Elbow position**: Elbows slightly forward (not extreme) creates stronger, more stable overhead position than elbows back
- **Scapular stability**: Activate external rotators (rotator cuff, infraspinatus) to lock shoulders and prevent impingement
- **Chest position**: Drive chest forward to maintain balance and center bar over midfoot
- **Bar catch location**: Catch bar *here* (vertical, stable position) not in front—front positions sacrifice 5+ kg and risk instability
- **Height variability**: Taller athletes (165–170+ cm) must catch with more forward lean; shorter athletes can sit more vertical. "Chinese" vs. "European" positions differ due to leverages—both valid if mechanically sound
