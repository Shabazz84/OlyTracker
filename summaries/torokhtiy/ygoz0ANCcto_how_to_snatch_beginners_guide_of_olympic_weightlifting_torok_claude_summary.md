# How to Snatch: Beginners Guide of Olympic Weightlifting / Torokhtiy & Rebeka

**Not about weightlifting coaching in English.** This is a Russian-language snatch progression tutorial with audio transcription errors making it largely unintelligible. 

**Salvageable technical points (despite transcription corruption):**
- Snatch grip width determination (90° elbow angle methods)
- 6-part snatch progression: top-down teaching (overhead position → starting position)
- Overhead squat, snatch pull, high pull, starting position work
- Cue: bar close to body during pull
- Cue: shoulders slightly in front of knees at start
- Cue: knees out, back straight, arms straight
- Suggested volumes: 3-4 sets × 8-10 reps with empty bar/light weight
- Progression: 50% of best lift for movement practice

**Relevance to this athlete:** Limited applicability given transcription quality, but the **top-down teaching approach and snatch pull progressions are sound for developing OHS (weak area)** and technique before heavier loading.
