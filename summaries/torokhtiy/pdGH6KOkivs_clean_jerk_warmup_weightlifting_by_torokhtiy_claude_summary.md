# Clean & Jerk WarmUp / weightlifting by Torokhtiy

# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **6-exercise warm-up complex** (sequenced progression)
- **Sets/Reps**: 2×4 front squat; 2×8 wall squat; 2 sets jerk work
- **Intensity**: Light (PVC/40-50kg) — mobility/technique focus, not loading
- **Structure**: Movement preparation → positional work → complex

## 2. Technique Cues

**Muscle Clean/Clean Position:**
- Elbows "as high as possible" through pull
- Rotate elbows *around* the bar (not under)
- Bar lands on shoulders, NOT clavicles
- Keep bar close to body throughout

**Front Squat (with straps):**
- Full grip width (clean grip, not snatch)
- Elbows high and forward
- Hips back, knees out
- Slow down, faster up
- Straps force proper rack position (prevents loose grip)

**Jerk:**
- Stop & breathe after clean (stabilizes core before jerk)
- Full extension: shoulders & knees vertical line
- Catch position: shoulders & knees aligned
- Power vs. Split: split jerk uses predetermined foot line (one forward, one back—parallel)

**Wall Squat:**
- Stay close to wall (prevents forward knee/chest drift)
- 90° angles at knees & hips
- Restrains excessive forward lean

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Press with 6-sec hold (shoulder mobility)
- Wall squat (fixes depth issues & controls forward drift)
- Front squat with straps (forces clean position)
- Split jerk mapping (footwork consistency)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- Shoulder press holds for mobility/flexibility
- Strap-assisted front squats (elbow assistance from partner if needed)

## 5. **Relevant to This Athlete**
⚠️ **CRITICAL:** This warm-up has **zero back-specific considerations** for chronic back pain. Wall squats, straps work, and breathing cues are good, but **missing:**
- No extension mobility (thoracic/lumbar)
- No posterior chain prep
- Breathing cue during jerk is helpful but insufficient for back stability long-term

**OHS limiter (50kg):** Front squat work with straps will improve rack position but won't directly address snatch mobility. Consider **snatch-specific** warm-up if this is primary goal.

**Night shift impact:** Not addressed; recovery between Wed–Sun training cycles should prioritize sleep quality.
