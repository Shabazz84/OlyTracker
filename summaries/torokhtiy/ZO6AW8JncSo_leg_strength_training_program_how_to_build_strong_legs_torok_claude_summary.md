# Analysis: Leg Strength Program (Session 2)

## Relevant to Athlete Profile

**Programming:**
- 12-session block structure (aligns with 6-week hypertrophy goal)
- 3-block format: warm-up circuit → thrusters → main lift + accessory
- Average intensity/volume approach
- Frequency: appears to be single sessions, likely 2–3x/week

**Exercises Recommended:**
1. **Warm-up circuit** (2 rounds):
   - Banded single-leg RDL (glute/core activation + unilateral work)
   - Squat jumps (explosive leg prep)
   - Ball mobility shoulder/elbow rotations (wrist/shoulder prep for rack position)
   - Front squat with straps (mobility + strength)
   - Thrusters (light, intensive sets)

2. **Main lift:** Front squat (emphasis on full-grip bar position, upright torso, high elbows)

3. **Accessory:** Deficit RDL + rocks (15–20 kg plate height; slow tempo, neutral spine)

**Relevant Cues:**
- Front squat: "keep lower back tight, elbows high, slow down, fast up"
- Full-grip emphasis (addresses OHS/rack position deficits)
- Mobility work for shoulder/elbow (addresses overhead position)

**Recovery/Mobility:**
- Shoulder/elbow rotations in warm-up
- Front squat with straps for flexibility development

---

## ⚠️ **Considerations for This Athlete:**
- **Back pain**: Deficit RDL + emphasis on neutral spine is appropriate; monitor ROM
- **OHS 50 kg limiter**: Full-grip front squat + straps work directly addresses this
- **Night shift**: Single 30–40 min sessions may suit recovery constraints
