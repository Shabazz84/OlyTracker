# WEEK #5 / TOROKHTIY_GANG training program

# Weightlifting Coaching Transcript Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Week 5 of 8-week block** (post-deload week 4)
- **Structure**: 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional accessory sessions (Tue/Thu)
- **Intensity**: Increasing; percentages 60-70% early in week, progressing to 90-95% on competition lifts
- **Volume**: Maintained but at heavier loads
- **Model**: Transitioning toward competition-style movements (2nd/3rd exercises mimicking classic lifts)
- **Philosophy**: Minimize excessive squatting to avoid leg fatigue; prioritize classic lift execution over volume

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Snatch pull**: Slow descent to knees, accelerate after knees; finish in receiving position; pause 2-3 sec in squat to feel position
- **Snatch (classic)**: Use straps for technique work; warm up 30-40kg for 4-5 sets; maintain angle during pull/extension; pause in squat during warm-up
- **Snatch pull error**: Don't artificially pull with arms post-extension—rely only on leg drive velocity
- **Push jerk**: Slow dip, bar travels **behind head** (not in front—common error); leg drive transfers energy to arms
- **Front squat**: Shoulders forward, hips back; mirrors clean receiving position and jerk dip angle

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Hyperextension** (all 3 sessions): Add lateral flexion and rotation variants
- **Snatch pull** (Session 1)
- **Classic jerk** (Session 1—control exercise)
- **Snatch from hang** (Session 2, no straps, 60-70%)
- **Push jerk** (Session 2; emphasize leg-arm interaction)
- **Front squat with straps** (Sessions 2 & 3; secondary role)
- **Classic snatch** (Session 3, with straps; control exercise)
- **Snatch pull** (Session 3, after snatch; 2-3 sets based on snatch volume)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- Emphasize sitting in receiving position during warm-ups with light weight to identify discomfort/positional issues
- Address any squat position limitations early before heavy loading

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
- **OHS limiter (50kg)**: Front squat work + receiving position practice directly addresses mobility/comfort in squat
- **Push jerk only / no split jerk**: Program focuses on push jerk refinement (behind-head bar position critical)
- **Chronic back pain**: Hyperextension + careful loading progression + emphasis on position comfort (not volume) supports spinal health
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: 3 main sessions strategically placed; optional Tue/Thu sessions provide flexibility for recovery window
- **Intermediate status**: Classic lift focus over accessory volume aligns with technical foundation needs before heavy strength block
