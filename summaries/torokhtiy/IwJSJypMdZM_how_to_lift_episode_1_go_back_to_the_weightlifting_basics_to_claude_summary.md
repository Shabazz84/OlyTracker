## EXTRACTED CONTENT

### 1. Programming Principles
- **Two-week structure:** Week 1 = fundamentals/technique review (PVC, empty bar, slow movement); Week 2 = intensity increase with load progression
- **Progressive loading:** Master technique at light weights before adding load
- **Recovery integration:** Adequate rest between heavy sessions; monitoring athlete feedback to adjust daily programming
- **Frequency note:** Coach requests feedback on soreness/feeling to customize next day's work

### 2. Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean Focus)
- **Grip:** Use "hook grip" (замок) — reduces forearm tension (30% vs 100%) and allows easier elbow bend during pull and catch
- **Pull mechanics:** "Elbows up, rotate elbows" → pull bar close to body
- **Receiving position:** Pause/hold in catch position to reinforce stability; prevents rushing
- **Common error:** Stopping the bar with excessive forearm/arm tension in standard grip
- **Catch depth:** Sit deeper, open chest; incorrect positioning causes bar loss

### 3. Exercise Recommendations
- PVC pipe drills (technical foundation)
- Empty bar work (movement pattern reinforcement)
- Tempo training (slow, controlled reps before speed work)

### 4. Recovery & Mobility
- Massage
- Stretching sessions
- Sauna/steam (temperature contrast recovery)
- Emphasis on parasympathetic activation
