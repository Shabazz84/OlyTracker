**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Structure:** 3 blocks per session (Special Warm-up Circuit → Main Strength Exercise → Explosive Auxiliary Routine).
*   **Frequency/Duration:** Program consists of 12 sessions over a 4-week block; each session is treated as a standalone workout.
*   **Intensity/Volume:** High load focus ("huge amount of load"). Warm-up circuits are performed for 2 rounds. Main exercises use heavy loads (bench back squat) or controlled tempos (front squats).

**2. Technique Cues**
*   **Front Squat:** "Big breath," move hips back, knees out, hold bar with full grip, direct elbows upward, control feet balance on the way down, speed up on the way up.
*   **Box Front Squat:** Touch box with toes, lower slowly, keep pelvis stretched back, torso and elbows forward (challenging balance).
*   **Bench Back Squat:** Focus heavily on the eccentric phase; focus on balance and speed deceleration on the way down; recover power from the bottom.
*   **Box Jumps:** Start jump from a half-squat or full squat position to maximize intensity; land on almost straight legs.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Warm-up Circuit:** Split squats, Box front squats, Front squats with straps (for mobility), Thrusters.
*   **Main Strength:** Front Squat, Bench Back Squat.
*   **Explosive Auxiliary:** Box Jumps.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Mobility:** Front squats with straps are recommended specifically to develop upper body mobility (elbows up).
