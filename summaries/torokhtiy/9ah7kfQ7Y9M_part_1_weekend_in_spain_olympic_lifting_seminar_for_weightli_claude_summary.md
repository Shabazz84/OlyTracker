## **Extracted Coaching Content:**

### **1. Technique Cues — Snatch (Primary Focus)**
- **Snatch to squat (full snatch) > snatch to stand:** Full squat reception allows ~20-25% heavier loads
- **Overhead position:** Shoulders relaxed; lean chest forward and retract scapulae for stability
- **Bar contact/pull:** Stay on full foot (no heel lift); drive through full foot, don't rush hip extension
- **Hip sequencing:** Keep hips DOWN initially; avoid premature hip rise while bar is still on platform (common CrossFit error from high-rep fatigue)
- **Landing:** Land on full foot with impact/contact sound = successful force transfer
- **Turnover speed:** Faster pull-under after receiving adequate bar height

### **2. Programming/Intensity Principles**
- **Energy conservation:** Scale warm-up intensity appropriately (40 kg ≠ max effort)—reserve energy for heavy lifts
- **Common beginner error:** Over-exerting during warm-up, fatiguing before heavy attempts
- **Accessory work mentioned:** Snatch high pulls, snatch pulls below knee, paused snatch pulls (address lower-body drive deficiency)

### **3. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
- **Low OHS (50 kg) limiter:** Full snatch technique emphasis directly addresses this weakness; pull-under speed + full squat positioning critical
- **Back pain concern:** Risk factor — emphasize full squat reception (distributes load) over stand reception; avoid excessive lumbar reliance (as warned vs. CrossFit style)
- **Night shift impact:** Energy management advice aligns with circadian fatigue recovery needs
