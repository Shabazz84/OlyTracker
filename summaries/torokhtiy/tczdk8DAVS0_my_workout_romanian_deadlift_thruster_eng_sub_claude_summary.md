## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS:

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- General warmup: 50 min low-intensity rowing/bike (no sprints)
- Jerk-specific prep: elbow rotations, box jumps, scissor squats, press from blocks (2×10 each)
- Main work: **Thrusters** at 50–60% of best jerk, then **Power Thrusters** at 20–30 kg (for stability/synchronization)
- Accessory: Romanian deadlifts from 10 kg platform (core/posterior chain emphasis)
- Focus: **One main lift concentration** (jerk from blocks over snatch due to time constraints)

### 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Thruster/Jerk descent:** Maintain neutral spine; shift to toes as bar descends, absorb via knee bend (NOT hip forward/knees back)
- **Squat as spring metaphor:** Core/stabilizers must match "spring" size to prevent lateral deviation
- **Thruster tempo:** Slow eccentric → explosive concentric → arm finish
- **Jerk balance pauses:** Use pauses in squat to verify neutral foot posture (full foot contact, not heel/toe bias)
- **Bar lowering safety:** Don't crash onto shoulders; maintain posterior pelvic tilt, shift to toes

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Power thrusters** (20–30 kg) for leg-arm synchronization
- **Jerk balance** (light) for shoulder stability & mobility
- **Romanian deadlifts from block** (low load) for lower back/core (chronic back pain consideration)
- Avoid heavy jerk balance work

### 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Emphasis on full-body tension during thrusters ("core soreness felt next day")
- Posterior chain work (RDL) for core stability

### 5. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- ⚠️ **Chronic back pain:** RDL from block is smart; avoid aggressive forward knee-over-toe positions
- **Push jerk only (no split):** Thruster work builds leg drive & stability needed for split jerk progression later
- **OHS 50 kg limiter:** Emphasis on squat mobility + synchronization (thruster/jerk balance) directly addresses this
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun):** 50-min warmup feasible only if schedule allows; may need adjustment
