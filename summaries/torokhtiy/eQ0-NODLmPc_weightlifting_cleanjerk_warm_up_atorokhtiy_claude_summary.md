# Weightlifting clean&jerk warm up / Особенности разминки в толчке / A.TOROKHTIY

**Language Note:** This text is in Russian (weightlifting coaching content).

---

## **1. Programming Principles**
- Clean pulls/high pulls: early session work, prioritized before heavy lifting
- Clean + front squat combinations: 2-3 sets to reduce fatigue before jerks
- High volume days: 10 sets × 130 kg clean (30 total reps)
- Preparatory period focus before strength block

## **2. Technique Cues**

**Jerk (main focus):**
- Rack position: bar on shoulders/rear delts, NOT on chest; scapulae retracted, shoulders elevated
- Risk: improper high rack position can compress carotid artery → syncope risk
- Split jerk vs. push jerk: split jerk allows greater hip/ankle mobility demands; coach transitioned from split (190 kg) to split jerk, progressed to 250 kg
- Cabinet/dip squat: knees OUT (not forward), hips back, torso vertical over midline

**Clean:**
- Pull (power position): no pause; explosive acceleration of bar
- Rack: shoulders back, scapulae packed, chest up
- Front squat: knees wide, hips back, bar stays midline

**General:**
- Wrist wrapping: 4-5 wraps per finger (prevents blisters/grip loss)
- Avoid excessive wrapping (diminishing returns)

## **3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Tall jerk/push jerk drills with light weight
- Clean pulls from floor (sound check: bar "rings")
- Front squat + clean combinations
- Relaxed-grip work (avoid excessive tension)

## **4. Recovery/Mobility**
- Shoulder mobility: emphasis on posterior deltoid flexibility for optimal rack position
- Finger conditioning: note about hands burning from unstrapped grip work

## **5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**

⚠️ **HIGH PRIORITY:**
- **Split jerk progression essential** — current push jerk only; coach demonstrates split jerk is viable path (requires ankle/hip mobility work)
- **Overhead position/OHS limiter** — focus on scapular packing, shoulder mobility aligns with improving weak 50 kg OHS
- **Back pain consideration:** Cabinet squat cue (hips back, vertical torso) may help reduce lumbar stress vs. forward-knees pattern
- **Night shift adaptation:** front squat + clean sequencing (shorter sessions) fits schedule better than standalone technique work
