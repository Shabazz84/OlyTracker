# Complex for the SNATCH / Рывковый комплекс - A.TOROKHTIY

**LANGUAGE & CONTENT**
Russian coaching transcript about snatch complexes and overhead squats.

---

**1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES**
- Complex structure: snatch pulls (no extension) + snatch bails + overhead squats in one session
- Rep scheme: Start with ~4 reps per movement; reduce to 2 reps as weight increases
- Progression: Begin with light weight, progressing gradually
- Load: "heavy weight, slow and smooth—no jerky movements"
- Structure: Pair snatch bailouts + OHS as supplemental work (not primary lift)

---

**2. TECHNIQUE CUES**

**Snatch Pulls (no extension):**
- Knees out, shoulders over bar, hips back at start; maintain this angle into pull
- Pull without extension; instead, accelerate with arms and back in the pull zone
- Stand tall, extend upward with weight

**Snatch Bailouts:**
- Half-squat into explosive descent under the bar
- Legs spread wide, torso upright, bar behind head
- Scapulae retracted, shoulders packed together
- Use light, relaxed arms ("like ropes, not tight")

**Overhead Squat (OHS):**
- Knees out, back extended, hips back
- **Key cue:** Balance/positioning is self-correcting—body auto-adjusts if bar position is off
- Scapular retraction improves stability (slight external rotation of shoulders)
- Build progressively: light weight → multiple reps → increase load

---

**3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS**
- **Complex pairing:** snatch pulls + bailouts + OHS together
- Treat as supplemental/accessory work, not primary strength
- Overhead squat emphasized for balance/proprioceptive feedback

**5. RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE**

✅ **Highly relevant:**
- OHS work directly addresses "primary snatch limiter" (50 kg OHS)
- Complex design targets snatch position, stability, mobility—ideal for transition to Olympic lifting
- Snatch pulls + OHS build positional awareness without high load on back
- **Caution:** Overhead squat volume may aggravate chronic back pain; recommend starting extremely light and monitoring

⚠️ **Considerations:**
- No split jerk mentioned; snatch bailouts may improve comfort transitioning to split jerk later
- Night shift fatigue: complex work requires high neural demand—schedule when alert
