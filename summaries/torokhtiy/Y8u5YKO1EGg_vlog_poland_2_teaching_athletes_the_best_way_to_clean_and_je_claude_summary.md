### **2. Technique Cues — Clean & Jerk Focus**

**Clean:**
- Start position: closer to bar in clean than snatch
- Checkpoints: feet/knees aligned, straight arms/back, shoulders/knees on same vertical line
- Pull sequence: first pull → second pull → extension → catch

**Jerk (Split Jerk Emphasis):**
- **Front rack mobility drill:** PVC with partner, 30–40 sec holds; elbow rotations forward/back keeping bar level
- **Catch position error:** avoid "spring" mechanics (hips back without knee bend); instead, **knees drive forward first** while maintaining torso
- **Split stance:** feet shoulder-width or slightly wider — **avoid "scissors" stance** (feet on same line); provides stability

- Front rack mobility work (PVC holds, elbow rotations)
- Wall drill practice (catch positioning)
