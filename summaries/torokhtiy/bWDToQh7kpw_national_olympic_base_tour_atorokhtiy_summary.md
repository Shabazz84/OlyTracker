# National Olympic Base Tour/ A.TOROKHTIY

This text is a promotional tour of the Ukrainian National Weightlifting Training Center ("Epitsentr") and does not contain specific coaching advice, programming principles, or technique cues for an individual athlete. It focuses on facility infrastructure (floors, barbells, sauna, jacuzzi), daily schedules for national team athletes, and general lifestyle descriptions rather than technical instruction for a 102.5 kg intermediate lifter with back pain.
