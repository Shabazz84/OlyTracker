# 200kg Double Jerk / Power Jerk Tips / Torokhtiy weightlifting

# Analysis: Russian Weightlifting Coach Training Video

## **Programming Principles**
- **Jerk-focused session** to 90% intensity
- **Structure**: Up to 60% = front squat (2 reps) + jerk (3 reps); 70%+ = jerk only (2 reps each set)
- **Progression**: Singles at heavy weights after 70%
- Built-in recovery pauses at 70% intensity level

## **Technique Cues**

**Push Jerk:**
- **Dip mechanics**: Rapid, forceful leg drive ("create noise")—faster legs = faster arm speed automatically
- **Bar positioning**: Keep bar close to body, preferably touching chest in front rack; reduces energy waste and balance compensation
- **Shoulder position**: Shrug shoulders (elbows UP) to shift bar pressure from clavicles to deltoids; prevents "blackout" (loss of consciousness from jugular compression)
- **Elbow position during jerk**: Elbows DOWN during initial press phase; shoulder height irrelevant during dip/drive phase
- **Common error**: Holding bar on wrists (not chest) in rack = premature bar drift forward during split

**PVC Drill Cue:**
- Treat bar as immovable object ("roof")—athlete moves down under bar, not pressing bar away
- Tempo work on footwork: count "1,2,3" dip to create rhythm and force

## **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Clusters preferred over thrusters** for warm-up (allows control of descent; thrusters demand continuous shoulder loading = increased low back stress)
- **Wrist wraps**: Used only 70–75%+ for additional stability; not needed earlier
- **PVC snatch-grip positioning** for overhead work

## **Recovery & Mobility**
- Cool-down: light stretching mentioned (no specifics given)
- **Breathing breaks**: Coach took several minutes between heavy attempts
