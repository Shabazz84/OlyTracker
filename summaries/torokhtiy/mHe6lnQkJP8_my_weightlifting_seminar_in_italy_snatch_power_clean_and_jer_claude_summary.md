# Weightlifting Analysis: Snatch Technique Coaching

## 1. **Technique Cues - Snatch**
- **Leg extension & bar contact timing**: Leg drive and PVC/bar contact to body must be *simultaneous* — not sequential
- **Full foot contact**: Land on full foot, not shift to toes (common error when bar "hits" chest)
- **Elbow positioning**: Elbows out 10–15° from body, NOT together; external rotation opens torso and aligns spine
- **PVC drill progression**: Master synchronization with PVC before loading; eliminates compensations

## 2. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Start with **PVC drills** focusing on timing and foot position before bar work

## 3. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- **⚠️ Critical for OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Poor elbow positioning and torso alignment directly restrict overhead stability and mobility
- **Back pain consideration**: Proper elbow width (external rotation) protects lumbar spine vs. narrow/collapsed elbow position
- **Snatch progression bottleneck**: Master these positioning cues before increasing load—his 50 kg OHS suggests motor pattern issues, not pure strength

## Key Takeaway
Focus drills on **synchronized leg drive + full-foot landing + external elbow rotation**. This addresses his OHS weakness and back safety simultaneously.
