# WEEK #8 / TOROKHTIY_GANG training program

# Analysis: Russian Weightlifting Coach (Week 8 Taper/Competition Prep)

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **8-week program structure**: 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional supplemental sessions (Tue/Thu)
- **Week 8 (current)**: 2 prep sessions + 1 competition/test day
- **Intensity approach**: Keep loads at 80–85% of competition lifts; can reduce further if needed
- **Key principle**: Avoid maxing out during taper; organism needs to "accumulate energy" for competition
- **Emphasis**: Quality over quantity; better to undershoot than overshoot ("лучше недопахать чем перепахать")

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Snatch/Clean**: Slow, smooth movements without jerky transitions; feel the bar pressure
- **Front Squat**: Descend slowly, ascend explosively
- **Jerk (push jerk implied)**: Slow "podsvyet" (dip), explosive drive; finish with 1–2 sec overhead lockout with tension
- **Breathing**: Inhale before movement, exhale on extension (flexible per individual)
- **General**: No straps except pulls (competition prep—hands/wrists must adapt to bare grip)

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Snatch (competition model) — low intensity, focus on movement quality
- Push Jerk — with strict lockout hold
- Front Squat (80–85% of jerk max) — to "awaken the legs"
- Control warm-ups with extended ROM

## 4. **Recovery & Mental Prep**
- **Sleep, nutrition, rest** emphasized heavily
- **Mental visualization**: Write competition plan on A4 paper (lift sequence, warm-up loads, attempts) and visualize 5–10 min nightly
- **Pre-competition routine**: Plan entire warm-up, lift order, on-platform behavior (hand wash, breathing, focus)

## 5. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- ⚠️ **Chronic back pain**: Front squat at 80–85% (lower than typical) and slow eccentrics align with pain management
- ⚠️ **Weak overhead position (50 kg OHS)**: No specific mobility drills mentioned; would benefit from dedicated shoulder/thoracic work
- ⚠️ **Push jerk only (no split)**: Coach doesn't address split mechanics; athlete should explore split jerk for power transfer
- ⚠️ **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Tuesday/Thursday supplemental sessions may conflict with sleep schedule—prioritize recovery
- ✓ **No-strap work**: Wrist/hand conditioning valuable before intensity phase

**Overall**: Taper week emphasizing technique, CNS recovery, and mental preparation for competition.
