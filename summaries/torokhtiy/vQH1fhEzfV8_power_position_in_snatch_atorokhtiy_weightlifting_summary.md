# POWER POSITION in SNATCH / A.TOROKHTIY (weightlifting)

**1. Programming principles**
*   **Frequency:** High focus on "feeling" the movement; suggests starting with low frequency of high-intensity pull drills until the sensation is mastered (implied 2-3x/week for this specific drill).
*   **Intensity:** Strictly low load for technique acquisition (max 80 kg suggested by author, even for elite athletes); emphasizes "small weight" to feel the interaction.
*   **Sets/Reps:** Not explicitly defined in numbers, but implies multiple sets of short, high-quality repetitions focused on specific phases (pull vs. pull-to-heel).
*   **Periodization:** This is a technical skill acquisition block; not a strength/hypertrophy cycle itself.

**2. Technique cues for Snatch (The "Pull" / Подрыв)**
*   **Starting Position:** Knees wide, hips back ("into the pocket"), load on full foot/thighs, shoulders covering the bar/knees.
*   **The Pull:** Push up with legs first, then immediately transition to pulling elbows up and high (not letting them fall back).
*   **Timing:** The arm pull must connect simultaneously with the leg extension; do not wait for the bar to rise before pulling.
*   **The "Hit" (Impact):** The bar should hit the thighs/hips at two specific zones: mid-thigh or just above the groin (depending on limb length). Avoid hitting the pubic bone directly if it causes pain (requires covering with shoulders).
*   **Elbow Path:** Elbows must point upward, not backward. Shoulders "cover" the bar after impact.
*   **Heel Rise:** Add a heel rise only *after* mastering the pull without heels; this increases bar height to chin level.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Barbell Pull Drill (No Heels):** Hold bar in snatch grip, drop hands off, sit into squat position, then stand up and pull elbows high. Focus on leg drive + elbow lift connection.
*   **High Pull with Impact:** Drop bar by inertia, hit thighs/hips ("hit" the bar), immediately pull elbows up. Add heel rise once stable.
*   **Snatch High Pull from Blocks (Plyo):** Use a block/knee-high platform to start. Use lifting straps to remove grip fatigue. Focus on full foot drive and elbow position.

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Bruising:** Expect bruising ("blue spots") and hair loss on thighs; this is normal for the "hit" phase, indicating correct impact location.
*   **Pain Management:** If groin pain occurs during the hit, it indicates the athlete is "open" (hips not covered); adjust to cover with shoulders to shift load to thigh/pubic bone safely.
