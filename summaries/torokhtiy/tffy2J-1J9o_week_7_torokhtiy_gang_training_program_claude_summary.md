# WEEK #7 / TOROKHTIY_GANG training program

# Weightlifting Coaching Transcript Analysis

**PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES**
- 8-week "Tarakhitgan" project: 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional (Tue/Thu)
- Week 7 structure: alternating snatch and jerk emphasis (jerk last week, snatch this week)
- Decreasing volume/intensity toward competition (10–14 days out)
- Recovery principle: jerk recovers longer than snatch, so alternate heavy focus weekly

**TECHNIQUE CUES**
- **Jerk lockout fixation:** Hold ~1–2 seconds overhead to stabilize; athletes often skip this
- **Snatch from hang:** Use pauses above and below knee in warmup to feel full foot contact
- **Jerk dip position:** Hips back (not knees forward), knees out to sides
- **Front rack:** Use straps in warmup to reinforce proper bar position, open elbows/shoulders

**EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS**
- Hyperextension: 2–3 sets, 20–40 kg (+ side rotations)
- Snatch: heavy single/double work Monday
- Jerk: lighter follow-up after snatch (% work)
- Front squat: decreasing volume/intensity as competition nears
- Snatch from hang, clean, jerk dip, back squat—all included

**RECOVERY/MOBILITY**
- Emphasize quality warmup and cooldown (stretching/mobility) as intensity increases
- Avoid overloading legs; use straps to reduce fatigue before competition
