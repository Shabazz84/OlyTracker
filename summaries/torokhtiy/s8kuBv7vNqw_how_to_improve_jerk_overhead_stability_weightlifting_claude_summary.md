# How to Improve JERK Overhead Stability / weightlifting

**This is a weightlifting coaching video transcript (in Russian) by Alexey Torokhtiy on jerk preparation.**

---

## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS:

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- Warm-up progression: low-intensity cardio (rowing) → mobility drills → submaximal work
- Jerk-specific work: 70% → 80% → 90% intensity
- Sets/reps structure: 3×6 for assistance; 2×4-5 at 70%; 2×2 at 80%; 2×1 at 90%
- Hold-in-rack position: 3–4 seconds after each rep to build stability

### 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Front squat control**: descend *slowly* (controlled), explode *fast* upward from bottom
- **Jerk rack position**: bar at chest/solar plexus level, quarter-squat dip, hold 3–4 seconds overhead
- **Breathing**: avoid holding breath in rack position; breathe to reduce head pressure
- **Bar placement**: on chest (not shoulders) during front squat holds

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Good-morning + squat combo** (20–40 kg): 3×6 for lower back/glutes/posterior chain
- **Band tricep mobility work**: 2×20–30 sec/arm pre-jerk
- **Jerk with band resistance** (60 kg base + 5–10 kg band tension): teaches stability overhead
- **Front squat holds**: 3–4 sec at top position

### 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- 15 minutes dedicated cool-down/stretching post-session
- Band mobilization for tight shoulders/triceps before jerks

### 5. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- ⚠️ **Back pain concern**: Good-morning + squat combo for core/lower back strength is *beneficial but load conservatively* (20–30 kg max initially)
- ⚠️ **Split jerk progression**: Torohktiy emphasizes overhead *hold stability*—critical before advancing to split jerk
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: These jerk holds & mobility drills directly address shoulder/thoracic mobility gaps
- **Night shift fatigue**: Emphasizes quality over volume—fits rest-day structure
