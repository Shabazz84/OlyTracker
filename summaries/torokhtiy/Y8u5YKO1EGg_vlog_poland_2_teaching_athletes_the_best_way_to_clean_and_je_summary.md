2.  **Technique Cues:**
    *   **Jerk (Push Jerk focus):** "Show me your shoulders, open your chest." Palms facing forward, elbow under the other elbow. Grip PVC bar from both sides; coach holds elbows to maintain position.
    *   **Setup/Posture:** Hold a squat position for 30–40 seconds while rotating elbows forward/backward keeping the bar level (no dropping).
    *   **Clean/Jerk Stance:** Stand closer to the bar in the jerk than in the snatch. Feet/knees aligned; arms/spine straight; shoulders over knees.
    *   **The "Spring" Concept (Crucial):** The dip must be a "spring" (*spruzhina*). Do not initiate by pushing hips back without bending knees. Knees must travel forward ("look like spring") to accumulate force before the drive.
    *   **Jerk Stance Width:** Avoid a narrow, single-line stance (like circus performers). Feet should be shoulder-width or slightly wider for stability in the split jerk.

    *   Wall drill simulation for dip mechanics.
