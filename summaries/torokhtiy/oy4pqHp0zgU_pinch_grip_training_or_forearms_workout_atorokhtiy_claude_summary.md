# Pinch Grip Training or Forearms Workout. Щипковый хват или качаем предплечья - A.TOROKHTIY

**Not directly applicable to Olympic weightlifting coaching.**

This text discusses forearm/pinch grip training (farmer's carry/pinch grip work) in isolation, not Olympic lifting programming or technique.

**Relevance to athlete:** Minimal. While grip strength has peripheral value, it's not a priority given:
- Primary limiters are OHS (50 kg) and jerk technique (push jerk only)
- Chronic back pain requires careful exercise selection
- Night shift work (Wed–Sun) demands efficient programming

**If considering grip work:** Would be supplementary only, *after* addressing OHS weakness and split jerk development.
