# BEND with a barbell or "GOOD MORNING" /  A.TOROKHTIY

# Analysis: Russian Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## Relevant to This Athlete

**1. Programming (Good Fit)**
- 8-10 sets total per exercise
- 6-8 reps at light weight, progressing to 3-4 reps at heavier weight
- Light weight: 20-40 kg starting point

**2. Exercise: Good Morning (Наклоны)**
- **Primary purpose:** Back strength for pull mechanics (addresses "back drifts back in pull" issue)
- **Secondary benefit:** Spinal mobility and lumbar lordosis management
- **Technique cues:**
  - Legs shoulder-width (or wider), knees out
  - Slow descent, explosive ascent (spring-like)
  - Hips back, chest forward, neutral spine
  - At light weight: add pause/sway at bottom for mobility
  - At heavy weight: continuous reps, no pause (prevent fatigue/form breakdown)
  - Avoid: simultaneous hip + shoulder extension; keep spine neutral

**3. Recovery/Mobility**
- Addresses chronic lumbar lordosis/back pain specifically
- Helps prevent disc compression through muscular stabilization
- Start conservatively (20-40 kg)—prioritize form over load

**4. Relevance to Profile**
- ✅ **Directly addresses OHS limiter** (50 kg): Back strength needed for overhead position
- ✅ **Chronic back pain management:** Described as effective for lordosis issues
- ✅ **Can fit 6-week hypertrophy block** as supplemental work
- ⚠️ Place after warm-up but before heavy oly lifting (pulls) to potentiate

**Overall:** This exercise fits well into early training sessions (shift work: do Mon/Tue if possible).
