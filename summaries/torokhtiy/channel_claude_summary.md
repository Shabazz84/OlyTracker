# TOROKHTIY COACHING PHILOSOPHY: Synthesis from 232 Sources

## **1. CORE TRAINING PHILOSOPHY**

**Five Principles:**

1. **Technique Quality Gates All Load:** Sub-maximal work (80–90% intensity) enforces movement integrity. Progress load *only* after positions are clean; "do it smaller but correctly" precedes heavier loading.

2. **Scapular-Driven Movement Patterns:** Retract and tension scapulae throughout pull phases; drive extension from upper back (thoracic) rather than lumbar spine. Prevents compensation injuries, especially critical for chronic pain management.

3. **Positional Mastery Before Complexity:** Teach positions bottom-up (receiving position first); master unloaded/light drills before adding load. No split jerk without push jerk excellence; no heavy snatch before OHS stability.

4. **Full-Body Stability Over Isolation:** Complexes (snatch pull + squat + OHS; clean + front squat + jerk) build integrated strength. Address upper back, core, and hip mobility as foundational—not accessory—work.

5. **Sustainable CNS Management:** High-frequency sub-maximal training (3 main sessions/week) compatible with recovery. Avoid daily max attempts; emphasize movement quality and sleep/nutrition to prevent burnout.

## **2. PROGRAMMING APPROACH**

**Structure:**
- **Periodization:** 8-week blocks typical (6 weeks hypertrophy/base → 2 weeks taper/peak)
- **Weekly template:** 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 2 optional supplemental sessions (Tue/Thu)
- **Session design:** Warm-up → main lift (technique focus) → accessory complex → mobility/cooldown

**Intensity Zones:**
- 40% (movement drills with mirror feedback)
- 70–75% (work sets, technique emphasis)
- 80–90% (competition simulation, sub-maximal testing)
- 95–100% (competition only; rare in training blocks)

**Load Progression:**
- 2–3 sets per exercise; 2–4 reps per set (complexes favor lower reps)
- Straps used early (confidence building), removed mid-block (grip/forearm adaptation)
- Benchmark: C&J should be 80–85% of front squat; snatch 78–83% of C&J (diagnostic of strength vs. skill gaps)

## **3. KEY TECHNIQUE CUES & EXERCISE PREFERENCES**

### **Clean (Squat Clean Emphasis)**
- Start: Knees wide, hips back, shoulders over bar (shoulders cover knees, not knees past)
- Pull: Vertical drive, knees out, hips back; extend fully before vertical rise
- Receiving: Elbows HIGH and forward (not tight to ribs); pull elbows up, bar stays close; squat catch with hips back, knees out
- Grip: Full hand grip (not fingertips); prevents slipping at catch

### **Snatch**
- Start: Feet shoulder-width, 10–15° toe-out; chest up, scapulae retracted
- Pull: Scrape the legs; maintain bar proximity throughout; full foot contact (no early toe rise)
- Bar contact: Mid-thigh/over hip (individual torso length dependent)
- Receiving: Wide squat stance, full depth; scapulae together, shoulders shrugged toward ears, elbows forward
- Key cue: "Snatch without jump" drill (forces complete leg extension before vertical jump)

### **Push Jerk (Primary for This Athlete)**
- Dip: Shallow (half-squat preferred over full squat for pain management); knees out, toes out, torso upright
- Drive: Fast, explosive; hip + knee extension simultaneous
- Lockout: 2–3 second pause; scapulae retracted, shoulders to ears, elbows forward
- Load transfer: Legs drive bar, not upper body; keep torso angle constant

### **Front Squat/Rack Positions**
- Upright torso, elbows high, shoulders elevated
- Knees out and splayed (distributes load away from lower back)
- Full foot contact; slow eccentric, fast concentric
- Straps + strap front squats lock in position without compensations

### **Overhead Squat (OHS)**
- Knees out, back extended (but not hyperextended), hips back
- Balance self-corrects when bar position is optimal
- Scapular retraction and external shoulder rotation improve stability
- Light load, multiple reps for proprioceptive feedback

### **Accessory Preferences**
- **Hyperextensions:** 2–3 sec hold at top; moderate load (20 kg max); slow eccentric; 10+ reps for core/posterior chain
- **Good mornings (Naklony):** Knees slightly bent, hips back; slow descent, explosive extension
- **Band shoulder circuit:** Lateral raises, reverse flyes, rotations (10–20 reps); overhead holds (minimal load, maximal time under tension)
- **Snatch/Clean pulls:** Often slow eccentrics; straps acceptable for recovery sessions
- **Snatch balance/tall snatch:** Refine receiving position, footwork, turnover speed

## **4. RECOVERY & MOBILITY APPROACH**

**Mobility Strategy:**
- **Pre-workout:** Band shoulder circuit, PVC drill work, elbow circles
- **Front rack focus:** Elbow forward positioning; shoulder external rotation; straps + strap front squats lock position
- **Hip/hamstring:** Ballet-inspired stretches (glute stretch 20–30 sec × 2; hip flexor stretch 20–30 sec × 2); post-session stretching 30+ min
- **Thoracic extension:** Scapular retraction drills, chest
