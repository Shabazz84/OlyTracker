# How to lift / Episode #4: Front SQUAT + JERK

# Weightlifting Coaching Transcript Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Session structure**: 3 main exercises (front squat + split jerk/push jerk choice, then front squat again, then plyometrics)
- **Intensity/Volume**: Front squat work at 60–80%, 2×2 reps (activation/warm-up)
- **Accessory**: Box jumps (9 reps), horse vault jumps (6 reps)
- **Post-workout finisher**: ~100 total reps split jerk footwork (2–3×5–6 reps)
- **Progression**: Incremental loading (suggested +5 kg above athlete's best on jerk attempt)

## 2. Technique Cues – Split Jerk
- **Foot placement**: Mark positions before and at toes; step forward with lead foot; both legs parallel
- **Hip position**: Drive hips back (posterior weight) before catch to maintain balance overhead
- **Shoulder shrug**: "Reach shoulders to ears" — emphasize upward pull before lockout
- **Landing**: Feet placement check before exiting split; full foot contact required
- **Footwork practice**: Post-workout drills—rise onto toes, spread legs explosively into split position

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Front squat with straps (several reps) to mobilize elbows/shoulders for jerk
- Wall squat, PVC dip footwork
- Box jump variations (scaled by ability: low-box vs. deep squat jump to horse)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Bar bending with forward lean (warm-up)
- Hack squats with light weight (10 kg disc)
- Strap work on front squats for shoulder/elbow prep

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
⚠️ **Critical for this athlete:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Split jerk footwork/shoulder mobility work directly addresses overhead stability
- **Chronic back pain**: Heavy emphasis on posterior chain positioning (hips back) and full-foot stability is appropriate
- **Push jerk only status**: Coach offers flexibility (can swap split jerk for push jerk) — good accommodation
- **Night shift schedule**: Single session outlined; manageable intensity for fatigue
- **Hypertrophy block goal**: Front squat volume (2 sessions + plyometrics) supports leg mass gain
