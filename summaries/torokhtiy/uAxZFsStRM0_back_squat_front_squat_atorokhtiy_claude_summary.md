# Back SQUAT & Front SQUAT / A.TOROKHTIY

**Language/Context:** Russian-language coaching content on back squat and front squat technique. Relevant to this athlete.

---

## **1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES**
- **Frequency:** Alternate back squat and front squat rather than front squat only (front squat is more demanding)
- **Volume/Intensity for mobility work:** 2–4 sets of 20 reps with straps on light weight, 2–3x per week = 2–3 weeks to improve mobility
- **Tempo:** Slow eccentric descent, fast concentric drive

---

## **2. TECHNIQUE CUES**

**Back Squat (Snatch Grip Position):**
- Bar rests on shoulders, NOT neck
- Arms lightly support; don't fully grip to avoid upper back/trap tension
- Lower back in constant tension; drive hips back
- Knees out; shoulders should track over knees (aspirational cue)
- Slow down, fast up

**Front Squat:**
- High elbows (tuck as high as possible); bar sits on shoulders like a rack
- Full grip + breath into chest for stability
- Hips back, knees out
- **Key cue:** "Belly between legs" — maintain torso position so abdomen drops between knees, NOT onto thighs (protects lumbar spine)
- Slow descent, fast ascent

---

## **3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS**
- **Front squat with straps** for mobility limitation (small weight, 2–4 sets/session)
  - Straps force full grip, prevent fingers-only grip due to mobility
  - Shoulder/under-collarbone discomfort normal; resolves in 2–3 weeks

---

## **4. RECOVERY/MOBILITY ADVICE**
- Include front squat with straps in warm-up if mobility limited
- Small weight + 3–4 sets before each session × 2–3 weeks improves mobility
- Minor discomfort expected; typical progression

---

## **5. ATHLETE-SPECIFIC RELEVANCE**
- **Chronic back pain:** Front squat "belly between legs" cue critical to avoid lumbar flexion under load
- **Low OHS (50 kg):** Shoulder/thoracic mobility work via front squat with straps directly addresses limiting factor
- **Back Squat (118 kg) vs Clean (80 kg) gap:** Front squat development supports clean rack position
