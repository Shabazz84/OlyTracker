# Weightlifting Programming Summary

## Key Points

- **8-week preparatory block** (currently Week 2) with 3 main sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri) + optional supplemental work (Tue/Thu)
- **Intensity progression:** Snatch pulls 100–105%, clean pulls 80% with explosive intent; front squat + jerk combinations using 2+1 rep protocols
- **Priority:** Base building and positioning work before technical/strength intensification in weeks 3–8
- **Back development:** Progressive hyperextension loading (2–3 sets/session, 2–3 month progression) to manage chronic pain and build extension tolerance
- **Periodized approach:** Preparatory phase emphasizes movement quality and anatomical adaptation over maximal loads

## Technique Cues

**Snatch pull from blocks:**
- Knees wide; belly passes through legs maximally
- Maintain back angle through first pull; rise onto toes
- Keep elbows high; avoid hand-pulling backward

**Clean pulls (with straps):**
- Elbows high, forearms relaxed; straps manage load (reduce hand grip intensity)
- Standard: 80% to waist; high pull variant: 78% to chest/chin with explosive intent

**Clean from blocks:**
- Maintain back angle and position; widen knees aggressively on dip

**Push jerk footwork:**
- Staggered stance (lateral + forward/back displacement); all toes in contact with platform; front foot turned inward

**Front squat:**
- Bar on shoulders (not chest); chest open, arms wide
- Knees out, hips back; straps recommended for elbow mobility development

**Hyperextension:**
- Avoid full lumbar extension; 2-second pause at top
- Progressive loading over 2–3 months for back tolerance building

## Programming Principles

- **Exercise structure:** 4-exercise circuits per session (pull variant + squat + jerk/complex + supplemental)
- **Rep patterns:** Front squat + jerk (2+1) progressing weekly; back squat 4×4 at 80–90% with eccentric emphasis
- **Supplemental work:** Light weight, movement quality focus on Tue/Thu
- **Post-squat protocol:** Jump drills on low platforms for rate of force development
- **Straps utilization:** Reduces grip demand, emphasizes pulling mechanics and elbow position development

## Athlete Relevance

**Critical for your profile (intermediate athlete, 102.5 kg BW, chronic back pain, night shift Wed–Sun, weak OHS at 50 kg):**

| Limitation | Targeted Solution |
|---|---|
| **Chronic back pain** | Hyperextension 2–3×/session; progressive loading "drop by drop" over 2–3 months builds tolerance without acute flare-up |
| **OHS limiter (50 kg)** | Front squat + jerk complexes + snatch pulls from blocks develop rack position mobility and core stability directly |
| **Push jerk only** | Jerk split footwork detailed; staggered stance and toe positioning teach lateral/forward displacement mechanics |
| **Clean/snatch positioning gap** | Block pulls and high-pull variants target extension angle deficits; critical for closing 80 kg clean → weak snatch disparity |
| **Night shift schedule** | Mon/Wed/Fri alignment fits Wed–Sun work; optional Tue/Thu supplements available if recovery permits |

**This preparatory block is ideal foundation work before your planned 6-week hypertrophy phase.** Back strengthening addresses chronic pain; positioning emphasis (blocks, complexes, pulls) directly resolves your OHS bottleneck.
