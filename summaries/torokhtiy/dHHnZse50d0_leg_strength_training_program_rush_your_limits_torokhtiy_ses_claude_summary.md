# Analysis: Alexis Tarati Leg Strength Session

## Relevant to Athlete Profile

**Programming:**
- 12-session program, 4-week base (extendable to 4 months)
- Single session structure: warm-up → main compound → accessory
- Back squat focus aligns with athlete's weak OHS/snatch overhead position

**Technique Cues (Relevant):**
- Back squat: feet shoulder-width, tight lower back, chest up, hips back/knees out, full squat, accelerate recovery
- Good morning + squat warm-up: "rounding off the back is NOT acceptable" — critical for chronic back pain management
- Muscle snatch (light): arch back, balance midfoot, one-motion bar path, elbows up, full foot contact

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- **Good morning + squat combo** — directly addresses back pain risk; prepare posterior chain
- **Back squat 6x6** — hypertrophy stimulus matches 6-week block goal
- **Snatch thrusters** — eccentric load on legs; builds OHS strength (primary snatch limiter)
- **Wrist curls** — grip accessory (useful for hook grip development)
- **Dark walk** in squat — mobility for deep positioning

**Recovery Note:**
- Suggests adding sets if sore from previous session (appropriate for night shift fatigue)

## Caution
Snatch thrusters at current OHS level (50 kg) may be technical overhead work overload. Prioritize OHS position first.
