# Weightlifting Analysis: Overhead Position & Setup Mechanics

## 1. **Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean/Jerk)**

**Overhead Position:**
- Bar behind head held by **joints/bones, not muscular stabilization** (more sustainable)
- Chest open, arms locked, "hook grip"
- Shoulders slightly behind knees
- Feel pressure on **sides of torso** (bar contact points), not front
- Avoid forward hip shift (loads fingertips; reduces stability)

**Setup/Pull Position:**
- Shoulders slightly *ahead* of knees
- Straight arms, straight lower back with slight arch
- **Feet/knees aligned in same direction** (prevents external foot loading)
- Knee-elbow nearly aligned (critical self-check for 165–183 cm athletes)
- Bar path: smooth lifting from floor, accelerating through groin zone
- Avoid jerky contact with bar (causes vibration = inefficiency)

**Balance Drill:**
- Small rock forward/back with bar to verify balanced weight distribution
- Transition from toes → full foot contact before pulling

## 2. **Relevant to This Athlete**

- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Overhead stability work via PVC → barbell progression aligns with this weakness
- **Back pain:** Emphasis on **neutral spine, chest-up positioning, proper spinal extension** reduces load on lumbar spine
- **Push jerk only:** Overhead mechanics cues directly applicable; split jerk requires additional lower-body positioning
