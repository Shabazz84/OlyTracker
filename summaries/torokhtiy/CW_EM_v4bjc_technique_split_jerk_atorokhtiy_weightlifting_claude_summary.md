## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- High-volume practice emphasized: "2,000 repetitions needed for motor pattern automaticity"
- Drill-based progression (footwork drills → adding arm drive → full lift)
- Split jerk (scissor position) taught—aligns with athlete's stated "push jerk only" limitation

### 2. **Technique Cues – Split Jerk**

**Rack Position:**
- Elbows high, shoulders elevated; load transferred to shoulders, not chest/neck
- Wrists straight (no hyperextension—injury risk)
- Hand relaxation test: wiggle fingers to confirm lack of tension

**Dip & Drive:**
- Full foot contact during squat; hips back
- Simultaneous arm extension + leg drive
- Legs must synchronize: rise on toes → drive/split

**Footwork (Scissor Stance):**
- Legs straddle along separate lines (base of support)
- Both feet turned inward slightly (stability)
- Weight distributed evenly across both legs
- Knees not collapsed inward
- **Recovery:** lead with front foot, then trail leg; head drives *up*, not back

**Press-Out/Lockout:**
- Scapulae retracted, shoulders shrugged
- Simultaneous arm + leg extension
- Drill option: jerk from behind neck (reduces distraction, emphasizes pressing strength)

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Footwork drill:** Marked lines for split positioning; leg drive from heel lift (2–3 reps)
- **Jerk from rack** (progression building)
- **Jerk from behind neck** (accessory to reinforce pressing lockout)
