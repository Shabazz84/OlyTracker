# Front SQUAT + JERK 180kg / Torokhtiy

**Weightlifting Coaching Content: Yes** (Alexey Torokhtiy jerk training session)

---

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES
- **Jerk focus**: Primary lift of the session (jerk from rack, goal 260 kg)
- **5-6 month prep cycle** toward max attempt
- **General prep approach**: Emphasizes leg strength + positional stability over peak intensity currently
- **Structure**: Warm-up → accessory circuit → main lift
- **Load on jerk**: Starting ~70-80% of recent best (160-180 kg range)

---

## 2. TECHNIQUE CUES (RELEVANT)

**Jerk from Rack:**
- Count to 3 mentally before stepping back to prevent bar bounce/instability
- Focus on solid bar hold during rack position
- Emphasis: bar stability > weight progression

**Front Rack Mobility/Position:**
- Elbow rotations (locaties back/forward) in front rack position
- Front squat + close-grip positioning essential for clean receipt

---

## 3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS

- **Hack squats** (knee warm-up, partial ROM)
- **Hyperextensions** (back health—relevant to athlete's chronic pain)
- **Front rack dips** (elbow mobility emphasis)
- **Front squat holds** (3-5 min easy cardio warm-up)
- **Power clean to front squat** (~50% intensity; elbow rotation activation)
- **Push press/standing press** (10 reps)
- **Jerk from rack** (main event)

---

## 4. RECOVERY/MOBILITY ADVICE
- Slow, controlled warm-up circuit with continuous joint prep
- Hyperextension work (back care)
- Front rack mobility prioritized

---
