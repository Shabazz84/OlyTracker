# Heavy C&J with Anton Pliesnoi // Learn CLEAN TECHNIQUE from European & World championships medalists

## Key Points
*   **Back Pain Mitigation:** Strictly avoid excessive lumbar arching during pulls and catches. Maintain a "covered" torso over the bar to minimize shear force on the spine, which is critical for an athlete with chronic back pain.
*   **Jerk Mechanics (Push Jerk Only):** Since split jerks are excluded, stability relies entirely on vertical force transfer. Feet must remain neutral or turn in minimally (5–10%); over-rotation compromises balance and power.
*   **Leg Strength Utilization:** Leverage the athlete's strong squat (118 kg) by utilizing a "low pull" position where power is generated via knee extension rather than high-bar pulling with the arms/back. This minimizes upper body strain while maximizing existing leg strength.
*   **Fatigue Management:** For night shift workers, prioritize "slow and controlled" execution over speed to prevent form breakdown caused by irregular sleep schedules.

## Technique Cues
*   **Pulls (Snatch/Clean):** Keep the torso covered over the bar throughout the entire pull; do not stand up fully before dropping under. The movement must be short and vertical, avoiding a "high swing."
*   **Clean Catch:** Do not "sit like a stool" (heels only). Catch the bar high on the chest/shoulders to utilize the stretch reflex ("give"). Catching too low prevents power generation from a dead stop. Drop shoulders down during the catch to prevent neck compression and maintain blood flow; never shrug or lift shoulders while catching.
*   **Jerk Drive:** Push through all three points of the foot (heel, ball under big toe, ball under little toe) with feet straight or minimally turned.
*   **Mobility Drill:** For elbow mobility, hold the bar in the middle closer to the wrist crease and pull elbows up against pressure from the bar to stretch the joint.

## Programming Principles
*   **Execution Priority:** Begin all sessions light to master mechanics. Increase weight only once full knee extension and proper hip position are achieved without compensation.
*   **Drill Focus:** Utilize high-repetition technical drills at low intensity to build proprioception ("feel the center of the foot").
*   **Specific Drills:** Incorporate "Pause Snatch/Clean Pulls" at the high point (just before dropping under) to ensure full extension and proper hip positioning without rushing. Practice strict cleans from the chest to emphasize the dip-to-drive transition.

## Athlete Relevance
*   **Profile Match:** The advice directly addresses the 102.5 kg intermediate lifter by prioritizing spinal safety (neutral spine, covered torso) over high-intensity pulling that could exacerbate chronic back pain.
*   **Limitation Addressing:** The specific cues for foot stability and vertical force transfer are tailored to the athlete's constraint of using only a push jerk, ensuring maximum power output without relying on split mechanics which may be unstable for this profile.
*   **Lifestyle Adaptation:** The emphasis on calm, controlled execution serves as a countermeasure to night shift fatigue (Wed–Sun), reducing the risk of technical failure due to sleep deprivation.
