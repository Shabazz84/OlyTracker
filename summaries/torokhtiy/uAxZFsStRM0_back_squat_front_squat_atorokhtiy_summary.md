**General Squat Cues Extracted (Applicable to all lifters):**
*   **Back Squat:** Bar on shoulders (not neck), hands supporting without gripping tightly, lumbar tension maintained, hips back, knees out, aim for shoulder-over-knee position, slow descent, explosive ascent.
