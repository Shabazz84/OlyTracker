## 4. **Recovery/Mobility & Pain Management**

- **Intervention**: If pain worsens, **switch to clean pulls only; remove jerk work**
- Coaching philosophy: Master body control before heavy loading
- Mindset coaching: Dissuades "always max out" mentality; prioritizes technique maturity over ego lifting
