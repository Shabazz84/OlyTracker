# Analysis

**Technique Cue - Jerk Dip:**
- Knee position in dip varies by anthropometry
- Long femurs → knees slightly out (wider base, chest stays high)
- Short femurs → knees forward (cleaner bar path, easier drive timing)
- Individual proportions determine optimal style

**Relevant to Athlete:**
- **Critical for push jerk progression** (athlete's only jerk style). Need to assess femur length and establish personalized dip mechanics before load increases in strength phase.
- May address forward tipping tendency under load if present.

**Not covered:** Sets/reps, intensity, frequency, periodization, snatch/clean technique, back pain management, recovery, or night shift considerations.
