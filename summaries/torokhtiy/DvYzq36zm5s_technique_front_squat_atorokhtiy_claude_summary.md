# Weightlifting Analysis: Front Squat Coaching

## Programming Principles
- **8-week training cycle** (Mon/Wed/Fri main sessions + Tue/Thu optional accessory)
- **Front squat load**: lighter than back squat (no specific percentages given)
- **Warm-up**: max 10% of best lift for that exercise
- **Exercise pairing**: front squats combined with power cleans from blocks (fatigue management)
- **Video feedback system**: athletes submit technique videos with hashtag for coach review

## Technique Cues (Front Squat)

**Setup & Rack Height:**
- Adjust racks for comfortable bar takeoff; avoid catching on safety pins

**Grip & Posture:**
- Full grip always (prevents arm opening → elbow flare injury risk)
- Bar rests on **shoulders, not chest**
- Chest up, elbows out to sides
- Shoulders elevated/engaged

**Movement Pattern:**
- Hips back from start (not front-loaded, avoiding typical CrossFit error of hip shift)
- Maintain torso angle (no forward head nod, no back rounding)
- Knee tracking: shoulder should cover knee (knees don't travel excessively forward)
- Breathing: inhale, then squat

**Footwear:**
- Weightlifting shoes with heel preferred; alternatively use heel pad under shoes

## Exercise Recommendations
- **Pre-lift mobility**: yoga-style plate holds for thoracic/shoulder prep
- **Band shoulder work**: slow band pull-aparts for upper shoulder girdle prep
- **Warm-up in straps**: helps reinforce full grip pattern and shoulder position

## Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Front squats **heavily load cardiovascular system** (more spinal/core demand than back squat)
- **Caution for weak heart/cardiac predisposition**: limit loading intensity
- Front squats stress core, spinal stabilizers, and anterior chain more than back squats

## Relevance to This Athlete

**Critical for profile:**
- **Chronic back pain**: Front squat's excessive spinal loading may be problematic; prioritize form over load
- **Low OHS (50kg)**: Front squat technique (thoracic mobility, shoulder position) directly transfers to snatch
- **Night shift worker**: 8-week structure provides consistent framework despite irregular schedule
- **Hypertrophy block goal**: Front squats excellent accessory but keep weight moderate given back limitations

**⚠️ Flag**: Coach emphasizes proper technique over loading—align with back pain management.
