# How to Practice Split Jerk / weightlifting by Torokhtiy

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**1. Technique Cues – Jerk**
- Split jerk progression: Draw line in front of toes → kneel to draw line in front of knees → step forward (both sides parallel) → land consistently at same spot
- Power jerk: Use start line only (simpler foot positioning)
- Pre-jerk breathing: Stop breathing after clean to stabilize core and feel bar better; prevents body vibration from transferring to bar

**2. Exercise Recommendations**
- PVC jerk practice: Deep position + pause + forward/backward sway to find center of balance before standing and splitting

**3. Relevant to Athlete Profile**
- **Critical note:** Athlete only does push jerk (no split jerk). This transcript teaches split jerk progression. Consider whether introducing split technique aligns with goals, OR continue push jerk focus given chronic back pain (split requires more spinal stability/mobility).
- Breathing cue may help stability for push jerk too

**4. NOT covered:** Sets/reps/intensity, periodization, snatch technique (athlete's primary limiter at 50kg OHS), mobility for back pain, night shift considerations.
