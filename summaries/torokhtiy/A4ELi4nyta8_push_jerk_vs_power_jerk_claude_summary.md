# Push Jerk VS Power Jerk

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**1. Jerk Technique Cues:**
- **Push jerk:** Deep drive, catch with feet in same place. Good for light-to-moderate weight and rep cycling.
- **Power jerk:** Dip, drive, feet jump to wider stance for catch. Requires more leg strength and balance; better carryover to split jerk.

**2. Critical Note for This Athlete:**
The coach emphasizes the power jerk has "better carryover to heavy jerks and split jerk." Since this athlete only does push jerk (65 kg), developing power jerk technique during the 6-week hypertrophy block could build the leg strength and positional comfort needed for eventual split jerk progression—a significant capacity gap.

**3. Application:**
Given chronic back pain + current push jerk limitation, prioritize power jerk work (wider catching stance, less spinal shear than split jerk) as a bridge before attempting split jerk. This aligns with technical development post-hypertrophy block.
