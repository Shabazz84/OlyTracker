# Relax your arms in the clean and jerk #pavlukhin #kharkiv #weightlifting ￼

**Language:** Russian coaching feedback

**Key Extractions:**

1. **Technique Cue (Jerk/Push Press):**
   - Arms are tensed/forced rather than relaxed during rack position
   - Athlete forcing wrists into dorsiflexion artificially → creates tension throughout upper body
   - Head extension being used to compensate (arching chest to assist wrist position) — counterproductive
   - **Correction:** Upper limbs must be relaxed; they should not interfere with bar flight

2. **Relevance to Athlete:**
   - **High priority:** Push jerk tension issues directly limit OHS (50 kg limiter). Relaxation cues could unlock overhead position, improve shoulder mobility for snatch
   - **Back pain consideration:** Over-tensioning upper back compounds existing chronic pain during overhead work
   - **Practical:** Implement relaxation drills in warm-up; address wrist/shoulder mobility before jerk work

3. **Not Addressed:** Sets/reps, hypertrophy periodization, night shift recovery adjustments

**Bottom Line:** Cue relaxation in rack position and address compensatory head/chest movement before load progression.
