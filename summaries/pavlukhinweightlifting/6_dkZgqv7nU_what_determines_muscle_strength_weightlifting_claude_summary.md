# What determines muscle strength? Weightlifting

# Merged Weightlifting Coaching Summary

## Key Points

- **Two-factor strength model**: Neural drive (ВПД) + muscle cross-sectional area (CSA). ВПД develops fastest (2–4 weeks max), then plateaus; muscle growth sustains longer.
- **Periodization sequence**: ВПД phase (1–2 rep maxes, no metabolic acidosis) → hypertrophy phase (pyramid loading, moderate reps to failure, metabolic stress).
- **6-week hypertrophy block timing**: Optimal for shift workers—neural adaptation exhausts quickly; muscle growth provides longer-term strength foundation before returning to oly technique.
- **Frequency for night shift workers**: 5 sessions/week preferred (not 6). Distribute volume across 10 days (~1–2 hard sets per session) to reduce fatigue/acidosis while maintaining hypertrophy stimulus.
- **Progressive overload principle**: Same equipment, same movement pattern—clearer load tracking and hypertrophy measurement than exercise variation.
- **Stress requirement**: Last reps must approach technical failure; subthreshold volume is wasted.

---

## Technique Cues

- **Back squat tempo**: Concentric phase ≤2 seconds. Exceeding 2 sec → spinal erector spasm risk (critical for chronic back pain management). Reduce load if speed drops below threshold.
- **Back squat mindset**: Calm external state, explosive internal readiness (no wasted movement).
- **Knee tracking**: Track forward naturally; if anterior chain fatigues, shift loading toward posterior chain (unilateral work).
- **No belt squatting**: Prioritizes core/spinal stabilizer strengthening over load ceiling.

---

## Programming Principles

- **ВПД phase (Weeks 1–3)**: Heavy 1–2 rep maxes with power intent. Short rest between sets (maintain CNS arousal). No metabolic acidosis. Recruits existing motor units.
- **Hypertrophy phase (Weeks 3–6)**: Pyramid loading, moderate-rep sets to muscular failure. Longer rest between sets. Metabolic stress focus. Builds CSA in high-threshold fibers.
- **Session design for shift workers**: Keep hard sets low (1–2 per session) to prevent cumulative fatigue. Quality over quantity.
- **Back pain state-based adjustments**: Monitor spinal erector tension/spasm. Taper load if inflammation worsens. Prioritize core/abdominal work at end of every session (identified weak link).
- **Exercise selection**: Back squat (primary), leg press (heavy, 210+ kg baseline), unilateral leg work (posterior chain emphasis). Avoid high-rep bent-position time under tension.

## Athlete Relevance

| Factor | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| **Chronic back pain** | No belt squatting; squat tempo ≤2 sec concentric; daily core/ab work; avoid prolonged bent positions. Monitor spasm as load-adjust trigger. |
| **OHS 50 kg (snatch limiter)** | Likely mobility + spinal stability deficit. Target 150 kg back squat before returning to Olympic lifts. Unilateral leg work addresses posterior chain/core stability. |
| **Push jerk only (no split)** | Suggests quad/core weakness. Hypertrophy block directly addresses this. |
| **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** | Reduce to 5 sessions/week. Prioritize 1–2 hard sets per session over volume. Account for chronic life stress in load management. |
| **6-week hypertrophy goal** | **Perfect timing**: ВПД→hypertrophy sequence aligns exactly. Neural adaptations will plateau ~week 3; muscle growth sustains weeks 3–6. Return to oly technique week 7+ when squat strength improves. |

**Key action**: Squat strength to 150 kg + core stability → unlock OHS and split jerk mechanics. Tempo discipline prevents back flare-ups during hypertrophy loading.
