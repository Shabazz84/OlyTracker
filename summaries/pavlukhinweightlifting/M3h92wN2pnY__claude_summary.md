# Траектория штанги в приседаниях #павлухин #приседания #харьковский

**Subject:** Weightlifting — bar path technique

**Extracted content:**

1. **Technique cue (Snatch/Clean):**
   - Bar must travel in a vertical line through the "sleeve" (midline of the bar)
   - Bar center should move straight up and down
   - Forward displacement = loss of balance and increased perceived weight
   - Greater horizontal distance = heavier load, harder to maintain balance, lower max lift capacity

2. **Relevance to athlete:**
   - **HIGHLY RELEVANT:** OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg). Vertical bar path problems directly impact receiving position and overhead stability
   - Back pain consideration: forward bar displacement increases spinal load and stability demands — reinforces importance of strict vertical path
   - Prioritize this cueing during 6-week hypertrophy block before technique/strength phase

**Recommendation:** Film snatch/clean pulls to assess bar path drift. Likely technical bottleneck limiting snatch development.
