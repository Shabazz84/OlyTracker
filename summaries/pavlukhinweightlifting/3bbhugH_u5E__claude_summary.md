# Merged Coaching Summary

## Key Points

- **Hypertrophy block structure**: Alternate weeks emphasizing classical Olympic movements with bodybuilding-focused volume work to accelerate muscle development. Classical lifts alone have insufficient time-under-tension for hypertrophy.
- **Training frequency**: 5–6x/week optimal during muscle-building phases; classical lifts insufficient on their own.
- **Recovery protocol**: 10–15 days full rest when muscles are inflamed; post-volume soreness is normal and does not impair strength return by next session.
- **Session organization**: Layer anaerobic work (short sets, creatine phosphate system) with developmental blocks (30–40 sec efforts, glycolytic, 5–10 min rest between).
- **Recovery modality**: Active movement (walking, breathing work) more effective than rest/immobilization for muscular inflammation; sleep and nutrition are primary anabolic drivers.

## Technique Cues

- **Snatch pull**: Pull legs and back together explosively; maintain bar close to body; full explosive hip extension.
- **Rack position**: Hands relaxed in hook grip at rest; full arm tension engages during the pull, not at grip setup.
- **Squat depth**: Control hip rotation (individual foot angle matters); achieve full depth consistently.
- **General principle**: Explosive intent with simultaneous full-body tension (arms, legs, back engaged).

## Programming Principles

- Classical OL movements + high-rep supplemental work (legs, back, arms, shoulders, core).
- **Bench press retention**: Maintains upper-body stability and shoulder-joint durability despite Olympic lifting focus.
- **Volume-before-strength sequence**: Hypertrophy block precedes classical lift progression.
- **Principle**: "Train less, lift more, build a little" — structured blocks prioritize quality over constant intensity.

## Athlete Relevance

| Factor | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| **Chronic back pain** | Emphasize movement quality over volume; ensure adequate inter-session recovery; address muscular tightness through movement, not immobilization. |
| **OHS limiter (50 kg)** | Prioritize shoulder/upper-back strength via supplemental work (high-rep pressing, rows); bench press as skill maintenance. |
| **Night shift (Wed–Sun)** | Sleep/nutrition consistency critical; structure recovery blocks around available recovery windows. |
| **6-week hypertrophy → strength** | Aligns with block structure: high-volume accessory work now, classical lift progression after. |
| **Push jerk only** | Pressing strength emphasis (bench, overhead work) is appropriate; no split mechanics needed. |
