# Приседания должны быть взрывными в тяжелой атлетике #павлухин #харьковский #приседания

**Text is about weightlifting — Olympic lifting focused.**

---

**1. Programming Principles**
- Squats should serve snatch/clean & jerk, not be trained in isolation
- Squats must match the angles and rhythm of competition lifts
- Squats should be explosive/dynamic in execution

**2. Technique Cues**
- Wider knee position → more vertical torso in squat
- This vertical positioning mirrors leg mechanics in snatch/clean & jerk
- Squat rhythm/timing must replicate the classic lift demands

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Squats as assistance, not standalone work
- Must be competition-specific (angles + explosiveness)

**4. Relevance to This Athlete**
- **Back pain consideration**: Vertical squat positioning (wide knees) is generally safer than forward knee travel — aligns with managing chronic back issues
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: This coach's vertical squat emphasis supports overhead stability development needed for snatch
- **Training focus**: Squat variation selection should prioritize snatch/jerk carryover over raw strength gains
