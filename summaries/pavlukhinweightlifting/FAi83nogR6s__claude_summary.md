## EXTRACTED ANALYSIS:

**1. Technique Cues (Clean):**
- Bar positioning: Lower placement on torso (mid-foot projection) prevents forward diving; allows vertical extension drive
- Torso angle: Keep upright to maintain vertical bar path—excessive forward lean causes failed catches
- Catch depth: Parallel squat minimum required
- Bar landing: Upper trapezius/shoulders (proper racking position to prevent trunk collapse)

**2. Key Principle:**
- All heavy lifts in weightlifting require vertical bar trajectory; horizontal displacement = failure
