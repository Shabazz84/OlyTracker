# Press the barbell to your body #weightlifting #sport #pavlukhin

**This text is about weightlifting coaching, specifically barbell positioning and lower back mechanics.**

**Relevant Extractions:**

1. **Technique Cue (Clean/Snatch):**
   - Bring the bar closer to your body to reduce back stress
   - Lighter loads (e.g., 20 kg) feel more manageable on the lower back when positioned close vs. away from body
   - Maintaining center of gravity over mid-foot enables easier vertical extension

2. **Directly Relevant to This Athlete:**
   - **Chronic back pain:** Close barbell positioning (vertical path, bar tight to body) is a primary strategy to reduce lower back strain—critical for this athlete
   - Suggests the athlete should prioritize bar proximity cues in all pulls and receiving positions

3. **Implication:**
   - May explain low OHS (50 kg) and back limitations; positioning/mobility work should precede heavy loading

**Recommendation:** Focus on front rack position and clean pull mechanics emphasizing bar proximity before increasing weights.
