# Why are percentages bad for #weightlifting #pavlukhin?

**Language:** Russian (weightlifting coaching content)

---

## **1. Programming Principles**
- Percentage-based loading (e.g., 70% of 1RM) provides clarity but must account for daily readiness
- Nervous system and biological variability mean athletes won't always perform at prescribed intensities
- Emphasis on **velocity feedback** as load indicator rather than percentages alone
- Referenced program: "25 workouts to a record" (periodized approach offered)

## **2. Technique Cues**
- **Squat/Recovery Speed** (relevant to OHS limiter):
  - 1.5 sec rise = good, quick recovery
  - 3 sec rise = approaching limit
  - Film yourself to monitor ascent velocity
  - Velocity varies by anthropometry; learn your baseline at 70/80–90% loads

## **5. Relevance to This Athlete**
- **HIGH PRIORITY:** OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg). Monitoring squat recovery speed (especially asymmetries) via video is practical for identifying mobility/strength breakdowns
- **Back pain consideration:** Slow squat ascent (3+ sec) may indicate core instability or posterior chain weakness—velocity monitoring helps flag this
- Percentage-based programming should be adjusted for night shift fatigue (Wed–Sun work)

**Key takeaway:** Use velocity/speed of ascent as a self-regulation tool, not just percentages.
