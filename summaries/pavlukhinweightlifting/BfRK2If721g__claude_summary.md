# Постановка ног штангиста в приседаниях со штангой на спине #павлухин #тяжелаяатлетика #приседания

**Language:** Russian (Olympic weightlifting technique discussion)

---

**1. TECHNIQUE CUES:**
- **Foot stance:** Wide foot positioning is unsuitable in classical weightlifting because: (a) you cannot squat deeper, (b) it mimics the classical pull incorrectly
- **Correct positioning:** Start with feet at shoulder width
- **Bar placement:** Bar positioned through mid-foot on platform
- **Movement pattern:** Bar rises vertically along the "bridge" (foot midline)

---

**2. RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE:**

**Critical for OHS limiter (50 kg):** Proper foot positioning and vertical bar path are foundational. The emphasis on shoulder-width stance and mid-foot positioning directly addresses technical precision needed before loading the overhead position.

**Relevant to back pain:** Correct bar positioning and foot stance reduce unnecessary anterior/posterior shear forces, supporting safer loading during hypertrophy block.
