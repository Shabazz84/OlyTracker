**2. Technique Cues (Clean/Snatch Extension):**
- Bar acceleration must **increase throughout leg extension** — not decelerate at the top
- Extension should be **explosive and jump-like** (прыжковое), mimicking the pull in snatch
- Maximum bar speed and power occur at **full leg extension** (not at midpoint)
- This contrasts powerlifting deadlifts, where lockout happens earlier; Olympic lifting requires continued acceleration

**3. Exercise Recommendations:**
- Squats should be trained with this **ascending velocity pattern** in mind (not grinding to lockout)
