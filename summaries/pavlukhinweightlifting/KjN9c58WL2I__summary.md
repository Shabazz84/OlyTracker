# Крепкие локти для тяжелой атлетики #тяжёлаяатлетика ￼

**1. Programming principles:**
*   **Frequency/Volume:** High frequency of triceps extension work ("constantly") to build tendon strength; low-to-moderate intensity focused on endurance/stabilization rather than maximal load.
*   **Periodization:** Immediate focus on injury mitigation (tendon strengthening) before increasing technical complexity or load.

**2. Technique cues:**
*   **Elbow Extension Timing:** Avoid "early extension" of the elbows during the push/jerk phase; the triceps should not engage until the bar is fully overhead and stable.
*   **Movement Quality:** Eliminate "sharp" or "rhythmic" (jerky) movements that cause the elbow to snap back, which strikes the olecranon process via the tendon.

**3. Exercise recommendations:**
*   **Triceps Extensions:** Specific isolation work for triceps extensions to strengthen the triceps tendon and olecranon area.
*   **Pull-ups:** Recommended as a supplementary movement to strengthen shoulders and elbows simultaneously.

**4. Recovery or mobility advice:**
*   None explicitly mentioned in this text excerpt, though the focus on strengthening tendons implies a need for gradual load progression to allow tissue adaptation.

**5. Relevance to athlete profile:**
*   **Chronic Back Pain/Elbow Issues:** The text directly addresses the "sharp" movement pattern causing elbow pain (likely olecranon bursitis or triceps tendonitis), which is critical given the athlete's chronic back issues and need for joint stability during a hypertrophy block.
*   **Push Jerk Limitation:** Since this athlete only uses a push jerk, the emphasis on stable elbow extension without "snapping" is vital to prevent injury when the load is moved overhead without leg drive (split).
*   **Transition Phase:** The advice to strengthen tendons before increasing speed or height aligns with an intermediate lifter transitioning to Olympic lifts who has developed strength but lacks the connective tissue durability for high-velocity movements.
