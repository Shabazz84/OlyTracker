**Weightlifting coaching transcript (Russian) — Technical/Neural Focus**

**Programming Principles:**
- Technique changes are neurological, not structural (fast adaptation vs. slow hypertrophy)
- Descending load strategy: build motor pattern at lighter weight before attempting heavier load
- Short rest between attempts allows rapid neural re-programming (5 minutes mentioned)

**Technique Cues:**
- Emphasis on explosive power/rate of force development ("мощно взорвался" — explode powerfully)
- Patience/no rushing ("не спеши")
- Motor pattern repetition before loading

- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** This neural/motor learning emphasis supports technical precision work before load increases — critical given weak OHS relative to Clean
- **Back pain consideration:** Descending-load approach may reduce injury risk by perfecting mechanics at submaximal intensity first
