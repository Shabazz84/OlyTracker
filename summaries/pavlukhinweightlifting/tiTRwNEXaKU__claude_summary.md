# Колени в тяге не будут мешать, если их разгибать #тяжелаяатлетика #спорт

**Russian coaching dialogue about deadlift/pull technique.**

**Relevant extracts:**

1. **Technique issue**: Athlete's knees are collapsing/not fully extending during the pull. Coach notes the movement is faster than in powerlifting (wider grip) and athlete loses position mid-pull.

2. **Technique cue**: "Суставы разгибать коленные" — focus on **full knee extension** during the pull.

3. **Correction method**: 
   - Film from the side
   - Video analysis
   - Practice with light bar focusing on joint mechanics

**Relevance to this athlete**: 
- Back pain + chronic issues = knee drive weakness could stem from compensatory patterns
- Snatch OHS limiter (50 kg) suggests weak pulling position or poor knee extension in receiving position
- Night shift work may exacerbate movement quality fatigue

**Recommendation**: Prioritize **pull position work** (snatch/clean pulls with 2-3 sec pause at knee) in hypertrophy block to build postural stability and address this knee extension issue before attempting split jerk progression.
