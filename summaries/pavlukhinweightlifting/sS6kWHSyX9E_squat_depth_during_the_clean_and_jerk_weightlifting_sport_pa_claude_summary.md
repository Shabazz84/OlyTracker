This text is a Russian-language coaching dialogue about squat mechanics, not directly applicable to structured programming analysis.

**Key technical point extracted:**
- **Squat depth/power trade-off**: Coach emphasizes shorter range of motion = faster bar speed, better elastic recoil (tendon usage), less energy waste. Avoid excessive depth that eliminates explosive drive.

**Relevance to athlete:**
- **Moderately relevant**: At 50 kg OHS (primary snatch limiter), this athlete needs OHS strength work. The "short, explosive squat" principle could support power development, but given chronic back pain, excessively shallow squats may mask mobility deficits. Back pain suggests limited thoracic/hip mobility—deep, controlled squats may be therapeutically necessary before prioritizing speed.
- **Caution**: Don't apply this principle to sacrifice depth needed for OHS stability and catching position development.

**Recommendation**: Use controlled full-depth OHS work (within pain tolerance) during hypertrophy block; speed is secondary to range mastery here.
