**1. Technique Cues (Foot Positioning):**
- Keep weight balanced on full foot during lifts—not on toes, heels, or outer edge
- Test: Box jump reveals inefficiency if weight shifts off center
- Weight on heels only = reduced power output
- Weight on toes/forward lean = reduced power output
- Weight on outer foot = reduced power output
- Principle: All heavy lifting requires full foot contact

- **Back pain consideration**: Proper foot positioning improves kinetic chain alignment and reduces compensatory spinal stress
- **Clean/jerk progression**: Solid foot base essential before progressing split jerk (currently doing push jerk only)

**3. Application:**
- Add box jump diagnostics to assess foot stability before snatch/clean work
- Cue full-foot contact during OHS warm-ups and light snatch technique work
