# The dead point is a large leverage point #weightlifting #pavlukhin

**This text is about Olympic weightlifting technique — specifically deadlift/pull mechanics and starting position analysis.**

**Key Points:**

1. **Technique Issue Identified:**
   - Low starting position creates a "dead point" (sticking point) at liftoff
   - Problem: mechanical disadvantage increases as hips rise (shoulder moment arm increases)
   - Solution: Adjust starting position to move past the mechanical disadvantage earlier

2. **Relevant to Athlete:**
   - **CRITICAL:** Low back pain + low start = increased shear stress. This athlete should favor a higher hip start to reduce lumbar demand and maintain neutral spine longer
   - Starting position modification directly addresses chronic back pain management

3. **Programming Implication:**
   - Technique correction (starting position) should precede hypertrophy block
   - Fix mechanics to avoid pain-reinforcing patterns during volume work

**Recommendation:** Implement higher hip starting position (similar to modern competition style) before 6-week hypertrophy block begins. Video analysis needed to confirm exact hip angle.
