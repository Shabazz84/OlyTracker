## Key Extractions:

**1. Programming Principle — "Attack" (Атака) Method**
- Technique to simulate competition conditions: long rest between attempts with cooling effect
- Ivan Abadjiev's method: warm-up lifts stay *close* to competition weight (minimal drop) to keep CNS engaged
- Soviet alternative: drop ~50kg (e.g., 150kg → 160kg competition lift), preserves muscle temperature but less CNS specificity
- **Relevance to athlete:** At 80kg Clean, "attack" warm-ups might be 70–75kg; helps bridge technique work before strength block

**2. Recovery/Practical Consideration**
- Addresses between-attempt recovery and mental readiness
- Implies higher intensity requires longer rest periods
