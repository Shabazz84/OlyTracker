# Одноповторная работа и нервная система #тяжелаяатлетика #спорт

**Language:** Russian (conversational coaching discussion)

**Context:** Coach explaining why athlete "Kristina" is slower rising from 70 kg after a 2-week volume block.

---

**1. Programming Principles**
- 2-week volume block (high reps/many sets, long rest) followed by strength work
- Volume work is more fatiguing than single heavy attempts
- CNS (nervous system) fatigue drops strength after volume phases—normal adaptation
- Expected strength dip after 2 weeks volume + absence of heavy squats
- Volume approach: many sets with long rest intervals (avoid lactate/fatigue accumulation)

**2. Technique Cues**
None provided.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Leg work during volume block (even non-squat variants) doesn't fully maintain squat strength
- Implication: include heavy squat work during hypertrophy phases to maintain CNS activation

**4. Recovery/Mobility**
- Nutrition timing: eat 2 hours post-session to support recovery within next session
- Systemic fatigue management: many volume sets don't accumulate lactate if rest is adequate

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
**HIGH RELEVANCE:**
- Back Squat 118 kg + chronic back pain = likely benefit from volume work with long rest (reduces load per rep, CNS stress)
- Warns: 6-week hypertrophy block may temporarily depress squat/strength numbers before technique phase
- Shift work (Wed–Sun nights) = manage recovery nutrition strategically between sessions
