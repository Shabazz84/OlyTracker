**EXTRACTED CONTENT:**

**1. Programming Principles:**
- **Wave loading pattern:** Heavy-Light-Heavy-Light for intermediate athletes; advanced athletes use Heavy-Heavy-Light-Light
- **Individualization:** Cycle structure adjusts based on athlete age, sex, training level, and recovery capacity
- **Frequency:** Minimum 2 sessions/week; scales with athlete level and recovery

**2. Relevance to This Athlete:**
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun) + chronic back pain** = likely needs Heavy-Light-Light-Light or Heavy-Light-Heavy-Light pattern (easier recovery than back-to-back heavy weeks)
- **Intermediate level with weak OHS (50 kg)** = prioritize lighter technical weeks for snatch positions before heavy blocks
- **6-week hypertrophy block ahead** = this wave structure fits well; use lighter weeks for mobility/technique work on weak overhead position

**3. Key Takeaway:**
Pattern is flexible—coach must individualize based on sleep disruption and injury history. Suggest loading the 2–3 training days per week with staggered intensity rather than consecutive heavy sessions.
