**1. Programming Principles**
- Advocates for hypertrophy across all muscle groups, prioritizing legs and back first
- Implicit support for supplemental arm/tricep/bicep work alongside main lifts

**2. Technique Cues**
- Proper shoulder/joint positioning is foundational ("техникой нужно вставлять суставы")

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Arm/tricep/bicep strengthening as joint protection and stability
- Arms function as "armor" (бронежилет) to stabilize loaded positions overhead

**4. Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- *None explicit*

**5. Relevant to This Athlete**
- **HIGHLY RELEVANT**: Weak OHS (50 kg) likely stems from arm/shoulder weakness. Tricep/bicep hypertrophy work directly addresses this limiting factor

**Key takeaway**: Arm strengthening is injury prevention + technique enabler for this athlete's weak overhead position.
