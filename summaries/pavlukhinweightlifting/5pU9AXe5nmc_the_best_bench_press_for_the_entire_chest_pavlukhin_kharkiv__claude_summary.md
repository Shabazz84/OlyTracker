# The best bench press for the entire chest. #pavlukhin #kharkiv #benchpress

**Language:** Russian (coaching dialogue about pressing mechanics)

**Relevant Extracts:**

1. **Technique Cue (Press/Jerk Position):**
   - Bar path: Keep bar close to shoulder; longer distance from shoulder to bar = greater shoulder joint stress
   - Elbow position: Elbows OUT to the sides (not tucked to body)
   - Rationale: Prevents disengaging lower/middle chest; maintains shoulder stability via full pectoral recruitment
   - Warning: Dropping bar to solar plexus disables lower/middle chest, overloads shoulder joint → injury risk

2. **Programming Principle:**
   - Train full pectoral musculature to stabilize shoulder joint during pressing (reduces compensatory shoulder strain)

3. **Athlete-Specific Relevance:**
   - **HIGH PRIORITY:** This athlete has chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg). Poor pressing mechanics → unstable shoulder → compensatory lumbar stress
   - **Jerk limitation:** Push jerk only; this technique cue directly improves jerk stability without split demand
   - Chest development needed to support overhead stability

**Recommendation:** Apply this elbow-out, chest-engaged pressing pattern to all jerk work and OHS to build shoulder stability and reduce back compensation.
