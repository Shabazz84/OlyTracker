**Translation & Analysis:**

The coach is discussing technique breakdown points and working weights. Key points extracted:

---

**1. Programming Principles**
- Establish "working weight" as the heaviest load where technique remains intact
- Once technique breaks down at a given weight, that weight becomes the ceiling for that session
- Competitive lift technique must be trained at competitive/near-competitive weights (not just lighter)

**2. Technique Cues**
- Monitor for technique breakdown as load increases—this is your immediate signal to stop ascending
- Technique quality at lighter weights doesn't transfer automatically; heavier weights require specific training

**3. Relevance to This Athlete**
- **Critical for OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Given chronic back pain + weak OHS, this principle is essential—don't chase heavy singles if form collapses; regress to 50 kg range and build volume/stability there first
- **For 6-week hypertrophy block**: Use submaximal weights where technique is stable (likely 70% of working max) to accumulate volume safely with chronic back issue
- **Night shift fatigue**: Technical fatigue accumulates—this approach prevents chasing loads when CNS is already fatigued mid-week

---

**Bottom line**: Find your technique ceiling first; build within it rather than above it.
