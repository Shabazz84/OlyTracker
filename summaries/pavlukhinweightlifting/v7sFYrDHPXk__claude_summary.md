# Merged Summary: Russian Weightlifting Coaching Content

## Key Points
- **4–6 weeks sufficient** for competition prep if technique is solid
- **Pre-competition structure**: heavy week → 2 light weeks → competition
- **Training frequency**: 2–5 sessions/week minimum; shorter, more frequent sessions outperform high-volume single sessions
- **Quality over quantity**: Avoid "greediness in weight"—fatigue injuries stem from over-training, not heavy loads with good technique
- Minimal content on chronic back management, night-shift recovery optimization, or hypertrophy-specific periodization

## Technique Cues
- **OHS**: Hip extension (glute drive) drives hips **upward only**, not backward; maintain chest-up position
- **Snatch**: Explosive leg drive from floor; bar path must travel overhead, not forward
- **Jerk**: Drive hard from chest; avoid deceleration
- **General**: Slow bar takeoff speed noted but not elaborated

## Programming Principles
- Post-hypertrophy block: use light weeks (deload) strategically before heavy/competition phases
- Prioritize session quality and recovery architecture over raw volume
- Cumulative fatigue is the primary injury risk, not intensity itself
