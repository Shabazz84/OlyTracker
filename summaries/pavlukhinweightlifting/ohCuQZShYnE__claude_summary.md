# Merged Weightlifting Summary

## Key Points

- **Standing press > bench press** for Olympic lifting: develops shoulder girdle strength without excess stiffness; historically central to the sport until 1972.
- **Wrist/forearm work critical** for snatch stability (your 50 kg OHS limiter): high-rep distraction exercises (20–30 reps) without axial load protect elbow joint.
- **Recovery philosophy**: Train through minor fatigue via self-assessment; reduce volume only on poor recovery days (fever, excessive fatigue). Percentage-based loading inadequate when fatigued.
- **Pressing transfers to jerk**: Standing press reinforces leg drive and shoulder stability—directly applicable to your push jerk progression (currently 65 kg).
- **Back pain management**: Avoid heavy lumbar arching; prioritize posterior chain pulls and core stability over pressing volume.

---

## Technique Cues

- **Wrist positioning in snatch**: Modify grip width/angle to reduce elbow strain (grip flexibility reduces OHS mobility demand).
- **Standing press form**: Thoracic extension emphasis (not lumbar arch); scapulae packed; foot drive from floor.
- **Shoulder mobility work**: Broad circles, shoulder distraction pulls—mobilize thoracic spine and shoulder girdle without aggressive ROM loading.

---

## Programming Principles

- **Upper-body split**: Standing press (2x/week) + pulling work (crossover pulls, rows) + wrist/grip distraction (high-rep, low load).
- **Accessory structure**: 
  - Dips (2 sets) for triceps/shoulder
  - Machine/preacher biceps curls (12 reps, 2–3 sets)
  - High-rep wrist/forearm work (20–30 reps)
- **Hypertrophy + mobility simultaneously**: Build muscle while improving shoulder/wrist stability (advanced approach—suitable for intermediate athlete).
- **Night shift compatibility**: Reduced volume on fatigued days; avoid percentage-based programming—rely on RPE/athlete assessment.

---

## Athlete Relevance

✅ **Highly applicable:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Wrist mobility + standing press + pulling directly address shoulder stiffness and stability.
- **Push jerk progression (65 kg)**: Standing press reinforces leg drive, transfers shoulder strength; safe alternative to split jerk learning.
- **Chronic back pain**: Posterior chain pulls + thoracic mobility (avoid lumbar arching) + core stability fit deload strategy.
- **6-week hypertrophy block**: Standing press + dips + biceps curls (8–12 rep range) + wrist work = balanced upper-body development.
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Recovery-first philosophy explicitly supports reduced-volume training on fatigue days.

**Action items:**
1. Prioritize standing press 2x/week over bench.
2. Add high-rep wrist distraction (20–30 reps) for snatch stability.
3. Emphasize pulling work (rows, lat pulls) for back strength and shoulder decompression.
4. Use RPE (not percentages) on night-shift fatigue days.
