# Не легко/тяжело, а быстро/медленно #тяжелаяатлетика

**Russian weightlifting coaching on squat tempo/velocity assessment**

**Programming Principles:**
- Uses squat **concentric time** as a load-management tool
- Slower rise time (4-8 sec) = heavy load with mechanical advantage/reserve
- Faster rise time (up to 2 sec) = lighter load, speed/explosiveness work
- Coach's example: 180 kg squat at 2–3 sec rise = ~99% effort; at 2.2 sec rise = only 75 kg feasible
- **Periodization implication:** Tempo varies by training phase intent

**Key Cue:**
- Measures **standing time only** (concentric phase), not descent
- Athlete must find their own tempo-to-load ratio

**Relevance to Athlete Profile:**
- **Highly relevant:** OHS at 50 kg is the snatch limiter; squat strength/speed directly carries over
- **Application:** Use tempo work in 6-week hypertrophy block—slower tempos (4–8 sec) can build positional strength for back pain management (reduced impact/ballistic stress)
- **Back pain consideration:** Slower, controlled squats with tempo prescription reduce injury risk vs. fast/heavy singles
