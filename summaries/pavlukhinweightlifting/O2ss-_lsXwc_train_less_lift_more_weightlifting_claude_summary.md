# Merged Summary: Russian Coaching Conversation on Periodization & Recovery

## Key Points

- **CNS fatigue is normal**: Heavy loading (e.g., 225 kg squat) produces expected "wooden" feeling; lighter work 6 days later is appropriate management.
- **Plateaus require patience**: 7+ months on same lifts is acceptable; consistency beats max attempts between competitions.
- **Chronic injuries limit positions**: Back pain and wrist pain (forward bar tip during snatch) are documented constraints requiring targeted mobility work.
- **Incremental progression works**: 1–5 kg jumps preferred when breaking plateaus over large jumps.
- **Video analysis is essential**: Self-review aids technique refinement.
- **Periodization structure**: 8-week cycles with progressive load increases; deload weeks integrated; cycles repeated with small increments.

## Technique Cues

- **Snatch**: Arm position forward on toes; increase depth; manage forward bar tip (linked to chronic wrist pain).
- **Jerk**: Foot placement instability noted; push jerk only (no split jerk progression mentioned).
- **Shoulder press**: Use snatch-grip and jerk-grip variations only; minimize heavy arm/shoulder volume to improve mobility.

## Programming Principles

- **Core work mandatory**: Abs + hyperextension; reduce heavy arm volume to prioritize wrist/shoulder mobility.
- **Arm work minimized**: Only mobility-focused flexion/extension (no hypertrophy emphasis).
- **Recovery prioritized**: Health over PR attempts; patience with CNS fluctuations.
- **No structured accommodation** for night shift work mentioned.
