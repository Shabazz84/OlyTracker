# Merged Summary: Limited Applicability

## Key Points
- **Periodization strategy**: Preparatory blocks (6+ weeks) can prioritize weak points (squat/leg development) with reduced or eliminated classic lift practice; technique is retained if "hard-wired"
- **Injury/strain management**: Continue cautious training rather than complete cessation; recovery accelerated by training stimulus + adequate protein
- **Knee valgus**: Common squat mechanics issue correctable via glute activation (abductors/external rotators)

## Technique Cues
- Push jerk is viable primary jerk technique (consistent with your athlete's current pattern)
- Knee valgus correction: prioritize glute work (activation/strengthening)

## Programming Principles
- Hypertrophy blocks can temporarily deprioritize technique maintenance for weak-point strength development when timeline allows (2+ months before competition)
- Soreness resolves naturally during tapering phases

## Athlete Relevance
