# Высокий или низкий старт #тяжелаяатлетика #павлухин

**Language:** Russian coaching dialogue

**Content Analysis:**

1. **Technique Cue (Clean/Front Rack Position):**
   - Knee tracking: keep shins **slightly forward, not excessively** to avoid overloading knees
   - Maintain **vertical shin angle** for even quad/posterior chain distribution
   - Avoid excessive knee valgus ("protruding knees") → chronic injury risk

2. **Programming Principle:**
   - Emphasis on **balanced muscular loading** (anterior + posterior chain equally) rather than isolated stress

3. **Relevance to Athlete:**
   - **HIGHLY RELEVANT:** Chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg) suggests quad dominance or poor posterior chain engagement
   - This cue addresses knee position → improved load distribution → reduced spinal compensation
   - Strengthening back side of legs supports OHS weakness and back health

4. **Recommendation:**
   - Front squat/clean positions: prioritize vertical shin alignment and balanced hip/quad drive to protect lower back
