# Двигательные единицы развиваются быстро #павлухин #харьковский #тяжелаяатлетика ￼

**Language:** Russian coaching content about pressing technique and motor learning.

**Key Points Extracted:**

1. **Programming/Intensity Principle:**
   - Work near maximal loads to develop neuromuscular adaptation
   - Brain establishes motor patterns within a single session (CNS plasticity)
   - Must train heavy weights to achieve high results—no substitute for near-limit work

2. **Technique Cue (Press):**
   - First attempt at 105 kg was "crooked" (unstable/poor form)
   - Dropped to 100 kg (successful rep)
   - Second 105 kg attempt was "beautiful"—demonstrates motor learning within session

3. **Transfer Principle:**
   - Motor learning principles apply across all lifts: press, snatch, clean, jerk, squat, pullups
   - Movement changes but the *principle* of skill development remains constant

**Relevance to This Athlete:**
- ⚠️ **Back pain concern:** Near-maximal pressing work requires excellent core stability; prioritize form over load
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** This philosophy supports high-frequency overhead work at challenging but controlled weights
- **Split jerk development:** Apply same principle—practice near-maximal jerk loads (not just push jerk) to ingrain proper pattern
- **Night shift fatigue:** CNS fatigue from shift work may slow motor learning; ensure adequate sleep post-training sessions
