# Weightlifting for over 30. Artem, 35

# MERGED SUMMARY

## Key Points
- **Early technique errors persist and cannot be fully eliminated**—compensation patterns must be managed, not eradicated. Focus on technique refinement through varied stimulus rather than correction alone.
- **Anthropometry dictates technique demands**: shorter limbs require faster bar turnover and quicker descent mechanics.
- **Varied intensity days + structured deload weeks** are essential for intermediate/advanced lifters to prevent burnout and CNS fatigue (particularly critical for night shift workers).
- **Accessory work targeting weak points** (shoulders, wrist, overhead pressing) is non-negotiable, especially when chronic pain is present.
- Recovery quality > activity volume for shift workers; deload weeks should include low-intensity modalities (sauna, swimming, cycling).

## Technique Cues
- Use **pulling from different angles** and **paused pulls** for technical development and angle-specific strength.
- Manage early compensation patterns through varied rep ranges and loading schemes rather than heavy singles that reinforce poor movement.
- **Overhead pressing variations and shoulder mobility work** must precede snatch/OHS attempts (addresses your 50 kg OHS limiter).

## Programming Principles
- Avoid high-frequency Soviet-style programming; prioritize quality variance over volume accumulation.
- Deload weeks are mandatory for night shift athletes; integrate low-stress modalities.
- **Post-heavy compound work**: Core and grip accessory emphasis (especially after squat days).
- Structure 6-week blocks with deliberate intensity variation to manage chronic back pain and CNS fatigue.

## Athlete Relevance
- **Chronic back + night shift**: Deload weeks non-negotiable; prioritize accessory robustness over max effort heavy compounds.
- **OHS 50 kg deficit**: Needs dedicated overhead pressing volume and shoulder mobility, not increased snatch attempts.
- **Push jerk only**: Accessory focus should include split jerk unweighting drills and stability work before split technique retraining.
