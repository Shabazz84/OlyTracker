**Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:**

**1. Exercise Recommendations**
- Romanian deadlifts (RDLs) to address quad dominance and posterior chain imbalance
- Rationale: Prevents excessive knee/hip joint stress and improves pulling strength in Olympic lifts

**2. Programming Principle**
- Work:rest ratio of 40 seconds work / 20-30 seconds rest (conditioning/metabolic style)

**3. Injury Prevention / Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **Critical for this athlete:** Quad-dominant imbalance increases knee and hip joint stress—directly problematic given his chronic back pain
- Strengthening hamstrings/glutes improves barbell pulling mechanics and reduces compensatory back loading

**Application to Profile:**
- RDLs fit well in 6-week hypertrophy block (hamstring/glute focus)
- Addresses OHS limiter indirectly (posterior chain stability)
- Important before pushing jerk → split jerk transition (requires posterior chain strength)
- Schedule around night shift work (Wed–Sun)
