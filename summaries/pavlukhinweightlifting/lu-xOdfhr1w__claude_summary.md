# Гигантские тяги для рывка бесполезны #тяжелаяатлетика #спорт

**Language:** Russian coaching analysis

**Key Points:**

1. **Programming principle:** Avoid excessive pulling volume when technique is underdeveloped—high reps of poor pulls don't build the skill.

2. **Technique concept:** Pull ≠ Snatch are distinct movements requiring separate motor patterns. The athlete can pull heavy (100 kg) but loses control when accelerating in the snatch (fails at 35 kg)—barbell disperses in flight due to lack of coordinated movement.

3. **Core issue:** Motor control/timing breakdown during extension acceleration causes loss of bar path control and technique collapse.

4. **Recommendation (implicit):** Prioritize snatch technique/coordination work over high-volume pulling before heavy snatch training.

**Relevance to athlete:** Directly applicable—OHS is listed as primary snatch limiter (50 kg). This suggests the issue isn't pulling strength but snatch-specific motor control. Recommends technique refinement over volume before the strength block.
