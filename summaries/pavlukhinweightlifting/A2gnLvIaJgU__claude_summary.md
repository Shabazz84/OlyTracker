# Merged Coaching Summary: Squat Mechanics for Olympic Weightlifting

## Key Points

- **Squat is sport-specific**: Must replicate snatch/clean leg drive—explosive, speed-accelerating throughout the entire range (not just breaking sticking points).
- **Foot position is foundational**: Bar/center of mass must track vertically over mid-foot. Heel lift = loss of power, balance, and increased lower back stress.
- **Ankle dorsiflexion mobility is critical**: Limited ankle mobility forces forward lean (hip-dominant shift) or heel lift, underloading quads and overloading lumbar spine.
- **Torso angle matters**: Minimize forward lean via wider stance (more vertical torso angle) and adequate ankle mobility. Quad-dominant loading is prerequisite for OHS and clean power.

---

## Technique Cues

| **Element** | **Cue** |
|---|---|
| **Foot position** | Full foot contact (midfoot); no heel lift. Vertical bar path over mid-foot. |
| **Stance width** | Wider (knee angle) → more vertical torso → better bar path & quad loading. |
| **Torso angle** | Minimize forward lean; maintain upright posture (not butt-back hip-dominant). |
| **Ankle dorsiflexion** | Differentiate calf (gastrocnemius) stretching from deep ankle joint mobility. Bent-knee ankle work targets soleus & capsule. |
| **Explosive intent** | Accelerate bar speed through entire ROM, especially upward from sticking point. Treat as jump preparation. |
| **Proprioception** | Closed-eye squats develop foot/balance awareness. |

---

## Programming Principles

- **Squat mechanics precede load**: Technical correction (foot position, ankle mobility, torso angle) unlocks strength without adding weight.
- **Sport-specific carry-over**: Vertical bar path, explosive intent, and quad dominance directly transfer to clean leg drive and snatch stability.

---

## Athlete Relevance

| **Constraint** | **Application** |
|---|---|
| **OHS 50 kg limiter** | Poor squat mechanics → weak quad drive → weak OHS. Ankle mobility (dorsiflexion) directly impacts OHS depth. Fix foot position & ankle mobility first. |
| **Chronic back pain** | Forward bar drift or forward lean overloads lumbar spine. Correcting squat positioning (vertical bar path, full foot contact, minimal forward lean, adequate ankle mobility) reduces lower back stress. |
| **Back Squat 118 kg (untapped)** | Technical breakdown likely present. Vertical torso, foot stability, and explosive intent can unlock 5–10 kg without load progression. |
| **Night shift fatigue** | Prioritize ankle mobility + closed-eye squat drills as low-fatigue proprioceptive work on heavy days. |

---

## **Immediate Action**
1. **Assess ankle dorsiflexion mobility** (bent-knee ankle flexion test—target ~15–20° beyond vertical).
2. **Establish foot position**: Film squats; verify full foot contact & mid-foot bar path before heavy loading.
3. **Add soleus-focused mobility** (bent-knee calf/ankle work) 3–4× weekly.
