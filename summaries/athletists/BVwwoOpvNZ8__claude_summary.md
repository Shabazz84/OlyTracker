# Merged Weightlifting Coaching Summary

## Key Points

- **Snatch pull mechanics**: Drive legs, hips, traps, and arms simultaneously ("in one clap")—not sequentially. Common error is releasing the bar then pulling with arms (too slow).
- **Receiving position (snatch/clean)**: Drop vertically like a stone after pressing bar away; avoid hard impacts that stress the spine. Relax into descent; don't hold muscular tension.
- **Overhead stability**: Shoulders must be forward of the bar, not behind it. Knees travel farther forward than shoulders for stable bracing.
- **Isometric pull work**: Snatch pulls with 5-second holds at knee level—critical for building overhead holding strength and often neglected.
- **Pause squats**: Build positional strength with 2 easy reps, then 3+ second pause on 3rd rep.
- **Belt usage**: Mandatory for heavy pulls; essential for athletes with back issues. Use correct placement to avoid spine damage.

## Technique Cues

**Snatch Pull:**
- Hip extension drives immediately into arm action (simultaneous, not sequential)
- Head stays neutral; avoid "pecking" forward

**Overhead Position:**
- Shoulders positioned forward of bar (not covering it)
- Knees travel farther forward than shoulders
- Receive with control; slight forward lean acceptable but avoid hard impacts

**Drop Mechanics:**
- Press bar away before descending into catch
- Drop vertically; don't delay descent with muscular tension
- Relax into position to avoid spine stress

**Push Jerk:**
- Stand narrow ("like a soldier on one line")
- Knee bend then powerful leg drive without spreading feet wide

## Programming Principles

- **Exercise progression**: Pull → Classic pull → Competition movement
- **Isometric integration**: Incorporate snatch pulls with holds regularly, not occasionally
- **Pause work**: 2 reps easy, 3rd with extended pause to build positional strength
- **Progressive loading**: Small incremental jumps; don't rush heavier weights
- **Timing**: Coordination/isometric work fits early in periodization (suits 6-week hypertrophy block)

## Athlete Relevance

✅ **Directly applicable to this athlete:**

- **OHS 50 kg limiter**: Shoulder positioning cues and isometric pull holds directly address overhead stability deficit
- **Chronic back pain**: 
  - Controlled vertical drop mechanics protect lower spine
  - Belt recommendation supported by coach's herniation experience
  - Avoid hard landing impacts
- **Snatch technique gaps**: Isometric pull work builds confidence under load and improves positional strength
- **Night shift recovery**: Post-training bar hangs and stretching protocols are low-stress, accessible tools
- **Push jerk focus**: Coach validates shvung as solid supplementary tool; aligns with current jerk style before split jerk training
- **Back squat foundation**: Pause squat work strengthens receiving position—prepares for increased snatch/clean loading

⚠️ **Caution**: Avoid excessive hypertrophy training in upper body that may reduce lockout speed/strength.
