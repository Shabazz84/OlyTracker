## 2. **Technique Cues for Snatch**
**Starting Position (Critical for this athlete):**
- Feet: hip-width stance, symmetric to bar midline, metatarsal heads under bar
- Benefit: vertical force vectors; maximizes bar height at extension (essential for small OHS 50kg)
- Knees: turned out aligned with toe angle (closes bar-shin distance)
- Shins/bar: nearly touching
- Shoulders: directly over bar
- Catching position: split jerk preferred over power jerk (more efficient bar height)
- Snatch duration: 2.5–3 seconds (vs. clean ~6–9 sec)

**Key insight:** Individual morphology (limb proportions) affects starting angles—no universal joint angle prescription, but universal principles apply.
