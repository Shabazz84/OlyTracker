## Technique Cues
*   **"Double Movement":** Initiate the pull with legs and back muscles simultaneously; avoid pulling with arms first. Focus on rolling the bar onto the chest immediately after the second pull.
*   **Jerk Drive:** Prioritize leg drive to generate momentum before engaging the upper body. Do not rely on arm strength or bar recoil.
*   **Torso Alignment:** Maintain a vertical, straight torso during the press/jerk phase; avoid leaning forward into an "unfavorable position."
