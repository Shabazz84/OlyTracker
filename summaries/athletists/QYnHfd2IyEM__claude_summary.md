# Рывковая тренировка. Рывок классический + рывок с виса выше колен

**Not about coaching this specific athlete.**

This is a Russian weightlifting coach's (Dmitry Berestov) vlog commentary about:
- Channel format changes (longer videos → shorter technique breakdowns)
- Personal relocation to St. Petersburg
- A snatch-focused workout he filmed 6 months ago

**Relevant coaching principles extracted:**

1. **Programming**: Snatch workout structure—
   - Pull from hip (3 reps, no leg/back drive, arms only) + 3 pulls + 3 pressing in low position = 6 sets (warm-up + shoulder prep)
   - Classic snatch + 2 snatches from hang above knee (1+2 format)
   - Front squats (chosen over back squats mid-week to avoid excessive fatigue)
   - Push press work (3x/week push variation)

2. **Technique cues (snatch pull)**:
   - Pull to hip height consistently ("pull to the groin")
   - Keep shoulders over bar at liftoff
   - Don't force violent contact—subtle acceleration acceptable
   - If back rounds/knees shoot forward = weight too heavy; reduce load
   - Maintain position, then extend; extension follows good position

3. **Recovery**: Front squat chosen to manage weekly fatigue vs. back squat
