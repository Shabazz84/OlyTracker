# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Technique & Programming

## Key Points
- Avoid max testing early in training blocks; load increases gradually, with PRs emerging naturally as fatigue permits
- Pre-final training day = moderate/light; final day = max attempts
- Jerk foot positioning: symmetrical "scissors" stance (equal distance front/back), torso vertical through center of gravity
- Snatch pulls: control descent with torso lean to prevent forward bar slide
- Knee sequencing: pass knees early, then explode—avoid driving knees fully before extension
- Squat descent: controlled amortization over bouncing to protect joints during heavy loading
- Sauna used post-session for recovery at training camps

## Technique Cues
**Snatch:**
- Incline snatch pulls emphasize torso lean control to prevent bar drift forward

**Jerk (push jerk focus):**
- Explosive leg drive outward > slow chest extension
- Narrower grip often more comfortable for developing jerkers; wider grip demands shoulder mobility
- Wrist relaxation critical to avoid tension
- Foot step sequence: back foot vs. front foot timing matters

**Squat:**
- Controlled descent (amortization phase) over bouncing—important for chronic back pain management

## Programming Principles
- Periodized structure: gradual load increase → PR emergence when fatigued
- Session structure: moderate/light days before competition/max days
- Recovery integration: post-session sauna use
