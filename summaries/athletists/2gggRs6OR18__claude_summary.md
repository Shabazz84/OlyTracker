## Technique Cues
- **Jerk/Press mechanics:** Symmetrical foot placement (equal distance front/back from centerline); maintain head position and eye gaze forward during lockout; avoid dropping chin during overhead positions.
- **Squat/Hip drive:** Sit back with hips (not just knees) to distribute load across posterior chain rather than loading knees excessively.
- **Snatch pulling:** Pulling below knee allows longer acceleration phase.
- **Stability:** Rigid shoes preferred over soft-soled for overhead lockout.
- **Press consistency:** "Push like normal jerk" — maintain mechanical consistency between variations.

---

## Programming Principles
- **Pressing variations at multiple heights** (pin press from varying rack positions) to manipulate difficulty and load relative to range of motion.
- **Static holds alongside dynamic movement** (e.g., overhead holds, low-position holds near squat depth).
- **Progressive loading** scaling from ~40 kg to 150+ kg across variations.
- **Training intensity:** "Train by how you feel" at 90–100% intensity; listen to body and consult coach.
