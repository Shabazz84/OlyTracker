# Как проходят сборы по тяжелой атлетике. Анапа, рывковая тренировка

## Key Points
- Snatch imitation (техника рывка) is a deliberate, slow-movement drill using light weight to develop proprioceptive awareness and mental rehearsal of body positions throughout the pull
- Common error: premature rise to toes before leg extension completes
- Pause holds in bottom position are beneficial for intermediate athletes and those with technique deficits; less necessary for advanced lifters
- Weekly structure should allow recovery before competition/max attempt days (e.g., Friday peaks)
- Wrist pain risk increases when progressing to heavy loads without adequate warm-up

## Technique Cues
**Snatch Imitation:**
- Emphasize deliberate positioning at each phase of the pull
- Correct early toe rise—ensure full leg extension before transitioning to triple extension
- Pause holds in bottom position aid positional awareness and stability

**General:**
- Bar path and head position referenced but incompletely documented in source

## Programming Principles
- Structure weekly training to peak on competition/max attempt days (minimum 2–3 days recovery prior)
- Morning sessions: snatch imitation + snatch from multiple positions + heavy pulls
- Prioritize exercise quality and recovery for intermediate athletes over exercise volume/economy

## Athlete Relevance
**High priority:**
- Snatch imitation + pause holds directly address your 50 kg OHS limiter (primary deficit)
- Pause holds in bottom position benefit chronic back pain management and technique foundation before hypertrophy block
- Your intermediate status warrants full-rep, paused work rather than advanced-athlete shortcuts
- Schedule strength/technique block after 6-week hypertrophy phase; plan Friday max attempts with 2–3 prior recovery days

**Medium priority:**
- Wrist warm-up protocols essential before jumping to heavy jerks (65 kg push jerk baseline)
