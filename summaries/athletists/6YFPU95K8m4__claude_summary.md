# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Coaching Content

## Key Points

- **Periodization over single-session PRs**: Build long-term progression; climbing attempts to psychological thresholds (e.g., breaking 70 kg) develop confidence even if unsuccessful.
- **Session structure**: Heavy lift (snatch) → lighter technical work → supplemental (jerk/pulls) if energy permits.
- **Attempt strategy with fatigue management**: Hold back on maxes if evening jerk session follows; avoid over-depletion. Monitor fatigue before secondary lifts.
- **Volume principle for preservation**: Minimize heavy volume at moderate percentages (60–80%); warm up thoroughly on light weights, then progress quickly to maximal attempts.

## Technique Cues

**Snatch:**
- **Pull phase**: Drive to explosion, closer to chest; finish higher for faster movement.
- **Foot position**: Start closer to barbell (2–12 cm difference materially affects final position).
- **Catch mechanics**: Full foot pressure, head up, linear arm force; don't relax grip until lockout.
- **Common error**: Starting distance too far shifts bar trajectory forward; avoid rotating in receiving position.

## Programming Principles

- **Session construction**: Heavy primary lift → positional/technical work → supplementals (if energy remains).
- **Recovery consideration**: Structure training to avoid over-depletion before secondary sessions (e.g., jerk work following snatch).
- **Exercise progression**: Chinese pulls (slow pulls) effective for post-max positional work and supplemental development.

## Athlete Relevance

- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Chinese pulls and positional overhead work directly support snatch receiving position stability—prioritize post-max snatch work.
- **Chronic back pain + Back Squat 118 kg**: Use lighter openers during 6-week hypertrophy block; fatigue management critical to avoid pain escalation.
- **Night shift (Wed–Sun)**: Structure Wed–Sun training with careful recovery between sessions; consider attempt density and session length to maintain quality.
- **Split jerk not yet trained**: Front squat/OHS work via hypertrophy block will build necessary receiving stability before split jerk introduction.
