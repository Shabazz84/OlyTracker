## 1. CORE TRAINING PHILOSOPHY (5 Principles)

**1. Address Weaknesses, Never Avoid Them**
Train through deficits, not around them. Your 50 kg OHS (vs. 80 kg clean) and 65 kg jerk (vs. 118 kg back squat) signal specific mobility, positioning, or strength gaps—not genetic limits. Direct targeted work to these weak points.

**2. Technique Precision Precedes Load**
Bar path, positioning, and movement quality establish the foundation for safe and effective strength gain. Your chronic back pain makes this non-negotiable: poor positions (excessive flexion, early extension, uncontrolled drops) accumulate spinal stress. Prioritize position perfection at 70–80% intensity before pursuing heavy loads.

**3. Individualize, Don't Template**
Generic programming fails. Your night-shift schedule (Wed–Sun), chronic back pain, and transition from strength training to Olympic lifting require custom programming decisions (grip width, squat stance, jerk variation, recovery modalities). Test and adapt.

**4. Train Fresh, End Hungry**
Heavy technical/explosive work requires a fresh nervous system; sequencing matters. Place snatch/clean/jerk early; squats/pulls later. Finish sessions with positive reps—avoid grinding attempts that kill confidence and recovery. This suits night-shift fatigue: train well within capacity, not to exhaustion.

**5. Recovery is Training**
Sleep, sauna, active mobility, and nutrition are non-negotiable inputs, not optional accessories. Night shift work already disrupts these—engineer what you can control. Consistency over intensity when fatigued.

## 2. PROGRAMMING APPROACH

**Macrocycle:** 6 weeks hypertrophy block → 4–6 week strength/technique phase

**Weekly Structure (3x/week frequency suits night shift):**
- **Session A (Snatch-focus):** Technical warm-up (pulls from positions) → Snatch from blocks (70–75%, high-quality reps) → Snatch pulls with holds (5-second pause at knee) → OHS accessories (2–3 variations, 2–3×5)
- **Session B (Clean/Jerk-focus):** Clean pulls from blocks or rack position → Power/push jerk from rack (70–75%, 3–5 sets of 2) → Front squat or back squat (moderate tempo, 3–5×3–5) → Jerk dips or leg accessory
- **Session C (Strength/Lower Body):** Back squat (tempo-controlled, 70–80%, 5–6×3–5) → Clean or snatch variation (lighter, technical emphasis) → Supplemental (pulls, carries, core) → Active recovery (stretching, sauna if available)

**Intensity Distribution (Hypertrophy Block):**
- Main lifts: 70–80% 1RM during technique work; light loading (50–65%) on high-rep accessory
- Avoid heavy singles (≥90%) until strength phase
- Frequency: Snatch and clean/jerk worked 2x/week each; back squat 2–3x/week

**Load Progressions:**
- 1–2 kg increments per session (sustainable for intermediate lifters)
- Use back squat as primary strength driver (117 kg baseline → target 125–130 kg by week 6)
- Jerk progression paired with front squat gains (65 kg → 70 kg expected)

## 3. KEY TECHNIQUE CUES & EXERCISE PREFERENCES

**Snatch:**
- **Bar path**: Vertical exit from floor; light contact at hip; bar settles naturally into power position before explosive finish
- **Common error**: Early back extension + forward bar drift. Fix: "Накрыв" (maintain posture/cover the bar)—keep chest up through mid-thigh, let gravity settle bar
- **OHS receiver**: Lead with shoulders forward (not covering bar); wrist locked tight; vertical torso
- **Exercises preferred**: Snatch pulls from blocks (knee/mid-thigh height) to target leg drive; snatch on blocks (reduces ROM, builds amplitude); snatch pull with 5-second pause at knee (develops receiving timing); OHS from blocks (addresses mobility limiter directly)

**Clean:**
- **Front rack position**: Elbows high and rotated forward to create shoulder platform (critical for back pain mitigation—prevents torso flexion)
- **Bar trajectory**: Slight backward arc off floor acceptable; bar returns into chest with elbows high
- **Catch**: Rebound position with active leg drive; drop early reps, fixate only final rep
- **Exercises preferred**: Clean pulls from blocks (reduces spinal load relative to floor pulls); rack position cleans (isolates pulling deficiency without catching complexity); front squat with pause (2 reps easy, 3rd rep with 2–3 second pause at bottom)

**Jerk (Push/Power Only Until Technique Phase):**
- **Dip phase**: Shallow, controlled descent (6–8 inches); explosive upward drive from legs, not arms; avoid forward collapse or excessive hip rise
- **Head position**: Pull back (not up) to clear bar path
- **Foot contact**: Stand narrow; equal distance front/back from centerline
- **Receiving**: Stable dip position catches bar with control; no passive absorption
- **Jerk from rack preferred** (isolates jerk strength without clean fatigue; reduces cumulative spinal load)
