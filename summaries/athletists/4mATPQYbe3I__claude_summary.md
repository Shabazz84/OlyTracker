## Key Points

- **Clean rack position work** develops trapezius power and bar velocity; reveals stability deficits (athlete demonstrated 35 kg rack vs. 57–58 kg full clean—significant gap aligns with 50 kg OHS limitation).
- **Split jerk footwork issue**: pelvis shifts diagonally ("scissors" pattern); cue is neutral hips, smooth descent, load from stable base.
- **Pull mechanics**: initiate from legs/posterior chain (not arms); keep arms relaxed on straps, avoid premature trapezius/arm tension during deadlift pull; shoulder over bar pre-extension.
- **Squat positioning**: knee valgus correction (knees in); acceptable high starting position if knee angles preserved through drive phase—relevant to back pain management.
- **Training consistency** prioritized over intensity, especially when returning from breaks or managing fatigue around night-shift schedule.

## Technique Cues

1. **Clean/Rack Position**: Drive legs first, maintain bar proximity, achieve full extension before receiving.
2. **Split Jerk**: Keep pelvis neutral (avoid diagonal shift), descend smoothly, load from stable base.
3. **Deadlift/Pull**: Relax arms early, drive legs hard, delay arm engagement, shoulder slightly ahead of bar at liftoff.
4. **Squat**: Drive knees inward (valgus prevention), preserve knee angles through ascent; high start acceptable if mechanics intact.

## Programming Principles

- Structure 6-week hypertrophy block prioritizing **rack position work** and **accessory strength** to close OHS/stability gap.
- Emphasize **consistency over intensity** given night-shift fatigue context; manage volume accordingly.
- Include **split jerk footwork drills** and **OHS progressions** before full technique blocks.
