# Тяжелый рывок и толчок. В плане 90%, делаем 100!

### Key Points
*   **Back Pain Philosophy:** Chronic back issues (protrusions/hernias) should generally be addressed through strengthening the "muscular corset" via proper squats and weighted exercises rather than avoidance, provided there is no nerve compression. Strength training acts as rehabilitation.
*   **Jerk Limitations:** Athletes restricted to a push-jerk often lack the flexibility or joint mobility for a split jerk; success relies on maximizing rack position and upper body stability.
*   **Pull Mechanics:** Starting pulls too low (below knees) over-relies on leg strength at the expense of back strength, leading to failure if the posterior chain is underdeveloped. Pulling from a higher hang (above knee) better utilizes the stronger back muscles for many athletes.

### Technique Cues
*   **Bar Path & Rack:** If the bar falls forward onto the shoulders during a Clean or Snatch, the torso is leaning forward. Maintain an upright torso and ensure the bar rests high on the traps, not low on the chest.
*   **Squat Posture:** Avoid forward lean during squats to protect the spine; keep the spine straight with knees tracking over toes ("knees out").
*   **Jerk Drive:** Utilize the pectoral muscles to push the bar slightly forward rather than relying solely on arm extension.
