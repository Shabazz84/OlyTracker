# Weightlifting is not just about strength and explosiveness.

# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## Key Points
- Pull variations (power clean pulls, snatch pulls) serve as CNS activation and coordination warm-up work, not pure strength building
- Lower-level athletes show minimal performance difference between explosive pulls (подрыв) and continuous-tension pulls; continuous-tension is safer for technique practice
- Session structure: prioritize base work (squats, pulls) before maximal attempts; multiple attempts at same weight remain productive; failed attempts are training stimuli
- Intensity can flex to 80–85% based on readiness rather than strict percentage adherence
- Complete the planned session rather than exhaust on singles and skip accessory work

## Technique Cues
**Pull (Snatch/Clean Deadlift):**
- Emphasize leg drive over back extension; minimize lumbar hyperextension
- Drive hips upward but not excessively backward; maintain tall/upright posture
- Avoid excessive back extension (protects lumbar spine—critical for chronic back pain)

**Jerk/Press:**
- Drive immediately upon receiving
- Prioritize upright torso; minimize excessive back extension
- Avoid rocking forward excessively after catch

**Front Rack/Clean Grip:**
- Keep wrist lock tight throughout descent; don't prematurely release
- Avoid straps (they restrict wrist mobility needed for pressing)

**Squat/Receiving:**
- Control depth; don't over-settle or excessively bend knees

## Programming Principles
- Warm-up progressively from empty bar; don't rush percentages
- Prioritize squats and pulls as base-building exercises
- Adequate warm-up mandatory before intensity work
- Readiness-based adjustments over rigid percentages
- Mental resilience and visualization support technical execution

## Athlete Relevance
✅ **Chronic back pain**: Coach cues emphasize vertical pull posture and leg drive, minimizing lumbar hyperextension—directly protective.  
✅ **Low OHS (50 kg primary limiter)**: Likely wrist lock/front rack position issue; cues on tight wrist position and continuous-tension pull work address coordination/stability deficits.  
✅ **Weak OHS vs. clean strength gap**: Continuous-tension pull variations optimal for technique refinement before hypertrophy block.
