## Technique Cues

**Snatch Imitation (warm-up only):**
- Slow, controlled movement with light weight/bar
- Memorize body position through each phase: pre-pull → at knee → at hip → transition to catch
- **Must include bottom squat fixation (2–3 sec pause each rep)** for movement pattern completeness; minimal value without it

**Snatch Pull & Receiving:**
- Snatch pulls: pause in 10cm segments with heavy emphasis on tension
- Avoid premature forward transition; coordinate leg extension with pull timing
- Bar acceleration upward (not foot stomping)
- Keep bar close during dip; smooth entry (not jerky)

**Supplemental Drills:**
- 3-position pulls (floor, knee, mid-thigh); use straps for hang work
- Head position: neutral during pull, adjust at fixation as anatomically comfortable

## Programming Principles
- Professional athletes can skip fixations/stand-ups (energy conservation); beginners need full reps
- Jerk loaded once weekly; snatch work in mornings
- Mix of technical and strength work per session
- Afternoon/evening training preferred (post-lunch neurological readiness)
