# Merged Summary

## Key Points
- **Programming approach**: Percentage-based auto-regulation; treat training plan as a draft, adjusting daily based on readiness and body feedback
- **Post-competition fatigue**: Expect accumulated fatigue requiring programming adjustment
- **Quarter-squat snatch variants**: Effective for speed/power work; emphasizes bar height and extension before diving into full depth
- **Caution on half-squat work**: Risk of knee stress; cap intensity at ~70% and monitor joint health

## Technique Cues
- Snatch in quarter-squat: prioritize trap/leg extension and bar height before committing to deep squat (avoids rushing into depth)

## Programming Principles
- Auto-regulate percentages based on daily readiness rather than rigid adherence to planned loads
- Use mixed-movement days (snatch pull → quarter-squat → push press → squat combo) for speed/power development
- Adjust volume/intensity post-competition or during high fatigue periods
