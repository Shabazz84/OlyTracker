## Key Points
- **Periodization flexibility**: Transition from strict rep-range separation (hypertrophy: 8–12; strength: 2–6) to hybrid model: strength-focused work with metabolic-accent sets when volume is low.
- **Metabolic stress programming**: Add metabolic sets (8 reps @ moderate load) in lower-volume sessions; skip in higher-volume sessions to avoid redundant acidification.
- **Warm-up non-negotiable**: Extensive warm-ups are critical for injury prevention, motor pattern preparation, and don't fatigue trained athletes when kept aerobic/low HR.
