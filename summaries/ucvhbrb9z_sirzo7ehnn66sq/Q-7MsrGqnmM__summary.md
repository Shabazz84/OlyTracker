## Key Points

*   **Metabolic Stress Management:** Prioritize controlled accumulation of metabolic stress (acidosis) without inducing severe tissue damage or "lactic acid burns." Excessive acidosis correlates with high soreness and injury risk, particularly for those unaccustomed to high-rep work.
*   **Block Periodization:** Strictly separate training goals into distinct blocks. Do not mix heavy single attempts (strength recruitment) with high-rep metabolic stress work in the same session or block. The athlete must complete a dedicated hypertrophy phase before transitioning to low-rep strength work.
