- **Recovery:** Rest days (1–2 per week minimum) are essential; avoid excessive volume
- **NOT ideal for this athlete:** No periodization structure, intensity guidance, or night-shift accommodation

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Clean High Pull:** Keep elbows high/out, bar close to body, explosive hip extension
- **Kettlebell Clean:** Drive through hips, catch in rack position, elbow close to body
- **Hang Snatch:** Start at thighs, explosive hip/knee extension, catch overhead in deep squat with extended arms
- **Power Clean:** Hook grip, explosive pull, catch in half squat (not full squat)

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
Most relevant for this athlete:

| Exercise | Why | Note |
|----------|-----|------|
| **Clean High Pull** | Builds pulling power without wrist/shoulder stress; addresses weak turnover | Good for weak jerk |
| **Kettlebell Clean** | Easier on joints (chronic back pain); unilateral option | Lower barrier to entry |
| **Hang Snatch** | Improves shoulder stability; addresses OHS weakness (50 kg limiter) | Requires good form; valuable |
| **Single-arm dumbbell clean** | Addresses imbalances; lower back stress | Promising for symmetry |

**Not recommended:** Power clean (starts from floor; exacerbates back pain)
