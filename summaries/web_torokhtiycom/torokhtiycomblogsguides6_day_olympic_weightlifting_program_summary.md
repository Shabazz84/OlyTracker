# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/6-day-olympic-weightlifting-program

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 6 days/week (High volume for advanced athletes).
*   **Structure:** Daily specific warm-ups followed by main lifts; Day 6 is active recovery/mobility.
*   **Intensity/Volume:** Pyramid loading within sessions (e.g., 50% x1, 65% x3, 75% x1) and percentage-based progression (2-4% weekly increases).
*   **Periodization:** Deload every 4–6 weeks (reduce load by 40–50%).
*   **Note on Fit:** This is a **competition prep template** for advanced lifters, not an intermediate hypertrophy block. It lacks the high-volume bodybuilding accessory work required for your specific "hypertrophy first" goal and may exacerbate chronic back pain due to frequency.

**2. Technique Cues & Variations**
*   **Snatch:** Focus on "Muscle Snatch + OHS," "Balance," and "Hang Snatch above knee."
*   **Clean:** Emphasis on "Hip Clean Balance," "Clean from blocks," and "Power Clean" progression.
*   **Jerk:** Includes "Front Squat + Jerk" combinations; no specific split jerk cues (aligns with your push-jerk preference).
*   **General Cue:** "Warm Body Cold Mind" philosophy; prioritize safety over load.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Lifts:** Snatch, Clean & Jerk, Back Squat, Front Squat.
*   **Accessories:** Good Morning, Box Jump, Hyperextension/Reverse Hyperextension, Gakk Squat, Military Press with bands, Sots Press, Barbell Squat Jumps (15-20kg).
*   **Mobility:** PVC drills for elbows rotation and overhead stability.

**4. Recovery & Mobility Advice**
*   **Routine:** Mandatory 10–15 min warm-up and cool-down/stretching after *every* session.
*   **Day 6 Protocol:** 20 min light cardio + 20 min focused stretching/flexibility for worked muscle groups.
*   **Strategy:** "Listen to your body," rotate accessory work every 4–6 weeks, and film lifts for self-assessment.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile (Critical Analysis)**
*   **❌ Not Suitable as-Is:** This program is designed for **advanced athletes** preparing for competition, not an intermediate lifter needing a hypertrophy block. It does not address your chronic back pain or night shift schedule.
*   **⚠️ Back Pain Risk:** The high frequency (6 days) and inclusion of heavy "Good Mornings" and "Back Squats with pause" without specific mobility modifications could aggravate your chronic back issues.
*   **❌ Hypertrophy Gap:** Lacks the volume/intensity for a dedicated 6-week hypertrophy block; it focuses on technical refinement and strength peaks.
*   **⚠️ Night Shift Conflict:** A rigid 6-day schedule offers no flexibility for shift work recovery or sleep management, which is critical given your night shift (Wed–Sun).
*   **✅ Snatch Limiter:** The heavy emphasis on OHS and overhead stability drills directly targets your specific weakness (50kg OHS), but the volume may be too high initially.

**Recommendation:** Do not start this program immediately. Adapt it by reducing frequency to 3-4 days, removing "Good Mornings" for back safety, and inserting a dedicated hypertrophy block before attempting this intensity.
