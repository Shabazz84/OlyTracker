# Analysis: Session Duration & Recovery Framework

**Relevance to Athlete Profile:** General programming principles applicable; some guidance useful for night-shift scheduling and chronic back pain management.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Session Duration:** 45–90 min optimal for hypertrophy blocks (athlete's stated 6-week goal)
- **Frequency:** Intermediate athletes = 4–5 days/week (strength) or 4–6 days/week (hypertrophy)
- **Rest Intervals:** Hypertrophy = 1.5–2 min between sets; strength = 3–5 min
- **Volume Focus:** Hypertrophy programs require higher set volume across multiple exercises per muscle group

**Athlete Context:** Night-shift worker (Wed–Sun) needs condensed 45–60 min sessions with strategic compound focus to manage fatigue and back stress.

## 4. **Recovery & Mobility Advice**
- **48–72 hours** between full-body sessions
- **Active recovery days:** Light walking, cycling, stretching, mobility routines
- **Sleep & nutrition critical** for recovery acceleration
- **Overtraining signals:** Unusual fatigue, soreness >72 hrs, decreased performance, insomnia

**Critical for this athlete:** Sleep disruption (night shift) + chronic back pain = prioritize active recovery and mobility on off-days.
