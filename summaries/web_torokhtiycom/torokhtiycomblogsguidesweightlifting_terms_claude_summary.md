# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Reference for Intermediate Olympic Lifter

## Key Points

- **Block Periodization**: Your 6-week hypertrophy block directly precedes technique/strength phases—structured progression.
- **Deloading**: Mandatory given chronic back pain and night shift fatigue; plan reduced intensity/volume weeks to prevent overtraining.
- **Progressive Overload**: During hypertrophy phase, increase weight, reps, sets, or reduce rest to drive adaptation.
- **Range of Motion (ROM)**: Critical for OHS deficit (50 kg); full ROM supports muscle growth and injury resilience, especially with back pain history.
- **Time Under Tension (TUT)**: Slow, controlled movements during hypertrophy phase enhance muscle gains.

## Technique Cues

- **Arm Bending (Snatch/Clean)**: Keep arms straight until power position; early bending reduces efficiency and injury risk.
- **Finish of Pull**: Fully extend hips, knees, ankles for maximum bar height before catch.
- **Fixation (Split Jerk Prep)**: Lock elbows, maintain rigid torso overhead—essential foundation before transitioning from push jerk.
- **Full Extension Position**: Prerequisite for optimal bar height; directly impacts catch position.
- **Core Stability**: Essential for managing chronic back pain during heavy lifts; proper bracing non-negotiable.

## Programming Principles

- **Frequency**: Balance session frequency with intensity/volume to avoid overtraining—prioritize recovery given night shift schedule.
- **Active Recovery**: Low-intensity work (cycling, yoga) between sessions promotes recovery and reduces soreness; suits night shift worker.
- **Isolation Movements**: Address asymmetries and shoulder stability to resolve OHS limiter.
- **Block Pulls/Cleans**: Reduced ROM variation useful for managing chronic back pain while building second pull strength.
- **Plyometrics**: Post-hypertrophy block for power development during strength/technique phase.

## Athlete Relevance

- **OHS Limiter (50 kg)**: Prioritize shoulder mobility/stability work and dynamic stretching before sessions; static stretching post-workout. Isolation exercises for shoulder/thoracic mobility essential.
- **Chronic Back Pain**: Avoid overreaching; emphasize deload weeks, proper bracing, core stability, and reduced ROM variations (blocks). Full warm-up/cool-down protocol non-negotiable.
- **Night Shift Worker (Wed–Sun)**: Emphasize active recovery sessions; deload strategically to manage fatigue accumulation.
- **Push Jerk → Split Jerk Transition**: Build fixation strength and overhead stability as secondary strength phase begins; split jerk introduces ROM demands requiring mobility prep now.
- **Strong Back Squat (118 kg)**: Leverage relative quad strength; ankle mobility (consider weightlifting shoes) supports deeper positions for snatch technique.
