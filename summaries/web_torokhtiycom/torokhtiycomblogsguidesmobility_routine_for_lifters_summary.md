**3. Exercise recommendations:**
*   **Warm-up/Pre-lift:** Dynamic stretching, 27 Squats (varied foot positions), Wall Slides.
*   **Post-workout/Recovery:** Foam rolling, Static stretching (hold >60s for flexibility/reduction of tension).
*   **Specific Drills:** 90/90 PAILs and RAILs (hip mobility), Deep Squat with Single-Arm Overhead Reach (thoracic rotation/squat depth).

**4. Recovery or mobility advice:**
*   Perform dynamic stretching *before* lifting to activate muscles; save static stretching for *after* the session to maximize flexibility gains without acute strength loss.
*   Use foam rolling post-workout to reduce tension and improve circulation.
*   Target hips, shoulders, ankles, wrists, and core stability.

**5. Relevance to athlete profile:**
*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The text explicitly links poor mobility in the spine/hips to lower back pain/injury; the "Deep Squat with Overhead Reach" and "Wall Slides" are highly relevant for thoracic extension and core stability.
*   **OHS Limiter (50kg):** The "Wall Slides" and overhead reach drills directly address shoulder/thoracic mobility limitations often found in the snatch/jerk overhead positions.
*   **Transition to Olympic Lifting:** The article emphasizes that mobility is a prerequisite for proper technique, which aligns with the athlete's need to fix form before increasing load.
