# Analysis: Upper Body Mobility for Weightlifting

**Relevance to Athlete Profile:** HIGH — Directly applicable.

---

## Key Extracts

### 1. **Recovery/Mobility Advice (PRIMARY VALUE)**
- **Thoracic Rotation** (quadruped): Essential for this athlete — addresses spinal mobility critical for chronic back pain and overhead positions (Snatch OHS limiter at 50 kg).
- **Shoulder Pass-Through** (with stick): Directly improves shoulder mobility needed for snatch receiving position and jerk dip.
- **Cat-Camel**: Spinal segmental mobility + posture — relevant for back pain management during training.
- **High-to-Low Rows** (resistance band): Upper back/thoracic stability for overhead work.
- **Dumbbell External Rotation**: Shoulder stability for jerk/snatch mechanics.
- **Standing Arm Swings**: Dynamic shoulder prep suitable for pre-session warmup.

**Timeline:** 2 weeks to feel meaningful changes with consistent use.

---

## 2. **Relevance to Specific Limitations**
- **OHS @ 50 kg (primary snatch limiter)**: Thoracic rotation + shoulder pass-through directly address ROM constraints preventing heavier overhead positions.
- **Chronic Back Pain**: Cat-camel + thoracic work reduce spinal stiffness; mobility precedes strength during hypertrophy block.
- **Night Shift (Wed–Sun)**: These mobility drills are short, equipment-minimal, suit fatigue recovery days.

---

## 3. **What's Missing for This Athlete**
- Hip/ankle mobility (critical for Snatch receiving position, Clean catch).
- Lower back-specific mobility (relevant to stated chronic pain).
- No programming periodization (frequency/timing within 6-week block).

---

## **Recommendation**
Integrate **thoracic rotation + shoulder pass-through + external rotation** 3–4x weekly as warm-up/recovery tool. Prioritize before OHS/Snatch sessions to unlock overhead mobility plateau.
