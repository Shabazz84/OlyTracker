# Analysis: Stretching for Weightlifting

**Relevance to athlete:** Highly relevant. This is a mobility/stretching guide applicable to Olympic weightlifting.

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency:** Stretch all major muscle groups 2–3× weekly
- **Duration per stretch:** 30–60s before workout; 2–3 min after
- **Pre-workout:** Dynamic stretches only (arm swings, leg swings, torso twists, kneeling T-openers); 2 sets × 15 reps
- **Post-workout:** Static stretches (hamstrings, hip/quad, lower back, chest, triceps)
- **Periodization note:** Mobility addressed in 3 categories—soft tissue mobilization, joint mobilizations, motor control. Pre-workout focuses on movement (categories 2–3); soft tissue work can impair performance if overdone.

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- None specific to snatch, clean, or jerk technique provided.

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**Dynamic (Pre-workout):**
- Arm swings (shoulder prep)
- Leg swings (hip flexor/hamstring activation)
- Torso twists (spinal rotation)
- Kneeling T-opener (thoracic mobility + shoulder)

**Static (Post-workout):**
- Sitting hamstring stretch
- Pigeon quad stretch (especially valuable for hip ROM limitations)
- V sitting stretch (lower back + hamstring focus)
- Chest stretch
- Leaning triceps stretch

---

## 4. **Recovery & Mobility Advice**
✅ **Critical for this athlete:**
- Static stretching reduces DOMS and accelerates recovery—important post-night-shift sessions
- **V sitting stretch** and **pigeon quad** directly address lower back tightness and hip ROM restrictions (limiting OHS @ 50 kg)
- Post-workout static stretches flush metabolic byproducts and initiate muscle repair
- Emphasis: joint mobility + stability balance needed; sometimes lack of ROM is joint, not muscular

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **Chronic back pain:** V sitting stretch, torso twists, kneeling T-opener prioritized
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Pigeon quad + thoracic mobility (kneeling T-opener) directly target snatch position prerequisites
- **Night shift recovery:** Post-workout static routine especially valuable for accelerating between-session recovery
- **Training block alignment:** Mobility work pairs well with planned 6-week hypertrophy block (tissue prep) before strength/technique focus

---

**Bottom line:** Actionable mobility framework. Prioritize pigeon quad stretch, kneeling T-opener, and V sitting stretch post-workout given OHS/back pain profile.
