# Weightlifting Analysis: 10-Week Olympic Weightlifting Program

## Relevant Extractions for Your Athlete Profile

### 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Weeks 1-2**: Foundation building (moderate intensity, technical focus)
- **Weeks 3-6**: Technique refinement & strength; 2-3% load increase weekly
- **Weeks 7-8**: Power emphasis at 65-75% 1RM, reduced reps (2-3), maintain intensity
- **Weeks 9-10**: Testing/peaking at 80-90% 1RM
- **Recovery**: Deload every 5-6 weeks; prioritize recovery between sessions
- **Frequency**: 3 days/week shown (Day 1: Snatch focus, Day 2: Clean focus, Day 3: Jerk + Squat)

### 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch/Clean/Jerk)**
- **Snatch**: Muscle snatch (turnover), power snatch + hip snatch (bar trajectory, under-the-bar speed), snatch push press + balance (overhead stability, drop-under)
- **Clean**: Power clean + hang power clean (mechanics, transitions), clean pulls at 95-105% (explosive pull strength)
- **Jerk**: Push press + jerk, then jerk at 80-85% for 6×3 (overhead stability emphasis)

### 3. **Accessory Work**
- Pause back squat (core/leg strength)
- Front squat at 90-100% C&J
- Split squats (single-leg stability)
- Barbell squat jumps (plyometrics)
- Hyperextensions, mobility/stretching 10-15 min daily

### 4. **Recovery & Mobility**
- Daily 10-15 min mobility/stretching post-session
- Deload weeks every 5-6 weeks
- Film sessions for form checks
- Listen to body; reduce load if technique falters

### 5. **Athlete-Specific Considerations**
⚠️ **Critical concerns for your profile**:
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Program addresses overhead stability well; front squats at high %C&J should help
- **Chronic back pain**: Pause squats (controlled tempo) and hyperextensions may aggravate—consider modifications or sub alternatives
- **Night shift worker**: 3x/week frequency is manageable; prioritize recovery sleep despite schedule
- **Push jerk only**: Program includes split jerk progressions; you'll need split jerk development post-foundation phase
- **Your stated goal (6-week hypo block first)**: This program skips that; consider modifying weeks 1-2 to emphasize volume/metabolic work

**Program fit: Moderate.** Good structure for your level, but adjust for back pain and consider pre-loading with hypertrophy block.
