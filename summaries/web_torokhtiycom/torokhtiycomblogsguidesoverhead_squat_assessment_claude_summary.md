# Analysis: OHS Assessment Article

**Relevance to athlete:** HIGH — directly addresses the athlete's primary snatch limiter (OHS 50 kg).

## Key Extracts:

**1. Exercise Recommendations (for OHS mobility deficits)**
- Calf stretches, hamstring stretches, single-leg balance reach
- Ball squats, lateral tube walking
- Reference NASM chart for lumbo-pelvic-hip complex (LPHC) corrections

**2. Assessment Protocol (relevant to diagnose OHS limitations)**
- Anterior view: foot/knee shifting
- Lateral view: back straightness, arm/shoulder position
- Posterior view: heel stability, asymmetrical weight shift
- **Critical:** Assess barefoot (removes shoe compensation)

**3. Technique Cue**
- Arms follow straight line from back; check for forward fall/overextension
- Maintain neutral back (avoid excessive forward lean, arching, or rounding)

**4. Specific to Athlete Profile**
- LPHC issues "toughest to correct" — relevant given **chronic back pain**
- OHS exposes mobility gaps across kinetic chain — explains 50 kg ceiling vs. 118 kg back squat
