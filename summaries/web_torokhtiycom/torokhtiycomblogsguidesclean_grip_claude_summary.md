# Analysis: Clean Grip Article

## Relevant to Athlete Profile

**1. Technique Cues (Clean)**
- **Hook grip vs. overhand:** Recommends hook grip for progression (more secure, better turnover, eliminates beginner mistakes like slow turnover and early arm bend). Article notes 2-week adaptation period for thumb pain—relevant given athlete's intermediate status and need to progress beyond current 80 kg clean.
- **Grip width:** Slightly wider than shoulder-width; individual variation based on proportions. Critical for:
  - Point of contact (upper thigh/hip for power generation)
  - Front rack position (high elbows, secure shoulder rest)
  - Bar path control
- **Setup cue:** "Thumb-length from thigh when arms at side" for initial width reference
- **Common mistake:** Gripping too tightly → stiff arms, less fluid lift. **Key for this athlete:** Keep grip firm but arms relaxed
