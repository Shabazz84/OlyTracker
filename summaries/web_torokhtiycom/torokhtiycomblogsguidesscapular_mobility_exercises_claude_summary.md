## EXTRACTION:

**1. Programming Principles**
- None specific to periodization, sets/reps, or intensity. General recovery/mobility work only.

**2. Technique Cues**
- None for snatch, clean, or jerk.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- **Shoulder blade squeezes** (10–15 reps, isometric 5–10 sec)
- **Wall angels** (10–15 reps, full ROM with wall contact)
- **Prone T-raises with resistance bands** (10–15 reps, 5–10 sec hold)
- Emphasis: start light/bodyweight, progress resistance slowly, maintain strict form.

**4. Recovery & Mobility Advice**
- Warm up with dynamic stretches (arm circles, shoulder rolls) before mobility work.
- Stop immediately if pain occurs; consult physical therapist.
- Timeline: 2–6 weeks to see mobility gains depending on frequency.
- Benefits listed: improved posture, reduced back/shoulder pain, increased ROM.

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- ✅ **Directly applicable:** Chronic back pain + weak OHS (50 kg) = scapular mobility/stability deficits likely limiting overhead strength. Wall angels & prone T-raises address postural dysfunction and shoulder stability.
- ✅ **Night shift recovery:** Low-effort mobility work fits rest days (Wed–Sun schedule).
- ⚠️ **Gap:** Article doesn't link scapular work to Olympic lifting mechanics or integrate into 6-week hypertrophy block.

**RECOMMENDATION:** Use these 3 exercises as **daily/EOD mobility work** (5–10 min) during hypertrophy block to prep for jerk progression and OHS improvements.
