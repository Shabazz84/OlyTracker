# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/3-day-olympic-weightlifting-program

### 1. Programming Principles
*   **Frequency:** 3 days/week (Day 1: Snatch focus; Day 2: Clean & Jerk focus; Day 3: Accessory/Strength).
*   **Periodization Structure:** The text outlines a generic 16-week block:
    *   Weeks 1-4: Foundation (Technique, lighter weights).
    *   Weeks 5-8: Strength Development (Higher intensity, variations like deficit pulls).
    *   Weeks 9-12: Power/Speed (Lower weight, faster pace).
    *   Weeks 13-16: Peaking (Low volume, high intensity singles/doubles).
*   **Intensity & Volume:** Specific session examples use percentages of 1RM (e.g., Back Squat progression: 50% x 4, 65% x 3x4, 75% x 4 reps). General advice suggests increasing weights conservatively by 2-3% weekly.
*   **Progression:** Linear progression within blocks with deload weeks every 4-6 weeks.

### 2. Technique Cues
*   **Snatch High Pull:** Focus on "explosive hip extension and vertical bar path."
*   **Power Snatch:** Work in "full extension and aggressive catch."
*   **Muscle Squat Clean:** Focus on "bar path feeling and elbows rotation."
*   **Push Press:** Control the dip & drive; enhance shoulder strength.
*   **General:** Maintain a vertical bar path during pulls.

### 3. Exercise Recommendations
*   **Snatch Day:** Snatch High Pull, Power Snatch (up to 70%), Overhead Squat (up to 60%), Snatch Deadlifts (up to 90%).
*   **C&J Day:** Muscle Squat Clean, Power Clean + Jerk (up to 70%), Clean Pull (up to 90%), Front Squat (up to 90%).
*   **Accessory/Strength Day:** Middle grip Muscle Snatch, Push Press, Deficit Romanian Deadlift, Back Squat (up to 90%), Rows.

### 4. Recovery or Mobility Advice
*   **Warm-up:** Mandatory dynamic stretches and mobility drills for shoulders, hips, ankles, wrists, and full-body activation before each session.
*   **Accessory Work:** Dedicated core, grip, and mobility drills at the end of sessions (up to 10 mins).
*   **Recovery Protocols:** Prioritize quality sleep; deload every 4-6 weeks; listen to body for fatigue/injury signs; adequate rest is "non-negotiable."

### 5. Relevance to Athlete Profile

*   **Chronic Back Pain:** The program prescribes heavy Deadlifts (Snatch/Back Squat up to 90%) and high-volume squats without specific modifications for back pain or alternatives to spinal loading.

*   **OHS Limiter (50kg):** The text suggests Overhead Squats at 60% of max, but for an athlete with a 50kg OHS limit, this may be too heavy or lack the specific mobility work needed to break through that ceiling without aggravating the back.
