# https://torokhtiy.com/blogs/guides/8-week-power-clean-program

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 3 sessions per week (Day 1, Day 2, Day 3).
*   **Structure:** 8-week linear progression model (Foundation → Intensification → Power/Strength).
    *   *Weeks 1-4:* Focus on technique and volume; intensity increases 2-3% weekly. Week 4 is a deload/recovery block (-10-15% volume/intensity).
    *   *Weeks 5-8:* Shift to high-intensity (75–85% 1RM), low volume (2–3 reps), focusing on speed and explosiveness. Ends with max-out testing in Week 8.
*   **Intensity:** Starts light/medium for mechanics; progresses to heavy loads for power.
*   **Periodization:** Block periodization moving from technical mastery to strength/power application.

**2. Technique Cues & Focus Areas**
*   **Bar Path:** Emphasize "reinforce the bar path" during high pulls (above/below knee).
*   **Timing/Catching:** Use "Hang Power Clean above & below knee" drills specifically to improve catch mechanics and timing.
*   **Transition:** "Clean Pull + Power Clean" complexes are used to refine the transition from pull to squat.
*   **Initial Pull:** Deficit Power Cleans (65% 1RM) are prescribed to develop a strong initial pull and full range of motion.
*   **General Rule:** Every rep must be performed with "solid technique"; prioritize form over load to prevent injury.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Olympic Variations:** Power Clean, Hang Power Clean (above/below knee), Deficit Power Clean, Clean High Pulls, Muscle Cleans, Clean Pulls.
*   **Strength & Accessory:** Gakk Squat, Front Squat, Paused Back Squat, Barbell Squat Jumps, Single-leg RDL, Reverse Lunges, Front Foot Elevated Split Squat, Good Mornings, Lat Rows, Pull-ups.
*   **Mobility/Prehab:** Reverse Hyperextension, Bird Dog, Elbow Stretch & Rotation (with rubber band/PVC), Total Body Mobility circuits.

**4. Recovery or Mobility Advice**
*   **Frequency:** 10–15 minutes of dedicated mobility/stretching at the end of every session.
*   **Specific Focus:** Shoulders and hips (Day 1), Wrists/hips/shoulders (Day 2), Hips/back (Day 3).
*   **Deload Strategy:** Explicitly plan Week 4 as a recovery block to prevent overtraining. Weeks 7–8 include tapering before the final max-out test.

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile (Intermediate, Back Pain, Night Shift)**
*   **Back Pain Mitigation:** The program heavily emphasizes **Reverse Hyperextensions**, **Bird Dogs**, and **Paused Squats**. These are excellent for stabilizing the spine and reducing compressive load on the lumbar region compared to heavy conventional squats.
*   **Night Shift Adaptation:** The text recommends "Total Body Mobility" and specific warm-ups (10–15 mins) which can be adjusted to the athlete's wakefulness window. However, the program assumes a standard 3-day split; for a Wed–Sun night shift worker, this requires shifting training days to align with peak energy/alertness rather than a fixed Mon/Wed/Fri schedule.
*   **Limitation:** The text focuses exclusively on the **Power Clean**. It does not address the athlete's specific **Snatch** or **Jerk (Push Jerk)** limitations (OHS 50kg). While the pulling mechanics transfer, the athlete will need to supplement this with dedicated Snatch and Push Jerk technique work to meet their specific goals.
*   **Hypertrophy vs. Power:** The text is a "Power Clean Program" focused on explosive power and strength, not pure hypertrophy. For the requested 6-week *hypertrophy* block first, the athlete would need to modify this program significantly (increasing volume, lowering intensity to 60-70%, adding more bodybuilding accessories) before transitioning into this specific power-focused cycle.
