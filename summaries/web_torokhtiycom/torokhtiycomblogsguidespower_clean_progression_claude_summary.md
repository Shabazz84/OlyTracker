## 1. Programming Principles
- **Frequency:** 1–3× per week (OLY context: 1×/week; functional fitness: 2–3×/week)
- **Intensity:** 60–80% of 1RM Squat Clean for 1–3 reps
- **Volume progression:** Increase sets/reps, load, tempo, or reduce rest
- **Periodization:** Integrate into strength/conditioning sessions with adequate recovery
- **Note:** No hypertrophy-block specific guidance (relevant to athlete's stated 6-week goal)

---

## 2. Technique Cues
**Clean-specific:**
- Establish stance: shoulder-width, toes slightly out, weight even
- Grip: hookgrip, elbows just outside knees at setup
- Chest up, elbows high in front rack
- Strong back, engaged lats, hip-driven second pull
- Bar close to body; smooth pull-to-catch transition
- Catch in stable half-squat position

---

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
**Progression sequence:**
Front Squat → Hang Clean → Clean Pull → Muscle Clean (hip) → Power Clean (hip) → Hang Power Clean (above knees) → Knee Pause Clean → Full Power Clean

**Supporting work:** Front squats, deadlifts, plyometrics, complexes (clean + front squat, etc.)

---

- Limited mobility may cause initial struggle with muscle clean elbow wrapping; improves over time
- Emphasizes consistent practice over speed of progression
