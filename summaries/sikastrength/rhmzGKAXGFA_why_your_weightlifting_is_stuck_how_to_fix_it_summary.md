# Analysis: Weightlifting GPP Framework

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**CRITICAL ISSUE - Back Pain & Programming:**
- Text emphasizes GPP (General Physical Preparedness) as primary limiter, not just strength
- Your chronic back pain + night shift fatigue = **work capacity is your biggest lever**, not heavy strength work yet
- Recommendation aligns: 6-week hypertrophy block first is sound

**Work Capacity (Most Urgent for You):**
- As an older/intermediate lifter with limited training history, you need **dedicated work capacity blocks** at start of training phases
- Conditioning 2x/week: assault bike "10-10s" (10 cal × 10 sets, minimal rest) OR zone 2-3 steady-state (15-45 min)
- **After dedicated block:** maintain with 15 min 2x/week (zone 2-3), add 5 min monthly
- Keep snatch/clean/jerk at ~70%, 1-2x/week during this phase

**Strength Block Trigger (For Later):**
- Don't chase ratios; monitor: Are snatch/clean/jerk/back squat/front squat all trending up?
- If **all plateau**, then add dedicated strength work (coordinate with work capacity block)
- Your issue likely isn't strength—it's capacity (you're gassed after power snatch, can't squat after)

## Exercise Recommendations

**Assistance Work (High Priority for OHS Limiter):**
- Pick 3 weakest areas: **overhead stability, ankle mobility, quad strength** for your profile
- Start: 4-5 × 10-12 reps, very light, end-of-session
- Progress slowly to 5-8 reps over weeks; don't fatigue yourself

**Mobility (Non-Negotiable):**
1. **Ankle distraction stretch** (band + light weight, 30+ sec)
2. **Supported bodyweight squat hold** (10 min accumulated, vary foot angles)
3. **Foam roller overhead stretch** (before every session)
- Mobility is "game of millimeters"—requires consistency, not just warm-up ability

**Speed Work:**
- Box jumps: 4-5 sets, 1x/week (low fatigue cost for night shift worker)
- Gymnastics basics (handstands, rolling): 1-2x/week, casual

**Coordination:**
- Basic gymnastics skills 1-2x/week (tumbling, cartwheels)—low resource demand

## Programming Principles for Your 6-Week Hypertrophy Block

1. **Start with work capacity** (first 2-3 weeks): conditioning 2x/week + higher volume squats/pulls
2. **Snatch/clean/jerk**: maintain only at ~70%, 1-2x/week
3. **Assistance focus**: OHS, ankle mobility, quad work (4-5 × 10-12, progress slowly)
4. **Monitor**: Can you complete squat work after Olympic lift sessions? If no—work capacity issue, not strength
5. **Avoid**: Extended time away from snatch/C&J (will detrain technique)

## Recovery Note
- Night shift + chronic back: prioritize sleep consistency, warm-up extension, mobility work timing (before sessions)
