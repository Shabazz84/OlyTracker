# Don't Make This Mistake On Your First 100kg Snatch! #holidaywithshorts #weightlifting

# Analysis

**Programming/Intensity:**
- Warm-up structure: 70, 75, 80 kg before maxing
- Maintain consistency through ~90% before attempting max

**Technique Cues (Snatch):**
- Avoid common warm-up deviations:
  - Don't rip bar off ground too quickly (early aggression)
  - Don't shoot hips up prematurely (loss of leverage)
  - Don't leave knees too far forward in first pull
  - Don't smash bar with hips
- Execute "crispy clean and perfect reps" at 70, 75, 80 kg
- Problems: Athletes often power snatch cleanly to 80% then introduce technical quirks when "amped up" or stimulated, causing disconnect at 90%+

**Relevance to Athlete:**
- **Critical for this athlete:** OHS 50 kg is primary snatch limiter—technical precision in warm-ups essential before attempting maxes
- Night shift fatigue may increase risk of technical breakdown; enforce strict warm-up discipline
- Chronic back pain + premature hip drive/poor positioning = injury risk; "crispy" reps non-negotiable

**Bottom line:** Nail technical consistency through warm-up loads; don't introduce new technical patterns when fatigued/stimulated near max attempts.
