## 2. Technique Cues: Clean

**Toe Angle (First Pull):**
- **Toes straight vs. toes out** are both viable; choose based on individual leverages
- Toes straight → knees more forward, better quad drive, stronger off floor
- Toes out → knees naturally back, easier knee clearance, better for long-limbed lifters
- **For this athlete**: Long limbs + chronic back pain suggest **toes-out may be preferable** to reduce anterior knee stress and excessive forward lean

**Barbell Path (First & Second Pull):**
- Must be a **sweeping S-curve back toward you**, not straight up
- Barbell moves back off floor → continues back through second pull → stays close to body at contact
- Shoulder must stay **in front of barbell** throughout
- **Key cue**: "Sweeping back" motion; avoid excessive forward hinge that causes early hip rise
- Check objectively: Does barbell move away from body at knee? If yes, technique is breaking down

---

## 3. Exercise Recommendations

**Recommended variations for technical work:**
- Clean pulls (standard)
- Clean deadlifts
- **Pause cleans at knee** (diagnostic + strengthening position-specific weakness)
- Low-hand cleans (if athlete prefers squat-like tempo)

**Rationale**: Extra reps with reduced load stress allow focus on first/second pull without systemic fatigue.

## Key Takeaway
**Diagnostic focus**: Film cleans from side angle. Check barbell path at knee. If barbell drifts forward, prioritize pull variations + pause cleans before adding intensity.
