# Coherent Summary: Weightlifting Programming & Technique for This Athlete

## Key Points

- **6-week hypertrophy block framework:** Progress rep ranges (10s→8s→6s) over 2-week windows, gradually reducing volume while increasing intensity. Avoid static volume throughout the cycle.
- **Work capacity directly impacts technical consistency:** Target 90-second to 2.5-minute rest between sets (vs. 5+ minutes) to manage night-shift fatigue and reduce CNS fatigue.
- **Avoid excessive deadlifting:** Core/midline stress aggravates chronic back pain; prioritize squat and Olympic lift variations instead.
- **Maintain Olympic lift frequency during hypertrophy block:** 1–2× snatch + 1× clean & jerk per week at 75–80% intensity while building conditioning capacity.
- **OHS (50 kg) is primary snatch limiter:** Limited by upper body stability/mobility and back squat positioning, not just overhead strength.

## Technique Cues

**Back Squat/OHS Positioning (addresses chronic back pain):**
- **Descent check:** Verify quad/hip/ankle mobility independently. If back angle *changes* during descent → mobility deficit. If angle holds at bottom but trunk shoots upright → quad/strength deficit.
- **Common error:** Knees collapse backward, hips scoop forward → "good morning" squat overloads lumbar spine.
- **Cue:** Drive knees aggressively *forward* out of bottom; maintain anterior tibial tension and leg tightness throughout ascent.
- **Priority sequence:** Ankle mobility → quad/back strength consistency → OHS progression.

## Programming Principles

- Standard block: 2 weeks per rep range before transitioning.
- Manipulate sets to manage intensity jumps (e.g., 4×6 → 3×6 heavier → 4×6 next session).
- Maintain Olympic lift technical work (1–2× snatch, 1× C&J weekly at moderate intensity) *concurrent* with hypertrophy/conditioning phases.
- Strategic aerobic work (3–4× weekly) improves recovery capacity without compromising strength gains—beneficial for night-shift recovery if scheduled appropriately.

## Athlete Relevance

- **Chronic back pain + forward-leaning squat:** Address ankle/hip mobility and quad strength before pursuing higher OHS loads; prioritize positioning over load.
- **Night shift fatigue:** Shorter rest intervals (90–150 sec) improve training efficiency; build work capacity to reduce metabolic fatigue between sets.
- **Split jerk progression:** Source does not address split jerk cues; continue push jerk until squat/OHS strength and positioning improve.
- **6-week hypertrophy block:** Use 10s→8s→6s progression framework with maintained Olympic lift frequency, then transition to 6s→4s strength phase.
