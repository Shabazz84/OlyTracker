## Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:

**1. Snatch Technique (Knee Passage/Bar Path)**
- Check shin angle at knee—aim for **perpendicular to floor**
- Shoulders should be **on or in front of bar** at knee
- Common error: **hips come forward too early** (should go up first)
- Pause snatches help expose this
- *Relevant:* Athlete has 50 kg OHS (primary limiter); bar path issues compound this weakness

**2. Back Pain / Recovery Principle**
- Speaker emphasizes: **find the root cause, don't just rest and resume**
- Unilateral pain suggests mobility/strength deficit, not just load tolerance
- Recommend: see a professional (PT/physio) before progressing
- *Directly relevant:* Athlete has chronic back pain; needs assessment before 6-week hypertrophy block

**3. Programming Note (Push Press as Example)**
- Front squat + strict press are primary drivers, not more push press volume
- Practice push press 1–2×/week only
- *Transferable principle:* Accessory selection matters more than direct work

**4. Nutrition / Body Weight**
- Don't fast before competition (glycogen depletion hurts most athletes)
- Avoid prolonged low body weight; recomposition better than long cuts
- *Relevant:* Night shift worker needs consistent pre-training nutrition
