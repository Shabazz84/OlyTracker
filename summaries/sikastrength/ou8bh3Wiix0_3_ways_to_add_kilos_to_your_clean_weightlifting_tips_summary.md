# 3 Ways To Add Kilos To Your Clean! (Weightlifting Tips)

# Weightlifting Analysis: Clean Technique & Common Errors

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Slow pull cleans**: 4-5 sets of 2-3 reps (warm-up or dedicated session). Light weight, emphasis on practice over intensity.
- **Power cleans to parallel**: Catch at parallel depth—don't ride down from high catch.
- **Hang cleans**: Can train at 85-90%+ of best clean; heavier loading improves efficiency.
- **No contact cleans**: 4-5 sets of 3 reps at 40-50% of clean, can be done every clean/jerk session.

## 2. **Technique Cues: Clean**

**Early Arm Bend** (priority fix for this athlete)
- Maintain anatomical arm bend until extension point; any *premature large change* kills bar speed
- Reduces launch velocity → barbell won't reach shoulders
- Shifts load from legs/back to arms (disadvantageous)
- Fix: "Stop doing it"—lighter weight + perfect reps, or repeat set. Slow pull cleans as practice.

**Overpulling**
- Minimize vertical distance between highest pull and catch position
- Mark of efficiency in advanced lifters; novices pull excessively high
- Fix: Power cleans to parallel; above-knee hang cleans at heavy loading (85-90%+)

**Hip Smash**
- Creates horizontal bar path deflection → slows barbell, ruins timing, causes crash
- Fix: **No contact cleans** (40-50%, every session). Cue: minimal visible distance from hips to bar; aggressive upper body/trap engagement.

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Slow pull cleans (warm-up tool)
- Power cleans → catch at parallel
- Hang cleans (emphasize pulling under without arm bend; go heavy 85-90%+)
- No contact cleans (can pair every clean/jerk session at 40-50%)

## 4. **Relevant to This Athlete's Profile**

**Critical for 102.5kg athlete with 80kg clean (80% of bodyweight), 50kg OHS, and chronic back pain:**

- **Early arm bend** directly undermines clean progress and likely contributes to form breakdown → back stress
- **OHS limiter (50kg)**: Overpulling and poor turnover exacerbate weak front rack positions; hip smash worsens shoulder stability demand
- **Chronic back pain**: Hip smash and poor pullunder mechanics increase horizontal shear forces on lumbar spine. Slow pulls + no-contact cleans reduce violent contact stress.
- **Night shift fatigue**: Hang cleans (heavier, shorter ROM) more sustainable than full-movement work; slow pulls require low intensity.
