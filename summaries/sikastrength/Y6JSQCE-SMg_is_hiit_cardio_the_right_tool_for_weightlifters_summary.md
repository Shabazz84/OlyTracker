# Is HIIT Cardio The Right Tool For Weightlifters?

# Analysis: Nutrition & Conditioning for Weight Loss

**Relevance to athlete:** Moderate—addresses body composition but not technique or Olympic lifting specifics.

---

## Key Points Extracted:

### Programming/Conditioning Principles
- **Priority: Diet first.** Assess caloric intake and macros before adding conditioning work
- **Avoid HIIT for weightlifters.** High-intensity interval training causes excessive fatigue and muscle loss during strength phases
- **Preferred conditioning:** Low-intensity, high-frequency activity (e.g., 15-min walks 3×/day, 6x/week)
- **Maximize NEAT:** Parking further, stairs, incidental movement > formal exercise for fat loss
- **Reserve high-volume work (5×20 back squat) for offseason only**—too taxing during technique development

### Exercise Recommendations
- Prioritize weightlifting training itself (snatch/clean/jerk technique)
- General physical activity beats additional modalities for this athlete's goals
- Avoid concurrent CrossFit or competing training styles
