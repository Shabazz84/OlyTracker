# (Probably) Why You Suck At Weightlifting! - Sh*t Talk

## Key Points

- **Programming simplicity over complexity**: Good weightlifting programming follows a consistent template across all levels. Progression stalls from poor program design, not lack of effort.
- **Variables that actually limit progress**: Genetics (minor), time available, external stressors, lifestyle/nutrition—*not* program complexity itself.
- **Intermediate plateau risk**: Athletes often plateau for years due to program-to-effort mismatch, not physical ceilings.

## Programming Principles

- Build simple, well-structured programs aligned with individual limitations (OHS/back pain in this case).
- Consistency + lifestyle management (night shift work, nutrition, recovery) matter more than "secret sauce" programming variations.
- Post-program progression depends on addressing **time availability, external stressors, and lifestyle factors**—not adding program variables.
- For your 6-week hypertrophy block: prioritize *consistency within constraints* over complexity.

## Athlete Relevance

**Night shift work (Wed–Sun)**: Programming itself remains straightforward despite lifestyle chaos; focus on *when* and *how much* you can train, not complex periodization.

**Chronic back pain + OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Simple programming allows intelligent exercise selection and load management without overcomplicating variables.

**Intermediate status transitioning to Olympic lifting**: Avoid the common trap of program-hopping. A well-matched simple program + consistency will outperform frequent changes. Your 6-week block should establish this baseline before pursuing technique/strength work.
