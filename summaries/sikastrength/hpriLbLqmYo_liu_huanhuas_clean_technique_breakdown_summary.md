## Technique Cues: Clean

**First/Second Pull:**
1. **Shin angle**: Move shins toward perpendicular as pull begins (not vertical throughout—knees move back)
2. **Shoulder-barbell relationship**: Shoulders must stay *in front* of barbell at knee position
3. **Barbell path**: Rearward trajectory toward you (not vertical). Vertical = barbell drifts away → loss of power
4. **Knee position**: Knees back (easier for most than knees out) to enable rearward knee/barbell movement

**Third Pull (Most Critical):**
1. **Connection**: Stay *actively pulling* on barbell throughout—no weightlessness/floating
2. **Trap emphasis**: Pull traps vertically first, *then* arms follow
3. **Timing**: Pull under the bar *while* pulling up (simultaneous seesaw motion, not sequential)
4. **Common error**: Releasing barbell after contact = lost connection = poor turnover

**For OHS/Snatch Limiter (50 kg):**

**Pause Cleans:**
- Pause *at knee* (not high, not low)
- Gold standard: "remote pause" position—no barbell drop, no hip drift
- Fixes forward knees/shoulders-behind-barbell issues

**Hang Power Cleans:**
- 12-18 reps/session
- Position: Above knee (lighter depth okay if back permits)
- Emphasis: Fast barbell speed, aggressive trap usage, pull under simultaneously
- Avoids heavy loading (doesn't slow third pull down)

**No-Contact Cleans (Warm-up):**
- 4-5 × 3 reps @ 40-60% best clean
- Fixes premature arm rowing; forces trap-first initiation

**Clean Pulls/Deadlifts:**
- Overload positions + reinforce heavy trap engagement at top
