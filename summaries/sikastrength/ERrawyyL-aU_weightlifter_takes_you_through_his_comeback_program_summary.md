# Weightlifting Training Analysis Extract

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES
- **Periodization approach**: 6-week hypertrophy/volume block (3-5 sets × 3-5 reps for squat) transitioning to heavier singles
- **Volume priority**: Starting minimal, building toward 12×2 wave structure at peak
- **Frequency**: 
  - Snatch: 3×/week (varied)
  - Clean & Jerk: 2×/week (mostly full C&J, minimal singles <5%)
  - Pulls: 2×/week (5×3 structure)
  - Squat: Moderate frequency, increasing toward block end
- **Specificity model**: Low specificity now (variation base), progressing to high specificity (12×2 singles)

## 2. TECHNIQUE CUES
- **Snatch**: Shoulder rotation instability at heavy weights → address via *more snatching volume*
- **Clean**: Over-pulling in second pull, too much arm tension → reduce tension, pull *under faster*, improve third pull speed
- **Snatch variations**: Low-hang (no-foot) snatches prioritized for speed development
- **Clean variations**: Power clean emphasis; no-contact work to fix over-pull pattern

## 4. RECOVERY & MOBILITY
- **Shoulder asymmetry**: Internal/external rotation imbalance → consult therapist
- **Mobility work**: Single-arm overhead walks for unilateral shoulder stability
- **Context**: Athlete working with therapist; chronic back pain not directly addressed but jiu-jitsu training noted as beneficial for GPP
