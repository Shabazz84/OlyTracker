# Our First Seminar (of 2024), Zombie Squats & Pause Snatch

# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Snatch frequency**: 2-3x/week; alternating full snatch → variation → full snatch (avoids monotony)
- **Snatch variations used**: no-foot, low-hang, high-hang, pause-at-knee
- **Lower body split**: Thursday & Sunday squat days
- **Session structure**: Snatch work → zombie front squats → back squats → conditioning
- **Rep ranges**: Pause snatches ~8 doubles at lighter loads; back squats mixing heavy singles with rep work (4-8 reps at 150kg+)
- **Volume management**: Reduced snatch volume on time-constrained shifts; prioritize leg strength over light technique work

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Snatch extension**: Rename "contact" → **extension** (emphasize full leg drive, not just bar contact)
- **Hip position**: Hips should be higher, not "lazy" low position (even for stronger athletes)
- **Pause snatch value**: Reinforces atomicity position and teaches position at knee (best: 140kg pause)
- **Bar path**: Sweep bar back toward body off floor; find pause position at knee

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Zombie front squats**: Warm-up tool; high reps (4-8 at 150kg+)
- **Back squats**: Primary lower body strength; top sets 200-210kg
- **Shoulder/elbow mobility work**: External rotation (from Dr. Steph); perform cautiously (currently causes elbow pain)
- **Soft tissue work**: Critical priority—adductors, hip, upper back/shoulders via neuromuscular therapist (not self-massage alone)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- **Back tightness**: Stiffened from heavy squat block; prioritize thoracic mobility for snatch overhead position
- **Hip dysfunction**: "Gunky" adductors; causing hip shift in bottom position; scheduling soft tissue therapy (2-hour round trip)
- **Elbow issue**: External rotation exercises aggravate medial elbow; soft tissue work essential before intensity
- **Recommendation**: Soft tissue work every 4 weeks minimum; addresses before loads increase

## 5. Athlete-Specific Relevance
✅ **Directly applicable**:
- Chronic back pain → emphasize thoracic mobility + soft tissue work (prioritize this)
- Night shift (Wed–Sun) → adapt volume/intensity on time-limited shifts; light snatch work not ideal without warmup
- OHS limiter (50kg) → pause snatches + high-rep overhead work address this
- **No split jerk** → focus on push jerk variants; not mentioned but zombie/front squat emphasis builds leg drive for jerk
- Hypertrophy block goal → zombie squats + rep-range work (4-8 reps) aligns with block intent

⚠️ **Caution**: Hip mobility + soft tissue work are urgent (shifting in squat); address before intensity increases.
