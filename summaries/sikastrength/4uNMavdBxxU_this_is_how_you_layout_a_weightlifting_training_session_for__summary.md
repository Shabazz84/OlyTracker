# Analysis: Olympic Weightlifting Session Structure & Programming

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**1. Programming Principles**
- **Session order (critical for this athlete):** Snatch → Clean/Jerk variations → Pulls → Squats
- **Frequency:** Snatch work 5-6x/week (high skill, high fatigue sensitivity); Clean/Jerk lower frequency but include snatch assistance on CJ days
- **Structure:** 4-day template suggested:
  - Day 1: Snatch focus (±light CJ variation)
  - Day 2: Clean/Jerk focus (with snatch assistance)
  - Day 3: Snatch + Clean/Jerk (competition simulation)
  - Day 4: Strength emphasis (heavier squats/pulls, minimal Olympic work)
- **Session length:** 90 min max; 2 hours absolute ceiling
- **Rest between sets:** 1–2 min for singles/heavy work (not excessive)

**2. Directly Relevant to Athlete's Limiters**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Include overhead squat variations on snatch days (e.g., muscle snatch + OHS pairing—low fatigue, high transfer)
- **Push jerk only:** Strength day perfect for rack jerk work + heavier back squats to build jerk foundation
- **Back pain + night shifts:** Keep sessions efficient; avoid fatigue accumulation. Snatch-first sequencing minimizes back strain on heavy squat/pull work later in session

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Muscle snatches + overhead squats (mobility + technique, low fatigue)
- Snatch pulls/deadlifts: perform *before* squatting (quality transfer > post-squat pulls)
- Back squats: place last on snatch-focus days (unless separate emphasis day)
- Clean deadlifts: may exacerbate back fatigue—monitor or defer to strength day
- Assistance: 2–3 exercises, ~15 min cap (e.g., back/core work)

**4. Recovery/Conditioning Note**
- If snatch warm-up or light pulls fatigue subsequent lifts severely → **General Physical Preparedness (GPP) deficit** (aerobic, muscular endurance, metabolic) must be addressed *before* blaming weakness
- Night-shift schedule: prioritize sleep + manage session density to avoid overaccumulation

**5. Key Takeaway for 6-Week Hypertrophy Block**
- High-frequency snatch + strength emphasis day (back squat/rack jerk focus) fits framework perfectly
- Avoid mashing all lifts into every session; distribute squat/pull emphasis across week
