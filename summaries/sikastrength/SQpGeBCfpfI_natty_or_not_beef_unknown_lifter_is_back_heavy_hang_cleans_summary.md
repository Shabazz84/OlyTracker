# Natty or Not BEEF - Unknown Lifter is back - Heavy Hang Cleans!

**Not about weightlifting coaching for your athlete.**

This is a entertainment/reaction show reviewing viral lifting clips from Instagram. While it contains some technical observations (wrist positions, jerk mechanics, snatch pulls), it's:

- Primarily commentary on elite lifters' videos
- No structured programming principles
- No periodization relevant to your athlete
- Only incidental technique cues (not prescriptive)
- Includes non-lifting content (jiu-jitsu drama, bench press, deadlifts)

**Not applicable to your 6-week hypertrophy block or intermediate Olympic lifting progression.**
