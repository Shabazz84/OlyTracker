# Weightlifting Coaches React - 85kg 2016 Rio

## Key Points
- **Opener strategy**: Progressive attempt increments (e.g., 205→210→215 kg) build momentum and reduce miss fatigue vs. opening heavy; improves overall success rates. Applicable to athlete's weak jerk (65 kg).
- **Front rack mobility limitations**: Stem from forearm supination/pronation, shoulder external rotation, and lat extension—*not* wrist flexibility. Directly addresses athlete's 50 kg OHS limiter.
- **Training philosophy**: Consistency over maximal load emphasis supports hypertrophy block effectiveness.

## Technique Cues
- Assess and mobilize shoulder external rotation and lat extension before attributing rack position issues to wrist mobility.
- Progress attempts conservatively to maintain technique consistency and reduce fatigue accumulation across attempts.

## Programming Principles
- Build momentum through graded attempts rather than attempting maximal loads early.
- Prioritize movement consistency and repetition quality during hypertrophy blocks over chasing heavy singles.

## Athlete Relevance
**High applicability:**
- Opener strategy directly supports weak jerk development (65 kg push jerk).
- Front rack mobility assessment addresses primary snatch limitation (50 kg OHS).
- Consistency-focused approach suits hypertrophy block structure.

**Not addressed in source:** Programming modifications for chronic back pain, night shift recovery optimization, or split jerk development pathway.
