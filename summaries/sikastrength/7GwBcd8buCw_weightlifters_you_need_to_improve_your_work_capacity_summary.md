# Weightlifting Analysis: Work Capacity for Weightlifting

## 1. PROGRAMMING PRINCIPLES
- **Work capacity** enables higher quality repetitions across complexes, sets, exercises, and training blocks
- **Rest time management**: Actively reduce/standardize rest between sets/exercises during high-volume phases (avoid 10+ min gaps)
- **GPP Phase** (4–6 weeks): Non-specific, high-volume work (circuits, conditioning, higher rep assistance)
  - Example: 3×12 snatch grip RDL, progressing load or density week-to-week
- **High-volume training**: 5 sets of 2s, 10–12 doubles, 4–5×10 back squat with conscious rest reduction
- **Conditioning**: 2× per week, 15–45 min steady-state (start slow; add 5 min/month)
- **Block-to-block recovery**: Better work capacity = faster inter-block recovery (reduce 4 weeks → 2 weeks)

None provided.

## 3. EXERCISE RECOMMENDATIONS
- **GPP exercises**: Snatch grip RDL, push press, every-minute-on-the-minute work, higher-rep squats, light sprints/running
- **Circuit approach**: Core + pull + press + isolation, completed quickly
- **Conditioning modality**: **Stationary bike preferred** (least impactful on back/joints vs. rower, ski erg, running)
- **Avoid rower** if chronic back pain present

## 4. RECOVERY/MOBILITY
- Stationary bike conditioning minimizes injury risk vs. running (joint impact) or rowing (lower-back pressure)
- Never do conditioning before/morning-of heavy sessions; can do after or separate day

## 5. ATHLETE-SPECIFIC RELEVANCE
**⚠️ CRITICAL FOR THIS ATHLETE:**
- **Chronic back pain + night shift + 50 kg OHS limiter** → GPP phase with **controlled load** and **stationary bike** (NOT rowing) is ideal
- **6-week hypertrophy block fits perfectly** after a 4–6 week GPP phase
- Work capacity improvements = better rep quality in OHS → addresses primary snatch limiter
- Night shift recovery strain = prioritize steady-state conditioning (low-CNS tax) over high-intensity GPP initially
- Recommend: **Start GPP with light-load circuit work + 2×15 min bike, then transition to hypertrophy block**
