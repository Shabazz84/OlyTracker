# Korean National Team Training Analysis – Consolidated Summary

## Key Points

- **Korean national team model**: Dual daily sessions (aerobic/track work 6am; strength/technique 10am+), structured 20-minute warm-ups, small coaching groups (6-8 athletes per coach)
- **Snatch technical emphasis**: Barrel moving backwards off the floor (initial pullback), shoulder positioning in overhead ("star position")
- **Programming structure**: High volume on the way up (triples/doubles before singles), regimented templates with visual intensity monitoring, assistants after heavy sessions (pulls post-C&J)
- **Facility/coaching tools**: Movable mirrors for positioning work, mini-platform contraption for reactive foot placement drills, dedicated athlete workstations

---

## Technique Cues

- **Initial pull**: Emphasize barrel backward movement off the floor (not just vertical)
- **Overhead positioning**: Korean model prioritizes shoulder positioning in "star position" (stable OHS mechanics)
- **Foot placement**: Reactive/intentional positioning drills (not passive sliding)—relevant to your OHS deficit (50 kg limiter)

---

## Programming Principles

- **Volume sequencing**: Triples/doubles before singles in heavy sessions (matches Ukrainian/Georgian approaches)
- **Intensity management**: Visual coach monitoring > verbal cues; assess daily readiness
- **Session structure**: Structured warm-up non-negotiable even on 2nd sessions; assistants (pulls, pressing) prioritized post-heavy work if recovery permits
