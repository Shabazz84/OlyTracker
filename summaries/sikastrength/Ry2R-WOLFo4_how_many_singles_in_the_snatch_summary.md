# Weightlifting Analysis: Training Vlog Transcript

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **8-week clean-focused program**, 2 sessions/week
- **High-volume approach** emphasized ("volume is the name of the game")
- **Example week structure**: 11×2 @ 110 kg (Tuesday) → 10×1 @ 120 kg (Thursday) → 6×1 @ 130 kg (Sunday)
- Singles and doubles used for technical refinement and load progression
- Accessory work: front squat, back squat, strict press

## 2. **Technique Cues**
**Snatch:**
- Higher hip position in catch
- First pull: experimenting with "pushing hard for legs" vs. "sweeping back"
- Maintain tight back position
- Overhead position work needed
- Issue noted: loose over-position on left side

**Clean & Jerk:**
- **Hook grip emphasis** in bottom clean for better connection through pulls and secure bottom position
- Front rack mobility work ongoing
- Jerk dip: hips should stay more behind heels (currently knees traveling too far forward)
- Strict pressing recommended to support jerk strength

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- Back squat priority (current ~240 kg; coach notes ideal ratio higher)
- Front squat (progressing well)
- Strict press (needs reinstatement; currently ~100 kg but needs improvement)
- Hook grip practice for clean (mobility-dependent)

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility**
- Front rack mobility ongoing work
- Hook grip holding practice (mobility-limited but beneficial)

## 5. **Relevance to This Athlete**
**⚠️ CRITICAL MISMATCH:** This vlog is from an elite/advanced lifter (130 kg snatch singles, 150+ kg clean work). **NOT appropriate for an intermediate athlete with:**
- 80 kg clean / 50 kg OHS (massive technique gap)
- Chronic back pain (high-volume snatch protocol risky)
- Night shift recovery constraints

**Applicable takeaway:** Back squat strengthening is justified here too—aligns with your OHS limiter.
