# Analysis: Chinese Weightlifting Programming & Systems

## Relevant to This Athlete's Profile

**Programming Principles:**
- **Lower frequency model**: Chinese national team trains snatch/clean-jerk ~2x weekly (Mon power snatch, Wed power jerk, Fri snatch; separate clean-jerk days). One session/day, ~3-6 hours.
- **Quality over quantity**: High volume *per session* but lower total weekly frequency than Kazakhstan/Romania models. Principle: maintain technical precision rather than maximize session count.
- **Key takeaway for intermediate athletes**: "Frequency isn't the answer"—quality, consistent sessions beat high-frequency mediocrity. Relevant for night-shift worker with limited recovery.

**Technique Emphasis:**
- Strict bar path adherence (S-curve pattern: barbell center of mass moves back toward lifter's center of mass).
- No technique variation allowed until corrected; coaches correct minor flaws immediately.
- High pull volume to reinforce lift-specific positions.

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- Dedicated snatch *and* clean-jerk days (minimal overlap).
- Heavy squat frequency (1x/week noted; some coaches alternate front/back daily).
- High pull volume as primary accessory.
- Limited variations; emphasis on main lifts + pulls.

**Recovery & Contextual Notes:**
- One active recovery day (Wed or Thu), one rest day (Sun).
- **Important caveat**: Chinese model assumes elite athletes with 10+ years developmental history pre-national team.

## Critical Limitation
**Not directly applicable**: This analysis discusses *elite* national team programming. Intermediate athlete needs different stimulus; exact prescription would be inappropriate without coaching oversight (especially given chronic back pain & night-shift constraints).
