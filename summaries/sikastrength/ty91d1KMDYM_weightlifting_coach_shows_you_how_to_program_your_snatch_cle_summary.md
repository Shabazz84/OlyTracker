# Weightlifting Programming Analysis

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Duration:** 12-16 weeks (optimal ~14 weeks)
- **Frequency:** Snatch 1x/week, snatch variation 1x/week, clean & jerk 1x/week
- **Volume strategy:** Start high (5x3), gradually reduce volume while increasing intensity across the block
- **Loading progression:** Add 2-3kg per week while maintaining volume; never significantly increase volume in a macro cycle
- **Mid-block reset (Week 6):** Include conservative singles (~90%) to check technique at heavier loads and reduce fatigue before ramping final weeks
- **Intensity benchmarks:** Doubles ~10kg below max, triples ~15kg below max

---

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Avoid staying in triples too long** → causes slowness, form breakdown (looping, back bending, weak lockouts)
- **Singles serve dual purpose:** Practice singles without maxing out; assess technique quality at 90%+ intensity
- **Keep variations (e.g., hang snatch) light after peaking** → preserve speed and support full lift, not replace it

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Hang snatch example:** Used for athletes slow above knee; goal 90kg (not matching full snatch max)
- **Clean & jerk bias strategy:** If clean weak, emphasize 3+1 (3 cleans, 1 jerk); if jerk weak, use 1+3 or 2+3 ratios
- **Post-peak variation loading:** 4-3 sets × 2 reps at light weight to maintain speed/quality
