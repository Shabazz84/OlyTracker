## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency:** Mobility work 2–3x/week *before* squat sessions
- **Assistance exercises:** 1–2x/week (split squats, rows)
- **Volume structure:** Heavy, high-volume rowing twice weekly
- No specific set/rep ranges provided; emphasis on consistency and quality

---

## 2. **Technique Cues: High Bar Squat (Two Styles)**

**Style 1 – Knees Forward (Classic)**
- Drive knees *over* toes, hips close to heels
- Upright torso, slight back extension
- Narrow stance (shoulder-width or inside)
- Toes slightly out
- **Common error:** Hips drift back → torso tips forward (kill quad drive)
- **Cue:** Exclusively push knees forward during descent

**Style 2 – Balance/Korean Squat**
- Knees forward *AND* hips back simultaneously
- More posterior chain involvement
- Can use slightly wider stance
- **Common error:** Over-shifting hips back without knee drive → good morning at top
- **Tall lifters:** Use Style 1 (knees forward emphasis); longer femurs magnify leverage issues in Style 2

---

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**Mobility (pre-squat, 2–3x/week):**
- Weighted paw squats (or supported if needed) — 2–3 min accumulated
- Thoracic/pec mobility on bench/foam roller with light weight — 2 min

**Assistance (1–2x/week):**
- **Front foot elevated split squat** — emphasizes forward knee drive (dumbbells/barbells)
- **Barbell rows** (multiple variations: upright, below-knee, floor, Pendlay) — heavy, high volume, 2x/week

---

## 4. **Recovery & Mobility Advice**
- Prioritize mobility foundation before technical work
- "If something starts hurting, don't do it"
- Progressive loading for mobility: shift to supported paw squat if weighted version uncomfortable
- Unilateral work recommended (split squats) to hedge bilateral sport demands

---

## 5. **Athlete-Specific Relevance**

⚠️ **Critical for this athlete:**
- OHS weakness (50 kg) likely stems from ankle/thoracic mobility + upright positioning — weighted paw squats + thoracic work directly address this
- **Chronic back pain:** The text emphasizes *upright positioning* and *quad-dominant drive* (Style 1) over posterior chain loading — align with Style 1 initially; posterior loading (Style 2) may aggravate back until stabilized
- **Night shift worker:** 2–3x/week mobility is sustainable; maintains consistency without overload
- Hypertrophy block (6 weeks): Rows + split squats fit directly; add mobility foundation now before strength phase

---

## Key Takeaway
Prioritize **weighted paw squats + thoracic mobility** to unlock OHS depth, then anchor technique to Style 1 (knees forward) to manage back pain while building quad-dominant strength.
