# Weightlifting Coaches Analysis of Two Russian Super Heavyweight Weightlifters

**Not about weightlifting coaching for this athlete.**

This is a reaction/commentary video about elite lifters (Misha Kovalev, Alexei Lebedev) training. While it contains some technical observations about pulls, squat positioning, and elbow injuries in weightlifting, it offers **no actionable programming, technique cues, or recovery advice** applicable to an intermediate athlete.

**Potentially relevant observations (tangential):**
- Block pulls below-knee emphasize second pull speed—useful for athletes needing acceleration work
- Heavy pulls prioritize volume over maximal intensity
- Emphasis on maintaining positions through varied rep ranges during squat buildup

**Not useful for this athlete's profile** (intermediate transitioning to OWL, chronic back pain, low OHS, push jerk only, night shift recovery constraints).
