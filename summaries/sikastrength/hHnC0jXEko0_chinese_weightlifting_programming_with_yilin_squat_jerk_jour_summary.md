# Chinese Weightlifting Programming With Yilin (Squat Jerk Journalist)

# MERGED SUMMARY: Chinese Weightlifting Methodology – Athlete Application

## Key Points

- **Quality-over-quantity model**: One high-quality session daily suits this athlete's night-shift recovery constraints better than high-frequency programming.
- **Lower lift frequency than Western models**: ~1–2 full lifts per movement weekly (Mon: power snatch; Wed: clean; Fri: full snatch) with 2x/week squat.
- **High-volume submaximal blocks precede intensity**: Many reps at 150 kg before attempting 160+ kg builds consistency and technical durability.
- **Variation for injury management**: Pulls from blocks or front-squat emphasis reduce lumbar extension stress—critical for this athlete's chronic back pain.

## Technique Cues

- **"Using your legs"** = second pull extension (legs driving bar upward), not back pulling.
- **Bar proximity**: Keep bar close throughout lift.
- **Push legs under the bar** rather than pulling body over it.
- **Drop-down sets post-heavy work** (~70%): Reinforce technique while fatigued to build motor pattern robustness.
- **Coach supervision at 60–70%** to catch form breakdowns before they ingrain.

## Programming Principles

- **Single daily session**: Aligns with intermediate recovery capacity and shift-work sleep disruption.
- **Squat frequency**: 2x/week (front Tuesday, back Saturday); consider front-squat emphasis given back pain.
- **Accessory work**: 1–2 bodybuilding movements + core/lower-back rotation daily post-main lift; athlete autonomy only on accessory selection.
- **Main-lift non-negotiable structure**: Athlete cannot modify primary snatch/clean/jerk sessions; standardization ensures technical consistency.

## Athlete Relevance

**Critical adjustments needed:**

1. **Snatch frequency increase**: Chinese model (1x/week full snatch) underdevelops weak overhead position (50 kg OHS). Recommend 2x snatch frequency to address limiter.
2. **Push jerk progression**: Current push-jerk-only approach incompatible with Chinese template (assumes split jerk). Add split-jerk introduction during 6-week hypertrophy block.
3. **Back pain management**: Front squat emphasis + pulls from blocks over heavy back squats; front squat aligns with snatch/clean pull mechanics.
4. **Night-shift accommodation**: Single-session-per-day model is optimal; avoid multi-session days despite intermediate status.
