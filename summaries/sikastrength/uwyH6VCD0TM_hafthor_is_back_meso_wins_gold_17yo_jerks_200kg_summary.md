# MERGED SUMMARY: Weightlifting Programming & Technique for 102.5kg Intermediate Athlete

## Key Points

- **High-volume training** (multiple pull variations + squat work) is foundational for hypertrophy blocks; Lee Sang protocol (210kg back squat 10 reps + heavy pull volume) provides applicable template.
- **OHS limiter (50kg) directly correlates to weak back/lat development**—prioritize lats, traps, erectors in 6-week hypertrophy block to unlock overhead stability and jerk security.
- **Push jerk→split jerk transition** requires 6–8 weeks dedicated jerk work twice weekly; front squat + jerk complexes build confidence before split jerk learning.
- **Substantial squat/C&J gap** (118kg back squat vs. 80kg clean) indicates front rack/receiving position weakness; front squat work essential.
- **Chronic back pain + night shift**: Warm training environment critical; control eccentric tempo; high-volume pulling requires mobility preparation first.

---

## Technique Cues

**Snatch/OHS:**
- Narrow frog stance (Japanese style) vs. wider Russian-style first pull—wider pull more efficient for taller/longer-limbed athletes; test both.
- Stretch reflex and elastic tendon loading matter, but pure strength (not momentum) needed at 50kg deficit.

**Clean/Jerk:**
- Minimize wasted movement; reset feet crisply without additional steps.
- **Jerk specifics**: Avoid rising onto toes at start (favors forward balance); get hands further under bar (mobility work); bar position behind head improves catch mechanics.
- **Squat jerk variation**: Control descent (vs. dropping); behind-neck variation spares front rack stress.

**Front Squat:**
- Set hips back, maintain slight torso inclination; avoid knee-forward-only initiation.
- Pairs well with jerk complexes for confidence-building.

---

## Programming Principles

- **Front squat + jerk complexes**: Early-phase confidence-building and conditioning tool before heavy C&J.
- **Multiple pull variations**: Snatch grip pulls, high pulls, clean delifts for volume accumulation without maximal load.
- **Sub-maximal precision**: Emphasize technical precision even on non-max attempts.
- **Back development priority**: "Never a point where someone's back is too big"—lats/traps/erectors critical for jerking and OHS.
- **Seated/strict press variations**: High-volume pulling pairs well with pressing work.

---

## Athlete Relevance

**6-Week Hypertrophy Block Focus:**
1. **Back/lat development** (primary OHS limiter): Heavy-volume rows, pull-ups, reverse pec deck, high pulls.
2. **Front squat work** (address squat/C&J gap): 3–4x/week with jerk complexes; builds receiving position strength and split jerk confidence.
3. **Pull volume** (snatch/clean grip pulls, high pulls, delifts): Accumulate reps without maximal fatigue.
4. **Jerk-specific work**: 2x/week dedicated jerk practice; behind-neck variations to spare front rack stress.

**Recovery Considerations:**
- Prioritize warm training space (mobility gains directly improve back pain tolerance).
- Control eccentric tempo on heavy squats (manage chronic back pain).
- Test mobility limitations before high-volume pulling (avoid aggravation).

**Post-Hypertrophy Block**: Transition to strength/technique phase with solid back base and front squat confidence for split jerk learning.
