# Analysis: Clarence Kennedy Clean Breakdown

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Speed work is non-negotiable** — must actively practice moving faster; won't happen naturally
- **Posterior chain emphasis** — referenced "anti-glass back" 16-week program for posterior strength development
- **Squat/deadlift ratios for intermediate lifters:**
  - ~160–180 kg back squat for 100 kg snatch
  - ~200 kg squat for 110 kg snatch
  - ~210–220 kg squat for 120 kg snatch
- **Progressive timeline:** Think in 6-month increments, not sessions; expect 16+ weeks for major changes
- **Avoid over-prioritizing lifts** — balance snatch/clean/jerk with accessory strength unless competing imminently

## 2. **Clean Technique Cues**
- **Second pull speed:** Accelerate barbell *past* the knee through contact point (not slowing down)
- **Speed under the bar:** Reduce distance from bar's peak height to squat catch — elite lifters show minimal "air time"
- **Avoid over-pulling:** Don't chase a high pull or long extension (common mistake); get under the bar *faster*
- **Contact point:** Aggressive speed through explosive phase, not a passive catch
- **Position maintenance:** Clean deadlift positions must mirror clean positions for transfer

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Clean pulls & clean deadlifts** — build posterior strength while reinforcing positions
- **Heavy back squats** — improve hip/knee extension for second pull acceleration AND getting under the bar
- **Deadlifts** (hook grip, clean positions) — strengthen posterior chain without disrupting snatch training
