# Analysis: Programming for Masters/Older Athletes in Weightlifting

## Programming Principles
- **Olympic lift priority**: Snatch, clean, jerk are non-negotiable; maintain weekly volume but potentially reduce overall
- **Volume adjustment**: Reduce working sets (e.g., 20→15-17→8-10 sets/week) rather than eliminate
- **Intensity progression**: Elongate periodization blocks (4-week → 8-week) by repeating microcycles rather than aggressive weekly jumps
- **Strength work**: May progress slower; adjust expectations
- **Recovery consideration**: Spend longer at each intensity zone before advancing
