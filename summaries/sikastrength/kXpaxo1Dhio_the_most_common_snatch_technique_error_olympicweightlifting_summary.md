# The MOST Common Snatch Technique Error #olympicweightlifting

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**1. Technique Cue (Snatch):**
- **Primary issue:** Knees too far forward in snatch (most common problem across all levels)
- **Correction:** Knees sweep back from first to second pull (not extreme/"frog stance," but deliberate posterior movement)
- **Cue model:** Japanese technique style—knees track back naturally without exaggerated shooting

**2. Why This Matters for This Athlete:**
- OHS is his primary snatch limiter (50 kg vs. 80 kg clean) → likely related to forward positioning affecting depth/bar path
- Chronic back pain + forward knee position = increased anterior load/spinal stress
- Correcting knee position could improve OHS stability and reduce back strain

**3. Training Implication:**
- Requires "lots of assistance work and reps" to correct—suggests ongoing technique work beyond main snatch sessions
- Prioritize cue/position work during 6-week hypertrophy block before strength phase
