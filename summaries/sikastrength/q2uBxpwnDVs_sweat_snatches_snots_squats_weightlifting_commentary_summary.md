## Potentially Relevant Takeaways:

**Mobility/Recovery (applicable to chronic back pain):**
- Lower back extension capacity is foundational for snatch positioning
- Hamstring/glute work addresses lower back function
- Soft tissue work + mobility are ongoing skills, not one-off fixes
- PT-guided hip extension work improved snatch mechanics

**Programming Note:**
- High-volume snatch pulls (matched to working set volume) prioritized over squats during technical phases
- Minimal equipment dependence (belt/shoes) when movement quality is good

**Not applicable to your athlete:** This coach works with 120+ kg snatches; your athlete is at 80 kg clean/50 kg OHS and needs **foundational technique + back-friendly loading** before heavy pulls or volume work.
