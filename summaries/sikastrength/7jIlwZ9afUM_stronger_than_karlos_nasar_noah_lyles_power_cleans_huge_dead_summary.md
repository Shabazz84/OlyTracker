## Key Points
- Front squat strength should support clean & jerk capacity; benchmark is hitting triples with your target CJ weight (e.g., 130 kg FS for 130 kg CJ)
- Efficient positioning under the bar and quick transitions are hallmarks of good snatch technique
- Clean requires aggressive hip contact without bar loop-out and controlled bar path
- Touch-and-go squats develop quad engagement and mind-muscle connection
- High-quality Olympic lifting technique directly supports force production

## Technique Cues
- **Snatch:** Get under the bar quickly with efficient positioning
- **Clean:** Drive hips aggressively into the bar without allowing it to loop away; maintain strict bar path control
- **Squat variants:** Touch-and-go reps improve quad engagement and body awareness

## Programming Principles
- Front squat strength should exceed or match clean & jerk targets
- Quality technique under fatigue is foundational to capacity building
