# Szymon Kolecki ( The Greatest Polish Weightlifter!)

**Not relevant to weightlifting coaching.** This is a YouTube commentary/tribute video about Szymon Kołecki's lifting career, technique, and MMA transition. While it contains analysis of his lifts, it's narrative entertainment rather than coaching instruction.

**Minimal actionable content for your athlete:**

- **Split jerk cue**: Drive fast into split, don't force depth—timing over squat-jerk depth
- **Bar position**: Lower back position (widest shoulder point) benefits tall lifters
- **Snatch weakness noted**: Kołecki's snatch technique was poor (hips too low start position)—relevant since your athlete's OHS (50 kg) is primary snatch limiter
- **Polish training philosophy**: High volume, periodized, many reps in training

**Not applicable to your athlete's profile**: Kołecki's back squat avoidance (coach's choice) and later MMA career are anecdotal, not programming principles for a 6-week hypertrophy block.
