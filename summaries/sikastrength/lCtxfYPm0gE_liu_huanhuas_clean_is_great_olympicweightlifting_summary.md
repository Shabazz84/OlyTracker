# Analysis: Clean Technique Cue

**Technique Cue (Clean):**
- **Third pull coordination**: Must actively pull under AND simultaneously turn the barbell over—both must happen together, not sequentially
- **Key actions**: 
  - Maintain active pulling through the turnover (don't "bail" on the pull)
  - Use trap engagement heavily in the turnover phase
  - Stay connected to the barbell throughout—never lose contact

**Relevance to this athlete:**
- Clean is 80 kg with only push jerk (65 kg)—likely lacks split jerk due to positional weakness or comfort
- Back pain may benefit from understanding proper trap activation in clean (stabilizes spine)
- Third pull weakness could explain modest clean:squat ratio (80:118)
- Turnover timing issues compound if lacking split jerk confidence

**Coaching note:** This athlete needs to focus on aggressive turnover mechanics to unlock clean potential before addressing jerk split mechanics.
