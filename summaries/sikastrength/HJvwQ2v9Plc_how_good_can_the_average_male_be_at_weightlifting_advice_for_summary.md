## 4. **Recovery & Injury Management**
- **Masters lifters with athletic history**: Prone to recurring back/joint flare-ups; require psychological resilience and patience to restart blocks slowly when injuries recur
- **Masters lifters without athletic background**: High risk of sudden, unexpected injuries despite apparent progress; require *injury preemption* (proactive mobility/stability work) rather than reactive programming
- **General advice**: Don't over-restrict intensity due to age; focus instead on anticipating injuries and building psychological resilience to setbacks
