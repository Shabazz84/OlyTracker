# How To Conquer Weightlifting Mentally!

# Merged Summary

## Key Points

- **Third gear principle**: Train in sustainable "third gear" (3–4x/week, daily work) most of the time; reserve max attempts for planned 8–16 week blocks with zero misses to prevent technical/psychological erosion.
- **Process over outcome**: Replace outcome fixation with 10–15 sub-goals (e.g., "no weekly overreach," "60–90 min sessions," "test-retest mobility weekly") and reframe missed maxes as cycle success if process was executed.
- **Intermediate stepping stones**: Break lift targets into 3–4 sub-goals over 6 weeks (e.g., 10 RM at 75–80%, triple at 90–95% of current max) rather than chasing single numbers.
- **Cue experimentation**: Test 3–5 different delivery styles (visual, kinesthetic, verbal) for the same technical issue before abandoning it.

## Technique Cues

- **OHS (50 kg limiter)**: Prioritize kinesthetic cues ("feel tall," "pull elbows forward") if visual cues underperform; address overhead stability as primary snatch constraint.
- **Push jerk (no split)**: Drive height extension cue ("extend up more") is more applicable than footwork-dependent cues.

## Programming Principles

- **Night shift accommodation**: Prioritize ONE focus area per block (hypertrophy vs. strength) and accept flexible frequency (1–2 sessions/week with strategic effort beats inconsistent volume); prep/recovery nutrition is non-negotiable with limited sessions.
- **Chronic back pain**: Structure to eliminate weekly overreach; use 8–16 week blocks to control fatigue accumulation; test-retest mobility weekly (Thomas test, ankle mobility, external rotation) to verify rehab efficacy rather than guessing.
- **Nutrition strategy**: Add small, repeatable post-training habits (shake, protein source) tied to training fatigue; don't fixate on absolute calorie targets.

## Athlete Relevance

Apply process-focused sub-goals to your 6-week hypertrophy block (e.g., 80 kg clean → 77 kg × 3, 73 kg × 5; 65 kg jerk → 62 kg × 3, 60 kg × 5). Test mobility and OHS mechanics weekly; experiment with kinesthetic cueing for overhead position. Align session frequency and intensity to Wed–Sun night shift and back pain constraints; one well-executed session beats rushed volume.
