# Why Sohrab Moradi Has One Of The Best Split Jerks

# Analysis: Ramorad Split Jerk Technical Breakdown

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Jerk volume**: 4–5 sets × 3 reps, 1–2×/week (from rack or appended to clean+jerk)
- **Front squat priority**: Heavy, frequent volume (Morad lifts 275 kg FS)
- **Plyometrics (secondary)**: Countermovement jumps only, 6×3, 1×/week, progressive effort
- **Periodization note**: Plyometrics third priority—after jerk and FS volume

## 2. Technique Cues: Jerk
- **Dip/drive tempo**: Fast, smooth direction change; minimize time at bottom (zero velocity point)
- **Elastic energy**: Stretch-shortening cycle—slower dip = lost elastic energy
- **Knee tracking**: Keep knees aligned with feet (avoid inward drift); prevents pelvic tuck, upper back collapse, and forward bar drift
- **Goal**: Efficient vertical power transfer to bar

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- Jerk from rack (4–5×3)
- Front squat (heavy, frequent)
- Countermovement jumps (6×3, 1×/week, last training day)
- *Avoid weighted jumps unless highly skilled*

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- None discussed

## 5. Relevance to This Athlete
- **⚠️ Critical issue**: Athlete only does *push jerk*; this article assumes split jerk mastery (not applicable)
- **OHS limiter (50 kg)**: Front squat work will improve rack position carryover
- **Back pain**: Knee valgus cues helpful; inward drift exacerbates lumbar stress
- **Night shift**: 6-week block matches timeline; jerk frequency (1–2×/week) fits recovery constraints
- **Bodyweight/strength**: 65 kg push jerk → front squat work highest ROI for jerk improvement
