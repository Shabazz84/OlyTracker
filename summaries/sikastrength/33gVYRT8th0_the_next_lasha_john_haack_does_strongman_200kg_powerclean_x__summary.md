# Merged Summary: Weightlifting Programming & Technique

## Key Points

- **Front squat emphasis** (4–5x/week) builds standing speed and dip/drive power for jerks; back squat frequency can be reduced (1x/week) without sacrificing performance—applicable to back pain management.
- **Work capacity development** via higher rep ranges (10–12 reps) during hypertrophy blocks improves adaptation foundation for later strength phases.
- **Hang variations mask poor technique**; low-block cleans are more self-correcting and enforce proper positions.
- **Peaking timelines extend longer at higher bodyweights** due to muscle mass accumulation—relevant for 102.5 kg athlete planning 6-week hypertrophy block.
- **Full-depth squats** reduce injury risk and improve tendon/joint integrity—critical with chronic back pain.

---

## Technique Cues

**Hang Clean / Clean Pull:**
- Hips move backward; knees move forward (not just tucking)
- Shoulders stay in front of bar through descent and extension
- Bar stays close (2–3 cm from quads) through turnaround
- Drive up immediately upon shoulder contact; avoid passive sinking

**Clean from Floor:**
- First pull: Knees back/out of way quickly; maintain vertical shin angle
- Catch: Active quads/hips with powerful dip/drive into jerk position

**Overhead Positioning (Jerk Relevant):**
- Sharp elbow positioning (elbows up/front) during rack position mirrors jerk mechanics

---

## Programming Principles

- **Kazakhstan model**: 1x/week back squat, 4–5x/week front squat (block periodization). Maintains standing speed without frequent high-load back squat stress.
- **Hypertrophy phase**: 10–12 rep ranges build work capacity and adaptation baseline before strength/technique phases.
- **Low-block over hang variations**: Self-correcting stimulus; enforces proper technique positions better than hang options.
