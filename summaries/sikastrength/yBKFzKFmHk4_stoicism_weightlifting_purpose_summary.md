# Stoicism, Weightlifting & Purpose

**This text is primarily a philosophical monologue about purpose and happiness—not a coaching article or structured programming guide.**

---

**Limited weightlifting content extracted:**

1. **Programming/Exercise:**
   - Pause snatches emphasized as primary assistance (favorite tool)
   - Pause clean jerks up to 120 kg
   - Hang snatches and low-hang work used frequently
   - Clean pulls (120 kg × 4×3, fast pace)
   - Front squats to 180 kg (single/double)
   - Back squats to 230 kg (triple)

2. **Technique Cues:**
   - Wider snatch grip (closer to center of mass) preferred over narrow
   - "Knees back position" at knee = primary weakness; pause lifts re-emphasize this
   - Shoulder retraction too early (due to limb length)
   - Narrow foot stance in snatch catch for better overhead position
   - Wrist pain management: adjusted grip width and catch mechanics

3. **General Approach:**
   - Going 1–2 days ahead of schedule if feeling good (within reason)
   - Pause lifts beneficial even when lighter
