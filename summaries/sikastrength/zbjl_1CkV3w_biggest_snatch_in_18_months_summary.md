# Biggest Snatch in 18 months!

**NOT ABOUT THIS ATHLETE'S PROFILE**

This is a coaching vlog from an advanced competitive weightlifter (snatching 130kg, likely 85kg+ bodyweight) reflecting on his own training. While it contains valuable technical and programming content, it's not directed at intermediate athletes or relevant to your athlete's current needs.

**POTENTIALLY USEFUL EXTRACTS:**

1. **Snatch technique cues:**
   - Keep shoulders over barbell, hips back in setup
   - Sweep bar back toward body in second pull (don't just stand up)
   - Maintain lower back control in catch (avoid butt wink) to preserve thoracic stability overhead
   - First rep quality = second rep quality (consistency focus)
   - Speed = moving the bar faster (requires intention, not just drills)

2. **Programming notes:**
   - Low-hang snatches as a variation tool (not for everyone, context-dependent)
   - High-pull work for second-pull speed/extension
   - Track/plyometric work 1–2x/week concurrent with lifting
   - Upper body done in supersets/antagonistic pairs, ~30min sessions
   - Multiple reps across varied loads (70kg → 90kg → 100kg progression in one session)

3. **Recovery/mobility:**
   - Elevated pigeon stretch (preferred over floor version) for hip tightness/IT band
   - Soft tissue work takes multiple sessions (6–7+ sessions for chronic restrictions)
   - Daily mobility work + rehab exercises run concurrent

**⚠️ MISMATCH:** Vlogger's OHS/snatch mobility far exceeds your athlete's (50kg). Your athlete's OHS limiter means heavy snatch work premature until overhead position stabilizes.
