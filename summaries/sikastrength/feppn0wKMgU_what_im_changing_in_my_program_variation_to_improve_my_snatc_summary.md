## RELEVANT TO ATHLETE PROFILE:

**Programming Principles:**
- Reset/deload approach after heavy lift (took 5 days off after 140 kg snatch)
- Volume-based block with light weights post-reset
- 3x/week snatch frequency with 1 full snatch + 2 variations
- Conservative starting intensity ("start lighter than you think")

**Technique Cues (Snatch):**
- **Back extension critical:** maintain upper/lower back curvature throughout pull to prevent early hip closure
- Keep **hips back** longer—rushing off floor causes early hip closure → early toe contact → poor catch
- **Arm relaxation:** loose arms improve transition speed/turnover
- Chin up during extension
- Maintain position through knee (avoid position loss at heavier weights)

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- **Snatch pulls:** 3x/week (one per snatch session)
- **Low block snatches:** light work, felt effective for position
- **Pause snatches:** planned inclusion for positional control
- Weighted/box/long jumps for explosive power
- Sprinting, upper body work, conditioning during reset

**Mobility/Recovery:**
- Back mobility work (thoracic extension, soft tissue work, rotational work)
- Mandatory rest after heavy lifts
- 5-day break post-140 kg attempt

**Relevant to Athlete:** Back extension issues directly relate to chronic back pain history. Light reset with positional focus suits this profile.
