## Technique Cues
- **Snatch**: Avoid dynamic start on heavy days (disrupts first pull); maintain consistent positions
- **Power clean**: Emphasizes "pull under" quality; fixing insufficient barbell pull-up under
- **Jerk (pause variation)**: Knees tracking over toes; glute/lower back position maintenance
- **Clean**: Work jerk-specific positions separately; pause jerk reinforces control

## Exercise Recommendations
- **Power clean** as primary clean variation (addresses weak pull-under)
- **Pause jerk** for positional work (knee tracking, back position)
- **Snatch pulls** high frequency (3+ sessions/week)
- Front squat secondary to back squat
