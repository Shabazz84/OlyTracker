# Does The Clean Need To Get Into The Hip? #olympicweightlifting

# Analysis

**Technique Cues for Clean:**
1. Contact point doesn't need to be extremely high into hip—varies by anatomy
2. **Avoid actively "rowing" the bar** with bent arms into the hip (common error)
3. High hip contact elongates the pull and increases height demand—counterproductive for most lifters
4. Clean relies more on **pull-under than pulling high**—don't chase excessive bar height into hip
5. Correct height > maximal hip contact

**Relevance to Athlete:**
- At 80 kg clean with chronic back pain, prioritize efficient pull mechanics over aggressive hip extension
- Excessive hip contact + rowing pattern increases back strain risk
- Focus on vertical extension and early pull-under to reduce compensatory lumbar loading
