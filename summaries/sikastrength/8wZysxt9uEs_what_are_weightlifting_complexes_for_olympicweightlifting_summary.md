# What Are Weightlifting Complexes For? #olympicweightlifting

**EXTRACTED COACHING CONTENT**

**1. Programming Principles**
- Complexes (multiple lift variations in sequence) accumulate higher work capacity and total volume than single derivatives
- Complexes are preferred in prep/hypertrophy phases; not typical in peaking phases
- Heavy singles/doubles replace complexes in peaking

**2. Technique Cues**
- None provided

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Complex example: Clean → Front Squat → Push Press → Clean (repeated)
- Complexes effective for building work capacity in prep phases

**4. Recovery/Mobility**
- None provided

**5. Relevance to Athlete Profile**
- **Highly relevant to 6-week hypertrophy block goal**: Complexes align with hypertrophy phase programming to maximize volume
- **Back pain consideration**: Front squat + push press complexes emphasize anterior stability; monitor load on chronic back issues
- **Night shift worker**: Complexes are time-efficient (condensed volume) — practical for fatigue management

**Key takeaway**: Use complexes during hypertrophy block; transition to heavy singles/doubles in strength phase.
