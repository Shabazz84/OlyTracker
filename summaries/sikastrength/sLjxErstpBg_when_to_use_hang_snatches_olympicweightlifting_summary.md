# When To Use Hang Snatches #olympicweightlifting

# Analysis for This Athlete

**Programming Principles:**
- High block/hang snatches emphasize speed over position
- Low block/hang snatches emphasize position over speed
- Context-dependent loading matters for training effect

**Technique Cues:**
- High block (above knee) hang snatches: accept slightly forward knees and upright torso to maximize speed development
- Low block/hang snatches: prioritize receiving position accuracy

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- **Highly relevant for this athlete**: Low block/hang snatches would address the OHS limiter (50 kg). Emphasizing position work in the catch is more valuable than speed work given the weak overhead position and chronic back pain.
- High block hangs less priority until OHS strength improves

**Recovery/Mobility:**
- None provided in text

**Profile Relevance:**
- ⚠️ **Back pain concern**: High block work with upright torso positioning may be preferable initially, but prioritize low-block work for position refinement to build OHS strength. Avoid excessive fatigue on night shift; high-block speed work may be less taxing than heavy position work.
- 6-week hypertrophy block ahead: Use low-block hangs with moderate loading (60–75%) for quality reps to build OHS mobility and stability.
