**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**1. Exercise Recommendations (Most Applicable)**
- **Back extensions** (prone, bodyweight holds): Critical for chronic back pain management + spinal erector capacity for squats/pulls
- **Quad nordics**: Tendon loading for knee resilience (relevant given heavy squat:OHS gap suggesting quad/tendon limitations)
- **Barbell rows**: Lat/back balance; addresses internal/external rotation imbalance from pulling
- **Split squats**: Single-leg work to address symmetry issues; especially useful for split jerk transition (currently push jerk only)
- **Strict press**: Build lockout strength and overhead stability—directly transfers to jerk/snatch positions

**2. Programming Principle**
- Accessory/hypertrophy work justified for natural athletes to maintain progression (aligns with stated 6-week hypertrophy block)

**3. NOT Addressed**
- Night shift recovery impact
- OHS limiter solutions (video doesn't discuss mobility/positioning)
- Split jerk coaching (only mentions imbalance data)
- Back pain management specificity

**Recommendation:** Use the 5 exercises listed above within your hypertrophy block, but seek athlete-specific coaching on OHS positioning and split jerk mechanics.
