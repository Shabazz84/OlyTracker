# Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## 1. Programming Principles
- **Frequency**: Heavy clean work mid-week; snatch work same session; max-out Friday
- **Intensity**: Working up to near-maxes (180 kg clean, 140 kg snatch) mid-week before Friday maxes
- **Structure**: Dog Pack programming reported generating high PR rates; coach has 7 years programming experience with 2 years of Dog Pack feedback data
- **Alternative**: "Complete Strength" cycle available (3 days/week, 5 exercises/day, strength-only, Olympic lift break option)

## 2. Technique Cues
- **Clean**: "Pull hard," aggressive leg drive emphasis; avoid over-the-bar positioning and excessive arm bend; focus on "pull in the clean"
- **Snatch**: Pool (pull) fatigue tracking; mental cue for control ("walk it out a little but felt light")
- **General**: Mindset—use missed lifts as motivation to attack harder rather than become demoralized

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Demon Back Protocol**: 3 days/week; emphasized as most important for bulletproof snatch/clean/jerk (stronger back > stronger legs for Olympic lifting)
- **Dumbbell incline press**: Mentioned as supplemental work (minimal impact on main lifts despite soreness)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility Advice
- Sleep prioritized for recovery
- De-stress emphasized
- Heat management (Texas humidity noted as manageable)
