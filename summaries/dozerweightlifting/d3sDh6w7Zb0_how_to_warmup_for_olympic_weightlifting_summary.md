# How To Warmup For Olympic Weightlifting.

**1. Programming principles**
*   **Warm-up Duration:** Strictly limited to 10–15 minutes total (excluding warm-up sets).
*   **Structure:** Foam rolling (problem areas only) → Dynamic movement (no static holds >1 sec) → Barbell activation.
*   **Intensity/Frequency:** No specific hypertrophy or strength percentages mentioned; focus is on "getting warm" and feeling open before training begins.
*   **Periodization:** Mentions a program ("The Dog Pack") utilizing auto-regulation (light/moderate/heavy options based on daily feel).

**2. Technique cues**
*   **Front Rack/Thoracic Spine:** Push elbows down/in, drive armpits toward the floor to lengthen triceps/lats; keep torso stacked/abs active to prevent excessive arching.
*   **Snatch Warm-up:** "Twist" bar overhead while standing (feet planted) to engage lats/thoracic spine; sit in bottom position with flat shoes/no shoes before adding lifting shoes.
*   **Split Squat:** Sit hip directly over the front heel, keep chest up, do not let back knee touch floor.

**3. Exercise recommendations**
*   **Foam Rolling:** Lats and Glutes only (1 min per side); avoid full-body rolling or IT bands unless specific issues exist.
*   **Dynamic Mobility:** Half-kneeling hip flexor bounces, alternating elbow-to-ankle rotations, squat bounces with knee drive, "W" throughs (knees to feet).
*   **Barbell Activation:** Overhead twists (standing), Snatch Press (SSP) in flats/no shoes, Tall Snatches (on toes), Front Rack PVC curls (dynamic), Wrist mobility drills.

**4. Recovery or mobility advice**
*   **Pre-Workout:** Avoid static stretching; it creates joint instability detrimental to Olympic lifts. Use dynamic movement only.
*   **Post-Workout:** Light static stretching is acceptable as a cool-down.
*   **Mobility Philosophy:** Mobility is trained *during* the lift (bottom positions); do not overdo pre-workout mobility work.
