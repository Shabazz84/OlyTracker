### 2. Technique Cues (Clean Focus)
*   **Arm Bend:** Do not add arm bend during the pull. Maintain a static elbow angle from the floor through contact, or start with no bend and keep it there. Adding bend off the floor causes the bar to lose speed as arms straighten.
*   **Setup Position:** Shoulders must be directly over the bar; weight must be flat through the feet (centered). Avoid being too far forward (pulling around knees) or too far back (seesaw effect/losing balance).
*   **Core Engagement:** Take a big breath, brace the core, and lock it in before pushing. This prevents shoulders shooting over or pulling backward.
*   **The Pull:** Drive with quads into the floor; do not "pull" with the arms initially.
*   **Transition (Elbows):** Keep elbows up and knuckles down until passing the chest. Do not drop elbows early (reverse curl motion).
*   **Hand Activity:** Hands should remain passive (bar hanging in hook grip) during the pull to keep the bar close.
*   **The Catch:** Once elbows pass the chest, snap them through quickly while maintaining the "knuckles down" position to rotate under the bar.

### 3. Exercise Recommendations
*   **Tall Clean:** The primary drill recommended to correct early elbow drops and passive hands.
    *   *Execution:* Stand flat feet with bar at hips/knees, lift elbows until they pass the belly button, then snap under. Focus on keeping knuckles down and elbows up during the ascent phase of the drill.
