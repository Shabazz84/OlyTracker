## 2. **Technique Cues**

### **Snatch**
- Maintain consistent torso angle from floor through knee (critical diagnostic point for problems)
- Keep chest over bar through extension and transition
- Weight directly in middle of foot at all times
- Avoid "cutting the pool" and moving fast unpredictably—commit fully to each rep
- Muscle snatch: keep arms **passive** in pull; transfer tension to back/lats rather than arm-driven
- Don't overthink lighter reps—bring athleticism, flow through, stay loose (avoid checklist mentality)

### **Clean**
- Timing and bar path: "keep chest on top of bar…through extension even in transition"
- Rack position: if catching on fingertips, front rack is too tight
- Low hang cleans: focus on timing and bar path; keep weight centered

### **Jerk**
- Back foot hitting early is acceptable **IF** front foot is off ground first (not vice versa)
- Coach's cue: back foot acts as anchor to drive front foot away
- Shift in split catch = pause 2 seconds at catch to diagnose; fix immediately
- Behind-the-neck power jerk: universal timing primer for snatch/clean/jerk catch mechanics
- Receiving position: avoid pressing out—should be crisp lockout with good shoulder mobility
