# The BEST weightlifting shoe?

**Weightlifting coaching content: YES**

---

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Friday max-out structure**: Heavy singles as competition prep (1 week out from nationals)
- **Lesson on restraint**: Coach stopped at 50 kg snatch (good lift), then ego-driven 3 unnecessary misses at heavier weights = wasted energy
- **Mental pacing**: Avoided preemptive attempts to preserve energy and respect training partner's motivation
- **Clean & Jerk recovery**: After snatch fatigue, refocused mentally to hit strong 185 clean (from 150 baseline), demonstrating comeback capacity

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Snatch positioning under load**: "Started standing up then said 'Nope, not yet' and sat back down"—requires years of experience to recognize and adjust position mid-lift
- **Shoe selection for movement style**: Coach prefers flexible shoes (flat, no carbon plate gap) over rigid carbon-heeled shoes because he "loses position hard" and needs shoes that allow squeeze-under mechanics (contrasts with lifters with "really good movement and great positions")

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Rear delt mobility**: Elbows between knees in deep squat position + deep breathing (⚠️ high risk; coach disclaims rotator cuff responsibility)
- **Mobility app**: Experimenting with "Goad" app; plans formal review after 20 hours

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- **Soreness management**: "Stay aggressive—once you give in, soreness gets worse"
- **Age-related stiffness**: Coach (28 yo) notes body tightness increases vs. late teens/early 20s
- **Heat application** used during session

## 5. **Relevant to This Athlete**
- ⚠️ **Chronic back pain context**: Coach's flexible shoe preference and emphasis on position adjustability may apply; rigid shoes could worsen back strain
- **Push jerk only**: Coach hits push jerks (185 kg); no split jerk discussion here
- **OHS limiter concern**: Rear delt/shoulder mobility work directly relevant
- **Night shift fatigue**: Coach mentions "training throughout the week wasn't great" but recovered—aggressive mindset during fatigue sessions noted as critical
