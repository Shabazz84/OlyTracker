## Technique Cues

### SNATCH (Setup through Extension)
**Starting Position:**
- Bar over first strap (top of shoelaces), shin-to-bar space maintained
- Shoulder directly above bar; torso angle locked and maintainable
- Weight in mid-foot (jumping position), slightly forward of heel — **"nothing athletic happens off heels"**
- Knees and arms in line; eyes on fixed point ahead

**First Pull:**
- Torso locked, legs push (imagine back pad resisting)
- Avoid hips shooting up prematurely ("stripper butt")
- Chest leads; quad tension initiates bar separation from floor
- Weight stays in foot; prevents forward drift

**Middle Phase (Key Limiter for This Athlete):**
- Clear the knees cleanly ("clear the road")
- **Acceleration matters more than initial speed** — accelerate through knee extension into hip
- Maintain backwards bar momentum as knees extend
- Don't yank prematurely (causes mid-lift deceleration)

**Extension:**
- Legs only drive extension (below waist); upper body remains mute
- Elbows high, hands up (backs of hands toward wall)
- **Knuckles down = straight bar path; knuckles back = backward drift**
- Shoulder blades set back/down pre-lift; arms stay relaxed

**Hang Work (High & Low):**
- High hang (hip): Drills dip-drive mechanics (universal across snatch/clean/jerk)
- Low hang (below knee): Find power position first, feel foot pressure, then shift and drop
- Pick up same way as floor — **legs drive, not upper body**

### JERK (Push Jerk Applicable)
**Front Rack & Dip:**
- Bar below knuckle line, elbows up/forward, shoulders active on platform
- Foot pressure: 55/45 distribution (slightly back); stay neutral, avoid heel bias
- Dip: "Basketball jump" feel — load quads/glutes, keep bar directly over foot pressure throughout
- Receive by punching shoulders into bar; squeeze triceps, stack shoulders over hips

**Receiving Position:**
- 50/50 weight distribution across both feet
- Equal knee bend both legs; knees track outward (glute activation)
- Use natural foot angle from clean catch — consistency > prescribed angles
