## 2. Technique Cues for Clean
**Start Position:**
- Feet hip-to-shoulder width
- Grip: thumb-width (narrow enough for high elbows in front rack)
- Bar over shoelace ends
- Shoulders directly on bar; weight even through feet

**First Pull (floor to knee):**
- "Leg press" mentality—push floor away
- Shoulders/hips rise simultaneously
- Torso angle stays consistent
- Tension in mid-back, quads, lats (NOT arms/upper back)

**Power Position (knee to hip/mid-thigh):**
- Chest on bar → shoulders on bar transition
- Shoulders/bar/ankle in straight line, flat feet
- Contact point varies by proportions (mid-thigh or hip acceptable)

**Extension & Turnover:**
- "Bounce the beach ball" = vertical punch (no whipping)
- "Pull your pants up" = elbows high, knuckles down
- Continue pulling until chest height, snap elbows around
- Feet land simultaneously with bar

**Receiving:**
- Meet bar at fixed point, ride down—don't pull excessively high then crash
- Common error: jumping back (caused by hips shooting forward instead of quad-drive extension)

**Drill for transition issues:** Tall cleans

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
- **Tall cleans** for jump-back/crashing/transition issues
- Progressive program structure with dedicated position work

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
