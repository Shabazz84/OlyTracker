# Merged Summary: Alex Lee Weightlifting Coaching Analysis

## Key Points

- **Strength is foundational** but technique precision matters more for intermediate athletes; build technical foundation first, then layer strength for longer progression
- **No maxing out daily**; critical for managing chronic back pain and recovery under night shift schedule
- **Return-to-training protocol:** Start conservatively with back squat thrusters, muscle snatches, and slow strength work before intensity
- **Warm-up structure:** Multiple singles/doubles spaced ~90 sec apart (feels better than traditional pyramids)
- **Clean→jerk continuity:** Maintain diaphragm brace from clean into jerk dip (don't exhale/reset); bar never comes to rest—treats as one lift, not two
- **Long-term periodization:** Final strength gains take 6–12 months and require perfect diet/conditions

## Technique Cues

### Snatch/Clean Start Position
- **Foot pressure:** Full foot engagement (spread toes, create arch pressure), NOT heels—prevents balance loss
- **Lever-dependent spacing:** Long limbs require MORE bar spacing from body and more compression/reach needed
- **Shin angle:** ~90° at knee is nearly universal
- **Balance principle:** Stay middle—avoid overcorrecting (e.g., too far back won't fix forward misses)

### Back & Bracing
- **Neutral or slightly rounded back > hyperarched** (directly addresses your chronic back pain)
- **Brace hard into neutral/slight flexion** = better tension distribution than excessive arch
- **Anterior/posterior balance:** Must maintain connection between both chains; avoid hyperarch that loses anterior engagement
- **Front squat (doubles/triples):** Universal bracing check—reveals poor brace immediately

### Shoulder Rotation (Critical for OHS/Snatch)
- **Internal rotation > external rotation** for shoulder stability and bar control
- **External rotation** causes: unhinging, hyperextension, disconnection, early drop in turnover
- **Cue:** "Front side pulls in short, back gets long"—internal rotation maintains this position
- **Best snatchers** (Rebakaov, Doga) use internal rotation; Ilia corrected this after injury

### Clean Rack Position
- **Elbows out and down** (not high/narrow) to create posterior trap shelf
- **Lats matter**—wider grip helps posterior connection
- Avoid forward shoulder lean

## Programming Principles

| Phase | Focus | Methods |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Re-entry/Movement Check** | Balance, body awareness, bracing quality | Back squat thrusters (slow/controlled), muscle snatches, hip raise + back squat |
| **Your 6-Week Hypertrophy Block** | Build posterior chain relative to squat (118 kg BS vs 80 kg clean suggests anterior dominance) | Front squat doubles/triples, controlled volume, proper bracing emphasis |
| **Technique/Strength Phase** | Layer strength onto technical foundation | Sport-specific intensity after mobility/positioning established |

- **Frequency:** Spread intensity; generous warm-ups with many singles/doubles
- **After competition/layoff:** Program balance checks before intensity
- **Sleep schedule impact:** Recover/mobility work non-negotiable given night shift (Wed–Sun) + chronic back pain

## Athlete Relevance

### Your Critical Limiters
1. **OHS 50 kg (snatch limiter)** → Poor shoulder internal rotation + squat mobility/stability. Use internal rotation cue ("front side in, back gets long") to improve catch position stability.
2. **Chronic back pain** → Emphasize neutral/slight flexion bracing (NOT hyperarch). Front squat work and proper anterior/posterior chain balance will distribute load safely.
3. **Push jerk only (65 kg) vs Clean 80 kg** → Continuous tension clean→jerk breathing strategy could improve jerk significantly (maintains kinetic energy, eliminates dead-stop). Goal: approach clean strength with split jerk.
4. **Back squat 118 kg >> clean 80 kg** → Suggests anterior-load dominance. Front squat doubles/triples + proper posterior chain cueing (elbows out, lats engaged, internal rotation) will transfer directly to clean.
5. **Night shift worker** → Hip flexor tightness likely restricts bottom squat position and back structure. Mobility screening essential; prioritize consistent movement checks.

### Immediate Action
Begin 6-week hypertrophy block with: back squat thrusters, front squat work (doubles/triples), muscle snatches, and hip mobility restoration. Emphasize neutral-spine bracing and internal shoulder rotation cues. Manage intensity around sleep schedule and back pain. Retest OHS and clean 1RM at block end.
