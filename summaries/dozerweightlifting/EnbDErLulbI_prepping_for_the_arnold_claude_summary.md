# PREPPING FOR THE ARNOLD

**NOT ABOUT COACHING ADVICE FOR THIS ATHLETE.**

This is a vlog-style content piece where the creator (Dozer) documents his own training week with minimal structured coaching information. 

**Extractable principles (limited):**

1. **Programming**: 6-week hypertrophy/volume block → heavier/lower volume phase. Volume emphasis early to build "solid base" before intensity increases. Lighter warm-up doubles before heavier singles to manage fatigue while maintaining technique.

2. **Technique cue**: Drilling snatch/clean movement repeatedly in flow state to make heavy positions "second nature" without overthinking.

3. **Philosophy**: "Training for a meet, not training for heavy weights"—confidence-building approach over max attempts.

**Relevance to athlete profile**: Minimal. This is Dozer's (elite lifter's) content, not tailored advice for an intermediate transitioning to Olympic lifting with back pain, night shift schedule, or weak overhead position.

**Recommendation**: Seek structured coaching program designed for your profile—especially addressing OHS limiter (50 kg vs 118 kg squat is significant) and chronic back management.
