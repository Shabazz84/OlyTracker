# 3 MISTAKES YOU'RE MAKING ON JERKS

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Focus:** The text is a technique analysis, not a full programming guide. It suggests a specific drill: **Pause Jerks** (holding the catch position for 3 seconds) on sets up to ~85% intensity to fix balance and weight distribution.
*   **Timeline:** Explicitly advises a **1-month minimum** of drilling these specific cues before expecting significant improvement, emphasizing that the jerk is too fast for immediate cognitive correction during heavy lifts.

**2. Technique Cues (Jerk Specific)**
*   **Foot Loading (Dip/Drive):** Do **NOT** shift weight to heels ("heels" cue is rejected). Load power from the **front half of the foot** before dipping. The bar must stay directly over this spot; if it shifts forward during the dip, you are loading on heels.
*   **Arm Timing:** Prevent early pressing. Keep hands relaxed (release tension) so the bar sits passively on shoulders. Do not let the bar leave the shoulders until hips and knees fully lock out. If elbows bend or separate from shoulders before hip extension finishes, arms are taking over.
*   **Split Balance:** Aim for **50% weight in each leg**. Front knee must stay directly over the ankle or slightly behind (never over the toe). Hips must drop straight down under the bar.
*   **Foot Placement Cue:** To avoid "short stepping," think of **"slapping" the front foot down** (heel-first contact) rather than sliding it in, forcing a longer split.

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
*   **Primary Drill:** Pause Jerks (hold catch for 3 seconds) up to 85% intensity.
*   **Video Analysis:** Record from the side to check for: bar shifting forward during dip, elbows bending before hip lockout, or front knee over toe in the split.

*   **Jerk Style:** The advice supports the athlete's current **Push Jerk** style by emphasizing leg drive over arm press, which is crucial for a push jerk where stability relies on leg extension rather than split mechanics. However, the "slap foot" and "50/50 weight" cues are specific to *Split* Jerks; if the athlete strictly does Push Jerks, these split-specific cues (front knee position) may need modification or ignored in favor of vertical dip/drive balance.
