## Actual Coaching Content (Sparse):

**Technique Cues:**
- **Tall snatches**: Forces upper body finish and follow-through; prevents "smacking and diving" (diving under without proper pull completion)
- **Patience in the catch**: Sit in the bottom position before standing; don't rush to stand—balance the bar first, then drive up
- Block snatch: Used to build pulling consistency off the floor

**Programming Note:**
- Block snatching session (Zach working toward 150 kg snatch)
- Follow-up: Clean & jerk work (targeting ~175 kg clean)
