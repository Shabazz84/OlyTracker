# Weightlifting Analysis: Dylan (Dozer) Training Transcript

## 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Block work early in phases** to build confidence and establish maximal capability before floor lifts
- **High snatch volume** (lighter loads, technically demanding) vs. lower clean/jerk volume (after technique solidified)
- **Minimums-based approach**: Pre-set realistic daily targets; hit them and leave
- **Warmup discipline**: 15–20 min max; exceeding indicates poor recovery/mobility outside gym
- **Return-to-training phase**: 4 weeks of habit-building before specificity (both lifts most sessions, squat, simple routine)
- **Battle weight strategy**: Only retry 1x/month if getting progressively closer; stop on drastic miss

## 2. **Technique Cues**
- **Block snatches**: "Pull as hard as you want" (no patience needed); mental mastery is key
- **Block struggles = mental block, not strength/position** → use blocks frequently to build confidence
- **Snatch overhead position**: Frequency drives comfort/stability more than strength
- **Front rack tightness**: Caused by back fatigue/muscle lockdown, not position
  - Fix: Light weight (20% clean) seated overhead press, 3×8 to activate thoracic extension under load

## 3. **Exercise Recommendations**
- **Block snatches** & **block cleans** (priority early phase)
- **Front squats with straps** (forces deep grip, establishes front rack): 200+ kg
- **Seated overhead press, 20% load** (3×8): corrects tight front rack
- **Banded dead bugs** & core-activation warmup: pre-hab for chronic back pain
- Mentioned: yoga/stretching for upper back tightness from prior upper-body focus

## 4. **Recovery/Mobility Advice**
- **Core-activation warmup** (hip movement + trunk static tension): used during 2016 back injury recovery
- **Banded dead bugs** for weak core or back pain
- **Stretching/yoga during downtime** critical for upper back/shoulder mobility
- **Post-heavy work muscle activation** forces acute discomfort (~5 min) to restore function
- Expects front rack normalized in ~2 weeks with frequency
