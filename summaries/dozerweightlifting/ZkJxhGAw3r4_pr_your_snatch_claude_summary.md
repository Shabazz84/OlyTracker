# Weightlifting Analysis: Snatch Turnover Technique

## Technique Cues (Snatch)
- **Elbows up**: Maintain high elbow position as long as possible during pull
- **Late hand turnover**: Hands are the *last* thing to rotate—keep them extended while pulling under
- **Arm mechanics**: Arms function like "whips"—passive extension, not active pressing
- **Catch timing**: Hands turn over *after* body is in receiving position; don't press into catch
- **Reference model**: Lasha Talakhadze demonstrates this pattern

## Relevance to Athlete
**Direct application to OHS limiter**: This athlete's weak OHS (50 kg) likely stems from poor turnover mechanics and overhead positioning. The emphasis on *not* pressing and late hand turnover suggests the athlete may be over-activating arms in the catch, restricting mobility/stability. Focus on passive hand turnover drills could improve catch position and translate to stronger OHS.
