# Analysis: Snatch Tutorial (Technical Focus)

## Relevant to Athlete Profile

**HIGHLY RELEVANT - OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg)**

The text extensively addresses mobility/stability in overhead squat catch position. Key takeaways:

1. **Bottom Position (Critical for this athlete)**
   - Tempo OHS: 5 seconds down, pause, repeat—progressively deepen range
   - Do NOT force; train comfortable depth with stability/confidence
   - Overhead grip rotation: neutral (between internal/external), athlete-dependent
   - Goal: sit with hamstrings on calves, relaxed

2. **Power Position (Position 1)**
   - Bar on shoelaces, shoulders over bar, flat feet
   - Lats tight, shoulders neutral (not shrugged)
   - Hands loose; gravity keeps bar close

3. **Catch Mechanics**
   - Elbows stay high longest; hands passive (whip-like turnover)
   - Catch at forehead height initially, then progress lower
   - Pause-catch → ride down drill identifies footwork issues

---

## Programming Notes

- **Progression logic**: Top-down (catch→power position→floor)
- **Drill sequence**: muscle snatch → power snatch → full snatch with pauses
- **No intensity/volume prescribed** in this tutorial

---

## Recovery/Mobility for Back Pain

**Caution**: RDLs and tempo OHS work hamstring/hip mobility—potentially beneficial, but **monitor spinal loading given chronic back pain**. Discuss with coach before implementing full snatch progression.
