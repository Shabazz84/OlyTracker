### 1. **Programming Principles**
- **Frequency:** 4 "real" training sessions/week + 1 ancillary day (front/back squat + pressing)
- **Rationale:** Avoid overtraining; quality over volume. High-frequency training (6+ sessions) causes focus degradation and mental fatigue
- **Intensity approach:** Go heavy frequently but train *infrequently*—better engagement per rep than high-volume splits
- **Periodization note:** Coach advocates for reduced volume compared to CrossFit-style high volume; "find your sweet spot"
- **Burnout intervention:** If overtrained, reduce to 3 intense sessions/week to reset

**→ APPLICABLE:** Your night shift schedule (Wed–Sun) limits recovery. 4 sessions/week fits better than 5–6. Prioritize quality on heavy days.

### 2. **Technique Cues (Snatch)**

**→ APPLICABLE:** Your OHS 50 kg is a major snatch limiter. Technique work at 40–50% should emphasize staying low and upright, not explosive overextension.

### 3. **Exercise Recommendations**

**→ APPLICABLE:** Back pain + night shift = protect recovery. Front squat may be safer than back squat; verify bracing position. Mobility crucial.

### 4. **Recovery & Mental Factors**
- **Overtraining risk:** Crossing the "red line" requires 1–3 weeks reset minimum; worse with age/experience
- **Workload by bodyweight:** Cleaning 200 kg ≠ cleaning 120 kg; mass matters for CNS recovery
- **Mental demand:** Heavy lifts demand 110% focus; sloppy reps at high intensity cause plateaus
- **Life balance:** Non-lifting outlets prevent monotony and burnout; pent-up energy from work transfers to bar

**→ APPLICABLE:** Night shift work = sleep disruption. Likely over-stressed. 4 sessions max. Monitor mood/motivation. Build non-lifting activities. Back pain may worsen with fatigue—prioritize sleep.
