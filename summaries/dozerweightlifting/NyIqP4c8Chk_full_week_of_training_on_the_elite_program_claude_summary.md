# Full Week of Training on the Elite Program

# Merged Summary: Dozer Elite Program Analysis

## Key Points
- **9 sessions/week program (NOT appropriate for this athlete):** Requires twice-daily training Mon/Wed/Fri, once daily Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun
- **Volume/Intensity/Frequency trade-off:** Can maintain only 2 of 3 high simultaneously; high volume + frequency = moderate intensity
- **3-month block structure:** Week 1 adaptation; ~2-week acclimation before full efficacy
- **Recovery non-negotiables:** 9+ hours sleep/night; 250g+ protein/day mandatory; 20min rest between snatch and clean/jerk maxes
- **Program incompatible with this athlete's profile** (see Athlete Relevance)

## Technique Cues
**Snatch:**
- Push through floor *after* bar separation; maintain connection through middle hip
- High-hang focus: bar at hip pocket forces connection in turnover
- Power snatches: emphasize bar acceleration through hip

**Clean:**
- Low-hang position: chest over bar, initiate squat as chest travels forward
- Straps on light sessions: relaxes forearms, opens front rack, redirects tension to lats

**Jerk:**
- Behind-the-neck jerks solidify overhead position and timing; overloading improves jerk development
- Lowering jerks (receiving bar on shoulders) superior for jerk development vs. other variations
- Clean + 2 jerks complex: excellent jerk stimulus

**Accessory emphasis:** Block snatches (effort/aggression off floor); muscle snatches; behind-the-neck power drive (universal timing across lifts)

## Programming Principles
- **AM sessions (light/technical):** Prime nervous system; front squats optimal (testosterone peak)
- **PM sessions (heavier):** Primary strength/volume stimulus
- **1–2 week acclimation phase** before program reaches full efficacy
- High frequency demands exceptional recovery; poor recovery = serious injury risk

## Athlete Relevance
⚠️ **NOT SUITABLE — Multiple contraindications:**

1. **Night shift work (Wed–Sun):** Incompatible with 9 sessions/week recovery demands (9+ hours sleep required)
2. **Chronic back pain:** High frequency + cumulative fatigue compounds spinal stress
3. **OHS 50kg (primary limiter):** Needs dedicated shoulder/mobility work, not high-volume general programming
4. **Stated goal (6-week hypertrophy block → technique/strength):** Program is elite competition prep, not hypertrophy-focused development

**Recommendation:** Pursue structured 3–4 day/week program with dedicated OHS/mobility work, prioritizing recovery compatibility with night shift schedule and chronic pain management.
