# Analysis: Weightlifting Coaching Transcript

## 1. Programming Principles
- **10-week peaking cycle** to Arnold competition (starting Monday)
- **2-week reactivation block** (low intensity, high volume in lifts/squats) → minimizes adaptation shock, preserves recovery capacity for later intensity
- **3x/week accessory work** (carries, lunges, farmers carries) as staple through competition
- **Periodization rationale**: Reactivation allows early program consistency vs. typical "survival" phase; Christmas travel accommodation noted
- **Recovery philosophy**: Target feeling of "could do more work and recover" rather than just surviving

## 2. Technique Cues for Snatch
- **Overhead position**: Increase internal rotation (thoracic extension → internal rotation) = shoulder easier to keep elbows up longer, cleaner turnover
- **Start position/torso angle**: Long-femured lifters must consciously maintain torso angle off floor; manipulate start position for consistency
- **Strap rule change**: Ban straps from floor singles/sets; only straps for complexes/blocks. Identifies consistency gap (52kg without straps easier than 55kg with straps, yet latter inconsistent)

## 3. Exercise Recommendations
**Accessory accessories** (3x/week):
- **Bamboo bar carries** (forward + backward walk): Serratus/upper back stabilization; backward walk critical. Carry-over: +10kg snatch, +9kg jerk in 3 months (2016 period). Regression: max-hold if no walking space
- **Single-arm farmers carries**: Anti-lateral flexion, oblique work; keep hips/shoulders level; use heavy load
- **Walking lunges + kettlebell front-rack lunges**: Hip stabilization, unilateral balance, core; keep torso vertical (avoid forward lean)

## 4. Recovery/Mobility
- **Minimal explicit guidance**, but reactivation block designed to reduce CNS fatigue
- Athlete note: "Still up from Monday" (sleep deprivation acknowledged but not addressed in programming)
- Recommendation: Get squats in during travel (Christmas) for maintenance; lifting optional but recovery valued
