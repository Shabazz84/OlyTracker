**TECHNIQUE CUES (Jerk focus):**
- **Breathing:** Don't exhale between clean and jerk; establish brace on catch, maintain it through dip/drive
- **Timing:** 2 whips of bar, then dip on 3rd (creates rhythm consistency)
- **Torso:** Stays vertical; feet must move equal distance to prevent torso drift
- **Leg bend:** Front and back legs bent equally in split
- **Foot placement:** Think both feet landing simultaneously (back will hit first naturally); slide feet, don't pick them up; let bar punch drives the foot movement

**RECOVERY/PROGRAMMING:**
- Bad training days happen; don't force through (risk overtraining/burnout)
- Strategic rest day > persistent poor sessions
- Recognition: coach competed same weekend, took pragmatic approach (stopped snatching after hand tear)
