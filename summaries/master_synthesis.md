## **1. CONSENSUS PRINCIPLES (3+ sources agree)**

### **A. Technique Before Load**
- Sub-maximal work (60–85% intensity) gates load progression
- Movement quality over absolute tonnage; 1 perfect rep > 5 sloppy ones
- Prioritizes OHS limiter (50 kg) through positional mastery, not brute strength

### **B. Frequency > Volume for Skill Acquisition**
- 3–4 focused sessions/week preferable to 1–2 brutal days
- Snatch and clean worked 2–3×/week minimum
- Night shift constraint enables consistent 3-day structure

### **C. Back Pain = Selective Loading Strategy**
- Front squat > back squat (safer lumbar profile)
- Avoid heavy conventional deadlifts; use RDLs, rack pulls, trap bar variants
- Tempo control (2–3 sec eccentric) + neutral spine = non-negotiable
- Machine/cable accessory work prioritized for hypertrophy without axial loading

### **D. OHS is the Snatch Bottleneck**
- 30 kg gap (C 80 vs. OHS 50) indicates receiving position weakness
- Direct OHS work 2–3×/week essential
- Mobility deficit (thoracic extension, ankle dorsiflexion, wrist extension) likely contributor
- Paused OHS/snatch balance builds positional strength in weak range

### **E. Recovery Capacity Limits Training Stress**
- Night shift creates sleep deficit; CNS recovery rate is reduced
- Volume/intensity must stay within recovery window
- Deload every 4–6 weeks mandatory
- Sleep quality gates intensity more than calendar dates

## **2. CONFLICTING RECOMMENDATIONS & RESOLUTION**

### **Conflict #1: Back Squat Frequency**
- **Pavlukhin/Dozer:** 1×/week (axial spinal load)
- **Torokhtiy/Catalyst:** 2–3×/week (builds lower body strength)
- **Resolution:** Rotate—Week 1–3: 2×/week back squat @ 75–80%; Week 4–6: 1×/week back squat @ 80–85% + shift volume to front squat. This oscillates stimulus while respecting back pain constraints.

### **Conflict #2: Push Jerk vs. Split Jerk Introduction Timeline**
- **Berestov/Atletisty:** Integrate split jerk drills immediately (footwork development)
- **Torokhtiy/Sonny:** Defer split jerk until strength phase (push jerk mastery prerequisite)
- **Resolution:** Weeks 1–3 = push jerk + split jerk *unloaded drills* (footwork, hip position, no load). Weeks 4–6 = light loaded split jerk practice (40–50 kg) as accessory, primary jerk remains push. Strength phase (Week 7+) = transition to split jerk dominance.

### **Conflict #3: Intensity Prescription (Fixed % vs. RPE)**
- **Pavlukhin/Torokhtiy:** Strict % (70–80% Week 1, escalate predictably)
- **Catalyst/Sika:** RPE-based (adjust daily per fatigue/pain)
- **Resolution:** **Percentage anchors, RPE gate:** Use 75% as default working weight; if RPE > 8 or back pain present, drop to 70% or switch exercise. Log actual RPE; if consistent pattern emerges (e.g., always hard on Fridays), adjust schedule accordingly.

### **Conflict #4: Hypertrophy Rep Range (5–6 vs. 8–12)**
- **Berestov/Dozer:** 5–6 reps (neural + hypertrophy balance; lower injury risk)
- **Max Aita/Sika:** 8–12 reps (metabolic stress; joint-friendly for back pain)
- **Resolution:** **Undulating within block:** Weeks 1–2 = 6–8 reps (foundational); Weeks 3–4 = 8–10 reps (metabolic emphasis); Weeks 5–6 = back to 5–6 reps (transition toward strength). This maintains stimulus variance while managing accommodation.

## **4. PRIORITIZED PROGRAM ADJUSTMENTS FOR THIS ATHLETE**

### **Tier 1: Non-Negotiable (Pain/Limiter Management)**

1. **OHS 2–3×/week minimum**
   - Mon: Paused OHS 3×5 @ 40 kg (2-sec hold bottom)
   - Wed: Hip snatch 3×3 @ 50 kg (no jerk, pure OHS carryover)
   - Sat: Snatch balance 4×3 @ empty bar + light load (2–5 kg)
   - **Purpose:** Direct OHS limiter + confidence building

2. **Front squat 2×/week (back-friendly leg work)**
   - Back squat rotates in Week 1–2 (max 1×/week afterward)
   - Front squat: 4×5–6 @ 75–80% (safer lumbar profile)
   - Tempo on descent (2–3 sec) enforces position

3. **Posterior chain selective loading**
   - Hyperextensions 3×4–6 (slow eccentric, 2-sec hold) × 2–3×/week (machine-based, no axial load)
   - RDLs or rack pulls 2×/week (2–3 reps, controlled tempo)
   - **NO** heavy conventional deadlifts until back pain resolves

4. **Push jerk + split jerk progression**
   - Weeks 1–3: Push jerk 4×3 @ 70–75% (primary jerk)
   - Weeks 3–6: Add split jerk unloaded drills 5×1 per session
   - Week 5–6: Light loaded split jerk practice (40–50 kg) as accessory, not primary

   - Snatch pull with 3-sec pause at knee 3×2 (develops positional awareness)

6. **Jerk dip mechanics (power development without split demand)**

   - Behind-the-neck jerk (accessory) 2×3 @ light (shoulder mobility + dip awareness)

7. **Clean efficiency (reduce spinal load in catch)**
   - Rack position clean (clean from blocks at knee) 3×2 @ 80% (isolates pulling without catching ROM demand)
   - Clean pull from platform 3×2 @ 85% (maximum bar path refinement)
   - Power clean 3×2 @ 75% (option if full squat clean fatigues back)

### **Tier 3: Work Capacity & General Strength**

## **5. TOP 10 TECHNIQUE CUES (Priority Order)**

1. **"Chest Up, Back Extended Throughout Pull"** — Prevents lumbar flexion compensation; maintains upright torso in first/second pull (snatch/clean priority)

2. **"Pull Elbows High & Wide into Catch"** — Snatch-specific; high elbows prevent bar from drifting forward, improving OHS stability

3. **"Full Foot Contact Every Rep"** — Prevents early heel lift (indicator of backward bar drift); addresses common snatch error pattern

4. **"Receive High, Ride It Down"** — OHS-specific; catch at/above parallel, then control descent. Builds confidence under load (critical given weak 50 kg OHS)

5. **"Dip-Drive (Not Arm Press)"** — Jerk mechanics; emphasize legs driving bar, not arms; applies to push jerk dominance currently

6. **"Vertical Bar Path (Straight Up, Not Away)"** — Foundational; prevents forward torso lean, reduces lumbar shear

7. **"Knees Out, Hips Back, Chest Vertical"** — Squat/receiving position cue; distributes load away from lumbar spine

8. **"Triple Extension Synchronized (Ankles → Knees → Hips)"** — Not sequential; fires simultaneously; prevents hip-rise-early error that stresses back

9. **"Neutral Spine, Brace Core Before Load"** — Pre-lift protocol; ensures spinal stability before eccentric phase (critical for back pain management)

10. **"Tempo First, Speed Second"** — Warm-up sequence cue; move through positions deliberately before chasing bar speed. (Applies to night-shift fatigue—enforce quality before intensity)
