# Quit Repeating Your PRs!

# Analysis: PR Strategy & Attempt Planning

## Relevant to This Athlete

**Programming principle:**
- Pursue frequent 1 kg PRs over sporadic large jumps (5 kg+)
- Reduces failed attempts, maintains motivation—critical for night shift worker managing fatigue and chronic back pain
- Build confidence through incremental wins during hypertrophy block

**Attempt structure (relevant to Clean/Jerk):**
- Plan jump sequence *before* starting lifts
- For smaller maximal weights (Clean 80 kg, Jerk 65 kg):
  - Take weight 1 kg under current best, then 1 kg over for PR
  - Example: Clean—79 → 81 kg | Jerk—64 → 66 kg
- Use decreasing increments (e.g., 70 → 75 → 78 → 80 kg) to keep final jumps ≤3 kg
- Avoid unfamiliar jump sizes that break rhythm

**Key advice for this athlete:**
- 1 kg PRs on technical lifts (snatch, clean, jerk) > chasing big jumps while learning split jerk
- Pre-planned jumps reduce overthinking between attempts (helps manage night shift fatigue)
- Confidence matters: 99%+ certainty before attempting

**Not directly addressed:** OHS mobility work, split jerk progression, back pain management
