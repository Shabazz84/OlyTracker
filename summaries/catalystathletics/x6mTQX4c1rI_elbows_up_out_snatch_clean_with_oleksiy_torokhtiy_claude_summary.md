# Elbows Up & Out - Snatch & Clean with Oleksiy Torokhtiy

**Extraction:**

**1. Programming/Intensity:** None specified.

**2. Technique Cues (Clean/Snatch):**
- **Contact phase:** Aggressive, vertical bar trajectory (bar to self, not self to bar)
- **Elbow positioning:** Pull elbows up and out post-contact
- **Benefits of high elbows:**
  - Keeps bar close (reduces forward drift)
  - Stops upward body movement quickly
  - Accelerates body descent under bar
  - Preserves bar's upward momentum through turnover

**3. Exercise Recommendations:** None.

**4. Recovery/Mobility:** None.

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance:**
- **High priority for this athlete:** Back pain + weak OHS (50 kg) suggest poor bar positioning/posture. The aggressive elbow-up cue addresses forward bar drift, which loads the lower back and compromises OHS positioning. Correcting this contact pattern could improve both clean efficiency AND overhead stability.
- **Night shift consideration:** Fatigue on Wed–Sun may degrade aggressive bar contact. Needs deliberate cueing under fatigue.
