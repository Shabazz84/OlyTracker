# Snatch-Grip Overhead Carry with Hanging Weights | Olympic Weightlifting Exercise Library

**Analysis:**

**Exercise Recommendation:**
- Band-suspended barbell overhead walk (isometric stability drill)
- Goal: Upper back/shoulder stability maintenance during loaded movement
- Application: Addresses chronic back pain management + OHS weakness (50 kg limiter)

**Technique Cues:**
- Lock bar forcefully into snatch overhead position
- Maintain continuous active tension (upper back, shoulders, arms)
- Minimize bar/torso oscillation during movement

**Relevance to Athlete:**
- **HIGH relevance** — OHS is primary snatch limiter; this drill builds overhead stability without heavy loads
- **Mobility/Recovery benefit** — Active stability work suitable for night-shift recovery protocols; low CNS demand
- **Back-friendly** — Isometric hold avoids spinal compression unlike loaded squatting

**Consideration:**
- Use light band resistance initially given chronic back pain; prioritize tension quality over distance
- Fits hypertrophy block prep (GPP/accessory work phase)
