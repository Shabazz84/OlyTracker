# Laying Out The Training Week Schedule for Olympic Weightlifting

**1. Programming Principles**
*   **Frequency:** 5 days/week is standard for effective volume/intensity distribution; 3–6 days are viable based on life constraints.

*   **Rest Days:** Strategically placed after consecutive training blocks (e.g., Thursday rest before a Friday heavy day; Sunday rest for weekly recovery).
