# Bench Press for Olympic Weightlifters?

## Analysis

**Relevant to this athlete: MODERATELY**

### Key Extraction:

**1. Exercise Recommendation**
- **Avoid bench press** — not necessary for Olympic weightlifting
- **Prioritize press & push press variations** instead for upper body strength/mass with lower mobility risk
- *Relevant*: This athlete has chronic back pain and limited OHS (50 kg), suggesting mobility constraints. Press variations are safer than bench pressing.

**2. Conditional Criteria for Bench Press**
- Requires: excellent overhead mobility + good shoulder health
- Only if: severe upper body strength deficit OR hypertrophy need + willingness to maintain mobility work
- *Relevant*: Not indicated for this athlete—mobility is already a limiter (OHS 50 kg vs. BS 118 kg = significant gap)

**3. Programming Implication**
- In the 6-week hypertrophy block: Use **press/push press over bench** to build upper body mass while protecting overhead mobility for snatch development
