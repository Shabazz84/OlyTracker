# Shoulder Position in Snatch & Clean Start

**TECHNIQUE CUE — Snatch & Clean Setup**

**Shoulder Position (Starting):**
- Shoulders should be **directly above the bar** (shoulder joint vertically aligned)
- Front of shoulder mass will be *slightly* in front of the bar
- Arms hang approximately vertical (side view)
- Taller athletes may shift slightly forward, but only *slightly*—no significant forward advantage exists
- Never move shoulders *back* from this position; no benefit to doing so

**Dynamic Start Consideration:**
- Shoulders may be slightly *behind* the bar at floor break (common in dynamic starts)
- This is acceptable *if* shoulders move into optimal position immediately and consistently

---

**RELEVANCE TO ATHLETE:**
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** This athlete needs to verify shoulder mobility in setup. A clean starting position with shoulders properly stacked will support later OHS development.
- **No specific accommodation for back pain mentioned**, but proper shoulder positioning (avoiding excessive forward lean) may help reduce lumbar stress.
- **Clean priority:** At 80 kg clean, solidifying this startup position is foundational before intensifying.
