# The Stupidest Way to Miss Jerks

1. **Programming principles**: None mentioned in this excerpt; text focuses solely on technical execution of a specific movement phase.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - **Jerk Recovery**: Stay active overhead while recovering from the split; do not "quit" pushing up aggressively into the bar.
   - **Bar Path Control**: When stepping back, push the bar slightly backward to keep it directly above the body (prevents moving the body out from under the weight).
   - **Upper Back**: Keep upper back locked in tightly at all times.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specific; implies practice of the split jerk recovery phase and overhead stability drills.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned.
5. **Athlete profile relevance**: 
   - **Critical for Push Jerk Only**: The cue to "push the bar back slightly" is vital for a push-jerk athlete who lacks the forward foot placement of a split jerk to stabilize, as they must rely entirely on vertical force and upper body tension.
   - **Back Pain Warning**: The instruction to keep the "upper back locked in tightly" directly addresses chronic back pain by promoting thoracic rigidity and spinal neutrality during high-load overhead phases.
