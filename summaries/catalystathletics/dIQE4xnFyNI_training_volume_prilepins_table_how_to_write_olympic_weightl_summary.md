For this athlete's hypertrophy block, ensure the "primary training" volume starts low and works up to avoid lingering fatigue masking true adaptation.

*   **Timing is Critical:** Do not perform high-volume accessory work (e.g., "blasting the trunk") the day before heavy squat or clean sessions, as local fatigue will mechanically limit performance. Schedule these on the same day as the lifts they affect to allow recovery time before the next iteration.
*   **Fatigue Management:** Start volume low and increase gradually. If an athlete feels fatigued early in a block, it is likely due to excessive starting volume rather than current load; reducing further will lead to under-training once fatigue clears.
