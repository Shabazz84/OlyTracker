# The Stupidest Way to Miss Jerks

# Analysis

**Technique cues (Jerk):**
- Stay active overhead during split recovery; bring bar with you
- Keep upper back locked tight; never stop pushing aggressively into bar
- Step back while pushing bar slightly backward to maintain vertical alignment
- Avoid moving body out from under weight (causes instability/escape)

**Relevance to athlete:**
- **Critical for this athlete**: Currently only does push jerk. These cues emphasize upper back stability and aggressive overhead positioning—foundational for transitioning to split jerk once technique/strength phase begins (post-hypertrophy block).
