# Leg Drive Not Jumping - Snatch & Clean

1. **Programming principles**: Not specified in text (focuses on movement mechanics).
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Visualize the bar as "stretching shoulders away from feet" rather than extending the body up.
   - Do not jump; jumping delays the pull under the bar and wastes upward momentum.
   - At the top of the pull, use arms to change direction immediately to begin pulling down.
   - Feet should leave the floor only when the body is already moving downward.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specified (text describes movement patterns for snatch/clean).
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned.
5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: 
   - The "no jump" cue is critical for an intermediate athlete with chronic back pain to reduce spinal shear forces during the pull.
   - Correcting the timing of foot movement (only when moving down) addresses common technique errors that could exacerbate injury risk in a novice Olympic lifter.
