# Stomping & Early Foot Lift in the Snatch & Clean

2. **Technique cues**: 
   - **Snatch/Clean Footwork**: Prioritize a stable, balanced receiving position with feet directly under the bar; avoid forward/backward/outward drift.
   - **Timing Error Correction**: Do not lift feet prematurely to "stomp harder." Maintain floor contact and upward push while opening hips; only lift feet momentarily after initiating the pull-down phase.
