# Find Your Snatch Grip Width

# Weightlifting Analysis

**Technique Cues (Snatch):**
1. **Grip width:** Position bar in hip crease during standing position
2. **Stability test:** Should maintain position without arm bend when leaning forward or lifting knee
3. **Bar placement:** Sit just above pubic bone in soft tissue—avoid direct pubic bone contact (reduces pain, maintains explosivity)
4. **Individual adjustments:** Long arms/short torso athletes may need wider grip; prioritize high thigh placement over "perfect" position
5. **Goal:** Maximize bar-body interaction, reduce unwanted bar bounce

**Relevance to This Athlete:**
- **Chronic back pain concern:** Proper hip crease contact is critical—poor bar positioning could exacerbate back issues during snatch pulls
- **OHS limiter (50 kg):** Correct grip/positioning improves receiving position stability and confidence
- **Actionable:** Verify grip width before hypertrophy block begins; correct positioning may unlock snatch progress

**Not covered:** Programming, recovery, or shift-work adaptations.
