## 2. **Technique Cues - Clean Pull**
- **Keep bar close to legs** without dragging excessively upward
- **Avoid rowing the bar** (common mistake: using arms to lift instead of pull)
- **Think "push bar back"** rather than "pull bar in" — tighten lats and retract bar at current height
- **Maintain relaxed arms** from shoulders to transmit leg/hip power efficiently
- **Allows proper positioning** for pulling under the bar
