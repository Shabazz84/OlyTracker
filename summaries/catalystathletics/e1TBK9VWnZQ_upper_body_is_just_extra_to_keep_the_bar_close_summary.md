# Upper Body is Just Extra to Keep the Bar Close

1. **Programming principles**: None explicitly stated in this excerpt; focuses on force vector mechanics rather than volume, intensity, or periodization structures.
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Minimize horizontal (forward) bar displacement by prioritizing vertical acceleration over upper-body "damage control."
   - The pull is the primary driver for bar path control; pulling under is secondary and additive, not a substitute for poor initial pulls.

5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: 
   - **Critical for Jerk**: As an athlete restricted to push jerks (no split), the "bar bouncing forward" issue is dangerous as it compromises stability and increases injury risk without a foot movement to recover balance.
   - **Back Pain Context**: Relying on upper body to counteract bar bounce places excessive shear force on the lumbar spine, directly exacerbating chronic back pain.
   - **Transition Phase**: Reinforces that technique (vertical pull) must precede strength gains; "damage control" via brute force is a barrier to transitioning from powerlifting-style lifting to Olympic mechanics.
