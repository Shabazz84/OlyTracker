# Always Start with Leg Push in the Snatch & Clean

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**1. Technique Cue (Snatch/Clean):**
- Initiate with leg drive (pushing straight down into ground)
- Benefits: maintains balance, prevents early shoulder retraction, prevents excessive hip drive, keeps bar closer and faster

**2. Specific Relevance to Athlete:**
- **OHS limitation (50 kg):** Proper bar path/positioning from this cue directly impacts overhead stability and snatch receiving position
- **Back pain consideration:** Excessive hip drive (which this cue prevents) can exacerbate chronic back issues; controlled leg drive is safer
- **Night shift work:** Clear, fundamental cueing like this is easier to execute when fatigued

**3. NOT Addressed:**
- No programming (sets/reps/intensity)
- No jerk-specific guidance (split jerk progression needed)
- No recovery/mobility advice
- No periodization for 6-week hypertrophy block

**Recommendation:** Use this leg-drive principle as foundational positioning work, especially in hang positions to groove the pattern with lighter loads before building volume in hypertrophy block.
