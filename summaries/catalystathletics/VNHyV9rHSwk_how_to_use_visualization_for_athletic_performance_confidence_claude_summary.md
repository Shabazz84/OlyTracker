# How to Use Visualization for Athletic Performance & Confidence

**Mental skills/psychology content, not programming or technique coaching.**

**Relevant to athlete profile:**
- **Recovery tool for night shift worker**: Visualization requires no physical recovery cost and can be done anytime (pre-sleep, post-wake, between sets)—useful given Wed–Sun night shift fatigue constraints
- **Confidence building for weak lifts**: Specific application for snatch/OHS (limiting factors)—use process visualization to mentally rehearse proper OHS positioning and snatch technique under load before attempting
- **Back pain consideration**: Visualization of *successful* recovery positions (clean catch, OHS stability) may reinforce proper mechanics that protect the spine

**Implementation suggestion for this athlete:**
- Start with single-lift focus: visualize OHS depth + stability (primary snatch limiter)
- Practice immediately pre-sleep or between training sets
- Include sensory details of bar contact, foot positioning, and the "feeling" of solid overhead stability
