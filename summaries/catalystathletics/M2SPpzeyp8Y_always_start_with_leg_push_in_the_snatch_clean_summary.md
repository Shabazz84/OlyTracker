# Always Start with Leg Push in the Snatch & Clean

1. **Programming principles**: Not specified in text (focuses solely on movement mechanics).
2. **Technique cues**: "Push straight down with legs" from any starting position to maintain balance, prevent early shoulder retraction, avoid excessive forward hip drive, and guide the bar close/faster.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None listed; general application applies to Snatch, Clean, Hang variations, and Blocks.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned.
5. **Profile relevance**: The cue "prevent bringing shoulders back too soon" is critical for this athlete's OHS weakness (snatch limiter) and chronic back pain management by ensuring proper torso angle before the pull transitions to vertical extension.
