# Does Your Dedication Match Your Goals?

This text is about weightlifting coaching principles regarding mindset and dedication, but it contains **no specific technical, programming, exercise, recovery, or mobility advice** relevant to the athlete's profile (e.g., no mention of sets/reps, cues for snatch/clean/jerk, specific exercises like OHS, or strategies for night shift work/back pain).

It focuses entirely on psychological commitment and lifestyle alignment.
