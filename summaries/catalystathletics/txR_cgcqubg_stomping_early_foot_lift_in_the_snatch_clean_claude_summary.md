# Stomping & Early Foot Lift in the Snatch & Clean

**TECHNIQUE CUES RELEVANT TO ATHLETE:**

1. **Foot Positioning (Snatch/Clean)**
   - Replace feet directly under bar in sound squat position
   - Prevents instability and balance issues in receiving position

2. **Foot Timing (Critical Issue)**
   - Continue pushing through floor while opening hips
   - Lift feet only *after* hip extension completes, not before
   - Common error: picking feet up too early to generate more power
   - Consequence of early foot lift: reduced bar acceleration, forward bar drift, slower turnover

3. **Method Agnostic**
   - Shuffle vs. stomp doesn't matter—consistency and timing do
   - Focus on *when* not *how*

**RELEVANCE TO THIS ATHLETE:**
- Given chronic back pain and weak OHS (50 kg), premature foot lift could cause forward bar drift → incomplete squat position → excessive low back strain
- Foot timing work is low-load, high-value technique refinement suitable for a night shift worker (manageable CNS demand)
- Prioritize timing cues during 6-week hypertrophy block before progressing loads
