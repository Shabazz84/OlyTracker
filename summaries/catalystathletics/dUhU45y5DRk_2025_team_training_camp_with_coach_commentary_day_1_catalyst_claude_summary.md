# Analysis: Training Camp Commentary

- **Camp structure**: Morning technique-oriented sessions → afternoon main sessions (2-3 hr back-to-back lifting)

## **Technique Cues**

- **Hip-dominant athletes**: Use complexes (jump→pull→snatch) to reestablish leg drive awareness
- **Philosophy**: No "one true way"—technical style varies among elites; correct only if benefit > performance cost
