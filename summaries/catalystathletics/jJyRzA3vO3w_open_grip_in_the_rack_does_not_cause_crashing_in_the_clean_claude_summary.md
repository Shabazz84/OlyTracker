# Open Grip in the Rack Does NOT Cause Crashing in the Clean

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS:**

**2. Technique cue (Clean):**
- Maintain bar contact with body throughout pull and transition—don't let gap form between bar and torso
- Open/relax grip ONLY after bar reaches shoulders and elbows drive forward
- Premature grip opening causes crashes, not grip width itself

**Relevance to athlete:**
- At 80 kg clean, this athlete likely needs contact consistency work before heavy rack position holds
- Prioritize bar path/positioning over grip variation during 6-week hypertrophy block
- This addresses a common intermediate error that compounds under fatigue (relevant for night shift recovery demands)
