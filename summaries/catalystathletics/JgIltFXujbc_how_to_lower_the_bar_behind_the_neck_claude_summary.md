# How to Lower the Bar Behind the Neck

**Relevant Extracts for This Athlete:**

1. **Technique Cue (Behind-Neck Press/OHS):**
   - Squeeze shoulder blades together to create traps shelf (not on neck/spine)
   - Keep trunk upright—only forward lean matching overhead position
   - Avoid ballistic negative (good morning collapse)
   - *Highly relevant: OHS is his primary snatch limiter (50 kg)*

2. **Safety Principle:**
   - Don't load behind-neck work if shoulder mobility/stability insufficient
   - *Relevant to chronic back pain + OHS weakness*

3. **Exercise Recommendation:**
   - Behind-neck pressing variation mentioned
   - *Could address shoulder/thoracic mobility for snatch development, but secondary to OHS improvements*
