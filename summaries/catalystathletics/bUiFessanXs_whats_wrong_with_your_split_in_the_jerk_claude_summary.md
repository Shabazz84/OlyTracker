# Analysis

**Not directly applicable to this athlete's current phase.**

## Relevant points for future reference:

**Technique cues (for later split jerk work):**
- Stronger leg typically forward (not necessarily dominant leg)
- Front shin vertical; back knee under hip in split position
- Feet slide/step directly into split (minimize upward trajectory)
- Front heel lifts early; back heel lifted, forefoot planted
- Balance unchanged from dip through split
- Two prerequisites: correct split position + adequate dip/drive power

**Programming implication:**
Split jerk introduction should wait until push jerk is solid AND dip/drive mechanics are strong enough to generate adequate acceleration. Currently, prioritize:
- Push jerk refinement
- OHS progression (50 kg is limiting snatch)
- Dip/drive strength in jerk position
- Back health management (chronic pain noted)

**Recommendation:** Shelve this for Phase 2 (post-hypertrophy block) when transitioning to strength/technique focus.
