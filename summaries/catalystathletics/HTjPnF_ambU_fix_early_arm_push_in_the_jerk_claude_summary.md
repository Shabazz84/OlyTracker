# Fix Early Arm Push in the Jerk

# Analysis

**Technique Cues (Jerk):**
- **Early arm push problem:** Cue athlete to open hands completely in rack position to reset
- **Stability drill:** Feel bar "settled and securely connected to trunk" before initiating leg drive
- **Behind-the-neck variation:** Teaches leg trust and stability; stronger feel than front rack
- **Application:** Use in warm-up sets to reinforce leg-drive priority over arm push

**Exercise Recommendations:**
- Behind-the-neck jerk variations (for this athlete: valuable given push jerk-only limitation; addresses stability/confidence for future split jerk progression)

**Relevance to Athlete:**
- **High priority:** Push jerk-only status suggests arm dominance issue. These cues directly address premature arm engagement.
- **Back pain consideration:** Behind-the-neck position may be worth testing for bar path comfort vs. front rack
- **Timing:** Incorporate into warm-ups during hypertrophy block and into strength block prep work

**Not addressed:** Frequency, sets/reps, or periodization specifics for jerk work.
