# Squats: Pause vs Bounce

**RELEVANT EXTRACTS FOR THIS ATHLETE:**

**1. Programming/Exercise Recommendations:**
- Prioritize **bounce/rebound front squats** over pause squats for Olympic lifting prep
- Pause squats alone are insufficient for clean development—they don't train reactive strength needed in heavy cleans
- Include bounce front squats as "a good portion of your training"

**2. Technique Principle:**
- Heavy cleans require resisting far greater downward force than static front squats due to impact velocity
- Explains why this athlete (80 kg clean vs. theoretical 118 kg back squat capability) likely collapses under heavy cleans—trunk/postural strength gap
- Bounce squats train timing, aggressiveness, and joint conditioning for that specific demand

**3. Critical for This Athlete's Profile:**
- **Back pain consideration:** Rebound squats create greater spinal loading than pause squats. With chronic back pain, monitor tolerance carefully and ensure proper bracing mechanics
- **Relevant limiter:** Weak OHS (50 kg) + collapsing in cleans suggests trunk stability is a primary constraint; front squat strength (especially reactive) should be priority

**RECOMMENDATION:** Include rebound front squats in hypertrophy block, but prioritize form and spinal stability given chronic back pain. May need modified tempos initially.
