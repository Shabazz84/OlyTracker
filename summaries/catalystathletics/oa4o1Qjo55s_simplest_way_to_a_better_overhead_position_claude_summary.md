# Simplest Way to a Better Overhead Position

**1. Programming Principles**
- Snatch push press recommended as warm-up accessory (frequency: each overhead session)

**2. Technique Cues**
- **Overhead position**: Squeeze upper inside edges of shoulder blades together; push head forward
- **Drive phase**: Bar travels straight up; head and trunk position unchanged
- **Support phase**: Imagine supporting weight with upper back; lock shoulder blades in
- **Hold**: 1-second isometric hold overhead before lowering

**3. Exercise Recommendations**
- Snatch push press as warm-up/reinforcement tool for overhead position strength

**4. Recovery/Mobility**
- None provided

**5. Athlete-Specific Relevance**
- **HIGHLY RELEVANT**: OHS is primary snatch limiter (50 kg). Snatch push press directly addresses overhead position strength/stability—critical prerequisite for improving OHS and snatch technique.
- **Back pain consideration**: Shoulder blade stability and upper back engagement cues may help reduce compensatory lumbar strain during snatching.
- **Practical fit**: 2–3 reps during warm-ups manageable for night shift schedule.
