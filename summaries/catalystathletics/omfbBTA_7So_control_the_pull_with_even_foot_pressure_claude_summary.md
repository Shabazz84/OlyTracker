# Control the Pull with Even Foot Pressure

**RELEVANT EXTRACTION:**

**2. Technique Cue (Snatch/Clean):**
- **Primary focus:** Push through whole foot evenly during pull phase
- **Benefit:** Maintains balance over midfoot, which naturally corrects bar position and body positioning
- **Application:** Simplifies cueing by reducing conscious thought—let proprioception organize the lift rather than overthinking multiple positions

**Relevance to athlete:**
- **Highly applicable** — This cueing approach suits intermediate lifters struggling with technical complexity
- **Especially useful for snatch** given OHS is stated as primary limiter; balanced positioning through pull sets foundation for overhead stability
- **Back-pain friendly** — Proper midfoot balance reduces spinal stress vs. forward-shifted or heels-elevated positions

**Note:** No programming, recovery/mobility, or frequency recommendations in this text.
