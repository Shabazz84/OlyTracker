# How to Re-Rack Jerks

1. **Programming principles**: None (text focuses on a single movement skill, not periodization).
2. **Technique cues**: "Stand tall on toes" to meet the bar; "guide the bar down with arms"; "keep trunk vertical and braced forcefully."
3. **Exercise recommendations**: Safe re-racking drills for jerks.
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None (text focuses on acute safety mechanics).
5. **Athlete profile relevance**: Critical for your chronic back pain; the cue to "absorb weight by bending knees" and maintain a vertical trunk reduces spinal shear forces during the high-stress re-rack phase of push jerks, which is safer than catching in arms or rounding the spine.
