# Don't Lift the Bar in the Snatch & Clean

1. **Programming principles**: Not specified in text (focuses on mindset over volume/intensity).
2. **Technique cues**: "Don't lift the bar, lift your body"; focus on driving through the floor and extending upward so the bar follows; treat the bar as attached to the shoulders rather than lifting it with arms.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: None specified (general principle applies to all Olympic lifts).
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None specified.
5. **Athlete-specific relevance**: Critical for correcting the athlete's tendency to "lift with arms" and upper body tension, which is likely contributing to their OHS limitation (50 kg) and potentially exacerbating chronic back pain by shifting load incorrectly; applicable during the upcoming technique/strength phase.
