# Your Knees Should NOT be Caving In During the Pull!

This text is not about weightlifting programming or coaching for an intermediate athlete; it focuses exclusively on knee valgus (knees diving in) correction and foot stability drills using resistance bands, depth jumps, and orthotics. It contains no specific data regarding sets/reps, intensity, periodization, or the athlete's specific lifting goals (hypertrophy block, split jerk transition).
