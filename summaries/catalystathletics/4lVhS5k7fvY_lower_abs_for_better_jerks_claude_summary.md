# Lower Abs for Better Jerks

**RELEVANT EXTRACTION:**

**Technique Cue (Jerk):**
- Focus on lower ab tension during bracing—push abs down rather than sucking in
- Purpose: stabilize pelvis/lower back, improve dip control and drive power

**Direct Relevance to Athlete:**
- High priority for this athlete: chronic back pain + push jerk only (no split jerk = heavier reliance on dip stability)
- Lower ab bracing addresses both jerk weakness AND back stability needs
- Should be integrated into jerk-specific accessory work during hypertrophy block

**Recommendation:** Implement this bracing cue in dip progressions (paused dips, pin dips, or jerk dips at 60-70% intensity) before full jerk attempts.
