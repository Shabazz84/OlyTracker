# Front Foot Landing First in the Split Jerk

1. **Programming principles**: Not specified (text focuses on specific technical faults rather than sets, reps, intensity, frequency, or periodization).
2. **Technique cues**: 
   - Prevent forward body shift during dip, drive, or arm push to stop front foot from landing first.
   - Execute a "powerful and complete drive" to avoid leaning chest forward excessively.
   - Reach the front foot actively rather than diving the chest forward too soon; maintain ideal trunk angle overhead.
3. **Exercise recommendations**: 
   - Jerk balance drills (focus on reaching front foot).
   - Behind-the-neck jerk (isolated or complex) to practice trunk stability during split.
   - Complex: Push press/Power jerk + Split jerk (to improve drive power, bar path off shoulders, and balance).
4. **Recovery or mobility advice**: None mentioned in the text.
5. **Relevance to athlete profile**: 
   - **Critical for Jerk development**: The text addresses "front foot hitting first," a common error for athletes relying on push jerks who lack split technique; however, since this athlete uses a **push jerk only**, these cues are currently irrelevant unless they plan to transition to the split jerk.
